For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, ModeX offers a concrete schema for evaluator-free selection traces. A reusable record should retain benchmark and item revision, target model and decoding parameters, seed, candidate count, every candidate token stream, prefix snapshots, per-pair 1/2/3-gram overlaps, adjacency matrices, Laplacian and Fiedler vector, proposed partitions, conductance, cluster-size and tie decisions, pruning timestamps, retained/rejected IDs, final weighted degrees, centroid ID, and task-specific outcome. Such traces support comparison with reward-model Best-of-N, studies of candidate diversity and modal error, threshold calibration, online-pruning audits, and test-time compute accounting. The official implementation enables reproduction of the algorithm, but absent released candidate and graph records, reuse remains code- and recipe-level rather than a ready-made reasoning-data corpus.
