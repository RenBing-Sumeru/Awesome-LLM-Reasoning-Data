Ablations compare conductance thresholds, pruning intervals, n-gram components, and embedding alternatives; appendix experiments extend to Qwen2.5-14B/32B and GPT-4 on AIME2025. These results show how the selector behaves under the reported candidate pools and metrics. They do not establish that modal clusters are correct, that candidate traces are complete or released, that lexical similarity is calibrated across domains, or that higher benchmark scores prove better data quality.
