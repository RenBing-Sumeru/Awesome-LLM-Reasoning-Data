The clearest headline result is on AIME24 average-at-8. The PDF reports that TDAR-8B with BACD and TCCF improves over the strong TraDo-8B baseline by 11.2 accuracy points while reaching a 2.26-fold speedup. Its front-page figure separately reports 11.7 percentage points and 1.71-fold speedup for the illustrated configuration, so readers should retain the exact configuration attached to either number rather than combine them.

This evidence supports the paper's efficiency-effectiveness claim within its TDAR benchmark setting. It does not establish that confidence-controlled diffusion decoding dominates autoregressive sampling on all models, nor that token-per-forward is a substitute for end-to-end serving latency. The released implementation is valuable because the block schedule is central to the claim and cannot be reconstructed from a final-answer score alone.
