The authors begin with TDAR-8B-Thinking, then apply Progressive Block Size Extension so that larger generation blocks remain viable. During decoding, BACD uses confidence to select denoising behavior. During reasoning, TCCF assigns coarse blocks to broad exploration and fine blocks to the critic-style refinement phase. These controls act jointly rather than treating speed and reasoning quality as separate post-processing problems.

Evaluation compares TDAR variants with diffusion and block-diffusion baselines using mathematical reasoning, reporting accuracy and token-per-forward style efficiency. The paper also releases code and models through the TDAR repository. For a replication, the relevant unit is not merely the output answer: it is the chosen sequence of block sizes, denoising work, and final refinement under the reported inference configuration.
