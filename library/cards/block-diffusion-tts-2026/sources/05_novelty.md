The closest work scales test-time reasoning by producing more full candidates or by changing a conventional decoder's sampling rule. This paper instead makes the diffusion model's internal generation resolution a first-class resource: BACD controls local denoising, and TCCF changes block size according to whether the trajectory is exploring or refining. That coupling is the auditable new decision surface.

The contribution is not a new external reward model or a generic claim that block diffusion is faster. Its novelty is the two-level allocation policy and the accompanying comparison on long reasoning. Before transferring the result, readers should inspect whether confidence remains meaningful at larger blocks, whether the refinement critic is robust to early errors, and whether the same schedule survives a different diffusion backbone.
