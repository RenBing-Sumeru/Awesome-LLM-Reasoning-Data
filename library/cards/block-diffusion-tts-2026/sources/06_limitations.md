The reported gain is tied to TDAR-8B, its mathematical reasoning setup, and the particular block and denoising schedules. Confidence errors can make BACD save work at precisely the wrong time, while coarse blocks may introduce mistakes that later refinement cannot repair. The paper reports speed through decoder-oriented measures; deployment should additionally benchmark batching, memory pressure, wall-clock latency, and model-quality changes at each block size.
