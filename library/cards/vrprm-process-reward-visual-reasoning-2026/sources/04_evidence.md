The authors compare a non-thinking PRM, an SFT-only CoT-PRM, and two-stage VRPRM from the same base. With only 3.6K CoT-SFT and 50K RL examples, VRPRM surpasses a non-thinking PRM trained on about 400K labels; in Best-of-N experiments, the largest relative improvement over the base model reaches 118%. The result supports the two-stage data recipe, although the relative gain depends on the candidate pool and tasks.

The authors also compare SFT-only and non-thinking-label configurations, supporting the complementarity of the two stages.
