VRPRM3.6K can add explicit review reasoning to an existing PRM, followed by second-stage RL on proprietary binary step labels, producing interpretable step scores or Best-of-N rankings. Non-thinking PRM, CoT-SFT, and two-stage variants should be compared on the same candidate pool, with audits of consistency between rationales and scores. Tasks whose reasoning cannot be visualized require a redesigned input representation.

The final output can be step rankings and candidate-selection reports with explicit reasoning.
