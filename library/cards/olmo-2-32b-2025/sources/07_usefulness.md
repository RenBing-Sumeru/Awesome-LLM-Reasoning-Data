Use this Card to distinguish three levels of evidence: an official release link, a data viewer exposing selected fields and records, and a reproducible construction or feedback contract. OLMo 2 32B is strong at the first two levels, while several ingredients needed for the third remain unresolved.

For post-training curation, it provides concrete objects to track: SFT `messages`/`source`, RLVR ground truth and constraints, on-policy preferences, date-cutoff filtering, and five-completion majority selection. The Card also identifies what cannot be assumed from those objects, especially GRPO reward behavior and source rights.

The linked artifacts can support record inspection and research reproduction of released components. They should not be represented as proof of a uniform licence, complete dataset provenance, fully specified RLVR, or end-to-end 32B checkpoint reproduction.
