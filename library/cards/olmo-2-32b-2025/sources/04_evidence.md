Ai2's release directly links base, SFT, DPO, and instruct checkpoints; pretraining, mid-training, SFT, preference, and RLVR datasets; and OLMo-core. The model and code are Apache 2.0. The linked SFT and RLVR viewers expose the stated train sizes and record fields, so this is evidence of selected inspectable post-training artifacts rather than a purely narrative claim.

The licensing evidence is mixed at the data level. OLMo-Mix, Dolmino, and the RLVR card show ODC-BY; the preference-mix card says ODC-BY-1.0 while also listing subset-specific licences and some non-commercial portions. Thus, an open model/code licence and publicly viewable data artifacts do not establish one uniform commercial or redistribution right for every source.

Ai2 also documents evaluation-tooling and formatting/extraction caveats for selected family comparisons. That disclosure is relevant to interpreting reported evaluation deltas, but it does not show that 32B's data recipe or reward process alone caused the results.
