OLMo 2 32B is notable for linking a broad artifact chain across pretraining, mid-training, SFT, on-policy preference, RLVR, checkpoints, and core code. It also gives concrete 32B-specific disclosures: a synthetic-date filter, five-completion majority selection for specified math data, and GRPO RLVR task families.

For this atlas, the important novelty is not a blanket claim of openness. It is the ability to audit several release layers separately: model/code licensing, data-card fields, mix-level licences, synthetic-data construction rules, and the remaining absence of a complete reward and provenance contract. That makes it a useful disclosure-limited frontier case rather than a fully reconstructed recipe.
