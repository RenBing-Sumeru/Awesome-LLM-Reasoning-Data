Claude 3.7 Sonnet is presented as a hybrid reasoning model: users can request a direct answer or expose a separately displayed extended-thinking block with a controllable token budget. For a reasoning-data atlas, the central question is not whether the model scores well, but what training objects, feedback rules, inference controls, and evaluation records its system card actually makes auditable.

The official report describes a proprietary source mixture, human feedback, Constitutional AI during reinforcement learning, reinforcement-learning-trained extended thinking, and several safety and capability evaluations. Most underlying prompts, candidates, traces, labels, rewards, environments, and logs remain closed. This Card therefore treats the report as a partial disclosure ledger, not as a dataset release or a reproducible recipe.
