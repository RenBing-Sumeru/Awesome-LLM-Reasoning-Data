For the Frontier Reports and Data Disclosure Ledger track, this Card is useful as a worked boundary map. It identifies the one relatively concrete training object—classifier-derived appropriate-harmlessness preference pairs—and keeps it separate from RL-trained extended thinking, a deployed streaming safety monitor, evaluation graders, and test-time scaffolds. That separation prevents a named classifier from being misreported as a fully specified reward model.

Use the Card to compare frontier reports on source disclosure, feedback contracts, reasoning-trace lineage, inference budgets, monitorability, reward hacking, and evaluation reproducibility. It can also supply an audit checklist for future releases: publish record schemas and counts, source rights, classifier versions and calibration, accepted and rejected examples, rollout settings, objective and checkpoint lineage, environment pins, split and decontamination evidence, and replay logs. Do not use benchmark performance to infer hidden data quality or reconstruct Anthropic's pipeline.
