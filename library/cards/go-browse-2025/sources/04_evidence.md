Table 1 reports 9,504 successful and 17,245 unsuccessful trajectories, totaling 26,749 trajectories and 196,462 steps; the successful/failure step counts are 39,339 and 157,123. The current raw Hugging Face train split independently exposes 196,462 rows, while the processed split exposes 164,533 step records, so those two releases must not be described as the same row set. The paper evaluates the successful-only SFT model on 812 WebArena tasks and reports 21.7% overall success versus 8.3% for the base Qwen model and 18.8% for the matched NNetNav-7B comparison; on the 300-task Online-Mind2Web evaluation, Go-Browse-7B scores 5.33%. These results support that the collection can train the evaluated agent under the reported setup, but they do not prove that every task, VLM judgment, trajectory, screenshot, or released row is correct or reusable. The official repository and Hugging Face pages verify that code, raw and processed datasets, and a Qwen checkpoint exist; they do not provide a full dataset license, provenance/privacy audit, checksumed manifest, or exhaustive mapping from the paper table to the mutable Hub revisions.
