Data collection runs on BrowserGym over five WebArena clones—Shopping Admin, Shopping, Reddit, GitLab, and Map—with 20 URLs explored per domain. Claude-3.7-Sonnet runs NavExplorer for at most 15 steps; PageExplorer uses GPT-4o for at most 20 steps and Claude-3.7-Sonnet for at most 10. Each proposed task receives up to three 10-step Claude feasibility attempts; a GPT-4o VLM-as-a-judge determines success, and at most 30 feasible tasks are retained per URL. For each feasible task, GPT-4o-mini and Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct Solvers produce two trajectories starting at the current page and two starting at the website root, all with a 10-step horizon and temperature 0.7. The raw exporter preserves graph, node, trajectory, per-step action/thought/observation metadata and PNG screenshots; the processed exporter converts trajectories to step-level prompt/completion records linked by `next_step_idx`, with reward, length, and step number. The paper trains Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct by SFT only on successful trajectories and evaluates at temperature 0 with WebArena task-specific reward functions. Reproduction requires a working BrowserGym/WebArena deployment, model/API access, filled path and credential placeholders in the repository, and a pinned dataset/repository revision; the README provides commands but no turnkey immutable run manifest.
