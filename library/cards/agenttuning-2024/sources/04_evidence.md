The decisive transfer comparison keeps the Llama-2-chat backbone family and evaluates the same six held-out tasks before and after AgentTuning. At 70B, the normalized held-out overall score rises from 0.51 to 1.40 (+176%); MiniWoB++ rises from 0.7 to 60.7, while the general-task normalized score changes from 0.95 to 0.96 (Table 3). This supports transfer beyond the six training environments without a measured aggregate general-capability loss, but it does not isolate teacher strength or prove transfer to substantially harder worlds.

The quality ablation compares unfiltered traces with filtered traces and with filtered traces plus CoT on the 7B setup. Held-out normalized score moves from 0.47 to 0.56 after reward filtering and to 0.65 when CoT is retained (Table 2), while held-in moves from 1.34 to 1.38 to 1.96. This supports the paper's filtering-and-rationale contract in this dataset, but reward-level correctness still does not validate every thought step.
