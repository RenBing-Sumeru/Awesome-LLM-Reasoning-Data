Use JudgeLM as a design reference for open judge training, judge-bias audits, and teacher-distillation evaluation surfaces. Preserve task seed, candidate answers, reference answer, GPT-4 teacher prompt/version, teacher judgment, model verdict, parsed score, answer order, bias-mitigation setting, and split. In atlas recipes it is most useful for studying how a feedback model is trained and audited, not for claiming objective correctness of downstream answers.
