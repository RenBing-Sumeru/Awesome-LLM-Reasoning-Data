The official repository reports JudgeLM as an ICLR 2025 Spotlight and states that JudgeLM-100K contains 100K training judge samples plus 5K validation samples with GPT-4-generated judgments. It reports over 90% agreement with the teacher judge and lists model-table results: for example, JudgeLM-33B with reference reaches 89.32 agreement, 84.98 F1, and 92.37 consistency in the shown table; JudgeLM-7B is reported to judge 5K samples in about 3 minutes with 8 A100 GPUs. The row-level evidence is agreement with a GPT-4-generated judgment, not a programmatic verifier. The evidence boundary is therefore the teacher prompt/version, response distribution, reference availability, parser, and bias controls; high teacher agreement does not certify factual correctness in every open-ended task.
