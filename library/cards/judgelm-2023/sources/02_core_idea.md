The contribution is to fine-tune open LLMs into scalable judges using GPT-4-generated judgments, then evaluate their agreement and bias behavior. The core mechanism builds JudgeLM-100K for training and a 5K validation/evaluation set, trains Vicuna/LLaMA-based JudgeLM models at 7B, 13B, and 33B scales, and adds mitigation techniques for position, knowledge, and format bias. The evaluation surface is judge agreement, precision/recall/F1, and consistency against the GPT-4 teacher or benchmark labels. Closest comparisons include PandaLM, Vicuna-style GPT-4 judges, MT-Bench-style LLM-as-judge evaluation, and later objective judge tests such as JudgeBench. The direction label is teacher-distilled open judge with explicit bias controls.
