The prior baseline was to call GPT-4 or use smaller preference models as judges, with limited systematic treatment of judge-specific bias. JudgeLM changes the workflow by distilling GPT-4 judgments into open models and making position, knowledge, and format bias first-class evaluation targets. The quality signal is the released data, model weights, training/evaluation code, bias-mitigation recipes, and reported scalability. What is not new: LLM-as-judge prompting, pairwise comparison, and GPT-4-as-teacher supervision already existed. Before reuse, inspect LLaMA/Vicuna and dataset licenses, teacher-judgment provenance, reference leakage, prompt/parser compatibility, and whether teacher agreement is acceptable for the target domain.
