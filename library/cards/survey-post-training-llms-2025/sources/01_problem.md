Post-training is often described by objectives—SFT, preference optimization, RL, or distillation—without a common map of the data objects that connect them. This 2025 arXiv survey is the primary source at https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06072. It asks how post-training techniques and datasets jointly address capability, alignment, reasoning, efficiency, and adaptation. It establishes a vocabulary for comparing instruction sets, preference pairs, traces, verifiers, and evaluations.
