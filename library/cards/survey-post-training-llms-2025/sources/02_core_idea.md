The survey organizes post-training into five paradigms: fine-tuning, alignment, reasoning, efficiency, and integration/adaptation. Its useful abstraction is not that these are disjoint algorithms, but that they consume and produce different evidence: demonstrations, preference or reward signals, reasoning trajectories, deployment constraints, and evaluations. The official abstract explicitly says it offers a structured taxonomy of techniques and datasets. The closest alternatives are narrower RLHF or reasoning surveys; this Card uses the paper as the broad entrance to the atlas.
