This is a literature taxonomy rather than a data-construction recipe. It traces post-training evolution and groups methods and datasets by the five paradigms, including reasoning systems described as large reasoning models. When reusing its framework, record the original source for every claimed data object, distinguish model-generated from externally supplied trajectories, and preserve whether a signal is a verifier, a reward, a judge, or an outcome label. The cited papers—not this survey—are the artifacts to inspect for licenses, splits, annotations, and contamination checks.
