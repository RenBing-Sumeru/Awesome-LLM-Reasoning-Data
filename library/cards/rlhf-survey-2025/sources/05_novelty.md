Wirth et al.'s 2017 preference-based RL survey and recent LLM alignment surveys are the closest predecessors. This work connects those traditions and organizes RLHF by the lifecycle of feedback, reward, and policy rather than by application alone. It does not introduce a new learner or dataset; the new value is a common vocabulary that reveals where data quality and statistical assumptions enter. The cross-domain view also prevents LLM practice from being treated as the whole history of human-feedback learning.
