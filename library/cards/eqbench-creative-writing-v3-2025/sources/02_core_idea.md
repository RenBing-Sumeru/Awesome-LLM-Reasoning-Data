The core contribution is a maintained creative-writing benchmark page with judge-based scores plus diagnostic columns for repetition and overused model-like phrasing. The mechanism is to generate prose for prompts, judge the output, and show leaderboard-level comparisons.

The closest comparisons are human-preference writing evaluations and general chat leaderboards. The change is a domain-specific creative-writing surface, with style-proxy columns that try to expose repetitive or formulaic output.

The feedback contract is an LLM-judge contract, so its meaning depends on the prompt set, judge model, rubric, generation settings, and leaderboard snapshot.
