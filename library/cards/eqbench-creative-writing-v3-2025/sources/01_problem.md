EQ-Bench Creative Writing v3 asks how to compare language models on open-ended creative prose quality. The primary source is the public EQ-Bench Creative Writing v3 leaderboard page.

The decision boundary is that this is a subjective generation leaderboard, not a factual verifier, programmatic benchmark, or training dataset. The evaluation surface is a set of writing prompts, model outputs, judge scores, and auxiliary style statistics.

It matters because creative-writing evaluation exposes a hard benchmark problem: useful judgments are qualitative, but leaderboard numbers can look more objective than their judge and prompt choices justify.
