The score is subjective and judge-dependent. A high score does not prove human preference, literary quality across cultures, or suitability for a specific writing task.

Repetition and slop columns are useful diagnostics, but they are proxies. They can penalize legitimate stylistic repetition or reward outputs that avoid known overused phrases while remaining poor writing.

Prompt set, judge model, generation settings, and leaderboard refresh policy were not fully verified, so snapshot discipline is required.
