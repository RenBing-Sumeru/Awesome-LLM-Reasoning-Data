The prior baseline is generic chatbot preference evaluation or ad hoc human review of creative writing. Creative Writing v3 changes the surface by making writing quality a dedicated leaderboard with style diagnostics.

The quality signal is that the page does not rely only on one score; it also exposes repetition and slop proxies. What is not new is LLM-as-judge evaluation itself.

Before reuse, inspect whether the style proxies match the intended writing domain, whether the judge rewards originality or just familiar prose markers, and whether the prompts have leaked.
