The paper’s central contribution is to transform scattered successful real-robot demonstrations into reusable supervision for training and evaluating general-purpose reward models. It keeps a rollout video fixed while generating alternative instructions that are only partially or not at all satisfied by the observed outcome, or truncates a successful trajectory to create intermediate completion states. The released RoboReward dataset contains 54,135 examples: 45,072 training examples, 6,232 validation examples, and 2,831 test examples. Each record includes a video, a task instruction, a discrete reward from 1 to 5, and the output of an automated validation check. The test set is additionally human-verified and released as RoboRewardBench. The dataset is used to fine-tune Qwen3-VL models at 4B and 8B scales and to evaluate 22 vision-language models.
