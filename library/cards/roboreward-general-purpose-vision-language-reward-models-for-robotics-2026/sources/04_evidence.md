The main evidence is a controlled comparison of 22 vision-language models on the same RoboRewardBench. Every model receives a task instruction and a complete rollout video, predicts a progress score from 1 to 5, and is evaluated using mean absolute error against human-verified labels. RoboReward 8B achieves an overall MAE of 0.665, improving over its Qwen3-VL 8B base model at 0.892 and slightly outperforming GPT-5 mini at 0.691. The 4B model improves from 1.032 to 0.845. In two unseen WidowX tasks, 6,000 steps of reinforcement learning using RoboReward increase success from 5% to 50% and from 10% to 80%, respectively. These results support the value of targeted reward supervision, but the real-robot evaluation covers only two tasks with 20 evaluation trials per condition.
