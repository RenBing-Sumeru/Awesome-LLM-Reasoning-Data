The proposer can generate dangerous or meaningless tasks, VLM evaluators can be misled by terminal appearance, and real-site privacy and terms require control. These issues directly affect training: false positives can turn incorrect steps into positive examples, while false negatives can discard difficult but valuable processes.

When reusing the data, audit it in strata by task type, error location, and data source, and retain rejected, failed, or unverifiable samples rather than publishing successful records alone.
