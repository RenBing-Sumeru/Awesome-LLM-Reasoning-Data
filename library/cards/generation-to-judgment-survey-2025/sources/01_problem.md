Reference matching and small learned metrics struggle with open-ended quality, safety, and reasoning assessment. LLM judges scale scoring, ranking, and selection, but their use has expanded faster than shared definitions, evaluation protocols, and reliability controls.

This survey organizes LLM-as-a-judge research by what is assessed, how a judge is built or prompted, and how it is benchmarked. It supplies a field map for auditing where judges enter the model-development lifecycle and where bias or vulnerability can invalidate their outputs.
