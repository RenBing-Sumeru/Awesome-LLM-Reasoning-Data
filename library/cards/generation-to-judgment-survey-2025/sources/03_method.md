2. Classify the target attribute: helpfulness, safety and security, reliability, relevance, logical quality, or overall quality.

3. Classify the method: the survey groups manual or synthetic feedback, SFT, preference learning, swapping, rules, multi-agent collaboration, demonstrations, multi-turn interaction, and comparison acceleration.

4. Classify benchmarks by general performance, bias quantification, challenging tasks, or domain-specific performance.

5. Synthesize challenges and directions. Reusers should inspect the cited benchmark protocol, judge prompt, candidate order, and human reference before carrying a taxonomy label into an evaluation claim.
