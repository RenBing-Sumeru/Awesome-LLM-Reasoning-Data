An evaluation designer can use the taxonomy to specify a judge contract: attribute, I/O format, method, benchmark category, and reliability tests. The inputs are candidate outputs, task context, a rubric, and a chosen judge; the output is a documented scoring, ranking, or selection protocol. It is useful for identifying missing bias or domain tests before deployment. Do not treat the survey as an implementation recipe or select a judge solely from its category without validating it on the intended task.
