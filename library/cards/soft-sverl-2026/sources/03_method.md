The pipeline generates a checklist, samples G responses, and samples J verifier traces per response-item pair. A positive replay label requires Yes-rate at least tau_positive; a negative requires at most tau_negative; uncertain middle cases are omitted. Generator reward combines full satisfaction with beta-scaled partial credit. Verifier updates mix gold and replay tuples. The shared objective combines generator optimization, lambda_v-weighted verifier co-training, and a negative lambda_p partition-style penalty. Exact G, J, thresholds, beta, lambdas, model, temperatures, seeds, gold composition, and buffer policy are unknown because no implementation or configuration is released.
