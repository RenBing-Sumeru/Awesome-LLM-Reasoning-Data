Prior methods rely on final answers, generic step labels, or a single LLM judge, leaving a mismatch between feedback and the target. This work centers supervision on prompts, criteria, weights, candidate responses, criterion scores, and review details, uses combining principle-guided coarse criteria, multi-model refinement, and difficulty evolution to generate and filter records, and releases RubricHub_v1. The novelty lies in the data object and acceptance rule rather than standard training algorithms.
