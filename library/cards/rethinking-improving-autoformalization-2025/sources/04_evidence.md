On 200 expert-labelled statement pairs, BEq improves accuracy from 82.50% to 90.50% and precision from 70.59% to 100%. With dependency retrieval, RAutoformalizer raises BEq@8 from 12.83% to 18.18% on ProofNet and from 4.58% to 16.86% on out-of-distribution Con-NF. These controlled results support both the metric and retrieval mechanism, although BEq remains limited by proof automation and cannot discover every true equivalence.
