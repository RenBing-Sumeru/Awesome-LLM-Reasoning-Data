Autoformalization faces two linked problems: Lean compilation alone cannot determine whether a candidate is semantically equivalent to the natural-language statement, and models hallucinate definitions or theorems when library dependencies are missing. The paper addresses both evaluation and generation by proposing Bidirectional Extended Definitional Equivalence (BEq), which formally checks equivalence between statements, and dependency retrieval with RAutoformalizer, which retrieves relevant formal objects before generation. It also releases the research-level Con-NF parallel benchmark for out-of-distribution evaluation.
