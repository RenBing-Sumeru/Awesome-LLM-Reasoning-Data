Tulu V1 and earlier instruction-tuning releases are the nearest baseline. Tulu 2 changes the mixture and joins it to a reproducible SFT, DPO, checkpoint, and evaluation stack, including a stable public 70B DPO run. The algorithms are not new; the novelty is an open empirical package that lets researchers distinguish instruction records, preference pairs, training stages, and evaluation claims. It therefore signals a practical standard for auditable post-training recipes rather than a new optimizer.
