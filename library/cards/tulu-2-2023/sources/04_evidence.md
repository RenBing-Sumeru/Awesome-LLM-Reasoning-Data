**End-to-end 70B comparison (Table 1).** Tulu 2 + DPO 70B averages `72.1` across MMLU, GSM8K, BBH, TydiQA, Codex-Eval, TruthfulQA, and ToxiGen, versus `72.3` for `GPT-3.5-turbo-0301`. The configurations are not identical: Tulu is an open Llama 2 70B model trained on the released SFT mixture and UltraFeedback DPO pairs, while the proprietary model's training data and size are unknown. The result supports competitiveness of the full open recipe, not a data-only causal claim.

**DPO scaling (Tables 3–4).** Holding the Tulu V2 SFT stage and model family fixed, adding DPO raises open-ended AlpacaEval and MT-Bench results at 7B, 13B, and 70B; at 70B, MT-Bench reaches `7.89` and AlpacaEval `95.1`. DPO uses UltraFeedback, three epochs, and learning rate `5e-7`; the paper also reports longer answers and weaker multilingual behavior, so the preference-stage gain is not uniform capability improvement.
