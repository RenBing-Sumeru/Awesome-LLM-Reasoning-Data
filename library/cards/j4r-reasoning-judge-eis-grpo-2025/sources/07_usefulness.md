For a reasoning-judge evaluation system, ReasoningJudgeBench can serve as training data, a hidden evaluation set, or a pre-deployment audit set. Users can map tasks, candidate responses, and evaluation evidence to preferences, scores, correctness labels, or critiques. Success should combine label agreement, classification or ranking metrics, unseen-data generalization, and real gains in training and evaluating position-robust reasoning judges. Tasks without reliable references, with high error costs, or with large distribution shifts require human, executable, or expert review.
