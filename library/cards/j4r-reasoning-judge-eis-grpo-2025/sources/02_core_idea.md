The central artifact is ReasoningJudgeBench, containing 1,483 positive–negative reasoning response pairs across eight benchmarks. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use objective label verification, answer-order swaps, and equivalent-state GRPO for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is training and evaluating position-robust reasoning judges.
