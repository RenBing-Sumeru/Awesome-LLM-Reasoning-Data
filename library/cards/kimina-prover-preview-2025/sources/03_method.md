The report starts from filtered NuminaMath 1.5 competition problems that are autoformalized into Lean4, plus human-annotated statements. It describes about 100K autoformalized statements and 10K annotated statements resampled to a 200K-prompt pool. Claude 3.7 Sonnet creates approximately 20K informal/formal cold-start traces, Kimi k1.5 supplies informal thinking, and experts author or refine selected statements and proofs. Source records and item membership are unavailable.

The final training record contains an informal problem, Lean4 statement, think block with informal reasoning and Lean snippets, and a final Lean4 proof. The paper says RL samples require at least one tactic block and tactic blocks must cover 60 percent of final Lean code. Candidate whole-proof rollouts are drawn in batches of 1,000, but rollout count and decoding settings are unknown. Minimal SFT and an informal-math mixture precede KL-constrained RL based on a Kimi k1.5 pipeline.

The RL terminal predicate is compilation of a completed proof using Numina/Kimina Lean Server and LeanREPL. The report does not release training-server version, mathlib snapshot, imports, execution images, core prompts, rollouts, reward logs, or complete rejection/retention records. Autoformalization filtering is separate: it uses compilation, QwQ-32B unanimous semantic judgment, Lean4 expert monitoring, and contradiction, negation, and triviality filters.
