The report describes a formal-reasoning recipe with approximately 200K Lean prompts, around 20K Claude 3.7 Sonnet cold starts, Kimi k1.5 informal thinking, a Qwen2.5-72B RL policy, Lean compiler terminal reward, and public 1.5B/7B distills. It combines autoformalized and human-annotated statement paths before proof generation.

The feedback contract has two distinct layers. During proof RL, a completed whole proof receives a binary reward when it compiles in Lean; this is answer-level, full-episode terminal feedback. During autoformalization construction, compilation is combined with QwQ-32B unanimous semantic judging, Lean4 expert monitoring, and contradiction, negation, and triviality filters. Neither layer should be equated with interactive step-by-step prover feedback, and compilation alone does not guarantee semantic correctness of the formalized statement.
