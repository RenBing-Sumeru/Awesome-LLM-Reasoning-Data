The public release does not contain the core prompt pool, source membership, Claude cold starts, Kimi-thinking mixture, RL rollouts, rewards, accepted/rejected proof records, judge prompts, thresholds, sampling parameters, temperatures, optimizer/KL values, or full retention yields. The reported environment is also insufficiently pinned: Lean, mathlib, imports, server versions, and execution images are incomplete.

Compilation answers whether Lean accepts a completed proof of the supplied formal statement in its environment. It cannot on its own catch a semantic mismatch introduced when informal mathematics is autoformalized. QwQ judging and expert monitoring may also have false positives or false negatives, but detailed error rates are unavailable. Public distills and proof files therefore do not make the training or feedback pipeline fully reproducible.
