The paper reports construction counts, verifier behavior, execution limits, language shaping, the asynchronous rollout path, and the Medium-to-Small lineage. It also reports controlled comparisons: Magistral Medium raises AIME 2024 pass@1 from 26.8 for Mistral Medium 3 to 73.6 and LiveCodeBench v5 from 29.1 to 59.4; for the 24B study, the SFT-plus-RL variant generally exceeds SFT-only and RL-only on the reported math and coding evaluations. These results support the claim that the stated training pipeline changed model behavior, not that its hidden records are complete, licensed, uncontaminated, or intrinsically high quality.

The release evidence is narrower than the training disclosure. Mistral AI publishes Magistral Small 2506 model weights, and the official model card identifies a 24B model with an Apache-2.0 weight license and a Mistral Small 3.1 24B Instruct base. The official announcement describes Magistral Medium as an enterprise variant. Neither the paper, release page, nor model card supplies the RL/SFT dataset, Medium weights, selected traces, verifier tests, reward logs, or record-level lineage.
