Reasoning reinforcement learning needs prompts that are verifiable, difficult enough to yield reward variation, and suitable for repeated online rollouts. Magistral addresses this with filtered mathematical expressions and contest-code tasks, then combines answer verification with format, length, and language controls. The report is unusually specific about the pipeline, but the prompts, generated trajectories, tests, and per-rollout rewards are not released. The central audit question is therefore what can be reconstructed from the recipe and what remains unavailable as data.
