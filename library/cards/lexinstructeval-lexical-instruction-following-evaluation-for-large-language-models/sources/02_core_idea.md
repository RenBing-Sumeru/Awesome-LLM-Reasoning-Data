The final benchmark contains 2,475 instructions: 1,243 English and 1,232 Chinese items, divided into 653 Easy, 744 Medium, and 1,078 Hard cases. The two languages are independently constructed rather than translated. Each constraint is represented as a `Procedure–Relation–Value` triplet spanning answer, paragraph, line, bullet, sentence, word, character, and related levels. The data and verifier support instruction-following evaluation, rule rewards, and verifier-robustness studies.
