Fine-grained instruction following often requires a particular paragraph, sentence, word, or character to satisfy several compositional constraints. Human scoring is expensive and inconsistent, LLM judges are prompt- and preference-sensitive, and existing rule benchmarks such as IFEval cannot express many hierarchical selections and relations.

LexInstructEval maps natural-language requirements into machine-readable triplets and evaluates outputs with a transparent program, providing compositional tasks, deterministic labels, and auditable failure locations.
