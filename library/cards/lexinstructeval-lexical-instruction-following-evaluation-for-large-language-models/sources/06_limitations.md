1. **Surface-constraint boundary:** The benchmark checks lexical and structural requirements, not factuality, reasoning quality, or helpfulness. A high score is not overall response quality.

2. **Parser dependence:** Sentence segmentation, Markdown lists, and Chinese/English character rules affect verdicts. Human agreement must be remeasured for new languages or formats.

3. **Template distribution:** Template generation may expose recognizable patterns that models can exploit. Training use should reserve unseen templates and combinations for testing.
