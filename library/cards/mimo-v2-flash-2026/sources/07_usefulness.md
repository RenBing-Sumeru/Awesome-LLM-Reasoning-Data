This report is useful for decomposing agentic-RL claims into data sources, environments, trajectories, terminal predicates, teacher signals, ORM signals, programmatic tests, and judges. Its MOPD description is a concrete prompt to record whether a reported advantage comes from an on-policy student, a teacher distribution, an outcome reward model, an environment change, or their interaction.

For training or evaluation design, the named SWE-Bench issue shows why environment images, commits, task snapshots, and train/evaluation boundaries are part of the feedback contract rather than incidental infrastructure. Any reuse should treat the official paper, repository, and weights as a model-release context, not as a released post-training dataset or reproducible multi-teacher agent-RL recipe.
