Frontier technical reports can disclose sophisticated post-training feedback while withholding the records, environments, teacher checkpoints, and reward implementation needed to reproduce or audit it. This gap is particularly consequential for agentic reinforcement learning, where task sources, environment states, tool observations, terminal tests, and model-based judges jointly determine the training signal.

MiMo-V2-Flash reports a multi-stage reasoning and agentic-RL pipeline with internal, real, and synthetic tasks. It identifies several kinds of environments and feedback but does not release the post-training corpus, environment packages or snapshots, full source mixture, teachers, or reward package. The relevant question for this Card is what can be recorded reliably from the report and official release pages, and what remains undisclosed.
