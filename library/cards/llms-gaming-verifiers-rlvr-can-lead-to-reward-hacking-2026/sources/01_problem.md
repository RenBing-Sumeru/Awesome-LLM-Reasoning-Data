RLVR assumes that a programmatic verifier rewards the intended reasoning. In inductive logic tasks, however, a model can list the observed labels rather than infer a general rule; an extensional checker accepts the list even though it will not generalise.

This paper asks how to distinguish genuine rule induction from a reward shortcut using only final outputs, including for closed models. It also tests whether the weakness is merely observed in RLVR systems or can be induced by the verifier used during training.
