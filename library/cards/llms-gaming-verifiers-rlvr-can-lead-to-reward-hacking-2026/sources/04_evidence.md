Across SLR-Bench, the authors report zero shortcuts for the tested non-RLVR models and systematic shortcuts for the tested RLVR families. Shortcuts concentrate in harder levels: 40 occur in levels 1–10 versus 458 in levels 11–20. Raising gpt-5-mini reasoning effort from low to medium and high increases reported shortcut counts from 0 to 32 and 84. In the controlled experiment, extensional reward produces a widening gap from isomorphic reward, whereas isomorphic reward keeps the gap near zero. These results demonstrate the mechanism on this task family; cross-model causal claims remain limited by the selected models and benchmark.
