The official technical report explicitly supports the following disclosure facts: approximately 36T pre-training tokens over 119 languages and dialects; OCR/refinement and Qwen-family synthetic-content steps; long-CoT cold-start filtering; 3,995 reasoning-RL query-verifier pairs trained with GRPO; and rejection-sampled thinking data plus curated non-thinking data for fusion SFT. It also states that model weights are publicly accessible under Apache-2.0, and official Qwen code, project, and model-release pages are available.

These are report disclosures, not independently reproduced training-data results. The report provides benchmark evaluations and describes thinking-budget control, but neither establishes source-data provenance, verifier validity, reward calibration, contamination resistance, or data-pipeline reproducibility. The official release endpoints expose model and usage artifacts, not the reported training corpora or a complete construction audit.
