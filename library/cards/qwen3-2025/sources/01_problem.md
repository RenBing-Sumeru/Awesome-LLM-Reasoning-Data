Frontier model reports often describe post-training outcomes while leaving the provenance, construction, and auditability of the data objects incomplete. For reasoning models, readers need to separate a stated stage or aggregate count from a reusable recipe: source data, retained and rejected examples, verifier behavior, reward calibration, and contamination controls can remain undisclosed.

Qwen3 is useful for this disclosure question because its report names several pre-training and post-training interfaces but does not release the associated training corpora or complete audit artifacts. This Card records the disclosed boundary and the remaining unknowns rather than treating the report's model results or thinking-budget interface as evidence of a reproducible pipeline.
