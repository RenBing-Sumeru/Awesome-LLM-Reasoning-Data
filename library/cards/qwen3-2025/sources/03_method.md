For pre-training, the report states that the approximately 36T-token mixture covers code, STEM, reasoning, books, multilingual text, and synthetic content. It describes extracting text from PDF-like documents with Qwen2.5-VL and refining it with Qwen2.5, plus synthesizing textbooks, question-answering, instructions, and code snippets with Qwen2.5-family models. It reports three pre-training stages, but not raw sources, mixture weights, source licenses, or manifests.

For long-CoT cold start, problems cover mathematics, code, logical reasoning, and general STEM and are paired with verified reference answers or code-based tests. Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct removes hard-to-verify or easy-without-CoT queries and labels domains. QwQ-32B generates candidate responses; when it repeatedly fails, human annotators assess accuracy. Response filtering excludes incorrect answers, substantial repetition, unsupported guesswork, thinking-summary inconsistencies, inappropriate language or style changes, and suspected similarity to potential validation items. Exact prompts, thresholds, sample counts, and yields are undisclosed.

For reasoning RL, the report says 3,995 query-verifier pairs satisfy separation from cold start, learnability, difficulty, and sub-domain coverage criteria, and that GRPO updates the model. It describes large batches, many rollouts per query, and off-policy training without values. The report then performs continual SFT for thinking/non-thinking fusion: thinking data is rejection-sampled from Stage-1 queries with the Stage-2 model, and non-thinking data is curated across several task types with automatically generated quality checklists. A later general-RL stage and logits-based strong-to-weak distillation are named at high level; their detailed data and optimization configuration are unknown.
