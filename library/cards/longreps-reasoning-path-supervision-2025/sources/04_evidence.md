With the same LLaMA and Qwen base models, LongRePS differs from outcome supervision mainly by training on reasoning paths. In-domain MuSiQue gains are 13.6 and 3.8 points, while average cross-domain long-context QA gains are 9.3 and 8.1 points. Benefits also grow with context length, although the conclusion depends on whether the filter correctly identifies evidence coverage.

The authors also stratify by context length and find that path supervision gains more over answer supervision as documents become longer.
