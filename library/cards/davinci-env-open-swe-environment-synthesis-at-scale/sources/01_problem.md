Verifiable SWE-agent training is constrained by environment construction. Real repositories are abundant, but dependency installation, test entry points, and pre/post-patch states usually require manual recovery. Small curated benchmarks do not supply enough environments for large-scale SFT or RL, while scraping issues and diffs does not ensure that agents can enter repositories and receive reliable execution feedback.

daVinci-Env treats environment recovery itself as a multi-agent synthesis task. It generates executable Docker images, evaluation scripts, and quality labels at scale and then collects interaction trajectories from qualified environments. The work asks whether open repositories can become tens of thousands of transparent and reproducible SWE training environments.
