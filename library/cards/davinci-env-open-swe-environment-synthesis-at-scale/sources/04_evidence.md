Scale evidence shows that the framework produces 45,320 Docker environments across more than 12.8K repositories and approximately 13K quality trajectories from about 9K environments. Models trained with these environments and trajectories reach 62.4% and 66.0% on SWE-bench Verified for OpenSWE-32B and 72B respectively. The authors also report out-of-distribution gains of up to roughly 12 points on mathematics and 5 points on science tasks.

These results support the claim that large executable environment collections can yield useful agent data. However, model scale, trajectory filtering, and data quantity all change together, so the entire improvement cannot be attributed solely to the number of environments. Evaluation remains centered on Python and SWE-bench-style tests.
