For the Rollout / Search / Test-Time Trace Data track, this work is useful because the released unit is a multi-turn action-observation episode rather than only a prompt and final answer. Researchers can study command choice, terminal feedback, correction, recovery, task/run multiplicity, context-length pressure, and the effects of mixing broad adapter traces with targeted skill-based traces. The paper directly tests whether retaining all attempted behavior can be preferable to selecting only complete or successful trajectories.

The demonstrated training use is supervised fine-tuning and terminal-agent training for Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-14B, and Qwen3-32B students. The data may support behavior cloning, trajectory distillation, interface modeling, and controlled studies of source mixture or context length, but RLVR is future work in the paper and should not be attributed as a demonstrated use. Failure-conditioned training also requires new labeling: the public rows cannot be reliably partitioned into successful, incomplete, and failed episodes from the published schema alone.

A careful reuse pipeline should pin the corpus and task revisions, retain `task`, `episode`, and `run_id` rather than treating rows as unique tasks, and separate adapter from skill-based configurations. It should recover or regenerate trustworthy outcome labels by executing pinned tests where available, keep self-declared completion separate from verifier success, and document what happens to adapter episodes without tests. Researchers should publish the exact row manifest for any subset or curriculum and record truncation decisions.

Replay-oriented work should replace mutable image tags with digests, lock packages, record harness and scaffold commits, specify network/reset/cache rules, and preserve verifier logs and task budgets. Auditors should extend decontamination beyond the disclosed TB2 14-gram screen, reconcile record-level sources and rights, and review security-task handling. Until the 124,366 seed-based stream and complete pipeline are released, comparisons should state whether they use the 366,154-row public corpus or the 490,520-trajectory paper mixture.
