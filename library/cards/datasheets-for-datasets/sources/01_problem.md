Datasets are commonly reused without a standardized account of why they were created, what they contain, how they were collected, and where they should or should not be used. The official arXiv record at https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010 proposes that every dataset be accompanied by a datasheet; it notes publication in Communications of the ACM in December 2021. This Card supplies a documentation framework for every reasoning-data release, not because it is itself a dataset. Its data object is a documentation record attached to a dataset and addressed to its downstream consumers.
