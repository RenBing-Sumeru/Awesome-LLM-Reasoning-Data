The paper proposes a structured questionnaire-style documentation process to be completed alongside a dataset. It asks creators to record the dataset's motivation, composition, collection process, and recommended uses, among other information. For a reasoning-data Card, operationalize that proposal by recording prompt and trace provenance, label and verifier contracts, filtering, splits, scale, license, intended and excluded use, maintenance, and contamination evidence. The primary paper does not release a universal datasheet-filled dataset, codebase, or data artifact; the official arXiv record and publisher DOI are the source artifacts.
