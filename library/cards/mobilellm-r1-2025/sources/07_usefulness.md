For the Data Construction and Open Release Recipes track, this paper is useful as a blueprint for experiments that separate four decisions: representative-probe construction, dataset-level mixture weights, model-dependent sample rejection, and stage ordering. A lab can hold architecture and compute fixed, replace one signal at a time, and test whether uniform, heuristic, loss-based, or influence-based selection produces different capability and forgetting profiles.
