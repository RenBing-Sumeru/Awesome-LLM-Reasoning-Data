The evidence is strongest for the recipe's model outcomes and component ablations, not for the quality of every selected record.

- **Mixture and filtering signals.** Figure 4 shows the influence-derived pretraining mixture lowering perplexity curves against uniform sampling on the paper's code, math, and knowledge evaluation aggregates. Figure 6 shows positive-influence mid-training subsampling maintaining higher MMLU than the original mixture, with and without the 8B teacher. These are author-reported curves; the released repository lacks the curation implementation needed for independent replay.
- **Post-training order.** In Table 1, staged Tulu-3 then math/science/code reasoning SFT reports 57.8 MATH, 68.5 GSM8K, 13.7 LCBv6, and 44.0 MMLU. Joint training reports 56.2, 53.1, 14.9, and 44.0 respectively. Staging helps the reported math scores but does not dominate LCBv6.
- **Controlled final-SFT comparison.** With one epoch of the same joint reasoning SFT, the 949M checkpoint reports 57.8 MATH, 68.5 GSM8K, and 13.7 LCBv6. OLMo-2-0425-1B-SFT reports 53.0/58.8/11.4, and SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct reports 41.4/50.5/7.4 (Table 2). This comparison supports a contribution from earlier training, while architecture and optimizer differences remain possible confounders.
- **Final checkpoint.** Under Table 9's zero-shot protocol, MobileLLM-R1-950M reports 74.0 MATH500, 67.5 GSM8K, 15.5 AIME'24, 16.3 AIME'25, and 19.9 LCBv6. Qwen3-0.6B reports 73.0, 79.2, 11.3, 17.0, and 14.9. Results are mixed by task rather than uniformly better.
- **Cost.** Appendix D.3 reports about 6,800 GPU-hours for representative-set construction, influence computation, and leave-one-out ablation, plus about 38,600 GPU-hours for model training. This makes the selection process substantial even though the final models are sub-billion scale.

Benchmark performance is evidence about the trained checkpoints under the reported setup; it is not proof that the mixture is uncontaminated, uniformly licensed, or optimally filtered. No independent reproduction is claimed here.
