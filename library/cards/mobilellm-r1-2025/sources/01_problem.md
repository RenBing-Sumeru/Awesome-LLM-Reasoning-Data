The official ICLR 2026 paper asks how 140M, 359M, and 949M parameter models can acquire useful mathematical, coding, scientific, and general reasoning behavior under limited capacity and a much smaller token budget than contemporary frontier recipes. Its data question is not merely which public corpora to name, but how to construct capability probes, estimate the marginal contribution of each source, change the mixture as the model learns, and sequence pretraining, knowledge distillation, general instruction tuning, and reasoning SFT.

The Atlas object is a phase-specific construction recipe. A conceptual record is source text or a prompt-response conversation annotated by source, capability domain, filter scores, representative-subset membership, checkpoint influence estimates, mixture weight, and training phase. Most of those process fields are described but not released per record.

This belongs in `data_construction_open_release_recipes` because it exposes source tables, mixture ratios, phase budgets, checkpoints, and minimal training code. It does not release a frozen combined corpus, a complete curation implementation, or a unified correctness verifier. The bilingual Card is `L4_chinese_review_ready` and supports recipe inspection and release-gap audit, but not exact data replay. (Paper §§2-4; Appendix A; official repository at commit `f518dc7e402876fc694a827385ed25de31242905`.)
