The reconstructable pipeline is:

1. **Source and probe construction.** Start from the public corpora listed in Appendix Table 5. For each source, apply FineWeb-Edu score `>4`, generic and domain-specific Ask-LLM top-10% selection, and cross-corpus semantic deduplication. Approximately 10,000 examples per source form representative subsets, which are combined into Code, Math, and Knowledge capability probes. The Ask-LLM model, exact prompts, deduplication model, and threshold are not disclosed.
2. **Pretraining selection and mixing.** Train leave-one-source-out models to measure source effects on probe NLL. Extend AutoMixer by estimating each representative sample's influence on all three capability probes at ten checkpoints; linearly increasing checkpoint weights favor later training. Aggregate token-weighted influence to dataset weights. Sample two 2T-token phases from the listed 1.8T-token pool, shifting phase 2 toward OpenWebMath, FineMath, Algebraic Stack, Facebook Natural Reasoning, and other specialized sources.
3. **Model-data co-evolution in mid-training.** Mix Dolmino subsets, Nemotron code/math, StarCoder, and eight named benchmark training splits. At each phase, retain records whose estimated influence is positive, aggregate influence into new dataset ratios, train, and repeat. Two 100B-token phases are reported. Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct supplies logits, and the student minimizes KL divergence in addition to the training objective.
4. **Post-training.** Run two epochs over 866K `Tulu-3-sft-olmo-2-mixture-0225` examples, then four epochs over a reported 6.2M-example mixture: OpenMathReasoning 3.2M, OpenScienceReasoning-2 802K, and OpenCodeReasoning-2 2.2M. The reasoning stage uses 32k sequences and full prompt-response supervision.
5. **Evaluation and artifacts.** Evaluate base and final checkpoints on math, code, commonsense, and knowledge benchmarks. The pinned repository provides minimal pretraining, SFT, and math-evaluation code and the Hugging Face collection provides base/final weights. Exact reconstruction additionally requires immutable upstream revisions, selected IDs, sharding, seeds, probe rows, influence logs, curation code, and a paper-matching configuration; these are unavailable.

This pipeline does not use one global reward or terminal predicate. Positive influence is the mid-training retention rule; KL divergence and token-level SFT loss are training objectives; benchmark metrics are evaluation outputs, not evidence that each selected record is correct. (Paper §§2-4; Appendix A; repository `pretrain/`, `sft/`, and `evaluation/`.)
