**Method and feedback limits.** FineWeb-Edu and Ask-LLM scores are proxies for educational value and reasoning relevance. They can admit persuasive but incorrect text and reject rare, terse, or domain-specific examples. The positive-influence threshold is conditional on the chosen probes, checkpoint, and approximation; a record with delayed or out-of-domain benefit may be removed. The Ask-LLM model and prompts, semantic-deduplication model and threshold, and sample-level influence distribution are undisclosed.

**Data and release limits.** Appendix Table 5 lists 1.8T source tokens, the paper rounds this to approximately 2T, and resampling consumes 2T in each pretraining phase; adding two 100B mid-training phases yields 4.2T exposure before SFT, not 4.2T unique tokens. No combined corpus, sampled IDs, random seeds, shards, probe rows, rejected examples, influence logs, or exact upstream revisions are released. Phase-2 mid-training includes eight benchmark training splits, but no end-to-end decontamination or overlap report is provided. FAIR Noncommercial terms cover the Meta research materials, not the heterogeneous licenses of all upstream datasets.

**Reproducibility and experimental limits.** At pinned commit `f518dc7e402876fc694a827385ed25de31242905`, the repository provides minimal training and evaluation code but no hierarchical filtering, leave-one-out, AutoMixer, or subsampling implementation. Its example pretraining script uses 480k steps, cosine scheduling, learning rate `5e-4`, and 1k warmup, whereas Appendix Table 4 reports 500k steps, linear decay, learning rate `4e-3`, and 2k warmup. The repository has no tagged release. Reported benchmark improvements are not an independent causal validation of the selected data, and some comparisons retain architecture and optimization confounders. These release findings are curator audits based on the official repository, not limitations explicitly acknowledged by the authors.
