The closest ingredients are established rather than wholly new: FineWeb-Edu supplies an educational-quality classifier, Ask-LLM supplies model-based selection, AutoMixer supplies scalable influence approximation, Dolmino supplies a mid-training base, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct supplies logit distillation, and Tulu-3/OpenMathReasoning/OpenScienceReasoning-2/OpenCodeReasoning-2 supply post-training data.

The concrete change is how these pieces are connected for small models. Representative subsets become capability probes rather than benchmark labels; self- and cross-capability influence is integrated across ten checkpoints to set source weights; positive-influence filtering is recomputed as the model changes; and the resulting phases are carried through from random initialization to long-context reasoning SFT. The paper also reports the source weights and token budgets phase by phase, making the construction hypothesis auditable at recipe level.

What is not new is the upstream corpora, SFT itself, KL distillation, or a correctness verifier. Nor does the work establish a new reusable dataset: the combined records, sampled IDs, rejected examples, and sample-level scores are absent. The direction signal for reasoning-data research is model-aware mixture design under tight capacity, but reuse requires checking whether the unpublished probe and influence choices drive the reported effect and whether the method transfers beyond these three capability domains and model sizes.
