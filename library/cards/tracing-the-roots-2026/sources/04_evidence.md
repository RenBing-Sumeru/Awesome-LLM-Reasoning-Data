The paper supplies evidence at three levels, which should not be collapsed into a single quality claim.

**Graph-scale and topology evidence.** Starting from 83 seeds, the authors report 430 distinct datasets and 971 inheritance edges. Table 1 gives average depth 1.05 for General versus 2.92 for Math, with 285 General, 99 Math, 98 Code, and 44 Science nodes; because datasets can span domains, these domain counts are not a partition of 430. These results support the descriptive claim that the observed General subgraph is broad and shallow while the Math subgraph is more recursively reused. They do not measure edge precision or recall against an independent gold graph.

**Literal-overlap and contamination evidence.** Section 4.2 reports that 17 of the 83 examined datasets have redundancy above 1% under exact `(instruction, input, output)` matching. Table 3's largest case is `open-instruct-v1` at 46.48%; `Fast-Math-R1-SFT` is 5.30%. For benchmark overlap, exact `(instruction, input)` matching finds contamination across five benchmarks and 19 datasets. Appendix Table 10 reports, for example, Omni-MATH matches in `DeepScaleR-Preview-Dataset` at 79.48% (3502/4406), `Big-Math-RL-Verified` at 57.97% (2554/4406), and the downstream `Caco-1.3M` at 37.95% (1672/4406). These are direct exact-match findings under the authors' snapshots; they are lower bounds for transformed overlap and do not show that every matching row affected a model score.

**Construction and diversity evidence.** Appendix E reports that 212 unique leaves are reduced to 31 sources, about 8.7M normalized rows, and then 570K instructions after filtering and two-stage deduplication. Table 4 compares the resulting instructions with eight datasets using Qwen3-Embedding-8B representations: the authors report Vendi Score 452.44 and Centroid Distance 0.6385, the largest values in that table. This supports greater embedding dispersion under those metrics and baselines. It is not evidence that answers are correct, licenses are compatible, the corpus improves SFT, or the graph edges are accurate; no downstream model-training experiment is reported.

The official release only partially supports reproduction. At inspected commit `681db3ab13eaa4391025d6c4b86ed5b58b73988e`, checked-in text-only outputs contain 443 dataset-metadata JSONL rows and 1092 graph-edge JSONL rows, while multimodal outputs contain 1161 and 2306 rows respectively. Those living-repository counts differ from the paper's 430/971 snapshot, and no 570K corpus is present. This is artifact-version evidence, not a correction to the paper's reported experiment.
