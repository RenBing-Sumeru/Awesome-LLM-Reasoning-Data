The closest prior baseline is caption-only audio pretraining and the earlier AudioSkills mixture. The paper changes the reusable object by making record id, audio waveform, duration, and ordered conversation turns explicit and coupling it to language/audio filtering, source-specific checks, duration limits, and dataset ablations. This is a direction signal because another team can train the stated consumer from the released records. The inherited backbone, source tasks, autoregressive objective, and public benchmarks are not new.
