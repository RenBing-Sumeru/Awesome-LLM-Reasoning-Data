1. Positioning: RMB uses multi-scenario candidate generation, preference verification, and pairwise plus Best-of-N protocols to create specialized feedback for failures that general comprehensive reward-model evaluation evaluation misses.

2. Method handle: collect tasks and candidates, generate feedback, verify boundary cases, and organize by capability; label review is the decisive quality control.

3. Data handle: roughly eighteen thousand preference comparisons across forty-nine scenarios, with records centered on inputs, candidates, evaluation evidence, and feedback labels.

4. Evidence anchor: RMB correlates positively with downstream performance, while pairwise and Best-of-N rankings differ, with conclusions bounded by model, candidate, and judge configurations.

5. Reuse decision: best suited to evaluating both local pairwise judgment and set-level ranking; contamination, false positives, and distributional stability must be checked.
