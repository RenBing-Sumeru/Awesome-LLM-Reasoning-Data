The central artifact is RMB, containing roughly eighteen thousand preference comparisons across forty-nine scenarios. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use multi-scenario candidate generation, preference verification, and pairwise plus Best-of-N protocols for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is evaluating both local pairwise judgment and set-level ranking.
