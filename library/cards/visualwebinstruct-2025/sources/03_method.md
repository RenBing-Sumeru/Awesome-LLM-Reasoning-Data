1. **Retrieve webpages.** Start from roughly 30,000 Stemez scientific images, request about 60 Google Lens results per image, and reduce 1,747,634 collected URLs to 758,490 unique educational candidates after domain filtering and deduplication.
2. **Extract records.** Convert HTML into accessibility trees that preserve relevant text-image hierarchy. Gemini 1.5 Flash extracts complete questions, relevant images, notation, and worked solutions, yielding 421,320 raw QA pairs; a second validity and image-relevance pass retains 361,015.
3. **Refine answers.** GPT-4o generates four solutions for each question. A GPT-4o judge keeps records when more than half of the conclusions agree, producing 1,041,598 expanded QA pairs before final alignment.
4. **Align and package.** Gemini 2.0 Flash compares synthetic responses with available web answers; inconsistent cases use the web answer. The final output is 906,160 QA pairs with 257,201 unique questions, 347,313 image-associated pairs, and 163,743 unique images.
5. **Consume and verify.** SFT trains LLaVA-OneVision-mid or MAmmoTH-VL for one epoch; the best run mixes VisualWebInstruct with modified LLaVA-CoT at 9:1.
