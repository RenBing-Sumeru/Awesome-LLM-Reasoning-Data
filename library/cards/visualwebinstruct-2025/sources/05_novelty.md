Earlier multimodal reasoning corpora were often small, domain-specific, or generated from fixed synthetic-image rules. VisualWebInstruct changes the construction surface by using images as web-search keys, then preserving webpage text-image structure before synthesizing and aligning worked answers. Its direction signal is the combination of large-scale natural visual retrieval and explicit SFT-ready reasoning targets, not a new model architecture or consistency algorithm. The official EMNLP 2025 Main record and controlled data ablations are quality signals, while source rights, teacher-correlated errors, and weakly specified decontamination remain the main reuse checks.
