Prior-work baseline and change: Benchmark refreshes and rewriting strategies are often presented as contamination mitigations, but prior comparisons can blur two different requirements: preserving the original measurement target and resisting exposure to the original items. This work contributes an intervention-based test of both requirements under controlled clean and contaminated models. Its contribution is therefore not another rewritten benchmark but a way to test whether rewriting actually preserves what a benchmark measures while reducing the measured effect of exposure. The test still cannot certify every natural pretraining route or every benchmark domain.
