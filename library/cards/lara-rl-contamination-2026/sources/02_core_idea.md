LaRA frames RL-post-training contamination as instance-level membership inference. For a target math problem, it builds structurally matched numerical variants, masks the same kind of key information, and makes blank-preserving paraphrases. Across all layers it measures target-relative shift magnitude (RSM), perturbation-direction alignment (DC), and local paraphrase variability (RSI), robustly standardizes them against clean examples, reverses RSI’s sign, and averages them into SLaRA. It is an audit protocol, not a training dataset.
