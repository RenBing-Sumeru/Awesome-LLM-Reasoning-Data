The corpus can train proof-validity reward models and verifiers, support process supervision from written or localized feedback, and evaluate transfer across solver families and competitions. It can also audit near-correct proofs, judge disagreement, and best-of-N selection. Reusers must preserve solver, source, split, judge count, uncertainty, and selection metadata; exclude benchmark and test subsets from training; and avoid treating unselected candidates as negatives unless they were actually graded. Formal-validity applications should add an executable proof checker rather than substituting human acceptance for one.
