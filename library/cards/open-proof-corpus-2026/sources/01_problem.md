Answer-only mathematics data cannot show whether a natural-language proof is valid, where it fails, or whether judges agree on subtle errors. Formal proof corpora provide executable checking but cover a narrower representation and problem set. Open Proof Corpus addresses the missing feedback surface by pairing competition problems with frontier-model proofs, expert correctness judgments, written feedback, and optional localized error annotations. The central challenge is to make this human judgment reusable without treating it as infallible formal verification.
