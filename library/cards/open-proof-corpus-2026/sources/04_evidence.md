The paper and official release describe proofs generated by six frontier models over material from more than twenty mathematics competitions plus selected MathArena and PutnamBench subsets. Thirteen expert judges assign binary correctness labels and written feedback; some records include uncertainty flags, sentence-level error annotations, or a second judgment. The paper reports approximately 10% duplicate grading, 90.4% inter-judge agreement, and an estimated 5% judge-error rate under its assumptions. The corpus also records solver, source, split, judge, and selection context, and includes best-of-N groups. These data demonstrate a substantial human-feedback surface for proof validity, but one judgment per proof remains the norm and natural-language acceptance is not equivalent to formal checking.
