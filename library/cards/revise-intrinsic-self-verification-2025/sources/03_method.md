The base model samples multiple reasoning paths from supervised prompt/label pairs. Answer extraction and task-specific outcome checks divide paths into correct and incorrect groups. Stage 1 constructs `D_verify` from these groups, with chosen/rejected continuations defined by `eos` versus `refine`. Stage 2 initializes from Stage 1 and constructs `D_correct`, where an incorrect prefix is followed by `refine` plus the supervised gold reasoning target.

Both stages combine SFT on the chosen sequence with DPO on the pair. The paper studies Llama-3.2-1B, Llama-3.1-8B, and Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct variants. Training/evaluation sources include GSM8K, MATH, and a 50K MetaMath subset with 3K validation examples. Exact dataset revisions, per-source path and pair counts, sampling temperature, random seeds, and rejected-path manifests are not present in the public Card metadata.

For MBPP, an external model generates intermediate reasoning and the construction retains 16 unit-test-passing paths per problem. This introduces a distinct teacher-plus-execution lineage: passing available tests is an outcome contract for code behavior, while the generated intermediate explanation remains teacher-authored and is not proven step by step. The official repository exposes code, but a public pair corpus, full generation logs, and trained checkpoints were not confirmed.
