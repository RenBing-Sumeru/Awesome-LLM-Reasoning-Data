The official ICML/PMLR paper defines the two datasets, curriculum order, SFT+DPO objectives, control-token semantics, and confidence-weighted decoding. The author repository exposes implementation material. A public transformed pair corpus, item-level source lineage, generation logs, rejection manifests, and trained checkpoints were not confirmed in the checked artifacts, so the paper recipe and the reusable training data remain separate claims.

The paper evaluates first-attempt accuracy, one or more refinement attempts, transfer, and confidence-weighted voting, and provides curriculum and DPO ablations. These results test whether the learned control actions and confidence correlate with correctness in the reported model/task settings. They do not independently calibrate the intrinsic verifier, verify intermediate reasoning, or establish that every preference pair is correct.

For data audit, the most relevant evidence is the construction rule: outcome labels decide which control action is preferred, and Stage 2 attaches a supervised correction after errors. Downstream benchmark improvement is not evidence for source provenance, split hygiene, teacher quality, or licensing of transformed paths.
