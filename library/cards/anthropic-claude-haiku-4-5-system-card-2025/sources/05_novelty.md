The system card is unusually useful because it joins data-source disclosure with post-training labor and agent-training behavior. Five source classes, human and AI feedback, contractor preference selection, prior-model reasoning traces, context-aware agentic RL, and recursive behavioral review appear in one official artifact.

The context-awareness intervention is a concrete example of state-action supervision beyond answer quality. Context usage becomes part of the information available to the model during agentic RL, shaping whether it persists or concludes. This is a meaningful agent-training contract even though the reward and environment are absent.

The later-stage recursive audit is also distinctive. Rather than sampling only benchmark responses, Anthropic summarizes the complete later-stage behavior corpus and asks another model to inspect every summary. It establishes a stage-level audit checkpoint, while simultaneously illustrating why raw traces, summary transformations, judge prompts, and calibration are necessary for independent verification.

Finally, the report explicitly reveals that a reward-hacking benchmark was selected from the training distribution. That disclosure prevents a misleading held-out-generalization interpretation and is itself valuable audit evidence.
