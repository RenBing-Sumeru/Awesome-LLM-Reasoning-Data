The report exposes five coarse training-source classes: publicly available Internet information through February 2025; non-public third-party data; data from data-labeling services and paid contractors; opt-in data from Claude users; and internally generated Anthropic data. Deduplication and classification are named as cleaning steps, while the public crawler follows robots.txt and avoids password-, sign-in-, and CAPTCHA-protected pages.

Post-training includes reinforcement learning from human feedback and AI feedback. Crowd workers engaged through data-work platforms perform preference selection, safety evaluation, and adversarial testing. The system card does not specify whether preference records are pairwise, listwise, or rubric-based in every stage, nor how human and AI signals are aggregated into rewards.

For agentic RL, Haiku 4.5 is explicitly trained with precise context-usage information. The goal is to wrap up appropriately when near the 200K-token release context limit and to persist when substantial context remains. This is a concrete state-awareness intervention, although task distribution, rollout environment, episode budget, reward, and algorithm are not disclosed.

The reasoning lineage has two additional layers. Some earlier supervised-learning data contain reasoning text generated by prior Anthropic models. Separately, rare very long extended-thinking traces may be summarized for users by a second Haiku 4.5 instance at inference. The former is training evidence; the latter is only a presentation mechanism.
