1. With the Mistral 7B backbone, CodeActAgent reaches 57.4 success on in-domain MINT and 32.4 on out-of-domain MINT, versus 18.8 and 9.7 for Mistral Instruct. On out-of-domain M3ToolEval it reaches 12.2 while the same-size instruction baseline scores 0.0. These controlled comparisons support the usefulness of the trajectory mixture for executable agent behavior.

2. The paper reports 24% and 119% relative improvement over same-backbone AgentInstruct and FireAct variants, while generic-task performance is largely preserved: the Mistral CodeActAgent records 59.1 MMLU, 34.7 HumanEval, 58.0 GSM8K, and 8.2 MT-Bench. This supports transfer beyond the source tasks, but the best training mixture also includes 69,230 general conversations, so gains cannot be attributed to CodeActInstruct alone.
