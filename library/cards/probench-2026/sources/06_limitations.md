- **No reusable artifact:** No official code, machine-readable task set, Process Provider/evaluator implementation, environment package, result file, or trajectory corpus was confirmed. Independent execution, verifier testing, and record-level error analysis are blocked.
- **Not process supervision:** Action descriptions are produced from the evaluated agent's behavior and shown only to the terminal judge. They are not gold actions, agent-visible feedback, dense process rewards, or released training labels.
- **Judge and converter error:** A11y metadata can be noisy, empty, or misleading, and detailed node extraction applies only to clicks. MLLM summaries and Gemini terminal judgments can hallucinate or omit requirements; reported validation is below 100% and lacks sample/protocol detail.
- **Manual reset:** Clearing in-app histories by hand does not guarantee clean server/account/cookie/personalization/notification/clock/locale/network state. English emulator and Chinese physical-phone runs also lack pinned Android/app/device/account snapshots, preventing deterministic replay.
- **No split or contamination audit:** State/Process and English/Chinese are evaluation strata, not splits. No train/dev/test or hidden-test policy, task hashes, formal lexical/semantic dedup report, external overlap analysis, Qwen3 contamination check, or evaluated-model exposure audit is released.
- **Failure retention:** The paper distinguishes Uncompleted, Failure, and Success and shows selected cases, but no per-episode actions, observations, process text, judge response, labels, or complete success/failure/uncompleted corpus is public.
- **Construction opacity:** Qwen3 checkpoint/prompt/settings, seed/candidate pools, rejection counts, annotator protocol, agreement, and adjudication are unknown. Reduced lexical/structural overlap is asserted without a machine-readable report.
- **Rights:** The AAAI paper states Copyright © 2026 AAAI, all rights reserved. With no separate code/data/task/trajectory release or license, reuse rights for task text, screenshots, application content, evaluator outputs, and future artifacts are unknown.
- **Privacy and safety:** Credential/account setup, PII/redaction, personalized-content review, consent, retention, takedown, purchases/forms/settings controls, rate limits, and misuse policy are undisclosed despite live email, maps, shopping, finance, social, and other apps.
