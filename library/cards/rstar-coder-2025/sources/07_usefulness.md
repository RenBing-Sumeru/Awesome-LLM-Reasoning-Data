For the **Data Construction & Open Release Recipes** track, rStar-Coder is useful as both a construction pattern and an audit case.

- **Test-construction baseline:** compare direct input-output generation with generated utility functions plus execution. Keep the same questions, candidate budget, and input-scale distribution, and evaluate against oracle outputs rather than downstream scores alone.
- **Verifier study:** reproduce complete-output-set voting, the released per-input plurality implementation, and trusted-oracle execution. Sweep the 40%/60% thresholds, retain full vote vectors, and stratify false accepts by source, rating, and input scale.
- **Conditional SFT reuse:** pin Hugging Face revision `3a7a0a0636ec96e3c1ec42ebe79ade467caa040d`, preserve `seed_source` and seed questions, join test configurations where keys exist, honor `verified`/`is_passed`, and re-execute every selected target in an isolated environment.
- **RLVR substrate:** `synthetic_rl` plus `synthetic_rl_testcase`, and the corresponding seed configurations, can seed a programmatic reward environment. Before training, check question/test cardinality, parse serialized JSON fields, remove ambiguous or constant-output cases only with documented policy, and calibrate reward false accepts.
- **Scaling experiment:** independently vary unique problem count, solutions per problem, and tests per problem. The paper's comparisons motivate this design, but its release does not provide the frozen membership needed for a clean replication.
- **Release audit template:** publish source URLs and rights, model snapshots and prompts, seeds, all candidate generations, generator/validator code, vote vectors, execution outcomes, rejection reasons, contamination matches, exact train manifests, and checkpoint hashes.

Reuse class: conditionally suitable for competitive-programming SFT, distillation, and RLVR after rights, version, join, and sandbox checks; strong as a verifier and data-lineage audit reference; unsuitable as a clean evaluation set because its problems and solutions are training material. The paper's benchmark gains should be treated as evidence of downstream utility under its protocol, not as proof that every released record is correct.
