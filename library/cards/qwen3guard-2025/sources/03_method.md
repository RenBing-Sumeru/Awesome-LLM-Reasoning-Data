For Gen, the report says the more-than-1.19M mixture contains human-annotated and synthetic prompt/response samples. It uses Self-Instruct: the safety policy is decomposed into a fine-grained taxonomy; seed prompts, category-specific keywords, and paired safe/unsafe prompts drive synthesis. Responses include human-authored and model-generated material. The report names Qwen2.5-72B-Base as an example generator for unsafe responses and names QwQ, Qwen3, DeepSeek-R1, and DeepSeek distills as sources of reasoning-content responses. It translates original content to 15 additional languages with Qwen-MT and describes language-mixing detection, an LLM judge, and random manual review. Source records, seed sets, prompts, sampling settings, item-level provenance, rights, and retention yields are not published.

For automatic annotation, the report uses multiple Qwen versions, including Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct and Qwen3-235B-A22B, and aggregates preliminary labels by voting. It splits data into disjoint A/B partitions, trains strict/loose models with reweighted safe/unsafe samples, and marks cross-partition prediction conflicts as controversial. It then uses Qwen3-32B for a further disjoint-partition label-distillation pass. Gen is vanilla SFT on instruction-tuned Qwen3. Its released output interface includes risk/category and, for response classification, refusal; the exact training record schema and labeler prompts are unknown.

For Stream, the report derives a boundary token for unsafe or controversial responses. For each prefix it samples rollout continuations from a diverse model ensemble and scores completed responses with Gen; an LLM judge, Qwen3-235B-A22B, reviews flagged prefixes without predicting future text. A boundary is accepted only if both signals call the prefix unsafe, after which the boundary and later tokens retain the sample-level label. Stream jointly trains query and response heads with cross-entropy: query loss is at the end-of-query token, response risk/category losses are token-wise, and category loss is conditional on unsafe/controversial ground truth. The rollout composition/count, threshold, judge prompt, calibration, optimizer details, and released token-label data are unknown.

The report separately demonstrates Gen as a reward signal for GSPO safety RL of Qwen3-4B. It creates eight answers per WildJailbreak training prompt in thinking and non-thinking modes, removes groups that are all safe or all unsafe, and retains 13.7k/6.7k samples. This is a downstream application construction rule; it must not be read back into the guard's own 1.19M corpus or SFT contract.
