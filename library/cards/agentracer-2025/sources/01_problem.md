AgenTracer asks a narrower question than whether a multi-agent system completed a task: after a failed execution, which agent induced the failure, and at what earliest decisive step would a correction change the terminal outcome? A single environment success/failure bit cannot answer that attribution question, while an unconstrained LLM diagnosis may be plausible without being causally connected to the outcome. The work therefore treats responsible-agent and decisive-step attribution as process-level supervision grounded by counterfactual replay (Paper Sections 3-4).

The paper-level data object is a complete trajectory `tau=(s0,a0,...,sT)` paired with a responsible agent `i*` and decisive step `t*`. Construction also uses the task ground truth, environmental feedback, a proposed correction or mutation, and the replayed terminal outcome. The public parquet is a narrower object: each row has `question_ID`, `question`, `ground_truth`, `history`, `mistake_agent`, `mistake_step`, and `mistake_reason`; each history item has `content`, `name`, `role`, and integer `step`. It does not expose the paired intervention, replay transcript, or terminal evaluator output.

This belongs in `environment_agent_trajectory_data` because labels are defined over named-agent actions inside executable episodes and are accepted or rejected by an environment-level outcome flip. It is not a general release of all agent-training trajectories, a purely programmatic verifier dataset, or evidence that free-form rationales are faithful. The mixed contract combines model-proposed interventions with task-specific execution.

The official ICLR page identifies the work as an ICLR 2026 Poster; `agentracer-2025` remains the stable atlas ID because the first arXiv version appeared in 2025. The Card is L4-content-ready on the strength of the official venue, OpenReview/arXiv paper and appendix, project page, pinned repository audit, and released-file inspection. That evidence supports the method and a limited public sample, not a claim that TracerTraj-2.5K, AgenTracer-8B, or the paper-scale RL workflow has been released.
