DEER is a training-free controller with three operations. A reasoning transition monitor marks possible exit points either from linguistic action-transition points such as `Wait` and `Alternatively`, or from high entropy at a reasoning-step boundary. An answer inducer temporarily appends an end-of-thinking/final-answer cue and asks the same model for a boxed trial answer. A confidence evaluator takes the maximum predicted probability at each trial-answer token and aggregates multi-token answers with a geometric mean. If confidence exceeds λ, the controller terminates thinking; otherwise it discards the trial branch and resumes from the saved prefix.

DEER-PRo performs several answer inductions with varied prompts, then subtracts a Mean Absolute Deviation-based fluctuation penalty from mean confidence to reduce prompt-noise false exits. The paper also describes Branch-Parallel Decoding: trial branches and continued reasoning are linearized under a special causal mask, then dynamic KV-cache pruning removes branches based on confidence. These components select inference branches; none supplies independent correctness verification.
