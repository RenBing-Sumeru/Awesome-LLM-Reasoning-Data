Fixed budget forcing ends reasoning at a predetermined length, while repeated-answer baselines stop after outputs stabilize at periodic probes. DEER instead uses model-state-dependent candidate points and the probabilities of an induced answer. Its data-facing contribution is an explicit selection record linking a semantic or entropy transition, temporary answer branch, token confidences, aggregation rule, threshold, branch pruning, and final outcome. DEER-PRo adds repeated prompt-conditioned measurements and a dispersion penalty.

Early exit, confidence thresholding, entropy monitoring, prompt ensembles, and KV-cache pruning are not individually new. The direction signal is their composition around a long-CoT branch decision without training a new policy. Because the same model generates the reasoning, trial answer, and confidence signal, the method remains self-assessment rather than an external verifier or reward contract.
