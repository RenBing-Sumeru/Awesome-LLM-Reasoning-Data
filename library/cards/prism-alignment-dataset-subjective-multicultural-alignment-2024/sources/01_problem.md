Alignment often treats feedback as a universal target, even though value-laden judgments differ across people and cultures. Convenience samples and anonymous aggregates make it difficult to separate disagreement from participant composition.

PRISM links survey profiles, stated preferences, live conversations, cardinal ratings, and fine-grained feedback. It supports participatory global, UK/US representative, and individualized analyses. It is pluralistic feedback data, not a license to infer identities or reduce variation to one reward.
