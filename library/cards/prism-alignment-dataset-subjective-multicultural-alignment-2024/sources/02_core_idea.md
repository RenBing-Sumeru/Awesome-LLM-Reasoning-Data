PRISM records who provides what judgment in live interaction. Participants survey first, choose a conversation type, rate responses from 1 to 100, and give fine-grained feedback. Its contract preserves individual context under usage restrictions. Unlike aggregate leaderboards, it enables representative and individualized analyses. The direction is to treat sampling and disagreement as alignment data, not annotation noise.

Official data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/HannahRoseKirk/prism-alignment. Code: https://github.com/HannahKirk/prism-alignment.
