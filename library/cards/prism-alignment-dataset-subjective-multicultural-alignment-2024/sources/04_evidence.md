PRISM links 1,500 participants born in 75 countries to 8,011 conversation trees with 21 LLMs and 68,371 scored utterances. Of surveyed participants, 1,396 reach the conversation stage.

UK and US samples support census-representative analyses, while profiles permit attribution of sampling artifacts. Case studies show conclusions depend on who provides feedback. This supports pluralistic evaluation, not causal claims about a demographic field.
