1. Survey 1,500 participants about demographics, LLM familiarity, self-description, and stated preferences.
2. Participants choose unguided, values-guided, or controversy-guided dialogue and write an opening prompt.
3. Four of 21 LLMs answer the first turn; participant rates 1–100 and the highest-rated model continues.
4. Later, the same model creates A/B responses at non-deterministic temperature; participants rate and explain.
5. Release survey, conversation, utterance, and metadata JSONL with privacy flags. Reuse needs terms compliance, no re-identification, and conversation-level splits.
