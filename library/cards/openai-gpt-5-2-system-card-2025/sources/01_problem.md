OpenAI released the 27-page *Update to GPT-5 System Card: GPT-5.2* on December 11, 2025. The living Deployment Safety Hub later added a Chain of Thought Evaluations section on April 24, 2026; that section is absent from the immutable launch PDF and is treated as a dated audit update.

Relative to `gpt-5-system-card-2025`, training disclosure barely changes. GPT-5.2 repeats public internet, partner-accessed information, and information provided or generated by users, trainers, and researchers, plus family-level reinforcement learning for reasoning. It adds no GPT-5.2 source manifest, mixture, record schema, retained CoT, reward, verifier, rollout, optimizer, or checkpoint lineage.

The meaningful delta is evaluation-data disclosure: two prompt-injection sets are splits of training data; cyber-safety evaluation is explicitly non-overlapping; selected biology sets have narrow held-out or uncontaminated claims; and production traffic, internal PRs, cyber environments, hidden tests, CoT monitors, and graders are described. These are evaluation objects, not training records. The Card belongs in the frontier ledger because it preserves that boundary and the living-page/PDF revision difference.
