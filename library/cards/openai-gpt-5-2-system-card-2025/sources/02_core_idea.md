The contribution is a disclosure delta, not a second generic GPT-5 summary. Training signals actually supported are limited to family-level reasoning RL and purpose-level cyber-safety training for GPT-5.2 Thinking integrations. Their prompts, labels, reward, verifier, weights, calibration, and optimization are unknown.

Evaluation scoring is separate and much richer: policy graders and `not_unsafe`; LLM graders/classifiers; example-specific and hierarchical rubrics; reasoning-based CoT monitors; human experts; manual review; secret flags; hidden unit tests; pass/fail, pass@1 and pass@12; and medal thresholds. Deployment safeguards—system protections, age controls, monitoring, enforcement, and Preparedness mitigations—form a third layer and cannot be attributed to model training.

The report's most reusable data object is therefore an audit record containing a prompt or environment state, response or internal CoT, tools/actions, final artifact, grader/monitor result, sampling stratum, and environment outcome. It is not a released dataset. Compared with the GPT-5 Card, the new value is explicit overlap, narrow held-out claims, production sampling, executable agentic predicates, and the later monitorability audit.
