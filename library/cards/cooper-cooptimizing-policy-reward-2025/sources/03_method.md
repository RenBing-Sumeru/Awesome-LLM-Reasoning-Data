Initial completions use temperature 0.7 and top-p 0.95. VerifyRM is reference-conditioned, initialized from Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B-Instruct, and labels only rule/Qwen agreement data. Cooper trains Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct and Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct policies on a random 10K DeepMath subset for 10 epochs, sampling 16 rollouts per prompt. VerifyRM scores rollouts for GRPO; Math-Verify selects a random correct positive and rejects assistant-generated corruptions. If no valid pair exists, the RM loss is masked. RM optimization uses a contrastive score-difference objective after the policy update.
