Dynamic positive-negative pairs, failed attempts, retry counts, rule outputs, decoded rollouts, and per-step RM checkpoints are not released as a versioned stream; only the static 58.7K VerifyRM data and implementation are public. Assistant corruptions may contain superficial artifacts rather than natural policy failures. The policy, assistant, and RM are closely related, so shared misconceptions and reward drift can survive rule anchoring. Math-Verify's high precision is imperfect and its low recall favors parseable answer formats. Repository Apache-2.0 does not replace upstream dataset terms, and no immutable release pins the run.
