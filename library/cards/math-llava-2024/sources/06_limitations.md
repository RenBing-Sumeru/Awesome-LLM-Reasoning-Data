# Limitations

- **Author-stated boundary:** released records contain images, questions, and short answers but no intermediate rationales; do not treat them as process supervision or use answer form as evidence of faithful reasoning.
- **Curator audit risk:** GPT-4V creates synthetic questions and answers without an independent verifier; sample image-answer consistency by operation type and report error rates before reuse.
- **Curator audit risk:** records inherit mixed source licenses and share images across many variants; resolve per-source redistribution terms and enforce image-level deduplication and splits.
