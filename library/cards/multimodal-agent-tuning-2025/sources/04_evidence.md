All performance claims below are author-reported and were not independently reproduced. The most direct selection ablation compares training data without versus with both verifiers: GTA accuracy rises from 50.00 to 52.56 and GAIA from 13.33 to 15.15 (Paper Section 4.3, Table 5). This is evidence that the filtering configuration affected downstream scores; it is not a measurement of judge precision, record correctness, or source quality.

The scaling ablation holds the reported training recipe while varying MM-Traj size: 6K, 12K, and 20K records yield GTA accuracies of 43.59, 48.08, and 52.56, with reported training times of 276, 532, and 946 minutes and 214 GB memory (Appendix B.1, Table 8). This supports a data-quantity trend under one setup, but subset IDs, sampling seeds, uniqueness, and independent reruns are absent.

For the human study, 30 programmers each rate 20 examples blindly mixed from retained and filtered groups. Tables report retained versus filtered task scores of 8.32 versus 6.36 and trajectory scores of 8.67 versus 6.38 (Paper Section 4.3; Appendix A.1, Table 6). However, adjacent appendix prose instead gives 7.96/6.30 and 8.64/6.24. Without the 600 ratings, assignments, agreement statistics, or corrected report, the study cannot calibrate either judge's false accepts and false rejects.

The release audit supplies a different kind of evidence. At the pinned HF revision, the JSON has exactly 21,168 records and unique IDs. `image` is a dictionary in 14,558 rows and a string in 6,610; this heterogeneity breaks the declared viewer schema. The remote ZIP contains 19,307 regular files, whereas the paper summarizes about 20K tasks and 15K files after a 23.5K-candidate funnel. These figures refer to different units and cannot be reconciled without a manifest.

Benchmark gains therefore do not prove that MM-Traj is correctly licensed, independently verified, decontaminated, complete, or safe to execute. The strongest supported conclusion is narrower: the paper demonstrates that executable, judged multimodal trajectories can train VLM tool controllers, and its ablations associate both filtering and more retained records with higher reported GTA/GAIA scores under the stated setup.
