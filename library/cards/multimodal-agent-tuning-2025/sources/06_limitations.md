- **Correlated feedback.** GPT-4o mini generates queries, non-image files, and trajectories and also performs both judgments. Curator inference: shared errors and stylistic preferences can pass through both filters, while valid trajectories unlike the teacher's style may be rejected. No independent oracle, model panel, precision/recall study, or per-row decision is released.
- **Execution is a weak terminal predicate.** The code gate detects runtime success, not whether the selected tool, observation, arithmetic, or final answer is correct. The trajectory judge remains semantic and model-based, creating false-accept, false-reject, verifier-gaming, and hidden-teacher-trait risks.
- **Verifier modality mismatch.** The appendix query-file prompt is written in terms of images even though MM-Traj includes PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, CSV, audio, and other files. This can systematically mis-handle non-image sufficiency and relevance.
- **Human evidence is internally inconsistent.** Appendix prose reports retained/filtered task and trajectory scores of 7.96/6.30 and 8.64/6.24, while Table 6 and the main discussion report 8.32/6.36 and 8.67/6.38. Raw ratings, agreement, judge confusion matrices, and corrected values are unavailable.
- **Release units do not reconcile.** The paper reports 23.5K candidates, about 20K retained tasks, and about 15K files; the pinned release has 21,168 JSON rows and 19,307 ZIP files. Attachment references and physical files are additional units. No row-file manifest explains duplicates, missing references, or the paper/release drift.
- **Schema and pipeline failures are visible.** `image` is a dictionary in 14,558 rows and a string in 6,610, breaking the HF viewer's declared schema. The checked final-verifier script points to prompt paths absent at its commit, so the advertised shell pipeline cannot run unchanged.
- **Rejected evidence is missing.** Filtered trajectories used in the human study are not released, and there is no accepted/rejected ledger, executable status, verifier response, reason, retry, or failure manifest. Selection bias and informative failures cannot be audited.
- **Licenses do not form one grant.** HF front matter says MIT while the card body says CC-BY-NC-4.0; code is MIT and the model is labeled CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0. SA-1B has a limited research license, and COCO disclaims ownership of image copyrights. None of these declarations resolves row-level upstream rights.
- **Privacy and safety are unknown.** Face, celebrity, landmark, web, and art sources lack record-level consent, attribution, PII review, redaction, takedown, and deletion records. Generated Python, web access, office/PDF inspection, image editing, face detection, and broad packages are released without a documented sandbox, egress policy, malicious-file screening, or incident ledger.
- **Contamination and replay are unresolved.** No exact or semantic overlap audit covers GTA, GAIA, Cauldron, open-LLaVA-NeXT, upstream images/captions, or base-model pretraining. Random sampling has no fixed seed; external APIs, search, retrievers, models, and packages introduce version drift.
- **Modeling scope is narrower than the label.** The authors state that intermediate multimodal results in trajectories are not handled. Reported benchmark gains therefore do not establish general multimodal-agent competence or certify the data's correctness, rights, provenance, or safety.
