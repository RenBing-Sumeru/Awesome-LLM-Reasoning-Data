Earlier multimodal instruction corpora usually provide one answer for perception, OCR, or short visual QA. OmniAlign-V changes the training object to open-ended visual interaction and, crucially, adds a model-sampled, judge-selected rejected answer beside each curated positive answer. The change is therefore the feedback record and its decision point, not a new DPO objective. Its 150K-pair release makes the comparison reusable, but the order is mainly model-generated rather than a broad human annotation of safety, factuality, or culture.
