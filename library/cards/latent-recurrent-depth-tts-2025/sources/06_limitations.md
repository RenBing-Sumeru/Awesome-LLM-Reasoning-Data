The method requires a depth-recurrent model trained for variable unroll lengths, so it cannot be applied as a prompt-only retrofit to most deployed transformers. Sequential recurrence can raise latency even when context use is lower, and the recurrent-depth budget is less transparent to users than a visible reasoning trace. The paper reports a 3.5B proof of concept; transfer to larger models, different data mixtures, or tool-using systems needs direct evaluation rather than extrapolation from parameter-equivalent compute alone.
