Exact-match and token-F1 score extractive QA answers by surface overlap. They can reject a semantically correct answer with a different form, so reported performance may understate a model's reading ability.

This study evaluates LLM-as-a-judge as an alternative on Quoref, DROP, HotpotQA, and 2WikiMultiHopQA. It compares judge verdicts with human judgments and with EM/F1 rather than training a new QA model.
