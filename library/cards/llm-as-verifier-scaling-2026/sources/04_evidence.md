On Terminal-Bench V2 pairwise verification, accuracy rises from 73.1% at one score token to 77.5% at twenty tokens, from 74.7% at one repeat to 77.5% at sixteen repeats, and to 78.3% with three criteria.

The paper reports 86.5% on Terminal-Bench V2, 78.2% on SWE-Bench Verified, 87.4% on RoboRewardBench, and 73.3% on MedAgentBench. These results support verification scaling across agentic domains, but depend on verifier-model access and benchmark protocols.
