Images and questions come from MMPR v1.1. The paper shows general VQA, science, chart, mathematics, OCR, and document examples, but does not provide a paper-snapshot mixture table. InternVL2.5-series models sample four independent step-by-step solutions for every image-question pair. Exact checkpoint sizes, assignment across sources, prompts, seeds, and construction temperature are unknown.

Each solution is split on step boundaries. If it contains more than 12 steps, adjacent material is evenly merged until at most 12 units remain. For each retained prefix, the generator samples 16 completions. A terminal checker compares every extracted final answer with the source answer; mc_i is the accepted fraction. The paper defines the positive label as mc_i > 0 and the negative label as mc_i = 0, and supervises every stored step rather than cutting the example after the first negative step.

This recipe yields about 400K solution samples and about 2M supervised steps. A response averages 126.9 words and 5.6 steps; a step averages 22.6 words; about 10% of steps are negative. These are aggregate paper statistics, not a released per-source or per-generator manifest. The original public dataset converts mc_i to `+/-` conversation targets. Its archive does not expose the 16 continuation texts, parser outputs, or seeds that produced each label.

The paper does not define the paper-time terminal checker for every MMPR source. Later official InternVL code selects match logic from dataset names and combines exact matching with task-dependent VQA, multiple-choice, numerical, LaTeX, and Math-Verify-style checks. That code confirms a plausible automated verifier family and exposes default 4/16/12 arguments, but it was released after the paper and current scripts also target later InternVL3/v1.1 construction. It cannot be treated as an immutable original-run specification.

VisualPRM is trained as a multi-turn generative classifier. It uses packed training for one epoch with AdamW, beta1 0.9, beta2 0.999, weight decay 0.05, learning rate 1e-5, a 5% linear warmup, and cosine decay to zero. At scoring time, the probability mass on the discrete quality tokens is converted to a scalar for each step and mean-aggregated over the response.

VisualProcessBench is built separately. It contains 2,866 questions from MMMU, MathVision, MathVerse Vision-Only, DynaMath, and WeMath, paired with model-generated solutions and 26,950 human step labels: 16,585 positive, 7,691 negative, and 2,674 neutral. Thirteen annotators with at least a university degree worked for three days. Authors spot-checked about 10% of each of ten roughly 300-sample splits and returned erroneous splits for re-annotation. The public test rows contain image paths, question, answer, response steps, process_correctness, policy_model, and data_source.

For test-time selection, policy models generate distinct reasoning candidates at temperature 0.7. N=8 is the default reported setting, with ablations through N=128. VisualPRM selects the response with the highest mean step score. Candidate sets, per-step scores, ties, and rejected responses are not part of the released paper artifacts.
