The paper and TeX source establish the exact construction counts: four initial solutions per image-question pair, 16 continuations per prefix, a maximum of 12 stored/merged steps, about 400K response samples, and about 2M supervised steps. They also establish the threshold mc_i > 0, all-step supervision, the reported optimizer schedule, and the separate human-annotation procedure for VisualProcessBench.

The official release surface is material. VisualPRM400K is public as an ungated approximately 72.7 GB repository with annotation and split image archives; its card says expected accuracies were converted to binary conversation labels. VisualPRM-8B is public as an 8B safetensors checkpoint. VisualProcessBench is public as a 510 MB artifact whose test JSONL reproduces all 2,866 rows, source counts, four policy-model families, 26,950 steps, and the 16,585/7,691/2,674 label totals reported in the table. Official InternVL code later exposes construction and post-processing scripts.

The empirical evidence tests two different capabilities. VisualProcessBench measures binary step-error detection with macro F1 after excluding neutral steps; VisualPRM reports overall F1 62.0. Best-of-N experiments measure whether its mean step score selects better complete responses than controls under the authors' candidate distribution. The paper reports N=8 gains across seven visual benchmarks and further scaling to N=128 for two policy models.

Those results support the claim that the learned scorer can be useful under the evaluated setup. They do not validate every automatic training label, prove the source mixture is uncontaminated, establish upstream rights, or show that the same scorer is calibrated for arbitrary generators. The paper itself attributes some modeling difficulty to noise in the automatically generated data, and stricter positive thresholds reduce aggregate results rather than resolving label truth.

Release verification also reveals version boundaries. The original VisualPRM400K release contains derived conversation labels. A later VisualPRM400K-v1.1 adds sources and prompts, and VisualPRM400K-v1.1-Raw exposes score, num_mc_correct, and num_mc_total fields. These later records demonstrate a richer schema but are not evidence that original-version raw rollouts were published.
