PaCoRe scales coordinated breadth, not just answer count. The compact message is the critical state object, and outcome-based RL is used to teach the model to exploit that state. Its strongest result is HMMT 2025 at high aggregate compute; the corresponding token cost must travel with the accuracy. The release includes checkpoints, data, and inference code, making it appropriate for auditing whether synthesis—not simple majority voting—creates the observed scaling behavior.
