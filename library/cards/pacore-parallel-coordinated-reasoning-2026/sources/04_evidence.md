At the reported high [32,4] setting, PaCoRe-8B reaches 94.5% on HMMT 2025 with 1.796 million effective tokens per problem, compared with the table’s 93.2% GPT-5 reference at 16 thousand tokens. The same table reports 78.4% on IMO-AnswerBench at 2.258 million tokens and gives low and medium budget points, so the paper presents an actual scaling curve rather than a single best-of-N comparison.

The evidence also identifies the cost boundary: the gain is bought with orders of magnitude more aggregate computation, even though the model context window is not exceeded. PaCoRe low already reaches 88.1% on HMMT with 243 thousand tokens, versus 75.4% for the RLVR-8B baseline at 48 thousand. These numbers support coordinated breadth under stated budgets, not a claim of lower total compute than frontier systems.
