For a problem x, the inference pipeline executes R rounds. Round r takes x and the previous compact message set, generates Kr full reasoning trajectories, and compacts their findings into messages for the next round. The final round is a degenerate K=1 synthesis. The reported low, medium, and high configurations are [4], [16], and [32,4], respectively, making the width and number of coordination rounds explicit parts of the budget.

Training uses outcome-based RL to teach cross-trajectory synthesis. Evaluation covers mathematics, coding, science, and open-ended tasks, reports total effective test-time tokens, and compares against RLVR-8B and frontier models. The paper releases checkpoints, training data, and full inference code, which allows a reproduction to inspect message prompts, trajectory sampling, cached first-round pools, and the exact relation between total branch tokens and final context length.
