A fixed context window limits ordinary sequential reasoning: every intermediate thought consumes the same ever-growing prompt, so search eventually stops even if more useful computation is available. Independent parallel samples avoid that depth limit, but a model may ignore their insights, fall back on majority voting, or solve the problem again from scratch rather than synthesizing the useful parts of many attempts.

PaCoRe asks how to scale effective test-time computation far beyond sequential context length while preserving a usable coordination channel. It targets a multi-round record in which many trajectories are compressed into compact messages, those messages guide the next round, and the final answer is produced by learned synthesis rather than an unstructured vote over candidates.
