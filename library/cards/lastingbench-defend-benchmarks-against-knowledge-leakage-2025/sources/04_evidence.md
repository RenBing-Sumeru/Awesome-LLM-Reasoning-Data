Claim: counterfactual rewriting removes a substantial memorization advantage while retaining answerable contexts. Setting: the pipeline revised 61% to 81% of entries across four long-context QA sets; authors manually inspected rewritten contexts. Result: 99% were judged to support the counterfactual answer, and DeepSeek-V3’s HotpotQA EM fell from 0.72 to 0.42 after defense. Boundary: performance drops are behavioral evidence of memory dependence under this rewrite protocol, not direct proof of a model’s training corpus.
