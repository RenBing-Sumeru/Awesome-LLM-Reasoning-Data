Human evaluation reports semantic formalization accuracy of 48.89% at TOP1 and 73.33% at TOP5. Training Goedel-Prover-V2 on the formalizations raises geometry proof success from 13.6% to 15.1%, and 525 of 780 one-pass proofs compile in the current Omni release. These results support downstream utility but also show that roughly half of top-ranked statements may remain semantically wrong; compilation rate is not formalization accuracy.
