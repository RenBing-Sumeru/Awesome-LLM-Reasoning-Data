The baseline pipeline trains DeepSeek-V2-Lite or Qwen2.5-3B on a large validated code-and-math corpus spanning pretraining and SFT. For each experiment, 20% of samples come from the candidate and 80% from the baseline corpus. Evaluation uses accuracy for knowledge, logical-reasoning, and mathematical tasks and Pass@1 for code. The paper lists MMLU, MMLU-Pro, CMMLU, GPQA-Diamond, HellaSwag, BBH, DROP, MATH, GSM8K, MathBench-a/t, HumanEval, and Sanitized-MBPP. It does not provide the baseline records, exact model-to-table mapping, optimizer, number of tokens or steps per run, resampling policy, seeds, or repeated trials.

For Math-Cosmo, the authors collect intermediate-algebra books, OCR their PDFs with Mathpix, denoise the text, and obtain 192 samples totaling about 0.13B tokens. Raw OCR pretraining produces little change. They then prompt Qwen2.5-72B to rewrite the material as educational articles with background knowledge, definitions, formulas, and examples, expanding it to 760M tokens. The appendix publishes the Chinese-corpus rewriting prompt, but the exact books, page IDs, OCR corrections, generation decoding, quality checks, and rejected outputs are absent.

For SFT distillation, QwQ-32B regenerates answers to NaturalReasoning questions. Instances whose regenerated answers are inconsistent are removed, producing NaturalReasoning-QwQ. The paper does not define answer normalization, how many answers are compared, whether agreement is with the original answer or repeated generations, or how ties and malformed traces are handled. Agreement therefore acts as a consistency filter, not a correctness verifier.

For weakness-guided construction, model failures on MATH become seeds. MathBERT embeddings and FAISS retrieve the top 20 similar examples per seed from other datasets, yielding more than 75,000 examples and 82M tokens. A second route asks unspecified LLMs to generate examples similar to seeds or retrieved items and then re-answer them; matching two answers marks the item valid. For textbook QA, chapters and sections are chunked, an LLM extracts a question and solution, another unspecified LLM quality filter is applied, and MinHash deduplicates the survivors. The exact source books, model prompts, retrieval corpus snapshot, thresholds, retention counts, and licenses are not published.
