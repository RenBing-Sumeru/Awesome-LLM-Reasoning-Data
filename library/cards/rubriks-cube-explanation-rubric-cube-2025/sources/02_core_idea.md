Rubrik’s CUBE introduces an education-inspired explanation rubric and has humans plus six open- and closed-source LLMs annotate 26K explanations along its dimensions, enabling analysis of how task, difficulty, and generator affect quality. CUBE contains about 26,000 explanations covering two reasoning and two language tasks. Records connect the item and explanation with task and perceived difficulty and include rubric-based judgments from humans and six model types. The official repository releases the dataset, rubric, annotation resources, and analysis code; ACL materials use CC BY 4.0.
