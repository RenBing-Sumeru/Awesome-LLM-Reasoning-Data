LLM explanations are widely used in education and reasoning, but final-answer checks or a single good/bad label do not reveal why an explanation is unreliable. Automatic evaluation may confuse fluency with pedagogical quality, and cross-task fine-grained rubric data jointly assessed by humans and models is scarce.

Rubrik’s CUBE introduces an education-inspired explanation rubric and has humans plus six open- and closed-source LLMs annotate 26K explanations along its dimensions, enabling analysis of how task, difficulty, and generator affect quality.
