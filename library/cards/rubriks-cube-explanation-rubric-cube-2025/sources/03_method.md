1. **Build the source pool:** Select four task families and collect explanations of varying quality and perceived difficulty from humans or multiple LLMs.

2. **Generate or reorganize feedback:** Define Rubrik from educational assessment principles, decomposing explanation quality into actionable dimensions rather than one overall score.

3. **Verify and filter:** Train human annotators and prompt six open/closed LLMs to apply the same rubric, retaining rater source and dimension labels.

4. **Train and evaluate:** Compare human–model agreement and task/difficulty effects to identify failure sources; the analytical models do not alter the released CUBE records.
