1. **Sample tasks:** Problems are selected from eight verifiable reasoning tasks, with initial model answers and correctness labels collected.
2. **Generate critiques:** Different models perform self-critique or cross-critique, identifying errors and suggesting revisions.
3. **Close the loop:** The problem, original answer, and critique are given to a solver to produce a revised response and correction outcome.
4. **Score effectiveness:** Critiques are measured by fixing incorrect answers, damaging correct answers, and changing overall accuracy rather than by text similarity alone.
5. **Analyze iterations:** Multiple critique-correction rounds test whether additional feedback accumulates gains or amplifies errors.
