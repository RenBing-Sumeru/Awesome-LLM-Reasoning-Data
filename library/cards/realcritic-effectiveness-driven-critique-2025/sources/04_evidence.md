Across eight tasks, critiques from conventional LLMs often sound reasonable without producing correct revisions. Self-critique is especially prone to reinforcing the original error, and additional iterations can reduce performance. Critiques from reasoning models such as o1-mini are substantially more effective, while gains depend on matching critic and solver capabilities. Closed-loop accuracy provides more direct evidence than prose quality, although outcomes remain confounded by the solver: a weak solver may misuse good feedback, while a strong solver may correct despite poor feedback.
