The paper builds a common map with four connected parts: foundation models, reasoning tasks, methods for eliciting or improving reasoning, and benchmarks. The useful move is to keep the task and its measurement beside the model claim. A chain of text may be helpful evidence in a math problem but irrelevant in a visual grounding task; an agent result may depend on tools and environment state. Readers should therefore compare papers by the full task contract, not by a shared label such as “strong reasoning.”
