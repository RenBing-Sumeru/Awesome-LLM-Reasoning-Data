This is a literature synthesis, not a training recipe. The authors arrange prior work by task type and by methodological choices, then connect each family to the models and benchmarks used to study it. Use the same procedure when reading a cited paper: write down the input, required intermediate representation, available external tools, final output, and score. Then ask whether training data teaches those steps, whether inference adds search or prompting, and whether the evaluator checks only the final answer or also the process.
