Start here when a project says “we need reasoning data” but has not specified the target behavior. Use the task map to choose a narrow capability, then define what an example contains and how success will be checked. For instance, a geometry proof, a retrieval question, and a tool-use trajectory should not inherit one another’s labels or metrics. The paper also helps students turn a vague literature search into concrete columns: task, input, intermediate signal, feedback, benchmark, and unresolved failure.
