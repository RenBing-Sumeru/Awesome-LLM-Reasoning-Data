Read the task and benchmark portions before the outlook. Make a two-column note for each relevant family: “what behavior is required?” and “what evidence can establish it?” If those answers are missing, do not treat a capability name as a complete specification. Next read the method sections with one practical question: did the claimed gain come from better examples, changed training, more inference computation, or stronger external tools? This habit prevents a broad survey from becoming a list of interchangeable techniques.
