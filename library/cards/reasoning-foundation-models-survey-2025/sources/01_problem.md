Reasoning is often reported as one number, even though a model may be answering a language puzzle, reading an image, calling a tool, or following a long plan. Those settings use different inputs, intermediate information, and scoring rules. This 2025 survey asks how foundation-model reasoning should be described without mixing those cases together. It collects reasoning tasks, methods, and benchmarks rather than releasing a new training set. For a student building or judging reasoning data, the key question is simple: what evidence does one task actually require before a score can be called reasoning?
