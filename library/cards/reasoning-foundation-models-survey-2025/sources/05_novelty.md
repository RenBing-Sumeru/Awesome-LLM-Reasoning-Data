Earlier introductions often isolated one reasoning technique or one application. This survey places language, multimodal, agentic, and alignment-facing reasoning in a shared frame. Its novelty is the comparison lens, not a new model. It makes an important distinction visible: two systems may receive the same final-answer score while relying on different data, representations, and external support. That distinction matters when deciding whether a result should influence data construction, model training, or only a particular evaluation setup.
