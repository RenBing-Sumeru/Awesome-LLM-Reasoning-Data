The main evidence is author-reported under the paper’s Qwen3 training setup. Table 1 gives overall benchmark averages from base to SFT+RL of 16.27 to 19.74 for 1.7B, 24.09 to 30.77 for 4B, and 29.23 to 33.40 for 8B. Direct RL gains are smaller and less stable than the complete SFT+RL path. These results show that the full recipe can improve evaluated tool use under the stated setup; they do not isolate environment synthesis, trajectory selection, reward code, optimizer, or data volume as the sole cause.

Reward ablation provides narrower mechanism evidence. With length penalty fixed and alpha varied over 0, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, and 1, alpha 0.5 reaches the reported peak of 41.38% on BFCL multi-turn; state-only and trace-only variants are worse. This supports combining action-reference and final-state signals for that experiment, but it is not an independent audit of false positives, false negatives, masked arguments, order invariance, or reward hacking.

Evaluation scope is incomplete. MCP-Atlas results use 30 of 36 servers and 291 of 500 tasks because of connectivity constraints. The Card therefore does not generalize those scores to the full benchmark. It also does not treat benchmark improvement as evidence that the public datasets have correct lineage, complete failures, adequate privacy controls, or safe licensing.

Release evidence is object-specific. The paper reports 85 environments, 842 tools, 1,622 SFT conversations, and 953 RL conversations. Pinned HF viewers expose 26,463 SFT-FILTERED rows, 53,412 SFT-ALL rows, and 3,092 RL rows. The SFT-FILTERED card prose says about 53,400 while its viewer has 26,463; the SFT-ALL card prose says about 26,500 while its viewer has 53,412. The main repository and collection follow the viewer-consistent mapping, while model cards still describe filtered data as 53.4k. This unresolved documentation conflict must not be silently reconciled.

The paper and release also disagree on SFT epochs and licensing. The paper reports 3 SFT epochs and initialization of RL from epoch 1, while current repository config and HF model cards state 1 epoch. HF datasets/models and the linked VeRL fork declare Apache-2.0; the main repository has no detected license, and the paper describes artifacts as using restrictive licenses. These are release-audit findings, not experimental results.

No independent reproduction was performed for this Card. Evidence supports existence of executable environments, public row schemas, reward inputs/code, and author-reported training outcomes. It does not prove semantic equivalence to production services, correctness of every selected trace, completeness of failure retention, or legal and privacy clearance.
