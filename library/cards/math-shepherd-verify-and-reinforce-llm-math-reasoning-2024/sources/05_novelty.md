Compared with assigning a whole chain of thought one correct-or-incorrect label, this work changes the unit or acquisition path of process evidence. Math-Shepherd generates step-level supervision from answer derivations and automatic verifiers, avoiding large-scale manual process annotation. Starting from a problem and its final answer, it generates candidate derivations and assigns intermediate-step values using answer consistency and Monte Carlo estimates. This is its direction signal; the reusable contribution is the data contract, quality control, and evaluation boundary.
