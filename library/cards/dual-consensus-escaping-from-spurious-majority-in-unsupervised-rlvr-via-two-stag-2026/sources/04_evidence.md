On DAPO-Math-14K, DCRL’s Qwen3-8B average across eight benchmarks is 50.9, above the label-free TTRL baseline’s 50.2 but below gold-label GRPO’s 53.5. Its MATH-500 score is 79.2 versus 78.3 for TTRL and 82.0 for GRPO. The ablation on the same model lowers the five-benchmark average from 56.3 to 56.0 without harmonic election, 55.4 without the conservative reward, and 53.4 without dynamic sampling. Test-time adaptation also exceeds TTRL on the reported four datasets, but the results remain an author-run comparison rather than evidence that pseudo-label errors are eliminated.
