If anchor and explorer share a wrong prior, dual consensus provides no corrective signal and can reinforce the error. On tasks far outside pretraining, the explorer may not discover a useful alternative path. Each update also needs two rollout groups and a temporary gradient step, increasing cost and making outcomes sensitive to answer extraction, probability clipping, and the consensus threshold. The training data are described as DAPO-Math-14K, but label-free reward construction should not be confused with proving that all data curation or evaluation is label-free.
