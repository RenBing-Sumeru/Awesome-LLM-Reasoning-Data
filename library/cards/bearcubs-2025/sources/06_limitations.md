- Public-release completeness: BearCubs_20250310.json.zip has 111 question-only records. Gold answers, text/multimodal labels, URLs, viable human paths, model outputs, run times, outcome labels, and full agent trajectories are missing, so the public artifact is not an episode corpus.
- Split and lineage: the paper reports 56 text-based and 55 multimodal items, 65 author-written and 46 freelancer-written items, and 108 top-level URLs, but none of those record-level labels or provenance fields is public. No train/dev/test split is defined.
- Success/failure retention: aggregate correct, wrong, uncertain/abstaining, and no-answer/loop counts are published, but complete successful paths, failed paths, loops, screenshots, outputs, timestamps, source labels, retries, and discarded runs are unavailable. Three examples for one question are not a representative corpus.
- Replay: the substrate is the mutable public web through proprietary product interfaces. No browser image, HTML or screenshot snapshot, account/network state, CAPTCHA policy, reset fixture, action API, executable terminal checker, or deterministic replay environment is released.
- Judge reproducibility: the optional autorater needs gated gold answers and model outputs, depends on a dated external GPT API, and has no artifact license, dependency lock, retry/error contract, API snapshot, or expected-output fixture. Temperature 0 and seed 1130 do not prevent backend or model drift.
- Verifier scope: final-answer entailment does not verify the intended source, modality, or path. One-short-answer tasks exclude unanswered, multiple-answer, and long-form cases and may penalize valid alternatives or formatting while concealing evidence quality.
- Comparison confounds: commercial-agent revisions, decoding settings, seeds, tool budgets, UI configurations, and retries are not pinned. The OpenAI Chat GPT Agent product receives 45 minutes rather than 15, and systems were run in February/March, May, and July against different web and product states.
- Contamination and version drift: questions are public and the authors acknowledge future contamination risk. The dated March question file, July website/paper updates, IDs 1–129 with 18 gaps, and the 24.3% versus 23.4% Operator change are not reconciled by a semantic version, changelog, replacement map, immutable manifest, official checksum, tag, or repository commit.
- Rights: the OpenReview paper page displays CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, but neither ZIP contains a license. Gold access is limited to case-by-case non-commercial research and redistribution/open-sourcing is prohibited. The paper license cannot be transferred to the question data, autorater code, web content, gold answers, model outputs, or trajectories.
- Human and privacy audit: freelancer payment and parts of the multilingual human study are disclosed, but consent, IRB or ethics review, demographics, total labor, rejected-work compensation, privacy review, and rights for captured live-web or proprietary-agent content remain unknown.
