Question construction is human-led. Authors wrote 65 of the 111 retained items and trained Upwork freelancers wrote the remaining 46; freelancer submissions had a 58.2% acceptance rate and were paid USD 4 per accepted question. Candidates had to be concise and sufficiently specified, have one concise and uniquely gradable answer, resist ordinary Google Search, and use publicly accessible evidence without paywalls, accounts, or login. Record-level writer identity, source URL, collection date, and rejected-work ledger are not released.

At least two authors checked each retained question, answer, viable trajectory, and link against the validity criteria. For multimodal candidates, OpenAI Deep Research was used adversarially to search for text-only workarounds. Thirteen proposed multimodal questions were removed, mostly when such workarounds were found. Exact reviewer assignment, disagreement adjudication, candidate-pool size, per-domain quotas, randomization, and freshness thresholds remain unknown.

Evaluated agents ran through their native commercial interfaces on the unrestricted live web. Computer-use agents consumed rendered pixels and controlled a virtual mouse and keyboard; other systems exposed provider-specific search or reasoning summaries. Agents were prompted to complete CAPTCHAs, accept prompts, and minimize user intervention, and received at most one directive to continue without help. A session ended on answer or abstention, a no-progress dead loop, or a second request for help. The computer-use cap was 15 minutes except 45 minutes for the OpenAI Chat GPT Agent product. This terminal rule is prose, not released executable code.

The baseline protocol fixed GPT-4o-2024-11-20 and DeepSeek R1 at temperature 0, with maximum outputs of 518 and 8,000 tokens respectively. Search-augmented baselines concatenated up to 10 Google result titles and snippets retrieved through Serper. Exact commercial-agent checkpoint revisions, decoding settings, tool budgets, seeds, retry policy, and total evaluation cost are unknown. Most agents were run from 23 February to 1 March 2025, Google Deep Research in late May, and the OpenAI Chat GPT Agent product from 18 to 20 July, so web and product state differ across comparisons.

The released autorater contains README.md, GetResponse.py, autorater.py, and autorater_prompt.py. It uses GPT-4o-2024-11-20, 17 in-context examples, temperature 0, seed 1130, and a default 518-token cap; the paper reports about 1 minute 30 seconds and USD 0.80 to label all 111 items. It requires an external OpenAI Chat Completions API plus openai, tiktoken, and tqdm. There is no artifact license, requirements or lock file, pinned API snapshot, retry/error contract, schema validation, or offline expected-output fixture.
