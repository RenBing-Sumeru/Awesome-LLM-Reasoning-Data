On the final COLM/arXiv v3 results, humans score 84.7% overall, the OpenAI Chat GPT Agent product 65.8%, OpenAI Deep Research 36.0%, and Operator and Google Deep Research 23.4% each. On the 55 multimodal questions, humans score 85.7%, the OpenAI Chat GPT Agent product 54.5%, Operator 12.7%, and Anthropic Computer Use and Proxy 9.1% each. Older arXiv abstract metadata reported Operator at 24.3%; this Card uses the final v3 table and project leaderboard value of 23.4%.

The optional GPT autorater is reported at 98.2% four-way accuracy and 98.7% binary accuracy overall, with per-agent four-way accuracy between 96.4% and 99.1%. The main study nevertheless says the published responses were manually evaluated, so the autorater is not the sole origin of the reported labels. Its high agreement on this sample does not establish stable behavior after model/API changes, independence from its 17 demonstrations, or access to the unreleased evidence required for full benchmark reproduction.

Trajectory analysis identifies several concrete failures. Agents avoid or fail videos, games, 3D scenes, and fine-control interactions; they struggle to filter interactive databases; they revisit irrelevant pages and repeat failed actions; and failure becomes more likely when a run does not succeed quickly. Commercial systems also expose trajectories at incompatible detail levels, from granular actions to short summaries or only top-level URLs, so step counts and causal failure attribution are not directly comparable.

Answer correctness also hides evidence quality. The authors report that some correct answers rest on secondary or ungrounded sources. For OpenAI Deep Research, Table 9 counts 15 of 40 correct answers in those categories, or 37.5%. The paper prose elsewhere says 38.5%, an internal inconsistency that should not be silently resolved. In either case, final-answer entailment does not prove use of an authoritative source or the intended multimodal interaction.

The artifact evidence is narrower than the performance evidence. The public question ZIP verifiably contains 111 question-only records, and the four-file autorater ZIP was inspected and hashed. Neither artifact contains the full task manifest or any complete agent-run corpus. Reported benchmark performance therefore supports comparative evaluation under the stated protocol; it is not proof that the public data are complete, that trajectories are high-quality training examples, or that the live environment is reproducible.
