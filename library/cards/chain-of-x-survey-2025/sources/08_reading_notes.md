Read the definition of Chain-of-X and the node taxonomy before browsing the application examples. For any cited method, write a short schema for one chain item and a short sentence for the task objective; this makes the two taxonomies operational. Next, ask what produces each node, what consumes it, and whether it is checked, executed, or simply shown to the model. These details distinguish a reasoning trace from an action sequence, a retrieval sequence, or an output format. The final discussion is useful for recognizing where a sequential design is genuinely necessary and where it mainly changes the presentation of intermediate work. Keep conclusions modest when the underlying studies use different tasks, models, or budgets.
