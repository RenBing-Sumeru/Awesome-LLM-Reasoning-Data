Its method is a literature review. It first gives background on Chain-of-Thought, defines Chain-of-X as a broader sequential pattern, and then organizes existing methods by node types and tasks before discussing findings and future directions. The recurring object is a chain: a prompt, one or more intermediate nodes, and a final output, with the node semantics determined by the specific method. The survey is useful as a reading procedure as well as a taxonomy. When examining a cited primary paper, record the input, the unit repeated in the chain, how one unit leads to the next, and the final task output. That schema exposes whether two apparently related methods actually share a mechanism or merely share sequential formatting. It also identifies where an implementation needs a generator, an executor, a checker, or no additional component at all.
