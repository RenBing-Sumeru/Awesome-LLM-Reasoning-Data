For the **Data Construction & Open Release Recipes** track, LIMO is useful as a small-final-set curation case, a base-dependence experiment, and a release-audit example. It is less useful as a directly reproducible construction package.

- **Conditional SFT reuse:** pin LIMO-v2 dataset revision `ef8bf143f8cc56ca8634795835b55083a3ae3061` and model revision `9510d41a1f5aca4bc86bbb52d63d0ab13da52ab1`; confirm 800 rows and three fields; deduplicate against target benchmarks; resolve upstream rights; and do not substitute the 817-row GitHub/v1 file.
- **Difficulty-filter study:** reproduce four-attempt and 32-attempt solve-rate bands with pinned models, prompts, decoding settings, answer checker, and seeds. Preserve per-attempt results and rejected problems so difficulty calibration and false decisions can be analyzed.
- **Trace-score ablation:** compare the published 30/20/25/25 lexical proxy against length-matched random selection, step verification, answer-only selection, human rankings, and semantic quality models. Publish complete keyword lists and score components.
- **Hidden-cost accounting:** report total candidate rows, inference tokens, teacher calls, hardware, GPU-hours, wall time, human examination effort, and acceptance rates. This turns “800 examples” into a measurable end-to-end efficiency claim.
- **Lineage schema:** attach source dataset/split/item/revision, source rights, teacher/checkpoint, candidate-group ID, solve counts, component scores, selection rank, correctness outcome, dedup result, and rejection reason to each retained or rejected candidate.
- **Base-dependence baseline:** train the same selected traces across base families and sizes while holding optimizer and token budget fixed. Separate “knowledge already present” from “reasoning elicited by demonstrations.”
- **Release repair:** replace or clearly label the bundled 817-row file, register the 800-row v2 dataset in `dataset_info.json`, replace the example dataset placeholder, and publish an exact command plus hardware/world-size manifest for global batch 64.
- **License audit:** reconcile the root MIT claim, missing root LICENSE, Apache-2.0 training subtree, and Apache-2.0 HF data/model; map each upstream contest/exam source to attribution and redistribution requirements.

Reuse class: conditionally suitable for answer-level mathematical SFT after source-rights, overlap, and row-quality review; strong for reading, controlled ablation design, and release audit; blocked as a faithful reproduction of the construction pipeline until code, parameters, lineage, and rejects are released. It does not directly support PRM, preference learning, RLVR replay, or agent training.
