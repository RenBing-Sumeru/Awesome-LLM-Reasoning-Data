The authors evaluate multiple frontier and open models and find that no model exceeds 34% pass@1 on code-generation tasks, with module reuse, testbench construction, assertion generation, and debugging especially difficult. Data cleaning provides controlled evidence: only 783 of 1,313 authored candidates remain after an overall filtering rate of about 40.4%, and pass@1 rises in most categories after filtering, indicating that invalid or ambiguous tasks reduce interpretability. This supports benchmark difficulty and quality control, but some comprehension tasks use an LLM judge and are not as deterministic as compilation or simulation results.
