Traditional miniF2F evaluation treats each complete theorem as one task, providing few examples and little information about where a model fails. IMO-Steps changes the granularity of supervision and evaluation by converting manually completed long proofs into dependency-structured local lemmas, each independently checked by Lean.

The novelty is not arbitrary line splitting or generation of easy exercises. Decomposition units follow the mathematical structure of complete IMO proofs, preserving hard-problem context while yielding more than a thousand trainable examples. The paper also distinguishes correct informal reasoning, valid Lean code, and complete-proof ability, avoiding the assumption that failure in formal language implies total failure of mathematical reasoning.
