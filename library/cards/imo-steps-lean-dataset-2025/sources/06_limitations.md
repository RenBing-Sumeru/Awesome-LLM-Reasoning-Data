1. **Path dependence:** Lemma decomposition reflects the authors’ reference proofs. Other valid proofs may not require the same intermediate results, making the data more suitable for diagnosing one route than enumerating all mathematical strategies.

2. **Uneven structure:** A few problems contribute hundreds of lemmas, so long proofs can dominate aggregate accuracy. Results should include macro averages by source problem and breakdowns by proof length.

3. **Version fragility:** The data explicitly depends on Lean 4.17.0 and its Mathlib version. Upgrades may break imports, tactics, or names. Reusers should recompile the entire corpus and record automated repairs separately from original proofs.
