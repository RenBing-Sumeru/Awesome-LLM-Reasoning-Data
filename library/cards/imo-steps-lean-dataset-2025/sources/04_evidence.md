The final dataset contains 1,329 lemmas. In the updated evaluation, DeepSeek-Prover-1.5 achieves the highest accuracy at roughly 39%, while model-level analyses show that systems disproportionately solve shorter or more automation-friendly steps. The dataset page reports that o3-mini produces correct natural-language reasoning for 75.5% of lemmas but valid Lean proofs for only 23.8%, exposing a large gap between mathematical understanding and compilable formal proof generation.

These results support the diagnostic value of decomposed lemmas beyond all-or-nothing full-problem scores. However, the lemmas follow the authors’ reference proof paths, and a model might solve the complete theorem through a different route. Local accuracy therefore cannot be converted directly into full IMO proof success.
