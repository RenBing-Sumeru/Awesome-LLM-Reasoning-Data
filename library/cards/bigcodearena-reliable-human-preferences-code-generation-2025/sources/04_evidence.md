The collection demonstrates feasibility at realistic scale: after more than five months of deployment, BigCodeArena recorded over 14,123 conversations from more than 500 unique IP addresses. It covers 10 frontier LLMs, 10 programming languages, and 8 execution environments. For reliable comparison, the authors filter out conversations with fewer than two turns or without code execution, yielding 4,731 voting samples; every evaluated model receives at least 700 votes. The paper also compares code judges with and without execution information and reports that most judges become more reliable when execution results are available. These results support execution-grounded preference collection and reward-model evaluation, not the stronger claim that passing execution fully captures code quality, security, or user intent.
