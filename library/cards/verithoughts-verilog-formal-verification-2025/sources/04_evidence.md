After fine-tuning Qwen2.5-14B on about 10K records, VeriThoughts-14B reaches 43.7% pass@1 on the standard VerilogEval benchmark, exceeding other open 14B baselines in the table, including 38.7% for DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B. It reaches 78.5% on the VeriThoughts test set. Controlled analyses also show that self-consistent data helps in some settings but not on every benchmark; some inconsistent samples perform better when the generated `V*` is treated as the target. The evidence shows that formal labels are analytically useful, but they are not a simple ranking of downstream data utility.
