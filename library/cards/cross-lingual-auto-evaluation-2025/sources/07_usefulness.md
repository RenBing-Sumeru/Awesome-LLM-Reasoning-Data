RECON can test whether a multilingual judge genuinely agrees with human ratings across languages and can serve as a small supervision set for calibrating a general evaluator. Hercule’s approach is useful for multilingual chatbot, summarization, and instruction-following evaluation when native references are scarce: retain an English canonical answer while scoring the target-language response directly. Researchers can extend the setup to new languages and test zero-shot transfer. Language-specific rubrics remain necessary for grammar, style, and cultural dimensions rather than relying only on English-reference semantics.
