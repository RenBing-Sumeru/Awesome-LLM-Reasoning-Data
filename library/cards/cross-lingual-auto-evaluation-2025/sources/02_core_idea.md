The paper introduces the CIA Suite, consisting of the cross-lingual evaluator Hercule and the RECON test set. RECON contains 500 human-annotated instructions spanning multiple task capabilities and six languages. Each record includes a prompt, a target-language response, and human judgment scores, supporting evaluation of both general multilingual LLMs and evaluator models. Hercule learns to score target-language responses using readily available English reference answers, addressing the scarcity of references in low-resource languages. Although modest in size, the dataset provides a rare cross-lingual human-judge alignment surface.
