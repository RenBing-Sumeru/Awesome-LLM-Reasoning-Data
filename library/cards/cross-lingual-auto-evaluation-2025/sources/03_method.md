1. **Task selection:** Five hundred instructions are selected across generation and reasoning tasks so the benchmark is not limited to translation quality.
2. **Language coverage:** Tasks are instantiated in six languages, with target-language responses of varying quality generated or collected.
3. **Human scoring:** Annotators who understand the corresponding language evaluate the responses, creating human judgment labels for RECON.
4. **Cross-lingual input:** Hercule receives both the target-language response and an English reference answer, learning cross-lingual semantic alignment and quality scoring.
5. **Generalization testing:** Correlation with human scores is compared among Hercule, proprietary judges, and other metrics on both seen and unseen languages.
