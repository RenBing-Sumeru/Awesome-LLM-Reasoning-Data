Previous multilingual evaluators often require same-language references, translate responses before judging, or validate only on machine translation. This work treats English references as reusable intermediates, allowing the evaluator to compare semantics across languages without explicitly translating the response. It also releases RECON human judgments for meta-evaluating evaluators rather than merely reporting downstream model scores. The novelty is the combination of cross-lingual reference-conditioned evaluation and multitask human-alignment data. Compared with large benchmarks, RECON functions more as a high-quality calibration set focused on evaluator validity rather than exhaustive language coverage.
