The 6T schedule is reported in Section 2.3: 1T code/math web plus 4T curated code at LR 3e-4, followed by 400B continued tokens after a sqrt(10) LR reduction and a final 600B at LR 3e-5. Ingredient disclosures include about 1T retained GitHub tokens, 100B commit tokens, 1.2T code-web tokens, 130B high-quality recalled tokens, and 1T long-context continued-pretraining tokens. These ingredients may overlap; the report does not provide a reconciled source-by-stage matrix.

Section 2.2 reports 74M commits from 140K repositories. The quality scorer trains one epoch on 222,066 oracle-labeled files and tests on 1,178 labels from the same DeepSeek-V2-Chat oracle, with overall MAE 0.91 and category-balanced MAE 1.37 on the original 0-10 scale. This measures oracle imitation; zero-score files remain difficult. The web recall experiment uses 10M candidates, 70/30 seed/validation, 99% recall and 45% precision.

Section 3 reports approximately 3M final SFT pairs, three SFT epochs at LR 2e-5, and approximately 20K on-policy DPO pairs. Section 3.2 reports thousands of warmup traces and GRPO batch 128, LR 1e-6, temperature 0.6, no KL, clip 0.28, 90 steps with 16 rollouts/prompt at 16K, then 160 steps with 32 at 32K. These conditions do not reveal the correctness/format reward equations or total retained trajectories.

The released Reasoning model exposes 65,536-token context; the paper reports that more than 10% of IOI/Codeforces evaluation samples truncate at 64K. The official repository also corrected an inconsistent BigCodeBench setting, showing that evaluation results depend on harness configuration. Scores are not evidence of training-data quality.
