The work supplies a schema for early-exit reasoning records. An auditable example should preserve the source prompt and split, full trace, sentence boundaries, every prefix, temporary end marker, normalized partial answer, convergence sequence, final full-trace answer, gold outcome when available, activation revision, stopper score, threshold, selected stopping point, saved tokens, and faithfulness review. These fields support calibration of false-early-stop and missed-stop errors across difficulty.

Reuse must keep stability, full-trace agreement, benchmark correctness, and rationale faithfulness as separate signals. The reported efficiency gains justify studying the controller; they do not establish that unreleased prefix labels or truncated traces are high-quality post-training data.
