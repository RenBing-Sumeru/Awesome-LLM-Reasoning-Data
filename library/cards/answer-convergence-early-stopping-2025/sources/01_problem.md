Long chain-of-thought decoding can continue after a model's predicted answer has stabilized. The paper asks how much of an explicit reasoning trace is needed before the model reaches the same answer it would give after full decoding, and whether that signal can stop generation without gold labels. The data object is a full reasoning trace split into sentence-level prefixes, plus the answer elicited from every prefix, convergence labels or activation features, and the chosen stopping point. Answer stability is a self-consistency property; it is not evidence that the stabilized answer is correct.
