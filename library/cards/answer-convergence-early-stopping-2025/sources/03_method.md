For the preliminary analysis, each model-generated CoT is segmented with the NLTK sentence tokenizer. Prefixes are built cumulatively; an end-of-reasoning marker is appended, and the model greedily produces a partial answer from each prefix. The earliest prefix whose answer remains identical through the final prefix defines the convergence label. The supervised variant records final-layer activations at chunk boundaries, feeds their sequence to an LSTM and sigmoid classifier, trains with binary cross-entropy, and tunes its stop threshold on validation data. The two unsupervised variants instead use repeated partial answers or an adaptive logit boost and do not modify the base model.

Experiments cover NaturalQuestions, GSM8K, MATH-500, GPQA-Diamond, and AIME 2024 with five open-weight models from R1-distilled Qwen/Llama and QwQ families. Reported outputs include accuracy, generated tokens, token reduction, convergence ratios, and stopping decisions. The paper provides an ACL record and a Hugging Face Space link, but this Card has not verified a downloadable release containing the chunked traces, prefix answers, activation tensors, train/validation labels, learned stopper weights, exact thresholds, seeds, or per-example stop logs; those artifacts remain unknown or unavailable.
