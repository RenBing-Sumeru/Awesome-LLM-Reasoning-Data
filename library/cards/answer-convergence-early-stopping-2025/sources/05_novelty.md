Prior efficiency work uses fixed token budgets, concise prompts, length-aware training, or external confidence. This paper makes prefix-answer convergence an explicit inference-time control object and compares three ways to operationalize it: repeated-output stability, end-token logit adjustment, and an activation-based learned stopper. The distinctive supervision for Learn-to-Stop is derived from the model's own final answer rather than gold correctness. Sentence segmentation, greedy decoding, LSTM classification, and early exit are not individually new. The direction signal is the trace schema linking prefix, partial answer, activation, convergence label, stop decision, and final outcome.
