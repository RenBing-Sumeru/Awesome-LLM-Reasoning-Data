Prior surveys commonly isolate a task family or a technique. This paper's change is to combine diverse reasoning paradigms with methods, benchmarks, and failure modes in one “periodic table” framing. This makes it a recent direction signal that the field needs cross-paradigm navigation, especially when apparently similar scores arise from different data and evaluation contracts.

It does not introduce chain-of-thought, agentic reasoning, reward modeling, or the cited benchmarks, and it does not establish a universal taxonomy for data provenance. The authors state that their literature analysis draws on several scholarly and paper-indexing sources; primary-source verification remains mandatory before reusing a cited work. The work is an arXiv preprint with no confirmed archival venue.
