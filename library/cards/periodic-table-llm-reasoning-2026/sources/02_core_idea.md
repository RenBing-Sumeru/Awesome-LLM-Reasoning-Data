The survey proposes a structured taxonomy covering chain-of-thought, multi-hop, mathematical, common-sense, visual, temporal, code and algorithmic, retrieval-augmented, tool-augmented and agentic, and reinforcement-learning-based reasoning. It connects those paradigms to trends in prompting, architecture, training objectives, reward modeling, and evaluation benchmarks.

Its feedback contract is indirect: it surveys how systems are scored or trained, but does not impose a common judge, reward, or terminal predicate. A key contribution is the connection between a paradigm label and its recurring failure surface, including reasoning hallucination, brittle multi-step inference, weak causal abstraction, and poor cross-domain generalization. The closest comparison is a post-training taxonomy; this Card uses the paper to route across reasoning modalities and failure questions.
