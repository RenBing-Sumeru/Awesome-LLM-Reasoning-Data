Visual-language models trained only on image-question-answer triples can match the final answer while hiding whether they localized the right object, read the right text, or performed the right calculation. This conclusion-aligned supervision is especially weak for small objects, OCR, counting, geometry, and charts, where an auditable intermediate visual operation matters.

CogCoM turns those intermediate operations into released demonstrations: GPT-4 proposes manipulation-assisted steps, GroundingDINO and PaddleOCR instantiate visual evidence, depth-first search keeps chains that terminate at the golden answer, and experts add graphical-math chains. The resulting record is a visual question plus structured manipulation findings and a final chain consumed by multimodal SFT.

L4 facts: Primary source: arXiv:2402.04236 and the ICLR 2025 final paper; venue/date: ICLR 2025, official OpenReview record; decision boundary: released evidence-bearing visual rationale records rather than a tool-only inference scaffold or model weights alone; atlas object/evaluation: image, question, answer, manipulation nodes, boxes/text/calculations, and final chain, evaluated by VQA, grounding, math, hallucination, and chain metrics; collection note: `L4_carded`, one Track 01 category.
