Under the paper protocol, 17 models are run three times on all 108 tasks with at most 100 turns, yielding 5,508 task-run attempts by arithmetic from the disclosed design. The reported metrics are mean Pass@1 with standard deviation, Pass@3, Pass^3, and average turns. Table 3 reports Claude-4.5-Sonnet at 38.6% mean Pass@1 and DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp at 20.1%; these are paper-snapshot results, not current Toolathlon-Verified leaderboard values (Paper §4.1 and Table 3, pp. 7–8).

The score level is evidence that the 108 tasks are difficult for the tested systems under this scaffold, not proof that every task, evaluator, or recorded trajectory is high quality. A task can fail because of model planning, tool invocation, context handling, credentials, initialization, mutable external services, or the evaluator itself. Conversely, a deterministic terminal checker can pass an unintended route if its predicate omits a material side effect. The paper reports no task-level false-positive/false-negative study or adversarial evaluator audit.

The paper's failure analysis identifies tool-call errors, long-context degradation, premature stopping, and overlong tool outputs. Depending on the model, roughly 15%–35% of trajectories encounter overlong outputs; the OpenAI Agents SDK scaffold truncates outputs above 100K characters and provides 10K-character paging. This is a concrete interaction between benchmark infrastructure and measured agent behavior, but the observed association does not isolate a causal effect (Paper §5.1–§5.2, pp. 8–9; Appendix B, pp. 14–15).

The later release provides a different evidence surface. Toolathlon-Verified is dated 2026-06-30 and states that prompts, ground truths, evaluators, and infrastructure were substantially revised. Its gated Hugging Face card lists 10 model archives with three complete 108-task runs each; 30 run summaries cover all 108 tasks, nominally 3,240 episodes. It also warns that some `summary.config_used` model labels are stale and some models have fewer than 324 valid AvgTurns records. These warnings support auditability, but the inaccessible raw archives prevent independent confirmation of file-level completeness, redaction changes, and corrupt or dropped episode handling.

Paper and current-release scores must not be merged into a single progress curve. The benchmark artifacts, evaluator behavior, infrastructure, provider defaults, and model generations changed, and no immutable manifest maps the paper snapshot to repository commit `3b647e6` and Hugging Face revision `01deb37`. All numerical results remain author-reported and not independently reproduced in the checked evidence. They establish comparative performance under specified snapshots; they do not establish contamination freedom, trajectory training value, reusable licensing, or stationary benchmark validity.
