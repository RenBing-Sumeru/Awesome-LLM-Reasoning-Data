The authors compare rule-based methods, ordinary LLM-as-a-judge systems, and memory-augmented AgentAuditor on ASSEBench and additional safety benchmarks. The results show that experiential memory and context-aware retrieval consistently improve judgment performance across different backbone models, reaching accuracy close to human experts on both safety and security tasks. The Strict/Lenient labels also expose policy differences hidden by a single ground-truth label. The evidence supports the mechanism that trajectory-level experience improves judging, but the reported results primarily establish evaluation accuracy rather than proving that the system can prevent incidents in real deployments.
