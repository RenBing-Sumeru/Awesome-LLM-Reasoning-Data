Previous safety benchmarks mostly evaluate single-turn harmful requests, refusals, or jailbreaks, while security benchmarks often focus on one attack type. AgentAuditor is novel in placing both safety and security within a unified agent-trajectory framework and evaluating how risks emerge through sequences of actions and tool use. It also represents policy interpretation through Strict and Lenient labels instead of forcing every borderline case into one answer. Methodologically, it does not train a dedicated judge from scratch; it organizes historical cases and their reasoning as retrievable memories, emphasizing case analogy and contextual auditing.
