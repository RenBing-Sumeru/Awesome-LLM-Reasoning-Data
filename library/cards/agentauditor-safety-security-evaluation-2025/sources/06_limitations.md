ASSEBench contains only 2,293 records, which is small relative to the diversity of real agent ecosystems, and its 29 scenarios cannot cover every tool, permission structure, or attacker strategy. Although Strict and Lenient labels express policy variation, they still reflect a predefined annotation guideline rather than every organization’s risk tolerance. AgentAuditor relies on LLM-generated experiential reasoning, which may store incorrect explanations and amplify them after retrieval; similarity search may also miss rare novel attacks. Reuse therefore requires local policy redefinition, auditing of rationale faithfulness, and independent safety tests in real execution environments.
