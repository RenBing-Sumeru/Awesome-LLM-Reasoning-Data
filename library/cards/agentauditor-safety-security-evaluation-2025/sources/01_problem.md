Existing agent-safety evaluation usually relies on rule matching or general-purpose LLM judges, yet agent risks are often distributed across multiple observations, thoughts, tool calls, and environmental feedback. An individual action may appear harmless while the combined trajectory causes privacy leakage, unauthorized operations, or an attack chain. Moreover, safety policies differ in how they treat ambiguous cases. The paper therefore asks how to construct trajectory-level evaluation data covering both safety and security, and how to enable an automated judge to identify compound risks by using experience, context, and alternative judgment standards in a human-expert-like manner.
