1. **One-sentence positioning:** It evaluates agent safety and security using 2,293 expert-annotated trajectories.
2. **Data takeaway:** The benchmark covers 29 scenarios, 15 risk types, and two boundary-label standards, Strict and Lenient.
3. **Method takeaway:** Historical cases are converted into reasoning-rich experiential memories and retrieved to judge new cases.
4. **Core value:** The evaluation unit expands from single-turn text to the complete behavioral process of tool-augmented agents.
5. **Main risk:** Coverage is limited, LLM-generated experiential explanations may be unfaithful, and the approach cannot replace execution-level safeguards.
