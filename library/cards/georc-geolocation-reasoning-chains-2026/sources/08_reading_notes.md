1. **One-sentence positioning:** A benchmark using GeoGuessr expert chains to test whether VLM geolocation explanations are genuinely grounded in image evidence.
2. **Data takeaway:** It contains 500 scenes and 800 expert chains spanning hundreds of geographic visual attributes.
3. **Method takeaway:** Candidate chains are matched step by step to expert chains, and human judgments calibrate LLM/VLM judges.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Near-expert location prediction does not imply reliable explanations; open models approach a “know the answer and invent reasons” baseline.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is high-quality expert reasoning-judgment data, but its scale is limited and the references are non-exhaustive.
