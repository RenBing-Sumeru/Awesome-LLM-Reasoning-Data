GeoRC contains 500 global street-view query scenes and 800 ground-truth reasoning chains written by three highly skilled GeoGuessr experts, including a world champion. The chains cover hundreds of discriminative attributes such as license-plate shape, road markings, architecture, soil, vegetation, and language. The dataset provides not only location answers but also expert observations and inferences, supporting step-level precision, recall, F1, human ratings, and meta-evaluation of LLM/VLM judges. It therefore moves geolocation evaluation from answer accuracy to evidence-chain quality.
