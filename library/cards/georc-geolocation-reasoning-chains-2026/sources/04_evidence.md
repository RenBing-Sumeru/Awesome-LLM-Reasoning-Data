Experiments show that large proprietary models such as Gemini and GPT approach human experts in location prediction, yet their reasoning chains remain substantially worse and often pair correct answers with incorrect evidence. Open-weight VLMs perform only slightly above a hallucination baseline that knows the location but cannot see the image, demonstrating how easily plausible geographic narratives can be fabricated. Among judge configurations, a Qwen 3 text judge correlates best with human ratings. These results show that the benchmark measures capability beyond prediction accuracy, although expert chains are not exhaustive sets of all valid cues.
