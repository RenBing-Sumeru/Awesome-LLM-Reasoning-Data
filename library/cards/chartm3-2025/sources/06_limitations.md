- **No inspectable release.** No author-confirmed code, dataset, project page, schema, license, or immutable snapshot was verified. Generator assignments, environment/library versions, prompts beyond the appendix, seeds, retry/timeout policy, file/split IDs, and row-level provenance therefore cannot be replayed or audited. A similarly named third-party dataset is not evidence for this paper.
- **The count lineage is incomplete.** Table 7 retained pools exceed Table 2 final train+test totals by 5,086 single-chart Q&A, 376 multi-chart Q&A, and 1,071 single charts; multi-chart counts also use ambiguous pair/image units. No later-stage attrition or membership ledger explains the differences.
- **Execution is not visual verification.** Data, rendering, and analysis programs can run while labels overlap, values are hidden, a chart is misleading, or the question relies on non-visible source values. The paper itself reports that even 72B MLLMs miss occlusion and layout defects.
- **Rationales can use privileged information.** The appendix instructs the answer generator to fully trust analysis-code output and present it as if derived from chart pixels without mentioning the code. This makes the explanation susceptible to post-hoc rationalization and visually unrecoverable calculations.
- **Learned filters have limited calibration.** The Qwen2-VL-2B quality classifier uses 1,200 corrected examples and reports validation metrics on 107 instances. The approximately 5,800-item instruction audit uses Claude 3.5 first and dual Claude/human review for initially incorrect cases; it does not establish whole-corpus error rates or independent validation of initially accepted items.
- **Reward and evaluation are mixed.** Qwen3-32B supplies open-answer RL rewards and Qwen-Max judges benchmarks. Exact model revisions, calibration, false-positive/negative rates, disagreement handling, prompt sensitivity, and adversarial robustness are unknown. The benchmark judge allows some expression/unit variation and a 5% numerical tolerance, whereas closed-form RL rewards use strict matching.
- **RL does not validate CoT.** GRPO excludes the constructed CoT paths; any required think block receives format reward based on syntax. The 30K set's rule/model reward counts are 13,692/16,308, only approximately balanced. RL gains support final-answer optimization under this reward, not explanation correctness.
- **Split and contamination controls are unknown.** No immutable split rule, template/source-family grouping, near-duplicate check, or overlap audit is reported for source charts, recreated styles, few-shot examples, prompts, ChartQA, ReachQA, CharXiv, or other evaluation sets.
- **Human and rights audits are incomplete.** The paper reports consent, privacy, compensation, anonymization, and manual benchmark correction, but not annotator counts, qualifications, instructions, agreement, or item-level edits. It states that artifact licenses were respected but publishes no source URLs or rights manifest for collected charts, recreated styles, templates, generated images/code, or redistribution.
- **Scope is narrow.** Rendering is primarily Python-based and focuses on statistical charts; flowcharts, relational diagrams, other visualization languages, and larger model/data-scale RL are explicitly left for future work.
