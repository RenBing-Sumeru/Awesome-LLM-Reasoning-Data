The [formal Findings of EMNLP paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.701/) verifies five authors, Alibaba Cloud Computing, pages 13046--13068, DOI, the full pipeline, and appendix prompts. No author-confirmed repository, dataset, project page, schema, or corpus license was found as of 2026-07-21; a similarly named third-party Hugging Face dataset is not attributed here.

Table 7 provides intermediate retention evidence. Single-chart quality filtering retains 34,064 of 38,452 charts (88.59%), and Q&A filtering retains 140,312 of 171,531 items (81.80%). Multi-chart filtering retains 3,777 of 4,336 chart pairs (87.11%) and 9,821 of 11,331 Q&A (86.68%). These retained-pool counts do not reconcile with Table 2's final train+test totals: 135,226 single Q&A, 9,445 multi Q&A, and 32,993 single charts; the multi-chart units also differ. Without item IDs or an attrition ledger, the gap cannot be assigned to later filtering, unused records, or a reporting mismatch.

For Qwen2.5-VL-3B, Table 3 reports ChartM3/ChartM3-Multi accuracy rising from 45.00/34.83 to 62.88/51.67 after full CoT-SFT. In the controlled 30K comparison, Table 4 reports 58.17/47.17 for SFT and 52.40/40.33 for GRPO, versus 45.00/34.83 for the base; SFT is stronger in-domain, while GRPO reports advantages on some external reasoning benchmarks. A CoT prompt alone produces 43.68/34.83, so gains are not explained by merely requesting a rationale.

These experiments show that the paper-described training mixtures change model behavior. They do not verify every chart/rationale, resolve the retained-versus-final count gap, calibrate learned judges over the complete corpus, or establish that code-derived reasoning is recoverable from pixels. GRPO results are particularly separate from trace quality because the RL objective ignores the supplied CoT paths.
