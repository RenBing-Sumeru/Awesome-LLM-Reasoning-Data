The intended data object preserves a long construction lineage: scenario and key question; retrieved chart template and attributes; contextual narrative; generated data code and CSV; visualization analysis; preprocessing/rendering code; chart image; task/question type and options; executable analytical code and output; step-by-step explanation; final answer; quality decisions; and difficulty score. The paper covers 60 visualization domains, 9 major and 62 minor statistical chart types, and 18 Q&A categories across visual recognition, data extraction, calculation, and data analysis, plus Chart-to-Markdown.

Table 2 reports 132,955 single-chart and 8,845 multi-chart training questions—141,800 exactly—and 2,271 plus 600 test questions—2,871 exactly. It lists 31,772/6,650 training charts and 1,221/333 test charts for the two subsets; the abstract rounds training scale to 38K charts and 142K Q&A. Average reasoning lengths are 202.40/266.43 tokens for single-/multi-chart training items and 236.03/274.88 for test items, measured with the Qwen2.5 tokenizer.

The construction trace and training targets are not identical. CoT-SFT consumes question, image, explanation, and answer records. The 30K GRPO subset consumes questions and final answers, while the paper explicitly excludes the supplied CoT path from the RL signal. A row-level schema, file inventory, hashes, and exact mapping from the generated lineage to SFT/RL/test records remain unknown because no official corpus was verified.
