AReaL is curated for the Frontier Reports and Data Disclosure Ledger as an asynchronous RL systems disclosure, not a released reasoning dataset. Its NeurIPS experiments name DeepScaleR for math and DeepCoder for code, but do not publish prompt files, record identifiers, preprocessing, source versions or a source-mixture ledger. A rollout worker continuously generates a single-turn question-to-reasoning-response trajectory. A weight update can interrupt decoding, discard and recompute the old-weight KV cache, then continue an unfinished sequence, so a stored trajectory can contain segments produced by multiple policy versions. The reported record fields include prompt, generated token, reasoning response, final answer, extracted code, unit-test result, reward, behavior and proximal policy version, staleness and trajectory. No paper-run rollout, token-logprob, policy-span or reward-event export is verified.
