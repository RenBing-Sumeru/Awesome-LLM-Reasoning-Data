For this atlas, AReaL's distinctive contribution is the explicit systems contract for stale and cross-version reasoning rollouts. It exposes an interruptible worker, controller-side freshness policy, replay-buffer handling, CPU reward service and a decoupled PPO objective that distinguishes behavior from proximal policy. That allows audit of operational feedback and policy-version boundaries that ordinary RLVR descriptions often omit. It remains a construction-recipe and verifier/reward disclosure: runnable high-throughput infrastructure, reported source names and hyperparameters are not equivalent to a released reasoning corpus or an auditable record-to-update ledger. Evidence-backed uses are RLVR and evaluation.
