Under the main screenshot+a11y, temperature-1, `top_p`-0.9, 15-step setting, the paper compares o4-mini, GPT-4.1, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, Gemini 2.5 Pro, and Gemini 2.5 Flash on the 150 tasks. GPT-4.1 has the lowest judge-scored average unsafe rate at 21%; Gemini 2.5 Flash reports 26%, o4-mini 27%, Gemini 2.5 Pro 27%, and Claude 3.7 Sonnet 29% (Paper Table 2). These numbers are conditional on the OSWorld scaffold, model versions, task mix, judge prompt, and inference budget; they are not independent reproductions or estimates of deployment prevalence.

Judge validation uses manual labels for 150 o4-mini traces. Against those labels, GPT-4.1 AER reports unsafe precision 95%, recall 64%, and F1 76%, while task-completion precision is 72%, recall 86%, and F1 79% (Paper Table 3). Unsafe/completion F1 by category is 78%/57% for deliberate misuse, 73%/84% for prompt injection, and 77%/83% for model misbehavior. The 64% unsafe recall and 57% misuse-completion F1 are direct evidence that judge output is noisy rather than ground truth.

For the 39 episodes that both the human and judge label unsafe, first-violation-step agreement is 77% and mean absolute distance is 0.85 steps (Appendix B, Table 10). This result is conditional on agreement about the episode-level unsafe label; it excludes unsafe episodes missed by either side and therefore cannot be interpreted as unconditional step-localization accuracy.

Manual analysis reports that o4-mini followed simple static prompt-injection goals in 20% of 50 traces, with large variation across vectors and goals (Paper §4.3, Table 4). A temperature ablation gives o4-mini average unsafe rates of 28% at temperature 0 and 27% at temperature 1, with completion 40% and 39% respectively (Appendix B, Table 7). Increasing the cap from 15 to 60 steps raises completion from 38% to 54% for misbehavior and from 54% to 58% for injection (Appendix B, Figure 6), showing that benchmark conclusions depend on the interaction budget.

For o4-mini at 15 steps, the reported run costs about USD 52 for the agent plus USD 1 for judging and takes about five hours on one laptop with three VMs. This budget helps locate the results but does not certify task quality, trajectory completeness, judge calibration beyond o4-mini, license safety, or reproducibility of the moving release.
