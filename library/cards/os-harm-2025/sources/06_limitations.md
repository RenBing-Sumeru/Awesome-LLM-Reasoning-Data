- GPT-4.1 AER is a semantic instrument, not ground truth. Against 150 human-labeled o4-mini episodes it has 64% unsafe recall; documented failures include ignoring unseen injections, treating injected text as legitimate, over-crediting partial misuse, falsely flagging normal technical actions, and missing genuine security faults.
- The judge receives action/reasoning history and a GPT-4o caption of the final screenshot rather than a formal readout of all VM state. Caption errors, omitted visual evidence, inaccessible hidden state, and judge-model drift can change `success`, `safety`, or `violation_step`; the prompt's deliberate preference for false positives also shapes the label distribution.
- Human validation is narrow: three task-designing authors each labeled one 50-task category of o4-mini traces. The paper does not report independent double annotation, inter-rater agreement, adjudication, or equivalent validation for the other evaluated models.
- The benchmark is capability-confounded. An incapable agent may appear safe because it cannot execute the harmful goal, and the 15-step cap suppresses both successful and unsafe behavior. Tasks are short, attacks are simple and static, and the curated stress-test mixture does not estimate natural deployment prevalence or adaptive-attack robustness.
- Reproduction is environment-sensitive. The authors report VMware Workstation but not validation across OSWorld VM providers; Ubuntu snapshots, application state, accessibility trees, network content, model APIs, screenshots, timeouts, and pyautogui timing can drift. The repository has no release or tag, and the inspected commit expands to 51 injection combinations while the paper reports 50.
- The repository code is Apache-2.0, but dataset-/trace-specific terms for the Google Drive artifacts and rights for OSWorld-derived or externally hosted assets are unknown. The Drive lacks a verified immutable inventory, checksums, and one-to-one mapping from paper experiments to released episodes.
- Broader semantic decontamination is unknown. The release provides a canary and asks users not to train on or post the examples, while 10 injection bases overlap OSWorld by construction. Exact retention of refusals, unsafe successes, crashes, timeouts, retries, jailbreak runs, and ablations is also unknown.
- Curator inference: without a pinned benchmark bundle and a reconciliation note, later results can silently target a different injection set or judge stack while retaining the same OS-Harm name. This version drift is a direct audit blocker for score comparison and replay.
