SCPO begins with a base model and unlabeled seed queries. For GSM8K and MATH it uses Llama-3 Base 8B as the seed model; for ZebraLogic it uses Llama-3 Instruct 8B. Llama-3 Instruct 8B generates additional problems from four-shot train examples for math or by one-shot rephrasing/perturbation for ZebraLogic. Responses and new problems are sampled at temperature 0.7 and top-p 0.9.

For GSM8K and MATH, k=8 responses are first sampled. If later models become too consistent to yield a rejected answer, another eight responses are generated. Initial generated-problem and pair filtering uses tau=0.5k; for second-iteration data it rises to 0.7k on GSM8K and 0.6k on MATH. ZebraLogic uses k=16 because exact table-answer collisions are rarer, with tau=2 for M1 and tau=0.5k for M2. The main loop trains M1 then M2 using the weighted DPO-plus-NLL loss. Gold-labeled semi-supervised pairs use correctness and weight one.

The paper evaluates GSM8K, MATH, and the test-only ZebraLogic benchmark, with held-out dev splits for math model selection. It reports ten training epochs and learning rate 5e-6 with cosine scheduling; complete reproduction still requires prompts, parser/equivalence rules, random cluster-member selection, alpha/beta settings, seeds, generated questions, all rollouts, and exact checkpoints. Appendix D provides prompts, but no official code, pair files, or versioned rollout release was confirmed.
