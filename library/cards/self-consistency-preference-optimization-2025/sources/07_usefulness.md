For the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track, SCPO is a direct example of converting repeated-sampling traces into optimizer-ready preference records. A reusable schema should preserve every response, parsed answer, answer cluster, vote count, chosen/rejected member, selection randomness, vote-margin weight, threshold, generator checkpoint, iteration, query provenance, and whether the label came from consensus or gold correctness.

Such data could support preference optimization, calibration of consensus confidence, comparisons between external reward models and self-consistency, threshold studies, and audits of diversity collapse across iterations. It can also expose leakage by separating ordinary train-split construction from transductive use of test queries. These uses depend on raw trace and split provenance; because no official pair corpus is confirmed, the paper currently offers a construction recipe rather than a downloadable preference dataset.
