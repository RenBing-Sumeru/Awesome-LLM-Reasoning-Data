Self-consistency previously aggregated multiple inference-time answers, while self-training methods used gold answers, an external reward model, model-as-judge scores, or consistency-selected targets for supervised NLL. SCPO turns final-answer frequency into pairwise preferences, weights each pair by the vote margin, combines weighted DPO with chosen-response NLL, and repeats the construction over model iterations. It also uses the same consistency signal to filter self-generated problems.

The direction signal is a precise conversion from rollout data to preference data: sampled response groups become chosen/rejected records with confidence-like weights and explicit filtering thresholds. This makes sample count, answer parser, cluster definition, vote margin, and iteration lineage essential audit fields. The semi-supervised variant also cleanly separates gold-derived pairs from proxy-derived pairs.

Neither self-consistency, DPO, NLL, iterative self-training, nor problem generation is new alone. The novelty is their vote-weighted preference construction for reasoning without training answers. The method's measured gains and vote–accuracy association are useful quality signals at aggregate level, but they do not prove each preference pair is correct or the unreleased corpus is reusable.
