At iteration t, the current model samples k responses for each problem. A vote function extracts final answers and counts exact answer agreement. The most frequent answer cluster supplies the chosen response, the least frequent cluster supplies the rejected response, and the pair is retained only if the chosen vote reaches threshold tau. When several responses share an answer, one response is sampled from that cluster. Pair weight w is the chosen-minus-rejected vote margin divided by k.

Training uses a weighted objective combining DPO against the current model as reference with a weighted NLL term on the chosen response. The resulting model generates the next iteration's preference data. SCPO also prompts Llama-3 Instruct 8B to create new problems; answer-consistency filtering removes generated problems without a sufficiently supported answer. In the semi-supervised variant, gold-correct and gold-incorrect responses form pairs with weight one, while unlabeled items retain the consistency rule.

Relative to inference-only self-consistency, SCPO converts rollout counts into persistent preference examples and distills the voting distribution into a single-sample model. Relative to reward-model self-training or LMSI, it uses answer frequency rather than an external scalar judge and optimizes weighted preference plus NLL rather than NLL alone. This directly fits Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data because repeated samples, answer groups, rejected responses, and vote margins define the training object.
