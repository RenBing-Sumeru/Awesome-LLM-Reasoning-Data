The primary modeling limitation is the ORM. Its binary 1/0 decision is based on instruction, action history, and final HTML, so a visually plausible final page can hide failed side effects, unmet conditions, or state not serialized into HTML. Approximately 80% aggregate accuracy on the reported evaluation, including 100 manual rollouts, permits both false positives and false negatives. Because the optimized policy supplies part of the ORM training distribution, shared blind spots and reward gaming remain plausible curator-inferred risks.

Release completeness is substantially weaker than method disclosure. The roughly 103 MB `scripts/webarena_lite_sft.pt` file is one PyTorch-serialized SFT/experience artifact; it is not the complete record of 8 phases by 500 retained tasks. Generated instructions, raw success and failure trajectories, ORM outputs, replay selections, failure sets, environment logs, and seed manifests are absent. Successful histories enter replay while failed histories are dropped from replay, so a released accepted-only view would conceal selection bias even if it later became available.

Split and contamination evidence is partial. The named split uses 1,186 training samples and 165 evaluation tasks over the same 5 sites, but generated tasks intentionally inherit domains and variable values from failures. Without an item-level lineage and similarity analysis, template or entity overlap with evaluation cannot be audited. The paper also does not report model-pretraining decontamination.

Rights and replay create separate blockers. The repository has no root license, the model cards say `license: other`, and the external VisualAgentBench Apache-2.0 license does not cover WebRL's whole release. The `.pt` file has no verified standalone data license and is consumed through `torch.load`; as a curator-inferred security precaution, it should be inspected as untrusted serialization in isolation. Browser traces may contain usernames, account state, HTML, and action history, yet no redaction, consent, retention, or takedown policy was verified. Finally, historical site images, credentials, reset order, browser version, and maximum episode horizon are not pinned, making exact replay unverified.
