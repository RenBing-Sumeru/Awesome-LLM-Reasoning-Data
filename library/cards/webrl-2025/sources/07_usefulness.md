WebRL is most reusable today as a construction and audit reference. A builder can reconstruct the separation among 1,186 SFT seeds, the 12,200-sample ORM dataset, current-policy rollouts, failure-conditioned generation, mixed environment/ORM feedback, and successful-only replay. The disclosed 8-phase by 500-task curriculum and the 0.05-to-0.75 task filter provide concrete baselines for testing whether adaptive task difficulty improves over a fixed task bank.

The replay design is also a useful controlled experiment: compare all histories, successful-only histories, and the released 0.5-to-0.95 confidence band while preserving terminal labels and local action diagnostics. Verifier research can reproduce the ORM input restriction, then add hidden-state checks, per-site calibration, adversarial final pages, and disagreement with executable predicates. Evaluation reuse should keep the 165-task holdout separate and freeze the full browser/site/reset stack.

Training-data reuse is blocked pending verification. The roughly 103 MB `.pt` artifact lacks a standalone license and does not contain the complete curriculum lineage; loading it also requires isolated inspection because of `torch.load`. The public checkpoints can support method comparison subject to their upstream terms, but they do not license or reconstruct the missing trajectories. Until task rights, full success/failure retention, ORM labels, split overlap, and environment versions are released, the safe class is reading/audit reference and reproduction scaffold, not a drop-in SFT or online-RL corpus.
