Vision-language reward models are often evaluated on a single preference task, making it unclear whether they understand images, detect process errors, or exploit answer length and style. Existing benchmarks also lack a unified collection covering multiple process-judgment formats, preventing comparison of local-step, whole-trajectory, and cross-modal consistency abilities.

VLRMBench constructs 12,634 questions across eight vision-language reward tasks and provides fixed JSONL data with labels such as step correctness for comprehensive VLRM evaluation.
