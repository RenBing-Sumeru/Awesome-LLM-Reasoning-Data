For the **rollout/search/test-time trace data** track, this work provides a useful schema for studying search behavior as two linked objects: a compact reasoning-flow plan and its expanded token trace. Researchers can vary outline count, retrieval match, local expansion context, or final-answer filtering while measuring correctness, diversity, length, and failure transitions. A responsible reuse should log the source row, retrieved seed, budget unit, generated flow, every expansion call, final answer, judge result, model/API revision, and random seed.

For the **data construction and open-release recipes** track, the paper is valuable as a staged recipe and as a negative release-audit case. A reconstruction can compare one-shot long generation with flow-guided expansion, use an independent symbolic or executable answer checker where possible, add sampled process review, and publish accepted and rejected candidates with rights and decontamination records. The unavailable original collection means the present work is best used to design controlled replications, not treated as a drop-in 100K corpus.
