The contribution is a data-construction decomposition, not a new verifier or RL algorithm. Instead of asking a short-CoT model for one very long completion, it transfers a teacher's high-level search shape through a retrieved outline and expands that outline locally. Thought budget is represented by outline count and is treated as a controllable data field rather than only an inference setting. This separates question sourcing, teacher-flow acquisition, flow retrieval, trace writing, answer filtering, and downstream use into inspectable conceptual stages.

The boundary matters: o1 still supplies the seed behavior and hidden budget signal; GPT-4o writes and judges the new traces; GRPO, OpenRLHF, RingAttention, latex2sympy, LLaMA-Factory, Magpie, and NuminaMath are inherited components. Because the promised artifacts are not actually linked, the paper contributes a comparatively detailed recipe and prompt appendix, not a verified open 100K data release.
