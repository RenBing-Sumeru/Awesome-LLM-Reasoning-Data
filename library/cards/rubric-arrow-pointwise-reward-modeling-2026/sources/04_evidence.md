The paper compares pointwise, pairwise, and rubric baselines on reward-model benchmarks and downstream policy training. RUBRIC-ARROW achieves competitive reward accuracy and consistent gains across policy post-training settings. Probabilistic aggregation reduces ties created by Boolean sums, and ablations show that alternating updates of both generator and judge outperform training only one component. Because model scale, preference sources, and RL budgets affect outcomes, gains cannot be attributed entirely to the released SFT data.
