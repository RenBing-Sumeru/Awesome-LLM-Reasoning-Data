The paper releases RubricARROW-Judge-SFT with about 119,120 records containing instructions, rubrics, candidate outputs, and criterion-level or aggregate scores for training rubric-conditioned pointwise judges in non-verifiable domains. The algorithm alternates optimisation of a rubric generator and judge: the generator creates criteria that discriminate candidate quality, while the judge predicts satisfaction probabilities and aggregates them to reduce ties. RL uses only pairwise preferences rather than human absolute scores, converting paired feedback into interpretable single-response rewards.
