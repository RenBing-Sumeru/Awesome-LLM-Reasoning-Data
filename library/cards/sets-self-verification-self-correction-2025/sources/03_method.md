The paper evaluates six task instances across five benchmark families: Trip Planning and Meeting Planning from NATURAL PLAN, LiveBench Reasoning, MATH 500, AIME 2024–2025, and LiveCodeBench TestOutputPred. These tasks have structured answers that the evaluation can check by exact match or rules; this external checker is not the online self-verifier. NATURAL PLAN uses Langfun controlled generation for structured outputs. Sampling, Self-Verify, and Self-Correct use task-tailored zero-shot prompts provided in the appendix.

Experiments include proprietary and open models, both non-thinking and thinking. The reported default temperature is 0.7 for all three SETS operations. For non-thinking models, the search grid uses m from 1 to 50 and n from 1 to 10; for thinking models, m is 1 to 25 and n remains 1 to 10. SELF-REFINE varies correction depth, while best-of-N baselines vary sample count; SETS searches both dimensions. Final solution equivalence uses exact matching because the benchmark outputs are structured, with random tie-breaking.

Scaling curves select the best tested hyperparameter setting for each model, dataset, and compute budget. Primary compute is average output-token count; appendices also report API-call and financial-cost views, while wall-clock time is intentionally excluded. The method does not train a verifier, policy, or correction model. No official code, complete generated trajectories, API snapshots, seeds, or executable reproduction environment was confirmed in the accepted artifacts.
