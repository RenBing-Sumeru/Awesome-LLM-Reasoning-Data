Self-verification and self-correction are produced by the same model, so errors can be correlated and reinforced. The rule that treats absence of the designated incorrectness phrase as correctness is brittle to wording, formatting, language, and API-model changes. A false positive stops correction early; a false negative consumes more budget and may damage a correct answer. Exact-match voting can split semantically equivalent free-form answers, while random tie-breaking introduces variance.

The reported compute-optimal curves choose hyperparameters after evaluating a grid on each dataset and model, so their frontier is not directly available without validation feedback on a new task. Average output tokens omit input-context growth and hardware latency; proprietary model versions and service behavior can drift. The benchmark suite has structured answers and does not establish behavior on open-ended judgment tasks. No confirmed release provides all prompts as executable code, branch histories, rejected revisions, seeds, costs, or licenses. Reported accuracy improvements therefore do not establish trace quality or verifier calibration.
