SETS is directly relevant to the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track because its useful object is a bundle of branches and revisions, not one final answer. It offers a clean comparison between parallel samples, sequential corrections, self-judged stopping, and final aggregation. Such records could support research on compute allocation, self-feedback reliability, failure preservation, and conversion of evaluation-time episodes into cautiously filtered training data.

A reusable record should preserve the benchmark item and split, model/API version, Sampling prompt and output, branch identifier, round number, Self-Verify prompt and full response, parsed judgment, stop reason, Self-Correct prompt with complete history, revised solution, temperature, token counts, m and n, exact-match normalization, vote groups, tie-break seed, final answer, and external ground-truth check. The self-judgment and external evaluator must be separate fields. With no confirmed trace release, the paper should be used as a schema and recipe rather than cited as an open dataset.
