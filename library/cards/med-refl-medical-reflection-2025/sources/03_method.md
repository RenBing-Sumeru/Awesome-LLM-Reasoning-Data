The pipeline samples medical questions and expands several candidate reasoning branches. It scores intermediate steps, generates reflections on weak steps, and evaluates whether the revised continuation improves correctness and reasoning quality. Better corrected paths become chosen records and inferior or uncorrected paths become rejected records. A medical reasoning model is then optimized with these preference pairs, while answer correctness and reflection scores act as the main acceptance signals. The released repository contains data, checkpoints, and scripts for construction and training.
