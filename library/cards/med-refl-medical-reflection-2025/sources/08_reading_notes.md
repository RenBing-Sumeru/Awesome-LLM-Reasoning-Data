Med-REFL treats reflection quality as a data object rather than an unverified prompt trick. Its key sequence is branch generation, step scoring, reflection, correction evaluation, and DPO pairing. The released artifacts include preference and reflection data plus code. Reported gains reach 4.11 points on average and 4.13 points beyond strong 7B/8B baselines. The main risk is that automatic evaluators can confuse answer agreement with clinically sound reasoning, so expert auditing of intermediate content is the required reuse check.
