Med-REFL is a tree-of-thought data-construction and training framework that evaluates fine-grained medical reasoning and reflection, then converts better and worse trajectories into DPO pairs. The authors release Med-REFL-DPO and related reflection data, allowing the supervision object to include not only final answers but also the quality of corrections applied to intermediate steps. The central contribution is an automated preference pipeline for medical self-reflection rather than a new medical base model.
