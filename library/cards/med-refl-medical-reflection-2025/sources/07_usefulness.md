Researchers can reuse Med-REFL-DPO to study preference optimization for medical reasoning or use the pipeline to construct domain-specific correction pairs from another medical QA set. The reflection records are also useful for training critics or evaluating whether a model can identify the first weak step. Success should be measured with held-out medical tasks and expert review of reasoning, not accuracy alone. The method is unsuitable when questions lack a reliable answer key or when no qualified reviewer can audit generated medical claims.
