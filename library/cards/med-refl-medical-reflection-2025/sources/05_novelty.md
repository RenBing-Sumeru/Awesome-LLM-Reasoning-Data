Previous medical reasoning methods commonly use final-answer supervision, whole-trace distillation, retrieval, or undifferentiated self-reflection. Med-REFL changes the supervision granularity by scoring individual reasoning branches and the corrections applied to them, then constructing explicit chosen–rejected records. Its novelty is the coupling of fine-grained reflection evaluation with preference-data generation. Tree-of-thought search, self-reflection, and DPO are individually established techniques; the new element is their medical data contract and automated pairing rule.
