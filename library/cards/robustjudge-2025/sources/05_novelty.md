Earlier LLM-judge robustness studies generally isolate an attack, a model, or a defense, making cross-setting conclusions hard to compare. RobustJudge changes the unit of analysis to a shared audit matrix that crosses attack, defense, model, template, and deployment. The novelty is this integrated and deployment-tested evaluation surface, not the invention of its individual attacks or defenses. Its value depends on preserving exact model versions, templates, and access permissions. Thus it exposes interaction effects that isolated clean evaluations omit.
