The central experiment evaluates 15 attacks and seven defenses across 12 judge models, holding the audit protocol fixed while varying judge and prompt template. PAIR and combined attacks are reported as especially effective; re-tokenization and LLM-based detectors improve protection. Changing only the prompt template changes robustness by as much as 40%, and the Alibaba PAI deployment revealed previously undiscovered vulnerabilities. These results support configuration-sensitive auditing, not a claim that every judge or defense is secure. The paper does not establish prevalence beyond the tested settings.
