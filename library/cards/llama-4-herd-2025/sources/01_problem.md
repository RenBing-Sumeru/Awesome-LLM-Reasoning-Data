Llama 4 is relevant to a reasoning-data atlas because its official release describes several distinct objects that are easy to collapse: a multimodal pretraining mixture, specialized-data mid-training, hard-example SFT, online-RL prompts and policy outputs, DPO feedback, safety-tuning records, Behemoth distillation targets, released Scout/Maverick weights, and benchmark results. An auditable Card must keep those objects separate.

The official model cards report about 40T pretraining tokens for Scout and about 22T for Maverick. The launch report separately says the overall text-image-video mixture exceeds 30T and covers 200 pretraining languages. None of these aggregate figures is a corpus manifest. Likewise, downloadable weights and evaluation tables do not expose the examples, feedback records, reward functions, or source-level rights needed to reproduce or audit the training pipeline.
