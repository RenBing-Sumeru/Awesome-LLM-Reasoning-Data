The official [Meta release report](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-4-multimodal-intelligence/) supports the early-fusion pretraining, more-than-30T overall mixture, 200-language coverage, specialized-data mid-training, Maverick SFT-online-RL-DPO sequence, greater-than-50% easy-data removal, continuous difficulty filtering, Behemoth codistillation, and the distinct Behemoth post-training recipe. It also says Behemoth was still training and not released.

The official [Maverick model card](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-4-Maverick-17B-128E-Instruct) and [Scout model card](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-4-Scout-17B-16E-Instruct) identify the public/licensed/Meta-product source classes, about 22T and 40T model-specific token totals, August 2024 cutoff, 12 supported languages, custom Llama 4 Community License, and released model characteristics. The official [Llama 4 collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/meta-llama/llama-4) confirms base and instruction-tuned Scout/Maverick weight releases; the [meta-llama repository](https://github.com/meta-llama/llama-models) supplies inference and license materials.

These artifacts do not release the training corpus, SFT/RL/DPO records, Behemoth targets, difficulty labels, reward stack, judge implementations, or split manifests. Benchmark tables therefore evidence reported model behavior under evaluation protocols, not the quality, provenance, or reproducibility of the hidden training data.
