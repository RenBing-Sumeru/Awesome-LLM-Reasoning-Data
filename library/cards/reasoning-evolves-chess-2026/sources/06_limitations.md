The checked sources do not fully disclose raw chess-position provenance, train/evaluation split construction, overlap controls, or decontamination. They also leave unknown the global teacher-generation rollout counts and decoding temperatures, fixed VABP depth/node settings, exact task thresholds except Predict Move's top-30% rule, the complete RL reward composition, and the full rejected/pre-filter teacher pool.

The paper reports hallucinations in rejection-sampled and Guided Synthetic teacher responses, including SAN/UCI format drift. Rejection sampling can shift retained positions toward cases the teacher handles well; the authors rebalance by move count and piece type, but complete raw distributions are unavailable. Multiple different responses may share a FEN board. Dataset, code, and named external sources have different licenses, so downstream reuse needs source-level review.
