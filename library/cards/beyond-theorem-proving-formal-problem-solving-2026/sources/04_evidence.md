The paper evaluates four formal theorem-proving models and two prompting methods. Best solving rates are only 23.77% on FormalMath500, 27.47% on MiniF2F-Solving, and 0.31% on PutnamBench-Solving. When the correct answer is provided, best proof rates rise to 47.55%, 53.60%, and 1.54%, showing that discovering an answer is harder than proving a known one. RPE reaches Cohen’s κ=0.9732 with human judgments. These results motivate the task, though low scores also reflect current prover and budget limits.
