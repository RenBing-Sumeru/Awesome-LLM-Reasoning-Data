The main contributions are the Formal Problem-Solving (FPS) framework, Deductive-FPS, the RPE answer-equivalence verifier, and three datasets: FormalMath500, MiniF2F-Solving, and PutnamBench-Solving. FPS represents the unknown answer as a metavariable, with each tactic advancing both hole filling and proof goals. D-FPS separates forward answer deduction from backward proof for readability. Restricted Propositional Equivalence uses limited formal proof to compare complex answer objects and avoids exact-match errors for sets, intervals, and related representations.
