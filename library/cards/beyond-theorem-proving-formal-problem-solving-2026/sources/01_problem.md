Formal theorem proving verifies a known proposition, whereas mathematical problem solving must also discover an unknown answer. Existing methods often produce an informal answer first and prove it separately, leaving intermediate reasoning and answer extraction unverifiable. The paper formulates problem solving as a deterministic MDP and introduces FPS and D-FPS, which place answer discovery and proof of answer validity inside Lean state transitions. It also constructs three formal problem-solving benchmarks so answers, processes, and terminal decisions are machine-checkable.
