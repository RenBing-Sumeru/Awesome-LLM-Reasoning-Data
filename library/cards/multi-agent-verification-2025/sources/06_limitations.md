The method can be expensive because every candidate may be inspected by several judges, and the value of extra agents depends on errors being sufficiently uncorrelated. Aspect definitions and judge prompts are design choices that can leak task knowledge or favor a preferred answer style. Aggregation can also hide disagreement that would be useful for abstention. Reproduction should report the full verifier panel, model versions, prompts, call counts, latency, and the extent to which verifier tasks overlap with the final evaluation, rather than treating “multiple agents” as a complete specification.
