Multi-Agent Verification assigns different evaluators to different aspects of an answer and aggregates their judgments when ranking candidates. A candidate need not look uniformly strong to one general-purpose judge; it can be compared through a panel whose members focus on complementary properties.

The proposed best-of-N multi-agent verification procedure combines candidate sampling with this verifier panel. Its premise is that a larger verification budget is useful when it adds meaningful diversity, not merely more copies of an equally biased scoring function.
