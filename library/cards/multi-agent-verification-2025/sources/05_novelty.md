The new decision is to scale verification along an aspect dimension, rather than only scaling samples or selecting with one scalar reward. This makes verification composition—who checks what and how their opinions are combined—an explicit object of test-time optimization.

Earlier best-of-N pipelines often hide the verifier behind a single score. Here the verifier panel is part of the algorithm and can be varied independently of generation. The contribution is not multi-agent conversation for its own sake, but a structured way to test whether diverse judgments improve candidate selection.
