1. **Prepare sources:** Collect multi-step problems from nine science and logic subdomains, covering knowledge, computation, and rule-based inference.
2. **Generate records:** Generate step-by-step trajectories with multiple models and normalize their step boundaries.
3. **Verify and filter:** Review each trajectory and record the error location, error category, and explanatory rationale, yielding about 3.6K examples.
4. **Organize and use:** Organize evaluation by domain, subdomain, and error type; the benchmark is intended for diagnosis rather than training.
