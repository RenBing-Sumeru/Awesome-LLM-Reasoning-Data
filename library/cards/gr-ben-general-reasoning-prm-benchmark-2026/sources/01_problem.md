A PRM trained on mathematics may rely on formulas and numerical patterns and still assign high scores to scientific knowledge errors, rule-based inference failures, or invalid logical relations. Mathematics-only benchmarks hide this transfer failure and cannot reveal whether the verifier lacks knowledge, computation, or logical checking.

GR-Ben covers nine science and logic subdomains and annotates complete trajectories with earliest-error positions, error types, and rationales to compare the cross-domain diagnostic ability of PRMs and general LLMs.
