The official [Hunyuan-T1 release page](https://tencent.github.io/llm.hunyuan.T1/README_EN.html) and [Tencent GitHub repository](https://github.com/Tencent/llm.hunyuan.T1) support the TurboS-base statement, the 96.7% RL-compute allocation, four reasoning domains, ground-truth feedback, difficulty/context curriculum, replay/reset, and T1-preview-plus-reward-model preference feedback. The official [Tencent Cloud launch record](https://cloud.tencent.com/product/events/detail/6702) dates the release to March 21, 2025, while the [product history](https://cloud.tencent.com/document/product/1729/97765) records later API versions; neither is a training-data release.

The official [Hunyuan-T1 Hugging Face Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/tencent/Hunyuan-T1) is an API-backed demo with a thin application README, not a model-weight repository or a substantive training model card. The later official [Hunyuan-TurboS technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15431) documents the TurboS family and refers to Hunyuan-T1 in TurboS's adaptive-CoT construction, but it does not retroactively disclose Hunyuan-T1's own RL dataset, reward implementation, or exact base checkpoint.

The release reports benchmark scores and a more-than-50% stability improvement. These are author-reported model/system outcomes. Without released records, metrics, baselines, verifier specifications, contamination analysis, or reward audits, they are not evidence that the hidden training data are accurate, representative, legally reusable, or high quality.
