The reported reasoning-RL input pool covers mathematics, logical reasoning, science, and code, ranging from basic mathematical reasoning to complex scientific problem solving. The official materials do not name a source corpus, distinguish public, licensed, internal, or synthetic items, or provide counts and mixture weights. They also do not identify a rollout generator, output format, correctness filter, split, or replay-buffer record.

The curriculum has two stated axes: increasing data difficulty and stepwise context-length expansion. Data replay and periodic policy resetting are said to improve long-term training stability by more than 50%, but the release omits the metric, baseline, reset cadence, replay policy, and curves. It also omits the RL algorithm, objective, rollout count, temperature, batch size, optimizer, learning rate, KL or clipping settings, total tokens, and hardware.

The feedback layer must be kept stage-specific. Ground-truth feedback is named for reasoning RL, but its checker is unspecified. During preference alignment, an early T1-preview evaluator scores outputs and an unnamed reward model supplies the other component of a unified reward system. Their prompts, checkpoints, aggregation weights, thresholds, calibration, error analysis, and score logs are unknown. No training code, verifier code, dataset, checkpoint, or substantive model card was found in the checked official artifacts.
