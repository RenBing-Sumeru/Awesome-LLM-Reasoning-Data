Across five LLMs, SafeRun achieves a 100% safety score, compared with a 79.1% average for prompt engineering and 97.6% for CodeAct, while maintaining competitive instruction following. The main controlled difference is whether hard constraints are delegated to a deterministic solver rather than a larger model, supporting the decoupled design. However, the benchmark contains only 100 synthetic/curated profiles, and safety scores measure rule compliance, not real injury rates or medical efficacy.
