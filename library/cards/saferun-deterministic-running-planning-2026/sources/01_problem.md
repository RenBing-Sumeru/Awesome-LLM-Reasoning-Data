Language models can interpret runners’ goals but may produce plans that violate progression, recovery, pace, or injury constraints; occasional failure is unacceptable in safety-critical planning. SafeRun separates user-intent interpretation from hard-constraint satisfaction: an LLM maps language to structured requirements, while a deterministic solver generates or repairs the plan. The authors also build a benchmark of 100 multi-week running plans with programmatic checks for weekly load and safety rules.
