On the fixed `SorryDB_2601` evaluation, the strongest Gemini Flash agentic strategy reaches 30.3% Pass@1 over 1,000 tasks, while its self-correcting variant reaches 27.9%. A deterministic collection of Lean tactics obtains 8.4%, and Goedel-Prover V2 32B obtains 2.7% Pass@1. The union of all listed approaches solves only 35.7% of the tasks, indicating complementary capabilities and a large unsolved remainder.

These results support the claim that rankings on competition benchmarks do not transfer directly to real projects: general agents, one-shot LLMs, specialized provers, and simple tactics solve different obligations. However, the numbers depend on the January 2026 snapshot, context preparation, and attempt budgets and should not be treated as the permanent difficulty of the continuously changing database.
