Single-turn instruction sets and smaller human chat corpora are the nearest baseline. UltraChat changes the construction object to a sector-conditioned multi-turn conversation, including synthetic user turns and retained context, and scales it to nearly 1.5 million dialogues. Teacher generation and SFT are not new; the contribution is the explicit topic/task-to-conversation pipeline and its corpus-scale implementation. It makes user-turn provenance and contextual consistency first-class data-audit targets.
