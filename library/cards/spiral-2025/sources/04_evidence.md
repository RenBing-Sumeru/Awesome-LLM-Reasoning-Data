Under the Table 1 evaluation protocol, Qwen3-4B-Base rises from a 34.0 average over MATH500, AIME24, AIME25, OlympiadBench, AMC-23, Minerva Math, GPQA-Diamond, and MMLU-Pro to 44.5 after multi-game SPIRAL; the 25k multi-game SFT baseline reaches 39.7. Qwen3-8B-Base moves from 39.5 to 49.6, while DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B moves from 60.4 to 61.8. AIME24/25 and AMC23 use AVG@32; the other math benchmarks use PASS@1, and evaluation uses temperature 0.6/top-p 0.95. These numbers establish model changes under the authors' setup, not the intrinsic quality of every generated trajectory. (Paper Table 1; Appendix D.3.)

The v3 appendix adds a three-seed check with seeds 14, 42, and 100. For Qwen3-4B-Base, multi-game SPIRAL reports 44.5 ± 0.5 average versus 39.6 ± 0.4 for multi-game SFT. On higher-complexity game variants, Table 8 reports an OOD average of 36.1 for SPIRAL, 18.4 for SFT, and 11.0 for the base model. These results support repeatability across the reported seeds and transfer to related game mechanics, but not open-ended or non-zero-sum environments. (Appendix E.3–E.5, Tables 8 and 10.)

The mechanism ablation is aligned with the claimed role of RAE. Without RAE, Figure 6 reports response length falling from about 2,000 characters toward zero within roughly 100 policy iterations and general-reasoning score falling from 44% to 40%; with RAE, response length stays near 1,300–1,500 characters and the score rises from 40% to 47%. Appendix Figure 9 reports math performance dropping from 35% to 12% around step 150 and gradients approaching zero after step 200 without RAE. This is evidence that RAE affects stability, not evidence that longer text is faithful reasoning.

The transfer explanation is weaker. GPT-4.1 classifies 290 Kuhn Poker game trajectories and 46,792 mathematical solutions into three discovered patterns. Appendix F says 50 random trajectory samples per checkpoint were used for discovery, followed by large-scale classification and manual spot checks. The labels and examples are judge-mediated post-hoc analysis; no blinded human agreement or causal intervention shows that the identified text patterns caused the benchmark gains.

Artifact evidence is mixed. The official repository exposes training and evaluation code, TextArena integration, game-state dumps, and a runnable command; its checked main commit is `068b06f6afce2e45ae3eb5d8bf677e9d69c83642` and GitHub shows no tagged release. The reported online rollout batches, seeds, rewards, optimizer states, and complete checkpoint lineage are not packaged as immutable per-run artifacts.
