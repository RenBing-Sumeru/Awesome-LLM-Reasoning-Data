The released probe inputs cover fifteen benchmark views: AGNews train/test; ARC-Challenge train/dev/test; BBH full; DROP train/dev/test; GSM8K train/test; HumanEval full; IMDB train/test; and MMLU test. Each `.txt` file has 100 JSON-formatted records, usually exposing `idx`, `question`, and optional `label` or `category`. Each companion Token Overlap CSV has ten records with `text`, `first_piece`, `second_piece`, and optional `label`. The paper and repository do not supply an upstream revision, row-level source ID, sampling script, or complete filter ledger.

For WPQ, LLaMA-3-70B-Chat produces four semantically similar perturbations, with up to five generation attempts. The original is inserted among four non-null choices and a fifth `None` option is added. Perturbation uses temperature 0.9 and top-p 0.9; evaluated-model inference uses temperature 0. HumanEval perturbs docstrings, DROP perturbs passage and question, and answer choices are removed before perturbing several multiple-choice/classification datasets. Accuracy is aggregated over 100 records.

Local Order samples a target and presents four candidate records, nominally including exactly one immediate successor. Options are restricted to the same category for MMLU and BBH, and the prompt includes a dataset description, name, split, and records. It uses 100 targets and a nominal 25% chance baseline. Reproduction additionally needs the complete original-order dataset bank, but the referenced `full_datasets` directory is not released.

Token Completion Overlap splits ten records into a prefix and reference suffix, obtains a general completion and a dataset-guided completion, and compares them with the suffix. GPT-4 at temperature 0 labels exact and near matches; one exact or two near matches are stated as detection thresholds. ROUGE-L scores are rounded to two decimals, then the released code independently resamples the two ten-score lists 10,000 times without a fixed bootstrap seed and reports a one-sided empirical p-value.

Min-K% uses `k=20` and 100 examples per split, retaining the lowest-probability fifth of tokens and reporting descriptive split-level mean and standard deviation. Canonical Order concatenates question-answer records with newlines, divides 100 examples into ten shards, evaluates one canonical plus 25 shuffled orders per shard, and performs a one-sided comparison over ten differences. The released statistic uses NumPy's population standard deviation (`ddof=0`) but a Student-t CDF with `n-1` degrees of freedom.

For the oracle, questions from the chosen benchmark fractions retain their original wording, while LLaMA-2-generated chain-of-thought replaces the answers. Four consecutive examples are packed per SFT item to preserve some local order. LLaMA-2-70B-Chat is fine-tuned for three epochs at learning rate `8e-6`. Optimizer, batch size, precision, sequence length, hardware, seeds, training command, corpus, checkpoint, and hashes are undisclosed, and no matched nonbenchmark SFT control is reported.
