The prior-work baseline is nearby benchmark and evaluation-surface datasets that share the same feedback contract. The change introduced by PIQA: Reasoning about Physical Commonsense in Natural Language is the particular task object and feedback contract: goal or question, two candidate physical actions, gold label, and split metadata where released. scored by answer-level accuracy against the gold choice. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.11641; venue: https://aaai.org/ojs/index.php/AAAI/article/view/6239; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1911.11641), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
