The release does not contain the data object most readers may expect. Human annotators execute reference screenshot trajectories and paper agents generate screenshots and logs, but neither corpus nor paper-run results are public. Only tasks, 40 cross-app subtask JSONs, and code are released. Consequently, public success/failure retention, attempt selection, corrections, and deletion history cannot be audited.

Reset and replay are incomplete. Snapshots are restored per experimental cycle rather than proven before every task, emulators are reset after assigned tasks, and externally stored app state sometimes needs manual cleanup. WhatsApp/OneNote English tasks and most Chinese tasks run on physical devices without snapshot support. The repository has no downloadable AVD/snapshot, APK hashes, Android image, account state, credentials provenance, or paper-run agent/evaluator manifest. Dynamic services, ads, pop-ups, recommendations, CAPTCHA, and network state can change behavior.

The verifier has both deterministic and model-mediated failure modes. OCR can miss correct text under language, rendering, or UI drift, or pass a screen containing strings without proving the required transition. Reverse scanning may find a valid earlier screen even if later actions undo it. GPT-4o outcomes depend on model revision, prompt/action presentation, screenshot selection, context length, and hallucination. Cross-app ordered segmentation may reject a valid alternative workflow; its calibration is dominated by more than 90% true negatives.

Unexpected network, Android, and CAPTCHA errors are rerun, while expected invalid actions count against the agent. Without a public attempt ledger, this selection boundary can hide unstable episodes or change aggregate success. The paper reports 1 run per agent-task setting and names multiple-run robustness as future work; attempt counts and seeds remain unknown.

There is no train/dev/test split for post-training. The 4 files are language and task-type evaluation strata, and all 340 tasks form the evaluation surface. No semantic deduplication, GUI Odyssey overlap report, evaluated-model exposure audit, public-prompt leakage analysis, or dynamic-content contamination policy is published. Training reuse would contaminate the benchmark and is not supported by demonstrated evidence.

The repository's MIT license covers distributed repository material but does not settle rights for GUI Odyssey-derived content, third-party apps and services, live UI content, accounts, screenshots, model/agent outputs, submodules, or a future snapshot/trajectory release. No benchmark-wide PII scan, consent, redaction, retention, deletion, or takedown policy is provided.

Mobile execution is side-effecting. Tasks touch contacts, email, messaging/social posting, shopping, health, travel, and location; automation can send messages, post comments, change settings/accounts, or approach purchases. The harness executes third-party code, ADB/shell commands, local model servers, network services, and OpenAI calls with secrets, while sandboxing, egress, secret redaction, malicious APK/dependency controls, CAPTCHA handling, and purchase/post prevention are unspecified.
