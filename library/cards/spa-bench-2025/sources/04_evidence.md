The final paper evaluates 11 integrated agents: 7 GPT-4o-based workflows and 4 fine-tuned agent-as-model systems. It reports M3A as strongest in the main table, with 0.544 overall single-app success and 0.150 cross-app success. These are author-reported results under specific devices, apps, accounts, model endpoints, and rerun rules; no independent reproduction was performed.

Verifier calibration against human decisions reaches best reported single-app F1 of 0.926 for English and 0.884 for Chinese, and cross-app F1 of 0.833 and 0.857 respectively. The cross-app calibration set has more than 90% true negatives, and no row-level human labels or per-app confusion data are released. Aggregate F1 therefore does not establish reliability on rare positives, every language/app, or future UI versions.

The evidence covers more than binary success. The paper reports successful-run step ratio, termination reason, premature and overdue completion, execution time, and API cost. It also analyzes failures, parsing/input errors, step-limit exhaustion, network/Android/CAPTCHA issues, and cross-app memory/context failures. This supports evaluation and diagnostic audit of agents, not a trajectory-training claim.

Success and failure handling has an important release boundary. The framework locally writes screenshots, action/error logs, finish/exit fields, and evaluator `S`/`F`/`E` details, and can resume incomplete sessions. Unexpected infrastructure errors are rerun. Yet the audited repository contains no paper-run `results/`, screenshot trajectory corpus, evaluator-response archive, attempt ledger, or public mapping of retained, overwritten, or deleted runs.

Repository evidence confirms 340 released task rows, 40 cross-app subtask JSONs, and evaluator/framework code at commit `4c292b6df37907768618669d5bcafcbd8a23bc19`. It does not confirm publication of human screenshot demonstrations, paper experimental trajectories/results, an AVD snapshot, app/account state, or a complete replayable environment. Benchmark scores cannot substitute for those missing artifacts or establish data quality, rights clearance, or safe side-effect behavior.
