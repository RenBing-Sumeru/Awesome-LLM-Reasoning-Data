Contamination narrows the computation used on leaked inputs: representation spectra concentrate, the resulting computation becomes lower-dimensional, and the linear coupling between representations and gradients weakens after whitening. The paper links this structural singular-value decay to reduced mutual information, then counteracts the narrowing to recover consistency with the base model. The official repository provides code and refers to clean and leaked data directories, but its release page does not specify a separately downloadable dataset, its scale, split, or license. The proposal is thus a diagnostic account of a failure mode: it asks whether the internal route taken for a leaked item has narrowed, instead of inferring genuine reasoning from the final answer alone.
