1. **One-sentence position:** FormalMATH uses human-in-the-loop autoformalization to build a 5,560-task cross-domain Lean 4 benchmark and shows that current provers are far from saturation.
2. **Method takeaway:** Specialized LLM generation, compilation, multi-LLM semantic review, negation-based disproof, and expert verification jointly determine inclusion.
3. **Data takeaway:** The benchmark is about 22.8 times larger than miniF2F and spans five major mathematical domains, with Lean statements and evaluation tools released.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Automated gates retain 72.09% before human review, while the strongest prover solves only 16.46% and exhibits strong domain bias.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is useful for cross-domain formal evaluation; statement-semantic defects are the main risk, requiring corrected versions and independent audits.
