The prior-work baseline is not “no self-training.” STaR-style methods already bootstrap on self-generated solutions, Self-Rewarding uses the model as a response-level judge, ScPO derives preferences from self-consistency, step-level preference methods construct local supervision, and search methods use future simulation or backtracking. The authors’ related φ-Decoding work also uses foresight to balance inference-time exploration and exploitation. Genius reassembles these ingredients around a different data boundary: general queries enter without an external outcome label, and same-policy future likelihood supplies a value for each local branch. (Paper §1, §5, Appendix B.1)

The concrete change has three parts. First, it materializes future continuations and length-normalized log probabilities at each response decision instead of judging only the final answer. Second, the same normalized foresight distribution drives both next-beam exploration and chosen/rejected trajectory construction. Third, ACO carries the estimated advantage gap into optimization rather than treating all synthetic preferences as equally trustworthy. The resulting release-oriented object is a provenance-rich weighted trajectory pair, not an answer-only rejection-sampling record.

What is not new should remain visible. Beam search, autoregressive sampling, future rollout, DPO’s policy/reference log-ratio, preference pairs, and self-training all predate Genius. The paper does not introduce an external verifier, a new execution environment, a correctness-labeled process dataset, or a demonstrated contamination-control method. Its evidence is an integration and ablation claim: in the tested model/data setting, foresight sampling and advantage-aware weighting improve the final benchmark averages relative to the named alternatives.

The direction signal for reasoning-data research is the attempt to manufacture supervision where only prompts and a policy are available. That makes the method useful for testing how far a policy-internal proxy can substitute for labels, but it also collapses generator, search policy, and evaluator into the same error source. Before reuse, a lab should compare the foresight ranking with an independent verifier on a labeled audit subset, test score calibration across checkpoints and lengths, preserve all rejected branches, and repair or explain the released code handoff. Without those checks, scale alone could amplify a stable proxy error.
