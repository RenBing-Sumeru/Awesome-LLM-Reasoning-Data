Prior math synthesis often perturbed questions directly, which can break hidden constraints, or used expensive closed teachers for every new item. MathGenie changes the reusable object by augmenting a known solution first, back-translating its explicit constraints into a question, and packaging both executable solving traces and executable verification rationales. Back-translation, self-consistency, code execution, and SFT are not individually new; the direction signal is their staged composition plus direct iteration, scale, augmentation, and filter ablations. The verifier traces are valuable audit material precisely because they are explicit but not perfectly reliable.
