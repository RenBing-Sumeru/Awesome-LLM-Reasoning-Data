MathGenie asks how to scale mathematical question-solution data without paying GPT-4 to directly invent every problem, while keeping new questions constrained and their code-integrated solutions reliable. The primary source is an ACL 2024 main-conference long paper, and the decision boundary is its released static MathGenieData rather than verified inference as an online algorithm alone.

The relevant data object combines seed code-integrated solutions, code-integrated verification rationales, and new question-solution pairs produced by iteratively augmenting solutions before translating them back into questions. Answer consistency and model-written executable verification decide retention, while diverse 7B-70B consumers are evaluated on two in-domain and three out-of-domain math sets; the typed demonstrations fit instruction, rationale, and process-supervision data.

L4 facts: official ACL 2024 main paper, DOI 10.18653/v1/2024.acl-long.151; official non-gated Apache-2.0 `MathGenie/MathGenieData`; about 281K records in one 810MB JSONL, with multiple anonymous actual rows read; nested system/user/assistant messages and typed text/code/execution content, construction, filtering, consumers, ablations, impact, execution risks, and license lineage verified.
