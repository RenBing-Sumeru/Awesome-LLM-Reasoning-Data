General LLM judges often evaluate every response using fixed dimensions such as correctness, relevance, and fluency, even though different questions require different knowledge points, reasoning steps, and deduction conditions. A universal rubric may reward superficially complete but incorrect answers and cannot explain specific score losses. SedarEval asks whether each question can receive a self-adaptive scoring scheme, similar to examination grading, in which required steps, key conclusions, and typical errors become positive and negative criteria. It then trains and applies a specialized evaluator to improve the consistency and interpretability of open-ended Chinese response evaluation.
