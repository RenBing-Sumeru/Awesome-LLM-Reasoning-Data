1. **One-sentence positioning:** SedarEval trains a Chinese automatic evaluator using question-specific positive and negative scoring criteria.

2. **Data scale:** It contains 1,000 open-ended Chinese queries across eight knowledge and reasoning categories.

3. **Data object:** Questions, candidate responses, adaptive rubrics, item-level human scores, and overall scores.

4. **Method takeaway:** It grades like an examination by separating primary points, secondary points, and typical errors instead of applying universal quality dimensions.

5. **Main risk:** Rubric construction is expensive and may omit equivalent solutions, while the evaluator may overfit to rubric templates.
