Traditional LLM-as-a-judge methods usually apply one evaluation prompt to every question. Although systems such as Prometheus incorporate rubrics, the criteria are often provided in advance by users. SedarEval more closely resembles examination grading: each question receives dedicated scoring points and deductions that reflect its solution structure, and these criteria support Chinese evaluation data and evaluator training. Unlike judges that output only holistic scores, it can identify which conditions a response satisfies or violates. The novelty lies in question-level adaptive scoring schemes, joint modeling of positive and negative criteria, and Chinese evaluator data rather than simply using a larger judge model.
