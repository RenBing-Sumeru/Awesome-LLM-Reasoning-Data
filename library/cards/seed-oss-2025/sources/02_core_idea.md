The release contains three 36B checkpoints under Apache-2.0: `Seed-OSS-36B-Base`, `Seed-OSS-36B-Base-woSyn`, and `Seed-OSS-36B-Instruct`. The Base checkpoint includes synthetic instruction data during pretraining, while Base-woSyn excludes that class. The pair makes synthetic augmentation inspectable at the weight level.

Pretraining totals 12T text tokens from three broad source classes: publicly available Internet data, purchased data obtained through external-vendor partnerships, and data generated by ByteDance Seed's in-house teams. The knowledge cutoff is July 2024. Exact datasets, vendor identities, generator models, category weights, languages, domains, and item-level provenance are not disclosed.

The Instruct model adds a controllable reasoning interface. It writes visible reasoning inside the literal `seed:think` tag and periodically emits `seed:cot_budget_reflect` records estimating consumed and remaining budget before producing a final answer. The release says training extensively covered budgets at multiples of 512, but it does not release those records or their training objective.
