1. Position — It answers fragmented reasoning-LLM literature with a System-1-to-System-2 field map, not a training recipe or a new model claim.

2. Method handle — Read construction through five families: structure search, reward modeling, self-improvement, macro action, and reinforcement fine-tuning; its historical bridge is symbolic logic, MCTS, and RL. The paper does not disclose a systematic review protocol.

3. Artifact handle — The official GitHub repository is a maintained resource index with method and benchmark lists. No official downloadable training dataset or executable reproduction package is released with the survey.

4. Evidence anchor — Table III covers seven text benchmarks and Table IV four multimodal benchmarks; Section IV also gives six task categories. Underlying models and inference budgets vary, so use the tables for source tracing, not causal ranking.

5. Reuse decision — Best for scoping a reading or evaluation plan. Before reuse, pin the arXiv version, repository date/commit, and every cited benchmark's version, prompt, metric, and test-time budget.
