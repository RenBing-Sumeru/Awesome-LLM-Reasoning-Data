Foundation LLMs can answer routine prompts quickly, but the paper argues that their heuristic, System-1-like behavior is inadequate for multi-step mathematics, coding, planning, and multimodal reasoning. Reported reasoning systems also use incompatible search, reward, training, and evaluation settings, so fragmented literature makes construction routes and performance claims hard to compare. This can lead readers to attribute differences in models, data, or test-time budgets to a method itself, and makes reusable data or verification choices harder.

The paper addresses this as a survey rather than a new training method: it connects early System-2 technologies to reasoning LLMs, organizes construction methods, benchmark categories, and metrics, compares reported model results, and maintains a companion literature index for later audit.
