Coverage evidence — Section II explicitly organizes the survey around historical foundations, reasoning-model features, five construction-method families, benchmark categories/metrics/performance comparisons, adjacent techniques, and future directions. Section IV names six benchmark task categories and associates them with task- and method-level metrics. The companion repository mirrors this breadth with sections for o1 replication, process reward models, RL, tree search, self-improvement, reflection, efficiency, benchmarks, safety, and multimodal reasoning. This supports the card's claim that the paper is a field map, not a single-method report.

Comparative evidence — Table III places basic and reasoning LLMs side by side on seven text benchmarks, including AIME 2024, MATH-500, LiveCodeBench, Codeforces, SWE Verified, MMLU, and GPQA-Diamond; Table IV does the analogous job for MMMU, MathVista, MathVision, and OlympiadBench. The tables support locating reported results, but not causal claims that any one training recipe caused a difference: models, versions, data, and inference budgets differ.
