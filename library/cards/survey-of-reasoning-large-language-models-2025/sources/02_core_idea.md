The core contribution is a System-1-to-System-2 map of reasoning LLMs: historical foundations, model behavior, five construction-method families, benchmarks, metrics, reported performance, and research gaps are placed in one taxonomy. It changes the focus from a single route such as RL or test-time scaling to the interaction among search, reward modeling, self-improvement, macro actions, and reinforcement fine-tuning, while separating capability reports from the benchmarks used to assess them.

No official downloadable dataset is released, and the paper does not construct a unified experimental corpus. Its output is a reading framework and paths to entries, not a cleaned dataset with a fixed schema, license, and sampling protocol that can be traced and checked. The companion GitHub repository is a maintained resource index, not a training corpus or executable reproduction package.
