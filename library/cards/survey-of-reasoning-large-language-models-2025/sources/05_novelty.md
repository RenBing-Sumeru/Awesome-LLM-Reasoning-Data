Nearby surveys commonly concentrate on test-time compute or reinforced reasoning, while technical reports often explain one o1-replication route. This paper instead makes the System-1-to-System-2 transition its organizing claim: it adds early symbolic logic, MCTS, and RL as predecessors, then uses five construction families to connect them to modern reasoning LLMs.

Its distinctive change is the breadth of the comparison frame and its link between historical roots, construction, and evaluation—not a new algorithm, dataset, or controlled evaluation. This lets readers locate a claim simultaneously by its precursor, training route, and test surface, but does not remove incompatibilities among the underlying reports or make them uniform. The maintained repository extends the survey as a living index, while the paper itself remains the citable fixed snapshot.
