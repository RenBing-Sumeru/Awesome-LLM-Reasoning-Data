**Claim.** Current automated web judges do not substitute for experts. **Setup.** On the 654-pair benchmark, evaluators receive both code and screenshots; their pairwise or single-answer prediction is compared with expert preference. Human pairwise agreement is 84.56%.

**Result and boundary.** GPT-4.1 reaches 70.34% pairwise agreement, versus 60.86% for single-answer grading; pairwise evaluation improves agreement by over 8.0 points on average. This demonstrates a gap on this curated benchmark, not an absolute ranking of models or a guarantee that expert labels are objectively correct.
