LLM judges are often validated on static, well-defined outputs, although web-development quality depends on code, rendering, and interaction. Human preference labels from web arenas are noisy, and a judge that only reads an output cannot verify whether a requested feature actually works.

WebDevJudge creates a meta-evaluation benchmark with deployed paired web implementations, query-grounded rubrics, expert preferences, code, screenshots, and interactive environments. It asks whether model or agent judges agree with experts under these complex conditions.
