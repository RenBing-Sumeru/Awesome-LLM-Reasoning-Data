Pre-training data are organized by target capability rather than published as a source manifest. Web image-text pairs are filtered by similarity, image and text criteria, exact or near-image deduplication, and URL/domain rules; rare concepts are identified with a precursor VLM and underrepresented domains are upsampled. OCR construction includes more than one billion in-house samples, over 200M synthetic text-heavy images, more than 100M chart examples, and over 50M rendered tables. Grounding uses selected public datasets and automatic web-image annotation: the report identifies 48M multi-task samples plus 200M auto-annotated samples, about 170M pointing instructions, and 8M counting samples. Other categories include generated or public 3D data, video captions and temporal tasks, over 100M processed K-12 exercises, synthetic STEM diagrams, and UI-TARS-derived GUI observations, thoughts, and actions.

The three VLM stages train the adapter for 16B tokens at length 32,768, all parameters for 3T tokens at length 32,768, then all parameters for 240B tokens at length 131,072 with a more balanced mixture including video, code, and 3D. The report supplies batch and learning-rate schedules, but not an immutable mixture or record ledger.

SFT begins with about 13k crowdsourced image-prompt-response records. A collected 1.5M open-source pool is clustered with a proprietary image-text embedding model, downsampled by task, rolled out by the SFT model, judged against references by an LLM, and ranked by a reward model to yield about 30k samples. Self-instruct combinations are manually checked. The final multimodal SFT set is about 50k records, mixed with internal text-only SFT and LongCoT data for two epochs. Separate system-level instructions switch concise and LongCoT modes.

RLHF uses listwise multimodal preferences from humans and heuristic synthesis. Human annotators apply a five-level scale, choose a minimally editable response, and mark problems such as hallucination or poor helpfulness; candidate responses are diversity-filtered before annotation. Synthetic preferences rank repeated generations by correctness and format against known answers and include caption pairs designed to reduce hallucination. A generative pairwise VLM reward model evaluates both response orders to reduce positional bias. RL prompts are capability-tagged, stratified, filtered when reward variation is too small, and later downsampled when reward and KL rise too quickly.

RLVR covers more than one million image-based STEM problems, grounding and pointing, regex-verifiable visual instructions, over 20k synthetic puzzles, and visual games. STEM multiple choice is converted to open response; text-only shortcuts, multi-question or complex-reference items, and questions solved 0% or above 75% across 16 SFT samples are removed. SymPy checks final expressions. IoU or point agreement grades localization, regex or string matching grades constrained outputs and puzzles, and interactive outcomes support games.

Hybrid PPO shuffles general and verifiable prompts together. A format gate requires a reasoning block and final solution; the reward model sees only the solution, not the reasoning trace. A shared critic handles reward-model and verifier scores normalized to [0,1]. General prompts use KL 1e-5 and one rollout; verifiable prompts use zero KL and four or eight rollouts. Each episode has 4,096 rollouts, eight updates with minibatches of 512, context length 8,192, and maximum output length 16,384. Correct verifier-approved RL traces feed four rounds of rejection-sampling SFT after regex removal of repetition, overthinking, and linguistic artifacts.
