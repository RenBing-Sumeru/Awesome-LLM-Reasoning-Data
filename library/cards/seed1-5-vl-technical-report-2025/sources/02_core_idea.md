Seed1.5-VL treats multimodal capability as a staged data problem. A 532M SeedViT and an internal MoE language model with about 20B active parameters are joined through an adapter, then trained through adapter alignment, 3T-token multimodal pre-training, and a 240B-token balanced long-context stage. Capability-specific data covers image-text knowledge, OCR, grounding and counting, 3D perception, video, STEM, and GUI interaction.

Post-training alternates between curated SFT and online reinforcement learning. General prompts receive a learned VLM preference reward; verifiable prompts receive symbolic, geometric, constraint, string, or environment rewards. Only the final generated output is scored. Correct RL responses are recycled into the next LongCoT SFT round, with the report documenting four such iterations.
