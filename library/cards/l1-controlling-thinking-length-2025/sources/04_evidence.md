The COLM 2025/OpenReview record, arXiv:2503.04697, official CMU L3 project page, cmu-l3/l1 repository, and l3lab Hugging Face collection establish the citation, method description, code surface, and model artifacts. The project page also supplies the official arXiv BibTeX. These sources verify artifact availability, not completeness of the training-rollout data.

The paper reports control curves over multiple budgets, approximately 3% mean length deviation on its mathematical evaluations, and 20-40% deviation on out-of-domain tasks. It evaluates AIME 2025, MATH, AMC, Olympiad-Bench, GPQA, LSAT, and MMLU; it also reports reward ablations, SFT-only failure, sampling-temperature robustness, and a 7B extension. These experiments support the claim that the trained policy responds to token instructions under the studied setup.

They do not establish that the generated chains are logically valid, faithful, licensed as a standalone corpus, deduplicated, or free of training/evaluation overlap. Benchmark gains are model-and-selector outcomes under a coupled base model, source mixture, reward, optimizer, and context budget; they are not evidence that every rollout is high-quality training data.
