1. **Synthetic supervision:** Feedback mainly comes from a strong visual model and may inherit object hallucination and OCR weaknesses.
2. **Image scope:** Static natural images dominate; generalization to charts, documents, medical images, video, and multi-image relations is unclear.
3. **Reference dependence:** Evaluation is more stable with high-quality references, which open creative visual tasks often lack.
4. **Correlation metric:** High Pearson correlation does not expose category bias, score calibration, or catastrophic failures and needs stratified analysis.
