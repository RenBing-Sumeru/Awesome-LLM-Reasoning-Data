Perception Collection can train multimodal judges, visual reward models, and automated critique systems. Reuse should preserve the image, rubric, reference, feedback, and score and report results by visual skill rather than only aggregate correlation. The model can evaluate image QA, captions, and visual assistants offline, but OCR, chart, or safety review requires specialized detectors and human checks. Extending to video requires temporal rubrics rather than assuming transfer from static images.
