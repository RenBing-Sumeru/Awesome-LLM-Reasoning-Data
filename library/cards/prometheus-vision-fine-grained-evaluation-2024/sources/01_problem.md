Long-form VLM responses must follow instructions and remain grounded in the image. Text-only judges cannot inspect the visual input, while general VLMs are not trained on fine-grained rubrics and may confuse fluency with visual correctness. GPT-4V can judge but is costly and version-dependent. The paper asks whether large-scale visual rubric-feedback data can train an open VLM evaluator that assesses both response quality and image grounding under user-defined criteria.
