Prometheus-Vision achieves the highest Pearson correlation with human and GPT-4V scores among open models in the paper’s fine-grained visual evaluation, showing that rubric-conditioned data can turn a general VLM into a stronger evaluator. Ablations indicate that the image, reference, and specific rubric all contribute, so response text alone is insufficient. The evidence supports open visual judging but is based mainly on static images and synthetic feedback and does not establish equal reliability on video, document images, or genuine human disagreement.
