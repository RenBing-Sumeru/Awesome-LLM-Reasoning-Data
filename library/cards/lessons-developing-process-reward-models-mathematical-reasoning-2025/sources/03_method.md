The preliminary MC setup reports approximately 500K golden-answer queries. For each query, mixed Qwen2-Math-Instruct and Qwen2.5-Math-Instruct 7B/72B models generate 6--8 responses. Responses are split at double line breaks. Starting at each step, the corresponding Qwen2.5-Math-Instruct model produces eight independent continuations, and the fraction reaching the golden final answer supplies an MC signal. A hard label is positive when at least one continuation reaches that answer; the preliminary pipeline removes steps after a zero-labelled error.

For consensus filtering, Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct reviews each step as a critic. The paper retains only records where the critic and MC method agree on the error-step location. It reports about 40 percent retention for one 860K comparison pool and 1.5M survivors from one 3M MC pool, but it does not release the records, source composition, final accepted-set manifest, or rejected outputs.

The reported 7B and 72B PRMs initialize from Qwen2.5-Math-Instruct checkpoints and train a hard-label binary classifier with cross-entropy at end-of-step tokens. The released interface separates steps and obtains a reward from a positive-class probability at a dedicated separator position. The paper does not disclose the final optimizer, schedule, batch size, epochs, seeds, response temperatures, or exact checkpoint revisions.
