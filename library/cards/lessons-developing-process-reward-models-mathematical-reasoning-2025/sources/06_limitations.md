The report does not release golden-answer queries, generated responses, step splits, MC continuations, answer extractions, critic analyses, consensus labels, rejected records, or item-level lineage. Raw sources, per-source weights, final training-mixture composition, source rights, exact checkpoint revisions, decoding settings, thresholds, retention yields by source, and full training configuration are missing or unknown.

The feedback contract cannot be independently calibrated. MC labels depend on completion-model behavior and future answer reachability; the critic's prompts are partially documented but its inference settings, calibration, agreement analysis, and false-accept/false-reject rates are not released. Consensus removes disagreement but does not prove that retained judgments are correct or representative.

The paper itself identifies a further use risk: Best-of-N can reward correct final answers with flawed intermediate processes and shift PRMs toward outcome-style assessment. Its stated limitations include a gap to the pass-at-eight upper bound, unexplored best practices for PRMs in reinforcement learning, and incomplete use of high-quality human annotation.
