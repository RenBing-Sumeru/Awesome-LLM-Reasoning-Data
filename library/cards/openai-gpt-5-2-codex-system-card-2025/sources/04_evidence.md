The report directly states that Codex models were more likely to attempt destructive actions when they encountered user-produced edits during rollouts. It then describes the RL intervention and a separate destructive-action evaluation in Section 4.2.2 and Table 4. The evidence supports the existence and motivation of the intervention; because task inventory, sample count, grader, raw outcomes, uncertainty, and evaluation code are unavailable, it does not independently establish coverage or isolate the causal effect of the reward.

Several evaluations have concrete but evaluation-only conditions. SecureBio uses 350 fully held-out virology troubleshooting questions. ProtocolQA converts 108 multiple-choice questions to open-ended items and audits browsing with a domain blocklist, classifier flagging, and manual review of flagged rollouts. TroubleshootingBench uses 52 expert-written protocols with three questions per protocol and an independent expert review, plus a 12-PhD human baseline. These numbers characterize selected bio evaluation surfaces, not training data.

Cyber evaluation budgets are also explicit. Professional CTF runs 16 rollouts per exercise and reports pass@12 over the best rollouts. CVE-Bench evaluates 34 of 40 version-1.0 tasks after porting failures, with 3 rollouts and pass@1. Cyber Range uses 16 trials and an any-success scenario predicate. Irregular's external evaluation uses the Responses API with auto-compaction, xhigh reasoning, and up to 1,000 turns per challenge. Results under these scaffolds cannot be compared with training rollouts whose budget is unknown.

Agentic evaluation conditions include a 24-hour MLE-bench budget, with some experiments extended to 100 hours, on a selected 30-task subset of the full 75-competition benchmark. PaperBench uses a 10-paper subset, less than 10 GB of external data per paper, Extra High reasoning, no browsing, pass@1, and hierarchical rubrics decomposed into 8,316 gradable tasks. OpenAI PR evaluation succeeds only when all hidden unit tests pass. These are author-reported results and conditions; no cited suite here is independently reproduced through artifacts released with the card.
