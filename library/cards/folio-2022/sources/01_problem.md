FOLIO asks how to test natural-language reasoning where examples can be represented and checked with first-order logic. The primary sources are arXiv:2209.00840 and the EMNLP 2024 ACL Anthology record. It belongs here as a logical-reasoning benchmark, not as a formal-proof corpus. A sample contains natural-language premises, a hypothesis or question, a gold truth label, and associated first-order-logic annotations used to verify consistency during dataset construction. Its atlas value is the bridge between natural language reasoning labels and formalizable semantic checks.
