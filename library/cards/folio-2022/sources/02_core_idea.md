The contribution is a benchmark of 1,430 examples built from 487 premise sets, with first-order-logic annotations used to validate the reasoning labels. The mechanism writes natural-language premises and hypotheses, maps them into FOL, checks annotations with an inference engine, and evaluates models by gold-label accuracy. The feedback contract for model scoring is answer-level classification; the programmatic component audits annotation consistency. Closest comparisons are RTE/NLI datasets, ProofWriter, and formal-logic benchmarks. The direction label is formalizable natural-language reasoning.
