Prior NLI datasets often rely on natural-language labels without a formal semantic audit. FOLIO changes the construction by pairing natural-language examples with FOL annotations. The direction signal is that reasoning benchmarks can use formal tools to audit label consistency. What is not new is multiple-choice or label-based reasoning evaluation. Before reuse, inspect translation fidelity, ambiguity in natural language, label balance, and whether FOL annotations are available for the intended split.
