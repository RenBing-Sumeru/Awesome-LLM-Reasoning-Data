The paper makes CoT compression an explicit data variable. Explicit CoT emits every atomic operation; Composed CoT groups several operations into one step but still shows their calculation; Implicit CoT skips intermediate operations and values. A deterministic task generator keeps the underlying dependency chain and answer fixed, so differences can be attributed to supervision granularity rather than question difficulty or teacher quality.
