For the `data_construction_open_release_recipes` track, this paper is most useful as an inspectable blueprint plus a release-audit case. A curator can recover the high-level object, three-attempt selection loop, answer-level feedback contract, human-check dimensions, staged SFT order, and several compute settings. The empty repository simultaneously shows why a paper description and a release claim are not substitutes for a usable artifact.

The recipe is relevant when a domain has gold final answers but scarce expert-written rationales: it offers a bounded way to distill teacher paths and a concrete curriculum hypothesis. Adaptation to another high-risk domain should add process-aware validation, immutable source and split manifests, record-level provenance, temporal and jurisdiction metadata, rejected-output retention, false-accept measurement, expert adjudication, and explicit licenses. Without those controls, the method is best treated as a research recipe rather than a ready-to-reuse dataset.
