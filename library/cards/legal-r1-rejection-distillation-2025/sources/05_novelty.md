The useful novelty is the combination of three choices in one legal-data recipe: a strong reasoning teacher, a bounded answer-matched rejection loop, and progressive bilingual SFT. The paper does not merely prompt a teacher once; it makes teacher success within three attempts a selection condition and discards unresolved inputs. It then distinguishes an initial legal-judgment stage from subsequent broader legal-task training.

For an auditable reasoning-data atlas, the card is also valuable as a release-status counterexample. The paper and ACL abstract state that Legal-R1 data and model are released through the official project link, yet the verified GitHub repository is empty. This discrepancy does not negate the described method or experimental evidence, but it sharply limits reproducibility, lineage inspection, license analysis, and reuse.
