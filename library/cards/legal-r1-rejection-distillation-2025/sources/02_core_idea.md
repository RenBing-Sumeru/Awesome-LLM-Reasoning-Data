Each source problem is formalized as an instruction, a question, and a ground-truth answer. DeepSeek-R1 generates a reasoning path and response. If the response matches the gold answer, the reasoning path is retained with the original instruction, question, and gold answer; otherwise the system retries, for at most three attempts, and discards the example after all attempts fail. The paper reports 96,533 retained records.

The resulting data supervise DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B through progressive SFT. Legal judgment prediction data are used first to form an intermediate model called Mcore; the remaining Chinese and English tasks are added in the next stage. Thus the contribution couples an answer-level rejection-distillation rule with an explicit curriculum-like training order. It is not a proof-producing verifier: neither the acceptance rule nor the released evidence checks every legal proposition in the retained reasoning.
