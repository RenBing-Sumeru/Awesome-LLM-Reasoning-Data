Traditional WMT tasks focus mainly on machine translation, while general multilingual benchmarks often use translated multiple-choice questions. MIST unifies structured linguistic reasoning, localized open-ended generation, multidocument cross-lingual summarization, and judge meta-evaluation in one shared task, releasing system outputs and human evaluations. Its most important novelty is the complete evaluation chain: it evaluates generators, evaluates judges that score those generators, and uses human ratings to audit the judges. It functions as standardized public experimental infrastructure rather than a paper proposing a single model.
