Multilingual LLMs are often evaluated only on translated English benchmarks, with inconsistent tasks, languages, and evaluation pipelines, making it difficult to determine whether they truly support cross-lingual reasoning, generation, and evaluation. Machine-translation scores do not represent open-ended writing, cross-lingual summarization, or linguistic reasoning. WMT25 MIST therefore asks how to establish a shared task spanning multiple capabilities and 30 languages under a unified submission protocol, and how human annotations can test the reliability of automatic metrics and LLM-as-a-judge systems in multilingual settings.
