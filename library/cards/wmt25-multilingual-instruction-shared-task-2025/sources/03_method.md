1. **Task design:** Five capabilities—MT, linguistic reasoning, open-ended generation, cross-lingual summarization, and judging—are defined so multilingual ability is not represented by translation alone.
2. **Language and prompt construction:** Nearly 20K blind-test prompts are prepared across 30 languages, including localized open questions and cross-lingual document inputs.
3. **First-round submission:** Participating models generate translations, reasoning answers, open text, and summaries, and all raw outputs are retained.
4. **Second-round evaluation:** Participating judges score MT, open-generation, and summarization outputs that lack unique answers.
5. **Human calibration:** Per-item human evaluations and multidimensional seven-point Likert scores are collected and compared with automatic metrics and judge outputs, producing meta-evaluation data.
