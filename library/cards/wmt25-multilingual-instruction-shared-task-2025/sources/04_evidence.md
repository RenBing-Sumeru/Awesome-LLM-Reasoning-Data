The shared task evaluates a diverse set of open- and closed-weight models, preventing conclusions from depending on one system. Results show substantial ranking variation across subtasks and languages: strong translation does not guarantee strong linguistic reasoning or open-ended generation, and judge reliability changes across languages. Human annotations further reveal that automatic metrics may disagree with people on open-ended generation and cross-lingual summarization. Unified data and submission protocols improve comparability, although the number of participating systems, per-language sample sizes, and model versions limit long-term stability.
