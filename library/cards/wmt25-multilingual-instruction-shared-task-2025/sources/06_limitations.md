Coverage depth is uneven across 30 languages, and distributing nearly 20K prompts across multiple tasks may leave small language-task cells. Human ratings for open generation depend on culture, annotator composition, and Likert-scale calibration, so scores may not be directly comparable across languages. Public shared tasks are also vulnerable to future training contamination, and leaderboards age quickly as models change. The judge round evaluates existing system outputs and may not cover future error types. Reuse should maintain hidden tests, refresh prompts, and report language-specific confidence intervals.
