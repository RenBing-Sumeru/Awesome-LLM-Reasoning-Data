MIST data can produce a multilingual capability matrix covering translation, reasoning, generation, summarization, and evaluation rather than one average score. Raw model outputs and human ratings are suitable for training multilingual reward models, calibrating LLM judges, or studying cross-lingual evaluation bias. The shared-task format can also organize internal model evaluations. For general judgment-data research, the most valuable components are open-ended generation, cross-lingual summarization, and their judge/human ratings; machine translation and deterministic linguistic problems are more conventional capability benchmarks.
