The prior-work baseline is document, chart, multimodal QA, and visual evaluation benchmarks. The change introduced by RealWorldQA: Real-World Spatial Understanding Benchmark is the particular task object and feedback contract: real-world image, natural-language multiple-choice question, answer choices, and easily verifiable gold answer. scored by answer-key matching for the selected multiple-choice option. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://x.ai/news/grok-1.5v; project: https://x.ai/news/grok-1.5v; data: https://data.x.ai/realworldqa.zip), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
