The evidence to trust is the official task release, data schema, and scoring contract, not only leaderboard numbers. The local audited scale is: initial release contains over 700 images, each with a question and easily verifiable answer; release page reports RealWorldQA benchmark scores for frontier multimodal models. Instance-level evidence is a single record containing real-world image, natural-language multiple-choice question, answer choices, and easily verifiable gold answer., the model output or trajectory, and the verifier/judge result from answer-key matching for the selected multiple-choice option.

Reported aggregate scores should be interpreted only within the official setup. Evidence boundaries include Exact split, hidden/public policy, live-refresh policy, and subset version should be pinned from current official artifacts before score reuse.; License and redistribution terms require artifact-level review unless explicitly recorded in official sources.; Public benchmark records can leak into future training data; post-release score claims require versioned contamination evidence. Additional audit note: xAI release page verifies RealWorldQA name, purpose, over-700-image initial release, anonymized vehicle and other real-world images, CC BY-ND 4.0 statement, download link, and reported zero-shot benchmark numbers. If a paper uses a judge or rubric, the judge prompt/model/version is part of the evidence; if it uses programmatic checking, the exact checker and runtime are part of the evidence.
