For a response of n tokens and vocabulary size V, self-certainty averages KL(U||p) across generation positions: -1/(nV) times the sum over tokens and vocabulary entries of log(V*p). A more non-uniform next-token distribution yields a larger score. Pure Best-of-N returns the highest-scoring response. For tasks with extractable answers, the paper ranks N responses by self-certainty, gives rank r the vote (N-r+1)^p, sums votes for identical answers, and returns the largest total; p=0 is majority voting and large p approaches pure score selection. This feedback contract is deterministic and requires no external reward model, but it estimates generator confidence rather than checking semantic or executable correctness.
