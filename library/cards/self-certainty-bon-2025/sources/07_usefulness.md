For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, the work provides a concrete schema for Best-of-N selection without an external judge. A reusable record should retain prompt and benchmark revision, generator/checkpoint revision, seed, temperature/top-p and maximum length, every candidate response, full token distributions or a reproducible score digest, extraction validity and normalized answer, self-certainty score and rank, Borda p and per-candidate vote, answer totals, selected output, and independent correctness where available. Raw self-certainty, answer-frequency consensus, learned-reward scores, and executable outcomes should remain separate fields. This supports selector comparison and calibration audit without treating intrinsic confidence as a verifier or inferring reuse rights from benchmark performance.
