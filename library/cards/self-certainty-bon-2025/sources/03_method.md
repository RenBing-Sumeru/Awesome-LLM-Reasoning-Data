
The main generator is Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. For each prompt, the study samples 64 responses at temperature 0.6 and top-p 0.9, then evaluates N=4, 8, 16, 32, and 64 subsets from identical sample pools; ZeroEval runs the evaluation and main results are averaged across five repetitions. Benchmarks are LiveBench-Math, GSM8K validation, MATH test, CRUXEval-O, and LiveCodeBench. Additional trials use DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B on MATH Level 3 and Qwen-2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct on LiveCodeBench. Invalid fixed-answer extractions are masked and receive negative-infinity confidence in the released selector. For fixed answers, Borda p is tuned by N and task; the paper suggests p=0.3 for N is at most 16 and p=1.2 for N is at least 32 as heuristics, while recommending validation tuning. The author repository extends ZeroEval with self-certainty, Borda, USC, LiveCodeBench parsing, and selection scripts; it contains no candidate-output dataset.
