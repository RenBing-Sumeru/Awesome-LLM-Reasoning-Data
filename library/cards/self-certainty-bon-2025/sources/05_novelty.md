Relative to average sampled-token log probability or perplexity, self-certainty uses each full vocabulary distribution and measures its divergence from uniform, then averages that signal across the response. Relative to self-consistency, it supplies a scalar for every response, including open-ended code outputs; Borda voting combines this rank with answer frequency instead of choosing between confidence and consensus. Relative to learned reward models, the selector is training-free and model-internal. The direction signal for this atlas is the explicit conversion of rollout token distributions into answer-level scores, ranks, votes, and selected/rejected candidates. KL divergence, Best-of-N, and Borda voting are established tools; the contribution is this full-distribution confidence metric and its use as a scalable selection object.
