1. **Author professional questions:** Financial professionals create 160 long-form questions requiring business and finance knowledge and write correct references.


2. **Collect multi-model responses:** Candidate answers are generated for BFF-Bench and difficult MT-Bench questions, covering a range of model abilities and error types.


3. **Obtain expert verdicts:** Domain experts inspect questions, references, and responses and label 1,200 correctness verdicts, handling ambiguous or partially correct cases.


4. **Compare judging conditions:** Multiple judges are tested without references, with synthetic references, and with expert references, stratified by whether the judge can solve the item. Reproduction requires fixed references and expert labels.
