LLM-as-a-Judge often correlates with humans on stylistic preference, but high-stakes domains require factual correctness. When a judge cannot answer a question itself, it may be persuaded by a fluent but wrong candidate; high aggregate agreement can persist because easy questions dominate and hide failure on difficult items.

The paper constructs an expert-annotated finance and general-QA set and compares judging without references, with synthetic references, and with expert references to test whether automated labels can be reliable without human grounding.
