1. **One-sentence position:** No Free Labels shows that judges are unreliable on questions they cannot solve and that human grounding cannot be removed for free.


2. **Method takeaway:** Expert finance questions, 1,200 response verdicts, and no-reference, synthetic-reference, and human-reference controls are central.


3. **Data takeaway:** BFF-Bench has 160 questions; VERDICTS covers 1,200 responses from finance and a difficult MT-Bench subset.


4. **Evidence anchor:** Qwen2.5-7B with expert references can outperform GPT-4o with synthetic references.


5. **Reuse decision:** Best for correctness calibration of judges; high-stakes items require high-quality references or human grounding.
