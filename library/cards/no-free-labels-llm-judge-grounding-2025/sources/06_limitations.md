1. **Domain and scale:** BFF-Bench focuses on business and finance and the MT-Bench subset is limited. Legal, medical, and code judging require separate validation.


2. **Reference cost:** Expert references improve judging but are expensive and may be disputed. High-stakes tasks should retain multiple expert versions and provenance.


3. **Correctness is not all quality:** VERDICTS primarily assesses correctness and cannot alone evaluate helpfulness, risk communication, or user preference.
