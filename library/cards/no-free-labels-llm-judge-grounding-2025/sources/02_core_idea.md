The paper releases BFF-Bench and VERDICTS. BFF-Bench contains 160 challenging questions and long-form answers authored by financial professionals; experts then label the correctness of 1,200 model responses on BFF-Bench and a difficult MT-Bench subset. Records preserve questions, candidate responses, expert references, and verdicts, directly testing correctness rather than general style preference. The key finding is that judging accuracy is strongly linked to whether the judge can solve the question, and high-quality human references can outperform a stronger judge paired with synthetic references.
