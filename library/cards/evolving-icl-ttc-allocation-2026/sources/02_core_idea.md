The method couples adaptive allocation with self-adaptive in-context learning. A warm-up stage gives every question a small fixed number of zero-shot generations. A verifier marks questions solved when any response passes, removes them from the active set, and places their successful question-response pairs into a growing demonstration pool. This first stage therefore serves as both inexpensive difficulty estimation and evidence collection.

In later rounds, computation is spent only on the active set. For each unresolved query, the method retrieves up to three semantically closest questions and their successful responses, inserts them as in-context demonstrations, and samples from the resulting conditional distribution. The pool changes as more questions are solved, so a fixed base model obtains a test-time distribution shift without fine-tuning or a separate learned allocation model.
