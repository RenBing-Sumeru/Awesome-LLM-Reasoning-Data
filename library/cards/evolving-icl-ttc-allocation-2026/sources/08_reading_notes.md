Remember that this is not only an early-exit method. It redistributes sampling from solved to unresolved questions and simultaneously changes the unresolved questions’ prompts with retrieved successes. Its main metric is coverage, which is easier to optimize with a correctness oracle than final-answer accuracy in open tasks. The appealing efficiency result should be read alongside the transductive protocol: the method’s strongest resource is information created from the test workload itself. The paper is most useful as a design pattern for controlled online batches, and least transferable to isolated, hidden-label, or contamination-sensitive evaluation settings.
