The paper makes the feedback object unusually explicit for a medical model: an interactive patient simulator, dynamically generated multi-dimensional rubrics, and a staged GRPO-based training design. It also distinguishes de-identified records, synthetic scenarios, knowledge sources, and LLM/expert rubric construction at a high level.

For this atlas, the novelty is a report-level dynamic-verifier ledger, not a claim of released medical data or reusable code. Its specificity exposes exactly which missing artifact and audit details block independent verification.
