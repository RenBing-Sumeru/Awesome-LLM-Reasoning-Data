Prompts are reported from medical records, knowledge bases, and synthetic scenarios. The paper reports LLM prompt/rubric generation, internal clinical-expert selection and weights, clustering/deduplication, scoring, verifier rejection sampling, consensus for ambiguity, and filtering of incoherent dialogue fragments. Thresholds, yields, prompts, source manifests, and reviewer policy are unknown.

The simulator has Termination Gate, Affective Unit, and Fact Unit. Rubric RL scores outputs with an unspecified LLM and combines rubric reward with a conditional length reward; rule-based RL uses specialized verifiers for ground-truth tasks. The evaluator, calibration, reward weights, GRPO settings, rollouts, and terminal-trigger details are not released.
