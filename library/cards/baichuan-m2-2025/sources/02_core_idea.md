Baichuan-M2 is a 32B medical reasoning model built around a dynamic verifier system. The Patient Simulator uses de-identified medical and doctor-patient conversation records; the Clinical Rubrics Generator produces multi-dimensional criteria for diagnosis, consultation logic, treatment, communication, empathy, ethics, and safety.

The report describes medical adaptation, rejection-sampled SFT, and rule-based, rubric-based, and multi-turn enhanced-GRPO RL. It reports a 4M+ candidate pool, DeepSeek-R1 as primary CoT generator, and a final 2M-sample SFT set with about 20% medical data. These are stage-level disclosures, not released records or an executable feedback system.
