Clinical reasoning needs feedback beyond static answer keys, but a simulated clinical environment is only auditable when the records, patient behavior, rubrics, rewards, and rights are disclosed. A report can claim de-identification and dynamic verification without making privacy, provenance, or reproducibility independently checkable.

Baichuan-M2 reports a patient simulator and clinical rubric system for medical reinforcement learning. Its primary paper describes the system in useful detail, but does not link an official release of weights, code, data, traces, rubrics, or simulator artifacts.
