The one-sentence contribution is an 11.5K-question multimodal benchmark spanning 30 college-level subjects and 183 subfields. The core mechanism is to collect expert-level problems where the image is not decoration but part of the evidence needed to answer.

The evaluation surface includes multiple-choice and open-ended or short-answer style items, depending on the released task record. The feedback contract is the official answer target and evaluator rather than a free-form human preference judgment.

Closest comparisons are MMLU for text-only academic breadth, ScienceQA/MathVista for narrower multimodal reasoning surfaces, and later MMMU-Pro-style harder variants. The direction label is multimodal expert reasoning benchmark.
