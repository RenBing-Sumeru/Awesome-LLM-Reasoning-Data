The prior baseline was split between text-only broad academic benchmarks such as MMLU and multimodal benchmarks with narrower domains or less expert-level subject coverage. MMMU changes the object by joining broad college disciplines with necessary visual evidence.

The direction signal is an evaluation surface for expert multimodal reasoning, not just captioning, VQA, or diagram recognition. The quality signal is the official CVPR 2024 Oral paper plus public benchmark artifacts and a reported human/model gap.

What is not new: answer-level scoring, academic exam questions, and image-question datasets were already established. Before reuse, inspect source licensing, image rights, split policy, hidden-test handling, answer normalization, OCR/visual preprocessing, and whether each item truly requires the image.
