Existing instruction-following benchmarks mostly check formats or keywords and poorly cover priorities, exceptions, and semantic constraints in complex, multi-turn, and system prompts. They also lack interpretable training rewards, allowing models to satisfy surface form while violating intent.

AdvancedIF builds a difficult expert-rubric benchmark and introduces RIFL, combining rubric generation, a specialised verifier, and reward shaping so the same criteria support evaluation and reinforcement learning.
