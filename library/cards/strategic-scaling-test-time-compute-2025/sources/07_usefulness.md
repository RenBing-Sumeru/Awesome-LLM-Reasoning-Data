For the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track, this paper supplies a concrete schema for global allocation logs. A reusable record should join each response to its query, allocation round, generator settings, oracle type and version, raw score or answer cluster, elimination decision, token cost, and global remaining budget. Coverage and final-answer accuracy should remain separate, as should oracle-predicted success and ground-truth correctness.

Such traces could support allocator research, counterfactual replay under alternative thresholds, verifier calibration, budget-normalized comparisons, and analysis of whether compute shifts toward solvable rather than merely difficult queries. They could also expose where performance comes from: more samples, a stronger oracle, a different aggregation rule, or the bandit policy itself. Those uses require released logs or a faithful reimplementation; the present paper and accepted artifacts do not by themselves provide a ready-to-train dataset.
