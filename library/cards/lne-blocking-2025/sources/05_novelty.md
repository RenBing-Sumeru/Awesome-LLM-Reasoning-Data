TED obtains non-memorized outputs by repeated sampling and filtering, which is costly and unstable under heavy contamination. LNE-Blocking changes the decision point: it estimates memorization from one greedy run and applies an LNE-conditioned number of token-level disruptions during a second run. Using entropy or constrained decoding alone is not new; the claimed novelty is their coupling as an adaptive contamination-mitigation evaluation procedure. Before reuse, inspect the task threshold and whether low entropy identifies leakage rather than ordinary confidence.
