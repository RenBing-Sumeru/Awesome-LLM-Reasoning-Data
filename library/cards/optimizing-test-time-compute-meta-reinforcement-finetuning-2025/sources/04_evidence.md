The paper compares MRT with outcome-only GRPO, length penalties, and STaR variants on mathematical reasoning benchmarks, and analyzes episode-conditioned progress on AIME 2024 and a 40-problem Omni-MATH subset. Ablations examine progress bonuses and compute allocation. This evidence tests whether the reward design changes model behavior. It does not validate the correctness or licensing of every source solution, generated episode, or progress label, and benchmark pass rates are not data-quality measurements.
