The setup assumes training problems, oracle solution traces ending in correct answers, and a queryable reward function. Output is segmented into episodes such as attempts, verification/generation blocks, or paths separated by backtracking. Progress is the change in a meta-prover's answer probability after observing another episode. The implementation collapses episode bonuses into one progress-adjusted reward for compatibility with outcome-RL systems.

For STaR experiments, the authors sample 10,000 NuminaMath problem–solution pairs and estimate backtracking progress with 20 rollouts per prefix. RL experiments use 4,000 NuminaMath problems for DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B and 919 AIME 1989–2023 problems for DeepScaleR-1.5B-Preview. Training and evaluation use a 16K maximum output budget. These are experiment-specific construction settings, not a released universal dataset specification.
