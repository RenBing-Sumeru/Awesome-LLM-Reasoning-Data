The construction pipeline begins with task viability. From 650 sampled Mind2Web tasks, the curators remove ambiguous, outdated, invalid, or strong-CAPTCHA cases, rewrite solvable tasks where needed, and add tasks from Mind2Web-Live and new sites. The 300-task release contains 167 selected, 24 rewritten, 34 Mind2Web-Live, and 75 new tasks over 136 websites and 12 domains, including 14 niche-site tasks. Later task replacements preserve website and approximate reference-length difficulty where possible, so a task revision must be part of any run manifest.

Six agents generate live episodes: SeeAct, Agent-E, Browser Use, Claude Computer Use 3.5, Claude Computer Use 3.7, and Operator. Open agents use Playwright, Claude Computer Use uses local Chrome, and Operator uses a remote browser. SeeAct, Agent-E, and Browser Use receive at most 25 steps; Claude Computer Use 3.7 receives at most 50 with thinking disabled; Operator's budget is undisclosed. The main comparison starts at the designated website and prohibits Google Search. Viewports and screenshot capture differ by agent.

Humans label every evaluated trajectory with at least 2 annotators and a third adjudicator on disagreement. WebJudge extracts key points, scores screenshot relevance, keeps frames scoring at least 3, and predicts success from visible evidence plus factual actions. WebJudge-7B fine-tunes Qwen2.5-VL-7B on synthetic relevance targets from GPT-4o, GPT-4.1-mini, Claude 3.7, and Qwen2.5-VL-72B. Its training trajectories come from SeeAct, Browser Use, and Claude Computer Use 3.5; Agent-E, Claude Computer Use 3.7, and Operator are held out. Training uses 4 H100 80GB GPUs, bfloat16, batch 256, sequence length 4096, 5 epochs, learning rate 5e-6, cosine decay, and 10% warmup.

The repository's v2 schema serializes each step as one object, avoiding the silent action/thought/screenshot desynchronization possible in v1 parallel arrays. Even so, reproducibility requires more than schema conformance: the task revision, page state, browser and viewport, account/cookie/locale state, agent/model settings, retries, full screenshot/action package, label provenance, and evaluator revision must all be retained.
