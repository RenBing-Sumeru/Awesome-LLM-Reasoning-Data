Audio-language reasoning lacked an open corpus that aligns spoken questions, long spoken reasoning, text annotations, and objectively checkable final labels.

SoundMind converts 6,446 LogiQA 2.0-NLI logical triplets into colloquial prompts, DeepSeek-R1 reasoning targets, and paired speech generated by MegaTTS 3. The decision boundary is whether a released record contains an auditable prompt, chain of thought, entailment label, and aligned audio that can train or evaluate an audio-language model; a model checkpoint or audio-only benchmark would not meet this collection goal.

L4 facts: primary source arXiv:2506.12935 and ACL Anthology 2025.emnlp-main.27; venue EMNLP 2025 Main Conference; data object SoundMind with 6,446 samples split into 5,184 train, 656 test, and 606 validation records; evaluation surface audio-to-text, text-to-audio, and audio-to-audio entailment reasoning; collection note: full paper, public JSON/WAV files, MIT terms, official repository, and exact result tables checked on 2026-07-27.
