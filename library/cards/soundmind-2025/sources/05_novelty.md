The closest baselines are CoTA, AudioSkills, LongAudio, Big-Bench Audio, and generic audio-language alignment. SoundMind changes the reusable object by aligning a logical prompt, long teacher reasoning, final binary label, and spoken input/output for every sample, then defines a rule-reward contract that consumes those fields. It is a direction signal because another team can inspect the released JSON/WAV bundles and train the stated consumer. LogiQA source problems, teacher distillation, text-to-speech synthesis, exact-answer rewards, and REINFORCE-style optimization are inherited components rather than individually new ideas.
