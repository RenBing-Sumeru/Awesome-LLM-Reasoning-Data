AoT relies on a model-generated decomposition and contraction, so an omitted dependency or an invalid answer-equivalence claim can propagate a mistake through later states. Some tasks are naturally sequential or require information from the full history, making the Markov approximation less appropriate. The framework also replaces long prompts with several structured calls; its token-level context saving should therefore be evaluated together with actual latency, price, and error rates of decomposition, not assumed to be a universal serving-cost reduction.
