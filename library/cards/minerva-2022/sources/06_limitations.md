Correctness holds only relative to the benchmark answer or grading rule. A correct final answer does not certify that the written reasoning text is faithful, and an incorrect final answer may hide partially useful reasoning.

The work does not release a reusable training dataset, reward model, verifier, or full trace corpus. Public benchmarks carry contamination risk, OCW problem selection is paper-specific, and any reuse must separate model-report evidence from data-release evidence.
