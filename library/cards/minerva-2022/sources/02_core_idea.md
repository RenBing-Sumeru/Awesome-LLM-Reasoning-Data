The contribution is a PaLM-derived quantitative-reasoning model and evaluation report showing that additional training on scientific/mathematical text plus step-by-step prompting and sampling can improve final-answer performance on math and science benchmarks. The core mechanism is not a new verifier: Minerva generates step-by-step text and is judged mostly by benchmark-specific answer checking or multiple-choice scoring.

Closest comparisons are prior large language model math evaluations on GSM8K, MATH, and MMLU-STEM, where the older pipeline was generic pretraining plus prompting. The direction label is model-report evaluation for programmatically or rule-scored quantitative reasoning, with the feedback contract limited to final answers rather than verified derivations.
