The paper reports 82.4K instructions, 67K unique images, and 399.4K preference pairs. Human–GPT-4V agreement is 0.76 on a 200-comparison general subset and 0.71 on a 200-comparison red-team subset, close to human–human agreement of 0.76 and 0.69.

DPO-trained Silkie improves the base model by 6.9% on MME perception and 9.5% on cognition, and improves MMHal-Bench. The paper also documents GPT-4V mistakes from visual misjudgment or conflicting rationales, so agreement is not proof of universal label correctness.
