Use VLFeedback to train or audit a VLM reward model or DPO pipeline with explicit grounding and safety checks. Input is image, instruction, candidate answers, scores, and rationales; action is aspect-specific analysis or pair construction. Success is better held-out multimodal quality without hiding hallucination or safety regression.

Do not use it as human-preference ground truth, a blanket license for source images, or a substitute for high-stakes human review.
