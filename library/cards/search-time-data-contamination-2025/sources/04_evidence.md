Across HLE, SimpleQA, and GPQA, the study finds direct labeled-dataset retrieval for about 3% of questions. On the contaminated subset, blocking Hugging Face reduces accuracy by about 15% (abstract); the HLE comparison is reported as differing by up to 20%. This is an intervention on one accessible source and supports that retrieval can inflate those scores. It does not measure all web leakage or establish a universal contamination rate for other agents, dates, or search engines.
