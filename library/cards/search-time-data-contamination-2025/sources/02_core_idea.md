The contribution is a contamination definition at retrieval time: an agent is contaminated when search surfaces the evaluated question or a near duplicate together with its answer. The authors inspect search-agent trajectories for such sources, identify publicly hosted benchmark copies with labels, and intervene by blocking Hugging Face before re-running the affected subset. The paper’s public artifact is its complete experimental logs for audit; it is not a new training dataset. Its central change from training-data contamination work is to treat retrieval provenance and the agent’s search trace as part of the evaluation contract, because a held-out test item can become answer-visible only at inference time.
