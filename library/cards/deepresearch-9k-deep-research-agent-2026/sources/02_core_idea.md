The principal data object consists of multi-hop research questions, difficulty levels, search queries, web evidence, long-form reasoning, complete search trajectories, and verifiable answers. DeepResearch-9K contains 9,000 problems divided into L1, L2, and L3 difficulty levels and includes search and reasoning trajectories. A useful record preserves the input, current prefix or environment state, candidate behavior, execution or evidence, feedback label, and provenance instead of only a final outcome.
