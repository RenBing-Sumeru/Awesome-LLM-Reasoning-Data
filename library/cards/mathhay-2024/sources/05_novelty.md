Prior long-context benchmarks often test retrieval, summarization, or generic reasoning, while math benchmarks often have short self-contained prompts. MathHay changes the object by embedding mathematical reasoning questions in noisy, long, real-world document haystacks.

The direction signal is evaluation of coupled retrieval and arithmetic reasoning under length and placement stress. The quality signal is the explicit task taxonomy, Python-solution consistency filter, verified subset, and controlled haystack construction.

What is not new: LLM-generated questions, Python execution as answer derivation, and Needle-in-a-Haystack style placement are existing ingredients. Before reuse, inspect generator prompts, date-based document lineage, answer normalization, verified/unverified split, absence or presence of public artifacts, and license terms.
