Current Lean provers can solve many individual classical inequalities yet may not learn human-intuitive composition. After variable duplication, algebraic rewriting, or multi-step combination of simple facts, the theorem remains straightforward to humans but prover success drops. Ordinary benchmarks cannot separate memorized theorem patterns from recombination of known components.

Ineq-Comp generates controlled variants from elementary inequalities and includes real problems and constituent-proof conditions to diagnose compositional generalization in formal proving.
