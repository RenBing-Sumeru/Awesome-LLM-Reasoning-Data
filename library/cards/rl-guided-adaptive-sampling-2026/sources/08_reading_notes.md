Remember four points. First, the model being sampled remains fixed; learning happens in a separate four-layer controller. Second, the controller sees final-answer frequencies and entropy rather than token-level internals. Third, its action space controls both whether to continue and how many responses to add in parallel, so latency is a first-class target rather than an afterthought. Fourth, the strongest empirical claim is a better accuracy–cost frontier against ASC and ESC on math benchmarks, not a proof that majority-based adaptive sampling works for every reasoning domain.
