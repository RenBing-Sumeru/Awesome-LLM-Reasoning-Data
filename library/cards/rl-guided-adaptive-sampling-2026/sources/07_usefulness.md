Use this paper when a reasoning service already samples several complete solutions but needs a policy for deciding whether another parallel batch is worth its cost. Its state/action/reward decomposition is a practical template: expose only answer-pool statistics, make stop and batch sizes explicit actions, and report accuracy, total samples, rounds, total tokens, and sequential tokens together. The official code is the starting point for reproducing the controller, but production reuse should replace its offline candidate-pool assumption with the serving stack’s real sampling and latency measurements.
