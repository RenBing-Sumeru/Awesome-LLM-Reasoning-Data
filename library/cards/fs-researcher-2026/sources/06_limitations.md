Persistence does not guarantee correctness. The Context Builder can archive irrelevant or incorrect pages, paraphrase them inaccurately, or attach a citation that does not support the note; checklist self-review can share the generator's blind spots. The Report Writer is constrained by what the workspace contains and may still synthesize unsupported claims. Live search results, page contents, Jina extraction, model versions, and external APIs can drift, complicating replay.

The open-ended benchmarks rely on judge models and the paper's scaling analysis is correlational across allocated sessions. More rounds may add redundant evidence and reduce readability. A complete audit needs task and split manifests, immutable page snapshots, prompts/instructions, tool and model revisions, seeds, every workspace revision, rejected sources, checklist outcomes, judge inputs/outputs, and licenses for benchmarks and web content. Availability and completeness of those record-level artifacts remain unknown unless verified from the release.
