One-sentence contribution: TabMWP turns table-grounded grade-level math word problems into a benchmark with gold answers and multi-step solutions, and PromptPG learns which in-context examples to place in a GPT-3 prompt using policy-gradient feedback from answer correctness.

Core mechanism: each problem pairs a semi-structured table with a natural-language question; free-text questions expect numeric answers, while multiple-choice questions expect a text span from the options. The dataset also stores the table as an image and as a structured spreadsheet-like form, making it useful for text-only, table-structured, and multimodal table reasoning.

Feedback contract: evaluation is answer-level accuracy. For free-text numeric answers, predictions and labels are normalized to decimal numbers with two-digit precision; for multiple-choice answers, the scorer maps the generated text to the most similar option. Category rationale: TabMWP is primarily a benchmark/evaluation surface for table-grounded mathematical reasoning, with optional gold solutions as rationale-like fields. Closest comparisons are GSM8K-style text math word problems, TableQA datasets such as WikiTableQuestions/TAPEX settings, and finance/table numerical reasoning datasets such as FinQA and TAT-QA.
