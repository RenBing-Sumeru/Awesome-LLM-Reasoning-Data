Changing tool latency changes the scaling winner. In zero-latency Detective games with 50 to 120 second budgets, Qwen3-8B is reported as the best model, outperforming 14B and 32B because it can complete more interactions. Under high latency, the 32B model is consistently best across the reported games because all sizes obtain similar interaction counts and action quality dominates. The smallest 0.6B, 1.7B, and 4B models remain limited by capability, showing that speed alone is not enough.

TimelyLM-8B improves over Qwen3-8B across the Timely-Eval categories. For example, its scores on Zork1, Advent, Enchanter, and Detective are 27.5, 48.5, 29.5, and 88.1, compared with Qwen3-8B's 2.3, 36.0, 5.2, and 54.1; it also improves the four reported machine-learning task scores. The result supports the paper's narrower claim: models do not naturally adapt to time budgets, but timed supervision and RL can make an 8B agent spend time more deliberately.
