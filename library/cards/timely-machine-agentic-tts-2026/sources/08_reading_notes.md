The key comparison is across equal wall-clock budgets, not equal token counts. Read the latency-sweep figures before reading the average benchmark table: they explain why an 8B model can win when it receives fast feedback and lose when a tool is slow. The paper is especially useful for separating three questions that are often conflated: how capable a model is per action, how many actions a deadline permits, and whether the model can alter its plan after observing time feedback.
