The benchmark simulates some tool delays and normalizes budgets by measured latency, so its rankings can change with hardware, service queues, caching, and real tool failure modes. The time-utilization reward may reward spending time near a deadline even when an early answer is safer or cheaper, and its teacher-generated timed traces can encode the teachers' planning style. The study does not replace production-level scheduling, cancellation, parallel tool calls, or safety constraints, all of which can dominate an actual wall-clock objective.
