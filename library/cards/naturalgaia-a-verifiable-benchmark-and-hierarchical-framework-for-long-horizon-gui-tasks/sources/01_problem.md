GUI-agent benchmarks often trade off two goals: realistic desktop tasks are natural but difficult to score accurately and reproducibly, whereas synthetic tasks are verifiable but lack contextual dependencies and nonlinear human intent. Checking only the final page also fails to identify where an agent deviated during a long trajectory.

NaturalGAIA decomposes real human GUI intents into logical causal pathways and verifiable atomic steps, then naturalizes them back into user narratives so long-horizon tasks retain realistic expression, automatic verification, and process diagnosis.
