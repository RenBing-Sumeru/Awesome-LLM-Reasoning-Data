1. **Collect realistic intents:** Long-horizon goals are drawn from common human GUI operations while preserving cross-page dependencies, branches, and contextual references.

2. **Build causal pathways:** Goals are decomposed into ordered atomic state changes, with programmatic checks written for every step and the complete path verified as executable.

3. **Naturalize task narratives:** Structured paths are rendered as nonlinear user language so prompts do not reveal the operation order directly.

4. **Generate and audit trajectories:** A hierarchical agent performs macro planning and micro execution, storing rollback and self-correction traces that humans review at critical states.

5. **Score hierarchically:** Success is computed from atomic steps and the whole pathway rather than final screenshots alone.
