Existing GUI benchmarks often use a fixed action sequence or final state as the oracle. NaturalGAIA represents correct process as a causal pathway that permits multiple implementations and separates it from natural-language wording: language may be nonlinear while underlying steps remain programmatically verifiable. It also releases high-quality self-correcting trajectories. The novelty lies in task representation, hierarchical verifiers, and data construction rather than one GUI-control module.
