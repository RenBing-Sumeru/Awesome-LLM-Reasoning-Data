1. **Platform dependence:** GUI, application versions, and layouts can invalidate checkers and trajectories. Reproduction must freeze images, account states, and resolution.

2. **Path completeness:** A predefined causal pathway may omit another reasonable solution and create false failures; equivalent states or multiple paths should be supported.

3. **Trajectory bias:** Training trajectories mainly come from the authors' hierarchical agent and may inherit its planning style. Reuse should mix human or heterogeneous-agent traces and prevent test leakage.
