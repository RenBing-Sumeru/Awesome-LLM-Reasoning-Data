HyPER predicts which allocation of human and LM labels will train the strongest downstream reward model, then routes each comparison to the selected source. Its feedback object is a prompt, two candidate answers, and matched source-specific preferences; MultiPref provides 10,461 English comparisons from 5,323 prompts, with human and GPT-4 judgments, overall/aspect judgments, and reasons. The resulting contract preserves who supplied the selected ordering, making the release useful for reward-model routing and audit rather than presenting either source as a universal preference ground truth.
