A reward-model team with a limited human-label budget can reuse HyPER when it has a seed pool judged by both people and an LM. Use the released tag features and fixed reward-model recipe to train a PPM, route the target pool, then compare the resulting model with all-human, all-LM, and budget-matched random mixtures on a held-out reward benchmark. The output is a budgeted labeling plan and a routed training set. Do not reuse the allocation unchanged across a new language, task, or judge without calibration.
