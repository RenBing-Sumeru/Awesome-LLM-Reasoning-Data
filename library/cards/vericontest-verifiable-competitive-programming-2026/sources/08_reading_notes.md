1. **One-sentence position:** VeriContest pairs 946 contest problems with Rust/Verus specifications, implementations, proofs, and positive and negative tests for end-to-end vericoding.

2. **Method takeaway:** Expert seeds, agent expansion, online judges, the Verus kernel, and Post2Exe counterexample checks jointly determine quality.

3. **Data takeaway:** It contains 690 LeetCode and 256 Codeforces problems and roughly 50 GB of specifications, code, proofs, judge information, and tests.

4. **Evidence anchor:** GPT-5.5 reaches 92.18% for code, 13.95% for proof, and only 5.29% end to end, making proof generation the main bottleneck.

5. **Reuse decision:** It suits verified-code evaluation and hierarchical RLVR. Problem exposure and weak specifications require joint judge, proof, and counterexample checks.
