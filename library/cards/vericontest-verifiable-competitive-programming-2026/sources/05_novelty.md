FVAPPS-style work translates programming problems into Lean theorems, often weakening original runtime and contest I/O constraints, while Verus datasets frequently use small verification examples. VeriContest preserves online judges as practical functional and complexity constraints, adds Verus specifications and kernel proofs, and actively audits weak specifications with positive and negative tests. The novelty is not the first use of Verus or contest problems, but the combination of three verification layers: judges check programs, Verus checks proofs, and Post2Exe with counterexample tests checks specification adequacy, reducing the risk of proving an incorrect or weak statement.
