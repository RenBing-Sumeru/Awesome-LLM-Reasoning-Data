Code-generation benchmarks usually use finite tests, allowing boundary errors to survive weak suites, while formal-verification benchmarks often contain small hand-written functions without competitive-programming algorithms, I/O protocols, or performance constraints. Program correctness, specification quality, and machine proof are typically evaluated separately, obscuring bottlenecks in end-to-end vericoding.

VeriContest converts 946 LeetCode and Codeforces problems into Rust/Verus tasks. Each provides a natural-language statement, formal specification, judge-accepted implementation, Verus proof, and extensive positive and negative tests. Construction combines expert seed tasks, coding-agent expansion, online-judge validation, Verus kernel checking, and counterexample-oriented specification testing, enabling separate evaluation of specification, code, proof, and complete verified generation.
