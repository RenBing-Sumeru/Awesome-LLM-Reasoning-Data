Initialization uses two separate behavior-cloning pipelines. For the sneaky generator, correct/incorrect step pairs sharing the same PRM800K problem and prefix are shown to `gpt-4-turbo-2024-04-09`. The teacher selects one of five predefined error types and explains a transformation from the correct step to the reference incorrect step. The authors report 13K generator SFT examples.

For the critic, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B first drafts long critiques of PRM800K steps. `gpt-4o-2024-08-06` rewrites these into concise standardized critiques with a final Answer-tagged Correct or Incorrect decision. PRM800K human labels filter the generations. The paper reports 21.8K critic SFT examples: 9.4K correct-step and 12.4K incorrect-step cases. Both roles are SFT-trained for three epochs with batch size 64, learning rate 5e-6, and maximum sequence length 4,096.

Self-play uses PRM800K training problems and initial solutions from Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, and Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct. Four solutions are pregenerated to discard problems with zero solver success. Problems with 1/4 or 2/4 success are “medium,” those with 3/4 or 4/4 are “easy,” and the final mix is about 90% medium and 10% easy. Medium cases receive 16 solver completions; easy cases retain the four pregenerated ones. A correct step is sampled from each successful solution, transformed, and filtered by the 75%-versus-0% continuation rule.

Round 1 collects 6.4K critic examples balanced between positive and negative rewards. Its 6K generator examples are divided equally among failed solver attacks, successful attacks caught by the critic, and successful attacks that fool the critic. Round 2 uses Sneaky-1 versus Critic-0 and collects 6.8K critic examples and another 6K generator examples with the same balance. The two rounds are merged for offline RL from the round-0 policies.

The RL objective uses an offline-policy importance ratio and an advantage formed from reward minus the mean reward and a KL penalty. Appendix C.3 and the released script specify batch size 64, learning rate 2e-6, KL coefficient 0.1, and SFT-loss coefficient 0.15; the script runs three epochs. Temperature, hardware, full seeds, and release-file JSON keys are not disclosed in the audited sources.
