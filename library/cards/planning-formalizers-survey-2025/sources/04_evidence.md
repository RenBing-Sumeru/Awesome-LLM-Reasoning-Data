The paper's evidence is an in-depth, systematic review of the current research and its stated focus on methodologies, critical challenges, and future directions. The official abstract grounds the discussion in the practical objective of supporting reliable off-the-shelf planners. It does not claim a newly measured performance gain, so readers should use it to compare designs and then inspect primary experiments for quantitative evidence.

Its evidence supports a map of methods and failure points, not a shared performance ranking. A reported plan outcome may reflect both the quality of formalization and the planner selected after it. When a claim matters, inspect the original evaluation to learn whether it checks the formal model itself, the resulting plan, or only a final task outcome.
