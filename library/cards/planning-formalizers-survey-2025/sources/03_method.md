As a survey, the paper synthesizes existing work instead of training one model. Its conceptual pipeline starts from a planning specification, uses a language model for formalization or refinement, and then relies on an automated planner as the downstream system. The official abstract does not report a shared dataset, codebase, reward model, prompt design, or sampling budget, so those must be recovered from individual cited papers.

For each primary system, record the natural-language input, target formal representation, model intervention, planner, and success criterion. This minimal pipeline makes the survey's comparison operational. It also prevents a result from being interpreted as a general planning improvement when it may instead rely on a particular formal language, planner capability, or problem specification.
