The paper evaluates 11 frontier model and agent systems, all averaging below 0.15 times the expert Speedup Ratio, meaning they recover only a small fraction of expert gains. Error analysis shows agents often select different, shallower functions than gold patches or introduce regressions while optimizing. The evolving project leaderboard later reports stronger systems, for example around 0.225 times, but still far below the 1.0 expert baseline; paper results and current leaderboard values must be separated. The 498 real workloads support external validity, but harmonic-mean aggregation, timeout and failure handling affect ranks, and expert pull requests are not guaranteed global optima.
