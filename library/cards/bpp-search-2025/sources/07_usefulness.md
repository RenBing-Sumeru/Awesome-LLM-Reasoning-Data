For the assigned rollout/search/test-time-trace track, BPP-Search provides a concrete decomposition of the search feedback contract: parser and Gurobi checks help construct solvable instances; objective-value consistency labels paths; a learned PRM ranks intermediate nodes; and a learned pairwise model selects the final candidate. Its width and full-tree call counts make inference budget a visible design variable.

The public StructuredOR rows may be studied as final structured mathematical-modeling examples, subject to the unknown dataset license and unresolved lineage. The paper’s described PRM and pairwise recipes are relevant to process supervision, reward modeling, preference learning, evaluation, and test-time search, but the underlying selector corpora and checkpoints are unavailable. Consequently, the release should not be registered or used as if it contained negative rollouts, raw tree supervision, or replayable verifier traces.

As an audit case, the card identifies the minimum additions needed for a reusable search artifact: stable record and family IDs; all nodes and edges; selected, pruned, and rejected branches; checker and solver outputs; PRM and pairwise scores; terminal reasons; prompts and model revisions; and per-example policy, verifier, token, latency, and stopping budgets. Those missing fields are central data requirements for this track, not optional implementation detail.
