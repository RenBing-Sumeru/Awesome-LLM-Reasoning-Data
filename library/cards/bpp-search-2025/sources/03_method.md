StructuredOR starts from abstract operations-research models. The paper reports 77 examples from the prior framework and 47 examples created by changing parameter distributions. GPT-4o generates set and parameter distributions, problem descriptions, and three semantically equivalent rephrasings. Simulation instantiates concrete values; a parser and modeling tool construct a concrete linear program; Gurobi checks solvability and correctness; string insertion produces numerical questions. Manual review, GPT scoring, and rule-based evaluation filter rephrasings. Exact prompts, the GPT-4o snapshot, temperatures, parser and solver versions, decision thresholds, and stage-wise rejection counts are undisclosed.

For search, the original Q -> S -> P -> V -> O -> C modeling path is combined into Q -> SP -> V -> OC. Each node has at most three children in the described substrate. GPT-4o is the fixed policy model in the main BPP-Search comparisons. A binary Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B PRM uses the sigmoid of its “correct” logit as a continuous node score; beam search retains the highest-scoring intermediate candidates. Completed candidates are compared in both directions by a second binary classifier, their comparison scores are averaged, and the highest-scoring candidate is returned.

The selector-training recipe is described but its records are not released. PRM data come from GPT, Llama, and Qwen-family CoT or ToT outputs over StructuredOR, NL4OPT, and MAMO-ComplexLP training problems. Objective-value consistency labels paths; correct paths receive cumulative intermediate segmentation, an identified wrong step propagates incorrectness to later steps, and manual perturbations add positive and negative examples. Pairwise data are formed from objective-consistent and objective-inconsistent final paths, placed in both orders and labeled by the position of the correct path. The paper does not disclose corpus sizes, splits, optimizer settings, seeds, checkpoints, or calibration.

Search width is an explicit experimental setting. A complete height-4 tree with branching factor 3 requires 39 policy-model calls, and branching factor 4 would require 84. Reported width-2 and width-3 BPP-Search settings use 15 and 21 reasoning steps. These are aggregate configurations, not record-level logs; per-example calls, tokens, latency, cost, retries, and stopping decisions remain unknown.
