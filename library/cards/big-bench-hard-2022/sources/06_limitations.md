BBH is a selected public subset, so it is useful for stress testing but weak as a long-term leaderboard. Correctness is tied to target-answer scoring, not to verification of the reasoning chain. CoT prompts can improve answer extraction or surface memorized patterns without proving faithful step-by-step reasoning. Aggregate BBH scores mix tasks with different output formats and error modes. Reuse should not treat the subset as representative of all hard reasoning or of current frontier models unless contamination and prompt policy are audited.
