The headline evidence is the 23-task hard subset and the comparison of direct prompting versus chain-of-thought prompting on these released tasks. The paper reports that chain-of-thought substantially improves performance on many selected tasks, while some tasks remain difficult. Row-level evidence is still the final answer matching the target after extraction/normalization; the generated reasoning trace is supporting text, not a checked certificate. Evidence is bounded by the selected BIG-bench version, prompt exemplars, final-answer extraction, model family, and the public nature of the task files.
