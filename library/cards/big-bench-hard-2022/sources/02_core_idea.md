The contribution is a curated 23-task subset of BIG-bench tasks where earlier language-model evaluations did not outperform the average human rater, paired with few-shot and chain-of-thought prompting baselines. The core mechanism is selection by difficulty from BIG-bench, then standardized prompt templates and answer scoring on released JSON examples. The feedback contract remains answer-level scoring against task targets. Closest comparisons are the full BIG-bench suite, MMLU-style static tests, and later CoT-sensitive reasoning benchmarks.
