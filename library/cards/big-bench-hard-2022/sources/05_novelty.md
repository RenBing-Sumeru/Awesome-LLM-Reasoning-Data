The prior baseline is the full, heterogeneous BIG-bench suite, where hard reasoning signals can be diluted by easier or unrelated tasks. BBH changes the object by selecting tasks where prior models were below average human performance and by packaging prompts for CoT evaluation. The direction signal is a compact stress set for reasoning-style prompts. What is not new: the underlying tasks mostly come from BIG-bench, and answer-level scoring is inherited. Before reuse, inspect task lineage, prompt templates, answer extraction, license, version, and whether BBH has become training contamination.
