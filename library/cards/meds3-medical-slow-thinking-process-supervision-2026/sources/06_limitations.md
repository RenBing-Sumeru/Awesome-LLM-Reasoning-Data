A step-value estimate expresses the current policy's solvability rather than clinical truth; it applies only to rule-verifiable QA and must not be used directly for diagnosis or treatment. These issues directly affect training: false positives can turn incorrect steps into positive examples, while false negatives can discard difficult but valuable processes.

When reusing the data, audit it in strata by task type, error location, and data source, and retain rejected, failed, or unverifiable samples rather than publishing successful records alone.
