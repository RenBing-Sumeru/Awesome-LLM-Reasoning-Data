LeMAJ operationalizes legal review as claim accounting. It splits an answer into Legal Data Points (LDPs), labels each as correct, incorrect, irrelevant, or missing against the contract, and derives correctness plus relevance F1. This makes omissions visible rather than rewarding fluent whole-answer prose. On a 959-QA proprietary contract set and a 170-answer LegalBench subset, it is compared with reference metrics and reference-free DeepEval. The released artifact is LDP annotations for the evaluated LegalBench subset, not a general legal-training corpus.
