UltraFeedback-CLAIR-32K can train an instruction-following policy or serve as a controlled data-quality baseline against ordinary UltraFeedback pairs. The CLAIR procedure can generate preference pairs when a curator has one response and wants a minimally improved counterpart. APO is useful for experiments separating increases in chosen likelihood from decreases in rejected likelihood. The approach is less appropriate when good and bad solutions require fundamentally different strategies that cannot be expressed through local revision.
