CLAIR depends on the reviser and evaluator correctly identifying the response weakness; systematic preferences of those models can be amplified. Minimal revisions may reduce diversity and favor tasks where quality can be repaired locally. APO requires choosing an objective variant based on assumptions about the policy and data, and a wrong assumption can move likelihoods in an undesirable direction. Reuse should inspect edit types, verify that revisions preserve task intent, and compare multiple judges and APO configurations rather than treating one recipe as universal.
