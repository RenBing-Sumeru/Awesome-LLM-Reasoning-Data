The paper separates two alignment problems: ambiguous pair differences and ambiguous loss behavior. CLAIR creates 32K minimally revised pairs; APO controls how chosen and rejected likelihoods move. Under matched scale and base model, CLAIR plus APO improves MixEval-Hard by 7.65% and closes 45% of the GPT-4-Turbo gap. Its largest risk is dependence on revision and judging models.
