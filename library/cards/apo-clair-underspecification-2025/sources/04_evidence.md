The paper controls base model, data scale, and evaluation while separately varying pair construction and optimization objective. CLAIR pairs produce the strongest result among the compared datasets, and APO consistently improves over less controllable objectives. The best model, trained on 32K CLAIR preferences with APO, improves Llama-3-8B-Instruct by 7.65% on MixEval-Hard and closes 45% of the gap to GPT-4-Turbo. These controlled comparisons support both mechanisms, although MixEval-Hard remains an automatic proxy for broader human preference.
