The official report supplies the central disclosure evidence: Table 1 lists 4.1T trained tokens; Table 2 lists 1.5T, 2T, and 0.6T across visual, multimodal, and long-context pre-training; and §2.3.1 states that SFT contains approximately two million entries. Sections 2.2 and 2.3 identify construction and selection interfaces, including internal scoring, synthetic generation, reward-model filtering, ground-truth matching, and DPO.

The report also evaluates the 72B model on multimodal, document, video, and GUI-agent benchmarks. These results support the claim that the authors tested the released system across those surfaces. They do not validate the provenance, licensing, calibration, rejection policy, or reproducibility of the underlying training objects.
