Computer-use task and trajectory data usually retain only final success or a completed answer, so they cannot reveal which step in planning, retrieval, tool selection, environment execution, or evidence synthesis creates value or introduces an error.

Rather than merely adding complete successful trajectories, this work records persona constraints, executable subtasks, subtask trajectories, composed long-horizon tasks, difficulty levels, and their computer-use trajectories, allowing each local behavior to be accepted, rejected, or corrected for an explicit reason.
