1. **Benchmark dependence:** The optimal anchor position may depend on Arena-Hard prompts, model pool, and human ranking. Code, medical, or multimodal evaluation requires re-estimation.


2. **Human-ranking noise:** An aggregate human leaderboard is not uncontested item-level truth. Confidence intervals and preference subgroups should accompany one correlation coefficient.


3. **Remaining cost:** Auditing many anchors with five judges is expensive. Practical deployments can first run small-scale power analysis instead of reproducing every judgment.
