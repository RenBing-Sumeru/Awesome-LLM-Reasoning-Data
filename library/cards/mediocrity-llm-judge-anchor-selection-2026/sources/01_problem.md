To avoid the quadratic cost of all-pairs comparisons, evaluations such as Arena-Hard and AlpacaEval compare every candidate with one fixed anchor. When the anchor is too strong or too weak, most outcomes become one-sided and reveal little about similarly capable models; ranking changes may be attributed to the judge when the real cause is reference selection.

The paper systematically varies anchors and judges on the same response set, measures agreement with human rankings, and releases large-scale judgment records for studying anchor informativeness, confidence, and required sample size.
