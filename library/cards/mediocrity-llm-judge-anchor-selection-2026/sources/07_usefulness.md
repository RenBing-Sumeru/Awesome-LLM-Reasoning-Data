1. **Choose evaluation anchors:** Pilot anchors at several ability levels and select a medium model whose win rates are not saturated and whose ranking agrees with human evaluation.


2. **Calibrate judges:** Use the released judgments to study position, confidence, and judge variation while reporting anchor and judge effects separately.


3. **Plan benchmark size:** Use power analysis to estimate the comparisons needed to distinguish target models rather than drawing leaderboard conclusions from small win-rate gaps.
