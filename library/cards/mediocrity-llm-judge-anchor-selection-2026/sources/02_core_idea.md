The study tests 22 different anchors on Arena-Hard-v2.0 and constructs roughly nine hundred thousand pairwise judgments containing candidate and anchor responses plus verdict and confidence information from five judges. The key finding is that medium-performing anchors are most informative: the strongest and weakest models almost always win or lose and therefore reveal little about relative rankings. The data support judge calibration, anchor selection, power analysis, and ranking-robustness research; the object is evaluation design rather than a new generator.
