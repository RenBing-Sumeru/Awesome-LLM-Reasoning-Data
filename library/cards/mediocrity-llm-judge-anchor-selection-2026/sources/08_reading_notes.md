1. **One-sentence position:** The paper shows that an LLM-judge anchor is not neutral and that medium models are usually more reliable than extreme anchors.


2. **Method takeaway:** Fix prompts and candidates, rotate 22 anchors and five judges, and compare resulting rankings with humans and statistical power.


3. **Data takeaway:** Roughly nine hundred thousand pairwise judgments preserve candidates, anchors, judge verdicts, and confidence information.


4. **Evidence anchor:** Anchor effects rival judge choice, extreme anchors saturate outcomes, and common benchmark sizes lack power for close models.


5. **Reuse decision:** Best for leaderboard design and judge calibration; rerun anchor pilots and power analysis in every new domain.
