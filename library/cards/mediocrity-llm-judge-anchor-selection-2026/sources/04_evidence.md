With the same Arena-Hard-v2.0 prompts and candidate models, changes caused by anchor selection are comparable in magnitude to changes caused by switching judges. Comparisons against the strongest and weakest anchors are highly saturated, while medium anchors align better with human rankings. Power analysis also shows that common benchmark sizes are insufficient for reliably distinguishing close models. Because only the anchor is controlled, the evidence supports the attribution, although it mainly covers single-anchor pairwise evaluation and this model pool.
