Use Omni-MATH as a hard-math evaluation recipe and as a schema example for answer-level mathematical benchmarks. Preserve problem text, reference answer or solution, subdomain, difficulty, source/provenance, split or release revision, prompt template, answer extraction rule, scorer type, judge version, and per-item correctness.

For atlas work, it is most useful as a benchmark card for measuring post-training progress and contamination risk in olympiad-level reasoning. It is less useful as raw reward data unless the scoring route and license are audited separately.
