The contribution is a universal olympiad-level math benchmark with fine-grained taxonomy, difficulty metadata, and an accompanying judge for answer evaluation. The core mechanism is not a new solver: the authors curate hard problems, organize them by mathematical subdomain and difficulty, evaluate models under controlled prompting, and release an evaluation-side judge to score free-form model answers.

The evaluation object is the problem-answer record plus its labels and scoring path. The feedback contract is answer-level: a submission is accepted or scored by rule-based matching where possible and by Omni-Judge for cases that need semantic equivalence judgment. Closest comparisons are MATH, GSM8K, OlympiadBench, and other hard-math benchmarks; Omni-MATH's direction signal is broader olympiad coverage and a more explicit hard-math evaluation contract.
