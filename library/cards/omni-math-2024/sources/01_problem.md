Omni-MATH is an arXiv 2024 benchmark paper whose primary sources are the arXiv paper, project page, GitHub repository, Hugging Face dataset, and released Omni-Judge model. The problem is that MATH-style public benchmarks had become too easy for frontier models and too coarse for diagnosing olympiad-level mathematical reasoning, while live contest tasks and informal hard sets often lacked a stable public evaluation surface.

The card includes it as a benchmark/evaluation surface, not as a training recipe or a proof-producing formal system. A benchmark item is a competition-style math problem with a reference solution/answer, difficulty and subdomain metadata, and evaluation by either rule-based answer checking or the authors' Omni-Judge model. Its atlas value is the combination of hard-math item curation, category labels, and an explicit answer-level feedback contract that downstream evaluations can audit.
