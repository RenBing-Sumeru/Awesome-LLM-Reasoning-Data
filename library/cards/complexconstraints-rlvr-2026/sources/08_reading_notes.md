1. **Position:**  Expert atomic rubrics improve complex-instruction evaluation and RLVR.
2. **Method:**  Hard cases, five principles, judge calibration, and rubric rewards.
3. **Data:**  About 75 prompts and 1,559 criteria, extended to roughly 1,000 training examples.
4. **Evidence:**  4B/235B improve 15.5%/12.2%, with transfer to three OOD benchmarks.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for non-programmable tasks; guard against judge specialisation.
