1. **Select hard cases:** Experts create complex instructions and enterprise-agent scenarios not fully judgeable by string rules.

2. **Atomise rubrics:** Intent, exceptions, and context are decomposed into 10–40 maximally viable atomic criteria.

3. **Calibrate judges:** Human disagreements and errors iteratively refine criteria and judge prompts.

4. **Use for RL:** Criterion compliance becomes rewards for models and agents; weights, judges, and environments must be fixed.
