- **Process-filter error.** Gemini 1.5 Pro Thinking judges whether an action is reasonable in context, not whether retrieved evidence is true, complete, current, or uncontaminated. A false positive can retain a plausible-looking wrong search step or final answer; process-only selection explicitly keeps some incorrect outcomes. (Paper §2.1; Appendix A)
- **Reward opacity and gaming.** The stage-2 generative reward prompt, scalar range, normalization, decoding, calibration, and model snapshot are undisclosed. This blocks reconstruction of what the policy maximizes and leaves reward hacking or verbosity/style preference unmeasured.
- **Correlated judge roles.** Gemini 1.5 Pro participates in process filtering, RL reward, GSM8K evaluation, and process-label analysis. Improvements measured with related model judgments can share blind spots; Appendix D's 100-example checks do not audit process-filter errors or reward overoptimization. (Paper §4.1; Appendix D)
- **Prefix weighting is unspecified.** Converting a K-action trajectory into K samples repeats long contexts and gives longer episodes more optimization records. It is a curator inference that this can reweight examples; the paper discloses no deduplication, per-trajectory normalization, or weighting rule that resolves the risk.
- **Environment drift.** QA depends on an unreleased article corpus snapshot, Gecko-1B checkpoint, index configuration, retrieval depth, and stored search results; math depends on undisclosed SymPy/Python versions and exception handling. Offline prefixes may therefore replay text while failing to reproduce the original environment behavior.
- **Split and contamination.** HotPotQA and GSM8K training questions seed the synthetic pool and also define in-domain evaluation surfaces. Exact revisions, source/evaluation ID maps, semantic deduplication, pretraining contamination, and retrieval-corpus answer leakage are not audited.
- **Release and rights.** No official code, synthetic trajectories, filter labels, rewards, retained/rejected manifest, vector index, configuration, checkpoint, or model/data license was verified. CC BY 4.0 covers the arXiv paper, not these absent artifacts or upstream retrieved text.
- **Generalization and reproduction.** Results come from author experiments on a bounded task family and often 300-item subsets. Smaller Gemma models show mixed out-of-domain results, optimizer details are missing, and no independent reproduction package is available. (Paper §4; Appendices B and F)
