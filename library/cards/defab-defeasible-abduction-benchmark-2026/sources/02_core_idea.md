The main contribution is over 372,648 abductive instances with formal gold standards, derived from 18 knowledge sources and about 33.75 million materialized rules. Construction pairs taxonomies such as OpenCyc, YAGO, and Wikidata with behavioral graphs such as ConceptNet and UMLS and generates three levels covering rule completion, default override, and more complex theory revision. Each logical instance is rendered in four surface forms to test linguistic robustness. The verifier checks derivability, preservation of unrelated expectations, and minimal hypotheses. The authors also release 235 DeFAb-Hard tasks and 560 Lean 4/Mathlib CONJURE instances. The MIT-licensed data supports benchmarking, DPO preference pairs, and exact RLVR or GRPO rewards.
