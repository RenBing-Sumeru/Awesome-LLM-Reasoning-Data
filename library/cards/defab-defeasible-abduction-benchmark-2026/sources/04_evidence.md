The rule solver achieves 100% on all evaluated instances in under 50 microseconds per task, showing that gold answers and verification are executable and deterministic. Four frontier models obtain only 7.8%–23.5% rendering-robust accuracy on Level 2 even when scores on a favorable rendering can approach 65%; variation across chain-of-thought prompts is about 36 percentage points, larger than inter-model gaps. On DeFAb-Hard, the best model reaches 53.3% while the symbolic solver remains at 100%, and the CONJURE pilot produces no novel concepts. Results demonstrate surface sensitivity and theory-destroying errors, but automatically composed logical solvability does not guarantee factual naturalness or open-world creativity.
