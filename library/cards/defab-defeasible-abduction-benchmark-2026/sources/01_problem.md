General models are often asked to infer minimal explanations for anomalies, but existing abduction benchmarks depend on natural-language judges and cannot rigorously check whether a hypothesis derives the observation, preserves unrelated knowledge, or avoids unnecessary assumptions. A model may produce fluent explanations by rewriting the whole theory, conflating creativity with logical rigor.

DeFAb converts public knowledge bases into decidable defeasible-abduction instances. Models must add or override defaults to explain anomalies while satisfying derivation, conservativity, and minimality. A rule solver verifies every answer in polynomial time. The release also includes multiple surface renderings, a hard set, and the Lean-kernel-verified CONJURE subset, enabling exact rewards without an LLM judge.
