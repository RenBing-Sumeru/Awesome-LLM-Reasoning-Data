Traditional abduction data often checks only a final label or asks an LLM judge to rate explanations, failing to reject overly powerful assumptions or damage to unrelated conclusions. DeFAb defines creativity as constrained theory revision and compiles three contracts—derivability, conservativity, and minimality—into an exact verifier. It also renders each instance four ways and evaluates worst-case rather than best-prompt robustness. This is not simply larger knowledge-graph question answering or symbolic classification: models must construct a new hypothesis object, and every candidate can be converted directly into an RL reward.
