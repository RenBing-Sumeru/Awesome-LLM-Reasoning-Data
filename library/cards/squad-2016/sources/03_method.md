Inputs come from Wikipedia passages selected by benchmark authors with crowd-written questions and answer spans. Outputs are paragraph, question, gold answer span or spans, model span prediction, exact match, and token-level F1. The substrate is static span-extraction reading-comprehension dataset.

Pipeline: 1. load or construct the task instance; 2. provide the model with the allowed context and scaffold; 3. collect the generated answer, patch, test suite, span, or trajectory; 4. execute or apply normalized exact match and token-level F1 over predicted answer spans; 5. report scores only for a pinned split, runtime, artifact revision, and scorer. Reproducibility requires paper=https://aclanthology.org/D16-1264/, project=https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/, code=https://github.com/rajpurkar/SQuAD-explorer, data=https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/, plus exact split, dependency/runtime, prompt/scaffold, and scorer version.
