SQuAD v1.1 assumes answerable questions and extractive spans; it does not test unanswerability, multi-hop reasoning, long-context retrieval, or calibrated abstention.

A passing score is not a universal correctness proof. It can miss untested paths, weak assertions, benchmark-specific shortcuts, dependency drift, hidden evaluator changes, or data leakage. Do not use the benchmark as a reward signal or training corpus without checking license, provenance, and evaluator stability.
