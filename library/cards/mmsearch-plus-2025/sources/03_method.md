**Inputs and construction.** Annotators browse public consumer videos and open-access arXiv papers for at least 10 and at most 20 minutes per task, select keyframes or figure/table screenshots, and write Spatial-Temporal Extrapolation questions with acceptable short answers. LLM assistance proposes search keywords; humans curate sources, difficulty, subtask, and SoM boxes. Faces and license plates are reportedly masked and minors/private spaces excluded when feasible. At least two closed MLLMs test no-search solvability; answerable items are removed or made harder through masking/obfuscation. The final set has 311 tasks, including 94 easy and 217 hard; the 239-task MMSearch-Plus-lite subset removes every item solved without search by any tested model.

**Released output.** One Hugging Face `train` split stores `question`, answer variants, `num_images`, `arxiv_id`, `video_url`, `category`, `difficulty`, `subtask`, and `img_1` through `img_5`. Text fields `question`, `answer`, `video_url`, and `arxiv_id` are XOR-decrypted by a public canary helper; current images are not encrypted. The release preserves a source reference, not the source snapshot, annotation/edit log, SoM boxes, or rights manifest.

**Evaluation rollout.** A frozen MLLM receives the question and images, then alternates among text search, image search, indexed zoom/crop, and final answer. SerpAPI supplies ranked text/image results and thumbnails; Gemini produces webpage and related-image summaries; a threaded state retains calls, crops, summaries, URLs/titles, and hypotheses. The paper caps combined search rounds/tool calls at 20, or 10 for Qwen full rollout. Text-search rounds may issue three to five queries. Search responses, cache snapshots, prompts, seeds, locale, browser state, and complete action-observation logs are not public.

**Feedback and use.** GPT-4o judges final-answer agreement with acceptable variants; optional rule matching is mentioned but not released. The terminal event is a final answer or budget exhaustion, while benchmark success is answer correctness—not citation completeness, provenance fidelity, or verified step transitions. No optimizer or post-training scaffold is used; the paper evaluates o3, GPT-5, Gemini-2.5-Pro, and Qwen-2.5-VL-72B-Instruct.

**Reproducibility boundary.** A faithful replay would need the pinned HF rows and source captures, released SoM boxes, rollout/evaluation code, SerpAPI responses, Gemini summaries, cache, model/API versions, prompts, seeds, query date/locale, browser/runtime lock, budgets, judge logs, and success/failure trajectories. The audited GitHub revision provides only README and a decryption helper, and Hugging Face `state.json` references two obsolete shard names while the current release contains five Arrow shards. Pin the verified data revision rather than treating `state.json` as a complete manifest.
