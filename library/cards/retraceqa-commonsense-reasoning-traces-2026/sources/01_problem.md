Small language models may choose the correct commonsense answer while relying on false knowledge, irrelevant cues, or invalid inference. Answer-only evaluation cannot determine whether their reasoning traces are trustworthy, and mathematical earliest-error benchmarks do not cover factual and pragmatic commonsense failures.

ReTraceQA collects commonsense QA trajectories generated by small models and uses expert annotation for earliest-error steps and error categories, explicitly evaluating correct-answer but invalid-process behavior and interpretable reasoning quality.
