First, earliest-error localization depends on reference solutions and verification prompts; with multiple valid solution paths, a legal branch may be mislabeled, so reuse requires human auditing. Second, InT-SFT has only 2,213 records and limited domain and error-type coverage. Third, a high-reward continuation after an intervention does not prove that the edited step was the unique causal error; multiple feasible interventions and their stability should be compared.

In addition, the training benefit still depends on the reliability of terminal outcome rewards.
