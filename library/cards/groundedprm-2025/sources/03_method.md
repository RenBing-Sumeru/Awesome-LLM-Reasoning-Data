For a MATH problem, the policy represents a reasoning step as a natural-language action extending the current partial state. UCT balances node value and an exploration bonus. Each expansion samples K=3 actions. A left-to-right simulation continues until a final answer, while each intermediate statement is converted into a structured math query and submitted to Wolfram Alpha. Parsed success or failure becomes v in {-1,1}; the final answer compared with ground truth becomes F in {-1,1}.

For a prefix, the hybrid rollout reward averages discounted future step-verification signals and adds beta times terminal correctness. Backpropagation applies a gamma depth discount, and the state-action value combines the aggregate with the local step signal. Multiple rounds explore both optimal and suboptimal paths. Incomplete, inconsistent, and tool-unverifiable traces are removed. Tool-derived rationales are consistency-filtered before the final record stores problem, full trace, signed correctness labels, and explanations.

The paper reports about 40K records. GroundedPRM is LoRA-fine-tuned from Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with LLaMA-Factory on four A100 80GB GPUs. At test time, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct samples eight candidate actions per step at temperature 1 and the PRM selects the highest-reward action. Construction R, c, beta, gamma, policy checkpoint, decoding parameters, seeds, and Wolfram Alpha/API parser revision remain unknown.
