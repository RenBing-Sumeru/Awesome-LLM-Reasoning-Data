The novelty is not decomposition by itself; reasoning systems already plan. The paper makes per-subquestion budget allocation the object of optimization and evaluates it against both accuracy and consumption, so “think longer” becomes a controllable decision rather than a global default.

This differs from a pure prompting trick because the method records a schedule that can be audited. It also differs from best-of-N selection: it allocates a sequential token budget inside a planned solution rather than choosing among a fixed set of completed candidates.
