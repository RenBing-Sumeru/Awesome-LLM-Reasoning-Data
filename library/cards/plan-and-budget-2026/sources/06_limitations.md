Complexity estimates can be wrong, planning can add latency and tokens, and an early decomposition can lock the system into an unhelpful route. The efficiency metric depends on what costs are counted and may not reflect wall-clock serving constraints, tool calls, or parallel execution. A model can also appear efficient by shortening explanations while preserving only benchmarks with short final answers. Reuse should publish the full cost ledger and inspect failures where the system underallocates effort to an initially misclassified hard subproblem.
