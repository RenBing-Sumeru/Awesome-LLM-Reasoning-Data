Use this paper when a reasoning product has a strict token or latency envelope and can expose a plan before solving. Compare fixed-length reasoning, adaptive allocation without planning, and the full method under the same end-to-end budget. Log the proposed plan and actual spend for each input so a reader can tell whether gains come from correct difficulty estimation or a hidden change in the answer-generation policy. It is less useful for tasks that cannot be decomposed meaningfully or require a single irreducible search process.
