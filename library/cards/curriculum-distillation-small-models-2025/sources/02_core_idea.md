The contribution is a difficulty- and stage-conditioned distillation schedule over a matched set of teacher outputs. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct supplies ordinary chain-of-thought solutions. DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B is elicited in three modes: Think retains full deliberation; NoRethink suppresses a small set of self-correction trigger words to encourage a more linear trace; NoThink prepends a trivial completed-thought template so the model proceeds directly to a solution. These are observable prompting/decoding conditions, not verified internal cognitive states. (Paper Section 3.1.1.)

Math-Verify supplies the feedback contract. Within up to four samples per problem and condition, it accepts an extracted final answer; a problem enters the 6,445-item pool only if Instruct, Think, NoRethink, and NoThink each yield at least one accepted solution. The verifier can observe answer equivalence after extraction, but it cannot certify the logical validity or faithfulness of intermediate text. Thus the process text receives SFT supervision, while selection remains answer-level.

Stage 1 uses Instruct traces for MATH Levels 1-4 and NoThink solutions for Level 5. Stage 2 changes Levels 1-4 to NoThink and Level 5 to NoRethink. The closest paper baseline, Mix-Long from Li et al. (2025), randomly mixes Instruct and Think outputs at 4:1. The essential change is to replace a static random long/short mixture with a curriculum indexed jointly by problem difficulty and training stage. (Paper Section 4.2 and Table 2.)
