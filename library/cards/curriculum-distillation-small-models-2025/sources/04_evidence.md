The mechanism study establishes the motivation but not the training result by itself. In Figure 1, Think has the highest MATH Pass@1 and token use, NoRethink lies between Think and NoThink, and NoThink exceeds its matched Instruct model by as much as 7 percentage points on harder MATH levels with only a small token increase. In the separate context-titration experiment, low-difficulty problems require a median 33% of a successful LRM trace before the Instruct model succeeds, versus 61% for high-difficulty problems. These are author-reported behaviors under the specified Qwen-derived models and sampling protocol. (Paper Figures 1-2, pp. 7415-7416.)

Table 1 shows that direct imitation is not monotonic in teacher reasoning length. For Qwen2.5-3B, the single-stage Instruct dataset averages 26.65 across six benchmarks, compared with 23.39 for NoThink, 21.94 for NoRethink, and 20.08 for Think. The corresponding Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct averages are 26.10, 24.22, 22.06, and 20.82. Thus stronger or longer teacher reasoning does not automatically produce a better student in this setup. (Paper Table 1, p. 7417.)

Table 2 provides the direct curriculum comparison. For Qwen2.5-3B, Stage 1 followed by NoThink+NoRethink Stage 2 scores 6.98 on AIME 2024, 3.44 on AIME 2025, 39.43 on AMC 2023, 64.02 on MATH-500, 26.88 on Minerva, and 29.51 on OlympiadBench, averaging 28.38. The single-stage Instruct baseline averages 26.65 and Mix-Long averages 25.22; Stage 2 alternatives average 27.68 with NoThink only and 27.56 with NoThink+Think. For Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, the same curriculum averages 27.96 versus 26.10 for single-stage Instruct and 25.85 for Mix-Long. (Paper Table 2, p. 7419.)

These results support a schedule effect under the reported models, data, and evaluation budget. They do not prove that every retained trace is correct, that the difficulty labels are causal, or that the constructed corpus has high data quality. No independent reproduction, corpus release, verifier-error analysis, or ablation that removes the all-mode intersection filter is provided.
