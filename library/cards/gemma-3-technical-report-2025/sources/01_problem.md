The 2025 *Gemma 3 Technical Report* documents open-weight 1B, 4B, 12B, and 27B models; the larger three combine a decoder with a frozen roughly 400M-parameter SigLIP vision encoder. Pretraining budgets are respectively 2T, 4T, 12T, and 14T tokens. The report, official model card, checkpoint, code, and terms are public, but training records and feedback assets are not.

The Atlas problem is to distinguish open checkpoints from an open training pipeline. Each pretraining token is supervised by 256 sampled teacher logits, while post-training uses a large IT teacher followed by improved BOND/WARM/WARP-style RL with weight-averaged human-feedback reward models, code execution, mathematical ground truth, and safety SFT/RLHF. Teacher identities, preferences, reward models, tests, math checker, rollouts, and RL settings remain private.

A record may contain source text/image, sampled teacher token IDs/probabilities, an instruction/response, a human preference or safety label, an RM score, code execution feedback, or a math-correctness reward. The exact schema, mixture, generator, and lineage are unreleased. This Card reaches L4 by documenting both the concrete supervision objects and the missing assets rather than treating weights or benchmark scores as data disclosure.
