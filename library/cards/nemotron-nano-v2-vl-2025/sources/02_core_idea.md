The core training idea is staged multimodal SFT around a hybrid Mamba-Transformer language model. A c-RADIOv2-VLM-H vision encoder and MLP connector turn dynamically tiled images or sampled video frames into interleaved visual and text embeddings. Each tile yields 1,024 visual tokens before 2x pixel-shuffle reduction to 256; training allows up to 12 tiles plus a thumbnail, while each video frame uses one tile.

The curriculum separates alignment, broad capability acquisition, context extension, and capability repair. Stage 0 warms only the connector. Stage 1 unfreezes the full model and mixes text reasoning with broad image, OCR, document, grounding, safety, dialogue, tool-use, and function-calling data. Stage 2 extends context and emphasizes video and multi-image tasks. Stage 3 uses code-reasoning data to recover coding behavior. Stage 4 uses very long text examples to recover long-context behavior.

The data contract is supervised conversation prediction, not a reported reinforcement-learning reward. Answers and reasoning traces come from existing annotations, humans, named model families, rules, parsers, or programmatic generation. There is no universal verifier: the paper does not supply a common correctness test, acceptance threshold, or rejected-candidate ledger across all sources.
