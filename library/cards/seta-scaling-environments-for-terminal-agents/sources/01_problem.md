The bottleneck in terminal RL is not language prompts but large numbers of environments with coherent instructions, runnable containers, and reliable tests. Pure synthesis repeatedly covers similar tasks, direct copying cannot control difficulty or diversity, and inconsistent verifiers make rewards incomparable.

SETA connects two pipelines through a shared verification mechanism: SETA-Synth standardizes diverse sources into RL environments, while SETA-Evol adaptively expands difficulty and task distribution from existing environments.
