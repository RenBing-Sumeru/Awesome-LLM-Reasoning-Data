AWS describes a core multimodal transformer combined with a speech encoder and speech renderer. A deployed interaction turn contains model-level instructions, chat history, or tool definitions; speech or text input; and generated text and speech responses. That runtime object is concrete, but the stored post-training example schema remains unknown.

The disclosure boundary is a three-part control story: curate broad training-source classes, adjust weights with SFT and RLHF, and tune privacy, profanity, and safety filters. The report also refers to reinforcement learning in the model-development description. It does not identify the human-feedback record format, reward or verifier, preference aggregation, calibration, or terminal predicate, so the named stages must not be upgraded into an RLVR claim.
