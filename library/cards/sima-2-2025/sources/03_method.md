The disclosed pipeline is:

1. **Ten training environments.** Three research worlds—Construction Lab, Playhouse, and WorldLab—and seven licensed commercial games—Goat Simulator 3, Hydroneer, No Man's Sky, Satisfactory, Space Engineers, Valheim, and Wobbly Life. ASKA and MineDojo/Minecraft are held out from initial training; The Gunk and Genie 3 support qualitative or self-improvement demonstrations.
2. **Interface.** The policy receives rendered 720p RGB frames plus prior language, reasoning, responses, and visual history. Structured output is deterministically parsed into chunks over 96 keyboard keys, mouse clicks, and discretized relative mouse movement. The policy receives no privileged game state, although external verifiers sometimes do.
3. **Human and Gemini supervision.** Humans generate most gameplay by volume through free play with post-hoc annotation, setter-solver instructions, predefined tasks, and dialogue data. A smaller bridge set selects successful high-quality episodes; Gemini Pro annotates causally consistent internal reasoning and dialogue, including post-completion no-op steps. Exact counts, prompts, and retention are unknown.
4. **Preprocessing and SFT.** Frames are resized, low-quality records filtered, sources reweighted, and most trajectories split into shorter instruction-centered spans. Gameplay is mixed with undisclosed Gemini non-gameplay/pretraining data to retain vision, dialogue, reasoning, and prompt-following.
5. **Online RLVR.** Curated objects pair an initial saved state, instruction, and verification function. Contractors propose feasible tasks from random states; verifiers locate completion in nearby human trajectories to expand tasks. Training rewards use task or question completion signals and, for some tasks, shaped instruction-following or controllability signals grounded in environment-state, OCR, pixel, or action checks. The separate five-rater human video judgments belong to evaluation, not to the disclosed online-RL reward stack. Initial RL runs only in the ten training environments.
6. **Self-improvement.** In ASKA or Genie 3 worlds, a Gemini task generator proposes state-conditioned tasks and can focus on weak skills. SIMA 2 produces trajectories; a Gemini reward model, calibrated on a small human preference set, scores completion and directedness from 0 to 100, with `≥50` treated as success. Experience enters a bank and subsequent policy training closes the loop. Generator/judge prompts, checkpoints, rollouts, acceptance, replay, and generations are private.

MineDojo evaluation uses 50 programmatic tasks and 15 random seeds each, totaling 750 task-seed configurations. Comprehensive timeouts, frame cadence, action-chunk duration, optimizer, RL steps, compute, and environment build identifiers are not reported.
