Commercial-game rights are a primary reuse barrier. The games were licensed for SIMA 2 training/evaluation and participants consented, but those agreements do not grant public rights to redistribute game-derived video, actions, annotations, builds, saved states, Gemini labels, or derivatives. No public artifact supplies code, trajectories, policy/reward checkpoints, task bank, verifiers, or environments.

The Gemini family generates labels, bridge reasoning, tasks, hierarchical instructions, and rewards. This creates correlated generator/judge bias; the small reward-calibration preference set, prompts, agreement, score distribution, `50` threshold sensitivity, and reward-hacking tests are undisclosed. Failed human, RL, and self-improvement trajectories may be discarded or reused, but the policy is unknown.

ASKA/MineDojo are held out at environment level, yet base Gemini may contain public game text, imagery, videos, walkthroughs, code, or Minecraft knowledge; no foundation-model decontamination exists. Exact game versions, patches, DLC/mods, graphics, controls, runtime, verifier implementations, world seeds, and saved states are absent, preventing reproduction and exposing results to environment drift. Capability-retention declines further show that embodied gains do not imply preserved general reasoning.
