1. Position: evaluate computer-use processes from full execution videos. Confirm that the contribution is process feedback rather than model weights alone.
2. Method: recording, synchronization, real failures, controlled negatives, and reward labeling. Data quality depends mainly on the labeler, tool, or environment signal.
3. Data: about 53K task–video–action–reward records. Audit scale, fields, splits, licensing, decontamination, and lineage.
4. Evidence: video rewards beat screenshot and action-text baselines. Evidence is bounded by reported models, budgets, and benchmarks.
5. Reuse: fits computer-use agents; privacy and UI shortcuts are main risks. Best for replayable process training, with label and error audits before reuse.
