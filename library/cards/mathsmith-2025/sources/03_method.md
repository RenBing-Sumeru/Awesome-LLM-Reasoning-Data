The source stage crawls PlanetMath mathematics pages, removes pages judged unrelated to mathematical concepts, and asks GPT-4o to extract concepts, short explanations grounded in each page, and one to three broad categories. For cold-start construction, five records are sampled. The prompt requires one unified, non-proof question with a numeric, symbolic, expression, or equation answer; it also requires at least two difficulty strategies among multi-step reasoning, cross-topic integration, reverse logic, distractors, abstract modeling, multiple solution paths, advanced manipulation, extreme conditions, and non-standard representation.

The paper reports LoRA rank 16 SFT for five epochs on 8 H100 GPUs, followed by GRPO with MathSmith-SFT as reference. Reward-time complexity and consistency use K=5 Qwen3-30B-A3B samples; the structural and reasoning weights are 0.7/0.3; policy and teacher inference use 20 H100 GPUs; the selected checkpoint is step 100. Downstream baselines are trained for five epochs with LLaMA-Factory at learning rate 1e-5.

The current repository is useful but not an exact run manifest. Its example GRPO script uses rollout group size 6, while its reward code defaults to three teacher samples, maximum generation length 32,768, and temperature 0.3; its SFT YAML uses eight epochs. These differ from the paper's K=5, 20-GPU, five-epoch account. The release therefore establishes an executable scaffold, but the exact published configuration remains partly unknown.

Problem datasets expose sampled_concept, rationale, and problem fields; solution datasets expose problem, answer, optional answer_dict/highest_freq, and sampled_concept. The checked pages provide a single train split without stable cross-release IDs.
