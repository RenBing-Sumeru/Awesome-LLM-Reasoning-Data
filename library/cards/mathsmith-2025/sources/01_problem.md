MathSmith addresses a data-construction bottleneck: difficult mathematical training questions are scarce, while many synthesis pipelines mutate existing benchmark-like problems and inherit their templates. It instead begins with mathematics pages from PlanetMath, filters non-concept entries, and uses GPT-4o to create about 11,000 concept-explanation-category records. A generation prompt samples five such records, allows the model to select a meaningful subset of at least two concepts, and requires at least two of nine difficulty strategies.

The released primary object is not only a question. HC and Hard problem records contain the sampled concepts, a construction rationale, and the final problem; companion releases attach Qwen3-30B-A3B short- or long-CoT solutions. This makes the work relevant to the data-construction and open-release-recipes track: it exposes prompt sourcing, generator training, reward design, released policies, intermediate problem/rationale records, and solution traces. It does not, however, provide one immutable manifest that joins each PlanetMath page, generated candidate, reward decision, retained problem, solution, and downstream training example.
