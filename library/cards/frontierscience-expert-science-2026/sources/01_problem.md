Existing science benchmarks are largely multiple-choice questions about published knowledge, are approaching saturation, and may be memorised. They do not measure derivation, judgment, and intermediate steps in open research subtasks. Expert-level novel questions are expensive, and final answers alone remain non-diagnostic.

FrontierScience has olympiad experts and PhD scientists author and verify original tasks, creates Olympiad and Research tracks, and uses fine-grained rubrics for research-task processes.
