For this track, the Card is a concrete template for logging search-generated training data: record the prefix, completion budget, completer models, terminal predicate, hard label, PRM probability, aggregation rule, acceptance threshold, rejected candidates, and compute. It supports controlled comparisons of answer-reachability labels against formal step verification, of all-positive rejection sampling against softer filters, and of product scores against calibrated or length-normalized trajectory values.

The released step labels can seed PRM/process-supervision experiments, and the SFT and DPO releases can support non-commercial research under their stated CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 metadata. Reuse is nevertheless conditional: verify upstream source terms, reconstruct item lineage, perform contamination checks, and repair/pin the code before training. The artifacts should not be used as an evaluation set because they expose train-only derivatives of benchmark training questions.

The safest present reuse class is **research and audit reference with conditional training reuse**, not a turnkey end-to-end recipe. It is especially useful for ablations that retain the raw candidate pool and failed continuations, because the official release does not. It is not evidence-backed as RLVR, online RL, or test-time inference data; the supported uses are PRM/reward modeling, process supervision, SFT, and DPO preference learning.
