The reconstructable pipeline is:

1. **Inputs.** Draw random training-question subsets of 1,003 FOLIO, 1,000 FOLIOv2, and 300 LogicAsker items. The item manifest and seed are not released. A one-shot prompt specifies Symbolic ReAct formatting and first-order-logic-style operations.
2. **Seed trajectories and prefix rollouts.** Llama3.3-70B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct generate Symbolic ReAct trajectories. At every intermediate prefix, sample 10 completions. Assign `+1` when at least one completion ends with the ground-truth label and `-1` otherwise. The released repository file contains 1,505 `input` records with aligned step (`value`) and sign (`label`) arrays, totaling 12,448 steps (Paper §3.2, §4, Appendix A).
3. **PRM.** Fine-tune Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with a binary classification head on those hard labels. The paper reports 10 epochs, batch size 8, learning rate \(5\times10^{-7}\), maximum sequence length 2,048, `adamw_torch_fused`, and development-accuracy checkpoint selection. The PRM checkpoint and calibration results are not released.
4. **Second-stage generation.** Llama3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct generate symbolic candidate trajectories for all training questions. Candidate count per question, temperature, top-p, seeds, and model allocation for the reported run are unknown. Monte Carlo labeling consumed about 250 AMD MI300X GPU-hours; later sampling took about 42 hours (Appendix A).
5. **Selection and outputs.** SFT retains only trajectories with all PRM step probabilities above 0.5 and a ground-truth-matching final answer, yielding 15,412 released rows. DPO multiplies step-positive probabilities to score each trajectory and pairs a higher and lower candidate when the score difference exceeds 0.25, yielding 21,472 released pairs. The product is not length-normalized in the reported contract.
6. **Training and evaluation.** The retained responses support full SFT; preference pairs support DPO. Evaluation covers FOLIO, the paper’s 900-example LogicAsker subset split evenly into train/development/test, and three claim-verification datasets. The two Hugging Face derivatives expose only a train split, and no decontamination procedure is reported.

End-to-end reproduction requires a pinned repair beyond fixing model versions. `generate_intermediate_react.py` and `generate_trajectory_samples_vllm.py` reference undefined `args.data_path` and a nonexistent `prompts/LogiAsker_react_example.txt` spelling; `dpo_training.py` references undefined `args.model_name`. Repository defaults of 5 samples, temperature 1.0, and Qwen3-32B are not the paper’s 10-completion 70B/72B PRM-labeling setting. A reproducer must therefore freeze the paper configuration, disclose the missing second-stage decoding settings, repair these entry points, and record raw candidates, scores, source IDs, and software versions.
