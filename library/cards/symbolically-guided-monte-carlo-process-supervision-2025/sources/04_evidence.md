The strongest evidence is conditional rather than a blanket data-quality result.

- **Released-object checks.** The pinned repository JSON contains 1,505 trajectories and 12,448 aligned step labels. Direct counting gives 10,709 positive and 1,739 negative labels; 1,164 trajectories are all-positive and 341 contain at least one negative label. The official Hugging Face APIs expose one train split with 15,412 SFT rows and one train split with 21,472 DPO rows. These checks establish release size and schema, not semantic correctness.
- **In-domain fine-tuning.** In Table 1, Qwen Symbolic Trajectory-FT reports 68.14 accuracy on FOLIO and 74.00 on LogicAsker; the Llama counterpart reports 63.24 and 63.67. The conditions use the paper’s generated symbolic trajectories and full fine-tuning. Benchmark gains do not by themselves validate every trace or the PRM labels.
- **PRM filtering ablation.** Appendix B, Table 5 reports Llama at 62.25/63.00 without PRM filtering versus 63.24/63.67 with it on FOLIO/LogicAsker, and Qwen at 65.20/73.33 versus 68.14/74.00. This is evidence for task-specific filtering benefit, with a small LogicAsker difference for Qwen; it is not evidence of formal step validity.
- **Negative result for pair construction.** Section 6.1 reports that SFT generally outperforms DPO and suggests that cumulative PRM probabilities may not distinguish trajectories adequately. This matters because the released DPO “negative” is only relatively lower-scored under a product of learned probabilities.
- **Out-of-domain behavior.** Table 2 reports Qwen SFT at 81.93 on Vitamin C, 58.07 on Climate-FEVER, and 60.40 on PHEMEPlus; Llama SFT reports 86.06, 78.27, and 72.00. Transfer is not uniform: Llama SFT is below process-DPO on PHEMEPlus (72.00 versus 73.47).

All performance numbers are author-reported and have not been independently reproduced for this Card. The artifact inspection verifies counts, fields, links, and script behavior; it does not upgrade the paper’s model results into proof of data quality.
