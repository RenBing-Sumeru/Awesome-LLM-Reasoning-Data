HoWToBench provides 1,302 Chinese writing instructions across 12 genres and three task formats: continuation, outline-guided writing, and open generation, together with an 852-item English extension subset. Instructions and reference materials are drawn from professional writing sources and cover both creative and functional text. Tree-of-Writing decomposes overall quality into hierarchical dimensions and leaf features, assigns explicit node weights, and lets judges evaluate each level. The framework reaches a Pearson correlation of 0.93 with human judgments and releases generations, rubrics, perturbation tests, and evaluation code.
