1. **One-sentence positioning:** A traceable multidimensional evaluation framework for long-form open writing using a weighted Tree-of-Writing.
2. **Data takeaway:** Twelve genres, 1,302 Chinese instructions, three task formats, and an English extension set.
3. **Method takeaway:** Instructions are curated from professional sources, expert-checked, and scored through leaf features, parent dimensions, and weighted aggregation.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Correlation with human ratings reaches 0.93, with greater robustness to textual perturbations than ordinary judges.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is strong rubric data and protocol, but cultural weighting and evaluation cost constrain generalization.
