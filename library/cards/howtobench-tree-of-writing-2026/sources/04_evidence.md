ToW achieves a Pearson correlation of 0.93 with human ratings, outperforming LLM judges that assign one holistic score or simply average subdimensions. The authors also construct textual perturbations and find that overlap metrics and common judges are vulnerable to superficial changes, whereas tree-based evaluation is more stable. Cross-model analysis shows that longer input material does not necessarily improve content scores in Guide tasks and can even correlate negatively, indicating that the benchmark is not merely testing copying. The evidence is strong, though weighting and human ratings remain centered on Chinese professional writing.
