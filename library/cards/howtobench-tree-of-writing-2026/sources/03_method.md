1. **Collect texts:** Contexts, outlines, and writing goals for diverse genres are drawn from professional publishing, writing instruction, and high-quality texts.
2. **Construct instructions:** Completion, Guide, and Open tasks are designed across 12 genres while controlling length, topic, and implicit writing requirements.
3. **Human quality control:** Experts verify writability, source quality, and task boundaries, with a final dataset quality-check pass rate of 96.85%.
4. **Build tree rubrics:** Overall quality is decomposed into parent nodes such as language, logic, content, and structure and into concrete leaf features, with explicit aggregation weights.
5. **Validate evaluation:** Ten representative models generate texts, human scores are collected, and perturbed outputs test the robustness of ToW, traditional metrics, and ordinary LLM judges.
