Every two steps, 256 reference questions receive eight rollouts to estimate adaptive difficulty. A frozen Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B-Instruct embedding backbone plus similarity attention predicts other questions. Sampling is proportional to exp(-|difficulty-0.5|/tau), tau=1e-3. Batches contain 512 groups, half fresh and half replayed; FIFO capacity is 256 or 512. Math-Verify supplies binary rewards.
