- **Release mismatch.** The main results use a 3.52M mixture including in-house CodeMix, but only PythonEdu-Reasoning is public. Reproduce both the public subset and a documented replacement mixture before attributing the paper's full average gains to downloadable data.
- **Verification is outcome-level.** Execution can confirm the predicted I/O while leaving an unfaithful or inefficient rationale untouched, and wrong final traces are deliberately retained. Sample traces for step-level consistency and compare training with correct-only, feedback-only, and full-revision variants.
- **Code and evaluation lineage can overlap.** Only LeetCode-O has an explicit training-overlap exclusion, while upstream repositories have heterogeneous licenses and provenance. Hash source files, scan all downstream benchmarks for semantic overlap, and retain source-level license records.
