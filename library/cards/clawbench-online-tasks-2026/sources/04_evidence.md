The arXiv record verifies the benchmark scale: 153 simple tasks across 144 live platforms and 15 categories. It also verifies the key design contrast against offline sandboxes and the final-submission interception layer.

The abstract reports that 7 frontier models were evaluated and gives Claude Sonnet 4.6 at 33.3%, supporting the claim that the live tasks remain difficult. This number is only meaningful under the paper's task set, agent scaffolds, live-site state, and safety interception setup.

The strongest evidence is a successful intercepted final submission attempt for a particular task. Aggregate success rate alone does not show whether failures came from navigation, document use, form filling, account state, or site drift.
