The prior baseline is web-agent evaluation on static pages, mirrored websites, or controlled sandboxes. ClawBench changes the setting by moving to production websites while intercepting the final high-stakes action.

The direction signal is that realistic web-agent evaluation needs both live environmental complexity and safety controls. The quality signal is the explicit scale across platforms and categories.

What is not new is browser automation itself. Before reuse, inspect artifact access, site terms, account setup, task stability, and whether interception truly prevents side effects.
