The contribution is the coupling of naturally occurring procedural text with environment execution. Earlier GUI datasets often depend on direct human demonstrations or static annotations; AgentTrek treats tutorials as weak plans that can be discovered, standardized, replayed, and converted into grounded state-action supervision. This creates a scalable bridge from web text to interactive data without asking humans to author every episode.

The recipe makes data quality a pipeline property rather than a single annotation. Tutorial retrieval, LLM labeling, FastText filtering, structured extraction, live replay, and VLM judgment are distinct selection layers. That decomposition exposes where recall, stale instructions, environment failures, or judge semantics can alter the corpus.

Web crawling, FastText, BrowserGym/Playwright execution, SFT, and VLM judging are not individually new. Relative to `learn-by-interact-2025`, AgentTrek preserves the tutorial's intended goal and expected result during replay rather than relabeling every subtrajectory after interaction. Its novelty is the integrated tutorial-guided collection route, scale, multimodal schema, and cost analysis.

The novelty must not be inflated into a complete open-release claim. The paper demonstrates a rich trajectory object and downstream SFT, while the public dataset contains only turn-level text. VLM judgment scales filtering but does not become a deterministic verifier merely because actions occurred in a live browser. Reuse requires inspecting the unreleased failure boundary, environment snapshots, rights, privacy, and paper-to-HF mapping.
