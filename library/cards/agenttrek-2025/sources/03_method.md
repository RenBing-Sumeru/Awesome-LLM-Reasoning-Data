The source pipeline begins with RedPajama. Heuristics for action/UI/platform terms, 200–5,000-word length, and tutorial-like URLs reduce 20.8 billion entries to 68.8 million; the paper reports 92.69% recall on 285 manually labeled samples (Paper §2.1.1). GPT-4o-mini labels 90,000 candidates and reports 88.5% F1 against human labels. FastText trained on a 95:5 split reports 89.5% validation F1 and identifies about 18.8 million deduplicated tutorial candidates (Paper §2.1.2–2.1.3).

GPT-4o-mini standardizes selected tutorials into platform, target type/object/URL, task description, prerequisites, step instructions, step count, and expected result. The extraction prompt asks for the first tutorial when a page contains multiple candidates (Appendix G.3). Exact RedPajama snapshot, page membership, deduplication keys, FastText weights, labels, and source rights are not released.

For trajectory generation, GPT-4o-2024-08-06 receives a structured tutorial and operates Chromium through BrowserGym. It observes screenshots and AXTree, selects Playwright actions, and the paper's collector records synchronized reasoning, screenshots, DOM snapshots, coordinates, video, network/native traces, and action history (Paper §2.2.1–2.2.2). The paper reports 8,027 tokens per interaction step, 86,114 per task, and 12.1 steps per successful trajectory, but no complete step/time cap, temperature, seed, retry protocol, browser image, or paper-run commit.

A GPT-4o evaluator reads the task description and full reasoning/action sequence, produces trajectory- and step-level analysis, identifies an earliest failure, and assigns success/failure. Human review covers 1,081 trajectories, including 558 with detailed justifications, and reported evaluator accuracy is 84.0% (Paper §2.2.3, Table 2). Appendix G.1 shows permissive rules: the judge may accept most of a task, one of two subtasks, omitted final save/post actions, or more than eight correct actions.

The headline funnel is 23,430 standardized tutorials to 10,398 retained trajectories over 127 websites and 11 categories (Table 1; §4; Appendix I). Successful episodes feed SFT for Qwen2-VL and Qwen2.5 agents. The official repository contains evaluation entry points and dependencies, but its Data Preparation and Training sections are empty; the tutorial miner, labeler, FastText pipeline, standardizer, replay collector, evaluator/filter, serializer, and training implementation are absent.

Reproduction must bind the source snapshot and page rights, GPT prompts/versions, FastText artifacts, tutorial IDs, browser/container/site state and replay dates, full raw trajectories, evaluator decisions, selected/rejected records, SFT subset IDs, code/data/model revisions, checkpoints, and evaluation runs. Individual Git/HF heads are pinnable, but no release-wide manifest connects them.
