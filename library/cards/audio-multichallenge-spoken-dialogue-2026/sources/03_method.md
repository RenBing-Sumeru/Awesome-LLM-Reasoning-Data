1. **Define capability axes:** Multi-turn failures are separated into memory, instruction retention, self-coherence, and spoken repairs, with audio-specific cue tasks added.
2. **Generate blueprints:** An agentic pipeline proposes conversation goals, hidden dependencies, and instance-level rubrics designed to expose model failures.
3. **Record human speech:** Forty-seven speakers perform natural interactions, preserving mistakes, corrections, prosody, and ambient sounds rather than relying on uniform text-to-speech.
4. **Human review:** Annotators verify naturalness, task determinacy, and rubric coverage, revising ambiguous instances.
5. **Evaluate models:** Systems receive the raw audio history and generate responses, which are checked item by item by rubric-based judges and aggregated by capability axis.
