End-to-end spoken-dialogue models increasingly process raw audio directly, yet existing evaluations often use synthetic speech, single-turn questions, or transcripts. They therefore miss pauses, repairs, ambient sounds, and long-range conversational memory. The paper asks how to build a natural, multi-turn, finely gradable audio benchmark that tests semantic and acoustic memory, instruction retention, self-coherence, and the ability to follow mid-utterance corrections or backtracking.
