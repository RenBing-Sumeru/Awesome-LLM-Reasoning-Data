Audio MultiChallenge extends the text-based MultiChallenge framework into an audio-native setting. It retains Inference Memory, Instruction Retention, and Self Coherence while adding Voice Editing. The benchmark contains 452 conversations from 47 speakers and 1,712 instance-specific rubrics, preserving natural mistakes, pauses, non-monotonic phrasing, and background audio. Each task is judged with concrete, checkable requirements rather than a single holistic score.
