The authors evaluate proprietary and open spoken-dialogue systems, and the strongest model, Gemini 3 Pro Preview (Thinking), reaches only a 54.65% pass rate. Voice Editing and Audio-Cue memory—where relevant information exists only in the acoustic signal—are particularly difficult, while self-coherence degrades with longer audio context. Human speech and instance-specific rubrics improve realism and interpretability, although automated rubric judging can still make errors and does not replace full human review.
