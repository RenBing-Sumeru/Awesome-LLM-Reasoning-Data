DeepResearcher studies end-to-end reinforcement learning for research agents in the noisy, changing open web rather than a fixed RAG corpus. The data-facing objects are distinct: 80,000 filtered source questions; the on-policy stream of reasoning, JSON tool actions, and observations; terminal answers and rewards; and the trained DeepResearcher-7b checkpoint.

The official repository releases train/dev/test prompt Parquets, code, and evaluation paths, and the official Hugging Face page releases a checkpoint. These artifacts do not establish a versioned release of the original training rollouts, rejected siblings, or web snapshots.
