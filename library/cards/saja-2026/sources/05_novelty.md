Prompt optimization preserves one-call inference but repeats costly task setup; LLM-RUBRIC uses multiple rubric calls and logits; FELIX removes logits but discovers features through multiple rounds. SAJA's specific change is to consolidate all rubric dimensions into one textual call and place every dataset-specific adjustment in a multi-dimensional calibration head. Structured prompting, parsing, and small supervised heads are individually established; the claimed novelty is their combination meeting all four deployment constraints.
