AutoL2S uses two stages. In SFT, DeepSeek-R1 supplies long correct reasoning and Qwen2.5-Math-7B-Instruct supplies k short candidates. Among candidates whose extracted answer matches the reference, the shortest is selected. When both modes exist, the training sequence marks the item with an EASY routing token and includes long and short reasoning; otherwise it uses the long path only. At inference, the SFT model emits either the long-mode trigger or the EASY decision, after which constrained decoding injects the short-mode trigger.

The second stage samples these automatically routed rollouts from the SFT policy and applies a clipped GRPO-style objective. Utility is binary final-answer correctness; positive and negative advantages reinforce correct rollouts and discourage incorrect ones, while the policy ratio is clipped with epsilon 0.2 to keep the new distribution near the SFT model. There is no explicit token-length reward in this objective: shorter behavior is inherited from the paired supervision and routing decision.

The feedback contract therefore has two layers: programmatic answer correctness selects the shortest valid short trace during construction, and answer-level binary utility supervises routed rollouts during refinement. The track fit is long-to-short or distill-from-search data, where the paired traces, rejected short candidates, routing token, and rollout mode must remain visible rather than collapsing everything into a final checkpoint.
