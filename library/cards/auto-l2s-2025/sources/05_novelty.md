Long-CoT distillation usually transfers one verbose teacher trace, while length-control approaches may prune, set a token budget, or add an explicit length reward. AutoL2S instead constructs both long and shortest-correct short supervision for the same question, trains a generated routing token, and then refines the induced modes with answer-level rollouts while keeping the policy close to SFT. This makes reasoning length an instance-level routing decision rather than one global target.

The paper does not introduce distillation, rejection sampling, shortest-correct filtering, special tokens, GRPO-style clipping, or binary correctness individually. Its specific contribution is their two-stage composition and the use of paired long-short order to teach routing without an explicit length term in the refinement reward.

For Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data, the direction signal is that expensive teacher traces can be transformed into compact trainable traces only if pair lineage and rejection evidence remain available. The reported accuracy-length curves are a method-quality signal, not proof of corpus quality. Reuse must verify teacher provenance, answer checking, candidate multiplicity, mode annotation, rejected traces, and whether compression removed necessary reasoning.
