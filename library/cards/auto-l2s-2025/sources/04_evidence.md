For Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Table 1 reports AutoL2S at average accuracy 0.573 and average length 2,103 tokens, versus 0.590 and 7,430 for Bespoke-Stratos-7B. This is a 71.7% average length reduction with a 1.7-point absolute average accuracy decrease. For the 3B setup, AutoL2S reports 0.415 and 4,803 tokens versus 0.413 and 10,219 for Bespoke-Stratos-3B. Per-benchmark effects are not uniform: some tasks improve, while AIME24 and others lose accuracy.

Ablations compare SFT-only, force-short, force-long, rejection sizes 0/4/8, and alternative annotation orders. They support the claim that adaptive routing differs from globally forcing one mode and that rejection sampling affects the accuracy-length trade-off. The paper also reports additional teacher and repeated-run analyses.

These measurements show a model-level efficiency trade-off under the chosen decoding setup; they do not establish that the shortest correct trace is semantically complete or that the paired training data are high quality. The ACL Anthology record, official code, and official model weights verify the paper and implementation path, but no complete paired corpus or per-example selection evidence was confirmed as public.
