Use this work when the serving system owns a reasoning model and must convert a quality target into a token-saving policy. Log the model revision, prompt family, every evaluated thought prefix, hidden-state feature, probe score, calibration threshold and risk level, stop decision, generated tokens, final answer, correctness, latency, and probe overhead. Evaluate separately on in-distribution and shifted tasks, with full-generation and fixed-cap controls. The most useful production metric is not average token reduction alone, but token reduction conditional on accuracy and on the rate of unsafe early stops.
