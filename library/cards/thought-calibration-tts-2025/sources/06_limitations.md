The approach requires white-box access to hidden states and adds probe computation, so it is not directly applicable to a black-box reasoning API or necessarily free in latency. Its risk control rests on calibration assumptions and a chosen loss definition; distribution shift, changes in prompting or model revision, and different answer parsers can invalidate the empirical relation between probe signals and useful continuation. It also measures whether reasoning has plateaued, not whether the current answer is correct. A careful deployment must therefore recalibrate after material model or task changes and retain a conservative fallback for high-risk queries.
