Prior methods rely on final answers, generic step labels, or a single LLM judge, leaving a mismatch between feedback and the target. This work centers supervision on problem-specific rubrics and complete reasoning trajectories, uses deriving task-specific rubrics from verified error patterns and using a generative Rubric Reward Model to assign calibrated scores to generate and filter records, and releases the rubric-reward dataset. The novelty lies in the data object and acceptance rule rather than standard training algorithms.
