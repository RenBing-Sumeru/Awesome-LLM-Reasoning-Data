Outcome rewards inspect only the final answer and therefore accept Miracle Steps that skip essential derivations but happen to be correct. When such trajectories enter RL, the model may reinforce answer memorization, unsupported jumps, and fabricated reasoning, while Verified Pass@K counts invalid proofs as successes.

The paper first derives a human-verified error taxonomy, then generates a rubric of required derivations and prohibited mistakes for each problem. A generative RRM evaluates the full trajectory criterion by criterion and assigns a 0–1 reward.
