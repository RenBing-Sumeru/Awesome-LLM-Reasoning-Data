The core experiments evaluate the method on mathematical process rewards and Verified Pass@K evaluation. AIME2024 Verified Pass@1024 increases from 26.7% to 62.6%, while Miracle Steps decrease by 71%. The results support supervision from deriving task-specific rubrics from verified error patterns and using a generative Rubric Reward Model to assign calibrated scores under the reported setup, but remain limited by the base model, data, and inference budget. The conclusion depends on rubric coverage, RRM calibration, sampling budget, and data quality.
