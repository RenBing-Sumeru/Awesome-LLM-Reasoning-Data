1. **One-sentence positioning:** Outcome rewards treat correct answers with invalid reasoning as successful miracle steps; it uses deriving task-specific rubrics from verified error patterns and using a generative Rubric Reward Model to assign calibrated scores.
2. **Method handle:** The pipeline covers source preparation, record generation, verification, filtering, and release.
3. **Data handle:** the rubric-reward dataset contains about 14.3K mathematical problem–rubric examples and centers on problem-specific rubrics and complete reasoning trajectories.
4. **Evidence anchor:** AIME2024 verified pass@1024 increases from 26.7% to 62.6%, while miracle steps decrease by 71%, with conclusions limited to the reported setup.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is most suitable for mathematical process rewards and Verified Pass@K evaluation; rubric omissions, reward-model bias, and uncertain transfer beyond mathematics must be checked before reuse.
