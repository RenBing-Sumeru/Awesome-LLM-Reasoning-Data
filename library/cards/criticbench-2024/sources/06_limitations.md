Correctness is only relative to the task-specific labels or metrics. A fluent critique can be wrong, incomplete, or persuasive without improving the corrected answer. Aggregate GQC correlation does not certify an individual critique as a reliable reward signal. Public reasoning datasets and generated responses can leak into later models. Domain metrics are heterogeneous, so mathematical, coding, commonsense, symbolic, and algorithmic scores should not be collapsed without checking their scoring contracts.
