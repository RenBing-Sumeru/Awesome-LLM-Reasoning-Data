The ACL abstract reports four main findings: GQC abilities correlate linearly, critique-focused training improves performance, correction effectiveness varies by task with logic-oriented tasks more amenable, inconsistencies decrease with model size, and stronger models tend to critique weaker ones better while weaker models can outperform stronger ones in self-critique. The benchmark scale evidence is 15 datasets, five domains, responses from three LLM families, and evaluation of 17 LLMs. Row-level evidence is the prompt, original response, critique, corrected answer, and scoring result. Evidence is bounded by prompt design, response sampling, metric validity, and any model-as-judge choices.
