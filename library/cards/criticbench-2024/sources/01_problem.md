Primary sources: ACL Anthology paper 2024.findings-acl.91, Findings of ACL 2024, pages 1552-1587, DOI 10.18653/v1/2024.findings-acl.91, plus arXiv:2402.14809 and the official project page. CriticBench asks how well LLMs can critique and correct reasoning, not only generate final answers. The evaluation surface is a generation-critique-correction record over five domains: mathematical, commonsense, symbolic, coding, and algorithmic reasoning. It belongs in the atlas as a judge/reward/meta-evaluation benchmark for feedback quality, not as a verified program-execution dataset. The evidence boundary is the authors' curated benchmark, prompts, reference labels/metrics, and reported model evaluations.
