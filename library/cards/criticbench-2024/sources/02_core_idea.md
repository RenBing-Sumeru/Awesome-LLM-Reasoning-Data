CriticBench contributes a benchmark for GQC reasoning: generation, critique, and correction. The ACL/arXiv abstract states that it compiles 15 datasets, incorporates responses from three LLM families, and evaluates 17 LLMs across five reasoning domains. The core mechanism is to present model-generated reasoning or answers, ask for critiques and corrections, and score the quality of the critique-correct pipeline with task-specific references or metrics. Closest comparisons are answer-only reasoning benchmarks and critique/self-improvement methods such as CRITIC/ToRA; CriticBench changes the object to the feedback step itself. The feedback contract is judgment-based and metric-based, not a mechanical proof checker.
