The Model Card reports July 2025 capability results under non-uniform evaluation budgets. HLE is 34.8%, IMO 2025 is 60.7% and described as bronze-medal grade, AIME 2025 is 99.2%, and LiveCodeBench v6 is 87.6%. The protocol states that comparisons exclude additional tool calls; most Gemini results on smaller benchmarks are averaged over trials. Crucially, IMO 2025 is **pass@1**, whereas the other matharena.ai results are **best-of-32**. The 60.7% IMO result and the 99.2% AIME result therefore must not be read as measurements at the same sampling budget, and best-of-32 is not evidence of the training rollout count or deployed branch budget.

The card reports detailed frontier-safety aggregates. Autonomous cyber offense results are 73/76 easy, 13/13 medium, and 3/13 hard; key-skills results are 6/8 easy, 17/28 medium, and 4/12 hard, with up to 50 attempts for Deep Think on hard key-skills tasks. RE-Bench reports an average normalized score of 0.96 over five of seven tasks, at least 20 attempts per task, and a 32-hour aggregate budget. Deceptive-alignment evaluation reports 3/11 situational-awareness and 1/4 stealth challenges solved. The card says the relevant cyber, ML R&D, and deceptive-alignment critical capability levels were not reached; CBRN Uplift Level 1 reached an early-warning threshold and required more evaluation.

These safety results have a version condition: FSF evaluation used a **preliminary Deep Think implementation** with a slightly different thinking prompt and serving configuration from the deployed user variant. Google states that the differences were not believed to materially change the assessment, but publishes no equivalence study. The August app model is also described as a faster variation of the model that spent hours reasoning for the IMO gold-level result. Reported manual inspection and Gemini 2.5 Pro triage found no invalid reward-hacked RE-Bench experiments, but sampling rates, transcripts, adjudication rules, and false-negative estimates are unavailable.

All numbers above are publisher-reported aggregates, not independent reproduction. They support claims about the evaluated configurations, not about training-data quality, reward validity, or the equivalence of app, preliminary FSF, and IMO variants.
