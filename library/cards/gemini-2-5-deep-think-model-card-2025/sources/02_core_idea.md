The report's central contribution to a reasoning-data atlas is a bounded disclosure ledger: Deep Think combines added reasoning and mathematics material, novel RL, and inference-time parallel hypothesis exploration, while leaving the record-level construction and reasoning reward undisclosed.

The concrete data surface includes multimodal instruction-response pairs, preference comparisons, tool-use records, multi-step problem-solving and theorem-proving examples, and curated mathematics solutions. At inference, the system generates multiple ideas, considers them in parallel, and may revise or combine them before returning an answer. Neither the branch representation nor its relationship to training data is disclosed.

The feedback contract has two evidence levels. The Deep Think Model Card directly names reinforcement learning from human and critic feedback but does not specify the labels, critic, rubric, aggregation, calibration, or reasoning-specific objective. The Gemini 2.5 family report separately describes RL*F using a Data Reward Model that amortizes human preferences and a prompted Critic that grades responses against predefined rubrics. That family-level mechanism may explain part of safety/helpfulness alignment, but the sources do not establish that it is the complete or exclusive reward for Deep Think's special reasoning RL. A critic can judge rubric compliance and candidate preference; it cannot by itself establish faithful internal reasoning, absence of contamination, or theorem correctness unless those properties are represented and calibrated.

The closest Atlas comparison is the broader `gemini-2-5-technical-report-2025` Card. That report covers Gemini 2.5 family training, reward, decontamination, and agentic capabilities; this Card isolates Deep Think's added reasoning data, parallel-thinking behavior, app/IMO/FSF version boundaries, and frontier-safety evidence. Its direction signal is the joint treatment of post-training data, feedback, test-time compute, and safety evaluation as separate auditable layers rather than a single model-performance claim.
