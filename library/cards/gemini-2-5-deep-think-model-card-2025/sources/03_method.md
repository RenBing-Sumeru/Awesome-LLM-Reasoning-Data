The disclosed pipeline can be reconstructed only at stage level:

1. **Inputs.** Broad public web documents, code, images, audio, and video support pre-training. Post-training adds vetted multimodal paired instructions and responses, human-preference data, tool-use data, more multi-step reasoning/problem-solving/theorem-proving material, and curated high-quality mathematics solutions.
2. **Processing and generation.** The report names deduplication, safety filtering, and quality filtering. It does not identify source manifests, generators or teachers, synthetic fractions, rules, thresholds, stage placement, or yields.
3. **Training.** Deep Think is an enhanced sparse-MoE Gemini 2.5-family model trained on Google TPUs using JAX and ML Pathways. Novel RL encourages extended reasoning paths. The RL algorithm, optimizer, schedule, rollout generation, model update rule, compute, and checkpoint lineage are unknown.
4. **Feedback.** The Model Card names human and critic feedback without defining a Deep Think reasoning reward or terminal predicate. Family-level RL*F uses a Data Reward Model and a rubric-prompted Critic, but its exact applicability to the special reasoning RL is unknown.
5. **Inference search.** Parallel thinking explores multiple hypotheses and may critique, revise, or combine them. Branch count, temperature, candidate distribution, critique schedule, aggregation, stopping rule, and hidden compute are not disclosed. The 1M context and 192K text-output limits are interface limits, not branch or rollout budgets.
6. **Outputs and uses.** The public output is a final text response, optionally with code execution or Google Search in the Gemini app. Disclosed uses include SFT, preference/reward learning, safety alignment, extended-reasoning RL, test-time parallel reasoning, capability evaluation, and frontier-risk audit. Product tools are not evidence of the training environment.

The card reports held-out assurance prompts and family-level decontamination methods, but no Deep Think-specific train/dev/test membership, overlap result, or audit of the added theorem and mathematics corpora. Reproduction would require pinned model/checkpoint identity, thinking prompt, serving configuration, branch and sampling settings, tools, reward versions, evaluation budgets, grader versions, and task snapshots. None of the training data, traces, weights, code, rewards, or evaluation records is released.
