Google DeepMind published the 20-page *Gemini 2.5 Deep Think - Model Card* on August 1, 2025. The document describes an enhanced Gemini 2.5-family reasoning model that uses parallel thinking and reinforcement learning, accepts text, image, audio, and video, supports a 1M-token context, and produces text outputs up to 192K tokens. It is a publisher model card rather than a peer-reviewed paper.

The Atlas question is not whether Deep Think obtains high benchmark scores. It is what data and feedback can actually be identified behind those scores. The card names broad pre-training sources; vetted multimodal instruction-response pairs, human-preference data, and tool-use data; additional multi-step reasoning, problem-solving, and theorem-proving material; and a curated corpus of high-quality mathematics solutions. It also names deduplication, safety filtering, quality filtering, novel RL, and reinforcement learning from human and critic feedback. It does not release records, schemas, quantities, mixture weights, source manifests, generators, reward definitions, branches, critiques, or item-level lineage.

A disclosed post-training record may therefore contain an instruction, multimodal context, response, human preference or tool use, and possibly multi-step reasoning, a theorem proof, or a mathematics solution. This is a class-level description, not a recoverable dataset schema. Parallel hypotheses and critiques are observable as product behavior, but the sources do not say whether they are stored, selected, distilled, or reused as training traces.

This Card belongs in `frontier_reports_data_disclosure_ledger` because it separates model-specific disclosure from family-level context and from inference behavior. It does not treat product access, a 192K output ceiling, or benchmark scores as evidence of a reusable data release. L4 is supported by the complete official model card, the official model-card index and launch post, and the official Gemini 2.5 family report; the missing data, reward, budget, and version facts remain explicit.
