General reward models often reward sanitized hate-speech explanations, even when mentioning a stereotype or harmful wording is necessary to explain why the source text is offensive. This makes the score disagree with explanatory fidelity and can train or select evasive explanations.

HARM studies this mismatch using controlled natural-language explanations with progressively richer human context, then builds a hate-aware reward model that reweights interpretable reward signals for this domain.
