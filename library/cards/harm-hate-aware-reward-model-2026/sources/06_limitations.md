The tier ordering is a directional proxy for explanatory fidelity, not a universal ranking rule. More explicit offensive language can be warranted in one context and harmful or inaccurate in another; deployment therefore needs human escalation and source-context checks.

The data is grounded in SBIC and English hate-speech explanations. Results may not transfer to other languages, policies, or moderation definitions. Before training on the release, verify dataset terms and audit whether the learned gate amplifies protected-group stereotypes.
