The lineage result is broad but paper-mediated. The paper reports 1,450,827 canonical instances, 20 atomic-source labels, more than 99.7% attributed, and 36,148 high-confidence leaked instances against 14 math benchmarks. The repository supplies canonicalizers and exact and semantic matching scripts, but not the final lineage dictionary, occurrence lists, source assignments, embeddings, match matrices, or author decisions needed to recompute the aggregates independently.

The downstream RLVR results support a within-study comparison. Excluding Math500 from Average*, DAPO++ scores 15.7 versus 15.0 for decontaminated DAPO with Qwen3-1.7B and 29.6 versus 29.3 with Qwen3-8B. Decontamination ablations raise Average* from 14.1 to 14.7 for DeepScaleR-1.7B, 14.6 to 15.0 for DAPO-1.7B, 24.4 to 26.1 for DeepScaleR-8B, and 29.4 to 29.6 for DAPO-8B. These results do not establish that each released row is correct, uncontaminated, licensed, or causally useful.

Q is evaluated on six datasets. Its Spearman correlation with Average* is 0.60 at 1.7B and 0.94 at 8B, while cross-dataset Average* standard deviation is 0.59 and 2.17. Q uses Math500-derived features, and Math500 also selects checkpoints. The stronger 8B association is therefore evidence for a scale- and benchmark-conditioned ranking proxy, not universal data quality or an independent verifier.

The released file can be checked directly. At commit d048f53f7b7ab53b2b82f5e8d5f6323333e5e0c4, DAPO_Plus.parquet is 3,507,245 bytes with SHA-256 3e6ecc481b4e9f51a53d210b27bff14fd9f12744ee88f5a40910f9ef0d68a2ab, 17,000 rows, 5 columns, 17,000 unique prompts and indices, one train split, one data_source value, one ability value, and 2,392 distinct ground-truth strings. It has no record-level lineage or SCA field. An otherwise English prompt contains a stray Chinese instruction marker; this is a mixed-language or text-noise observation, not evidence of byte-level encoding corruption.

The code release is not an end-to-end reproduction package. Benchmark_Scoring/analyze_features.py requires an unreleased stage4_final.json and stops after 800,000 entries; compute_scores.py hard-codes feature and Math500 summaries. Paper Table 9 and the GRPO scripts also disagree on steps, mini-batch, KL, batch size, and evaluation settings. File existence and benchmark movement therefore do not verify the claimed selection history.
