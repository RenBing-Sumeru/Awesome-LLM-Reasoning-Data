ATLAS starts from public RLVR datasets and documented predecessors. Because schemas and appended instructions differ, the authors inspect 30–50 records per dataset and manually define extraction into final question-answer pairs. A 40-character SHA-1 prompt key supports chronological exact matching, and the lineage dictionary stores every observed occurrence. Unmatched prompts are embedded with Sentence-BERT, retrieved by cosine similarity, and reviewed case by case. When the unmatched set remains too large, candidate authoritative sources are added and earlier stages rerun. The stopping threshold is 1%; the final residue is labeled unknown. The paper reports 1,450,827 records, 20 atomic-source labels, and fewer than 1% unknown.

Leakage auditing compares the RLVR universe with 14 math evaluation benchmarks. The reported 36,148 high-confidence matches emphasize similarity at or above 0.90, while the paper notes transformed leakage near 0.80. DAPO++ removes detected leakage from DAPO-Math-17k and randomly draws non-MCQ, SCA-01-style learnable replacements from the Stack Exchange atomic source. The seed, eligible-pool revision, exact removed and inserted counts, rejected candidates, and replacement mapping are unknown.

For SCA, a Qwen3 base model and a source-specific GRPO checkpoint form a counterfactual pair. Correctness under the two checkpoints produces 00, 01, 10, or 11 labels. Q combines the resulting learnability feature with answer consistency, MCQ prevalence, cross-dataset reuse, source or domain diversity, confirmed leakage, and Math500 Mean@4 and Pass@4 gains. Because Math500 also selects checkpoints, the score is partly coupled to its validation surface.

RL training uses Qwen3-1.7B-Base and Qwen3-8B-Base, GRPO on VERL, 8 online rollouts per prompt, temperature 1.0, and Math-Verify scalar reward. The paper reports learning rate 1e-6, 500 steps, batch 128, mini-batch 64, KL 0.0, clip 0.2, a 4,096-token prompt limit, and a 16,384-token response limit. Released scripts instead use 510 steps, mini-batch 16, and KL 0.001; the 8B script uses batch 32. Paper-aligned evaluation uses temperature 0.6, seed 0, top_p 0.95, and top_k 20, while training-script validation uses top_p 0.7 and top_k 50.

The released DAPO_Plus.parquet contains 17,000 unique train rows with five top-level fields: data_source, prompt, ability, reward_model, and extra_info. Every row has data_source=DataLineage, ability=math, one user-role prompt, rule-style ground truth, and a dapo-prefixed unique index. It stores no online rollout, atomic source, predecessor, transformation, SCA label, match score, retained-versus-replacement status, selection probability, source URL, or license.
