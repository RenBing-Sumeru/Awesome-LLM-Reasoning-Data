Correctness is only correctness under the dataset's official answer and metric. A high score does not certify safe legal advice, legal reasoning transparency, or ability to handle updated statutes, local practice, or litigation strategy.

The benchmark is centered on Chinese legal materials and should not be generalized to other jurisdictions without re-curation. Task files can mix formats, so parser failure and answer-normalization policy can affect results.

The official GitHub repository carries an Apache-2.0 license, but legal source materials and downstream redistribution obligations should still be inspected at task level. Public task release creates contamination risk for future model evaluations.
