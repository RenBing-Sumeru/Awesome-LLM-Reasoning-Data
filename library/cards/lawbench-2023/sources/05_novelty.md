The prior baseline is general legal QA or professional-exam subsets that under-specify the cognitive level being tested. LawBench changes the evaluation design by organizing Chinese legal tasks into memorization, understanding, and application, then attaching standard prompts and OpenCompass-compatible scoring.

The direction signal is jurisdiction- and language-specific benchmark construction with a controlled task taxonomy. The quality signal is the public project page, code/data repository, and task-level organization that allow auditors to inspect prompts and answers.

What is not new: answer-key benchmarks, task-level metrics, and OpenCompass-style model evaluation are existing infrastructure. Before reuse, inspect source legal materials, data license, task balance, answer parser, prompt language, law-version currency, and any overlap with model training corpora.
