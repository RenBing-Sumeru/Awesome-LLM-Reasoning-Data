LawBench asks how well large language models handle Chinese legal knowledge, legal understanding, and legal application tasks. The primary source is the September 2023 arXiv preprint and the official OpenCompass LawBench project/repository.

The evaluation surface is a benchmark suite of legal tasks grouped into three cognitive levels. A sample can be a multiple-choice question, classification item, extraction prompt, entailment-style judgment, reading-comprehension task, or legal-application query, with an official answer or task-specific metric.

The atlas boundary is legal-domain benchmark work, not a legal-advice system, not a bilingual law corpus, and not a reward model. Reuse must preserve jurisdiction/language scope, prompt template, task split, evaluator script, and whether the tested model was allowed retrieval or tool use.
