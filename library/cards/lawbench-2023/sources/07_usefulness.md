Use LawBench as a blueprint for legal benchmark schemas that separate knowledge recall, text understanding, and applied legal reasoning. The useful fields are task name, cognitive level, jurisdiction/language, prompt template, source text, target answer, metric, parser, and repository commit.

It is also a contrast case for LegalBench: LawBench emphasizes Chinese law and OpenCompass evaluation, while LegalBench emphasizes a collaboratively built broad legal-task suite. Together they help separate domain taxonomy from language/jurisdiction coverage.

For atlas work, LawBench is most useful as an answer-level evaluation surface and audit checklist for legal benchmark contamination, prompt sensitivity, and scoring-script dependency.
