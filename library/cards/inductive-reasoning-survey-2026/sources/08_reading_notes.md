Read the three improvement categories first, then the benchmark summary and the sandbox-based evaluation proposal. Keep two linked questions in mind: what exactly is observed before the model generalizes, and which unseen cases test that generalization? For every primary paper mentioned, note the intervention point, the observation set, and the condition under which an answer is counted as a successful generalization. The discussion of simple architectures and data is useful for separating a capability claim from a claim about a particular prompting style or inference budget. Do not read the taxonomy as a recipe that guarantees improvement. Read it as a comparison tool: it tells you which assumptions changed, which comparisons are fair, and what further detail must be checked in the cited work before drawing a conclusion about inductive ability.
