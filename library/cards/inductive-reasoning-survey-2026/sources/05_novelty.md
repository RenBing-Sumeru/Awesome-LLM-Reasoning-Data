The authors present this as the first comprehensive survey dedicated to inductive reasoning for LLMs. Its contribution is not a new optimizer, model, or benchmark release. Instead, it provides a shared map that places post-training, exploration, data augmentation, benchmarks, and possible sources of inductive ability in one account. This is more than a list of papers: it makes the intervention point explicit. Two studies can both claim better generalization while changing different parts of the system, using different observations, or asking different forms of extrapolation. The survey gives readers language for separating those claims before comparing their scores. Its sandbox-based evaluation perspective also shifts attention from whether an answer sounds rule-like to whether the proposed rule survives controlled tests beyond the examples used to infer it.
