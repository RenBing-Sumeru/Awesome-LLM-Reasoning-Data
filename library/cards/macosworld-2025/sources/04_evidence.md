All figures below are author-reported and were not independently reproduced. The comparisons use the paper's common 171-task multilingual slice, not every language-specific task. With the configurations in §5.1 and Appendix G, Table 3 reports average success rates of 37.1% for Claude CUA, 33.8% for OpenAI CUA, 18.5% for Gemini 2.5 Pro, 5.3% for GPT-4o, 4.8% for UI-TARS 7B DPO, and 1.1% for ShowUI 2B. The multi-app category average across agents is 3.7%, identifying long-horizon cross-application interaction as a difficult evaluated condition rather than proving that task data are intrinsically high quality (Paper Table 3, pp. 6–7).

Holding the six-agent evaluation setup to the common slice, Table 4 reports mean success rates of 19.3% in English, 17.7% in Russian, 17.2% in Chinese, 15.8% in Japanese, and 13.7% in Arabic. The paper describes Arabic as 28.8% below English and links part of the gap to mirrored layouts. This comparison is conditioned on the translated instructions, localized AMIs, model-specific prompts/adapters, and the exact app versions; it does not isolate translation quality from UI grounding or planning effects (Paper Table 4 and §5.2, p. 8).

For the 29 English safety tasks, Table 5 reports distracted rates of 72.4% for Claude CUA, 69.0% for OpenAI CUA, 58.6% for UI-TARS, 41.4% for ShowUI, 17.2% for Gemini, and 0% for GPT-4o. GPT-4o's 0% coincides with 100% unhandled, and Gemini has 79.3% unhandled, so “not distracted” cannot be read as successful defense unless `gold`, `distracted`, and unhandled are analyzed separately (Paper Table 5 and §5.4, p. 8).

Failure analysis reports planning/action-format problems for open-source agents, imprecise grounding for general VLMs, excessive step use for proprietary CUAs, and Arabic mirrored-layout failures (§5.3; Appendix E). These observations support the benchmark's diagnostic use. They do not validate evaluator false-positive/false-negative rates, establish decontamination, certify rights, or turn the released task harness into a complete trajectory dataset.
