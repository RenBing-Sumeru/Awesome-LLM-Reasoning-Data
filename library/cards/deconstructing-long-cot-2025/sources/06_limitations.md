Rule-based final-answer checks do not validate intermediate mathematics, while LLM parsing, classification, and clustering introduce judge error that lexical source-preservation thresholds cannot detect. The paper reports three-run experiments but does not expose row-level variance, retained indices, rejected traces, or a complete final-corpus manifest. The repository is not an end-to-end frozen pipeline: users must supply several LLM inference stages, model revisions and service behavior are not fully pinned, and defaults visible in individual scripts do not make the executed configuration unambiguous. The Step 1 object is an LFS input file, not the final optimized dataset; code/data licenses, source-to-output lineage, splits, and decontamination remain unknown. The study also leaves substantially different model designs and downstream reinforcement-learning use untested.
