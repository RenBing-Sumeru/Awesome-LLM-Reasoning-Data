The evidence is an ablation over the paper's own construction pipeline, not proof that the data or judges are intrinsically high quality. For QwQ data with Qwen2.5-14B, the reported baseline scores on AIME2024/MATH500/GSM8K are 33.3/85.3/96.2, while removing all redundant approaches yields 40.0/87.7/96.8; for R1 data with the same student, the corresponding comparison is 46.7/91.7/97.6 versus 53.3/91.4/96.8, so the effect is not uniformly positive across benchmarks. After maximal redundancy reduction, also deleting erroneous approaches lowers the QwQ-student scores from 40.0/87.7/96.8 to 33.3/84.5/96.3 and the R1-student scores from 53.3/91.4/96.8 to 40.0/88.2/93.8, supporting the narrower interpretation that later-corrected or diverse branches can be useful in the tested setup. The arXiv record, official BibTeX, SODE code, README, scripts, and Step 1 LFS input are accessible; a final optimized corpus is not.
