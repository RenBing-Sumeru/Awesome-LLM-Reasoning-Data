Long-CoT distillation can transfer mathematical reasoning to smaller models, but the useful training object is not simply “a longer answer”: teacher traces mix repeated approaches, abandoned branches, verification, corrected mistakes, and final conclusions, and their transfer can depend on student-model homology. This 2025 arXiv paper asks which structural parts should be preserved or removed before SFT. For this atlas category, the decision boundary is explicit: DLCoT is an offline trace-construction and ablation study over QwQ-32B-Preview and DeepSeek-R1 outputs, not a test-time search method and not evidence that a complete optimized corpus has been released. The auditable objects are the original teacher trace, its parsed approach/verification units, cluster and label decisions, the reconstructed trace, and the terminal final-answer check.
