Large-model post-training is often framed through optimizers, while the actual bottleneck is frequently the cost, quality, and reuse of its data. The official ACL 2025 record at https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1493/ asks how to make post-training data-efficient under expensive annotation and diminishing returns from scale. This Card supplies a vocabulary for data selection and generation; it does not document a particular instruction or reasoning-data release. Its evaluation surface is the lifecycle of a training-data record, from acquisition and filtering to synthetic generation and compression.
