The reconstruction pipeline has three task-specific front ends and one shared verifier objective.

- **Algorithmic inputs and candidates.** For Last Letter Concatenation, the authors sample 350 queries at each list length 2, 3, and 4 and generate 128 Gemma-2B attempts per query, yielding about 50K records after deduplication; evaluation uses length 6. For Word Sorting, they generate 4,096 queries at each training length and 64 Gemma-2B attempts per query, yielding about 100K records after deduplication and invalid-response filtering; evaluation uses length 5. Programmatic procedures provide correctness labels and verification rationales. (Appendix A)
- **GSM8K inputs and candidates.** The original split is about 7.2K training, 128 validation, and 1.3K test problems. Gemini 1.0 Pro generates 50 candidate solutions for each training problem; at most 16 correct and 16 incorrect candidates are randomly retained. Test evaluation uses 16 candidates per problem. The same Gemini 1.0 Pro receives the question, candidate solution, and another solution that reaches the correct answer, then writes a stepwise verification rationale. The correct reference is privileged synthesis information and is removed from verifier fine-tuning and deployment inputs. A generated rationale is retained only if its final verdict matches the candidate's known label. (Section 3.3; Appendix A, Table A.2)
- **Balance and serialization.** Training always mixes 50% correct and 50% incorrect candidate pairs. Direct targets are `Yes` or `No`; CoT targets are the full verification rationale followed by the final correctness question and verdict. The official GSM8K JSONL release stores this material in `inputs` and `targets`, plus `question_id` and `model_output_id`; correct/incorrect and rationale/direct-target families are packaged separately.
- **Training.** Gemma-2B is used for algorithmic verifiers; Gemma 2B, 7B, and Gemma-2 9B are used for GSM8K. The loss is verification next-token SFT plus lambda times SFT on correct-solution generation, with lambda=1/3 for algorithmic tasks and 1/4 for GSM8K. Appendix B reports a learning-rate sweep of 2e-6, 1e-6, and 5e-7, usually selecting 2e-6; weight decay 1e-2; no dropout; Adam with decoupled weight decay; gradient clipping 1.0; 1,000 warmup steps; batch size 64; 300K steps; and a 200K cosine-decay period.
- **Inference and use.** Direct GenRM returns `p(Yes)` for candidate ranking. GenRM-CoT samples verification rationales and returns the average conditional `Yes` probability, normally over 32 samples. A fixed generator supplies the same candidate set to competing verifiers, and the maximum-scoring candidate is selected for Best-of-N evaluation. MATH500 and MMLU mathematics measure transfer from GSM8K-trained verifiers; they are not reported as training sources.

Reproduction should pin the ICLR/arXiv version, task split, generator and verifier model versions, prompt templates, candidate/rationale sample counts, lambda, optimizer settings, rationale filter, and the data-repository commit. Training and rationale-generation temperatures, random seeds, broader decontamination, and hardware are not disclosed. The official repository at commit `083c3a24db0560a31c9ec70675d566671b386e22` confirms the GSM8K records; no official implementation or trained verifier checkpoints were confirmed.
