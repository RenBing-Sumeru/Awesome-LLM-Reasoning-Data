The paper itself states that model-generated GSM8K rationales can contain errors and shows that unguided rationale synthesis is weak. Reference guidance improves the teacher's final verdict, but it also gives the teacher a correct solution that the trained verifier will not see at test time. A fluent target can therefore teach comparison-to-reference patterns rather than independent verification. Joint generation and verification is not monotonically helpful either: the generation-mixture ablation shows that excessive solution-generation weight reduces verification performance.

Several verifier errors follow from the feedback contract. A false positive can occur when a wrong derivation reaches the right final answer, when a plausible critique misses the first invalid step, or when the candidate uses a style favored by the verifier. A false negative can occur for a correct alternative derivation, unfamiliar formatting, or a synthetic critique that propagates a teacher error. These are curator inferences grounded in the answer-level label and model-mediated rationale. The paper does not report adversarial verifier gaming, cross-domain calibration, or a guarantee that averaging 32 samples removes systematic error; all votes share a model, prompt, and training distribution.

The release has concrete audit gaps. Correct and incorrect candidates are both present, but rationales with incorrect final verification decisions are filtered out and rejection logs or retention rates are absent. The repository README explicitly mentions MIT licensing for GSM8K data, while the generated-critique license scope is not separately stated. There is no reported decontamination beyond stated splits, deduplication, and invalid-response filtering. No official implementation, trained verifier checkpoints, immutable tagged release, checksums, complete per-file manifest, training temperature, rationale-generation temperature, random seeds, or hardware configuration was confirmed.

Experimental scope is limited to two string-manipulation tasks, GSM8K, and transfer to MATH500 and MMLU mathematics. That evidence does not establish robustness for code, open-ended judgment, safety, factuality, or interactive environments, which the conclusion names as future work. Model, prompt, and repository version drift can change both rationale text and `Yes` probabilities; without pinned artifacts, a later reproduction may silently implement a different verifier.
