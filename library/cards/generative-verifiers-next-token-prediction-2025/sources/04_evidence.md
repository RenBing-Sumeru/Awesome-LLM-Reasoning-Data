The paper evaluates verifiers with fixed candidate generators, so the primary metric is the percentage of problems solved after Best-of-N selection rather than candidate-generation pass rate. In Figure 1, Gemma-2B verifier results averaged over Last Letter and Word Sorting are reported at Best-of-32, while a Gemma-2 9B verifier trained on GSM8K is evaluated at Best-of-16 on GSM8K and Best-of-32 on MATH500. The headline comparisons go from 5.0% to 45.3% on the algorithmic average, 73.0% to 93.4% on GSM8K, and 28.0% to 44.6% for GSM-trained transfer to MATH500. The candidate solutions are held fixed across verifiers. (Figure 1)

The comparisons include discriminative outcome RMs, DPO verifiers, self-consistency, and Gemini 1.0 Pro as LLM-as-a-Judge. Direct GenRM is comparable to or slightly better than the discriminative RM and stronger than DPO in Figure C.1; GenRM-CoT with majority voting supplies the larger gain. On four MMLU math subsets, Table C.1 reports Disc-RM versus GenRM-CoT as 90.6% versus 91.1% for elementary mathematics, 74.8% versus 76.1% for high-school mathematics, 53.0% versus 56.1% for college mathematics, and 50.0% versus 53.5% for abstract algebra.

The ablations narrow the causal claim. Joint solution-generation SFT improves verification in the reported default range, but Figure C.2 shows that too much generation mixture can reduce verifier performance; lambda=1/4 works best in the shown GSM8K setting. Reference-guided rationale generation is substantially stronger than unguided synthesis, and the paper reports gains from additional rationale samples, additional training solutions, and larger Gemma verifier models. It also explicitly states that synthetic GSM8K rationales may contain errors.

These are author-reported results, not an independent reproduction. They support the tested verifier architecture and sampling procedure on string manipulation and mathematics. They do not demonstrate that every released critique is correct, that the `Yes` score is calibrated across domains, or that 32 votes are independent. Artifact inspection confirms that the GSM8K training objects exist, but benchmark performance is not evidence of license completeness, decontamination, or per-record data quality.
