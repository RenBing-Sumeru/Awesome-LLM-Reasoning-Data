For `rollout_search_test_time_trace_data`, AlphaProof supplies a precise schema for what a formal-search record should preserve: formal statement and provenance, Lean/Mathlib version, tactic state, sampled tactic, validity and execution result, resulting subgoals, per-step reward, tree visits and values, adaptive budget, proof/disproof/timeout outcome, replay inclusion, and final independent verification.

The TTRL loop is especially relevant to post-training data design because it turns generated variants and verified search experience into problem-specific adaptation at inference time. Reuse or replication requires releasing the curriculum, accepted and rejected attempts, tree and matchmaker logs, replay sampling, environment pins, model checkpoints, and compute accounting. Benchmark results alone are method evidence, not trace-quality proof.
