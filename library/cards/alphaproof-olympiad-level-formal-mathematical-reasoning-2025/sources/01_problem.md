AlphaProof targets elite formal mathematics in Lean, where an agent must choose tactics from a large open-ended action space and solve all generated subgoals under a finite compute budget. For reasoning-data curation, the relevant object is not only a final verified proof: it is the sequence of pretty-printed tactic states, sampled tactics, Lean transitions, proof/disproof/timeout outcomes, tree-search statistics, and replay-selection decisions.

The work addresses two data bottlenecks. Human-written Lean proofs are too small for broad RL curricula, so a Gemini-based auto-formalizer expands informal problems into formal statements. Hard target problems then receive problem-specific test-time reinforcement learning (TTRL) over generated variants. The paper documents this recipe, but the main synthetic curricula, replay buffer, failed search attempts, and trained model are not released as an open data package.
