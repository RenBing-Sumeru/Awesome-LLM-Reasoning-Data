The [Nature article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09833-y) directly documents the Lean state/action/reward contract, model and tree search, 300K-pair SFT set, approximately 80M-statement main-RL curriculum, replay mixture, TTRL variant curriculum, and independent proof verification. Its data-availability statement links corrected miniF2F and formal-imo benchmarks and a pinned PutnamBench source; supplementary material includes algorithm pseudocode and hyperparameters. The synthetic curricula and RL proof replay are described as system-generated, not released.

At IMO 2024, AlphaProof solved three of five non-geometry problems after expert formalization and multi-day TTRL; AlphaGeometry 2 solved the geometry problem, giving the combined system 28/42. This supports the effectiveness of the documented method under substantial compute. It does not prove that every auto-formalization is faithful to its informal source or that the hidden replay buffer is reusable, representative, or contamination-free.
