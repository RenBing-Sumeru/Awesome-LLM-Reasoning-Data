The approximately 80M formal statements need only be valid Lean problems; the paper explicitly notes that fidelity to the original informal statement is not required for use as RL training. The source mixture for the one million informal problems, duplicates, rights, formalization-error distribution, and contamination controls are not fully exposed. Failed attempts are filtered from learning, obscuring candidate attrition and failure structure.

The main synthetic curriculum, TTRL variants, replay buffer, actor logs, trained weights, matchmaker statistics, and full distributed environment are not released. Although pseudocode and hyperparameters are available, the bespoke training scale is beyond most academic groups, and TTRL takes multiple days on the hardest problems. Success is concentrated in competition mathematics within a fixed library; alternative proof diversity, transfer to research mathematics, and replay reproducibility remain open.
