Lean verification, tactic prediction, neural tree search, expert iteration, and auto-formalization all predate AlphaProof. The distinctive combination is scale and routing: tens of millions of valid formal statements form a broad AlphaZero-like RL curriculum, successful proof/disproof experience updates both policy and value, and hard targets trigger a separate TTRL loop over generated problem variants.

For the rollout/search track, the important novelty is that inference compute can create a temporary, target-specific training dataset rather than merely sample more final answers. The paper also makes the selector visible: Lean validates transitions and final proof objects, while the learner admits successful proofs/disproofs and filters failed attempts. This is a direction signal for auditable test-time adaptation, not an open trace release.
