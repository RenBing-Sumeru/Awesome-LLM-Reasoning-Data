Training begins with next-token pretraining on about 300B tokens of code and mathematical text, followed by SFT on about 300K Mathlib state–tactic pairs. A Gemini-based formalization model, refined with human and synthetic data, auto-formalizes roughly one million informal problems into about 80 million valid Lean statements. A matchmaker assigns statements, adaptive budgets, and prove/disprove roles to distributed actors; proof-network-guided AND–OR tree search samples and validates tactics in Lean.

Proofs, disproofs, and timeouts provide environment feedback. The learner samples 10% Mathlib SFT pairs and 90% actor-generated proof/disproof replay, training the policy by tactic cross-entropy and the value head on returns; failed attempts are excluded. For TTRL, a variant generator builds relevant formal variants and focused RL adapts the prover to the target. Final proofs are independently checked with the Lean command-line tool and restricted to three accepted built-in axioms. Exact released replay contents are unavailable.
