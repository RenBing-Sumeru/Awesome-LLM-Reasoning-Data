ChemBench asks how far general LLMs can be trusted on chemistry knowledge and reasoning tasks, using the arXiv paper submitted April 1, 2024 and revised November 1, 2024 as the primary source. The gap is the lack of a systematic, chemistry-specific benchmark that compares model answers with chemist expertise rather than only with broad academic QA. The data object is a curated chemistry question-answer item with topic metadata, answer key or scoring rule, model output, and report-level score. This card is an evaluation surface, not a chemistry tutoring corpus or a safety approval. Its atlas value is the domain-expert feedback contract and the explicit audit issue that high average scores can coexist with basic failures and overconfident predictions.
