The contribution is ChemBench, an automated framework and benchmark suite for evaluating chemical knowledge and reasoning in LLMs against chemist performance. The mechanism is to curate more than 2,700 chemistry question-answer pairs, group them by topic, run model-specific prompters, and save topic-level reports. The feedback contract is programmatic scoring against answer keys or task-specific metrics in the ChemBench package. Closest comparisons are general exams such as MMLU-style chemistry subsets and ad hoc chemistry prompts without expert calibration. The direction label is domain expert benchmark with answer-level feedback and calibration/audit risk.
