DeepDistill's useful distinction is the joint data object: sourced prompts, capability-spanning teachers, repeated attempts, heterogeneous verification, and teacher-specific success rates at unusually large claimed scale. Instead of asking one model for a subjective difficulty score, it estimates difficulty from observed response outcomes and uses variation across repeated DeepSeek-R1 attempts to locate unstable examples. The two-stage selection recipe then moves from a broad, correctness-filtered mixture to a smaller high-variation annealing set. This is a construction-and-selection contribution; it is not a domain-neutral verifier and does not establish that CV is intrinsic difficulty.
