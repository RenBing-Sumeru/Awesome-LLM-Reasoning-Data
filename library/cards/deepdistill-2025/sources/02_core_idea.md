The intended corpus contains about 3.34 million unique queries and roughly 40 million generated responses. DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, and DeepSeek-R1 each produce four attempts per query. Every response carries the question and answer, source and teacher identifiers, category, available ground truth/tests/constraints, three teacher pass rates, its own `verify_score`, PPL, and model name.

Difficulty is represented by observed success rather than a free-form difficulty label. Category-specific verification turns each of four attempts into a pass indicator; their mean is a teacher-specific pass rate. For the DeepSeek-R1 attempts used in the paper's experiments, the coefficient of variation (CV) of verification scores identifies unstable questions. Selection then combines a correctness threshold with CV, yielding a data object that supports both SFT and audits of teacher capability, verifier behavior, and curriculum rules.
