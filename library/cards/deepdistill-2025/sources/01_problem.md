Reasoning SFT needs more than a large pile of solutions. Prompts vary in domain and difficulty, teachers vary in capability, and a scalar from one judge can confound intrinsic difficulty with evaluator preference. Training indiscriminately on easy, unsolved, or noisy items also spends compute without establishing that the selected traces are learnable and correct. DeepDistill asks how to construct a large mixed-domain reasoning corpus whose records retain prompt provenance, repeated teacher attempts, category-specific feedback, and difficulty signals that can drive data selection for base-model SFT.
