Queries are aggregated from named public corpora covering mathematics, code, science, instruction following, multi-turn dialogue, and general reasoning. Exact duplicates, empty or incomplete prompts, URLs, tables, and prompts above an undisclosed Unicode-ratio threshold are removed. For AIME2024, the authors apply exact/near matching and remove queries whose bge-m3 similarity exceeds 0.9. This leaves about 3.34 million queries. Three teachers then generate four responses apiece; the prompts and decoding settings are not reported.

Feedback depends on category. Math first uses Math-Verify and sends failures to Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct for a second binary decision. Code runs the first ten available public tests, or all tests when fewer exist; Python supports stdin/output and assertions, while C++ uses stdin/output. Science answers are scored from 0 to 5 by Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct. Instruction-following uses ifeval, including constraints generated by Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct. Multi-turn and other examples receive coherence, correctness, and helpfulness scores from Decision-Tree-Reward-Llama-3.1-8B, averaged after normalization. A response passes above 0.99 for math/code/instruction following, above 4.99 for science, or above 0.7 for multi-turn/other. Four binary outcomes are averaged into a pass rate.

Quality filters discard responses with PPL above 20, repeated 20-token strings occurring more than 20 times, missing think or answer spans, and malformed multi-turn records. Stage I first requires at least one passing response. High-CV queries keep only passing responses; low-CV math/code/science/instruction-following queries are dropped, while 50% of low-CV multi-turn/other queries are sampled. The paper reports 5 million Stage-I samples. Stage II keeps nearly 200K high-CV, passing examples and randomly chooses one response per query. Category-specific CV thresholds are not disclosed.

Qwen2.5-32B and Qwen2.5-72B are fine-tuned in Stage I for one epoch at learning rate 8e-5, with 32K packing, global batch 64, cosine decay, and 5% warmup. Stage II starts from the 32B checkpoint, removes packing, keeps a 32K limit, and trains two epochs at 8e-6. Although all three teacher families supply metadata, the reported training and analysis use only DeepSeek-R1 responses.
