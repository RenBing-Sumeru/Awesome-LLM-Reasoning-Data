Within the data-construction/open-release track, this card is most useful as a worked example of a large trace-plus-feedback release. Builders can study difficulty-aware SFT, compare teacher capability curves, construct curricula from pass rates, or audit where programmatic and learned verification disagree. The explicit source, teacher, category, score, and pass-rate fields make it possible to preserve lineage while resampling the corpus.

Reuse should start from a pinned Hugging Face revision and the files actually accessible there, not the 40M headline alone. Users should group by unique question before splitting, audit `ground_truth` and public tests, interpret each category's score separately, and retain upstream source identifiers for license review. DPO and GRPO are mentioned as possible downstream applications, but the paper demonstrates SFT only; they should remain hypotheses rather than recorded training evidence.
