The Alternative Annotator Test (alt-test) turns “an LLM can replace human annotators” into a task-specific statistical procedure using a modest human-annotated subset. The paper also introduces an interpretable comparison measure for LLM annotators and judges. It demonstrates the procedure on ten language and vision-language datasets, six LLMs, and four prompting techniques. The feedback contract is comparison to human annotation under the studied protocol; passing supports the stated substitution decision, not correctness on every task. The direction label is LLM-as-a-judge reliability. Compare it with generic model-human agreement reports, rubric benchmarks, and direct human-preference collection.
