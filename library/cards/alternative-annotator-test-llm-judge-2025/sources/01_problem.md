LLM-generated annotations are used in evaluation and research, but correlation with humans alone does not establish that a particular model may replace annotators for a particular task. The primary source is the ACL 2025 paper at https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.782/. Its focus is judge and annotation reliability: it supplies a test for a substitution claim, rather than a reusable preference dataset or a general reward model. The evaluation surface is an item with multiple human annotations, an LLM annotation produced under a stated prompt, and a statistical comparison. The decision itself must still be rerun for the intended task and annotator population.
