Inputs are a task with human annotations, a candidate LLM annotator or judge, and a stated prompting setup. The pipeline takes a modest annotated subset, compares LLM and human annotation behavior with alt-test and the paper's comparison measure, and then reports whether the substitution claim is statistically supported under that setup. Outputs are task-specific comparison results rather than a new post-training corpus. The human annotations are the reference surface; the LLM is the candidate judge, and the test is the evaluation mechanism. This is for evaluation and audit, not SFT or reward-model training. Reproduction requires the ACL paper's task, model, prompt, human-reference, and statistical-test details; transfer to another task, model, or prompt requires a new test.
