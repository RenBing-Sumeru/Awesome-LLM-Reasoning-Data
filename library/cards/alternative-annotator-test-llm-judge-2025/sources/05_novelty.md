Prior LLM-judge practice commonly reports agreement or correlation with humans. The change here is to formulate annotator replacement as a statistical decision backed by a human-annotated subset and an interpretable comparison measure. The direction signal is that a judge label should carry an explicit substitution test, not merely a high aggregate score. The paper does not make LLM judges universally interchangeable with human annotators. Before reuse, inspect the annotation type, reference-annotator setup, prompting condition, model version, and the test's assumptions.
