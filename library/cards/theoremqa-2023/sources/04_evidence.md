The paper and artifacts describe 800 question-answer pairs grounded in 354 theorems across mathematics, physics, electrical engineering, computer science, and finance, with 51 multimodal questions involving diagrams. The official repository and Hugging Face dataset provide public artifacts for reproducing the benchmark surface.

The decisive instance-level evidence is an extracted final answer matching the reference answer under the answer-type-specific evaluator. The evidence boundary is answer extraction: a model can reason correctly but format the final answer in a way the evaluator misses, and a model can sometimes reach the right closed-form answer without demonstrating theorem understanding. Scores also depend on prompt format, Program-of-Thought tooling, evaluator version, and dataset revision.
