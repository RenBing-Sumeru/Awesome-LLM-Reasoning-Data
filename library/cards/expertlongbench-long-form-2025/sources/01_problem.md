Expert tasks often require legal, medical, educational, or materials reports exceeding 5,000 tokens. Reference similarity cannot handle many valid formulations or localise omissions, factual errors, and domain-requirement violations, while holistic LLM judges can be biased by length and fluency.

ExpertLongBench centres domain-expert-designed or validated rubrics and introduces CLEAR, which converts model and reference outputs into structured checklists for item-wise comparison.
