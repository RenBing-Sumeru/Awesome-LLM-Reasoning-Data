1. **Select tasks:** Eleven tasks requiring long-form deliverables are selected from law, medicine, education, materials, and other applications.

2. **Create rubrics and references:** Domain experts design or validate requirements and provide reference outputs and context materials.

3. **Extract checklists:** CLEAR extracts rubric-aligned facts and requirements separately from model and reference outputs.

4. **Compare and score:** Checklist correctness is compared item by item and aggregated as F1; extractor, comparator, and task versions must be fixed.
