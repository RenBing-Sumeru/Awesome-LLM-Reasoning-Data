Labels depend on annotator instructions, cultural expectations, and prompt distribution. They can reward over-refusal, polished wording, or caution instead of risk reduction. Audit harmful and benign prompts separately and test whether length, style, or refusal form predicts a choice.

Red-team data is not exhaustive. Check conversation provenance, sensitive-content handling, release terms, and benchmark overlap. Do not use a reward trained on these pairs as the only deployment gate or as proof of policy compliance.
