The paper presents helpfulness and harmlessness comparisons as supervision for reward modeling and RLHF, and evaluates trained assistants in its reported setting. The public release exposes the paired-conversation data form. This supports the bounded claim that human comparisons can drive the described post-training pipeline.

It does not establish harmlessness under every adversarial prompt or represent every affected community. The Card does not pin a single size or license statement; check the current official dataset revision before making quantitative or reuse claims.
