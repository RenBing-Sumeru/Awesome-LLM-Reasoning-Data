The closest alternative is treating helpfulness as one response-quality target or applying a generic safety filter after generation. HH-RLHF makes the trade-off explicit in human pairwise comparisons, including red-team contexts, then connects them to reward-model training and RLHF.

It is not the first use of human evaluation or reinforcement learning. Its distinctive object is public, context-bound helpfulness/harmlessness comparison data; it does not replace policy design, adversarial testing, or independent safety review.
