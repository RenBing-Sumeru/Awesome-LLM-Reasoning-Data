Most multimodal reward models are vision-centric, scalar-only, and dependent on expensive human preferences. The paper asks how to build a rubric-grounded reward model spanning text, image, video, and audio.

Omni-RRM releases Omni-Preference, containing response pairs, modality-aware rubrics, dimension-wise reasons, and preference judgments across text, image, video, and audio. Training uses SFT followed by GRPO on difficult low-contrast pairs.
