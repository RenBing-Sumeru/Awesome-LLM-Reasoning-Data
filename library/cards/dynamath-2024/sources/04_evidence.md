The arXiv abstract reports 501 high-quality multi-topic seed questions, 5,010 generated concrete questions, and evaluation of 14 state-of-the-art VLMs. The project/GitHub sources report that 227 seeds come from established visual-math datasets, 274 are newly collected or developed from public resources, 470 programs include dynamic plotting, and 31 use fixed images with randomized text elements.

The decisive row-level evidence is answer correctness for a generated variant, with ground truth produced by the seed program. The robustness evidence is per-seed worst-case accuracy: a seed counts only if all sampled variants are answered correctly. This evidence is bounded by the validity of each program, the answer parser, the sampled seeds/variants, and the model prompt/interface version.
