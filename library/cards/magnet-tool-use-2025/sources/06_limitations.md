- **Environment replay is blocked.** The paper describes executable calls but releases no API implementation/version manifest, tool services, response snapshots, or replay logs. Rewriting BFCL-v3 names/descriptions further prevents a released record from being directly matched to the original environment.
- **Feedback can fail.** Simulation-tested function availability does not validate generated queries, argument values, summaries, or multi-turn state. Gemini reference-relative judgments can give false positives/negatives, while keyword filtering is only a rough exclusion. Neither calibration nor per-turn acceptance records are released.
- **Preference-pair provenance is incomplete.** Ten SFT rollouts per instance are sampled, but their IDs, decoding settings, rejected actions, judge decisions, and final negative-trajectory linkage are unavailable. It is impossible to distinguish coverage of natural model failures from artifacts caused by wrong hints.
- **Limited scope and disclosed behavior failures.** The authors state signatures are mainly English and pure text. They observe that the trained model can favor internal knowledge over conflicting tool output, and that training lacks reflection-and-restart exploration data. (Limitations)
- **Evaluation is not data-quality proof.** BFCL-v3 and ToolQuery scores are aggregate results. Appendix C's FSP study does not establish no query/trajectory overlap, and ToolQuery's unseen-function assertion cannot be checked without the construction records.
- **Rights are unknown.** Licenses and redistribution terms for transformed StableToolBench/BFCL-v3 functions, generated trajectories, teacher outputs, and model artifacts are not disclosed in an author release.
