- **Training-set scale.** The reported SFT set has 20,000 single-turn, 7,800 multi-turn, and 6,200 irrelevance instances (34,000 total). The mDPO set has 1,556, 2,250, and 750 pairs (4,556 total). Reported multi-turn examples average 4.71 turns/15.13 calls for SFT and 5.22 turns/14.98 calls for mDPO. This describes an unreleased corpus, not an independently inspectable data package. (Table 1)
- **BFCL-v3 result.** Magnet-14B-mDPO reports 68.01 overall and 37.88 multi-turn success, versus Qwen2.5-Coder-14B-Instruct at 51.88 overall and 5.38 multi-turn. Magnet-14B-SFT reports 66.83 overall and 33.38 multi-turn, so mDPO adds 1.18 overall and 4.50 multi-turn points in this table. The comparisons are author-reported evaluation results, not evidence that all generated trajectories are correct. (Table 2)
- **ToolQuery result.** On 60 ToolQuery cases, the paper reports success/progress of 73.3/78.7 for Magnet-14B-mDPO, versus 51.7/68.7 for Qwen-Coder-14B-Instruct and 68.3/74.6 for Gemini-1.5-pro-002. The authors state ToolQuery functions are unseen in training; no item-level release was found to independently audit that assertion. (Table 3; §4.3)
- **Component ablation.** In Table 4, removing positive context distillation from Magnet-14B-SFT changes overall/multi-turn from 66.83/33.38 to 60.26/18.88. Removing negative context distillation from Magnet-14B-mDPO changes 68.01/37.88 to 67.35/36.25. These are within-paper ablations and do not calibrate teacher-judge errors or the quality of every preference pair. (Table 4)
- **Contamination evidence is narrow.** Appendix C reports 0.3% exact FSP matches for BFCL-v3 basic multi-turn cases, 0% for other BFCL-v3 multi-turn categories, and 5.3% 2-gram overlap. It is not a complete cross-benchmark, query-level, or release-level contamination audit. (Appendix C)
