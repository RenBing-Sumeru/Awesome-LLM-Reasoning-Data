Text-only scores do not establish whether an LLM agent can plan, call tools, retain state, recover from errors, and safely complete a user goal in a changing environment. Agent benchmarks also differ in task data, environment dynamics, interface, and success checks, making a headline success rate hard to interpret or compare. They can hide cascading failures, non-compliant actions, and costs incurred before an apparently correct final result.

The paper responds with a survey rather than a new benchmark. It maps core capabilities, application-specific and generalist evaluations, shared benchmark dimensions, and developer frameworks, then identifies gaps in granular, scalable, cost-aware, safe, and robust assessment for reproducible deployment decisions, transparent audit, cross-system review, practical use, and checking.
