The contribution is a faithfulness audit: attach provenance (Expert/Human/LLM/Unknown) or recency (New/Old) cues to unchanged response pairs, compare complementary assignments, and require rationales to surface the cue if it matters. Verdict Shift Rate measures choice sensitivity; Cue Acknowledgment Rate measures explanation faithfulness. The work uses public ELI5 and LitBench subsets but does not release a new dataset.
