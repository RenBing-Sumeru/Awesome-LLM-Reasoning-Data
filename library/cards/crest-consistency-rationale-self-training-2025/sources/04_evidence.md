The official proceedings record verifies the title, author order, NAACL venue, April 2025 publication, pages 10519-10539 and DOI. The paper PDF supplies the four-stage method, formulas for `z`, `z_tilde`, the SFT set and DPO objective, dataset counts, hyperparameters, aggregate rationale statistics and limitations. The author repository at main commit `a6afada860235ef613752ee11ba2da8ba2e045c1` exposes the generation, filtering, option-probing, SFT, preference-pair and DPO code; GitHub lists no release.

Table 1 reports that, for Meta-Llama-3-8B, moving from consistency-filtered `M_SFT` to mixed-preference `M_CREST` changes accuracy from 66.10 to 69.50 on ReClor, 81.40 to 81.91 on ARC-Challenge and 79.36 to 81.41 on CommonsenseQA. For Gemma-7B, the corresponding changes are 67.90 to 70.00, 84.22 to 84.47 and 80.51 to 80.67. The Phi-2 appendix reports the same ordering across the three datasets. These are downstream model results, not labels of individual rationale quality.

The ablations support sensitivity to the construction choices. ARC-Challenge and CommonsenseQA peak at tolerance `t=2`, while ReClor peaks at `t=3`; including `z_tilde=0` rationales reduces reported performance. On ReClor, overall accuracy peaks at `lambda=0.6`, while larger consistency-pool weight helps the Hard subset and hurts the Easy subset. These findings motivate dataset-specific filtering and mixture selection rather than a universal threshold.

For the separate 100-question ReClor FLASK analysis judged by GPT-4o, `M_CREST` receives robustness/correctness/efficiency scores of 2.95/3.51/3.33, versus 2.92/3.28/3.29 for `M_SFT` and 2.66/3.17/2.88 for RFT. The paper also reports improved follow-up accuracy when generated rationales are re-probed. This is evidence under one external judge and one behavioral probe; it does not establish causal faithfulness or independent step correctness.

Artifact inspection changes the release claim. `resources/data` contains prepared ReClor, ARC and CommonsenseQA JSONL files; the README says a local `outputs` directory will hold rationales and evaluation results after running stage 1 and local `models` directories will hold trained adapters. No generated rationales, option-wise predictions, `z_tilde` vectors, filtered SFT files, pair pools, sampled DPO data or checkpoints are present in the public tree. Benchmark gains therefore motivate the recipe but do not certify a released CREST dataset, because that derived dataset is absent.
