- Behavioral consistency is not rationale faithfulness. `z` and `z_tilde` score final option decisions after conditioning on a rationale; they do not check whether the written steps are valid, necessary or causally used.
- Evaluation errors are correlated. The same checkpoint generates all 16 rationales, predicts the original answers and answers every option probe. Ground-truth comparison makes the terminal labels objective, but it does not supply an independent evaluator.
- The released pipeline computes option-wise predictions only for `z=1` candidates. The `z=0` pool has no consistency vector, so the method cannot distinguish an incorrect rationale that rejects most distractors correctly from one that fails every option.
- `z_tilde` discards option identity. The paper explicitly notes that two rationales can receive the same count while only one correctly recognizes the true option. Equal weighting also ignores different difficulty between positive and negative option judgments.
- Public parsing is brittle: binary predictions are classified through case-sensitive substring checks for `not` or `incorrect`, and answer extraction selects the leftmost option label occurrence. Parser accuracy, malformed-output rate and manual audit results are unknown.
- Positive tolerance retains known misses. The best `t` changes across datasets, and the paper reports that including candidates with `z_tilde=0` is harmful; a threshold tuned on one model/dataset is not established for another.
- DPO construction can create a large, redundant set of within-question pairs. Pair-pool sizes, exact sampled pair IDs, rejection reasons and score ties are not released, so the final optimization stream cannot be audited or reconstructed exactly.
- The paper reports six SFT epochs and searches up to 5,000 DPO steps, while public scripts default to four SFT epochs and 3,000 DPO steps. The exact settings for every model-dataset result, software revisions and rationale-generation seeds remain unknown.
- Exact, near-duplicate and semantic decontamination are unknown. The release provides no overlap ledger covering train records, few-shot examples, validation and test splits.
- License lineage is incomplete for a reusable derived corpus. Repository code is CC BY-SA 4.0, while upstream dataset terms differ; no generated corpus or record-level rights manifest is published.
- Scope is English multiple-choice QA with supplied answer options. Mathematics and open-ended questions are future directions, not demonstrated construction surfaces.
- Reported benchmark and GPT-4o judge gains do not prove data quality record by record. They can reflect model, parser, threshold, mixture, checkpoint-selection or task-specific effects.
