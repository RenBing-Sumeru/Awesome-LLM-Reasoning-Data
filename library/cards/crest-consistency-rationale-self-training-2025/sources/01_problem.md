The primary sources for this Card are the official [NAACL 2025 proceedings record](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.528/), the 21-page proceedings paper, arXiv v4, and the author [CREST repository](https://github.com/JaehyeokLee-119/CREST). ACL Anthology records the work as a long paper in the 2025 Conference of the Nations of the Americas Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics: Human Language Technologies, pages 10519-10539, DOI 10.18653/v1/2025.naacl-long.528.

Answer-only self-training treats any rationale that leads to the labeled option as suitable supervision. That boundary is weak: a model can reach the right multiple-choice answer through incomplete, contradictory or option-insensitive reasoning. CREST asks a narrower operational question: after conditioning on one generated rationale, does the same model also classify every original option correctly as valid or invalid, and can that behavioral consistency make SFT filtering and preference construction more selective?

The construction surface is the train split of three English multiple-choice datasets: 4,638 ReClor, 3,370 ARC and 8,520 CommonsenseQA questions. For each question and base checkpoint, CREST samples 16 rationales, predicts the original answer for every rationale, and applies option-wise probes only to originally correct candidates. The downstream objects are tolerance-filtered SFT triples and within-question DPO pairs; they are generated during a run but not published.

The work belongs in `data_construction_open_release_recipes` because it exposes the code path from prompt sourcing through trace generation, label-derived feedback, filtering and pair construction. The bilingual Card is `L4_chinese_review_ready`, but its release remains `partial`: the official paper and implementation are verified, while the repository contains prepared upstream QA data rather than generated CREST rationales, scores, selection decisions, preference pairs or checkpoints.
