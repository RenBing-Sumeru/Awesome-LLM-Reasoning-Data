The paper derives posterior moments and the uniform-prior ordering equivalence. Experiments include simulations with known success rates and repeated evaluation on AIME'24/'25, HMMT'25 and BrUMO'25. Appendix G describes twenty model configurations and 80 trials per question. Ranking convergence depends on the task, trial budget and model pool, and some comparisons remain unresolved within the budget. The categorical experiments show that changing the rubric can change the comparison target and resulting rankings.
