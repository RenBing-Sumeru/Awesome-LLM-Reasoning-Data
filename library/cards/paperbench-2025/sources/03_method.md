The construction pipeline filtered candidate ICML papers for empirical substance, single-machine feasibility, accessible dependencies, and from-scratch reproducibility. The team contacted 42 author groups and retained 20 papers whose authors agreed to help refine rubrics and addenda. The main comparison targeted 6 models, 20 papers, and 3 runs per pair, while additional IterativeAgent, extended-budget, and human conditions contributed to the reported total.

Each main rollout received up to 12 hours in Ubuntu 24.04 with 1 A10 GPU, Internet access, and optional credentials. The required submission contains a reproduction script and generated results, plots, tables, and logs. Reproduction receives another 12 hours in a fresh container; one extended o1 condition ran for 36 hours. Grading uses o3-mini-2025-01-31. If a submission exceeds judge context, model-based relevance ranking selects only the top 10 files, creating an information bottleneck. The blacklist monitor searches recorded logs for normalized URLs near selected download commands, rather than enforcing a complete network policy.
