PaperBench is directly useful for evaluation and audit of long-horizon research agents: the public release provides 20 evaluation task packages, a current 3-paper dev split, rubrics, addenda, assets, blacklists, container and orchestration code, judge and monitor implementations, and a 5-example JudgeEval. These materials support reproducing the benchmark interface, studying verifier design, and diagnosing whether an agent can turn a paper into executable evidence.

It should not be represented as an off-the-shelf SFT or RL trajectory source. The complete 646-run corpus is absent, including failed and disqualified cases, and the public rubric would contaminate any future evaluation if used as a training target. Training reuse would first require run-level artifacts, immutable lineage, rights clearance, secret redaction, stable replay manifests, and stronger judge calibration. Under current evidence, the defensible training-use labels are evaluation and audit only.
