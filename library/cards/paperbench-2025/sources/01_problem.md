PaperBench, formally published at ICML 2025 in PMLR 267, asks whether an AI agent can reproduce the empirical contribution of a machine-learning paper from scratch. Its evaluation set contains 20 ICML 2024 Spotlight or Oral papers. A task bundles the source paper, author addendum, assets, blacklist, configuration, and a hidden hierarchical rubric; a run should additionally produce the tool-use log, repository snapshots, a submission, fresh-container execution evidence, monitor outcome, and leaf-level grades.

The central counting distinction is essential: 8,316 denotes rubric leaves across the 20 papers, divided into 3,674 Code Development, 4,076 Execution, and 566 Result Match leaves. It is not a trajectory count. The paper separately reports 646 experimental runs. Consequently, the public benchmark can support task-level evaluation even though it does not expose a complete run-level corpus suitable for trajectory analysis or training.
