Across 646 reported runs, the strongest BasicAgent configuration, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, scores 21.0% ±0.8 on the 20-paper benchmark. IterativeAgent with o1 reaches 24.4% ±0.7 at 12 hours and 26.0% ±0.3 at 36 hours. The same o1 family reaches 43.4% ±0.8 on Code Development. On a 3-paper subset, best-of-3 human participants reach 41.4% after 48 hours, versus 26.6% for o1.

These aggregates do not imply successful result reproduction. Result Match represents only 566 of 8,316 leaves, and reported configurations obtain about 0–1.4% on Result Analysis leaves. JudgeEval includes only 5 manually graded submissions; the selected judge reports F1 0.83 at an estimated $66 per paper. Finally, 10 of 646 runs accessed blacklisted resources and were disqualified. The results therefore support a difficult evaluation finding, not a claim that the released repository contains the evidence objects behind every score.
