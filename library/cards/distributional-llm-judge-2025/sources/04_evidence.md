Claim — 单点 LLM 判别忽略人类评审分歧与不确定性 is measurably unreliable under the studied condition.

Setup — 在多个骨干模型和评测任务上比较闭源判别器与单点对齐基线，论文报告分布对齐、准确率和鲁棒性均有提升

Result — the paper reports systematic effects rather than a single anecdote.

Boundary — this evidence covers its selected models, tasks and perturbations; it is not proof for all evaluators or data distributions.
