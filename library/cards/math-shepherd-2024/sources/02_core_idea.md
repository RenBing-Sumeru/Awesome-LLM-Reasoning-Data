The contribution is an automatic step-level supervision pipeline for training and using a mathematical process reward model. The core mechanism estimates a step's promise by rolling out continuations from a partial solution and checking the final answer; the resulting signal trains a verifier that can rank or reinforce reasoning paths. Closest comparisons are PRM800K-style human process labels and outcome-only reward models. The feedback contract remains answer-derived: a step is rewarded because continuations from it succeed, not because a formal checker proves the step valid.
