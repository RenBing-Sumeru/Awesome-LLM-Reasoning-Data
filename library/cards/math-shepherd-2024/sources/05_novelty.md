The prior baseline is either expensive human step labels, as in PRM-style datasets, or cheap outcome labels that supervise only the final answer. Math-Shepherd changes the feedback layer by deriving step rewards from continuation success. The direction signal is scalable process supervision with no human step annotation. The quality signal is that every pseudo-label traces back to answer-checked rollouts. What is not new is final-answer grading or verifier-guided selection. Reuse should inspect rollout budget, answer checker errors, label calibration, distribution shift between generator and PRM use, and license/artifact availability.
