A high reward means continuations from a prefix often reached the reference answer under the sampled policy; it does not prove the current step is logically valid. Poor generators can under-credit good prefixes, and lucky continuations can over-credit flawed prefixes. The method inherits all final-answer checker limitations from the underlying math tasks. Public artifact, split, and license details should be pinned before reuse. Scores obtained with PRM reranking or reinforcement are budget-dependent and should not be compared to single-sample baselines without labels.
