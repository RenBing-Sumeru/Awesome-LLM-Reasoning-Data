For the Rollout / Search / Test-Time Trace Data track, this release is useful as a compact answer-level repeated-sampling corpus. Researchers can reconstruct the 16-attempt success distribution for every problem, compare capability strata, study how a fixed sampling budget produces a quantized selection signal, and retain negative final-answer evidence rather than only the selected answer. Because raw completions are absent, it cannot support trace-length, reasoning-pattern, branch, token-cost, latency, or process-error analysis.

For post-training, the three hosted datasets, image archives, EasyR1 launchers, and answer-plus-format reward provide a concrete staged RLVR scaffold. They can be used to compare merged, capability-ordered, difficulty-ordered, and two-dimensional curricula under the reported training budgets. Reusers should keep the repeated-sampling scorer distinct from the five-response on-policy GRPO training group: the former produces offline curriculum metadata, while the latter produces optimization rollouts.

For verifier and curriculum audits, the vectors expose disagreement and model-relative difficulty that a scalar alone would hide. Useful follow-up studies include rescoring the same rows with other policies, calibrating pass rate against held-out outcomes, testing alternative ordering directions or adaptive schedules, and measuring sensitivity to extraction and verifier errors. Such work requires additional sampling metadata for strict replication.

Reuse should remain conditional on per-source licensing, decontamination, and lineage review, especially for Stage-3 images and the DocVQA discrepancy. The artifact is suitable for L4 card-level study because its source, record, verifier, construction path, budget, intended RLVR use, and missing information are identifiable; its unresolved release boundaries do not support promotion to a higher audit state.
