**Stage 1 — perception.** The pipeline starts from DOCCI images and fine-grained captions. Qwen2.5-72B converts captions into four-way perception MCQs. A question is retained only in the intersection where both Qwen2.5-VL-7B and Qwen2.5-VL-32B answer incorrectly from the image but correctly from the caption. The hosted artifact contains 3,360 rows and references 14,847 downsampled DOCCI images. Captions are construction inputs but are absent from the documented row schema.

**Stage 2 — textual reasoning.** The release redistributes 13,181 ORZ-Math-13k text problems. These rows use the same answer, repeated-prediction, correctness, and pass-rate fields but no image input.

**Stage 3 — visual reasoning.** The hosted release has 16,195 rows: Math PUMA 6,696, GeoQA170K 6,499, CLEVR-Math 2,000, and ArxivQA 1,000. The paper's method section also names DocVQA, but the Hugging Face card for the released 16,195-row mixture does not. The card therefore treats the four-source hosted list as the released data object and records DocVQA as an unresolved paper-versus-release lineage discrepancy.

**Difficulty records and ordering.** Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct produces 16 answers per problem at temperature 1.0. Only extracted final answers, their boolean correctness labels, and their mean are stored. Pass rate is quantized in steps of 1/16 and depends on this model and sampling policy. The two-dimensional schedule first fixes the perception-to-text-to-visual capability order, then sorts higher-pass-rate examples before lower-pass-rate examples within a stage.

**RLVR scaffold and verifier.** EasyR1 runs GRPO with five on-policy responses per input. The released reward extracts a boxed final answer, uses MathRuler for answer equivalence, checks for the required reasoning-and-boxed-answer syntax, and combines accuracy and format scores with weights 0.9 and 0.1. This is an answer-and-format verifier, not a visual-grounding or process verifier. Released Qwen3 launchers keep the vision encoder open in Stages 1 and 3 and frozen in Stage 2.

**Budgets.** The paper reports a maximum response length of 2,048 tokens, and Appendix Table 6 lists a maximum prompt length of 2,048. Staged runs use 90, 375, and 465 steps, matching the merged baseline's 930 total steps. The Qwen3 launchers specify 16, 15, and 15 epochs across the three stages. The repository quickstart estimates about 24 GPU-hours on eight H200 GPUs for staged Qwen3-VL-8B reproduction. Seeds and several sampling controls for pass-rate generation, as well as RLVR rollout temperature, are undisclosed.
