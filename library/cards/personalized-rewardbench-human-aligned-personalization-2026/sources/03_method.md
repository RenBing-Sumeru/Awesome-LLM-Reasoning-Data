1. **Build the source pool:** Collect requests that admit multiple reasonable preferences and define a clear, decidable personal rubric for each request.

2. **Generate or reorganize feedback:** Generate two generally high-quality candidates, one strictly following the rubric and the other violating only the target personalization condition without obvious factual errors.

3. **Verify and filter:** Have humans verify that personal preference is the discriminating factor and remove pairs with general-quality differences or ambiguous isolation.

4. **Train and evaluate:** Evaluate pairwise RM accuracy and test predictive validity against downstream Best-of-N and PPO performance of the same RMs.
