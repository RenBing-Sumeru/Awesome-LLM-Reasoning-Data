# 08 Reading Notes

Do not read PRM800K as a purely evaluation-only benchmark or as proof that every released chain of thought is correct. It is a labeled process-supervision corpus that can be used for training, analysis, and reward-model evaluation, depending on the split and protocol.

Read the paper first for the supervision comparison, then the GitHub README and annotation instructions for the data contract. Keep step label, final-answer grade, PRM score, and best-of-N selection outcome separate.
