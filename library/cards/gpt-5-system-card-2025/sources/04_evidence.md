The [official GPT-5 System Card](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-5) supports the launch architecture, data-source classes, filtering, reinforcement-learning reasoning, router signals, safe-completions, evaluations, and safeguards. Its Model Data and Training section is the evidence for training disclosure; the many later evaluation sections are not substitutes for missing training records.

The card reports several concrete evaluation results. On production-representative factuality prompts with browsing, an LLM grader had 75% human agreement on factuality judgments; relative to GPT-4o and OpenAI o3, gpt-5-main and gpt-5-thinking respectively had 44% and 78% fewer responses containing at least one major factual error. On SimpleQA without web access, reported accuracy was 0.55 for gpt-5-thinking versus 0.54 for OpenAI o3, with hallucination rates of 0.40 and 0.46. For sycophancy, offline scores were 0.145 for GPT-4o, 0.052 for gpt-5-main, and 0.040 for gpt-5-thinking; preliminary online prevalence for gpt-5-main fell 69% for free users and 75% for paid users relative to GPT-4o. These results characterize evaluated behavior, not training-data quality or reproducibility.

The report also records counterevidence and scope limits. Some gpt-5-main disallowed-content categories regress relative to GPT-4o, including statistically significant regressions for hate/threatening and sexual/exploitative. Comparison values for live predecessor models may differ from their launch values. Ordinary safety evaluations were not rerun for gpt-5-thinking-pro; gpt-5-thinking results are treated as proxies even though pro uses parallel test-time compute.

OpenAI treats gpt-5-thinking as High capability in the Biological and Chemical domain as a precaution and activates associated safeguards while stating that definitive evidence for the threshold is absent. This is a risk-management decision supported by capability and safeguard assessments, not a disclosed training-data label.
