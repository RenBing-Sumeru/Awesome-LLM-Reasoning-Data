At the model-data layer, OpenAI reports a diverse proprietary mixture drawn from the three source classes and a filtering pipeline that targets quality, risks, personal information, and harmful or sensitive content. The report does not disclose preprocessing rules, thresholds, deduplication, mixture weights, versions, yields, train/evaluation membership, or item-level provenance. It says the reasoning variants learn through reinforcement learning to refine their thinking, try strategies, recognize mistakes, and follow model policies, but it does not publish the trajectories, feedback, rewards, or optimizer.

At the system layer, the router chooses among gpt-5-main and gpt-5-thinking paths according to conversation type, complexity, tool needs, and explicit user intent. Continuous router training uses model switches, response-preference rates, and measured correctness. Once usage limits are reached, mini variants handle remaining queries. The report does not define the routing record schema, how correctness is measured, how conflicting signals are combined, the update cadence, exploration policy, thresholds, or failure logs.

At the safety-training layer, safe-completions replace a brittle prompt-intent comply/refuse boundary with an output-centric objective: maximize helpfulness under safety-policy constraints. The system card reports production comparisons and controlled experiments, but the separate official safe-completions paper is methodological context rather than a release of GPT-5 training records. Targeted post-training is also described for sycophancy and graceful failure on infeasible tasks. Exact datasets, labels, graders, reward models, and stage weights remain unknown.

At inference, gpt-5-thinking-pro applies parallel test-time compute. The number of parallel attempts, selection or aggregation rule, scheduling, and budget are not disclosed. At deployment, biological-risk controls add model training, system-level protections over production traffic, account enforcement, API identifiers, and access programs; this stack is not a single training verifier.
