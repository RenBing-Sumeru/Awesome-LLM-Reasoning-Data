One-sentence contribution: InstructGPT separates demonstrations, human rankings, reward modeling, and policy optimization into a durable data lineage for instruction-following alignment. Core mechanism: It first fine-tunes on demonstrations, then learns a reward model from ranked outputs, and then optimizes the supervised policy with that learned reward signal. The important distinction for this atlas is between the paper's conceptual contribution and a concrete downstream release that must still disclose provenance, labels, splits, and license.
