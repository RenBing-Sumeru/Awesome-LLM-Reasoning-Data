Exploration runs log post-gating candidate lists and a verifier label for every surviving move. A small MLP scores state, move, and goal embeddings; pairwise ranking loss trains accepted candidates ahead of rejected candidates, with an optional remaining-step auxiliary signal. At inference, the system generates or retrieves moves, applies structural and constraint gates, ranks survivors, chooses k from the score dispersion, verifies only that top-k set, and commits a verified move or retries/backtracks.

The controlled evaluation shares the same structured interface, generator, and PRM verifier across all methods. It uses Llama 3.2 1B as the default generator, MATH-500 with two-fold difficulty-bin cross-validation, and GSM8K for variation. Baselines are zero-shot CoT, 64-sample best-of-N, 64-sample majority vote, and beam search; verifier invocations are the primary cost measure rather than treating all tokens as equivalent.
