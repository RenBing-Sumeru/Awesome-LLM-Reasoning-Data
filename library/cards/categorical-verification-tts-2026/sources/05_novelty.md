Earlier test-time allocation commonly chooses a rollout count per problem or uses a fixed search width. This work changes the allocation unit to an intermediate move and makes verifier calls, not merely samples, the explicit scarce resource. Its reusable record is a verifier-labeled candidate list tied to one structured state, from which a score and a local call budget are learned.

The contribution is not a more accurate verifier by itself. The verifier is held fixed across the principal comparison; the change is the gate–rank–allocate scaffold around it. Before reuse, inspect whether the target domain actually has a structured move interface, whether deterministic gates are sound enough to avoid rejecting useful moves, and whether score variance is calibrated as uncertainty rather than an artifact of the embedding model.
