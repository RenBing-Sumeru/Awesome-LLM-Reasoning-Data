Remember that this is a verifier-budget paper, not a generic prompt-time optimization method. The verifier remains the authority; learned scores merely decide who gets checked. The strongest evidence is the staged ablation from verify-all to gates, scoring, and state-k allocation. Its natural next test is a domain with executable intermediate actions, where gate precision and verifier false positives can be measured directly. Treat the reported 44% call reduction as a result under the paper’s structured interface and PRM protocol, not a portable percentage for arbitrary LLM evaluation.
