HoneyBee uses one fixed teacher, Llama-4 Scout, to turn data design into a controlled search. The authors first compare six candidate context sources under 50K-example caps, decontaminate exact evaluation-image matches with pHash, generate CoTs with the same teacher, and train PLM-3B and PLM-8B under the same evaluation protocol. ViRL gives the best average result among the tested sources; mixing the top sources does not improve on it. The final construction therefore starts from ViRL rather than treating all available multimodal corpora as equally useful.

The recipe then scales three axes separately. It increases unique images by taking progressively larger subsets, increases questions per image by generating new questions conditioned on an image and seed question, and increases solutions per image/question pair by sampling additional CoTs. Caption-and-solve is the selected perceptual intervention: Llama-4 Scout generates a detailed caption independently of the solution, and the two texts are concatenated so that the student learns to emit caption then reasoning.

For the 39K original ViRL tuples, the teacher samples 16 CoTs and only traces whose final answer matches the known answer survive, leaving roughly 400K instances. For each image the pipeline creates 14 new questions, so the target is 15 questions including the original. Each new question receives four CoTs; an answer occurring at least three times becomes a proxy label, and only matching CoTs are retained, yielding roughly one million more instances. Adding captions produces the vision-language subset. The paper's exact table reports 1.440M vision-language plus 1.040M text-only records, or 2.480M total, around 28K unique images and 350K unique questions.

The text-only component is not copied unchanged from OpenThoughts3. Its 1.04M questions are re-annotated with Llama-4 Scout so the generator is consistent across modalities. The public dataset nevertheless preserves upstream pointers, so recipe-level consistency does not imply a self-contained release.
