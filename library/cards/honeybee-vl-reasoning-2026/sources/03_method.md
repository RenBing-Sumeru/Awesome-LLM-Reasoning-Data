The construction study begins with ViRL, Math-LLaVA, R1-OneVision, ThinkLite-VL-Hard, LLaVA-CoT, and MMK12. For each source, the authors remove records whose images exactly match validation images according to pHash and sample at most 50K contexts. Llama-4 Scout, described as a 109B-total/17B-active MoE model, generates the comparison CoTs. PLM-3B and PLM-8B are then fully fine-tuned and scored with an identical boxed-answer prompt and greedy decoding across MathVerse, MathVista, MathVision, MMMU-Pro, and We-Math. This downstream average is used for hill-climbing over recipe choices, not as a per-record validator.

After ViRL is selected, several interventions test perception and problem-solving changes. Visual perturbation, synthetic text-rich images, filters for questions solvable without an image or with only a caption, distractor insertion, long-trace selection, and uniform-difficulty sampling do not beat the baseline in the reported comparison. Independently generated captions concatenated with independently generated solutions improve the average; mixing re-annotated OpenThoughts3 data improves it further. These are empirical choices within the reported setup, not universal guarantees.

The final vision-language pipeline has two answer contracts. Existing ViRL questions carry source answers: 16 teacher traces are sampled per tuple, their final answers are extracted, and traces that do not match the source answer are discarded. Newly synthesized questions lack gold answers: four traces are sampled, an answer must appear at least three times, and matching traces are kept. Because one model produces all four candidates, this is self-consistency filtering rather than independent verification. The paper does not disclose generation temperature, top-p, maximum generation length, request seeds, rejected outputs, or extraction logs.

Image captions are generated once per image independently of the solutions, then prepended to each retained solution. OpenThoughts3's 1.04M questions are re-annotated by the same teacher. The released schema has five fields—`q_source`, `q_id`, `image_path`, `question`, and `cot`—with `q_source` values observed as OpenThoughts3, ViRL, or Ours. Null question or image fields signal that upstream material must be resolved separately.

For model training, the supplement reports full-parameter PLM fine-tuning for five epochs. The archived 3B configuration pins seed 777, 6,075 steps, learning rate 4e-5, 607 warmup steps, and parameters consistent with a global batch of 2,048. The paper's GRPO experiment is downstream use: it trains for one epoch on a separate verified multimodal dataset with rule-based terminal rewards. It should not be read as a reward annotation contained in HoneyBee.
