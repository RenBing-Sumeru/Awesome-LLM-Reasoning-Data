The strongest evidence for the construction claims comes from the official CVPR paper and supplement. Table 2 compares six context sources under the controlled PLM-3B/8B setup; ViRL is highest at 40.1 average accuracy and MMK12 lowest at 36.0. Table 3 reports caption-and-solve at 41.4 versus 40.1 for the original data, and the text-only mixture at 43.1. Figure 3 reports monotonic improvement over the tested image, question, and CoT scaling points. These results support the tested recipe decisions, although source selection and intervention effects could vary with other teachers, students, or evaluation sets.

Table 4 supplies the non-rounded composition: 2,480K total instances, 1,440K vision-language, 1,040K text-only, 28K unique images, and 350K unique questions. The paper rounds these to 2.5M, 1.5M, and 1M in prose. The official Hugging Face dataset confirms that a train release exists with the five stated fields, but its Dataset Viewer conversion is partial: at revision `3ac2b01b117893adcd02c62f7e04bd500b2a5240`, it materializes 1,360,000 rows and estimates 2,463,128. This does not reconcile exactly to Table 4, so the Card keeps paper and live-artifact counts separate.

Model results are evidence of utility, not a correctness certificate. The paper reports that HoneyBee-trained PLMs improve over their bases and selected competitors, and that performance continues improving across 50K, 250K, and 2.5M training subsets. It also reports a test-time shared-caption experiment on MathVista with 64 samples at temperature 0.7: reusing the first caption reduces the tested token/FLOP total by 73% while giving similar performance. Neither result validates every released rationale.

The official repository corroborates a narrower reproducibility surface. At archived commit `987a1cba97a496c3f9ad43ade65c4504a4428ce0`, it contains evaluation code, three PLM YAML configurations, and the license, but no data-generation implementation, rejected-candidate logs, trained HoneyBee checkpoints, or formal release. The README explicitly says trained VLMs are not planned for release. The 3B YAML provides concrete SFT settings, while generation sampling remains unknown.

The supplement directly acknowledges that final-answer filtering does not settle reasoning faithfulness. It reports a GPT-5.1-mini judgment that 80% of HoneyBee-8B CoTs on MathVista were faithful. That is a model-judged, model-output evaluation on one benchmark—not a human audit of the released training corpus—and it implies that final-answer agreement can coexist with unfaithful reasoning.
