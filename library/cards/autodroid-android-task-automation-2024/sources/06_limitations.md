Correctness is relative to the Android environment, app versions, and task-specific success detectors. Dynamic apps, login flows, network state, permissions, and UI redesigns can invalidate traces. The system assumes apps can be explored and analyzed, which may not hold for proprietary or security-sensitive apps. Risky-action confirmation reduces harm but does not prove privacy safety or semantic correctness of every action. The release path and license for DroidTask, VM snapshots, and third-party apps must be checked artifact by artifact before redistribution or training use.
