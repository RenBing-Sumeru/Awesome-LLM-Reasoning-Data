The paper reports DroidTask with 158 common tasks from 13 open-source apps and manual step labels linking GUI states to actions. In the main benchmark, AutoDroid with GPT-4 reports 90.9% action accuracy and 71.3% task success, outperforming GPT-4-powered baselines by 36.4 to 39.7 percentage points in task completion and reducing average query cost by 51.7%. The paper also reports that UI pruning and merging reduce the average action space from 36.4 to 13.2 choices per GUI state. Row-level evidence is an executable action trace and the task success detector, not only a text judgment. Evidence is bounded by the released apps, VM/environment, prompts, online model versions, and the authors' success definitions.
