Multimodal agent reports can disclose architectures, evaluation budgets, tools, and reinforcement-learning objectives while withholding the training records, teacher versions, environment configurations, reward rubrics, and rollout logs that determine whether the claimed pipeline can be reproduced or audited. These missing artifacts matter especially when text, images, video, code, tool use, and multi-agent coordination share one post-training system.

Kimi K2.5 provides unusually detailed descriptions of its multimodal SFT/RL and Agent Swarm framework, but it does not release SFT/RL datasets, teacher outputs, the Unified Agentic RL Environment, sandboxes, task manifests, GRM rubrics, reward calibration, or trajectory logs. This Card records the disclosed data and feedback interfaces without treating the open checkpoint as a reusable post-training release.
