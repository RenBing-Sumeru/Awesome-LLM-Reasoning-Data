This report is useful for decomposing multimodal agentic claims into source modality, trace author, visual/tool environment, terminal signal, GRM or verifier, orchestration policy, and release boundary. It shows why a released model can still have an opaque post-training substrate even when the report explains the broad optimizer and reward design.

For future releases, a reproducibility package would need source and rights manifests, SFT/RL prompts and traces, visual task and evaluation separation, teacher/version records, environment and container revisions, tool/judge specifications, all reward rubrics and calibration, rollout/rejection logs, and seeds or immutable snapshots. Users should keep K2.5 in Track 12 as a disclosure ledger rather than treating it as a Track 8 reusable recipe.
