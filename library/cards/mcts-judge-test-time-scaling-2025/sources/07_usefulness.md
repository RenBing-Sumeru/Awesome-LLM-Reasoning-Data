For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, a reusable MCTS-Judge record should preserve the problem and candidate code; benchmark item and reference availability; model/version and full prompts; action inventory; parent-child edges; node analysis and binary sub-decision; visit count, Q, UCT, local self-assessment, and selection probability; generated test cases and all validation attempts; sampled cases; each simulated variable trace and predicted output; per-case vote; h(x); trajectory aggregate; terminal reward; backpropagation; and final trajectory choice.

This schema would enable auditing reward leakage, self-confirmation, action coverage, and whether more test-time compute helps because of broader analysis or repeated correlated judgments. It could also support judge or verifier research, but only after separating actual execution results from simulated outputs and keeping hidden benchmark labels out of search. Without the official tree and test-case release, the paper currently supplies a recipe and risk model rather than a reusable feedback dataset.
