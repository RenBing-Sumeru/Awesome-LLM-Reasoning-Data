- **Release boundary:** The 26 apps, seed data, 133 tasks, 1,123 criteria, runner, evaluator, MCP servers, and local trajectory writer are released. The complete experimental corpus of successful, failed, timed-out, safety-blocked, judge-error, and retried episodes is not; only 16 curated trajectory views are public. Public success/failure selection and retry retention are unknown.
- **Judge fidelity:** GPT-5.4 Mini is a closed, unpinned API judge. Human comparison still finds 79 criterion false positives and 69 false negatives, with category-dependent bias and higher semantic/report error. Rubric omissions, visually plausible but state-incorrect outcomes, off-screen state, and harmful intermediate actions can escape the primary contract.
- **Replay and environment drift:** Reset wipes and reseeds app data rather than restoring a complete immutable simulator. No tagged release binds built-app digests, simulator image, Xcode/iOS/Appium/WebDriverAgent versions, model APIs, seeds, retries, task/result checksums, and judge calls. Screenshots, XML trees, actions, and labels can drift.
- **Split and exposure:** The 133 task records have category and difficulty fields but no train/dev/test split. No decontamination, evaluated-model training-overlap, prompt-leakage, or future leaderboard exposure policy is disclosed. Public tasks, rubrics, evaluator prompts, and seed state permit memorization and judge gaming.
- **Construction and attribution:** The Claude Code prompt/snapshot/settings, 175-candidate ledger, correction/removal diffs, reviewers, and record-level provenance are absent. The root is Apache-2.0 and nested MIT notices exist, but the Notes README references a missing root `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`; complete source, seed, visual-asset, gallery, and generated-trajectory rights are not itemized.
- **Safety and generalization:** All data and the Jordan Avery persona are synthetic, which avoids real-user consent claims but does not establish real-device safety. Tasks include purchases, payments, reservations, and messages. MCP prepare/confirm pairs are not the default expanded interface and legacy risky verbs remain exposed; direct persistence tools may bypass human-visible UI. Results from one fictional persona and one iOS/Xcode generation should not be generalized to real services or users.
