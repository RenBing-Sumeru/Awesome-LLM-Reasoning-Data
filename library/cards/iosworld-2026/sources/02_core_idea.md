iOSWorld's contribution is an executable native-iOS evaluation contract that binds a persistent fictional identity, deterministic app state, multimodal agent interaction, task-specific rubrics, and a post-hoc episode judge. The 26 apps share connected synthetic records rather than presenting independent mock screens. Before each normal task, the runner reboots the simulator, wipes manifest-listed app data plus the shared workspace app-group container, launches every app to reseed, terminates them, and returns to the home screen; this is an app-data reset, not restoration of an immutable simulator image.

The feedback contract is `judgment_required`. GPT-5.4 Mini receives the goal, full sequence of screenshots and actions, final answer, and 4-13 task-specific criteria. It returns an overall success decision and per-criterion satisfied flags with reasons. The scalar reward is the satisfied-criterion fraction; strict pass requires every criterion. Supervision therefore attaches to the `full_episode` and produces `scalar_reward` plus binary success, not verified state-action labels or a demonstrated training reward.

This judge can assess visible progress and semantic reports across a trajectory, but it does not directly execute a programmatic database predicate. Off-screen state, omitted rubric conditions, alternate valid paths, harmful intermediate side effects, and API-model drift remain outside or imperfectly observed. Human validation on 128 Opus 4.6 vision+XML trajectories found 89% task agreement and 86% rubric accuracy, so the judge is calibrated evidence rather than ground truth.

Relative to Android mobile benchmarks, the concrete change is a native iOS substrate with one connected persistent persona. Relative to AndroidWorld-style programmatic checking, the primary success interface is a rubric-conditioned trajectory judge. Optional per-app MCP servers add typed operations and raise a separate question: they can bypass visible UI transitions, and risky prepare/confirm pairs exist while expanded confirmation is off by default and legacy direct verbs remain available. The work combines established simulator, accessibility, LLM-judge, and tool-server components; its direction signal is their inspectable episode-level binding, not any one component in isolation.
