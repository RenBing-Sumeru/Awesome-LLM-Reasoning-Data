The paper evaluates the selector on two non-overlapping LiveCodeBench releases and several open- and closed-weight models. Pass@1 and cost comparisons support the narrower claim that the procedure can alter final selection with less additional LLM inference than the studied selectors. They do not prove that behavioral consensus is a correctness oracle or that fuzz inputs are high-quality reusable data. The paper links the official repository at https://github.com/SecurityLab-UCD/DiffCodeGen and states that reproduction artifacts exist, but the Card metadata still records code and data as null; complete release of candidates, fuzz inputs, matrices, rejections, and environments remains unconfirmed.
