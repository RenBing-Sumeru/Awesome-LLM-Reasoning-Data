Use STaR to build reasoning SFT data for a task with many questions and reliable gold answers but few written rationales. Feed in questions, answer keys, a base model, and a small rationale prompt; generate traces, filter them by answer correctness, rationalize failures, and output a round-tagged rationale dataset plus a fine-tuned model. Success means held-out task accuracy rises without collapsing trace diversity or acceptance coverage. Do not use terminal-answer filtering alone when answers are open-ended, the checker is noisy, or process faithfulness is required.
