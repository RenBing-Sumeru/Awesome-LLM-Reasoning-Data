**CommonsenseQA comparison (Table 1).** Claim: selected self-generated rationales improve a 6B model beyond answer-only fine-tuning, and rationalization adds coverage. Controlled setup: GPT-J is the shared 6B base; direct fine-tuning uses answer-only data, STaR trains on accepted traces, and the rationalization variant conditions failures on gold answers. Dev accuracy is 60.0 for direct fine-tuning, 68.8 for STaR without rationalization, and 72.5 with rationalization while using 86.7% of training questions; the paper compares 72.5 with 73.0 from a roughly 30× larger GPT-3 model. This supports answer-checkable bootstrapping, not step faithfulness or tasks without gold answers.
