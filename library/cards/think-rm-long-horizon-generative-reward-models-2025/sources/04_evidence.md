The core experiments evaluate the method on reward-model evaluation and pairwise RLHF. Think-RM improves by about 8% over Bradley–Terry and shallow generative reward-model baselines. The results support supervision from generating long comparative reasoning and filtering it by the original preference label and output format under the reported setup, but remain limited by the base model, data, and inference budget. The conclusion is limited to the reported preference data, teacher model, reasoning length, and output format.
