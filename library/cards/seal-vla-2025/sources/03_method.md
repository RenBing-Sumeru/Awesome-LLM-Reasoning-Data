Gemini receives a demonstration video and its high-level instruction, then jointly proposes one-sentence subtask plans and their end timesteps. The authors report manually verifying all generated annotations. Each reasoning record follows three fields: Plans, What has been done, and Now I need to do. The released LIBERO-R package contains libero-10-r, libero-100-basket-r, and libero-100-r; each includes episode data and metadata, while cot_simple.json indexes reasoning segments by episode and stores start/end steps plus current and updated textual content.

The reasoning VLA is trained with named think and act control tokens, cross-entropy loss for text, and flow-matching loss for actions. During SEAL inference, the policy autoregressively samples candidate action sequences until it emits the next think control token. Parallel LIBERO instances predict the resulting observation sequence. GPT-4o acts as a proxy verifier and receives the episode's initial image, the candidate's predicted final image, and the current text plan; it returns a binary alignment judgment. The implementation can execute the first asynchronously verified candidate rather than waiting for every candidate. Exact candidate count is experiment-dependent; real-world use would require a sufficiently accurate simulator, learned world model, or digital twin.
