SEAL first annotates LIBERO demonstrations with a changing reasoning state—an overall plan, completed subgoals, and the next subgoal—and fine-tunes a reasoning VLA that switches between text and action generation. At runtime, each newly generated text plan conditions multiple variable-length candidate action sequences. Parallel simulation predicts their outcomes, and an off-the-shelf VLM judges whether each predicted final state fulfills the plan. The selected sequence is executed without additional policy training.
