SEAL makes plan–action faithfulness an explicit runtime verification contract. Unlike a critic that scores short action chunks for generic progress, its verifier compares the predicted outcome of a complete plan-conditioned sequence against the model’s own current subgoal. The accompanying LIBERO-R release also exposes a reusable segment schema linking demonstration timesteps to evolving textual plans. The method is best-of-N steering with a learned semantic proxy, not a proof-producing safety verifier.
