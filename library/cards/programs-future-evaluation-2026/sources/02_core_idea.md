PAJAMA (Program-As-a-Judge for Automated Model Assessment) moves judgment from repeated direct LLM calls into a panel of executable programs. An LLM synthesizes programs from distinct, code-expressible criteria; the programs run locally on answer candidates, and weak supervision combines their noisy judgments. This moves cost from each evaluated item to a reusable synthesis-and-review stage, while exposing the decision logic for inspection and testing. The framework is aimed at improving consistency and reducing bias and cost in repeated evaluation; it does not claim that programs eliminate every semantic judgment or replace human calibration.
