For `environment_agent_trajectory_data`, WeaveBench is an evaluation-environment and episode-schema reference. A reproducible record should preserve task ID and provenance, P1-P3 atoms, task bundle revision, VM hash, application/service state, screenshot and tool observations, normalized GUI plus CLI/code/file/browser actions, complete chat trace, deliverables, logs, judge prompt/model/version, clause and dimension evidence, hack decision, scalar score, terminal threshold, and whether the episode succeeded, partially succeeded, failed, or timed out. Keeping those fields together enables replay studies, cross-interface failure analysis, shortcut auditing, and environment-version comparisons.

For `benchmarks_evaluation_surfaces`, the mixed judge is a concrete design to audit rather than a ready-made trustworthy reward. Researchers can compare full-episode judgment with outcome-only checks, inspect the eight dimensions and clause evidence, measure false positives and false negatives against independent human adjudication, perturb prompt and judge versions, and test whether hidden-anchor isolation prevents gaming. The paper's 33.3%-to-53.5% outcome-only change supplies an ablation target, not a calibration certificate.

Task construction can be reused as an evaluation recipe: define hybrid cooperation archetypes, require P1 channel non-substitutability, P2 interleaved phases, and P3 cross-application state, package self-contained assets and anchors, run independent blind review, and pilot with multiple agents. A derived benchmark should additionally publish record-level provenance, rejection reasons, pilot identities/settings, immutable environments, evaluator tests, and complete rollout retention because those items are incomplete or unknown here.

The current reuse class is **evaluation and audit only**. The official paper does not train with WeaveBench, and neither released task bundles nor displayed best trajectories should be treated as author-endorsed SFT, RL, preference, or reward-model data. Training reuse is blocked pending a train/evaluation partition, decontamination analysis, complete trajectory manifest including failures, record-level rights, judge calibration, and reconciliation of GPT-5.5 versus Claude Opus 4.7 and threshold 0.8 versus 0.5.
