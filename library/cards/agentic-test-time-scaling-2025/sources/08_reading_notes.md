This paper is most useful for its negative results: step-wise expansion and constant reflection are not free improvements. In its setup, spend parallel budget on diverse full rollouts, invoke reflection only on low-score states, and prefer a list-wise comparison when multiple candidate trajectories must be merged. The key unit of analysis is an entire agent episode, so it belongs with test-time scaling but should not be read as evidence about static math sampling alone. Reproduce the GAIA environment before comparing absolute scores.
