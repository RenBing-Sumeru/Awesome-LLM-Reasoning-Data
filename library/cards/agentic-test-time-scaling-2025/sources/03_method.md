The framework places BoN, step-wise BoN, beam search, and diverse verifier tree search inside a CodeAgent setup. It also compares step-based reflection with score-triggered reflection at thresholds below 8, 5, and 2. For verification and merging, it tests majority voting, scalar PRM scoring, and a list-wise PRM that directly compares candidate thoughts or trajectories; diversity is increased through sampling width and heterogeneous rollout models.

Most experiments use GPT-4.1 with a simplified SmolAgents CodeAgent on 165 GAIA validation examples. Parallel-search comparisons use width four and beam size two. The paper also evaluates mixed Claude and Gemini rollout models. It records final GAIA score by difficulty level, then uses ablations to separate the effects of search algorithm, reflection frequency, verifier form, merging rule, and rollout diversity.
