The central artifact is VAR, containing roughly 56K question–reference–long-response–correctness records. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use multi-model generation, automatic initial labeling, and multi-round human review for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is training lightweight answer verifiers for reasoning-model evaluation.
