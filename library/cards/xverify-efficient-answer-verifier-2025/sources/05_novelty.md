Closest prior work usually uses general preference pairs, final-answer correctness, or one holistic score without data specifically designed for the key failures and usage conditions of answer verification. This paper changes the feedback object and evaluation contract by centering VAR and placing task input, candidate response, and auditable evidence in one record, with labels produced through multi-model generation, automatic initial labeling, and multi-round human review. The contribution is the combination of dataset construction, fine-grained organization, and practical use rather than merely another model or pairwise format.
