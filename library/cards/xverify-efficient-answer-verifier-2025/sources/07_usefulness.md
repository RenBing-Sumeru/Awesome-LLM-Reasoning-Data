For a answer verification system, VAR can serve as training data, a hidden evaluation set, or a pre-deployment audit set. Users can map tasks, candidate responses, and evaluation evidence to preferences, scores, correctness labels, or critiques. Success should combine label agreement, classification or ranking metrics, unseen-data generalization, and real gains in training lightweight answer verifiers for reasoning-model evaluation. Tasks without reliable references, with high error costs, or with large distribution shifts require human, executable, or expert review.
