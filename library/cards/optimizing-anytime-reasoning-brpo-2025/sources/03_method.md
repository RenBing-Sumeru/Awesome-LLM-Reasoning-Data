Training starts from DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B and 40,315 DeepScaleR math problems. The model is trained for one epoch with 64 questions per policy iteration. Four thinking budgets are used: 2,000, 4,000, 6,000, and 8,000 tokens. For each question, the procedure samples eight complete thinking processes; at each budget it samples four summaries, limits summaries to 128 tokens, extracts the first answer, and applies a rule-based 0/1 verifier.

The implementation is built on Verl and uses PPO. Thinking and summary generation have separate budgets; a truncated thought is closed with an ellipsis and the named end-think control token before summary generation. BRPO forms its advantage baseline from discounted previous-budget rewards and a group-average future return. The summary policy uses within-budget GRPO groups under a uniform budget prior. Training used eight A100 80GB GPUs for about 30 hours per experiment according to the paper.
