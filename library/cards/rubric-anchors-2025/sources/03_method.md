The reported source substrate is a proprietary corpus of more than 900K instances from broad classes including community Q&A forums, examinations, and conversational datasets. The paper says its rubrics were generated by human experts, LLMs, or hybrid workflows; their relative composition, prompts, and item-level provenance are not disclosed.

For offline filtering, the base model answers each candidate instruction–rubric pair and critic models produce a score distribution. The procedure retains a calibrated central quantile and excludes very high- and very low-scoring items. Training is described as two-stage: constraint-following with programmatic checks and static rubrics, then open-ended tasks with reference-based and instance-specific criteria. The report does not specify the actual critic checkpoints, calibration, optimizer, rollout counts, temperatures, KL treatment, budgets, or exact stage mixtures.
