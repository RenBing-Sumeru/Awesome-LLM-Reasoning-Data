1. **One-sentence position:** EXP-Bench tests whether agents can complete an AI experiment from hypothesis through result analysis rather than merely write a research-code fragment.
2. **Method takeaway:** Paper-structure extraction, repository alignment, starter-code construction, trial runs, and stage scoring are central.
3. **Data takeaway:** It contains 461 tasks from 51 papers with research questions, code, configurations, procedures, and evaluation assets.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Individual stages reach about 20%–35%, while complete executable success is only 0.5%, within compute-budget limits.
5. **Reuse decision:** It suits research-process supervision; lineage must be preserved and execution scores separated from subjective rubrics.
