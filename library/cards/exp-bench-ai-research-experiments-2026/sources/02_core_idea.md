The main contribution is to decompose paper experiments into executable agent tasks with stage-level scoring. EXP-Bench’s 461 records derive from 51 papers and preserve research questions, experimental goals, starter code, components to complete, execution configurations, expected analyses, and grading information. A semi-automated pipeline extracts hypotheses, variables, data, models, and metrics from papers, aligns them with official repository files, and uses human review to confirm fidelity and executability. Evaluation provides sub-scores for design, implementation, execution, and analysis while also requiring complete experimental success, localizing failures to specific research stages rather than relying only on a final textual judge.
