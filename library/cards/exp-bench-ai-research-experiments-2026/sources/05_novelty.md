ResearchCodeBench focuses on local implementation of novel modules, while PaperBench approaches full reproduction but often requires expensive holistic judging. EXP-Bench decomposes complete experiments into multiple executable intermediate objectives and uses a semi-automated pipeline to scale extraction to 461 tasks. Its novelty is not simply asking agents to do research; it establishes a design–implementation–execution–analysis data contract that reveals whether failures arise from hypotheses, code, environments, or interpretation. The evaluated models and agent scaffolds are not the main innovation.
