The authors run 461 tasks with leading LLM agents such as OpenHands and IterativeAgent. Individual dimensions such as experimental design or implementation occasionally reach 20%–35%, yet only 0.5% of tasks result in complete executable experiments, showing that isolated capabilities do not automatically compose into a research loop. Tasks span 51 papers and preserve stepwise procedures, enabling statistical localization of failures rather than a single aggregate score. This establishes a large gap between current agents and complete research experimentation, but outcomes depend on compute resources and timeout budgets, so the 0.5% figure must remain tied to the paper’s setup.
