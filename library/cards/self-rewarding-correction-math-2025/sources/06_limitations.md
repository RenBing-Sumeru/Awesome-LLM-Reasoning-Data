- The public final set is selected and packed, not raw. It omits upstream prompt IDs, candidate IDs, per-stage samples, verifier outputs, evaluation labels as structured fields, trajectory type, rejection reasons, and deterministic joins to the 16.3K raw and turn-level companion releases.
- Paper and release counts do not reconcile: the paper reports 50K IFT prompts and about 32K accepted trajectories, while official repositories expose 40K SFT prompts, a 16.3K raw example, and 31,990 final rows. Subtraction cannot recover stage-wise rejects because these objects need not be nested partitions.
- Sequential-sampling rollout count, decoding temperature, seeds, total candidates, token volume, compute, and monetary cost are unknown. Evaluation temperatures and greedy decoding are separate contracts and cannot fill these construction fields.
- ToRA/SymPy checks final-answer equivalence, not the validity of every reasoning step. Parser and formatting errors can create false accepts or rejects, and the release provides no per-record checker output or verifier-error audit.
- Exact, near-duplicate, and semantic decontamination are unknown. Training comes from NuminaMath-CoT and evaluation includes MATH500, OlympiadBench, and Minerva Math, but no overlap/removal ledger is released.
- The arXiv manuscript is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, but repository, dataset, generated-output, and upstream prompt licenses are unknown. An empty dataset card and a public repository do not establish redistribution compatibility.
- DPO uses only the 40% to 60% of prompts that yield different correctness scores. This selection changes the prompt distribution and makes DPO and PPO data utilization non-equivalent.
- Self-evaluation is class-asymmetric: wrong-answer recognition is much lower than correct-answer recognition in the reported Qwen results. Aggregate final accuracy can conceal premature stopping on wrong answers.
- The paper demonstrates reward hacking for a modified PPO bonus that encourages an incorrect-first/correct-second path. Correctness alone does not make every multi-turn reward design robust.
- Main public data are a Qwen2.5-Math-7B-base example. Llama experiments use different models, formats, and much larger synthetic sets, so their results do not validate every public Qwen record or establish transfer to other domains.
- GitHub has no tagged release, and the paper delegates DPO and parts of PPO to other repositories. At the checked commit, the first prompt-preparation script contains an unterminated string literal and other scripts retain unresolved user-supplied path sentinels. Exact checkpoint-to-table mapping and an immutable, unmodified end-to-end build remain unknown.
