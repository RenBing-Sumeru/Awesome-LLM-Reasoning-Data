For `data_construction_open_release_recipes`, this work is useful as a worked decomposition of a self-correction dataset. It shows where prompt sourcing, component generation, a programmatic answer checker, branch selection, trajectory packing, SFT, pair construction, and scalar-reward RL enter the pipeline. It is especially valuable for distinguishing inference-time self-evaluation from training-time ground-truth filtering.

The 31,990-row final release can support SFT studies of generative correctness labels, conditional revision, correct-to-correct conservatism, and learned stopping. The raw and turn-level companion repositories can help prototype alternative cleaning or packing logic. They should not be treated as a complete replayable build until their records are joined to the exact prompt revisions, candidates, verifier outputs, decisions, and final rows.

An auditable reconstruction should preserve the upstream NuminaMath-CoT record ID and revision, source subset and license, generator checkpoint, rendered messages, decoding settings, seed, every candidate completion, ToRA/SymPy version and raw parser result, normalized answer, branch label, acceptance/rejection reason, selected trajectory ID, and final packed row ID. It should separately record SFT checkpoint selection, each PPO rollout/reward, and every DPO candidate pair rather than conflating them with the released IFT set.

Builders can use the paper's negative results as audit tests. Measure correct-to-wrong transitions, recognition accuracy separately for correct and wrong answers, prompt utilization in DPO, reject rates at every component, and whether a proposed reward encourages intentional first-attempt failure. Add a reject-all or escalation path when the checker is uncertain, and manually audit symbolic-equivalence edge cases.

For atlas readers, the main lesson is that "self-rewarding" describes the deployed control loop, not the provenance of its training signal. The construction oracle is external ground-truth correctness. Downstream benchmark gains motivate the recipe, but safe reuse still depends on lineage, decontamination, licensing, verifier calibration, and a pinned build.
