FINE-CoT can serve as a hidden test for CoT-faithfulness detectors that output an instance label, cause, and evidential steps from a question, answer, and trajectory, and its expert evidence can train lightweight classifiers. Evaluation should be split by domain, generator, and access level and report accuracy, calibration, and compute. When the task is only factual correctness of reasoning text, FaithCoT-Bench's process-faithfulness definition is not equivalent; a grounding benchmark is required. Reports should include recall by cause, transfer across generators, and per-trajectory cost so expensive methods do not appear superior from offline scores alone. Success checks should report detection F1, calibration error, cause recall, and per-trace compute by domain, generator, and black-box or white-box access. Grounding data is needed when the goal is textual factuality.
