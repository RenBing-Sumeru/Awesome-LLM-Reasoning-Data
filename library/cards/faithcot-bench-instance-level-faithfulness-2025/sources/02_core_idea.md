The central FINE-CoT dataset contains more than 1,000 trajectories generated by four representative models across four domains, including over 300 expert-confirmed unfaithful instances. Each unfaithful case records a cause and step-level evidence, shifting evaluation from whether an explanation looks credible to whether this specific trajectory is faithful. The benchmark compares 11 representative methods spanning counterfactual, logit or internal-signal, and LLM-as-judge paradigms. Expert annotations connect trace-level decisions to local evidence, allowing analysis of whether unfaithfulness concentrates in planning, knowledge use, or answer rationalization. The four-domain design also supports analysis by knowledge dependence and task verifiability.
