Previous CoT-faithfulness studies usually report average intervention metrics without instance-level ground truth, making it unclear whether the metric measures faithfulness, importance, or linguistic plausibility. FaithCoT-Bench makes a concrete trajectory the decision unit and provides expert causes and step evidence for unfaithful cases. Its contribution is not a new detector but a common ground-truth surface for comparing detection paradigms. The unified data allows testing whether a method works only on one generator or genuinely detects instance-level unfaithfulness across models. Counterfactuals, logit analysis, and LLM judges are not new. The contribution is unified instance-level ground truth with local evidence, allowing methods with different access budgets to be compared on identical traces.
