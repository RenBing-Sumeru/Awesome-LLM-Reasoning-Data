The controlled budget experiment uses three reasoning models on the 624-prompt AirGapAgent-R-small subset, with three seeds and budgets from 0 to 1,050 tokens (Section 4). Raising the budget from 175 to 1,050 tokens increases answer privacy by 9.85 percentage points on average but causes at least one sensitive field to appear in reasoning 12.35 points more often; two models also lose 7.8 and 3.5 utility points. This supports a channel-specific trade-off under budget forcing, not a claim that every reasoning model or deployment exposes traces in the same way.
