The survey examines a recent strand of LLM reasoning research in which intermediate "thought" tokens are treated as part of the reasoning process rather than as ordinary generated text. It brings together three elements that are often discussed separately: the construction of reasoning trajectories, reinforcement learning for learning to reason, and the use of a larger inference-time token budget. The paper describes how trial-and-error search can generate trajectories for training and how extended deliberation at inference time can affect reasoning accuracy. It is a survey of this research direction, not a release of a model, benchmark, or trace dataset.
