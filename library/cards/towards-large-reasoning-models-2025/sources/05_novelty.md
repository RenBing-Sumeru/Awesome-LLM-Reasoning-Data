Chain-of-thought prompting and reinforcement learning both predate this paper. Its contribution is a 2025 synthesis that places automated trajectory construction and test-time scaling alongside reinforced learning-to-reason, rather than treating them as unrelated topics. The resulting account emphasizes that a reasoning trace may be generated by a teacher, sampled from a model, or discovered through search, and that these origins affect how the trace is used in a system. Because the paper is a survey, it does not establish the reliability, licensing, or reproducibility of every source it discusses.
