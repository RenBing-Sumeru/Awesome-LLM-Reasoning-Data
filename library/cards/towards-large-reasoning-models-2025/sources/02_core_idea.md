The paper's organizing idea is that large reasoning models combine training-time and test-time mechanisms. It presents intermediate thought sequences as the medium through which a model can explore a problem, receive reinforcement signals, and spend more computation during inference. Its taxonomy groups the literature around automated data construction, learning-to-reason techniques, and test-time scaling. This framing separates the origin of a trajectory, the learning procedure that uses it, and the inference process that produces a final answer, even when all three appear in the same system.
