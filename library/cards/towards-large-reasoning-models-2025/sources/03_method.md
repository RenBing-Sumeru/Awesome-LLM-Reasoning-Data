The survey begins with background on LLM reasoning and then reviews the technical components that the authors associate with large reasoning models. Automated data construction covers methods that obtain reasoning trajectories through model generation or search. Learning-to-reason covers reinforcement-learning approaches that use such trajectories or reward signals during training. Test-time scaling covers approaches that allocate additional tokens or search effort during inference. The paper also discusses open-source large-reasoning-model projects and closes with open challenges; it does not introduce a unified experimental protocol of its own.
