Relative to PRM-guided DVTS, GG replaces an external step scorer with statistics already produced by the policy: token-probability confidence plus inexpensive token novelty. Relative to Best-of-N, it branches and prunes at reasoning-step boundaries rather than retaining every full completion, then applies confidence-weighted voting. The training contribution is not generic GRPO but a nonlinear reward that couples programmatic final-answer correctness to chain confidence and strongly penalizes overconfident errors. The atlas direction signal is the explicit conversion from token probabilities and lexical novelty into step-selection records, together with a separate calibration lineage from LIMO prompt to correctness-conditioned GRPO reward. DVTS, LoRA, GRPO, token confidence, and majority voting are existing components; the contribution is their particular calibration-and-search composition.
