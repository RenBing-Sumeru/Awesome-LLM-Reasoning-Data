Test-time scaling can improve mathematical reasoning by sampling or searching over multiple attempts, but Best-of-N spends compute on many full completions and PRM-guided search loads a separate learned verifier. Guided by Gut (GG) asks whether the policy's own generation statistics can guide a lower-cost search. Its relevant record is a prompt followed by multiple step candidates, token probabilities, per-step confidence and novelty, the retained branch in each DVTS subtree, termination reason, candidate final answers, chain confidence, and a confidence-weighted final vote. The paper also creates training records from 817 LIMO prompts: eight complete outputs per prompt, their final-answer correctness, chain confidence, and a scalar GRPO reward. The search records and GRPO rollouts are not released as a standalone dataset.
