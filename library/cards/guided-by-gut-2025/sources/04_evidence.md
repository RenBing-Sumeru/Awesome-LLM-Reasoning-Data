The paper reports four-seed means and maxima on AIME24/25 plus results on AMC23 and MATH500. Under its settings, GG often matches or exceeds the studied CoT, Best-of-N, and PRM-guided baselines while using less GPU or KV-cache memory. The more diagnostic evidence is the ablation: on AIME24 with the 1.5B model, N=32 and M=2, the full confidence reward scores 58.9 versus 54.5 without RL, 54.9 with correctness-only reward, and 54.0 when the incorrect-answer confidence penalty is removed. Novelty weight 0.5 and beam width 2 also outperform the tested alternatives. These observations support sensitivity to the proposed reward and search choices; they do not show that confidence is a correct process label or that released artifacts contain complete traces. The appendix itself shows an incorrect arithmetic step receiving confidence 0.89. Code, recipes, and two model checkpoints are official, but no raw rollout or per-step score release was verified.
