Confidence calibration uses all 817 LIMO mathematical-reasoning prompts with DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B and 7B. GRPO samples G=8 outputs per prompt at temperature 0.6 and trains LoRA adapters for three epochs with rank/alpha 128, learning rate 2e-6 and cosine scheduling, prompt/completion limits 768/8096, batch size 8 per device, and eight accumulation steps on two A100 80GB GPUs. At test time, GG operates over model-delimited reasoning steps. Main experiments allocate 16k tokens and at most 200 steps, using N=32 or 64 total paths with M=2; N=32 therefore creates 16 independent subtrees. A subtree stops on a final-answer token, depth or token limit, or degeneration such as repetition; near a limit, a model-specific cue such as "Final Answer" forces completion. The PRM comparison instead uses Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B-Instruct with about 4k tokens, 50 steps, and N=16 or 32. The author repository publishes training/search code, YAML recipes, and 1.5B/7B checkpoints, but not the raw calibration or search trajectories.
