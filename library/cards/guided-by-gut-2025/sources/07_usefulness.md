For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, GG provides a concrete schema for self-scored tree search. A reusable trace should preserve the prompt and dataset item ID, model/checkpoint and revision, seed and decoding settings, subtree ID, every candidate step and token probability, confidence and novelty components, combined score, retained/rejected decision, depth/token budget, termination reason, forced-finalization event, extracted final answer, chain confidence, vote weight, and correctness only when an independent answer checker is available. Training records should separately retain IsCorrect output, confidence statistic, nonlinear GRPO reward, and adapter revision. This separation supports comparisons of intrinsic selectors, PRMs, Best-of-N, and budget allocation without converting a model's confidence into verified correctness.
