Zhang, Xiangcheng et al. 2025. “Inference-time Scaling of Diffusion Models through Classical Search.” arXiv:2505.23614, with v2 as the current public version. The paper uses annealed Langevin MCMC for local refinement and BFS/DFS for global exploration, testing inference-time scaling in planning, offline reinforcement learning, and image generation. Paper: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23614>; project page: <https://diffusion-inference-scaling.github.io/>; code: <https://github.com/helixml/diffusion-inference-scaling>. The authors are affiliated with Helixon US Inc., Stanford University, and Harvard University.
