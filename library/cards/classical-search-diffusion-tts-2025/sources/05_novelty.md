Compared with treating diffusion inference as a fixed sampling chain or applying Best-of-N reranking only at the end, the paper offers a unified classical-search interpretation: local Langevin updates climb within a current mode, while tree search allocates branches and backtracking across a multimodal generative space. It also places BFS resampling, BFS pruning, and threshold-triggered DFS backtracking in one framework, making the number of samples, local iterations, branches, and backtracking depth auditable test-time compute decisions. The novelty is not a new diffusion training objective or a new general verifier; it is the use of search structure to connect existing diffusion models with task-verification signals, tested across planning, control, and image tasks.
