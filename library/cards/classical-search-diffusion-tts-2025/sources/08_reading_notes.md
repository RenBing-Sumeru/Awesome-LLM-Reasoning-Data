Five points are worth retaining. First, the goal is not to replace the diffusion model, but to add verifier-guided local and global search around a fixed model. Second, local search changes the update direction at every denoising stage through annealed Langevin MCMC; global search uses BFS resource reallocation or DFS backtracking to avoid remaining in one mode. Third, Best-of-N is a necessary baseline because it also spends repeated sampling budget but does not use intermediate states. Fourth, maze, D4RL, and image results demonstrate cross-task applicability of the framework, not reliability of the verifier itself. Fifth, before reproducing results, check whether budgets are matched, verifiers are calibrated, search success coincides with reduced diversity, and any added gain exceeds the actual latency.
