Evidence-backed uses are RLVR, agent training and evaluation at the v1 framework and reward-contract level. The Card supports comparison of multi-turn ToolEnv state transitions, final exact-match/format feedback, tool-augmented traces and mask-based credit assignment. It does not establish that the 51,200 examples, KILT corpus, retrieved documents, tool calls, traces, masks, rewards or checkpoint lineage are reusable or reproducible. A v1 reuse decision requires immutable source and retrieval manifests, rights, split and contamination evidence, verifier and stop contracts, paper-run trace/mask/reward logs, version-pinned dependencies and checkpoints.
