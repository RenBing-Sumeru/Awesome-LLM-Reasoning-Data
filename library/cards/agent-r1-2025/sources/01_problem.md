Agent-R1 is curated as the 2025 arXiv v1 report, not as the substantially revised 2026 project report. V1 states that Multi-hop QA training has 51,200 samples drawn equally and randomly from HotpotQA and 2WikiMultihopQA training splits; it evaluates full HotpotQA/2Wiki development sets and out-of-domain MuSiQue. This is a reported mixture rather than an immutable Agent-R1 data release. V1 policy rollouts produce multi-turn natural-language reasoning and tool actions. NousToolEnv executes actions, returns feedback, calculates reward and packages next state for replay. A trace can include prompt, observation, action, reasoning, tool call and feedback, environment state, reward, termination flag, next observation, action mask, advantage mask and trajectory. The paper-run queries/results, traces, rewards, masks and rollout-to-update links are not released.
