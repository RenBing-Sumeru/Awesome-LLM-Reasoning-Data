The v1 verification contract is programmatic sparse final feedback. It combines exact-match answer correctness with a format score. The format score averages binary indicators for correct answer presentation and valid tool-call syntax. When the format score equals one, reward is exact match between predicted and gold answer; otherwise reward is the format score minus one. ToolEnv may also support environment-defined intermediate process rewards, but no canonical cross-task reward implementation, decision log or v1 verifier snapshot is released. Its stop method can terminate for LLM output, task completion, errors or predefined limits; exact paper-run stop reasons and turn limits remain unknown.
