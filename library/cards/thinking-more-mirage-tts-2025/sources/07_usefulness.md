Use this paper when building a test-time controller that must decide between longer deliberation and broader sampling. A useful experiment log should preserve the prompt, budget-control policy, per-trace token count, continuation prompts, all final answers, aggregation outcome, correctness, latency, and cost. Plotting accuracy and diversity against the same total budget is more informative than reporting only the best accuracy obtained at one chosen length, because it exposes the overthinking region and makes alternative allocation policies comparable.
