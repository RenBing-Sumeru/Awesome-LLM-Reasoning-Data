Agent trajectories are collected across diverse tasks and paired with assessments of intermediate reasoning and tool decisions. Agent-RRM is trained to generate an explicit analysis, a focused critique identifying the main flaw or improvement, and an overall score. Reagent-C appends critique to support a revised attempt; Reagent-R converts scores into optimization guidance; Reagent-U combines the textual and scalar channels during agentic RL. The authors train and evaluate agents across twelve benchmarks, comparing sparse outcome reward and the three integration strategies.
