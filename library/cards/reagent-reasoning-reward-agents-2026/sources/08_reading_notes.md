Agent-RRM produces three feedback surfaces: analysis, focused critique, and score. Reagent-C consumes text, Reagent-R consumes reward, and Reagent-U combines both. Evaluation spans twelve benchmarks; Reagent-U reaches 43.7% on GAIA and 46.2% on WebWalkerQA, providing the main evidence for complementarity. The largest risk is shared judge error across critique and score. Reuse should keep environment success checks separate and measure whether critiques are correct before using them as training targets.
