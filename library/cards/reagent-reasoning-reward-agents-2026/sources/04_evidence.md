The paper tests the framework on twelve benchmarks rather than one agent environment and directly compares critique-only, reward-only, unified feedback, and outcome-based training. Reagent-U reaches 43.7% on GAIA and 46.2% on WebWalkerQA, with broad improvements indicating that the two feedback channels are complementary. Releasing code, models, and datasets supports replication. The evidence shows useful structured supervision in the tested agent stack, but learned critiques and scores may share judge biases and do not constitute environment-grounded correctness by themselves.
