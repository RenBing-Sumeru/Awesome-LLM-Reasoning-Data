Gemini Robotics 1.5 asks whether one vision-language-action system can acquire reusable physical skills across substantially different robot embodiments while also reasoning in language before it acts. The challenge combines heterogeneous observations and action spaces, long-horizon planning, online success detection, tool use, recovery, and safety under physical-world distribution shift.

The report presents two specialized Gemini-derived models. Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5 plans, performs embodied reasoning, estimates progress, detects success, and can use external tools. Gemini Robotics 1.5 translates open-vocabulary natural-language subtask instructions and robot-camera observations into continuous robot actions, optionally emitting natural-language thinking before action. The models can be composed into a long-horizon physical agent.

For a data atlas, the key question is how multi-embodiment robot sensor/action records, Internet multimodal data, thinking traces, captions, progress/success labels, and adversarial examples are constructed and connected. The report makes these object classes visible but leaves their schemas, counts, mixture, provenance, and rights largely private.
