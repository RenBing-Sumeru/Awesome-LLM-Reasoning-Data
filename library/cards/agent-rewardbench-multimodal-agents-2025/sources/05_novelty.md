Closest prior work uses general preference pairs, terminal success, or step accuracy without representing the domain-specific states and failure modes of multimodal agent rewards. This paper changes the record structure and feedback contract by centering Agent-RewardBench and combining inputs, candidates, environmental or evidential state, and labels in one auditable example through constructing better–worse behavior pairs for real multimodal-agent tasks and verifying scenario-specific failures. The contribution is task-specific data construction, capability decomposition, and practical evaluation rather than sample count alone.
