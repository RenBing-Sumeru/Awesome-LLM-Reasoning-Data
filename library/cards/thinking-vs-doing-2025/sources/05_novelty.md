The paper's prior-work baseline is test-time scaling that allocates more tokens or repeated reasoning to a single decision, plus online agent training under a fixed interaction budget. TTI changes the scaling axis: it treats environment actions and the new observations they unlock as the test-time resource, then couples that resource to training through an expanding-horizon curriculum.

The concrete data innovation is not a new browser action language or a new per-step verifier. It is the selection pipeline `current-policy rollout -> terminal success -> successful-episode replay -> per-step action likelihood`, with the permitted horizon raised from 10 toward 30 and recent/domain-balanced sampling shaping replay. This makes the collected supervision non-stationary: later iterations can include behaviors that earlier horizons could not express, while the replay buffer controls which earlier successes remain visible.

The distinction from RLVR matters. A verifier does produce binary terminal reward, but the optimizer does not apply a policy-gradient objective to successful and failed returns. It discards failures and maximizes cross-entropy on actions from retained successes. Calling the released recipe `sft` plus `agent_training` preserves the actual data path and avoids treating any verifier-filtered update as RLVR.

The paper also separates two feedback interfaces within one recipe. WebArena's programmatic ground-truth evaluation checks final task state; WebVoyager's prompted multimodal judge estimates success from responses and screenshots. The shared binary output hides different error surfaces, so evaluator identity must travel with every episode or checkpoint lineage record. This is a useful direction signal for reasoning-data research: interaction scaling cannot be audited from horizon alone; feedback provenance and replay selection determine what the longer interaction budget teaches.

What is not new includes chain-of-thought prompting, behavior cloning, replay buffers, browser environments, and binary terminal evaluation. The result is best understood as an integration and scaling study with a specific curriculum. Before reuse, verify the exact split manifest, task-generator lineage, successful/failed episode counts, evaluator checkpoint and prompt, paper-versus-script settings, environment snapshot, and artifact licenses. Without those checks, neither the novelty claim nor the benchmark gain licenses reconstruction of the reported data distribution.
