Prior work uses length penalties, fixed budgets, concise demonstrations, pruning, or learned stopping. SBT's distinctive construction is to combine a first-correct-step structural signal with a lexicon-based overthinking signal, create fixed and adaptive trace boundaries, retain a masked negative segment, and add a natural-language self-braking cue for SFT. OpenR1-Math, supervised fine-tuning, loss masking, and stop cues are not individually new. The direction signal is the explicit mapping from source trajectory to score, boundary, preserved segment, masked segment, cue, and trained response.
