The source is OpenR1-Math. The authors filter problematic formats and sequences above the 16,384-token training limit, yielding 92,064 instances. Trajectories are segmented into a Foundation Solution and subsequent Evolution Solutions or steps. Construction locates the first standard-answer occurrence, computes reasoning-efficiency and overthinking-marker ratios, combines them into a score, then applies either the fixed SBT-E or adaptive SBT-D boundary rule. A short redundant segment remains in the input with loss masking, while a natural-language braking cue is placed between retained and masked reasoning.

Models include Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B/7B-Instruct and Llama-3.2-1B/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. The reported Megatron-LM setup trains three epochs with learning rate 1e-5, cosine decay, 0.03 warm-up ratio, and maximum length 16,384. Evaluation samples eight outputs per question at temperature 0.7 on GSM8K, MATH500, AMC23, and AIME 2024/2025. Official code is linked under Apache-2.0, but the metadata does not confirm an immutable data release containing every upstream ID, computed score, boundary, mask, rejected record, or source-to-derived lineage; those artifacts remain unknown.
