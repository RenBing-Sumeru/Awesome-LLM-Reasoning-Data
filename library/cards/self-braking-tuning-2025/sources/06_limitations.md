The first textual occurrence of a correct answer need not mark completion of valid reasoning; later verification can expose a mistake or supply justification. Marker lexicons are language-, style-, and model-dependent, and reconsideration words can indicate useful correction rather than waste. The 90/10 weighting and SBT-D thresholds are heuristic. Masked text remains in the context and may influence predictions even though it contributes no loss, while natural-language cues may be imitated without reliable self-assessment.

The study focuses on mathematical traces and selected model families. Step segmentation, answer extraction, upstream OpenR1-Math provenance, filtering manifests, decontamination, derived-record licenses, boundary ledgers, and exact dataset availability are incomplete.
