Large reasoning models can reach a correct intermediate solution and then continue reconsidering, verifying, or exploring alternatives. Self-Braking Tuning turns this overthinking diagnosis into supervised trace rewriting: it identifies a braking boundary, retains productive reasoning, exposes but masks a small redundant segment, and inserts a natural-language stop cue. The primary data objects are OpenR1-Math source trajectories and two derived 92,064-example datasets, SBT-E and SBT-D. A first correct intermediate answer and marker frequency are construction heuristics, not proof that the removed reasoning is unnecessary or wrong.
