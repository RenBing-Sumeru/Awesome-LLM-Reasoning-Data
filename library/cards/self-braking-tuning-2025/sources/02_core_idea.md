SBT scores each trajectory with a weighted overthink measure. The reasoning-efficiency component compares steps needed to reach the first correct answer with total thinking steps; the marker component counts curated reconsideration/verification expressions, with reported weights 90% and 10%. SBT-E keeps the Foundation Solution plus one Evolution Solution and masks the start of the next segment. SBT-D adds steps while recomputing the score, stops when it crosses a primary threshold (reported as 0.2), and masks a following score band. Both insert an epiphany-style braking sentence at the boundary and train only on unmasked tokens.
