Prior methods rely on final answers, generic step labels, or a single LLM judge, leaving a mismatch between feedback and the target. This work centers supervision on problems, candidate solutions, stepwise critiques, and revised outputs, uses separating candidates with reference answers, generating step-by-step error analyses, and verifying revised solutions to generate and filter records, and releases critic_cot. The novelty lies in the data object and acceptance rule rather than standard training algorithms.
