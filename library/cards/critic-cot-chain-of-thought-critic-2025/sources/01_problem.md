An overall score or a statement such as “the reasoning is wrong” cannot identify which step invalidates later conclusions or explain how to repair it. In iterative reasoning, a critic that only predicts success or failure allows the generator to repeat the same mistake or rewrite without justification.

Critic-CoT generates step-by-step error analysis and revision suggestions for candidate solutions, verifies the corrected result, and uses these trajectories to train a critic that can locate, explain, and support repair.
