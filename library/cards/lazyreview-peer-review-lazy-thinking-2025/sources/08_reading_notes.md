1. **Position:**  Detect lazy thinking in peer review through a fine-grained taxonomy.
2. **Method:**  Review analysis, guideline iteration, 500 gold, 1,276 silver, and feedback rewriting.
3. **Data:**  Eighteen phenomena, sixteen lazy-thinking types, with context and rationales.
4. **Evidence:**  Fine-tuning gains about 10–20 points; rewrites win about 85% on constructiveness and justification.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for review diagnosis; reannotate across disciplines and retain human appeal.
