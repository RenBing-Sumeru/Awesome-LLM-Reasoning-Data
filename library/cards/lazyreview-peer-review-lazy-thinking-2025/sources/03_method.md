1. **Derive lazy patterns:** Real NLP reviews are analysed to derive unsupported criticism, generic advice, paraphrase, templating, and related patterns.

2. **Iterate guidelines:** Experts conduct iterative pilot annotation and refine definitions, examples, boundaries, and multilabel rules.

3. **Build gold and silver data:** Humans create five hundred gold segments, after which models produce 1,276 filtered silver annotations.

4. **Detect and rewrite:** Models predict lazy-thinking types and rewrite reviews from the diagnosis; taxonomy version, context scope, and silver-label thresholds must be fixed.
