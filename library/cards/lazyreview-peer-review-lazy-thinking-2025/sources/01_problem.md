Peer reviews may use templated criticism, unsupported assertions, shallow paraphrase, or recommendations that avoid substantive reasoning. Such lazy thinking differs from poor grammar or impoliteness but reduces actionability and trust. Existing review-quality data provide holistic usefulness or acceptance scores and do not localise these patterns.

LazyReview defines a fine-grained taxonomy, has experts annotate review segments, and trains models to detect and rewrite low-quality feedback.
