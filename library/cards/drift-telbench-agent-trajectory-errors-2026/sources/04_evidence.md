The paper compares span detection, earliest-error accuracy, and final-failure identification across models and auditing frameworks. DRIFT’s claim-centric checks improve span localization and first-error accuracy by up to 30 percentage points in some settings over general LLM judges and trajectory classifiers. The result supports evidence–claim alignment, although it depends on expert boundaries between harmful errors and exploratory failures.

Category experiments also show distinct failure patterns across agent frameworks and backbone models.
