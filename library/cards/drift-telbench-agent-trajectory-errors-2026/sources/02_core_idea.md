The authors collect 2,790 real trajectories from two agent frameworks, three backbone models, and three benchmarks and annotate harmful spans through LLM-assisted expert review. They build TELBench with 1,000 human-verified instances containing semantic spans, error types, earliest errors, and effects on final claims. The resource supports trajectory auditing, first-error localization, and reliability evaluation for deep research.

Records also preserve dependency and support relations between every span and the final claims.
