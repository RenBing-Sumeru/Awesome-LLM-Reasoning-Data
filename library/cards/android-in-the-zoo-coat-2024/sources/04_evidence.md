The official repository reports 18,643 screens with CoAT-driven semantic labels and over 2,500 instructions, stored as screenshots and JSON annotations taking about 2.6GB. It gives subset counts: 1,998 train episodes and 13,919 train screens, plus 506 test episodes and 4,724 test screens; Single has train screens but no test split. The repository's comparison table on 50 sampled AITW episodes reports higher GPT-4V action-type accuracy for CoAT than CoA or CoT. These are useful evidence for the prompting and annotation idea, but not a certificate that every AitZ label is semantically correct or that CoAT improves live task completion.
