The paper reports 1,875,014 items totaling 371GB in v2: 1,070,262 Claude Code edits, 669,615 Codex edits, and 186,044 Cursor Agent edits. Median patches touch two to three files and span 67 to 102 changed lines depending on the agent; descriptions are far longer than earlier commit corpora. The paper also reports task-label analysis on 5,000 sampled commits per agent and shows diverse categories including new features, bug fixes, documentation, tests, configuration, and refactoring.

Downstream evidence comes from fine-tuning experiments. In the reported protocol, AgentPack training improves DeepSeekCoder-1.3B from 0.19 to 0.32 pass@1 on HumanEvalFix-Py and DeepSeekCoder-6.7B from 0.39 to 0.50; on JavaScript HumanEvalFix, AgentPack-trained variants outperform their base models. These numbers are bounded by the lightly filtered subset, 4,096-token cutoff, prompt/completion format, sampling protocol, and benchmark choice. They do not prove that every individual edit is correct.
