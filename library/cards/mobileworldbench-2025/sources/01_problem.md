The canonical source is the 2025 arXiv paper, supplemented by the official MobileWorld repository, MobileWorld and mobileworldbench Hugging Face datasets, and released Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct-MobileWorld-SFT model at the revisions pinned in `paper.yaml`. The paper separates two products: **MobileWorldBench** is an offline evaluation benchmark, while **MobileWorld** is a model-annotated training release for supervised fine-tuning.

The paper targets a narrow component of mobile-agent reasoning: predicting the semantic result of one action from the current Android screenshot. The atomic record is a **single state/action/next-state transition**, not a complete episode. Next-State-Generation maps current screenshot plus natural-language action to a short description of expected UI changes; Next-State-QA asks a Yes/No fact about the next state. Source records come from human demonstrations in Android Control and Android in the Wild, but the released benchmark does not execute actions or expose a live device.

MobileWorldBench samples 250 generation transitions and 500 QA transitions. The released repository contains 250 generation rows and 1,787 retained QA rows. One generation row references two absent screenshots, so only **249 of 250 generation rows are image-complete** in the audited official benchmark-image snapshot. MobileWorld is described as roughly 1.4M training items—rounded components of 543k QA pairs and 942k descriptions—and is distributed as three Parquet members inside one tar archive.

This work belongs in `environment_agent_trajectory_data` only at transition level: it serializes observation, action, predicted next-state semantics, and feedback, but provides no full action sequence, environment transition API, reset/replay contract, or terminal success predicate. The bilingual body is prepared at L4 review depth while canonical metadata remains `L3_summary_ready`; supported uses are SFT and evaluation only.
