**Source transitions and benchmark construction.** Current/action/next screenshot triplets are sampled from human demonstrations in Android Control and AiTW. For generation, 250 transitions are selected, their action descriptions are human checked, and all 250 are retained. For QA, 500 source transitions yield 4,000 machine-generated candidates; GPT-4o self-check/relevance filtering leaves 2,458, and qualified Mechanical Turk correctness, relevance, and ambiguity filtering leaves 1,787. The released `qa.csv` contains 1,162 Yes and 625 No labels but omits source dataset, source split, next screenshot, annotator, and filter-decision fields.

**MobileWorld training construction.** Training transitions come from the source training portions of AiTW and Android Control. Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct annotates 90% and Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B annotates 10%. Three state-change descriptions and eight QA candidates are generated per transition with before/action/after evidence. A VLM selects the best description under accuracy/completeness/relevance; QA candidates undergo self-check and relevance filtering. Unlike benchmark QA, the MobileWorld training annotations are not human-filtered. The reported rounded totals are about 543k QA pairs plus 942k descriptions, summarized as 1.4M items.

**SFT recipe.** Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct is fine-tuned for two epochs with AdamW, batch size 128, 1280px images, 6% warmup, and cosine scheduling. The rendered paper does not expose a usable learning-rate value; training-annotation decoding parameters, token limits, random seeds, and checkpoint selection are unknown. The official training archive contains three Parquet members inside `mobileworld.tar`, but has no dataset card or exact transparent 1.4M-row manifest.

**Evaluation.** Each row requires one model call. Generation adds one proprietary judge call using the current/next screenshot, reference description, prediction, and rubric. The paper specifies **`gpt-4o-2024-08-06`**, while `scripts/score_gpt4o.py` defaults to **`gpt-4o-mini`** when no judge configuration is supplied; exact reproduction therefore requires an explicit model/config pin. QA uses temperature 0.0 and exact Yes/No accuracy; generation uses each model's default sampling parameters.

**Release and replay boundary.** Evaluation reads CSV rows and static screenshots. No emulator snapshot, executable action, app fixture, reset routine, full source trajectory, or deterministic replay interface is released. At benchmark-image revision `8a9e39c5f09cf63e9b16fd3ad47cf4eb23adaf13`, one `gen.csv` row lacks both expected images, leaving 249/250 generation rows runnable. Reproduction should pin all repository/data/model revisions, image hashes, judge prompt/parser/model/retry configuration, model decoding, and split IDs; several of those fields remain unknown.
