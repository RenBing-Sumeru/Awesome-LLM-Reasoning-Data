Long-form factuality evaluation commonly follows a decompose-decontextualize-verify pipeline, but decomposition models may miss cross-sentence relations, coreference, and qualifying conditions, or treat incomplete fragments as standalone facts. Existing metrics also emphasize precision—whether stated facts are correct—while largely ignoring recall—whether important facts required by the question are omitted. The paper therefore investigates how to improve fact extraction from long responses and how to build a benchmark with reference fact sets so evaluation can measure both false statements and missing essential information.
