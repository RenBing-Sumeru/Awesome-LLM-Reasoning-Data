A complete reference-fact set is difficult to exhaust for open questions; even the union of strong-model and human answers may omit alternative explanations or newly emerged facts. Fact granularity materially affects precision and recall because splitting or merging a relation changes the denominator. VeriFact still relies on an LLM to detect omissions and restore context, which may introduce information absent from the original response. Correctness also depends on the freshness and reliability of retrieved evidence. Reuse should therefore fix a fact-granularity guideline, record evidence provenance, and update reference sets for time-sensitive questions.
