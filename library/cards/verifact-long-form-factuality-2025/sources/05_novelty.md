Prior methods such as FActScore and SAFE mainly improve retrieval or verification of individual claims and often assume that fact decomposition is reliable. Benchmarks such as FactBench also emphasize whether generated claims are correct. VeriFact moves the error analysis upstream to fact extraction itself, explicitly repairing insufficient decontextualization and missing relational facts. FactRBench’s central novelty is its reference fact sets, which extend long-form evaluation from one-sided precision to a precision-recall trade-off. It asks not only whether stated claims are factual, but also whether the answer includes the important facts it should contain.
