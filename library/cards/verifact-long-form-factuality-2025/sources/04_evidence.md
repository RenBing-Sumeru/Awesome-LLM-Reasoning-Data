Experiments show that VeriFact extracts more complete fact sets and preserves complex relational information that conventional atomization often destroys, improving the agreement of downstream factuality verification with human judgments. Model comparisons on FactRBench further show that larger models within a family often improve both precision and recall, but high precision does not guarantee high recall: a model may make few false claims while omitting substantial essential content. This observation demonstrates the need for a two-sided benchmark. The evidence is strongest for the selected long-form QA distributions, and the reference fact sets may still be incomplete.
