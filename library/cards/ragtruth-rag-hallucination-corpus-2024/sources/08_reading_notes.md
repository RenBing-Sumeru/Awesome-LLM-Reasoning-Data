1. **Position:**  RAGTruth provides response-, word-, and severity-level human hallucination labels for real RAG outputs.
2. **Method:**  2,965 sources, six-model multi-temperature generation, two-level annotation, and mitigation.
3. **Data:**  17,790 responses, 7,664 hallucinatory responses, and 14,289 spans under MIT.
4. **Evidence:**  A small fine-tuned detector competes with prompt-based GPT-4 and reduces hallucinations.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for RAG verifiers; resample new models/retrievers and inspect source errors.
