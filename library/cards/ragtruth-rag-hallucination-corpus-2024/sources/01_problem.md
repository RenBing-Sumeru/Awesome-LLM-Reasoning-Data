Even with retrieved source text, RAG models generate content unsupported by or contradictory to the evidence. Earlier hallucination corpora often use synthetic errors, one task, or response-level labels, limiting training of general detectors that localise spans and represent natural errors across models and temperatures.

RAGTruth collects natural outputs from six LLMs on four RAG tasks and annotates them at instance, word-span, and error-type levels.
