Compared with assigning a whole chain of thought one correct-or-incorrect label, this work changes the unit or acquisition path of process evidence. The work constructs process rewards without manual process labels by inferring per-step training signals from terminal correctness and pairwise comparisons. It samples multiple solution paths, organises positive and negative traces by final-answer verification, and decomposes path-level preferences into learnable intermediate states. This is its direction signal; the reusable contribution is the data contract, quality control, and evaluation boundary.
