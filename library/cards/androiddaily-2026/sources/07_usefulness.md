AndroidDaily is useful as a checklist for evaluating agents in opaque, real-world mobile applications. A reusable record should preserve task id, app id and version, category, starting state, account/region assumptions, goal text, guideline tiers, visual trajectory frames, actions, evaluator prompt/model/version, step-level diagnostics, final success label, human-audit sample, and privacy redactions. For atlas work, it is a strong example of replacing hidden-state success predicates with observable process obligations, while keeping the judge and app-version boundary explicit.
