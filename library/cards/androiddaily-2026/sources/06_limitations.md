Correctness is relative to GRADE's observable guidelines and the visual trajectory available to the evaluator. Closed-source apps can change UI, prices, policies, login requirements, content feeds, and regional behavior after the benchmark is written. A visual judge may miss state changes that are not visible, may over-credit plausible-looking trajectories, or may be biased by the evaluator model. The checked official sources did not provide a stable public code or data release, so independent reproduction depends on later artifacts or careful reimplementation. Privacy and terms-of-service constraints are central because real apps and accounts may expose personal or regulated data.
