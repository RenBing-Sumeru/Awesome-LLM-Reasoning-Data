The arXiv abstract reports 350 realistic daily-use tasks over 94 high-frequency Android applications. It reports that GRADE reaches 87.37% agreement with human evaluators, and that the strongest evaluated model reaches 62.0% success on AndroidDaily. These are the main trust signals: benchmark breadth, human-aligned evaluator agreement, and a model-performance gap. Row-level evidence is the observable trajectory checked against task guidelines, not a hidden-state verifier. Evidence is bounded by the exact closed-source app versions, evaluator model/prompt, human-agreement sample, task guideline quality, and the absence of a durable public code/data artifact in the checked sources.
