The contribution is AndroidDaily plus GRADE, a guideline-grounded evaluator for closed-source mobile workflows. AndroidDaily supplies 350 realistic daily-use tasks across 94 high-frequency Android applications; GRADE converts each task into observable evaluation guidelines with three tiers: operational obligations, output quality, and negative constraints. The feedback contract is process-aware trajectory judgment: the evaluator inspects the visual trajectory and produces diagnostic judgments rather than relying on hidden app state. Closest comparisons are AndroidWorld, AITW/AndroidControl-style action datasets, and GUI benchmarks that depend on open-source or simulated environments. The direction label is verifiable real-app mobile-agent evaluation.
