1. Inputs: a real-world Android task, the target closed-source application, screenshots or video-like visual observations from the agent trajectory, executed actions, and task-specific guidelines. 2. Pipeline: define daily-use tasks across app categories such as transportation, shopping, local services, entertainment, content creation, social media, and utilities; run mobile GUI agents; collect visual trajectories; apply GRADE's guideline tiers to diagnose whether required operations were completed, output quality is acceptable, and forbidden or negative conditions are avoided. 3. Outputs: task-level success or failure, step-level diagnostic judgments, and aggregate benchmark scores. 4. Feedback: GRADE is the judge, calibrated against human evaluators in the paper. Reuse requires pinning app versions, accounts or region settings, device/emulator state, prompt/scaffold, visual recorder, evaluator prompt/model version, task guidelines, and privacy handling for trajectories.
