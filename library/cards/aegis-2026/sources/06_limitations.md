- **Attribution by construction:** Gold labels equal the planned target-agent/error-mode injections. When several agents are perturbed, terminal failure shows that the combined intervention defeated the evaluator, not that every label was independently necessary or causal. Controlled MAST modes also underrepresent emergent, interaction-induced, or unseen failures; the paper states the fixed taxonomy and controlled plans as limitations.
- **Failure-only selection:** Only interventions that turn a successful baseline into evaluator-confirmed failure enter the 9,533-row corpus. Counts and records for non-failing injections, self-corrections, retries, and rejected attempts are unknown. This removes resilience evidence and can make intervention artifacts or evaluator weaknesses predictive of the label.
- **Mixed-verifier risk:** Math normalization, code tests, scientific tolerances, multiple-choice extraction, and the GPT-4o-mini GAIA judge have different false-positive and false-negative modes. The exact evaluator versions and GAIA judge prompt/model snapshot are unpinned; a format or judge error can determine both retention and the apparent ground truth.
- **Split and contamination risk:** The approximate 7,147/1,786/600 train/validation/test split is non-overlapping only at the reported trajectory level. No grouping by upstream task or successful baseline, deduplication, or decontamination method is disclosed. Multiple injected variants of shared source content may therefore cross splits, while all in-domain splits reuse the same six benchmark families.
- **Incomplete lineage and replay:** Public rows do not verify stable baseline-to-variant links, exact GPT-4o-mini/manipulator snapshots, attempts per baseline, reset state, dependency lock, or record-specific evaluator version. Mutable GitHub and HF histories lack one release manifest, so identical end-to-end construction cannot be assumed from the available code.
- **Release and license boundary:** HF data and GitHub code declare MIT, while arXiv v6 is CC BY 4.0; rights for upstream benchmark items, framework outputs, and model-generated traces are not reconciled. The paper says models are open-sourced, but no official model artifact link was verified. Dataset/code availability does not settle model availability or upstream redistribution rights.
- **Label-audit gaps:** DyLAN post-hoc refinement lacks a reconstructable procedure. The three-expert 100-trajectory study reports κ values but not recruitment, raw labels, per-mode errors, disagreement adjudication, compensation, or consent, limiting audit of rare labels and human comparison.
- **Generalization and experiment drift:** SFT overfits synthetic style on Who&When after two epochs, and all tested models miss the Appendix A.2 numeric root cause. The main text and Appendix D disagree on whether DCL used four RTX 4090 or four RTX 3090 GPUs. Reported benchmark improvements therefore do not prove broad failure coverage, data quality, or exact reproducibility.
