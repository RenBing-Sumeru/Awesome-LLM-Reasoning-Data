MultiChallenge constructs a realistic multi-turn benchmark targeted at weaknesses of frontier models. It organizes failure modes into four challenge categories and provides instance-level rubrics for each conversation. Tasks require models to track information across turns, resolve ambiguity and corrections, and obey persistent or updated constraints rather than merely repeat context. The authors also validate agreement between automatic judges and experienced human annotators, enabling lower-cost evaluation at scale. Its main data contribution is the combination of complete dialogue trajectories, target responses, and conversation-specific scoring criteria for studying multi-turn instruction following, context-state management, and LLM-as-a-judge systems.
