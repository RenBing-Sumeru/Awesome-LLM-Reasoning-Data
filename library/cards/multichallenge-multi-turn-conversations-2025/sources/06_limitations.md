1. **Coverage:** Four challenge categories cannot represent every real dialogue setting, such as long-term relational memory, tool use, or multi-party collaboration.

2. **Static scripts:** Fixed trajectories cannot model users dynamically changing later questions in response to model outputs, weakening the interactive feedback loop.

3. **Judge dependence:** Instance-level rubrics reduce subjectivity, but complex semantic errors are still judged by an LLM and may exhibit model-family bias.

4. **Language and time:** The data primarily reflects English usage and frontier models from a particular period. Some examples may become saturated as models improve.

Users should combine human review, per-category reporting, and real online dialogue logs rather than relying only on the aggregate score.
