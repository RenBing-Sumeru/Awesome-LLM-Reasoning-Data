Models trained for long reasoning often stop benefiting when test-time output length exceeds their maximum training budget. e3 asks how post-training can teach a model to use extra tokens for in-context exploration - generating, checking, revising, or trying another hypothesis - rather than merely extending a single path. For this track, the operative data object is a prompt-conditioned group of sampled responses with outcome rewards and relative advantages under a specified token budget. The official datasets publish prompt-level static records and aggregate statistics, not those training-time groups.
