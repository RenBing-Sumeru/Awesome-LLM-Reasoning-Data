For Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data, e3 provides a concrete design for studying how failed and successful response groups, task difficulty, and token budgets jointly affect RLVR exploration. Reuse can compare fixed versus coupled curricula or positive-only versus full GRPO, but should log every completion, reward, normalized advantage, truncation flag, policy checkpoint, and update membership. The public Parquet files are useful as staged prompt/ground-truth inputs and aggregate difficulty metadata after provenance and rights review; they must not be described as a released corpus of the online grouped rollouts.
