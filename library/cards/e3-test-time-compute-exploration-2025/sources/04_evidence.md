The paper reports that retaining negative gradients increases response diversity, verification attempts, length, and extrapolation in settings with a verification-generation gap, and that the coupled curriculum improves 32k evaluation after training only to 16k. Ablations and the released e3-1.7B checkpoint support the proposed recipe as an empirical method result. They do not show that every long trace is valid, that the static solution field is correctly sourced, or that the public data reconstruct the online feedback process. Artifact inspection verifies two training datasets, one 60-row HMMT/AIME 2025 test artifact, code, scorer, scripts, project page, and model, while confirming the absence of frozen grouped rollouts and per-trace advantages.
