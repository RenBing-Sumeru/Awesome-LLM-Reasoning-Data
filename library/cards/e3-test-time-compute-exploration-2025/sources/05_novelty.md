Its contribution is to connect a rollout-level optimization effect - negative advantages on failed responses - with a staged data-and-budget curriculum so that the policy learns to implement exploration inside one long response. Unlike static SFT trace distillation, failed on-policy samples directly influence the next policy update; unlike ordinary fixed-budget GRPO, task difficulty and maximum response length change together. The novelty claim should therefore be evaluated at the feedback and curriculum contract, not inferred from benchmark rank.
