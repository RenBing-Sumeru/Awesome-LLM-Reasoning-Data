
The official e3-math-easy and e3-math-medhard Parquet releases contain 12,853 and 2,500 rows. Each row stores a chat prompt, rule ground truth, solution field, scalar reward, aggregate length/correct_length/incorrect_length, and metadata; these are static prompt-level records, not the current policy's grouped trajectories. Training uses verL GRPO with vLLM, temperature 0.6, learning rate 1e-6, KL loss coefficient 0.001, and output budgets of 8192 then 16384 tokens. The custom scorer requires the closing think tag, extracts a final boxed answer, and applies MathD-style or SymPy equivalence for reward 1 versus 0. Stage 2 samples 32 responses per prompt and retains negative-advantage learning; stage-1 rollout count and some 8k hyperparameters conflict across paper prose, Table 5, and the released script.
