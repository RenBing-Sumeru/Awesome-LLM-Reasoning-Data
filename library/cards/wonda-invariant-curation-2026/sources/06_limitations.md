**Method and verifier.** UAutomizer is the sole invariant source and acceptance oracle. Sound conclusive decisions do not imply completeness: timeouts and `UNKNOWN` can drop useful candidates, and changing solver heuristics may change both grades and runtimes. Grade 2/3 establishes induction and sufficiency for the encoded 32-bit `unreach-call` query, not semantic equivalence to the raw invariant, readability, minimality, or usefulness under another verifier. Cast removal is acknowledged as potentially non-semantics-preserving; re-verification protects accepted candidates but not the interpretation of rejected transformations.

**Data and release.** The raw table, curated-full table, evaluation data, models, and code are public, but release boundaries matter. Curated-full has 7,763 rows and can include grade-1 candidates; the paper's 7,284-sample V2 experiment additionally requires grade >= 2 and a 1,024-token limit. SFT-ready caches are model-family-specific local builds, while legacy Hub caches remain available. Treating these artifacts as one interchangeable dataset can silently change the target population. Rejected grade-0 candidates and complete API-generation traces are not a first-class release.

**Split, provenance, and rights.** The paper reports an 80/20 sample split but not whether all variants from one program stay in one partition. Exact-match candidate deduplication does not address semantic duplicates or related program families. Training and evaluation descend from InvBench/SV-COMP-related sources, yet no item-level overlap/decontamination manifest is published. The repository is MIT; that does not establish redistribution/training rights for upstream programs, Hub tables, model outputs, or benchmark derivatives. No verified per-source dataset-license manifest was found.

**Measurement and attribution.** Grade 3 and the Easy/Hard boundary depend on hardware, UAutomizer version, concurrency, timeout, and baseline timing. VBP is a virtual per-instance oracle portfolio; VBP-E2E adds measured model latency only for qualifying instances and is not the same as a deployed scheduling policy. Model comparisons do not hold correctness constant—Qwen3-14B-V2 matches GPT-5.2's VBP-E2E while having much lower correctness. Three-run means do not characterize all sampling or solver variance.

**Reproduction and drift.** The checked official `master` commit has no release tag. Current training defaults do not fully reproduce the paper's stated LoRA assignment for Qwen3-8B and full tuning for Qwen3-14B, making commit/config pinning necessary. The served Kimi K2 Thinking revision, API nondeterminism, random seeds, and exact training/evaluation artifact hashes are not disclosed. These are curator-identified reproducibility risks; they are not evidence that the reported results are wrong.
