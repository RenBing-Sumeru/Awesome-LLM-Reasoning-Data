The main evidence is a controlled V0→V1→V2 target ablation on the 123-instance Hard split, averaged over three runs. For Qwen3-4B, base/V0/V1/V2 correctness is 22.8/29.3/33.1/44.4%, while speedup rate is 11.1/13.6/12.7/24.7%; validity falls from 99.2% for the base model to 81.3% under raw V0, recovers to 97.6% under normalization, and reaches 100% under V2. For Llama-3.1-8B, V2 raises correctness from 14.9% to 45.5% and speedup rate from 5.4% to 18.4%. Mistral-7B shows the same raw-target failure: validity changes from 93.8% base to 68.8% V0, then 95.9% V1 and 99.5% V2. These results support the claim that target construction matters; they do not isolate the LLM rewrite from every change in sample count or target distribution.

Table 3 gives end-to-end conditions. Qwen3-4B-V2 lowers VBP-E2E from 185.7 to 165.7 seconds and raises correctness from 22.8% to 44.4%. Qwen3-14B-V2 reports VBP-E2E 162.9 seconds, close to GPT-5.2's 163.4 seconds, but its correctness is 43.4% versus 72.4% for GPT-5.2. That comparison supports a portfolio-time claim, not model parity. Qwen3-0.6B is a useful negative result: V2 correctness is slightly lower than base (27.9% versus 28.5%) even as speedup rate improves (14.1% versus 12.2%) and VBP-E2E drops from 183.0 to 174.1 seconds. Correctness and operational utility are therefore demonstrably different labels.

The data-yield evidence is also auditable. Starting from 4,000 raw invariants, 2,995 verbose items generate 11,980 candidates; 6,584 (55.0%) reach grade >= 2. Another 1,005 compact items are checked directly, 983 of which reach grade >= 2. The pipeline emits 7,763 curated rows; grade and length filtering leaves 7,284 V2 SFT samples, of which 4,516 are grade 2 and 2,768 grade 3. Mean target length is 15.8 tokens. The grade-3 subset averages 2.13× verifier speedup, with a reported maximum of 41.39×.

The authors also report limits to the observed gain. Only a few of 20 baseline-timeout Hard instances are solved per run; Qwen3-14B-V2 averages about 3.3. On the Easy split, V2 improves correctness, but direct verification already averages about 6.15 seconds and speed metrics are omitted. All numbers are author-reported under one UAutomizer/hardware setup; this Card did not independently reproduce training or verification.
