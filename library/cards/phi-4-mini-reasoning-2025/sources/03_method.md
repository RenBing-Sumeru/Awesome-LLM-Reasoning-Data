Section 4 names eight public resources: AquaRAT (98K), Ape210K (210K), MetaMathQA (395K), MathInstruct (262K), TAL-SCQ5K (5K), OpenR1-Math (220K), Bespoke-Stratos-17k (17K), and OpenThoughts-114K (114K), plus several in-house seed datasets. Existing reasoning annotations are used directly when present. Otherwise only the question is retained and DeepSeek-R1 (671B) samples approximately eight CoT responses. The report totals about 10M rollouts across 1.6M samples. A math-verification tool checks verifiable answers; because it can reject complex correct solutions, GPT-4o-mini rechecks initially incorrect rollouts. Records are also labeled for domain, difficulty, and repetitive patterns. Exact mixture weights, item-level provenance, prompts, yields, and licenses are unknown.

Mid-training consumes the full constructed set and retains correct responses through rejection sampling. It uses packed 16K sequences, batch size 128, learning rate 1e-5, five epochs, and 0.1 warmup. SFT selects an undisclosed compact subset above college difficulty and uses unpacked 20K sequences with the same reported batch, learning-rate, epoch, and warmup settings. DPO uses high-school-or-harder questions, correct/incorrect pairs, 16K sequences, learning rate 5e-7, and one epoch; pair counts and selection yields are not reported.

For RL, the paper discusses GRPO and reports three stability problems: within-group positive responses spanning roughly 12K-20K tokens, zero gradients when group rewards are uniform, and an exploration/evaluation temperature mismatch. Its recipe samples several candidate prompts and keeps those with relatively uniform response lengths; oversamples difficult prompts, retains all positives, and samples an equal number of negatives; filters overly easy groups above an example 50% accuracy threshold; and anneals temperature from 1.0 to 0.6 over the first half of training. RL uses learning rate 5e-7 and 25K sequences. The final RL algorithm code, rollout-group size, prompt pool, verifier implementation, and remaining optimizer settings are not released.
