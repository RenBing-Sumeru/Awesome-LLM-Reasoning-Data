Training-data disclosure stops at named source classes and aggregate scale. Several in-house seeds are undisclosed; public-source mixture weights, per-source retained counts, item IDs, collection dates, exact SFT and DPO subset sizes, RL prompt and rollout counts, split membership, and an integrated license ledger are absent. No contamination or benchmark-overlap audit is provided. The paper's direct-reuse description and the later model card's exclusively-DeepSeek-R1 description cannot both serve as a complete item-level provenance account without a manifest.

The feedback layer is not reproducible. The math verifier and its parser are not released, even though the paper acknowledges false negatives. GPT-4o-mini is a proprietary dependency for rechecking and difficulty filtering; prompts, model snapshot, calibration, disagreement rates, and adjudication rules are unknown. Final-answer reward does not establish the validity of intermediate reasoning and can be vulnerable to answer-format parsing or reward exploitation. DPO pair statistics and the complete RL algorithm configuration are also missing.

The experiments are limited to mathematical reasoning benchmarks and do not establish transfer to other domains, languages, or high-stakes uses. The staged ablation is informative but does not isolate data composition from optimization, compute, and sampling changes. The official release provides model weights and inference-oriented assets, not the training corpus, stage manifests, training code, verifier, or replayable environment; independent reproduction of the reported pipeline is therefore not currently possible.
