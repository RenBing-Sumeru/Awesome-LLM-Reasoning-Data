The contribution is a four-stage sequence tailored to a small base model. First, Phi-4-Mini performs packed causal-language-model mid-training over the full accepted long-CoT collection. Second, unpacked SFT uses a compact subset described as diverse and above college difficulty. Third, Rollout DPO reuses rejected generations: GPT-4o-mini keeps questions labeled high-school level or above, correct rollouts become preferred responses, and incorrect rollouts become dispreferred responses. Fourth, online RL optimizes final-answer correctness while adding prompt selection, reward-group rebalancing, and temperature annealing to address small-model instability.

The construction object is therefore not one homogeneous dataset. It is a lineage of source questions and existing annotations, DeepSeek-R1 trajectories where traces are missing, verification and descriptive labels, selected correct traces, preference pairs, and fresh RL groups. The feedback contract is mixed: programmatic answer checking is supplemented by a proprietary LLM recheck, preferences are answer-outcome-derived, and RL receives an answer-level scalar signal. This supports classification as distillation, preference learning, and RLVR in the frontier-report disclosure ledger, but not as process supervision or a fully open recipe.
