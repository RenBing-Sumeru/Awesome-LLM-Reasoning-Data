The official [arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21233) and [HTML text](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.21233) support the four stages, named source table, approximately eight generated rollouts per missing-trace question, 10M-rollout/1.6M-sample aggregate, math-tool verification, GPT-4o-mini rechecking, preference construction, RL stabilization measures, and training settings. The paper's staged table reports AIME 2024 / MATH-500 / GPQA Diamond moving from 10.0 / 71.8 / 36.9 for Phi-4-Mini to 30.0 / 82.9 / 42.6 after mid-training, 43.3 / 89.3 / 48.3 after SFT, 50.0 / 93.6 / 49.0 after DPO, and 57.5 / 94.6 / 52.0 after RL. These are author-reported behavior measurements under the stated evaluation protocol, not independent evidence that the data or verifier is high quality.

The official [Hugging Face repository](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-mini-reasoning) releases the 3.8B model weights under MIT and provides a model card, inference files, and the report PDF. Its model card reports 128K context, 128 H100-80G GPUs, two days of training, 150B training tokens, and approximately 30B tokens of retained math content; those figures are not all reconciled with stage-level counts in the paper. The later official [Data Summary](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-mini-reasoning/blob/main/data_summary_card.md) confirms public and synthetic data use, February 2025 acquisition, and cleaning.

An audit-relevant inconsistency remains. The paper states that existing reasoning annotations are directly reused and DeepSeek-R1 generates answers only where trajectories are absent. The model card and Data Summary instead describe the training data as exclusively synthetic mathematical content generated by DeepSeek-R1. No released item manifest resolves this difference. The official repository does not contain the 10M rollouts, selected SFT subset, preference pairs, RL pool, training code, verifier code, or decontamination report.
