FMC contains 3,922 natural-language problems and 9,787 compilable Lean formalizations, with 64.46% assessed as at least above-average quality. Controlled analyses show that few-shot examples, compiler-error feedback, and additional samples improve formalization success and quality, so the final scale is not merely unchecked LLM output. Experiments with three automated theorem provers remain challenging, supporting value as a proving benchmark. The boundary is that compilability and quality ratings do not guarantee complete semantic fidelity, especially when natural-language conditions are implicit.
