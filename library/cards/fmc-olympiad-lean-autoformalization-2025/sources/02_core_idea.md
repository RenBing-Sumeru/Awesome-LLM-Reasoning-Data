The main contribution is a large Olympiad-level natural-language–Lean many-to-many alignment corpus and its error-feedback construction pipeline. Multiple formal candidates may be retained per problem, so 3,922 problems correspond to 9,787 Lean statements. Every record undergoes Lean syntax and type checking, and 64.46% are assessed as at least above-average quality. The repository releases problems, formal files, and construction code for replay in a specified Lean environment. Multiple candidates support study of equivalent formalizations and allow compilation errors, sampling counts, and quality grades to serve as filtering or curriculum signals.
