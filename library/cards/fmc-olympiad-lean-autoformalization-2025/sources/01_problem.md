Automated theorem proving requires large aligned corpora of natural-language problems and formal statements, but manual Lean formalization is slow and expert-intensive. One-shot LLM translation of competition problems often produces syntax errors, type mismatches, undefined symbols, or missing conditions. Retaining only compilable outputs also does not establish whether a formalization is faithful or too weak. Limited data prevents both autoformalization and theorem proving from scaling to Olympiad-level mathematics.

FMC introduces a training-free automated pipeline in which an LLM samples Lean statements, compiler errors drive revisions, and compilable candidates receive quality assessment. The final dataset aligns 3,922 natural-language competition problems with 9,787 Lean formalizations for autoformalization and prover benchmarking.
