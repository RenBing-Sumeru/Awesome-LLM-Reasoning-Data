Earlier autoformalization datasets rely on manual alignment, cover simpler problems, or perform one-shot generation followed by compilation filtering. FMC places Lean error messages in an automatic repair loop and retains multiple compilable candidates per problem rather than forcing a single formulation. It also combines scale with difficulty: nearly four thousand Olympiad problems yield almost ten thousand Lean statements. The pipeline trains no specialized translator and is therefore portable, but compiler feedback mainly addresses formal validity, leaving semantic fidelity to additional quality assessment.
