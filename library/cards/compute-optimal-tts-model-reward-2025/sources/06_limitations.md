The work is an extensive empirical study, but its compute-optimal choices are evaluated on the stated mathematical benchmarks and PRM/model pools. A PRM's behavior can change under a new prompt format, decoding setting, model revision, or domain, so the reported choice map cannot simply be copied into another serving environment. The headline cross-scale comparisons also depend on the paper's selected budgets and protocols. Verification cost, accelerator throughput, batching, and latency can differ from proxy comparisons, and exact-answer mathematics may not predict behavior on open-ended tasks.
