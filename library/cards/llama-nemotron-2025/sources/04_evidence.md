The official arXiv report is strong evidence for the staged recipe. It specifies the reasoning-toggle prompts, domain sources, candidate counts for math, code decoding parameters, named generators and judges, filtering operations, SFT schedules, LN-Ultra GRPO rewards and curriculum, instruction-following RLOO, and preference-optimization reward model. It also reports the 33,011,757-example domain table and explicitly distinguishes the three model-specific training paths.

The official Hugging Face dataset is a real downloadable artifact, not merely a report claim. It has SFT and RL configurations, versioned files, a dataset card, per-row license and training metadata, and visible examples. The SFT preview exposes `input`, `output`, `category`, `license`, `reasoning`, `generator`, `used_in_training`, `version`, and `system_prompt`. The RL instruction-following preview replaces response output with structured constraint arguments. The current viewer estimates about 3.91M SFT rows, while RL full generation fails because one shard's structured fields cannot be cast to the declared schema. Raw files may still be downloadable, but this failure affects convenient programmatic audit.

Official NVIDIA model cards verify released weights for Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B-v1, Llama-3.3-Nemotron-Super-49B-v1, and Llama-3_1-Nemotron-Ultra-253B-v1, with NVIDIA Open Model License terms and applicable Llama terms. The NVIDIA collection groups the family. NVIDIA also identifies NeMo, NeMo-Aligner, Megatron-LM, and NeMo-Skills as relevant codebases. However, the report used a NeMo-Aligner development branch, and the exact commits, configurations, and end-to-end run manifest are not pinned by the Card's checked sources.

Benchmark tables support claims about the released models under stated decoding and evaluation settings. They do not verify that every data row has correct reasoning, complete provenance, compatible rights, or no overlap. Likewise, ablations on curriculum or reasoning mode support training-design choices but do not reconcile the released-data counts or expose rejected candidates.
