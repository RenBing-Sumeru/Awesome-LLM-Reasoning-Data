A builder can reuse the documented recipe to generate profile-conditioned candidate reasoning, separate answer filtering from model judging, derive a compact tag vocabulary, and bootstrap tagged syntax before RL. The paper also gives two concrete ways to construct preference pairs: profile usage and within-profile output quality.

An auditor can separate three questions: whether a trace obeys the tag grammar, whether a sequence receives a favorable user-aware score, and whether the reasoning is semantically valid and faithful to the profile. The paper supplies supervision for the first two; the third needs additional evidence.

Reproduction should begin from the paper and legally reusable source datasets, then verify missing artifacts and exact settings. This card offers a construction recipe, not a downloadable corpus or a certified step-correctness benchmark.
