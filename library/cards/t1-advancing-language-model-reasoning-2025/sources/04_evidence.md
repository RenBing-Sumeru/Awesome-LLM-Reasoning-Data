The main reported evidence is conditional on the authors' models, benchmarks, and greedy Pass@1 protocol. In the author repository's result table, T1 with Qwen2.5-32B reports 92.4 on MATH500, 50.6 on AIME, 49.6 on OmniMATH500, and 56.1 on GPQA; its listed SFT-only counterpart reports 83.4, 24.9, 34.6, and 49.5. These are author-reported benchmark results, not a quality audit of the released data.

Two diagnostic results are more informative for the recipe. With top-p 0.95, the reported temperature sweep for the 14B setting gives MATH500/AIME/OmniMATH values of 78.2/19.1/32.0 at 0.9, 84.6/29.0/37.8 at 1.1, 86.4/29.3/38.6 at 1.2, and 84.6/24.3/36.4 at 1.3; changing to min-p 0.05 at temperature 1.2 gives 78.8/11.5/31.6. This supports a setting-specific exploration claim, not a universal temperature rule. (Paper Table 2.)

The bad-response penalty ablation reports, at 120 RL steps, 81.2 accuracy and 1.6% overlong ratio with the penalty versus 76.4 and 16.3% without it; the no-penalty run is not reported at 160 steps. (Paper Table 3.) Figure 6 shows increasing average generated tokens on Qwen2.5-32B from roughly 2k to 7k raises AIME average from 24.3 to about 50, while MATH500 rises from 86.1 to 92.4. These observations describe the reported setup only; no independent reproduction or contamination audit was found.
