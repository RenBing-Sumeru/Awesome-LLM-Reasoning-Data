Open-SWE-Traces combines four properties in one public episode schema: 207,489 trajectories, nine programming languages, two agent-harness families, and paired thinking/non-thinking teacher modes. The schema retains conversation roles, tools, model and reference patches, source-repository metadata, and a three-valued outcome, so users can inspect both the action process and terminal result.

Its most consequential design choice is to release unresolved and unavailable-outcome episodes instead of restricting the corpus to successful demonstrations. The paper then ablates resolved-only versus full-data training, creating a direct connection between a curation choice and downstream behavior. The integrity pipeline also targets agent-specific shortcuts—test editing and git-history inspection—rather than applying only generic text deduplication.

What is not new should remain visible. SWE-rebench-V2 provides the executable task substrate; OpenHands and SWE-agent provide the interaction frameworks; MiniMax-M2.5 and Qwen3.5 provide teacher behavior; repository tests provide outcome verification. The paper's contribution is their multilingual, dual-mode synthesis, filtering, normalization, release, and distillation study. Scale and benchmark performance alone do not establish higher data quality than prior software-agent corpora.
