- The paper's success rate is defined through all-step correctness and the intended final screen, but the released evaluator computes static step success. A parsed action can satisfy the scorer without demonstrating that an Android episode reaches the terminal state; conversely, a valid live interaction outside the retained alternatives can be scored false.
- Human/LLM correction is underspecified. The reviewer identity, prompt, decoding, expert-run count, panel size, sampled fraction, rubric, agreement, and correction threshold are unknown, while Algorithm 1 and the prose do not make the verification scope fully consistent (Paper §2.1.2 and Algorithm 1, pp. 3–5).
- The 2,400 Magma-R1 training samples come from AndroidControl-Curated, which is also the evaluation surface. Exact IDs, selection rule, Easy/Hard mapping, train/test separation, and decontamination are unknown; training/evaluation overlap therefore remains a material leakage risk.
- The release is static. No emulator image, Android/app-version manifest, account state, reset protocol, live environment runner, or terminal-state checker was verified, so screenshot/action records do not establish replayable episodes and may drift as apps change.
- Artifact identity has drifted. The paper and root model config concern a 3B/Qwen2.5-VL model, while the current redirect is named `Magma-R1-4B-AndroidControl` and contains later Qwen3-VL-4B material. Table 1 attribution requires an immutable checkpoint mapping.
- Dataset and code licenses are unknown because no repository LICENSE or dataset-card license was verified. The model card's MIT declaration does not grant rights to curated JSON, screenshots, upstream AndroidControl material, or third-party app content. Privacy/consent and record-level redaction are also undisclosed despite contact-like strings in released records.
- Point, box, and task-improved files have unequal row counts without a conversion or split manifest. The official evaluation entry point covers only the task-improved map slice and does not reproduce all reported tables; failed rollouts, rejected proposals, and complete human decisions are not released.
