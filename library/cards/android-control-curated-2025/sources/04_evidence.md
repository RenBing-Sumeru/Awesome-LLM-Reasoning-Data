Table 2 reports the effect of the two purification stages on the Hard subset. Qwen3-VL-235B success rate rises from 61.2 on original AndroidControl to 71.7 on Curated-Box and 76.5 on the fully curated benchmark; Magma-R1 rises from 57.6 to 69.1 and 75.3 under the same sequence (Paper Table 2, p. 6). These deltas show that metric and label choices change the reported evaluation outcome; they do not by themselves prove that every corrected record is high quality.

Under the authors' full curated setting, Table 1 reports Hard type accuracy / grounding accuracy / success rate of 84.2 / 84.8 / 75.3 for Magma-R1, compared with 88.2 / 83.6 / 76.5 for Qwen3-VL-235B. On Easy, Magma-R1 success rate is 88.0 versus 87.2 for Infi-GUI-R1 (Paper Table 1, p. 6). These are author-reported results and were not independently reproduced for this Card.

The paper attributes more than 70% of inspected high-risk cases to three deficiencies: 24.13% unclear tasks, 8.12% multiple valid actions, and 37.63% wrong ground truth (Paper §3.4.1 and Figure 4, pp. 7–8). The sampling denominator, panel size, per-item decisions, and agreement statistics are not separately released, so these percentages characterize the reported audit rather than certify the full dataset.

The paper reports post-training a 3B Magma-R1 on 2,400 selected samples for 60 H20 GPU-hours, approximately USD 60 (abstract; §3.1, p. 5). The official release audit finds five JSON views: 8,435 high-box rows, 7,708 high-point rows, 8,377 low-box rows, 7,708 low-point rows, and 8,377 task-improved rows. Within the task-improved file, 527 rows have revised tasks, 573 rows retain non-empty candidate actions totaling 1,233 alternatives, and 86 rows retain original and GPT-corrected ground-truth actions (official HF dataset revision `62aeecdcafa4097d340c5a4389690a5f14e938ed`; audited file hashes in the evidence ledger). Unequal view counts and the absent split manifest must be preserved as release facts, not silently normalized.
