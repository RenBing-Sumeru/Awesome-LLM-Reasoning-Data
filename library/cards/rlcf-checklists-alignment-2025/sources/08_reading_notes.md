1. **One-sentence positioning:** RLCF replaces a universal scalar reward with instruction-specific checklists and releases 51,071 WildChecklists records.

2. **Data object:** Each example preserves not only a prompt and response but also atomic criteria, item-level judgments, and the resulting preference relation.

3. **Method takeaway:** Checklist generation, semantic judges, programmatic verifiers, and reward aggregation form the feedback pipeline.

4. **Evidence anchor:** The method improves all five evaluated benchmarks, with notable gains on FollowBench-hard, InFoBench, and Arena-Hard.

5. **Main risk:** Missing or incorrect checklist items directly contaminate the reward, and item-level compliance does not guarantee holistic response quality.
