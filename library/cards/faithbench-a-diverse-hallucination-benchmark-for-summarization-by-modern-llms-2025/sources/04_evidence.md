Claim: popular detectors struggle on the ambiguity they are used to score. Setting: 660 expert-annotated hard summaries from 66 passages; a sample counts as hallucinated when unwanted or questionable. Result: the best reported detector, MiniCheck-Deberta-L, reached 54.95% balanced accuracy and 54.90% macro-F1; GPT-4o had the lowest unwanted-hallucination rate at 39.34% among the tested models. Boundary: the set is deliberately selected for detector disagreement, so neither detector scores nor model rankings estimate average performance on all summaries.
