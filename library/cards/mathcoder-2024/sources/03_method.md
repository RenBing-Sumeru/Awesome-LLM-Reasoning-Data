# Method

1. **Construct verified seed traces.** Input: GSM8K and MATH training questions, their ground-truth answers, and GPT-4. Process: ask GPT-4 for LCE solutions that alternate reasoning, Python, and execution output. Output: 49k problem-solution records. Check / stop rule: retain a solution only when its final answer matches the source ground truth.
2. **Interpolate and solve new problems.** Input: a relatively easy GSM8K problem, a harder MATH problem, GPT-4, and MathCoder-Initial trained from stage 1. Process: GPT-4 writes a problem intended to fall between the pair and judges relative difficulty; MathCoder-Initial generates three LCE solutions. Output: 31k additional records. Check / stop rule: keep solutions only when all three final answers agree; agreement does not prove correctness.
3. **Serialize and fine-tune.** Input: the combined 80k traces and Llama-2 or CodeLlama. Process: delimit natural language, code, execution, blocks, and messages with special tokens, concatenate examples with cross-question masking, and train for three epochs. Output: MathCoder-L and MathCoder-CL consumers. Check / stop rule: compute cross-entropy on text and code while masking execution-result loss in the final recipe.
4. **Replay execution during inference.** Input: a math prompt and a generated LCE block. Process: generate greedily until block end; when a code block appears, execute it in a persistent Jupyter state, append the actual result, and resume generation. Output: a trajectory of at most 32 blocks and a final answer. Check / stop rule: terminate on the answer/message boundary or block limit, then score exact task answers on five benchmarks.

**Reproducibility:** verify OpenReview, the `train_80k.jsonl` snapshot, nested message schema, special tokens, answer extraction, source splits, interpolation prompt, n=3 agreement filter, execution sandbox, checkpoints, and the reported SFT settings. The paper does not disclose GPT-4 version, sampling temperature, random seeds, retry counts, total construction tokens, or generation cost.
