# Limitations

- **Author-stated boundary:** answer agreement filters interpolated traces but cannot establish correctness; re-execute code and verify final answers with independent solvers before treating records as trusted supervision.
- **Curator audit risk:** the public `messages` schema omits source, interpolation status, teacher, and filter metadata; reconstruct or add those fields before mixture analysis, group splitting, or per-source licensing decisions.
- **Curator audit risk:** trajectories contain executable Python and the paper reports no contamination scan for generated questions; sandbox every replay and rerun overlap checks against the intended evaluation set.
