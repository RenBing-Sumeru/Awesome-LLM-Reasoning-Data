The paper does not provide a universal confidence scale, a universal correctness definition, or a guarantee that a calibration result carries across tasks. Calibration can differ with the task, the answer format, and the way correctness is operationalized; factual errors remain a practical source of risk when an application acts on a confidence signal. The survey's coverage is also bounded by the literature available at publication time. It should therefore not be used to assume that showing a number to users automatically improves reliability. Before deployment or comparison, readers still need task-specific evidence that the signal is evaluated against the outcome and decision at issue.
