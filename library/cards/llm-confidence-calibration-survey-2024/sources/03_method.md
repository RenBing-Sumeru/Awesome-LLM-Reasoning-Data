This is a literature survey, not one new calibration procedure, dataset, or prediction interface. Its method is to gather and synthesize work on confidence estimation and calibration for LLMs, summarize the technical advances and challenges described in that work, and discuss applications and promising future directions. The survey's value is comparative: it gives readers shared terms for separating the production of a confidence signal from evidence that the signal agrees with correctness. It does not replace the primary papers for operational details. Exact model inputs, answer formats, correctness labels, calibration measures, and experimental settings must be checked in the cited studies before adopting any individual method.
