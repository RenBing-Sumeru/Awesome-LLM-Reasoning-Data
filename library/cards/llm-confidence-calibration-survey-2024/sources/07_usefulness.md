Use this survey when deciding whether a system should answer, defer, or expose a confidence signal to a user. It provides a vocabulary for checking whether the signal is merely estimated or also calibrated, whether it was evaluated on the intended task, and whether it is tied to a decision that a user or system can act on. It is particularly useful for preventing an interface from presenting confidence as a blanket promise of correctness. The survey does not prescribe one threshold or product policy. Instead, it helps translate a proposed use of confidence into testable questions about the target outcome, evaluation setting, and consequence of a low-confidence answer.
