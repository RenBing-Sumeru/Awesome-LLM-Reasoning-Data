Read the challenges before adopting a confidence number in an interface, evaluator, or decision rule. For each cited method, keep notes on the task, the model output being judged, the observed correctness target, the confidence estimate, the calibration measure, and what action follows from low confidence. Separate evidence that a signal is available from evidence that it is calibrated for the intended task. Then consult the original papers for the estimator's assumptions and the evaluation details. This reading habit prevents a confidence result from being generalized from one answer type or correctness definition to a different application without supporting evidence.
