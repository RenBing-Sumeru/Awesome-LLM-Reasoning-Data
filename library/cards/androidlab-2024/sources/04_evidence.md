The paper reports 138 benchmark tasks across nine apps, predefined virtual devices, and an Android Instruct collection described as 10.5k traces and 94.3k steps, with 6,208 steps from benchmark apps used for fine-tuning experiments. Reported training evidence is that six open-source LLM/LMM models improve average success rates from 4.59% to 21.50% for LLMs and from 1.93% to 13.28% for LMMs. The decisive row-level evidence is not the aggregate score alone: it is whether an executed trajectory reaches every required sub-goal under the evaluator. Evidence is bounded by the paper's app snapshot, virtual-device setup, prompt mode, model scaffold, and evaluator version.
