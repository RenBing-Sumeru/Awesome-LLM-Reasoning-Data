The evidence to trust is the official task release, data schema, and scoring contract, not only leaderboard numbers. The local audited scale is: paper reports 50,000 questions over 12,000+ document images; challenge paper reports 50,000 question-answer pairs over 12,767 images for task 1. Instance-level evidence is a single record containing document image, natural-language question, one or more text answers, split/task metadata, and evaluator output such as answer similarity., the model output or trajectory, and the verifier/judge result from answer-level comparison, commonly ANLS-style string similarity against ground-truth answers.

Reported aggregate scores should be interpreted only within the official setup. Evidence boundaries include Exact split, hidden/public policy, live-refresh policy, and subset version should be pinned from current official artifacts before score reuse.; License and redistribution terms require artifact-level review unless explicitly recorded in official sources.; Public benchmark records can leak into future training data; post-release score claims require versioned contamination evidence. Additional audit note: arXiv verifies WACV 2021 paper, authors, size, and human accuracy; official DocVQA site verifies challenge context, people, and dataset/challenge portal. If a paper uses a judge or rubric, the judge prompt/model/version is part of the evidence; if it uses programmatic checking, the exact checker and runtime are part of the evidence.
