The prior-work baseline is document, chart, multimodal QA, and visual evaluation benchmarks. The change introduced by DocVQA: A Dataset for VQA on Document Images is the particular task object and feedback contract: document image, natural-language question, one or more text answers, split/task metadata, and evaluator output such as answer similarity. scored by answer-level comparison, commonly ANLS-style string similarity against ground-truth answers. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00398; project: https://www.docvqa.org/; data: https://www.docvqa.org/; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.00398), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
