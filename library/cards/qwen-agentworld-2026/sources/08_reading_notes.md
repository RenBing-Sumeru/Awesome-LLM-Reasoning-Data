- Start with Sections 2.2 and 2.3 of the [paper HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.24597v1) to identify the five-part system-level instruction, alternating action-observation schema, and next-observation target.
- Read Sections 3.1-3.4 together: source collection and filtering precede three distinct pools, SFT retains 7,094 of 10,250 candidate queries, and the RL train pool contains 92,308 trajectories with one target turn each.
- Inspect the reward-stability discussion, not only the reward formula. The paper reports self-praise reward hacking and uses rule anchoring, content-type classification, and tag extraction as mitigations.
- Compare the more-than-10M training claim with the separate 2,170-row [AgentWorldBench release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Qwen/AgentWorldBench). The former is unreleased training data; the latter is a released test split with real-observation references.
- Treat the official [repository](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-AgentWorld) and [35B model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-AgentWorld-35B-A3B) as evidence for released weights, prompts, and evaluation tooling—not for release of the training corpus, RL judge, verifier coverage, or 397B checkpoint.
