A released process reward model can offer a useful step-level scoring interface without revealing the examples, labels, or calibration that make its scores trustworthy. Qwen2.5-Math-PRM is a V2 migration overlay for a legacy model-release seed: its central evidence is Qwen's 2025 release of 7B and 72B mathematical PRMs, not a claim that the linked technical report's full research artifact is the same record.

This Card separates the deployable inference interface from unreported PRM construction. In particular, it does not turn a model card, Best-of-N evaluation context, or ProcessBench evaluation reference into evidence for released training traces, label semantics, or a reproducible verifier recipe.
