ClaimDB covers 80 real-world databases spanning governance, healthcare, media, education, and natural sciences and releases 51,369 claim–database–evidence or label records. Each claim requires compositional operations over large structured data and receives an entailment, contradiction, or insufficient-evidence label together with schemas, execution resources, and a leaderboard. It moves factuality judging from reading evidence text to generating executable verification programs with abstention, supporting structured grounding and data-agent evaluation.
