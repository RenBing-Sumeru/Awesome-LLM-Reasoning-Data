The paper also trains an 8B Chinese reward model and manually annotates CRBench for evaluation; that manual annotation belongs to the evaluation benchmark, not to COIG-P’s construction pipeline. Reproduction requires the exact query sources, model families and prompts, filtering and judging rules, quality-control thresholds, release revision, and domain assignment. The paper does not disclose every serving configuration in the Card metadata, so those details require source-level verification.
