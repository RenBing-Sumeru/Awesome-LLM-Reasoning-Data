Chinese preference resources have been limited by scale, domain coverage, and data validation, while human annotation constrains expansion. COIG-P addresses how to construct Chinese chosen/rejected supervision at a scale suitable for preference optimization and reward modeling without using human intervention in the pipeline. Its decision boundary is the ranking of generated candidate responses for Chinese queries, not a claim that the chosen response is universally truthful or aligned with every human value.

The paper targets Chinese alignment and Chinese reward models across diverse tasks. The relevant data surface is a Chinese query, candidate responses, chosen/rejected labels, domain information, and quality-control metadata. It does not establish that synthetic LLM judgments are equivalent to direct human preference, nor that the resource covers every Chinese dialect, domain, risk, or deployment context.
