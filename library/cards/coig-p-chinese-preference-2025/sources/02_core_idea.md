COIG-P releases one million Chinese preference pairs built from 92,000 curated Chinese queries. Multiple LLMs generate and score candidate answers in a pipeline with no human intervention; each usable example retains a query, candidates, chosen/rejected relation, domain, and quality-control information. The contribution is a large synthetic preference object, not a collection of independently human-verified answer keys.

Its feedback contract is model-generated comparison and scoring. The paper reports six task domains, including chat, coding, and mathematics, and uses the resource for direct preference optimization and Chinese reward modeling. This qualifies as preference and reward feedback data because the label expresses an ordering between alternatives, while its synthetic provenance remains essential to interpretation and audit.
