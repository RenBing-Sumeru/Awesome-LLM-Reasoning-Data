The paper reports one million preference pairs from 92,000 queries and evaluates COIG-P from two directions. Through DPO, Qwen2/2.5 and Infinity-Instruct series obtain 2%–12% gains on AlignBench and outperform the compared Chinese preference datasets in the reported setup. The authors also train an 8B CRM and report robust scoring on their manually annotated CRBench; they state that data constructed by that CRM is comparable in quality to GPT-4o-produced data.

These results support the authors’ tasks, model series, comparison set, and evaluation protocol. They do not show that all synthetic labels are human-aligned, that every domain benefits equally, or that a CRM will transfer to unseen Chinese tasks. The official dataset repository confirms that COIG-P is a released text dataset of 803 MB, but not every construction detail is exposed in its viewer.
