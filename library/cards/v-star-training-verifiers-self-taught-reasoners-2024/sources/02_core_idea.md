V-STaR maintains two buffers. `D_GEN` contains the original supervised solutions and all self-generated solutions labeled correct; it trains the next generator. `D_VER` contains the original solutions plus every labeled generation, correct or incorrect. After iterative collection, solutions for the same problem are expanded into every correct-versus-incorrect combination, producing synthetic preference pairs for a DPO-trained verifier.

The verifier is a language model, not a scalar classification head. DPO raises the likelihood of correct solutions relative to incorrect solutions under an SFT reference policy. At inference, the likelihood of a complete candidate given the problem is used as its ranking score; the highest-scoring candidate in a sampled set is selected. The native objects are therefore a terminal binary label, a same-query pairwise preference, and a learned sequence-likelihood score.

The main loop does not let the learned verifier decide which examples are correct. GSM8K final answers and MBPP test execution provide the construction labels, while the verifier is trained after accumulating the buffers. An optional verifier-in-the-loop experiment is separate and produces no substantial MBPP gain. This separation reduces direct feedback-loop gaming during data collection, but it does not remove terminal-label error or test-time score gaming.
