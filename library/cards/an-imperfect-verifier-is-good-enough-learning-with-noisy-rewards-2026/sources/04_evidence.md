The decisive experiment holds the MBPP task, Qwen3-8B base model, training data, budget, and recipe fixed, then replaces clean group-level code-verification rewards with independently resampled symmetric noise. This controlled design isolates reward error within the stated recipe, so the difference is principally attributable to the reward decision rather than changed data or compute. Clean rewards reach a mean validation unit-test pass rate of 0.901, while 10% noise reaches 0.886 (Table 1); across the sweep, the best validation score remains within two percentage points of the clean baseline up to 15% noise. This supports tolerance to moderate noise in this coding configuration, not robustness to persistent or asymmetric errors, larger models, or arbitrary verifier failures.
