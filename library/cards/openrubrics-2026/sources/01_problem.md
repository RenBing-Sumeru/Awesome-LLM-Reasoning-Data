Scalar and pairwise reward labels conceal why one answer is preferred. Rubrics make evaluation criteria inspectable, but expert-written rubrics are expensive and directly prompting a frontier model gives uneven quality and cost.

OpenRubrics supplies synthetic, preference-grounded rubrics and trains a rubric generator plus a rubric-conditioned reward model. The goal is scalable reward feedback that preserves explicit instruction constraints as well as higher-level response qualities.
