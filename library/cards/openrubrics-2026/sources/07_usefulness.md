For a team with prompt-response preferences but no explanation of the decision, apply CRG to generate hard rules and principles, then retain only records whose rubric-conditioned judgment recovers held-out labels. The inputs are licensed preference pairs, a capable rubric generator, and a validation split; the output is auditable pairwise reward data and a judge. Success is label agreement and target-benchmark improvement against a same-backbone direct judge. Do not use it when labels are unreliable or the task needs calibrated absolute scores; first measure label quality and rubric failure modes.
