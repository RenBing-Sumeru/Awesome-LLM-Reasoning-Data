Inputs come from WebArena WA-TRAIN and the official ToolBench training splits. The paper reports a 612-task WA-TRAIN/200-task WA-HELDOUT split stratified by category with seed 0, then collects 3,000 failed plus 500 successful WebArena trajectories and 5,000 failed plus 2,000 successful ToolBench trajectories with gpt-3.5-turbo-0125. These counts describe the experiments; neither the complete source corpus nor the promised WebArena split JSON is present in the inspected repository snapshot.

The offline construction has four stages. First, a failure detector assigns one of six failure types, recoverability, and severity; irrecoverable runs and major flaws with paper severity weight below `delta=0.3` are rejected. Second, gpt-4o-mini extracts actual achievements and key observations, with prompts instructing it to stay grounded in observations. Third, gpt-4o-mini proposes a hindsight prompt matched in style and complexity to the original; Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct independently judges whether the whole recorded episode satisfies it. The relabeler uses `T=0.3` for the first attempt and `T=0.7` for retries, the verifier uses `T=0`, and at most three attempts are made. Both confidences should reach `theta=0.5`, subject to Algorithm 1's `0.4` best-candidate fallback. Fourth, deterministic converters emit SFT, DPO, or ShareGPT records.

The output preserves full-episode supervision but its serialization differs by format. SFT pairs the hindsight prompt with an assistant reconstruction and scales loss by severity. DPO uses the hindsight-goal conversation as chosen and the original-goal conversation as rejected, with the same episode and a severity-scaled margin. ShareGPT interleaves the hindsight prompt, assistant thought/action turns, observation turns, and a final answer when present. In the public code, SFT and DPO assistant strings truncate every observation to 300 characters; ShareGPT retains full observation text. The SFT/DPO artifacts therefore are not full replay logs even when the source input is longer.

Reported open-weight training uses LoRA rank 16 and alpha 32, SFT for 3 epochs, DPO for 1 epoch with beta 0.1, maximum sequence length 4,096, ms-swift, and 8 A100-80G GPUs. GPT-4o uses the OpenAI fine-tuning API, and an 8-shot ICL condition is also evaluated. Exact model and tokenizer revisions, OpenAI API snapshot, environment step/token budgets, and complete training/evaluation commands are unknown.

The verifier/environment boundary is crucial. Benchmark environments establish original outcomes, but the hindsight goal is not executed again. Learned judges assess support in the serialized observation record, so the release lacks an executable terminal predicate for the new goal. Reproduction also requires separate relabeler and verifier clients, pinned prompts/models, raw and rejected records, the task split, environment adapters, resets, tool/API versions, and replay manifests. The inspected package provides none of the experimental data, fine-tuning scripts, evaluation scripts, or annotation assets.
