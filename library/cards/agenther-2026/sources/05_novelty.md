The prior baseline is success-only agent demonstration collection: an episode that fails its original WebArena or ToolBench goal is discarded even if its actions achieved a coherent subgoal. Hindsight experience replay provides the broader idea of relabeling achieved goals, while LLM-as-judge pipelines provide learned selection. AgentHER combines those ideas for natural-language tool-agent episodes and turns the result into explicit supervised and preference records.

What changes is the unit of reuse. The method does not repair or rerun a failure; it preserves the original thought-action-observation sequence, extracts what was actually accomplished, writes a new goal, and filters the pair using two confidence-bearing judges plus a severity weight. It then exposes three downstream views: severity-weighted SFT, cross-goal DPO, and ShareGPT. Failed behavior becomes a positive demonstration only for a goal supported by the recorded observations.

The concrete direction signal is that data construction can treat failure retention and relabeling policy as first-class agent-training infrastructure. The paper's cross-model judge ablation, confidence-filter ablation, human precision audit, false-negative audit, looping-failure slice, and iterative redeployment results make the selection contract inspectable. They are quality signals about the reported procedure, not proof that every generated pair is correct or that an unreleased dataset is reusable.

What is not new includes WebArena, ToolBench, gpt-3.5-turbo-0125 rollouts, SFT, DPO, ShareGPT formatting, LoRA, or the general principle of hindsight goal relabeling. The paper does not contribute a new environment, executable relabeled-goal verifier, step-level supervision, replay system, RLVR objective, public experiment corpus, or dataset license.

Before reuse, the paper recipe and released implementation must be treated as separate artifacts. A faithful implementation needs distinct gpt-4o-mini and Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct clients, a second-judge acceptance check that includes `is_valid`, severity semantics aligned with the paper, and untruncated observations where full episodes are claimed. It also needs the raw and accepted/rejected data, immutable split and replay manifests, exact model/tool versions, training/evaluation scripts, and rights documentation.
