Read the paper as a benchmark for Lean4 theorem-development progress, not as a broad mathematical reasoning score. The most important distinction is parent theorem success versus subtheorem coverage: a model can make meaningful formal progress without completing the whole theorem group.

When comparing numbers, first check the task view, Lean environment, prompt/scaffold, sample count, and timeout. A compiled proof is strong row-level evidence, but the benchmark score still depends on how theorem groups and premises were extracted.
