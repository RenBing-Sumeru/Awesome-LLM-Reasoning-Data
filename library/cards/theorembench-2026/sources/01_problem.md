TheoremBench asks how to evaluate large language models on Lean4 theorem proving when the target is not a short contest answer but a formal proof accepted by a proof assistant. The primary source is the 2026 arXiv preprint "TheoremBench: Evaluating LLMs on Theorem Proving in Formal Mathematics" (arXiv:2606.09450).

The benchmark targets classical mathematical theorem developments inspired by Wiedijk's list of famous theorems, extracting Lean4 proving instances from formalized theorem groups. The boundary is formal evaluation: a model succeeds only relative to Lean4 acceptance under a pinned environment. It is not a natural-language math QA benchmark, not a subjective proof-quality rubric, and not a training recipe. The evaluation object is a Lean theorem task with explicit context, candidate proof text, and compiler verdict.
