The paper reports 6,141 examples from 31 datasets and evaluates major text-only and multimodal foundation models. The headline result in the original release is that GPT-4V reaches about 49.9% accuracy on MathVista, below the reported human baseline of about 60.3%.

Evidence also comes from task-level and reasoning-type breakdowns: performance varies across arithmetic, algebraic, geometry, logical, statistical, scientific, and numerical commonsense reasoning, so the benchmark is diagnostic rather than one scalar leaderboard alone.

Instance-level evidence is the reference answer and official evaluator verdict after answer extraction/normalization. The evidence boundary is answer-level matching; it does not certify intermediate reasoning or visual grounding unless separate annotations support it.
