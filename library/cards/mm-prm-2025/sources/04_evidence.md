The paper reports approximately 747,000 MCTS-derived step annotations. The released MM-PRM checkpoint's `all_results.json` independently records 747,779 training samples, one completed epoch, and a training loss, so the model artifact is consistent with a training run of that scale. This metadata does not expose the rows, trees, labels, source IDs, or selection decisions that produced the checkpoint.

Under Best-of-16, the paper reports that MM-Policy accuracy rises from 33.92 to 42.80 on MM-K12 and from 15.41 to 24.00 on OlympiadBench after MM-PRM selection. InternVL2.5-8B rises from 27.01 to 37.80 on MM-K12. The soft-label ablation reports 42.8 with MeanOdds versus 37.0 for hard labels, while other aggregators show different absolute results. These are downstream selection results, not evidence that every step label is correct or that the released seed set has clean provenance.

The official artifact set is concrete but partial. MM-K12 is a public 10,000-row, single-split dataset with `id`, `question`, `answer`, and `image`; its card declares MIT. MM-PRM is a public 8B InternVL-family checkpoint whose configuration includes the prm marker token and whose card declares MIT. The GitHub repository is MIT-licensed and contains the MCTS, traversal, formatting, PRM training, and evaluation code. It contains no paper-run annotation-tree archive or sampled process-label dataset.

A qualitative paper example assigns high scores to two early geometry steps and a low score to a later erroneous step. It illustrates the intended interface but is one selected example and cannot establish calibration, false-positive rates, or transfer reliability.
