The construction has four auditable stages.

1. **MM-Policy construction.** The appendix lists 16 public data sources totaling 5,135,768 examples. Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct restructures solutions into explicit steps and answers. InternVL2.5-8B is then fine-tuned with its vision encoder frozen; the experiment section describes approximately 4 million cleaned examples, one epoch, batch size 128, and learning rate 4e-5. The difference between the appendix total and the reported used count is not itemized.
2. **Seed and tree generation.** MM-K12 contributes 10,000 multimodal K-12 problems, reported as 5,000 fill-in-the-blank and 5,000 multiple-choice questions, plus a separate 500-item test set. For each seed, MM-Policy samples structured continuations. The paper uses temperature 1.0, top-k 50, top-p 0.9, c_puct 0.125, 16 rollouts per Monte Carlo estimate, at most 200 search expansions, and at most 1,000 total rollouts. A text-only Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct prompt judges terminal answer equivalence; binary search expands failed branches while preserving the image in the rollout model's context.
3. **Tree-to-record sampling and PRM training.** The released serialization code defines tree fields for `partial_solution`, `mc_value`, `visit_count`, judged-correct and judged-incorrect rollouts, and children; the generated paper-run trees themselves are not released. The traversal script removes roots with MC 0 or 1, ends negative paths at MC 0, drops paths of at most 10 words, deduplicates process strings, and balances retained positive leaves against negatives. A formatted training item contains an image, a Question/Process conversation, and the list of step labels. The paper reports PRM initialization from MM-Policy, one epoch, batch size 512, and learning rate 4e-6.
4. **Best-of-N evaluation.** Each policy produces 16 candidate traces. MM-PRM emits one score per prm marker token, and Min, Average, Max, SumLogPr, SumLogOdds, or MeanOdds converts the sequence to a path score. The selected final answer is evaluated on MM-K12, OlympiadBench, MathVista, MathVerse, and MathVision.

The public recipe is not an immutable paper-run manifest. `run_data_pipeline.sh` matches the paper's 200-search/1,000-rollout caps but leaves the policy model at the unresolved literal `/path/to/model`; direct invocation of `run_data_pipeline.py` defaults to only 20 searches and 200 rollouts. The public PRM training shell uses three epochs and learning rate 2e-6 rather than the paper's one epoch and 4e-6.
