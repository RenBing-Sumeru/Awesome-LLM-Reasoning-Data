The primer's main contribution is a relational view of data quality. Correctness is relative to a verifier, difficulty to a base model, trace value to a trajectory and training objective, and coverage to source lineage. Construction is then decomposed into prompt source, trace author, search or environment substrate, feedback contract, and selection policy, making recipes comparable without erasing their boundaries.
