**Breadth and organization of evidence.** This paper does not claim a new controlled experiment. Its evidence is a synthesis of more than 150 public studies and system reports, routed through four questions about object, utility, construction, and scaling. The useful check is whether a conclusion is supported by identifiable primary sources with the same model, verifier, and budget context. Boundary: literature count is not effect-size aggregation, and source coverage is not guaranteed exhaustive.

**Cross-study consistency.** The framework explains recurring findings without forcing one universal quality score: correctness changes with the verifier, difficulty with the learner, trace value with the trajectory distribution, and scaling with unique-data reuse and compute topology. This makes apparently conflicting results testable as configuration differences. Boundary: the primer summarizes reported evidence and does not reproduce the cited experiments.
