Expert annotations show that defects in LLM reviews go beyond style and include misinterpretation of paper content, unsupported criticism, and recommendations disconnected from identified problems. When asked to critique another review, models still fail to cover many expert-identified issues and may introduce new unsupported objections. The 100 papers and 23-category taxonomy provide more diagnostic evidence than simple preference studies, although annotation cost limits scale and different fields may weight review defects differently.
