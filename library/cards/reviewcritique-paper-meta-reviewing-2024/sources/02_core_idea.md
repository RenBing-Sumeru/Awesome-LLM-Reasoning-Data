The paper collects human- and LLM-generated reviews for 100 NLP papers, with domain experts annotating deficient review segments, explanations, and 23 fine-grained error types. The data supports both review-generation analysis and meta-reviewer detection of review defects. The authors find that LLMs can produce superficially complete reviews yet frequently misread facts, make unsupported claims, offer unactionable suggestions, or miss important issues, while LLM-based second-order critique is also unstable.
