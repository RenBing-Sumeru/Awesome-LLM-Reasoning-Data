1. **Sample papers:** One hundred NLP papers are selected with their full text and review context, covering varied topics and quality levels.
2. **Generate reviews:** Real human reviews are collected, and multiple LLMs are prompted to produce corresponding paper reviews for comparison.
3. **Expert annotation:** Domain-familiar researchers mark deficient review sentences or segments, explain the problem, and map it to a 23-category taxonomy.
4. **Define quality tasks:** The study measures defect distributions when models act as reviewers and their ability to detect human-labeled defects as meta-reviewers.
5. **Analyze errors:** Models, prompts, and review sources are compared across factual errors, reasoning weaknesses, lack of specificity, and omissions.
