1. **One-sentence positioning:** A dataset using expert defect annotations to evaluate LLM reviewers and meta-reviewers.
2. **Method takeaway:** It combines 100 NLP papers, human and model reviews, segment labels, and 23 error categories.
3. **Data takeaway:** Records include papers, review text, problematic spans, defect types, and expert explanations.
4. **Evidence anchor:** LLM reviews often appear professional while containing unreliable factual claims or recommendations, and second-order critique is also unstable.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is useful for review assistance and critique training but should not replace domain experts.
