The empirical study concentrates on four Olympiad-style mathematics benchmarks with binary correctness, so rankings under partial credit, open-ended answers, tool execution, or noisy human judgments may behave differently. A dense design with up to 80 trials is valuable for analysis but can be expensive to reproduce for large closed models. The greedy prior is also not universally safe: its usefulness depends on measuring agreement between deterministic and stochastic behavior rather than assuming that one decode summarizes a model’s test-time distribution.
