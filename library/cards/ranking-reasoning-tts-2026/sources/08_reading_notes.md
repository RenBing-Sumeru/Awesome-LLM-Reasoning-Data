Read the paper as an evaluation-methodology contribution, not as evidence that one ranking rule always dominates. Inspect the response-tensor definition, the construction of the high-budget reference, the trial subsampling protocol, and the conditions under which the greedy prior helps or hurts. A useful reproduction fixes models and prompts, varies only the trial count, and plots rank agreement, rank uncertainty, cost, and greedy–stochastic alignment together. That plot makes clear whether more inference samples improve model capability, the confidence of the evaluator, or both.
