The study evaluates 20 reasoning models on AIME’24, AIME’25, HMMT’25, and BrUMO’25, collecting up to 80 trials for each relevant model–question pair. It then compares ranking procedures over the resulting dense response tensor. The high-trial Bayesian ranking serves as a reference for measuring how well a method recovers the intended order after the available number of trials is reduced.

Scorio provides the implementation surface for the methods, including Bayesian, paired-comparison, item-response, voting, graph-based, and spectral options. The analysis measures agreement using Kendall’s tau and examines the effect of a greedy-decoding empirical prior. The operational object is therefore a trial budget together with a ranking rule; the work does not alter the evaluated models’ weights, but it changes how their added inference computation is consumed and reported in an evaluation decision.
