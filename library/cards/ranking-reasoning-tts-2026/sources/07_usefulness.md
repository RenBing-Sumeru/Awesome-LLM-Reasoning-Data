Use this paper when selecting a reasoning model or publishing a test-time scaling comparison. Log every model–prompt–trial outcome and report the number of samples, decoding policy, ranking method, uncertainty interval, and any empirical prior. A small pilot can test whether greedy and stochastic rankings agree before a greedy prior is used to reduce evaluation cost. Scorio is particularly useful for checking whether a leaderboard conclusion survives when the trial budget is reduced to the one available in deployment or regular regression testing.
