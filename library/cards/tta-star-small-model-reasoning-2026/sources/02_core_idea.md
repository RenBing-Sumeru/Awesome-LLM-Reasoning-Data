TTA* recasts textual reasoning as goal-directed A* search. Nodes encode partial or imperfect derivations; edges apply the same model's exploration, critique, and refinement operations. An A*-style priority combines accumulated path cost with a heuristic estimate of future potential, allowing the search to revisit and improve a promising line instead of committing immediately to its first completion.

The framework is deliberately training-free and does not require an external verifier or multi-model architecture. Its feedback signal is self-reflection from the small model, so the contribution is a search scaffold that exposes where test-time computation is spent. This also creates a clear boundary: success depends on whether the model's heuristic and critiques are informative enough to guide the tree.
