The paper and inspected artifacts support the following pipeline.

1. **Assemble the candidate pool.** Collect exactly 59,029 questions from 16 sources, led by NuminaMATH (30,660) and MATH (11,999), plus OlympicArena (4,250), OmniMath (4,238), AGIEval (2,385), and smaller science, code, logic, crossword, and quantitative sources. New sources include 182 Stanford Statistics PhD qualifying-exam probability questions and 23 paper-counted s1-teasers.
2. **Decontaminate and deduplicate.** Remove word-level 8-gram overlaps with MATH500, GPQA Diamond, and AIME24, then exact-question duplicates. Checked code also includes LiveCodeBench test questions and cross-source overlap filtering. Removed IDs, matched n-grams, and review decisions are not released.
3. **Generate teacher traces.** Call Google Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking Experimental once per question and store its thinking part and answer. The paper cites `gemini-2.0-flash-thinking-exp-1219`; code calls the unversioned alias and does not pin temperature, top-p, seed, retry ledger, or API snapshot.
4. **Apply quality filters.** API/trace failures reduce 59,029 to 54,116. String heuristics for ASCII art, missing image references, and inconsistent numbering reduce 54,116 to 51,581.
5. **Probe difficulty.** Have Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct attempt every remaining question. Claude 3.5 Sonnet compares each attempt with the reference solution. Remove a question if either model is judged correct, leaving 24,496.
6. **Seed high-quality examples.** Fix 384 examples from judged-correct AIME/GPQA generations and judged-correct MATH generations whose Gemini trace exceeds 5,600 Qwen-tokenizer tokens.
7. **Label diversity and sample.** Claude assigns MSC-style domains. The paper uniformly samples an available domain and then samples within it with weight proportional to `2^-rank`, favoring longer traces, until 1,000 unique items are selected. The notebook instead uses uniform domains only to 700, then an AIME/GPQA-derived distribution to 1,000, and sets no NumPy seed.
8. **Tokenize and train.** Render think/answer delimiters, mask loss on the question, and train on reasoning plus answer for five epochs/315 steps. Settings are global batch 16, bf16, AdamW `β1=0.9`, `β2=0.95`, learning rate `1e-5`, 5% warmup then cosine decay, weight decay `1e-4`, and maximum length 32,768. Training reportedly takes 26 minutes on 16 H100 GPUs, about 7 H100 GPU-hours.
9. **Optionally apply budget forcing at inference.** At a maximum budget, append the end-of-thinking delimiter and optionally `Final Answer:`. To extend thinking, suppress the delimiter and append `Wait` when stopping is attempted. This changes decoding only; it does not alter s1K or the SFT objective.

Paper-exact reconstruction is blocked by multiple drift points. The public generated full pool has 58,986 rows, 43 fewer than the 59,029 raw pool. `qfq/train` and `qfq/train_featurized` are readable, but the feature columns are misspelled relative to notebook expectations. The notebook depends on unauthenticated-inaccessible `qfq/geminiall`, differs from Algorithm 1, and has no sampling seed. Reuse must pin repository commit `77272c6e925d610257a50b520bad15330b513389` and the individual artifact revisions.
