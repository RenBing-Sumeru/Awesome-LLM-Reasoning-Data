The canonical publication is the EMNLP 2025 main-proceedings paper in ACL Anthology, pages 20275-20321, with DOI `10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.1025`. The arXiv preprint, official repository, pinned Hugging Face datasets, original s1-32B model, and result files provide the surrounding artifact record.

s1 asks how a very small supervised-distillation set can be selected from a broad reasoning pool, and whether a separately trained model's thinking time can then be controlled at inference. The original data object is one answer-keyed source question plus a Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking Experimental reasoning trace and response. Public s1K rows contain `question`, source `solution`, `cot_type`, `source_type`, stringified source `metadata`, null `cot`, a one-element `thinking_trajectories` sequence, and `attempt`; the tokenized release adds rendered training text.

The construction funnel is the central evidence: **59,029 source questions → 54,116 successful Gemini generations → 51,581 after format-quality filters → 24,496 after removing questions solved by either Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct or Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct → 1,000 selected s1K rows**. The final step combines 384 fixed judged-correct AIME/GPQA and long MATH examples with Claude-assigned domains and a trace-length-weighted diversity sampler.

The 1,000 training rows are not all verified solutions. A later Claude 3.7 audit judges only **53.6%** of s1K generations correct; the authors intentionally retain incorrect generations because their target is reasoning-pattern imitation rather than a fully correct solution corpus. This is answer-/episode-level SFT data, not process supervision, RLVR, or a proof-verified dataset.

This work belongs in **Data Construction and Open Release Recipes** because it exposes source counts, staged selection, judges, ablations, training configuration, data/model/code, and release discrepancies. It also includes budget forcing, but that is a separate inference-time stopping/continuation control over the trained model, not a data field, training label, reward, or selection rule. The Card is L4 bilingual-review ready; paper-exact selection and release reconciliation remain partial.
