The authors evaluate more than twenty models on MIR-Core and MIR-Extended and plot curves from 4 to 2,048 shots. The central result is that every model, including the OpenAI o1 family, stops improving after a certain number of shots, showing that context length is not sufficient for information integration. MIR-Core selects 300 functions and includes low-variance checks across three runs. Controlled prompting also finds that most ordinary models perform worse with forced CoT than with direct transductive answering, with the gap increasing as shots grow. API truncation and model-specific context policies still affect long-context results.
