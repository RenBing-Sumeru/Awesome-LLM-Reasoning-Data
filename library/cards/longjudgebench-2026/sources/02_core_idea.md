LongJudgeBench is a 1,944-instance, 1,966-output meta-evaluation benchmark for long-form judging, with a 9,249.7-token mean candidate length. It normalizes instruction, content, and response while retaining pointwise, pairwise, and listwise protocols across six datasets from five scenarios. Authors release code, self-collected data, annotations, metadata, outputs, and protocols under MIT where permitted.
RealDeepResearch contains 40 research tasks, four prompt styles, and four generation models, yielding 640 long documents and preserving DOCX. Other scenarios cover surveys, writing, long-chain analysis, and medical reviews; normalization tests one judge under common extraction and comparison rules without erasing task differences.
