The authors state that six scenarios cannot cover all real-world long-form tasks and that retrieval-augmented and multi-agent judges remain untested. The benchmark also exposes operational failures: context overflow and safety refusals invalidate scores, while GPT-4o-mini has 78.7% order inconsistency on WP-Bench. Reuse therefore needs target-domain coverage and failure logging, not only aggregate accuracy.
Even the best setting reaches only 0.6721 accuracy, so the benchmark exposes a reliability gap rather than supplying a human substitute. The paper counts overflow and refusal as incorrect; dropping those cases in replication would overstate deployment reliability.
