The main experiment evaluates 32 model–setting combinations on the six-dataset benchmark. The best, Qwen3-Max+Reference, reaches 0.6721 average accuracy; the 32-way mean is 0.5627. Reference improves the average from 0.5313 (Vanilla) to 0.5843 and Verify from 0.5491 to 0.8169, but Reference+Rubric falls to 0.5784. This shows auxiliary context is helpful but not uniformly additive.
