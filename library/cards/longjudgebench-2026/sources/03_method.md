1. Collect six datasets across deep research, scientific survey, creative writing, long-chain analysis, and systematic review; preserve their protocols while normalizing every instance into instruction, content, and response.

2. Instructions carry the source query, protocol, and optional rubric, weights, references, constraints, or formatting; the judge returns rationale plus an extracted score, preference, or ranking.

3. Evaluate eight base-model judges at temperature 0 under Vanilla, Rubric, Reference, and Reference+Rubric settings.

4. Compare predictions with expert reference judgements using accuracy, Spearman, and Kendall; pairwise items are judged in both orders to reduce position bias.
