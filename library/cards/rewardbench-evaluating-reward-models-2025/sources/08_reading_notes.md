1. Positioning: RewardBench uses mixed public preferences and verifiable hard pairs for bugs, facts, and refusals to create specialized feedback for failures that general reward-model benchmarking evaluation misses.

2. Method handle: collect tasks and candidates, generate feedback, verify boundary cases, and organize by capability; label review is the decisive quality control.

3. Data handle: 2,985 chat, reasoning, and safety preference triplets, with records centered on inputs, candidates, evaluation evidence, and feedback labels.

4. Evidence anchor: reward models show clear trade-offs among chat, safety, and reasoning, with conclusions bounded by model, candidate, and judge configurations.

5. Reuse decision: best suited to unified evaluation of explicit reward models, implicit DPO rewards, and generative judges; contamination, false positives, and distributional stability must be checked.
