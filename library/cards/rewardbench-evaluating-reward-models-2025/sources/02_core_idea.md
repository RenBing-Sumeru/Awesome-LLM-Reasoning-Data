The central artifact is RewardBench, containing 2,985 chat, reasoning, and safety preference triplets. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use mixed public preferences and verifiable hard pairs for bugs, facts, and refusals for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is unified evaluation of explicit reward models, implicit DPO rewards, and generative judges.
