For a reward-model benchmarking system, RewardBench can serve as training data, a hidden evaluation set, or a pre-deployment audit set. Users can map tasks, candidate responses, and evaluation evidence to preferences, scores, correctness labels, or critiques. Success should combine label agreement, classification or ranking metrics, unseen-data generalization, and real gains in unified evaluation of explicit reward models, implicit DPO rewards, and generative judges. Tasks without reliable references, with high error costs, or with large distribution shifts require human, executable, or expert review.
