The official NeurIPS 2025 paper and author repository are the primary sources. Parallel test-time search repeatedly expands partial solutions under a fixed rollout budget, but allocation rules operating on individual candidates can overfund a reasoning direction simply because it has many near-duplicate candidates. The target objective is the probability of obtaining at least one correct solution, not the average PRM score or the number of superficially distinct strings.

The auditable object is a test-time search episode: prompt, active partial traces, completed traces, step PRM scores, embeddings, similarity and affinity matrices, uniqueness-adjusted weights, integer allocations, continuations, and final vote. DORA belongs to `rollout_search_test_time_trace_data` because these decisions govern which traces receive more compute and which alternatives disappear. The official repository exposes code and recipes, but not raw experimental trajectory and allocation logs.
