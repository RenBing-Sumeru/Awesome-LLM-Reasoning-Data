PPO uses a critic and raw rewards, while GRPO removes the critic but normalizes scores within a group. GPRS changes the optimization signal itself: it converts reward-model scores into within-group preference relations before shaping policy rewards. That is closer to the pairwise supervision used to train RLHF reward models and targets instability caused by interpreting noisy scores as cardinal values. It does not replace the reward model, provide a verifier, or establish that pairwise comparisons are faithful in every domain.
