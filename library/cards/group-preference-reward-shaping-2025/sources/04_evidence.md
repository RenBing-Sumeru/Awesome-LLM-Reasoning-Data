Claim: preference shaping is more robust than score normalization when reward quality varies. Using Pythia-2.8B with reward models from 410M to 6.9B, Figure 1 compares GPRS, GRPO, and Reinforce++ on Anthropic-HH and shows GPRS maintains the strongest win rate across the reported sizes. The paper additionally evaluates RLHF and reasoning tasks, where GPRS consistently beats other critic-free algorithms or is comparable to group normalization. This evidence supports reduced sensitivity in these experimental recipes, not immunity to reward misspecification or reward hacking.
