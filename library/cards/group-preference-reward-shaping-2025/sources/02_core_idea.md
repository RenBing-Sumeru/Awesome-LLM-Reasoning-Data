GPRS samples a group of policy responses, ranks or compares their reward-model outputs, and shapes each response's training reward from its preference relation within the group instead of treating the raw score as exact. The feedback object is a relative ordering, aligned with pairwise preference supervision; no extra critic or value model is required. It is a training method, not a released dataset: the official paper reports RLHF and reasoning experiments with varied reward-model sizes to test robustness.
