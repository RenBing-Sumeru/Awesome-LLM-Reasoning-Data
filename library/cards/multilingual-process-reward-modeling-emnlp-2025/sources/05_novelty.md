Compared with assigning a whole chain of thought one correct-or-incorrect label, this work changes the unit or acquisition path of process evidence. The work builds multilingual process-reward evaluation and training settings to test whether step supervision transfers beyond English expressions, number notation, and reasoning conventions. The data cover seven languages and eleven evaluations, preserving problems, CoTs, step judgements, and language identifiers. This is its direction signal; the reusable contribution is the data contract, quality control, and evaluation boundary.
