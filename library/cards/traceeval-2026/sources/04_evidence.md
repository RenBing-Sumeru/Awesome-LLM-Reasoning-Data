The arXiv abstract reports 10,583 programs from 1,600+ repositories, a held-out zero-shot evaluation over 10 LLMs, Claude-Opus-4.6 at 72.9 average F1, and Qwen2.5-Coder fine-tuning gains up to +55.6 F1.

Instance-level evidence comes from the released task object and the scorer: execution tracers mechanically witness positive runtime call edges; normalized edge sets are scored with precision/recall/F1. Aggregate scores are meaningful only for the reported split, model setup, and harness. Public artifacts support inspection, but they do not remove contamination, dependency, or license checks.
