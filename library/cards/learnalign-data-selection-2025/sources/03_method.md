The GSM8K path warms Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct on 300 random training prompts, then scores the 7,473-example train pool and selects 100, 500, 1,000, or 2,000 prompts. The DAPO path warms Qwen2.5-3B or Qwen2.5-7B on 1,000 DAPO-MATH-17K prompts and selects 1,000. In both paths, the warmed-up policy generates eight rollouts per prompt at temperature 1.0, and ground-truth matching supplies p.

GSM8K generation uses separate XML-style `think` and `answer` fields; DAPO requests a boxed final answer. The paper does not disclose the exact extraction/equivalence implementation. For every prompt, a GRPO gradient from the warmup checkpoint is randomly projected. The efficient DAPO configuration uses one correct rollout rather than every rollout; handling is not specified when a prompt has no correct rollout.

Downstream GRPO uses AdamW with learning rate 1e-6, beta values 0.9 and 0.999, epsilon 1e-8, KL coefficient 0.04, clip ratio 0.2, cosine warmup ratio 0.1, and two epochs. Batch size is 48 for GSM8K and 64 for DAPO-MATH-17K; maximum prompt length is 512, while maximum response length is 1,024 and 2,048 respectively. Evaluation uses greedy decoding on GSM8K and temperature 0.8/top-p 0.95 for the five-benchmark setting.

The nominal score matrix costs O(n^2 d) time and O(n^2) memory after projection. At the reported 10^3–10^4 scale, Step 4 is one batched matrix multiplication; the paper proposes Nyström and cascade approximations for larger pools but does not report those as LearnAlign experiments. Projection dimension, projection seed, training seeds, selected IDs, scores, rollouts, and gradients are not released.
