1. **Aggregate cross-domain traces:** Collect complete failed executions from different tools and environments and normalize observations and actions.
2. **Locate the decisive step:** Humans identify the action that sends the task into an unrecoverable or incorrect direction.
3. **Classify root causes:** Map failures to a cross-domain taxonomy including planning, tool use, perception, and environment anomalies.
4. **Train the diagnostic model:** Predict the step, category, and explanation from a trajectory and test transfer to unseen environments.
