Inputs are prompts or candidate outputs plus the stated feedback source; outputs are labeled pairs, scores, weights, or a selection decision. PKU-SafeRLHF separates the two labels, adds 19 harm categories and three severity levels, and collects dual/single preferences. A reproducible release should retain source row IDs, generation settings, label/rubric version, filtering decision, split, and artifact revision. Training use must be kept separate from evaluation use.
