PKU-SafeRLHF: Towards Multi-Level Safety Alignment for LLMs with Human Preference treats preference/reward feedback as a data-construction problem. Its data object is 44.6K prompts, 265K QA pairs, and 166.8K safety/helpfulness preference records. This makes provenance, annotator or teacher identity, and split policy part of the result rather than implementation detail.
