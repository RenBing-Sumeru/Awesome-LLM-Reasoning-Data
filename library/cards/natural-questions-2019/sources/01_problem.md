Natural Questions: A Benchmark for Question Answering Research defines an evaluation surface in open-domain-qa, search-queries, reading-comprehension: whether model outputs or agent trajectories satisfy the task-specific feedback contract. The primary source and artifacts are paper: https://aclanthology.org/Q19-1026/; acl: https://aclanthology.org/Q19-1026/; project: https://ai.google.com/research/NaturalQuestions; code: https://github.com/google-research-datasets/natural-questions; data: https://ai.google.com/research/NaturalQuestions; doi: https://doi.org/10.1162/tacl_a_00276; the local metadata records the source as 2019 / Transactions of the ACL / Google Research.
