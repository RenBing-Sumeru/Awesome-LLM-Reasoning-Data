The prior-work baseline is nearby benchmark and evaluation-surface datasets that share the same feedback contract. The change introduced by Natural Questions: A Benchmark for Question Answering Research is the particular task object and feedback contract: real user query, Wikipedia page, long-answer span, short-answer span or yes/no/no-answer label; Benchmark size: 超过 300K 个真实搜索问题；常见训练集约 307K，dev/test 各约 7.8K。 scored by long-answer and short-answer F1 under official scorer. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://aclanthology.org/Q19-1026/; acl: https://aclanthology.org/Q19-1026/; project: https://ai.google.com/research/NaturalQuestions; code: https://github.com/google-research-datasets/natural-questions; data: https://ai.google.com/research/NaturalQuestions; doi: https://doi.org/10.1162/tacl_a_00276), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
