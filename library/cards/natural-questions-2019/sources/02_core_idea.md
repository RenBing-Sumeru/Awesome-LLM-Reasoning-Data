The contribution is to provide Common public Natural Questions release has 307,373 training examples, 7,830 development examples, and 7,842 test examples, about 323k total; pin public/hidden split and Wikipedia snapshot. for open-domain-qa, search-queries, reading-comprehension under a reusable evaluation surface. The core mechanism is the pairing of task inputs and model outputs with the following feedback contract: long-answer and short-answer F1 under official scorer.

The data object or environment is real user query, Wikipedia page, long-answer span, short-answer span or yes/no/no-answer label; Benchmark size: 超过 300K 个真实搜索问题；常见训练集约 307K，dev/test 各约 7.8K。 The closest comparisons are nearby benchmark and evaluation-surface datasets that share the same feedback contract. The direction label is benchmarks_evaluation_surfaces with a programmatic feedback contract. The main thing to preserve is not only the headline score, but the task object, evaluator, split/version, and artifact lineage.
