On ProcessBench, the paper reports 75.6 average F1 for FlexiVe and attributes the result to GRPO on 1,526 samples; comparisons with GenPRM report about 2.3× lower TFLOPS and 15× less training data. The ablation compares the same base model and 1.5K data across discriminative PRM, SFT, and GRPO settings, which is evidence about the tested training objective rather than proof that every verification rationale is correct.

For end-to-end SDV, the AIME 2024 table reports 83.3% accuracy at N = 16, versus 80.0% for solver-only majority voting and 66.7% for GenPRM-32B Best-of-N in that setup. Iterative results rise from 60.0% at two attempts to 73.3% at four attempts, and the paper reports correcting 25% of initially incorrect AIME 2024 solutions. These results support utility and compute allocation under the specified models and prompts. They do not establish item-level validity of completion probes, error indices, explanations, or revised traces, and they are not record-level data-quality evidence.
