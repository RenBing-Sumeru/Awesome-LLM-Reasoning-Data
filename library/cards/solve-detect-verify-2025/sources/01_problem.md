Long-form reasoning at test time creates two linked costs: solvers can continue after a usable answer has already appeared, and reliable generative verification can consume thousands of additional tokens. Solve-Detect-Verify (SDV) treats stopping, verification, and revision as one inference trajectory rather than sampling many finished answers and ranking them once.

For atlas purposes, the relevant object is not a released corpus. It is a potential episode record containing the problem, the solver trace, hesitation-triggered completion probes, the stopped prefix, FlexiVe outputs, the first predicted error index (or −1), textual diagnostic feedback, revisions, and the final answer. The paper separately uses BIG-Bench Mistake to train FlexiVe; that training set should not be conflated with SDV rollout records.
