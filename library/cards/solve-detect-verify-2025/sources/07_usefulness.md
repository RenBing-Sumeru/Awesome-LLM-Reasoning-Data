This work belongs in the rollout/search/test-time-trace track because the decision record is a budgeted feedback loop: when to stop generating, how much verifier compute to spend, whether to accept a trace, and how feedback changes the next attempt. It is useful as a schema for collecting adaptive-verification trajectories and for comparing static Best-of-N with sequential correction.

For reuse, maintain separate fields for completion-probe outcome, verifier outcome, verifier rationale, refinement action, and final task correctness. That separation supports audits of premature stopping, correlated verifier consensus, feedback-induced regressions, and cost accounting. The paper supports test-time compute and verifier audit uses; it does not by itself establish an SFT or RL training corpus of SDV episodes.
