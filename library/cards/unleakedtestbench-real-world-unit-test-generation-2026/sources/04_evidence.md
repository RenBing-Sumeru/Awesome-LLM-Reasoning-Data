Across evaluated LLMs, average test accuracy, statement coverage, branch coverage, and mutation score on ULT are only 41.32%, 45.10%, 30.22%, and 40.21%. The corresponding values are much higher on TestEval, and PLT is also easier. Test-generation performance on ULT correlates with coding ability at 0.79 (p=0.002), stronger than on TestEval or PLT. These results support ULT as a harder generalization test, although scores still depend on environment reconstruction and mutation-operator choices.
