Verbalized confidence, self-consistency, confidence-weighted voting, restart-based test-time scaling, and web agents all predate BrowseConf. The distinctive contribution is to use a final attempt-level self-assessment as an online stopping/routing signal in a long-horizon browsing environment, then compare three memory policies: no carryover, summarized trajectory context, and explicit avoidance of earlier low-confidence answers. For trace curation, this exposes a useful linkage among attempt, confidence, carried context, retry decision, and selected output. It is a training-free allocation method and evaluation study, not a new confidence-labeled dataset, calibrated verifier, or RL training recipe.
