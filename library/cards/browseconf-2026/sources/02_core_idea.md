Each attempt produces an answer and an integer confidence score from 0 to 100. A threshold chosen on a 500-item SailorFog-QA validation subset triggers another attempt when confidence is too low; execution stops when the threshold is met or after at most ten attempts, then returns the highest-confidence answer if the budget is exhausted. BrowseConf-Zero restarts from scratch. BrowseConf-Summary carries a structured summary of evidence, contradictions, unresolved reasoning, and promising URLs. BrowseConf-Neg carries previous low-confidence answers as negative constraints. Correctness is used for evaluation and threshold analysis, not supplied online as a verifier during deployment.
