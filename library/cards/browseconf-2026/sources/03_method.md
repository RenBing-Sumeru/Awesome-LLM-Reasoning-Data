The study evaluates gpt-oss-120b and DeepSeek-V3.1 on BrowseComp and BrowseComp-zh. The web harness exposes Google search, returning ten titles/snippets/URLs per query, and a visit tool that extracts pages with Jina before a summary model selects goal-relevant content. Inference uses temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95, and a 128k context limit. A context-overflow attempt is assigned confidence -1; the three variants handle that failed attempt differently. Baselines include Pass@1, oracle-style Pass@10, ten-sample self-consistency, and confidence-weighted self-consistency.

A reusable trace needs the validation-set revision and threshold-selection rule; per-attempt answer, confidence, token and interaction counts; search queries, returned URLs, page snapshots, visit objectives and summaries; previous-answer or trajectory-summary context; stop reason; chosen answer; and judge outcome. The paper and metadata do not establish a public code/data package, immutable web snapshot, complete query/action logs, or per-run seeds, so those artifacts remain unknown or unavailable.
