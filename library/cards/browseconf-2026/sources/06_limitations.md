Verbalized confidence is systematically overconfident and its ranking quality can shift across models, languages, domains, prompt formats, tools, or web states. A threshold selected on SailorFog-QA may not transfer to BrowseComp or deployment traffic. High confidence can terminate on a wrong answer; low confidence can discard a correct one. Selecting the maximum-confidence answer after budget exhaustion further depends on relative, not absolute, calibration.

Summaries can omit or distort evidence and may anchor later attempts; negative constraints can suppress an answer that was actually correct. Repeated live-web attempts are not independent, and token/attempt counts omit some tool, summary-model, latency, and API costs. Evaluation uses LLM-as-a-judge following benchmark practice. Public code, full attempts, queries, page snapshots, summaries, rejected outputs, thresholds by model, licenses, decontamination, and replay manifests are not established by the Card and remain unknown.
