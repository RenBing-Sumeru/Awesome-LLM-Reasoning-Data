The paper describes two routes for obtaining the length direction. With annotated reasoning data such as GSM8K or the math portion of PRM800K, a model is fine-tuned toward shorter chains as a cold start. Without explicit explanations, the update between a base model and its corresponding reasoning model, such as LLaMA-3.1-8B versus DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B or Qwen-32B-Instruct versus QwQ-32B-Preview, is used as the direction. Varying its coefficient generates several solutions per question from short to long; CoT-Valve++ adds the normalized length factor during tuning, and CoT-Valve+P consumes successively shorter solutions.

The official Hugging Face collection exposes three datasets under Apache-2.0 labels. MixChain-Z-GSM8K has 6,863 questions and five solutions per record: the original answer, four generated solutions, their token counts, and an extracted target. MixChain-Z-PRM12K has 12,000 math questions and the same five-solution layout, plus correctness flags for the four generated solutions; its dataset card says it is unfiltered and retains incorrect solutions. MixChain-C-LIMO exposes two configurations with 474 and 564 question-solution records and reported average CoT lengths of 2,994.7 and 4,890.6 tokens. The public cards warn that generated solution lengths are not strictly monotonic. No official training code or complete sampling configuration was verified, and the exact answer extractor, seeds, parameter coefficients, model checkpoints used to generate every released row, and decontamination procedure remain unknown.
