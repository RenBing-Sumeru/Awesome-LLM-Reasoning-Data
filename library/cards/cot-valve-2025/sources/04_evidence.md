The paper reports that, for QwQ-32B-Preview on GSM8K, one CoT-Valve+P setting reduces the average trace from 741.1 to 225.5 tokens while accuracy changes from 95.1 to 94.9; on AIME24, a GSM8K-trained setting reports 4,629.6 tokens and 13/30 correct versus an Overthink baseline at 5,154.5 tokens and 13/30. Other tables cover LLaMA-3.2-1B, LLaMA-3.1-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, and a LIMO-trained Qwen model. These results support controllable compression under the reported models and evaluation settings. They do not show that every shortened trace preserves necessary reasoning, that correctness flags are error-free, or that the released mixtures are contamination-free. The official ACL record and the three author-owned Hugging Face datasets establish publication and partial data availability; they do not establish a fully reproducible generator.
