The paper states that extreme compression can reduce accuracy, especially on complex tasks; the method is bounded by the original model's ability to produce useful reasoning, and its parameter-space mechanism lacks a complete theoretical interpretation. The released PRM12K subset intentionally retains incorrect solutions, while length ordering is not always monotonic. The LIMO release has a different, smaller schema than the GSM8K and PRM12K releases. Exact generation coefficients, prompts, seeds, checkpoints, answer-normalization rules, split construction, source-license propagation, and decontamination are not fully disclosed in the verified artifacts. There is no verified official training-code release. Accuracy-token trade-offs and model-based step scores are evaluation evidence, not proof that the released reasoning traces are faithful, minimal, or safe to reuse.
