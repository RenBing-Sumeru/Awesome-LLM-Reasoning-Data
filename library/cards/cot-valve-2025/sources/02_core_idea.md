CoT-Valve treats the parameter update between a long- and a short-reasoning model as a length-control direction. Scaling that update with an interpolation or extrapolation coefficient produces traces of different lengths for the same question, which are assembled into MixChain instead of relying only on repeated sampling or handwritten deletion. CoT-Valve++ trains with a normalized per-trace length factor so intermediate positions on the direction are supervised; CoT-Valve+P progressively trains on shorter traces rather than jumping directly to the shortest one. The feedback contract is answer-level: released PRM12K records expose Boolean correctness indicators for generated solutions, while the public schema does not expose step-level verifier labels. This makes the work relevant to the rollout/search/test-time-trace track as a long-to-short distillation and budget-control recipe, not as evidence that compressed traces are faithful reasoning.
