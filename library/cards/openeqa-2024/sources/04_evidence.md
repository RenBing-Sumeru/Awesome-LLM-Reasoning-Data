The evidence to trust is the official task release, data schema, and scoring contract, not only leaderboard numbers. The local audited scale is: project page reports over 1,600 human-generated questions over more than 180 real-world environments; HM3D questions are shared across EM-EQA and A-EQA settings. Instance-level evidence is a single record containing episodic memory or active-exploration setting, environment/video or scan context, open-vocabulary question, ground-truth answer, model answer, and LLM-Match evaluation output., the model output or trajectory, and the verifier/judge result from LLM-Match automatic evaluation protocol correlated with human judgment, plus human judgment as reference.

Reported aggregate scores should be interpreted only within the official setup. Evidence boundaries include Exact split, hidden/public policy, live-refresh policy, and subset version should be pinned from current official artifacts before score reuse.; License and redistribution terms require artifact-level review unless explicitly recorded in official sources.; Public benchmark records can leak into future training data; post-release score claims require versioned contamination evidence. Additional audit note: Official project page verifies title, authors, FAIR Meta note, CVPR 2024 citation, 1600+ questions, 180+ environments, EM-EQA/A-EQA settings, code/benchmark links, and LLM-Match description. If a paper uses a judge or rubric, the judge prompt/model/version is part of the evidence; if it uses programmatic checking, the exact checker and runtime are part of the evidence.
