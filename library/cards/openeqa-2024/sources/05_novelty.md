The prior-work baseline is agent-environment, tool-use, and benchmark-harness papers in the same atlas category. The change introduced by OpenEQA: Embodied Question Answering in the Era of Foundation Models is the particular task object and feedback contract: episodic memory or active-exploration setting, environment/video or scan context, open-vocabulary question, ground-truth answer, model answer, and LLM-Match evaluation output. scored by LLM-Match automatic evaluation protocol correlated with human judgment, plus human judgment as reference. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://open-eqa.github.io/; project: https://open-eqa.github.io/; code: https://github.com/facebookresearch/open-eqa; data: https://github.com/facebookresearch/open-eqa), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
