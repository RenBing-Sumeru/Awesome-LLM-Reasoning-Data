The primary paper is arXiv:2509.06501v3, dated September 2025. OpenReview confirms that a version was submitted to ICLR 2026; it does not currently establish acceptance. The arXiv PDF and the official GitHub/Hugging Face citation list 15 authors, including Zijia Wu, whereas the current OpenReview metadata lists 14 and omits that author. This Card therefore uses the 15-author paper citation and records the discrepancy instead of silently reconciling it.

The concrete problem is the shortage of difficult information-seeking questions and successful long-horizon search demonstrations for training open web agents. The paper argues that initial multi-website questions remain too easy when they expose direct search clues. WebExplorer replaces explicit graph construction with model-driven exploration from Wikipedia entities, then repeatedly removes or obscures clues while keeping the same answer (Paper §§2.2–2.4; Appendix B).

Three linked data objects must be kept separate. First, WebExplorer-QA is reported as approximately 40K evolved query-answer pairs. Second, supervised fine-tuning uses approximately 13K rejection-sampled, correct ReAct trajectories containing thoughts, search/browse calls, observations, and a final response. Third, reinforcement learning directly consumes approximately 12K QA items and generates online episodes scored by format compliance and DeepSeek-V3 final-answer judgment. The official public data, however, contains only 100 rows with `id`, `query`, and `answer`; it is not a release of the SFT or RL trajectories.

This belongs in `environment_agent_trajectory_data` because the paper specifies the trajectory schema, live search/browse substrate, training attachment points, and episode reward. It is not a packaged resettable browser environment, a process-label dataset, or a complete public trajectory corpus. The bilingual sections are collection-ready, but the artifact remains L3 because paper-scale data, splits, teachers, training code, deterministic replay, and contamination controls are unresolved.
