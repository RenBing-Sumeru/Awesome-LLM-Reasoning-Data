The construction evidence is measured with fixed models and a live web scaffold, not by human validation of every record. In Table 1, Claude-4-Sonnet's accuracy falls from 86.6% on Initial QA to 67.1% on Evolved QA, while average tool-calling turns rise from 7.9 to 9.9. The paper interprets this as evidence that five-round clue removal and obfuscation increase search difficulty. It does not establish factual correctness, uniqueness, or training value for every QA pair, and Evolved QA remains easier than BrowseComp-en in the Figure 4 comparison (Paper §2.4, Table 1, Figure 4).

Table 2 reports Avg@4 accuracy for the same 8B model before and after RL, using DeepSeek-V3 as LLM-as-Judge. SFT-to-RL changes are: BrowseComp-en 7.9→15.7, BrowseComp-zh 21.3→32.0, GAIA 43.7→50.0, WebWalkerQA 59.8→62.7, FRAMES 72.6→75.7, XBench-DeepSearch 47.5→53.7, and HLE 16.0→17.3. These are author-reported evaluation results under the paper's scaffold; they are not independently reproduced and should not be read as direct estimates of dataset quality (Paper Table 2, pp.9–11).

Figure 5 shows that average tool calls per RL trajectory rise from roughly 11 to more than 16 and average trajectory length exceeds 40K tokens as training proceeds; BrowseComp-en/zh Avg@4 also rises. This supports a correlation among training progress, longer trajectories, and score improvement. It does not isolate whether the cause is the QA data, GRPO, reward design, progressive context budget, additional sampling, or live-tool behavior (Paper Figure 5 and §4.2, pp.10–11).

Artifact evidence is narrower than paper evidence. The official GitHub repository announces 100 released QA pairs and includes a 100-line `data/data_sample.jsonl`; the Hugging Face dataset card exposes the `id/query/answer` schema, states that only 100 samples are released because of company policy, and marks the dataset auto-gated under Apache-2.0. WebExplorer-8B weights and inference/evaluation code are public. The approximately 40K QA corpus, approximately 13K SFT trajectories, approximately 12K RL set, reward logs, synthesis code, and training code were not found in the official release.
