Table 1 reports average accuracy over GSM8K, MATH, GAOKAO, and ZHONGKAO of 39.50 for Base2, 41.30 for response diversification, 42.67 for query expansion, 39.12 for retrospective enhancement, and 50.60 for tutorship amplification. This supports that three operators improved the tested mixtures and tutorship produced the largest observed gain, while retrospective enhancement did not beat the control. It does not establish intrinsic record quality: tutorship adds 13.90B synthetic tokens versus 4.78B–6.82B for the other arms, and no independent teacher-error audit is reported. (Paper Table 1.)

For stage allocation, Table 8 reports Base1 at 19.11 average, Base1-SFT at 35.62, Base2 at 39.50, and their 1% SFT controls at 23.73 and 43.54. The authors estimate SFT's capability gain at about 60% of CPT's after subtracting the instruction-following effect. Difficulty experiments report Easy-SFT 27.03 versus Easy-CPT 30.79, and Hard-SFT 29.66 versus Hard-CPT 41.51. These comparisons support the paper's setup-specific stage finding, but CPT and SFT use different optimizers, exposures, and checkpoint selection, so they are not a stage-only causal proof. (Paper §5.1–§5.3, Table 3, Table 8.)

The final model result is broader but less diagnostic: MathGPT-8B scores 70.62 average over the four math sets versus 32.91 for Llama3-8B, and MMLU is reported as 0.6222 versus 0.6211. The final recipe changes base model, context length, data sources, operator mixture, difficulty filtering, and scale together. Its benchmark performance therefore cannot isolate any one synthesis operator or verify individual records. (Paper Table 4.)

Evaluation itself is judge-mediated. The paper uses `Tianqiao/DeepSeek-7B-Math-Compare-Answer` to normalize irregular outputs; its public prompt explicitly says to ignore errors in the student's reasoning and focus on the final answer. The paper then takes the better zero-shot or few-shot score per dataset. Reported accuracy is thus answer-level model judgment, not step-level rationale verification.

Artifact evidence is split. The MathGPT-8B Hugging Face repository was rechecked on 2026-07-22 and contains configuration, tokenizer, and two BF16 safetensor shards under Apache-2.0. No source/seed corpus, method-specific synthetic corpus, rejected-output log, synthesis code, or item-level lineage is released. `artifact_verified: true` refers to the model checkpoint, not to the training records.
