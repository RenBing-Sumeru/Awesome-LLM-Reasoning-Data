The training pool has three branches. General data draws on RedPajama ArXiv, C4, and Wikipedia together with AlgebraicStack and StarCoder code. OpenWebMath supplies the mathematical corpus. Problem-solving data combines NuminaMath, Lila, and proprietary records. Seeds are filtered for completeness and reasoning-step count, but exact thresholds, source proportions, and item manifests are absent. The main experiment repeatedly says 14.7B mathematical-corpus tokens; Appendix A.1 says 13.7B, an unresolved internal discrepancy. (Paper §2–§3; Appendix A.1 and C.)

The four operators are materially different. Response diversification asks for two correct, distinct extra solutions and requires an initial response tag whose value is `accept` or `refuse`. Query expansion creates two new question-solution pairs and self-checks each with Accept/Refuse plus a reason. Retrospective enhancement constructs a `[back]` retry trace by inserting later steps into earlier reasoning, but its full prompt, acceptance parser, and filtering are not disclosed. Tutorship samples a weak-model attempt; a strong teacher starts with a check tag whose value is `correct` or `wrong`; for a wrong attempt it identifies erroneous steps, corrects them, and continues. Generator, student, and teacher checkpoints, decoding, retries, parser behavior, and per-method yields are unknown. (Paper §4; Appendix G.)

Reported outputs are 14,018,544 records/6.82B tokens for response diversification, 24,459,192/4.78B for query expansion, 14,707,792/5.04B for retrospective enhancement, and 11,942,328/13.90B for tutorship amplification. Each arm adds its output to the Base2 CPT mixture; the unequal token budgets are part of the experimental treatment and prevent a quality-only comparison.

Controlled CPT uses Llama2 for at most 25,000 steps, global batch 1024, context length 4096, a 95/5 train-validation split, and learning rate warmed to 1e-4 then cosine-decayed to 1e-5. It selects the lowest validation-loss checkpoint. The stage comparison allocates 7.2B problem-solving tokens to CPT or SFT and adds 0.072B-token 1% SFT controls for instruction following. SFT uses batch 256, three epochs, cosine decay from 1e-5 to 1e-6, and reports the best of ten checkpoints.

The final MathGPT-8B starts from Llama3-8B, prioritizes problems with more than five reasoning steps, uses response diversification, query expansion, and especially tutorship amplification, and trains for 25,000 CPT steps at context length 8192. The paper specifies 39.6B general, 46.7B mathematical-corpus, and 51.1B problem-solving/synthetic tokens and describes the total as about 140B, including 100B math-related tokens. The model card repeats the 140B/100B statement.
