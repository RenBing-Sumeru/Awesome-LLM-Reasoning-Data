Use this card to separate report-level process disclosure from reusable training evidence. It is particularly useful for checking whether a claim about RLHF, small-model-derived alignment, filtering, or a safety evaluation has enough supporting artifacts to count as a data, feedback, or verification contract.

The missing ledger identifies priorities for future audit: manifests and rights; SFT, new-supervision, and RLHF schemas; teacher/reward/verifier specifications; filters and rejection yields; rollout and optimization logs; train/evaluation splits; decontamination; and releaseable evaluation environments and records.
