The useful contribution is the combination of stage-level disclosures with unusually concrete examples of evaluation contracts. Readers can see both what OpenAI names about post-training—SFT, RLHF, new supervision, smaller-model-derived alignment data, and safety training—and what remains withheld.

The evaluation details sharpen, rather than close, the disclosure boundary. An autograder, hidden unit tests, or a fixed CTF rollout protocol are evidence about evaluation design; they cannot be promoted into a disclosed training reward, verifier, environment, or data pipeline.
