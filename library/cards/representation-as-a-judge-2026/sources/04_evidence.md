Claim: latent representations of small LMs can predict evaluation signals more effectively than prompting those same small models. On reasoning benchmarks GSM8K, MATH, and GPQA, INSPECTOR substantially outperforms prompting-based small-LM baselines and approaches full LLM judges (paper §§1–2). The paper additionally reports downstream SFT gains after filtering noisy reasoning data. These experiments support fidelity to the chosen strong-judge labels in these settings; they do not establish human-grounded correctness, because the probes inherit the teacher judge's preferences.
