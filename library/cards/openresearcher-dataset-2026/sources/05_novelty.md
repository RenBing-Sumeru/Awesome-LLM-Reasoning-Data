The closest prior baseline is online-only deep-research trajectory generation. The paper changes the reusable object by making question id, question, answer, interleaved messages, latency, error, attempts, status, and chunk metadata explicit and coupling it to final-answer checking, retrievability bootstrapping, error/status fields, and trajectory filtering. This is a direction signal because another team can train the stated consumer from the released records. The inherited backbone, source tasks, autoregressive objective, and public benchmarks are not new.
