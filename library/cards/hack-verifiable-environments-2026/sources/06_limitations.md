The benchmark detects only shortcuts specified by its designer; an agent may exploit a different unintended behavior without being counted. The present implementation transforms disclosed TextArena games and filesystem-mediated actions, so its distribution differs from open-ended tools, web systems, and undiscovered vulnerabilities. A deterministic flag proves the chosen action occurred, not its deliberateness or general safety implication. Reuse should document the threat model and manually inspect a sample of trajectories before treating hack rate as an alignment score.
