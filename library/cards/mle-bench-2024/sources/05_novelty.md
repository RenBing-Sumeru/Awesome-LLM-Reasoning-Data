The prior-work baseline is code-generation and executable-program benchmarks with unit-test or judge feedback. The change introduced by MLE-bench: Evaluating Machine Learning Agents on Machine Learning Engineering is the particular task object and feedback contract: official records expose Kaggle-style ML engineering tasks with competition metrics, along with prompt/task metadata and an evaluation target. scored by scoring is defined by the official benchmark evaluator, judge, answer key, environment predicate, or task-specific metric. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07095; code: https://github.com/openai/mle-bench/; data: https://github.com/openai/mle-bench/; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2410.07095), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
