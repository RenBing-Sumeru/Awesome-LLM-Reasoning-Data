**Inputs and initialization.** The paper reports sampling RL prompts from public MATH-train and NuminaMath. The checked launcher reads `datasets/train/train_30k.jsonl`, using `text`, `label`, and `data_type`; it is not accompanied by a row-level mapping to the paper-named sources. The policy generates M complete initial chains for each prompt.

**Search and feedback.** EPTree masks near-end positions, ranks eligible tokens by negative log probability as its uncertainty measure, forks continuations from selected positions, and forms leaves. The paper assigns each leaf final-answer reward 1 or 0. Descendant correctness becomes a node value, then global plus local advantages; repeated non-leaf updates receive square-root reweighting. The launcher invokes private extractor and binary-judge services, so the deployed answer-normalization and equivalence behavior are unknown.

**Training settings.** The reported RL configuration uses 16 prompts per update, temperature 1.2, top-p 0.95, maximum length 8192, learning rate 1.5e-6, and KL coefficient 1e-4. It compares 30 EPTree leaves with 16 independent responses at similar generation-token budget, but reports a TreeRL training batch of 480 versus 256 for ChainRL; equal inference cost does not imply equal training computation. The checked launcher agrees on model family, leaf-setting parameters, temperature, top-p, maximum length, and learning rate, but sets KL=0.0 and `NUM_TRACE=16`. A paper-matched command, service deployment, raw-tree serializer, and checkpoint were not verified.
