For **Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data**, TreeRL is a useful recipe for experiments in which the record must preserve more than a final answer. A reproduction or comparison should log prompt provenance; initial chains; fork scores and positions; parent links; all leaves; terminal extraction and verdict metadata; selected and rejected traces; token and wall-clock budgets; and the exact policy, verifier, and launcher revisions.

It can support a controlled comparison of independent-sampling RLVR against tree-derived process supervision, or an ablation of entropy selection, branch count, expansion rounds, and reward reweighting. The appropriate reuse class is **recipe/audit reference and controlled experimental baseline**, not direct training-data reuse: `train_30k.jsonl` may be used as input only after source provenance, empty-record handling, split semantics, decontamination, and rights are independently audited.

The Card also provides an audit checklist: ask whether an observed gain comes from a better branch selector, a stronger terminal service, more generated leaves, more optimization traces, or a process-credit formula. Do not use benchmark performance as a substitute for releasing and validating the underlying trace records.
