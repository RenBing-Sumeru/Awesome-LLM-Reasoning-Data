The final paper supports the following reconstruction, while leaving a substantial reproducibility boundary.

1. **Collect source resources.** SWE-bench-Lite uses non-repeated Python files. The other environments use public documentation, tutorials, and FAQs for services and applications including GitLab, Google Maps, Amazon, Reddit, Chrome, GIMP, LibreOffice, Ubuntu, Thunderbird, VLC, VS Code, dbt, Dagster, Astronomer, Airbyte, Superset, Metabase, Snowflake, BigQuery, and JupyterLab. Exact snapshots, crawl dates, URLs per record, and rights dispositions are undisclosed.
2. **Generate tasks.** Claude-3.5-Sonnet samples three instructions from every document. Across 28,635 documents, this produces 85,905 attempted tasks and therefore 85,905 raw trajectories in the ICLR final. Temperature is reported as 0; exact model revision, other decoding parameters, retries, and seeds are unknown.
3. **Interact with environments.** Claude-3.5-Sonnet receives the instruction, current observation, history, and environment-specific action space, predicts an action, and observes the resulting state. Episodes end on the agent's stop action or the environment maximum-step limit. Environment/container revisions and benchmark-specific limits are not reported.
4. **Construct candidates backward.** For every contiguous subtrajectory, the model creates either a step summary or an abstraction of the segment's purpose. This can replace a misaligned initial goal with one that matches the observed behavior. The final reports 1,456,808 candidate pairs.
5. **Filter.** Consecutive duplicate action-observation states are removed. Claude-3.5-Sonnet and Gemini-1.5-Pro then assess coherence, naturalness, reasonableness, and instruction alignment; only unanimous acceptances remain. The final retained total is 440,008.
6. **Use for ICL.** At each agent step, observation-based retrieval can contribute up to five examples and model-based retrieval up to five. The latter has an LLM formulate a query from the instruction, history, and current observation, then uses `stella_en_1.5B_v5` for dense retrieval.
7. **Use for SFT.** Convert the pair to next-action prediction and fine-tune CodeGemma-7B or Codestral-22B with LoRA. Reported settings are batch size 128, learning rate \(2\times10^{-5}\), warmup ratio 0.03, maximum length 8192, three epochs, and 80GB H100 machines. LoRA rank, GPU count, runtime, total tokens, cost, and full execution commands remain unknown.

A faithful reproduction needs source snapshots, environment images, exact prompts and API revisions, raw-to-derived IDs, committee outputs, rejected candidates, seeds, and immutable manifests. None was found as an official reusable artifact.
