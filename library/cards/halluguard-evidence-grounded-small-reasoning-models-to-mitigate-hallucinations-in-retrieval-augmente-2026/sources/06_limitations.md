First, the dataset is synthetic and English-only. Claims and reasoning responses generated by a limited model family may contain stylistic shortcuts that differ from naturally occurring RAG hallucinations. Reuse should therefore test human-written claims, other languages, and unseen response generators.

Second, labels indicate whether a claim is supported by the supplied document, not whether it is true in the real world. A false claim can be classified as grounded when the document itself contains misinformation. The model should therefore be used as a document-grounding guardrail rather than a general fact checker, with human oversight in sensitive domains.
