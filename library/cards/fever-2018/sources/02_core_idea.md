The contribution is a large benchmark of 185,445 human-annotated claims derived from Wikipedia, with labels and evidence requirements. The mechanism edits or constructs claims from Wikipedia, annotates veracity, and requires systems to retrieve evidence for supported/refuted labels. The feedback contract combines label accuracy with evidence correctness through the FEVER score. Closest comparisons are recognizing textual entailment datasets, fact-checking corpora, and later claim-grounding benchmarks. The direction label is evidence-coupled factual verification.
