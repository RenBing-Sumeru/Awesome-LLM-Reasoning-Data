The paper reports 185,445 claims with split counts of 145,449 training, 9,999 dev, 9,999 test, and 19,998 reserved claims. The row-level evidence is the gold label plus evidence sentence set; aggregate accuracy alone is insufficient because evidence retrieval is part of the task. NEI claims have no supporting evidence requirement. Evidence is bounded by Wikipedia at construction time, annotation policy, and the official scorer's treatment of evidence sets.
