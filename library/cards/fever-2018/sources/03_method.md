1. Inputs: Wikipedia pages, constructed claims, human labels, and evidence sentence annotations. 2. Pipeline: generate or edit claims, label them as SUPPORTS, REFUTES, or NOT ENOUGH INFO, annotate evidence for verifiable claims, and evaluate systems on label plus evidence. 3. Outputs: train/dev/test/reserved splits, evidence annotations, and scorer outputs. 4. Feedback: FEVER score gives credit when the predicted label is correct and, for supported/refuted claims, sufficient evidence is retrieved among the allowed predictions. 5. Reuse requires pinning dataset release, Wikipedia snapshot assumptions, scorer version, maximum evidence predictions, and split policy.
