FEVER asks how to evaluate fact verification as claim labeling with retrieved textual evidence. The primary sources are the NAACL-HLT 2018 ACL Anthology paper, arXiv:1803.05355, the FEVER dataset page, and the official scorer. It belongs here as an evidence-grounded fact-verification benchmark, not as a free-form generation or RAG benchmark. A record contains a Wikipedia-derived claim, a label among SUPPORTS, REFUTES, and NOT ENOUGH INFO, and evidence sentence sets for supported/refuted claims. Its atlas value is the explicit coupling of answer label and evidence retrieval in the scoring contract.
