Limitations center on benchmark drift and scaffold sensitivity. Live web tasks may change after publication; simulated web tasks may overfit to the released environment; and browser-agent scores can move with prompt format, vision settings, action abstraction, retries, and evaluator implementation.

For this Card, unresolved audit items are: review task consistency protocol, failure taxonomy, license, and comparison setup. Any downstream use should separate evaluation-only evidence from trainable trajectory data and should avoid treating public benchmark instances as contamination-free.
