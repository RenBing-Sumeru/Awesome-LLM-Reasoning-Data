Use this paper when a retrieval failure looks like more than a vocabulary mismatch. It is especially helpful before choosing whether to improve a retriever, add inference-aware reranking, or redesign an evaluation set. The taxonomy gives a reader concrete comparison questions: what evidence is needed, where inference occurs, and what trade-off the system accepts. Begin with a failed query and describe the indirect link that a useful result must satisfy. Then locate the likely failure point: candidate generation, reasoning over candidates, or final ordering. The survey helps frame that diagnosis and select comparable studies, but it does not substitute for testing the proposed change on a benchmark whose relevance definition matches the application.
