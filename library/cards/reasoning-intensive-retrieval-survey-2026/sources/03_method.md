This is a literature synthesis. Its working object is a query, candidate evidence, an inferential connection between them, and a ranked result. The paper first groups benchmarks by knowledge domain and modality, then classifies methods by where and how they integrate reasoning into retrieval. The resulting map includes retrievers and rerankers as well as the evaluation settings that motivate them. For a cited study, record the query form, the evidence unit, the point at which inference is introduced, and the retrieval metric or downstream measure. This avoids treating a benchmark, a retriever, and a reranker as interchangeable. The survey provides a map of relationships; individual papers remain the source for training details, retrieval budgets, and exact implementations.
