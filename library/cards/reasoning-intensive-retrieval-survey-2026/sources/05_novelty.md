The contribution is a common framework for a fragmented area. Rather than grouping work only by model family, the survey connects benchmark properties with the exact point at which reasoning is inserted into a retrieval pipeline. That comparison makes it easier to see whether a reported gain comes from better candidates, better inference over candidates, or a different task definition. The novelty is a vocabulary for separating interventions that are often blended together in retrieval discussions. It is not a new universal retriever, a shared dataset, or proof that reasoning should be added at every stage. Readers can use the framework to identify what is genuinely new in a primary paper and what remains inherited from its benchmark or pipeline.
