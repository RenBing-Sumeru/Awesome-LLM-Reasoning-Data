Software-engineering benchmarks may occur in an LLM's pre-training corpus, inflating evaluation scores; most models do not disclose enough training data for a direct audit. The field lacked a broad, manually checked measurement across languages and benchmark sources.

LessLeak-Bench studies 83 Java, Python, and C/C++ benchmarks against StarCoder pre-training data, labels suspected duplicates, and releases cleaned benchmark records plus a leakage-detection dataset.
