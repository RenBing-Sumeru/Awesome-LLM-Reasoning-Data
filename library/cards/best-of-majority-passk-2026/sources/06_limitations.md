The guarantees require a particular Pass@k abstraction, a reference policy with useful coverage, and a reward model whose error fits the analysis. Frequency can discard a rare but genuinely correct answer, while reward ranking can still fail when the surviving candidates exploit scorer mistakes. The mathematical experiments do not settle costs for long free-form responses, semantic equivalence detection, or domains where several valid answers are expressed differently. A deployment should therefore report the equivalence rule, candidate count, submission count, reward-model version, and total generation and scoring cost.
