Use this work when a system may generate many solutions but can submit only a limited number for evaluation, such as a Pass@k coding or mathematics workflow. Keep the candidate budget N, the submission limit k, the answer-normalization rule, and reward-model calls fixed when comparing it with majority vote or best-of-N. It is most useful for readers who need to decide whether extra samples are buying coverage, being spent on unreliable reranking, or both; it is not a substitute for training a better generator or auditing the verifier.
