RePanda reaches 84.09% accuracy on the TabFact test set. Without additional fine-tuning, it obtains 84.72% on WikiFact, the claim-based reinterpretation of WikiTableQuestions, approaching the zero-shot result of 671B DeepSeek-Chat. After training on PanWiki, direct answer retrieval reaches 75.1%. These results show that executable table reasoning can be distilled into a smaller model, although comparisons also change training data, model type, and output interface and therefore cannot be attributed only to pandas representation.
