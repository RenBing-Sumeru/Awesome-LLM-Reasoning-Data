Multilingual reasoning asks a language model to solve a logical task across languages, not merely to translate a final answer. The authors identify an early-stage field shaped by cross-language misalignment, bias, and limited resources for low-resource languages. This Findings EMNLP 2025 paper surveys the methods, training resources, and benchmarks needed to understand that gap; it does not introduce a new model or dataset.

The paper therefore sets a useful boundary between language coverage and reasoning coverage. A system may accept many languages while evidence exists for only a small set of tasks or resource-rich languages. Read the survey when deciding which languages and reasoning tasks an evaluation must include; it cannot by itself demonstrate equitable performance for every language community.
