This is a literature survey rather than a training procedure. Its inputs are existing multilingual reasoning studies, their data resources, and their benchmarks; its output is a systematic overview plus analysis of state-of-the-art methods and performance. The paper supplies no common reward model, verifier, sampling budget, codebase, or new data release, so those details must be checked in each cited primary work.

For reusable reading notes, record the languages, task formulation, training resource, benchmark, and scoring rule for every primary paper. That compact schema reflects the survey's organization and exposes missing information early. It also keeps distinct the survey's synthesis from the operational choices of the systems it summarizes, which can differ substantially even under similar labels.
