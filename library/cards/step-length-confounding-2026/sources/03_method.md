Prompts come from LIMO-v2 and a random 10K subset of AceReason. Four source-model families generate responses; Math-Verify retains answer-correct candidates. Target models assign token probabilities, responses are split into reasoning steps, and baseline or ASLEC scores rank candidates. Public releases include MSMS-LIMO-v2-SFT and MSMS-AceReason-20K-SFT plus generation/scoring code.
