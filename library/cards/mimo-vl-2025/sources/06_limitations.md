The largest limitation is disclosure. Corpus source inventories, URLs, licenses, language/domain counts, exact mixture weights, teacher model identities, generation prompts, rejection thresholds, stage-wise yields, and rejected records are unknown. The 80K visual-reasoning set is described but not released, and its proprietary K–12 component cannot be independently audited. Preference data sizes, annotator involvement, judge calibration, reward-model accuracy, and disagreement handling are also not reported.

Verifier coverage is heterogeneous: symbolic tasks have executable checks, but learned reward models stand in for human preference, and the report gives no false-positive/false-negative analysis for any reward route. phash image deduplication reduces exact or near-exact visual overlap but does not establish semantic decontamination across text, questions, videos, or transformed samples. The authors further observe cross-domain RL interference and unequal response-length and difficulty dynamics, so simultaneous gains are not guaranteed. Released code and model licenses do not establish rights to reproduce or redistribute the underlying corpus or trajectories.
