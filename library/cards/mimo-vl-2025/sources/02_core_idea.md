The central idea is to make reasoning supervision part of the later pre-training distribution rather than a small post-hoc instruction-tuning add-on. Open-source questions spanning perceptual QA, document QA, video QA, visual reasoning, web content, and literary works are filtered; a large reasoning model regenerates explicit long-CoT answers; and multi-stage checks retain answers and reasoning processes judged correct, clear, non-redundant, and consistently formatted. Stage 4 then increases the reasoning share and introduces long-form reasoning under a 32K sequence length.

Post-training uses Mixed On-policy Reinforcement Learning (MORL), which jointly serves rule-verifiable tasks and preference alignment. A task-aware router selects Math-Verify, spatial/temporal overlap, exact counting, or one of two learned reward models, allowing text, image, and video tasks to share a fully on-policy GRPO training loop.
