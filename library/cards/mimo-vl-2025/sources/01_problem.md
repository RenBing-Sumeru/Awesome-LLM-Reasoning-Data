MiMo-VL addresses a coupled data problem in compact vision-language modeling: preserving broad visual perception while transferring the language backbone’s reasoning ability into image, video, document, GUI, and text settings. The report argues that short-answer multimodal QA is insufficient supervision for this goal. Its alternative is a staged mixture of large-scale multimodal pre-training, synthetic long-chain-of-thought reasoning data, long-context SFT, and mixed on-policy reinforcement learning.

For a data atlas, the important object is not the 7B model alone. It is the stack of caption, interleaved, OCR, grounding, video, GUI, reasoning, preference, and RL query collections, together with modality-specific filters and reward routes. The report gives useful pipeline-level disclosure, but does not publish the training corpus, item manifests, mixture proportions, teacher identities, preference pairs, or reward logs.
