MiMo-VL’s contribution is primarily a disclosed training-data composition and reward integration recipe rather than a new public dataset. Two choices are especially relevant: placing substantial synthetic long-CoT multimodal reasoning data inside later pre-training, and routing heterogeneous rule-based and learned rewards through one mixed, fully on-policy RL run. The report also specifies different verification contracts for symbolic answers, boxes, points, counts, temporal segments, and human-preference proxies.

This is distinct from treating all multimodal examples as generic QA or scoring every domain with one judge model. However, the novelty claim should remain bounded: many component techniques—re-captioning, rejection sampling, GRPO, Bradley–Terry reward modeling, and task-specific verifiers—are established. The report’s value lies in how these components are combined and scaled for a compact VLM.
