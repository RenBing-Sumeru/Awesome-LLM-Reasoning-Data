This report is useful as a design reference for multimodal reasoning-data programs that must combine perception, reasoning, localization, video, GUI interaction, and preference alignment. It provides a practical map from task object to verifier: symbolic equivalence, spatial overlap, containment, exact counting, temporal overlap, or a modality-specific learned reward. Its staged token budgets and separation of alignment, general multimodal learning, long-context reasoning, and RL also support recipe planning.

It is less useful as a source of reusable data. No training corpus, manifest, preference set, synthetic trace collection, or reward implementation is available. The Card should therefore be used to compare construction patterns and audit questions—not to claim a reproducible dataset or to infer that benchmark gains alone validate data quality.
