Pre-training consumes 2.4T tokens in four stages: 300B for projector warm-up on image-caption pairs; 167B for vision-language alignment with interleaved data; 1.4T for all-parameter multimodal pre-training; and 550B for 32K long-context SFT. The third stage mixes OCR, grounding, video, GUI, limited QA/instruction/reasoning data, and some text-only data. The fourth adds long text, documents, high-resolution images, extended videos, and a much larger share of long reasoning data.

Curation is modality-specific. Captions are deduplicated with perceptual hashes and text filters, re-captioned, checked for linguistic consistency and repetition, and distribution-balanced with bilingual metadata. Interleaved pages, books, and papers are filtered for readable, knowledge-dense text and informative, safe, relevant images. Video data receives event-level captions with timestamps; GUI data includes grounding, standardized cross-platform action trajectories, and synthetic Chinese examples. Benchmark images are separately checked with phash-based deduplication.

For RLVR, the report gives a concrete 80K visual-reasoning set: open and proprietary K–12 questions are converted toward free-answer forms, filtered by model-estimated difficulty, stripped of image-irrelevant items, cleaned, and category-balanced. Rewards use Math-Verify for text/visual reasoning, GIoU or point-in-box for grounding, exact count accuracy, and temporal IoU for video localization. RLHF queries come from open instruction sets and in-house writing, are screened and clustered, balanced across Chinese/English and helpfulness/harmlessness, answered by several models, and pairwise ranked by an advanced VLM. Text and multimodal reward models use a Bradley–Terry objective. MORL runs a fully on-policy GRPO variant with one update per rollout, no KL loss, dynamic sampling, easy-data filtering, and resampling; rewards are routed as services and no separate format reward is used.
