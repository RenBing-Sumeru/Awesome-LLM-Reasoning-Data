R1-Reward reformulates multimodal reward modeling as rule-based reinforcement learning and introduces StableReinforce. It aggregates 200K preference examples; the released R1-Reward-RL records are RL training trajectories rather than a new human preference corpus.

The model first learns the judgment format from preference data. StableReinforce then modifies the loss, advantage estimation, and reward design so that generated reasoning and preference conclusions can be optimized without collapse.
