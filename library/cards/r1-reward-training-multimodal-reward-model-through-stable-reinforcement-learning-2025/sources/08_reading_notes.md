1. **Positioning:** Multimodal reward models are usually trained with supervised pairs, while the value of long reasoning and RL remains unclear.
2. **Method handle:** The decisive actions are aggregate multimodal preference data, cold-start reward reasoning, apply stablereinforce, followed by scale inference and evaluate.
3. **Artifact handle:** R1-Reward reformulates multimodal reward modeling as rule-based reinforcement learning and introduces StableReinforce.
4. **Evidence anchor:** R1-Reward improves over prior state of the art by 8.4% on VL Reward-Bench and 14.3% on Multimodal Reward Bench.
5. **Reuse decision:** Rule rewards can encourage formatting or answer shortcuts, and stability is demonstrated for specific models and tasks.
