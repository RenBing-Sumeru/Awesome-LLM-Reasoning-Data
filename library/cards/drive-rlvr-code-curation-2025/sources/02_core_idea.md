DRIVE first distills 1.27M unnamed open-source prompts with DeepSeek-R1-0528 and uses repeated arena-style failure retention to reduce them to 470K, with hard examples duplicated. RLVR then has two stages: broad GRPO over about 9K prompts to expand entropy, followed by Pre-GRPO phases that repeatedly retain the lowest-pass-rate LiveCode V6 problems, shrinking from 175 to 72, 50, and 25 cases.
