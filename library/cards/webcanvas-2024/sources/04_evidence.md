The arXiv abstract reports 542 tasks, 2,439 intermediate evaluation states, and a best agent result of 23.1% task success and 48.8% task completion on the Mind2Web-Live test set.

Instance-level evidence comes from the released task object and the scorer: key-node checks over intermediate states plus task success and efficiency metrics. Aggregate scores are meaningful only for the reported split, model setup, and harness. Public artifacts support inspection, but they do not remove contamination, dependency, or license checks.
