The paper reports evaluation on nine open and closed LLMs and states that CodeAgent improves CodeAgentBench pass@1 by 2.0 to 15.8 points, with a maximum 72.7% relative gain for GPT-4 over a documentation-only baseline. Instance-level evidence is the unit-test verdict for each generated program in the sandbox, not the aggregate pass rate alone. Evidence is bounded by the selected five Python projects, authors' tests, tool strategies, prompts, runtime environment, and possible memorization or public-task contamination.
