- **Difficulty is conditional, not intrinsic.** `K` depends on the policy checkpoint, critic, prompt template, temperature, top-p, expansion width, ten-step simulation cap, and 50-iteration budget. Only 5.4K rows overlap between the 7B and 72B selected sets, and cross-scale training is weaker. A future Qwen revision or a different seed can change the curriculum.
- **The critic is a learned bottleneck.** Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct sees question, ground truth, and generated rationale/answer but not the image. No calibration, human agreement, adversarial test, or per-source error rate is reported. A false positive can stop search early and label an item easy; a false negative can push an easy item into the hard set. Fluent rationales may also influence a true/false judgment without establishing visual grounding.
- **Unsolved conflates multiple causes.** Failure after 50 iterations may indicate genuine target-policy difficulty, but also an incorrect or ambiguous answer, lossy multiple-choice conversion, image corruption, critic error, parser/format mismatch, or insufficient search. Because 5.6K unsolved items make up more than half of Hard-11K, independent label and image validation would materially affect reuse.
- **The released selector drops a required stratum.** In `mcts.py`, only rows with a non-null solution are appended to output. Unsolved rows are not written with a failure status, even though the paper's final rule retains them. The repository therefore cannot reproduce Hard-11K without an undocumented additional path or source artifact.
- **Decision lineage is missing.** The release does not provide the all-item `K` table, critic decisions, random seeds, tree expansions, failed traces, explicit rejects, selection reasons, source/split row IDs, or per-source survival counts. Hard-11K is an accepted subset, not an auditable accepted/rejected ledger.
- **Release completeness is asymmetric.** The official collection includes the 70K pool, Hard-11K, and both model checkpoints, but no 72B-selected 7.5K dataset was found. The paper states that both filtered training sets would be released. The GitHub project has no formal release and names mutable upstream model repositories without immutable revisions.
- **Training reproduction remains under-specified.** The paper identifies Easy-R1, GRPO, 32 rollouts, and 8x80GB A100 GPUs, but not the exact reward, answer extractor, normalization, complete hyperparameters, seeds, wall-clock filtering cost, total sampled tokens, or checkpoint selection. The authors themselves identify filtering and GRPO rollout overhead as a limitation. (Appendix D)
- **Evaluation uncertainty is limited.** Tables report point estimates. The paper contains an internal 64.18 versus 63.89 inconsistency for the 7B selected setting, and the checked results do not expose repeated-run error bars despite the checklist's positive statistical-significance response. Small margins across benchmarks should therefore not be over-interpreted.
- **Contamination and rights are unresolved.** No cross-source deduplication or train/evaluation overlap ledger is reported. The Hub pages declare MIT, but they do not map the licenses and redistribution rights of eight upstream datasets, images, annotations, and transformed questions; the GitHub repository has no top-level license. A blanket metadata label is not sufficient evidence for training reuse.

These limitations do not erase the reported matched-budget result. They narrow its meaning: the paper supports a promising selector under the authors' policy, critic, data mixture, and evaluation stack, while exact data provenance, verifier reliability, and end-to-end reproducibility remain partial.
