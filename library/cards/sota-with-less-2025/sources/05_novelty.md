The prior-work baseline is difficulty or quality filtering by one-shot accuracy, flat self-consistency, loss/confidence, static metadata, or random sampling. In the paper's closest multimodal comparison, MM-Eureka filters by zero-shot accuracy; ThinkLite-VL argues that a tree-search budget exposes a finer continuum among immediately solved, late-solved, and still-unsolved prompts. The paper also directly compares random, full-pool, offline self-consistency, online self-consistency, and threshold variants. (Paper §2 and §4)

The concrete change is the selection interface. A prompt is no longer represented only by a source label or empirical pass rate; it is associated with the target policy, search scaffold, critic, first accepted iteration `K`, unsolved status, and selection threshold. The selection pass is repeated for 7B and 72B rather than treating difficulty as intrinsic. The 5.4K overlap and cross-training degradation make that policy-relative interpretation empirically consequential. (Paper §3.2, Table 5)

For reasoning-data research, the direction signal is to spend bounded construction-time compute on measuring where a policy struggles before allocating RL rollouts. The useful object is a versioned difficulty decision ledger, not merely the final accepted Parquet. This can support curricula, comparative selector studies, and audits of whether hard examples are genuinely informative or merely mislabeled. The public release only partially realizes this object because `K`, critic outputs, rejected rows, and failed searches are not packaged as a complete manifest.

Several components are not new individually: MCTS, GRPO, Qwen2.5-VL, answer-conditioned judging, open-source visual QA datasets, and hard-example training all predate this work. The paper does not introduce a process reward model, a new formal verifier, or a new visual-reasoning benchmark. Its contribution is the integration of MCTS solve effort with model-specific prompt filtering and an ablation program showing that the chosen late-solved-plus-unsolved band can outperform larger or flatter selections under the reported setup.

The quality claim must remain conditional. Search depth is a construction signal only if the critic, ground truth, prompt conversion, and sampling budget are reliable. It can also encode critic errors, visual blindness, label noise, or stochastic search variance. Before reusing the recipe, check critic calibration, source-specific survival rates, stability across seeds and policy revisions, the effect of preserving failed traces, matched-compute comparisons, and whether gains survive an independently verified and decontaminated pool.
