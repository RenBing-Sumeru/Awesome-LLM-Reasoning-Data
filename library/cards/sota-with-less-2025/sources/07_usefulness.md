For `data_construction_open_release_recipes`, ThinkLite-VL is most useful as a worked example of policy-aware prompt curation before RLVR. It shows how a team can turn search behavior into a selection feature, compare equal-size and larger alternatives, and test whether a difficulty band transfers across policy scales. The most reusable design principle is not the fixed threshold of five; it is to version the policy, critic, search budget, and selection decision together.

An implementation study can start from the public 70K pool and selector, but should repair the record contract before training. Preserve upstream dataset/split/row ID, image hash and license, normalized prompt and answer, policy and critic revisions, sampling seed, every expansion and terminal judgment, `K` or unsolved status, selected/rejected decision, and failure reason. Store unsuccessful searches rather than discarding them. Then measure critic agreement and source-specific false positives/negatives on an independently reviewed sample.

The paper supports several concrete controlled experiments: replace the text-only critic with rule-based answer verification where available; add image-aware judging; compare MCTS effort with flat pass rate under matched tokens; vary the threshold while matching selected count; measure stability across seeds and checkpoint revisions; separate invalid/noisy unsolved rows from valid hard rows; and test cross-policy transfer after matching source mixture. Table 5 and Appendix Table 9 provide baselines for these questions.

The public datasets also support release-audit work. The 70K viewer exposes only task fields and one train split, while the Hard-11K page is minimally documented and lacks a working viewer. Researchers can use this gap to define a decision-ledger schema, trace upstream rights, audit duplicate images/questions, and distinguish a released prompt subset from unreleased construction trajectories and downstream online rollouts.

Reuse class: **reading/audit reference and controlled research prototype; training reuse is blocked pending provenance and license verification, and exact paper reproduction is blocked pending selector fixes plus the missing decision and training records**. The released checkpoint can be evaluated under an independently pinned harness, but benchmark results should not be treated as certification of source cleanliness, critic accuracy, or rationale faithfulness.
