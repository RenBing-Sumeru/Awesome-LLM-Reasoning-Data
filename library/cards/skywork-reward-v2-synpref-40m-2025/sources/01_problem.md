Skywork-Reward-V2: Scaling Preference Data Curation via Human-AI Synergy treats preference/reward feedback as a data-construction problem. Its data object is SynPref-40M and a curated 26M-pair training mixture. The concrete boundary is Open RMs are limited by narrow, synthetic, and weakly controlled preference data. This makes provenance, annotator or teacher identity, and split policy part of the result rather than implementation detail.
