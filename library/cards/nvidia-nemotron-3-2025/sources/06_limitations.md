The family paper does not publish an immutable end-to-end manifest linking every source, sample, synthetic trace, environment revision, rollout, reward, checkpoint, and benchmark result. Per-environment terminal predicates, reward functions, calibration, thresholds, false-accept or false-reject behavior, rollout groups, and reward-hacking audits are incomplete or unknown.

The release is not uniform. Some Nano data is public, some is gated or approval-required, and documentation lists private third-party and NVIDIA-internal data. The family-level redistribution-rights mapping, source licenses, and allocation to Nano, Super, and Ultra are unknown. An open-data reference recipe cannot reproduce runs that used additional proprietary data.

The Nano model card presents 141 datasets and 10.648T tokens in one data table but separately states approximately 25T training tokens. Without an immutable mixture ledger and definitions of these figures, the relationship between the two counts cannot be resolved from the official materials. The table's Training 100%, testing 0%, and validation 0% statement does not substitute for task-level public train/evaluation splits or a family-wide overlap audit.
