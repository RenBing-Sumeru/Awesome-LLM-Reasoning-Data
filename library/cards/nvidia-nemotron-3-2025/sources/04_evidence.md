The official family paper identifies Nano, Super, and Ultra and reports multi-environment RL, simultaneous training across task families, asynchronous train/inference decoupling, GRPO, masked importance sampling, up to 1M-token context, and inference-time reasoning-budget control. It also states an intention to release weights, software, recipes, and data for which NVIDIA holds redistribution rights.

The official Nano model card gives a more detailed but model-specific disclosure. Its data table lists 141 datasets and 10,648,823,153,919 tokens, while separate text states that Nano was trained for approximately 25T tokens. It identifies public, crawled, synthetic, private third-party, and NVIDIA-internal data classes, and says that final pre- and post-training data are released with a mix of ungated samples and gated or approval-required material.

The official developer repository documents that its open recipes use only the open-source subset of the data and can differ from technical-report results that used additional proprietary data. These sources support a partial-release, mixed-data assessment. They do not establish a complete family-wide mapping of sources, rewards, environments, rollouts, and benchmarks.
