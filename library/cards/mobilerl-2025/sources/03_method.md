The current arXiv v2 pipeline can be reconstructed conceptually as follows.

1. **Reasoning-free SFT.** AndroidControl training records are converted to MobileRL's screenshot/XML and bounding-box action format and combined with sanitized human-annotation/model-exploration records. The stage contains 97.9k action steps: 21.3k AndroidControl-Low, 14.3k AndroidControl-High, and 62.2k human-origin steps.
2. **Reasoning SFT.** An undisclosed off-the-shelf Instruct model samples reasoning-action candidates. A candidate is retained only when its action matches the expert action; an initial reasoning policy is trained, then generates new candidates whose best correct explanation is added iteratively. The final 23.6k-step corpus—7.2k, 4.1k, and 12.2k by the same source groups—is trained for two epochs.
3. **RL task construction and split controls.** AndroidWorld task templates vary substitutable content and initial states; instances with the same specific content and initial state as any of 116 test tasks are removed, leaving 2,000 training tasks. AndroidLab applications generate candidate tasks that humans check for feasibility and non-overlap with 138 test tasks, yielding 1,103 training tasks. This is an exact/manual split defense, not a semantic or pretraining-contamination audit.
4. **Online interaction.** Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct or GLM-4.1V-9B-Base, after the two SFT stages, observes a screenshot plus compressed XML and produces structured GUI actions. XML preprocessing removes off-screen/redundant nodes, simplifies attributes, merges text/content descriptions, and retains bounds. Rollout settings are temperature 1.0, 16 samples per task group, 4,096 maximum new tokens, and 50 turns.
5. **Terminal verification.** AndroidWorld returns a rule-based binary success reward. For AndroidLab training tasks, several medium-capability agents generate traces; three strong proprietary VLM judgments are majority-voted for training items, with reasoning retained. A GLM-4.1V-9B-Thinking reward model is fine-tuned on several thousand labels and selected at 86% accuracy on a verified 1,000-trace set.
6. **Reward transformation and selection.** SPA with `alpha=1.0` down-weights longer successful paths relative to the shortest success and leaves failures at zero. Group-relative advantages drive GRPO. AdaPR uses buffer size 256, `gamma=1.0`, and `kappa=0.25` to retain top-advantage trajectories. Low-advantage negatives are randomly pruned to keep at most a 1:2 positive-to-negative ratio. FCF cools down and then removes persistently all-zero tasks.
7. **Optimization and infrastructure.** The Verl-based setup uses FSDP, learning rate `1e-6`, train/validation batch size 256, PPO micro-batch size 4 per GPU, one PPO epoch, gradient clip 1.0, clip ratio 0.2, and KL coefficient 0.001. Four rollout machines each provide 1 TB RAM; each AVD needs over 5 GB memory and 30 GB storage. The system stably sustains up to 256 parallel rollouts, with a step taking over 10 minutes and 100 training steps over 25 hours.

The official repository currently supplies an AgentRL-based evaluation stack, Docker configurations/images, AndroidLab evaluation code, and pinned patched AndroidWorld dependencies. Reproducing training still requires the unreleased corpora/tasks, teacher manifests, reward model, rollout/replay records, training implementation, AVD/APK versions, seeds, optimizer state, and paper-era environment/result manifests.
