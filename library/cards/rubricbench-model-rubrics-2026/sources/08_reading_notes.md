1. **Position:**  Diagnose criterion generation and judging with human gold rubrics.
2. **Method:**  Five-domain candidates, hard filtering, instruction-only expert annotation, and A/B evaluation.
3. **Data:**  1,147 cases with instruction, two responses, label, domain, source, and rubrics.
4. **Evidence:**  Model-generated criteria lag experts, and human rubrics improve discrimination.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for rubric reward models; control source bias and leakage.
