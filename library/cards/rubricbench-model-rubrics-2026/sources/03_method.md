1. **Collect candidates:** Instructions and response pairs are collected from five categories of preference and capability data and normalised.

2. **Filter hard cases:** Samples are filtered by input complexity, response closeness, and misleading surface bias to retain 1,147 hard pairs.

3. **Expert annotation:** Annotators derive atomic criteria from the instruction alone, avoiding post-hoc rationalisation from the known winner.

4. **Standardised evaluation:** Systems generate or receive rubrics and choose A/B; scripts compute total and domain accuracy, requiring fixed prompts and judges.
