The release boundary prevents full replay or provenance audit: the two SFT JSONL files do not include raw live trajectories, rejected rollouts, per-iteration/query mappings, timestamps, captured browser state, model checkpoints, or a row-level real-versus-synthetic manifest. Llama-3.3-70B judges its own training successes without reported calibration or human agreement, so false acceptance and false rejection can be amplified through later SFT. A language-model world model can hallucinate web state, and the paper's intrinsic scores fall below 0.50 beyond depth two; the authors argue that noise may diversify training, but do not establish item-level correctness. Live websites change, 48 Mind2Web and five WebVoyager sites are excluded for access reasons, and experiments are repeated only in roughly aligned time windows. The dataset and code declare MIT, but rights or consent for third-party web content embedded in message text, decontamination, duplicate controls, and evaluation-query overlap are unknown. GPT-4o evaluation and WMLA scoring also introduce an external judge distinct from the self-improvement loop.
