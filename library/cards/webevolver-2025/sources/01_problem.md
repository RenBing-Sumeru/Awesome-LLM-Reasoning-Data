WebEvolver was published at EMNLP 2025 and addresses stagnation in web-agent self-improvement when a policy repeatedly trains on its own successful live-web trajectories but explores too little and does not exploit knowledge already encoded in the LLM. Its paper-level data object is a sequence of queries, accessibility-tree observations, chain-of-thought/action decisions, completion judgments, and world-model-generated next observations. Its released object is narrower: Hugging Face exposes two JSONL SFT files for the agent policy and world model, represented as chat `messages`, rather than the raw real/synthetic trajectories, rejected attempts, or replayable browser states. This distinction makes the work relevant to `rollout_search_test_time_trace_data`: multiple real and imagined rollouts shape training and WMLA action selection, but only their SFT serialization is reusable from the official data release.
