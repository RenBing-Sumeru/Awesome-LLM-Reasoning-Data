ACL Anthology verifies the EMNLP 2025 paper, authors, pages 8959-8975, and DOI; arXiv links the Tencent repository. The official Hugging Face release declares MIT, shows one train split of about 13.5k rows with a single `messages` feature, and contains `webevolver_policy_sft.jsonl` (23.9 MB) plus `webevolver_world_model_sft.jsonl` (76.2 MB); the accepted metadata records 13,465 combined SFT rows. In the paper, WebEvolver reaches 42.49 on the 473-query text-only WebVoyager set versus 32.98 for the zero-shot Llama-3.3-70B agent, while WMLA at depth two reaches 51.37; experiments are also reported on a filtered 53-query Mind2Web-Live set, GAIA-web, and SimpleQA. The world-model evaluation reports declining structural, similarity, and overall scores beyond depth two. These results support the recipe under the tested models, filtered websites, judges, and time window; they do not show that every released SFT row, synthetic state, or self-judgment is correct, and benchmark performance is not evidence of data quality.
