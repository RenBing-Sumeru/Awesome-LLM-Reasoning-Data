Training starts from 1,516 queries drawn from the Mind2Web training set and self-instructed queries for websites represented in WebVoyager and Mind2Web. Cognitive Kernel operates a Playwright browser over live websites using accessibility-tree observations and `click`, `type`, `scroll`, `goback`, `restart`, and `stop` actions; interaction terminates at completion or a step limit. Llama-3.3-70B samples trajectories and also supplies binary completion judgments for rejection sampling. Successful examples accumulate across three reported self-improvement iterations and are optimized with ordinary SFT, not end-to-end RL. Real trajectories are also converted into world-model examples, with the original Llama-3.3-70B supplying transition rationales. Synthetic generation alternates the iteration-2 world model and iteration-1 policy for at most seven steps and filters the result again. At inference, WMLA samples up to three candidate actions: the first at temperature 0 and alternatives at 0.7 with a diversity instruction, then simulates depths one to three and selects the highest GPT-4o score. The official repository provides scripts for sampling and conversion, but per-query rollout counts, accepted counts, training-time temperature, full fine-tuning settings, and checkpoints remain unknown.
