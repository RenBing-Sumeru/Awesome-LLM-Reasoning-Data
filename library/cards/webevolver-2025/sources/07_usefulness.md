For `rollout_search_test_time_trace_data`, WebEvolver is useful as a schema and audit case for a two-branch trajectory system. A reproducible record would retain query and website provenance, timestamped accessibility trees, policy thoughts/actions, termination reason, Llama completion judgment and rationale, iteration, acceptance status, world-model transition rationale and prediction, real-versus-synthetic flag, WMLA candidate action, simulated depth, GPT-4o score, and model/checkpoint identifiers. Such records could support SFT mixture studies, selector and judge audits, world-model drift analysis, rejected-rollout research, and replay under frozen browser snapshots. The current official release directly supports policy and world-model SFT, while the paper supports agent training and test-time look-ahead. It does not make raw trajectories, rejected samples, checkpoints, or replay environments reusable, and downstream users should not convert the reported benchmark gains into quality labels for the released rows.
