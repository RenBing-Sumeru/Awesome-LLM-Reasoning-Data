WebEvolver does not introduce iterative SFT, rejection sampling, web-agent world models, or look-ahead scoring in isolation. Relative to OpenWebVoyager-style self-improvement, its specific change is to co-evolve a transition-predicting LLM from the same interaction loop and use that model both to manufacture additional policy-training trajectories and to simulate candidate actions at inference. Relative to WebDreamer and WMA, the paper emphasizes that its world model participates in training-data construction and produces up-to-seven-step synthetic rollouts, rather than serving only as a static one- or short-horizon prompting component. For this Track, the useful novelty is the shared lineage across real rollout, judged acceptance, world-model SFT, synthetic rollout, second acceptance, and policy SFT. That lineage is described in the paper and partially implemented in code, but the released SFT files do not carry a manifest that makes every stage auditable per row.
