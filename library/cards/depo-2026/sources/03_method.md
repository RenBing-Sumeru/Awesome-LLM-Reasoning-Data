The method first scores an upstream RLVR prompt pool for diversity, influence, and difficulty, then retains a smaller subset; the reported final subset is 20% of the original pool. Online RLVR samples answer trajectories from the current policy and evaluates them with verifiable rewards. An explorability signal estimates whether a prompt yields useful outcome variation, filters low-explorability samples from immediate updates, and identifies insufficiently explored examples for replay. To reproduce the recipe, a release would need upstream prompt IDs and splits, exact score definitions, selection thresholds, verifier and answer-normalization rules, rollout budgets, retain/drop decisions, and replay-buffer history. These artifacts are not identifiable in the inspected official repository.
