DEPO is useful for designing auditable RLVR prompt curation and rollout scheduling. A downstream implementation should log every prompt's provenance, offline scores, selection decision, rollout count, reward distribution, explorability estimate, filter reason, and replay events. It provides hypotheses for reducing training compute, but it is not directly reusable training data until a concrete upstream pool, verifier contract, rights review, overlap controls, and sample-level histories are supplied.
