The central data object is a triple: preceding context, an implicit rationale inserted at a sentence or solution-step boundary, and the following context. Llama-3-8B-Instruct proposes the rationale. A 0.9-decayed future-token score compares the continuation with and without that rationale; the intended filter keeps rationales that improve continuation prediction by enough to pass a source-specific threshold.

The paper reports about 79K retained rationales: about 65K from six Pile subdomains and about 14K from GSM8K and ECQA. These targets supervise an 8B RATIONALYST model to predict a rationale from the current context. At test time, RATIONALYST supplies an implicit rationale, the agent samples three candidate next steps, and a rationale-conditioned likelihood score selects one. Thus the learned object is used both as an SFT target and as a search-side latent guide.

The feedback contract is deliberately weak. It measures whether a rationale explains or anticipates the observed continuation, not whether every intermediate assertion is valid. The released artifacts sharpen that distinction: the linked dataset exposes only `preceeding`, `rationale`, and `following`, without the scores, thresholds, source identifiers, or retention decisions needed to reconstruct the filter.
