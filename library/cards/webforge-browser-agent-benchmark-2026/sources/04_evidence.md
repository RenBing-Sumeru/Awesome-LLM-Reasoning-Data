The arXiv abstract reports 934 tasks across 7 domains and 3 difficulty levels, with multi-model experiments showing that difficulty and domain stratification reveal capability profiles hidden by a single aggregate score.

Instance-level evidence comes from the released task object and the scorer: validation replay and final-state or submitted-answer comparison against ground truth. Aggregate scores are meaningful only for the reported split, model setup, and harness. Public artifacts support inspection, but they do not remove contamination, dependency, or license checks.
