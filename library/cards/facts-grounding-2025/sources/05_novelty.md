Prior factuality benchmarks often evaluate short claims, retrieved passages, or public answer sets. FACTS Grounding changes the setting to long-form answers grounded in long private/public documents with a leaderboard submission process. The quality signal is the hidden private split and explicit eligibility-plus-grounding judge. What is not new is using LLM judges for factuality. Before reuse, inspect judge prompts, aggregation, private split policy, unsupported partial-credit behavior, and whether answers are penalized for harmless omissions or style.
