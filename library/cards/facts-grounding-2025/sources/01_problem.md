FACTS Grounding asks whether long-form model responses are fully grounded in a provided long document and user request. The primary source is arXiv:2501.03200, with the official Kaggle FACTS Grounding benchmark/leaderboard as the public evaluation surface. It belongs here as a grounding benchmark with public/open and private/blind splits, not as a claim-extraction dataset or a general QA corpus. A task contains a user request, a full source document of up to 32K tokens, a model long-form answer, and judge outputs for eligibility and grounding. Its atlas value is the hidden-split, judge-ensemble feedback contract for document-grounded factuality.
