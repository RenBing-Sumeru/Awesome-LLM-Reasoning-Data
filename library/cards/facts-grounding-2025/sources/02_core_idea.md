The contribution is an online leaderboard for long-document response grounding with a two-stage judging contract. The mechanism first filters answers that fail the user request, then judges whether the remaining answer is fully supported by the source document; multiple judge models are aggregated to reduce judge-specific bias. The reported dataset has 860 public/open examples and 859 private/blind examples. Closest comparisons are FActScore, FEVER-style verification, RAG factuality metrics, and long-context QA benchmarks. The direction label is hidden-split grounding evaluation for long-form answers.
