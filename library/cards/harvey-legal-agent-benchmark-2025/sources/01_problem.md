Primary source: https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/hlab. Venue/status: Vals AI / Harvey benchmark page, 2025. The problem is to evaluate Legal work product, file outputs, citations, or supporting work product demanded by the task. under a benchmark surface whose feedback is criterion-level LLM-judge grading with strict task pass policy. The decision boundary is evaluation and audit: it is not a training recipe, not a general proof of model reliability, and not a reusable reward unless the official source separately releases that contract.

The data object is composed of legal task, workspace files, tool trace or output files, criterion-level grades, task pass indicator. The benchmark matters because it measures a concrete surface that ordinary static QA or single-function coding tests do not fully cover. For screening, read the card with the source version, scorer, split policy, and released artifacts attached to any claimed score.
