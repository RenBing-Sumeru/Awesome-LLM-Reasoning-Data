One-sentence contribution: Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark tests legal work products over documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and file-system tools, using task criteria and LLM-judge grading. The core mechanism is to turn a realistic task substrate into an observable input-output-scoring contract rather than only reporting model impressions.

Inputs come from Legal work task with task-specific documents and criteria.; outputs are Legal work product, file outputs, citations, or supporting work product demanded by the task.; feedback is Two LLM judges grade criteria; task passes only when all criteria pass under the official policy. It belongs under benchmark/evaluation surfaces because the central artifact is the measurement contract. Closest comparisons are other benchmark surfaces in the same domain, but this card should be read at the level of the released task object and scorer rather than as a broad model capability claim.
