- **Teacher error amplification:** the same model invents tasks and answers them, so factual errors and stylistic biases can reinforce themselves; only 54% of audited items have every field judged valid. Add independent answer checking and stratified human review before training.
- **Weak diversity guarantee:** ROUGE-L below 0.7 removes close wording but not semantic duplicates, and the generator underproduces long-tail or unfamiliar tasks. Audit semantic clusters, task coverage, languages, and difficulty rather than trusting instruction count.
- **Artifact and transfer boundary:** record-level source and redistribution terms are not fully clear, and results come from GPT-3-era models and benchmarks. Pin the repository revision, verify licenses, and rerun matched baselines on the intended modern model.
