1. **Matched model improvement:** vanilla GPT-3 scores 6.8 ROUGE-L on SUPER-NATURALINSTRUCTIONS, while the same model family fine-tuned on Self-Instruct data scores 39.9—an absolute gain of about 33 points and performance comparable to InstructGPT-001 in the paper's setup. The controlled difference is synthetic instruction SFT, although training/API details of the private InstructGPT baseline are not matched.
2. **Dataset-quality audit:** human annotators judge only 54% of sampled generated items valid in every field. This does not negate the aggregate model gain, but it sets the data boundary: heuristic similarity and format filters leave substantial instruction, input, or output errors, so downstream reuse needs stronger record-level validation.
