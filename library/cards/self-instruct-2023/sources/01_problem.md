Instruction-tuned models depend on human-written tasks and demonstrations, which are costly and often narrow. Self-Instruct asks whether a pretrained language model can expand a small human seed set into enough varied instruction-input-output records to improve its own instruction following without collecting a large new annotation campaign.

The paper answers with an iterative synthetic-data pipeline: prompt GPT-3 with accepted tasks to invent new instructions, remove malformed or lexically similar candidates, generate corresponding inputs and outputs, filter invalid instances, and fine-tune GPT-3 on the retained records. Starting from 175 human seed tasks, it produces 52,445 instructions and 82,439 instances. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10560; venue: ACL 2023.
