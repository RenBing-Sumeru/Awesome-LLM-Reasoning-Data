The paper reports experiments across nine environments and 1,012 tasks, comparing closed and open LLM agents under the same board. A key empirical claim is that progress-oriented metrics reveal behavior hidden by binary task success, including cases where an agent reaches useful intermediate states but misses the final condition. The authors also report that open-source models can exceed GPT-3.5 in parts of the evaluation while still trailing GPT-4 overall.

The row-level evidence is a trajectory with environment observations, actions, terminal outcome, and metric computation. This supports diagnostic analysis better than a single pass/fail label, but it remains bounded by the benchmark's task decomposition and metric implementation. Reported scores should not be reused without the same tasks, prompts, model snapshots, environment code, and turn/budget limits.
