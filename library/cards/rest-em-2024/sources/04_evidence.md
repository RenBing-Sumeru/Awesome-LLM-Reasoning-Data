1. **Model-scale result:** relative to each base model under the paper's greedy pass@1 evaluation, ReST-EM improves MATH by 5.94 points for PaLM 2-S and 6.34 for PaLM 2-L; on APPS it improves PaLM 2-S* by 5.6 and PaLM 2-L by 6.4. The larger models start from stronger baselines yet gain at least as much, supporting scalability of verifier-filtered self-training on these two tasks.
2. **Iteration ablation and stop boundary:** on MATH with PaLM 2-L, one SFT round using three times as many base-policy samples reaches 40.3% pass@1, while two and three iterative rounds reach 41.0% and 41.9%. Iteration therefore adds value beyond collecting more stale-policy data. On APPS, however, the second round regresses on both APPS and HumanEval, showing that the loop must stop by held-out performance rather than training acceptance rate.
