1. **Choose verifiable prompts.** Input 7,500 MATH training problems or 2,342 APPS Introductory training problems. Pair MATH with final-answer matching and APPS with executable tests; reject tasks lacking a reliable binary check.
2. **Generate candidates (E-step).** From the current PaLM 2 policy, sample 32 solutions per MATH prompt or 64 programs per APPS prompt using temperature 0.7 and top-k 40. Output candidate prompt-solution pairs for verification.
3. **Filter and balance.** Keep only MATH solutions with the correct final answer or APPS programs passing every test. Retain at most ten correct samples per problem so easy prompts do not dominate; the accepted records form the iteration's SFT set.
4. **Improve the policy (M-step).** Fine-tune the original/current policy by next-token prediction on the accepted solution targets, with the prompt and few-shot context as input. Output an updated policy that becomes the generator for the next iteration.
5. **Repeat with a validation stop.** Regenerate, verify, and fine-tune for a few rounds while monitoring held-out pass@1 and transfer tasks. Stop when validation improvement saturates or regresses; APPS already regresses in round two despite increasing training performance.
