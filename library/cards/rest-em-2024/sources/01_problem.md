Fine-tuning on human solutions is limited by how many correct, diverse traces people can write. On mathematics and code, however, a model can sample many candidate solutions and receive cheap binary feedback from a final-answer checker or unit tests. ReST-EM asks whether those verified self-generated outputs can improve large policies beyond human-only SFT, and how many iterations remain useful.

The paper implements an expectation-maximization-like loop: sample 32 MATH or 64 APPS solutions per problem, keep verified outputs, fine-tune the current PaLM 2 policy on at most ten accepted solutions per prompt, and repeat. The direct data object is a problem—solution—binary-result record that becomes an SFT target only after verification. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06585; published in TMLR, April 2024.
