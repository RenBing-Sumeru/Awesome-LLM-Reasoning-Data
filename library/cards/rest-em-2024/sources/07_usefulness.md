Use ReST-EM when the target task has many unlabeled prompts and a precise binary checker, such as final-answer mathematics or executable code. Sample multiple outputs, cap accepted examples per prompt, train one round, regenerate from the new policy, and stop by held-out performance; preserve rejected outputs and verifier logs for audit. The output is an iteration-versioned SFT dataset and policy. Do not use the recipe unchanged for open-ended tasks whose judge is subjective or easy to exploit.
