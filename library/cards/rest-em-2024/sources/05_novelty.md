One-shot rejection-sampling fine-tuning generates and filters data from a fixed policy once. ReST-EM changes the data producer after every M-step, then samples a fresh verified dataset from that updated policy. Its novelty is the scalable iterative data loop and the empirical analysis of model size, rounds, and overfitting, not binary verification or SFT by themselves. The method also makes the stop rule a data-governance decision because later accepted samples can still reduce test performance.
