The release evidence is concrete but asymmetric. The official NeurIPS 2025 proceedings record identifies BMMR as a Datasets and Benchmarks Track paper. The live Hugging Face Dataset Viewer exposes exactly 88,991 rows in one `default/train` split and three columns: string `id`, a list of `{from, value}` conversation messages, and a list of image-path strings. The raw repository tree separately contains `trainset/bmmr-train-80k.jsonl` plus an image archive, and `testset/BMMR.tsv` plus `BMMR_mini.tsv`. The GitHub README explicitly points to both evaluation and training sets, and its code expects fields such as `question`, `image`, `answer`, `task_type`, `cot`, `language`, and `category_id` in the evaluation TSV.

The paper reports detailed composition statistics: 109,449 total questions; a Train:Eval split of 88,991:20,458; 29 image types; and five Eval difficulty counts of 5,783, 3,824, 3,321, 3,462, and 4,068. It reports 58,740/10,685 multiple-choice and 30,270/9,773 open-ended or fill-in-the-blank questions in Train/Eval. These figures establish the intended object and match the Train viewer count, but the public Train schema does not expose question type, language, taxonomy, difficulty, original source, or quality-control state for independent stratified verification.

The downstream model evidence supports BMMR-Train as a usable SFT intervention under the reported setup. The authors fine-tune five Qwen2.5-VL and InternVL2.5 variants and report gains across disciplines; for example, they report a 72.28% relative improvement for Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct on ICTs and 43.34% for InternVL2.5-78B on Health. The main benchmark table also shows low absolute scores for many current models and substantial discipline variation. These are trained-model and benchmark findings, not record-level validation of trace faithfulness, source cleanliness, or license compatibility.

The verifier evidence is agreement, not ground-truth calibration. BMMR-Verifier is compared with GPT-4o and human annotations: the paper reports 91.67%/89.21% response-/step-level consistency with GPT-4o, 95.00%/93.71% with humans, and averages of 93.34%/91.46%. The human comparison uses 1,000 randomly sampled trajectories from a 50k pool generated by Gemini-2.5-Flash, InternVL3, Qwen2.5, and InternVL2.5. Because the released artifacts omit the sampled cases, annotator labels, disagreement matrix, verifier scores, decision threshold, and checkpoint, these figures cannot currently be reproduced or used to estimate false-positive rates.

Artifact inspection also narrows what is reproducible. The public evaluation script implements final-answer extraction and equivalence checking, not process scoring. No verifier checkpoint, training set, rollout corpus, or step-label file appears in the checked official repository or dataset. The repository has no formal release, while the dataset has mutable Hub commits. Thus, venue, Train, Eval, and answer scoring are verified; BMMR-Verifier and the full decision lineage remain only partially evidenced.
