Use LiveBench when an LLM comparison needs a contamination-aware, reproducible evaluation surface. Pin a release, preserve prompts and scorers, record model endpoint and decoding, and report task/category results before the aggregate score; for longitudinal evaluation, rerun anchor models on both old and new releases. The output is a versioned regression record, not a timeless capability number. It is unsuitable for claims such as safety or factuality when the selected tasks and checkers do not measure them.
