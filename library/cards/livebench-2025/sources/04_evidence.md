1. **Difficulty and coverage:** on the fixed 1,000-question, 18-task, six-category paper release, o1-preview obtains an overall score of 64.7 rather than saturating the benchmark. This shows that the objectively scored mixture remains challenging for a leading model under the paper's aggregation, but the number is meaningful only for that release and model version.
2. **Refresh stability:** across two reported updates, model-ranking correlations with the preceding release remain above 0.997 while approximately one sixth of questions are replaced per update. This supports refreshability without wholesale ranking disruption for the evaluated model set; it does not prove that every future task mix preserves the same construct.
