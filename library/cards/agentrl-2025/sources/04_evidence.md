The evidence bundle verifies the official arXiv paper and the THUDM AgentRL and AgentBench repositories and licenses. AgentRL releases an MIT training/environment-deployment framework. The linked first-party AgentBench repository is Apache-2.0 and states that its Function Calling version is integrated with AgentRL and supports the five named task families in containerized deployment. The paper exposes multi-turn rollout/training mechanics and a task-environment contract, while its official README does not link a Hugging Face release, paper-run trajectory download, complete mixture manifest or consolidated reward/termination-event ledger. The public repositories therefore verify reusable framework and environment code, not a complete reproducible paper run.
