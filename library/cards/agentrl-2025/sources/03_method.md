The reported data mixture directly adopts ALFWorld and WebShop training sets, uses o3 and Claude Sonnet 4 to sample and filter Self-Instruct instances for OS, Knowledge Graph and DB, and adds BIRD training samples to DB. Smaller task datasets are replicated to roughly the largest task size and then sequentially interleaved; exact retained identifiers, replication counts and final ratios are unknown. The rollout engine produces current-policy trajectories asynchronously, and cross-policy sampling can select action steps from current and stale-policy engines. Reported GRPO settings include eight trajectories per rollout, temperature 0.8, task advantage normalization, dynamic batches, SGLang inference and FSDP training. The controller limits queue size and moves trajectories to training each step to limit staleness. This is a method and framework disclosure, not a released static corpus, a paper-run rollout download or a pinned runtime stack.
