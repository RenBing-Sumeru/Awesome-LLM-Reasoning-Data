1. **Scale against a public recipe:** with Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and the paper's matched evaluation, 500K randomly selected NaturalThoughts records score 48.6/83.1/62.3/35.2 on GPQA-Diamond/MATH500/MMLU-Pro/SuperGPQA. OpenThoughts3-1.2M scores 46.9/91.2/59.1/33.5, so NaturalThoughts wins three of four tasks with fewer records but remains weaker on MATH500.
2. **Difficulty-based mixed distillation:** on GPQA-Diamond, pure System-2 training reaches 37.6% in Think mode with 8,740 mean output tokens. Difficulty-based System-1/System-2 mixing reaches 38.9% with 7,562 tokens in Think mode and can also produce 799-token No-Think responses at 34.5%. The comparison changes target form by question difficulty, supporting controllable efficiency but only on this task and student setup.
