1. **Start from broad questions.** Input 2.8 million NaturalReasoning prompts across mathematics and general STEM; retain source and task information needed for sampling. Output the common question pool used by every selection policy.
2. **Generate teacher targets.** Ask DeepSeek-R1 for a complete reasoning response and final answer, discarding responses truncated by the maximum length. Output serialized question-trace-answer demonstrations; teacher errors can remain because no universal step verifier is applied.
3. **Compute selection signals.** Characterize records by random sampling, reasoning-strategy labels, response length, or disagreement among models used as a difficulty proxy. Output ranked or stratified candidates; these signals describe selection, not ground-truth trace quality.
4. **Build matched subsets.** Select 1K, 10K, 100K, and 500K records under the compared policies. For mixed distillation, replace selected long System-2 traces with final-answer-only System-1 targets according to random ratios or question difficulty.
5. **Train and evaluate students.** Fine-tune Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with matched recipes, then measure pass@1 on MATH500, GPQA-Diamond, MMLU-Pro, and SuperGPQA; mixed models are also evaluated under No-Think, Adaptive-Think, and Think modes. Stop conclusions at the tested teacher, students, domains, and budgets.
