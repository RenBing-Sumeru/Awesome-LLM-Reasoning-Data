Reasoning distillation often copies long traces from a strong teacher without knowing which questions or traces transfer useful skills to a smaller student. Small curated sets such as LIMO emphasize selectivity, while large releases emphasize scale; neither alone explains how size, difficulty, and diversity interact on general STEM reasoning.

NaturalThoughts answers with a controlled selection-and-scaling study over a 2.8-million-question NaturalReasoning pool. It uses DeepSeek-R1 traces, builds subsets from 1K to 500K examples under several selection policies, fine-tunes Llama and Qwen students, and compares their reasoning accuracy and response length. The direct object is a question—teacher-trace—answer record selected by a named policy. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01921; no formal venue beyond arXiv was confirmed.
