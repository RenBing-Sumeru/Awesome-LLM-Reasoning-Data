Small curated datasets such as LIMO and s1K test whether a few traces suffice, while OpenThoughts emphasizes a scalable recipe. NaturalThoughts changes the experimental object to matched trace subsets selected by scale, difficulty, reasoning-strategy diversity, or verbosity across general STEM. It also makes System-1 versus System-2 target form a per-question data decision. The new signal is therefore comparative selection evidence, not a new teacher model or SFT algorithm.
