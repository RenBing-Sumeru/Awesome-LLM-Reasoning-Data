The report combines an adaptive reasoning data path with a heterogeneous reward system. For reasoning SFT, Hunyuan-Base first attempts a short response. A correct attempt is retained; after an incorrect attempt, Hunyuan-T1 continues the reasoning until a correct answer is obtained. Failed attempts and the correct response are concatenated to train an adaptive long-short teacher, whose outputs supply the first Hunyuan-TurboS SFT stage. This is teacher-generated supervision, but the report does not name a separate final-model distillation stage or disclose a distillation-only corpus size.

The model then participates in a deliberation flywheel. Hunyuan-TurboS, Hunyuan Large, Hunyuan Turbo, and Hunyuan T1 answer the same curated prompts. Hunyuan-TurboS-based judges score pairwise outputs for accuracy, helpfulness, harmlessness, coherence, conciseness, and instruction adherence; consensus and human experts identify weakness profiles, which guide new expert-annotated SFT batches. Judge versions, scoring weights, prompt sets, iteration counts, and added-data totals are unknown.

Reinforcement learning is supported by a General Reward System spanning 16 subtopics and more than 30 scoring services. It combines a reference-conditioned GRM, GRM-CoT, an Answer Consistency Model, a tool-using critic, a 36-language sandbox, domain rules, classifiers, and score fusion. Stage I GRPO focuses on reasoning; Stage II expands to general instruction following. These are concrete report-level contracts, but not released reward checkpoints, per-sample reward vectors, or replayable rollouts.
