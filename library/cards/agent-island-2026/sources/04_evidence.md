The static paper result is defined over **999 completed games involving 49 unique models** (paper §4 and §6; frozen 999-ID manifest). Under the paper's Bayesian Plackett-Luce analysis, `openai/gpt-5.5` has posterior mean skill 5.64, followed by `openai/gpt-5.2` at 3.10 and `openai/gpt-5.3-codex` at 2.86 (paper Table 1 and Figure 2). These are author-reported winner-model estimates under one matchup schedule and ranking model; they are not independent reproduction and do not establish the quality of the released trajectories.

The behavioral audit uses information beyond the winner-only ranking. Restricting analysis to final-round votes in games whose finalists come from different providers, the paper reports that jurors are **8.3 percentage points** more likely to vote for a same-provider finalist (paper §7, Table 2, Figure 3). This result is evidence that provider composition affects votes. It is also a stress signal for a Plackett-Luce specification that has no matchup-effect term; it does not by itself identify why the preference occurs.

Artifact evidence makes the paper set unusually concrete. Dataset version 1.0.0 declares 999 game IDs and manifest SHA-256 `73ee6cc97ada604830e933131b19ed81839049b19dfcc1a3c970a72dfce862ad`. The public index supplies a direct HTTPS URL for every frozen log, and Croissant metadata describes schema, preprocessing, use cases, and CC BY 4.0 licensing. A verified sample log exposes game/player/stats/history objects, private/public visibility, parsed choices, reasoning-extraction failures, token/cost metadata, and the final selected player. These release facts support auditability; they do not prove that excluded failures are negligible or that the traces are suitable for training.

The replication archive is pinned by GCS generation and SHA-256 `5b2c7c5262a46c65b51b659b9c3c0b4edb7eb8cf443cb6d2a4489d2d673e8b2d`. Its documented pipeline downloads the published logs and regenerates posterior samples, metadata, CSV/JSON analyses, paper macros, tables, figures, and appendix excerpts. The full pipeline was not rerun for this Card, and the ZIP explicitly does not generate games. Thus the evidence establishes an analysis-replication route, not end-to-end episode reproduction.

The live benchmark is a separate observation. On 2026-07-20, direct retrieval of `rankings/latest.json` returned **1,270 games, 62 ranking rows, and 59 rows passing the site's minimum of 10 games**; the source timestamp was **2026-07-02 15:26:25.976235+00:00**. The live site normalizes skill so the top median is 1, ranks by the 25th-percentile skill, and displays 50%/95% intervals, which differs from the paper's Table 1 ordering by posterior mean. Earlier live states must not be used as current evidence.
