Outcome-reward RL can improve mathematical answer accuracy without teaching a model to recognize whether its own reasoning is sound. The paper calls this gap superficial self-reflection: a model may produce or repeat a plausible answer while failing to identify an error in its own solution.

RISE studies whether one online RL loop can train both solution generation and self-verification. Its evidence concerns mathematical tasks with programmatically checkable final answers; it does not establish that natural-language self-critique is generally reliable.
