The paper compares standard SFT, prior error-learning methods, and LEMMA under matched base models and training-data scales. On LLaMA3-8B, LEMMA improves average accuracy across mathematical benchmarks by up to 13.3% and retains gains on out-of-distribution sets; error analysis shows reductions in representative interpretation, formula, and calculation failures. The evidence supports structured correction data but does not fully isolate the long-term effect of each correction strategy.
