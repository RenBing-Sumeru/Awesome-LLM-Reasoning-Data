Existing error-learning methods often raise sampling temperature to collect failures or rely on MCTS and external critics for online repair. LEMMA first uses the student’s error distribution to decide which mistakes to synthesize and then connects each first error to two valid recovery paths in an SFT-ready sequence. The novelty is error-type-grounded data construction and smooth reflection connections rather than reflection prompting itself.

The resulting training data is therefore closer to the target model's actual failure distribution.
