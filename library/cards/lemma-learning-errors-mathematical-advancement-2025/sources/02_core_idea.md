LEMMA organizes error data as a smooth correction trajectory containing the problem, faulty prefix, earliest error, reflection phrase, and correct continuation. The official dataset contains 88,904 records totaling about 87.3 MB. Errors come from the student model or are deliberately introduced by a teacher according to problem-specific error distributions; corrections use Fix & Continue or Fresh & Restart and support self-correction SFT rather than simple step classification.

Records also distinguish student-generated errors from teacher-injected errors, enabling separate realism audits.
