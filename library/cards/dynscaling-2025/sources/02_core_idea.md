DynScaling couples integrated parallel-sequential sampling with inter-query UCB allocation. For each sampling unit, half of the responses are generated independently; the other half condition on synthetic thought segments formed by randomly selecting and concatenating initial responses. Across the query batch, the variation ratio `1 - max_answer_count / sample_count` measures answer disagreement, and a UCB-style score adds an exploration bonus for queries that have received less budget. Higher-ranked queries receive another sampling unit until the total budget is exhausted, after which majority voting selects each answer. “Verifier-free” here means that no external correctness verifier guides inference: response disagreement is an allocation signal and majority vote is a selector, not evidence that a response is correct.
