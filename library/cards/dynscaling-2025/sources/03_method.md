Experiments use GPQA Diamond and a benchmark formed by combining AIME 2024 with AIME 2025. Gemini 1.5 Flash and Gemini 2.5 Pro sample at temperature 0.6, with maximum output lengths of 8,192 and 32,768 tokens respectively; thinking mode is enabled for Gemini 2.5 Pro. Algorithm 1 first gives every query one `B_unit=8` integrated-sampling unit. Algorithm 2 generates `B/2` initial responses, constructs each synthetic thought segment by concatenating `k=4` randomly selected initial responses, and generates `B/2` conditioned responses. The allocator computes `a_i = u_i + c sqrt(log(B_used)/B_i)` with default `c=1/4`, selects a top-ranked subset of queries, and repeats in eight-sample increments until `B_total` is spent. Answers are extracted with pattern matching; majority voting returns the final answer and strict ground-truth equivalence is used only for benchmark evaluation. The paper reports no training, teacher, response-quality filter, code, or trace release, and it does not disclose the selected-subset size or tie handling.
