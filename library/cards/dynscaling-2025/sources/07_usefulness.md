For `rollout_search_test_time_trace_data`, DynScaling supplies a useful schema for batch-level inference traces: query and benchmark provenance; model and decoding settings; every initial response; the sampled indices and order used in each synthetic thought segment; every conditioned response; parsed answer; per-round variation ratio and UCB score; selected-query subset; cumulative sample and token budgets; final vote; and ground-truth evaluation kept separate from inference signals. Such records could support selector comparison, compute-allocation audits, and replay studies. The paper itself supports `test_time_compute`, not SFT, RLVR, reward-model training, or a claim that unreleased responses are reusable. Benchmark accuracy should remain an evaluation outcome rather than being retroactively treated as a quality label for intermediate traces.
