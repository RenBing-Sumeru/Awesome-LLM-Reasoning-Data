The evaluation covers 198 GPQA Diamond questions and a combined 60-problem AIME set, using Gemini 1.5 Flash and Gemini 2.5 Pro. Accuracy-versus-output-token curves compare DynScaling with majority-vote best-of-N and three sequential/parallel baselines; all reported results are averaged over three independent runs and smoothed with a moving average. Component ablations report degradation when either dynamic allocation or integrated sampling is removed, and analyses compare variation ratio, normalized entropy, inverse margin, and several exploration ratios. The paper also measures what fraction of additional budget goes to queries that remain incorrect. These results support the recipe under the studied configurations; they do not validate each uncertainty score, synthetic context, allocation decision, or generated response as high-quality data. No released trace set is available for item-level auditing.
