The official arXiv v1 record is dated 19 June 2025, and Google Research also lists the work as a 2025 publication. DynScaling addresses two coupled test-time decisions: how to combine breadth from independent sampling with depth from sequential prompting, and how to divide one fixed inference budget across a batch instead of giving every query the same number of samples. Its auditable object is therefore not one answer but a batch trace containing full model responses, synthetic conditional contexts, parsed-answer disagreement, allocation priorities, per-query budgets, and majority-voted outputs. That makes it directly relevant to `rollout_search_test_time_trace_data`, although the paper does not release those traces.
