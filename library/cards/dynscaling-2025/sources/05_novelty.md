The work does not introduce majority voting, sequential self-refinement, or UCB by themselves. Relative to independent best-of-N, SP1-SP3 sequential baselines, and prior adaptive methods that require a trained difficulty classifier, generative reward model, or constrained-RL controller, its specific change is to use only sampled answer disagreement for inference-only allocation across queries while constructing new conditional contexts from permutations of existing responses. The direction signal for this track is the coupling of trace construction and budget logging: the samples influence both later prompts and which query receives further compute. Reuse still requires the exact prompts, answer parser, subset-selection rule, sample accounting, seeds, and model/API version, none of which is fully released.
