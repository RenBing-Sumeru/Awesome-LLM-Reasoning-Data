IPG makes the solution program, rather than an LLM's confidence, the verification surface. A generator may vary the narrative, variables, and formula chain, but the accepted record must execute against a fixed Formula-as-Code library, produce a finite value in a physically valid range, and have a non-duplicate formula/target signature.
