For each prompt, the method samples multiple reasoning paths and parses them into candidate answers. Perplexity Consistency uses the language model's internal path probabilities together with answer agreement to score answer confidence. The paper shows that this combination can retain the answer-space advantage of self-consistency while improving the convergence behavior of the confidence estimate in the favorable regime.

Reasoning Pruning models the probability distribution of sampled paths and removes low-probability paths before final selection. The implementation exposes separate computations for self-consistency, perplexity, and RPC, plus data processing and evaluation scripts. The relevant inference record is therefore explicit: sampled trace, extracted answer, probability, consistency-derived confidence, pruning decision, and selected answer. No parameter update is required at deployment.
