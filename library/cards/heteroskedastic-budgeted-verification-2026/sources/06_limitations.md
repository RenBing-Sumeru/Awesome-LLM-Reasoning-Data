The study is a controlled diagnostic, not a universal routing recipe. It uses cost strata defined from a particular proxy, two benchmark families, three models, injected label and proxy noise, and a small set of uncertainty features. Stratification can harm performance when its partition does not align with meaningful heterogeneity, as the Qwen3-MATH results show. The theory is a local upper-bound analysis under explicit distributional assumptions, so it neither proves that CST always wins nor settles how to discover the right strata in open-ended agent workloads.
