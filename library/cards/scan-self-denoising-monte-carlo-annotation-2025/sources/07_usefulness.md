When many mathematical trajectories are available but PRM800K-scale human labeling is unaffordable, SCAN’s lightweight continuation and boundary-softening strategy can produce continuous-score training records, evaluated by performance gain per annotation token under a fixed PRM architecture. It also provides an MC-annotation baseline for studying rollout count, annotator size, and softening range. The pipeline should not be used when terminal outcomes cannot be checked reliably, because the Monte Carlo success signal itself becomes invalid.
