1. Positioning: RM-Bench uses content-neighbor construction with independent control of length, formatting, tone, and structure to create specialized feedback for failures that general reward-model robustness evaluation misses.

2. Method handle: collect tasks and candidates, generate feedback, verify boundary cases, and organize by capability; label review is the decisive quality control.

3. Data handle: 1,327 prompts and 11,943 subtle-content and style-controlled preference comparisons, with records centered on inputs, candidates, evaluation evidence, and feedback labels.

4. Evidence anchor: conventionally strong reward models still prefer worse answers because of style, with conclusions bounded by model, candidate, and judge configurations.

5. Reuse decision: best suited to auditing sensitivity to subtle errors and stylistic shortcuts; contamination, false positives, and distributional stability must be checked.
