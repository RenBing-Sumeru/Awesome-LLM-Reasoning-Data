The central artifact is RM-Bench, containing 1,327 prompts and 11,943 subtle-content and style-controlled preference comparisons. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use content-neighbor construction with independent control of length, formatting, tone, and structure for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is auditing sensitivity to subtle errors and stylistic shortcuts.
