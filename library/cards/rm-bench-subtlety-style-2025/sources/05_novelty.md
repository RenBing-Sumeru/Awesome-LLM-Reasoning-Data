Closest prior work usually uses general preference pairs, final-answer correctness, or one holistic score without data specifically designed for the key failures and usage conditions of reward-model robustness. This paper changes the feedback object and evaluation contract by centering RM-Bench and placing task input, candidate response, and auditable evidence in one record, with labels produced through content-neighbor construction with independent control of length, formatting, tone, and structure. The contribution is the combination of dataset construction, fine-grained organization, and practical use rather than merely another model or pairwise format.
