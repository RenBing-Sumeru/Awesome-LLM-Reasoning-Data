The paper reports leading or competitive results among Qwen2.5-based open models at 7B and 32B on AIME24, AIME25, and MATH, while using fewer tokens on correct answers. The most relevant controlled evidence comes from stage ablations: length progression and the repetition penalty improve stability and token efficiency together rather than simply extending context. The claim is tied to the 719K/62K recipe and Qwen2.5 backbone; stronger bases or different reward parsers require renewed validation.
