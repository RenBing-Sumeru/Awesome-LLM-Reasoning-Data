Prior methods rely on final answers, generic step labels, or a single LLM judge, leaving a mismatch between feedback and the target. This work centers supervision on mathematical problems, original steps, controlled correct/incorrect variants, and diagnostic labels, uses constructing and verifying controlled perturbations for simplicity, soundness, and sensitivity to generate and filter records, and releases PRMBench. The novelty lies in the data object and acceptance rule rather than standard training algorithms.
