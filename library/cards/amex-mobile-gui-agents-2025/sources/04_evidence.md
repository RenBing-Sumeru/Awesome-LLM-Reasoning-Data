The official sources report over 104K high-resolution screenshots from popular Android applications, about 711K element-wise functionality annotations, roughly 3K unique instructions, and stepwise action chains with an average length near 13. The project page describes human filtering of GUI boxes and human checking of GPT-generated descriptions; the HF dataset card exposes sample folders for instruction annotations, element annotations, and screenshots, with the full dataset distributed as large split zip files. These are dataset-release and annotation-process signals, not row-level guarantees for every action chain. Evidence is bounded by the public artifact revision, the availability of split files, evaluator code, and the fact that downstream metrics may depend on action-space conversions.
