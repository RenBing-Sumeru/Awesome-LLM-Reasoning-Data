On MMLU-Pro, the 70B chain-of-thought baseline scores 64.3%. At comparable high budgets, self-consistency reaches 68.7%, debate reaches 70.0%, and mixture-of-agents reaches 71.4%, a 7.1-point gain over the baseline. Relative to self-consistency under comparable compute, debate gains 1.3 points and mixture-of-agents gains 2.7 points. Self-refinement is reported below the chain-of-thought baseline despite using additional compute, showing that extra sequential work is not automatically useful.

The allocation analysis is equally important. Self-consistency saturates near ten sequences and debate near four agents, whereas mixture-of-agents continues improving through five models and four layers. For easy, medium, and hard items, increasing to 15–20 times chain-of-thought compute changes accuracy by 2.2, 8.5, and 9.0 points respectively. These curves support adaptive routing: spend high budgets principally on difficult tasks, and prefer more agents to more debate rounds when extending a debate pipeline.
