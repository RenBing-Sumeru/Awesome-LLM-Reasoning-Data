Runtime is estimated rather than measured end-to-end on a particular deployment stack, and the main evidence is limited to multiple-choice reasoning, two model sizes from one family, and a 1,000-item MMLU-Pro sample. Debate and mixture-of-agents implementations may behave differently with heterogeneous models, tool use, longer outputs, or alternative selection rules. The empirical ratio rule should therefore guide a configuration sweep, not replace one, and the cost of searching many pipeline settings is not itself included in the per-task frontier.
