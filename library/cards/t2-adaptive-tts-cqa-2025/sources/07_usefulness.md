T2 is useful for applications that answer document-grounded questions with widely varying structural complexity and need a traceable alternative to fixed chain-of-thought budgets. A replication should log the parsed template, generated analogues, rejected candidates, selected plan, extracted evidence segments, prompts, model calls, tokens, latency, and final answer. That audit trail lets a team compare a plan-selection policy with a direct budget predictor, identify whether expensive planning is being used only on difficult cases, and replace individual components without losing the connection between observed cost and the reasoning decision that caused it.
