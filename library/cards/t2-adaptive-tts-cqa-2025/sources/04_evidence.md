The official EMNLP paper evaluates T2 on seven contextual QA datasets: SQuAD, HotpotQA, NewsQA, Gaokao, HQA, TriviaQA, and BioASQ. It compares Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct and GPT-4o configurations with direct prompting, few-shot prompting, chain-of-thought variants, self-consistency, proCoT, Tree of Thoughts, and MCTS; it also reports results when adding T2 to QwQ-32B-Preview. The reported table shows higher ROUGE-L values for T2 across the listed Qwen2.5-32B settings and most stated comparison conditions.

The efficiency evidence is not only a token assertion. The paper reports average inference time: on Qwen2.5-32B, T2 takes 34.52 seconds versus 78.92 seconds for MCTS, a 56.3% reduction; on QwQ-32B it reports 45.03 versus 90.58 seconds, a 50.3% reduction. Its abstract summarizes a reduction of computational overhead by up to 25.2%, while the introduction reports up to 21.3% accuracy improvement over other test-time strategies. These results are task-specific comparisons, so a deployment decision should reproduce the strategy-generation cost and answer-quality metrics on its own corpus.
