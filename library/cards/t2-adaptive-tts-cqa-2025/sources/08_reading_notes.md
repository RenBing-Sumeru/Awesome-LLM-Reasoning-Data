When reading the full paper, separate the cost of selecting a strategy from the cost of executing the selected answer plan. Inspect the exact prompts and thresholds for analogous-question generation, similarity validation, coverage, uniqueness, and evidence extraction; these determine whether adaptation is genuinely question-specific or largely a fixed overhead. The most useful reproduction reports accuracy, grounded-evidence precision and recall, strategy depth, number of generated analogues, tokens, API calls, and wall-clock time per difficulty band. It should also test whether strategy choice remains useful when the source documents are noisy or when the required reasoning skill falls outside the stated taxonomy.
