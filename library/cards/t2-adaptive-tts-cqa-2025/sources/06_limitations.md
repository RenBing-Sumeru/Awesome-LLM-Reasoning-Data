T2 adds several generation, validation, and selection steps before answering, so its claimed savings depend on the relative cost of those steps, the base model, and the comparison policy. Its synthetic similar questions and generated support documents can be malformed, insufficiently analogous, or factually unreliable, while the structural parser and seven-skill taxonomy may not express every question type. The evaluation focuses on contextual QA benchmarks and reported time comparisons; it does not establish that the same strategy-selection signal transfers unchanged to tool use, free-form writing, mathematical proof, or environments where evidence cannot be cleanly extracted from a supplied document.
