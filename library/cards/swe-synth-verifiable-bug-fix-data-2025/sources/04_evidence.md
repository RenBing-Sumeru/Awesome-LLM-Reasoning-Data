The authors fine-tune a 32B model on 2,136 successful trajectories and compare it with similarly sized real repair data on SWE-bench. The SWE-Synth-trained model reaches 22.2% on SWE-bench Verified, versus 18.2% for the real-data model and 16.4% for the base model. On SWE-bench Lite it reaches about 24.33%, compared with 23.67% for real data. The abstract reports an overall Lite advantage of about 2.3 points under another configuration, so exact differences depend on the subset and setup. Results show transfer from synthetic verified trajectories, but sample counts, successful-task difficulty, and configurations are not uniformly controlled; the small Lite gap does not prove synthetic data is generally superior to real issues.
