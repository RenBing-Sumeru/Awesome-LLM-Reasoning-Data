- **Feedback limitation:** offline single-reference matching can mark an alternative valid route or corrective action wrong, while rewarding an action that matches the trace but would later fail. The metric is next-action imitation, not a live task-success verifier; this creates both false negatives and false positives relative to the intended UI-control behavior (Paper §5, p. 10; curator inference from Appendix D.3).
- **Release and replay limitation:** screenshots, accessibility trees, and actions are available, but app/APK versions, accounts, reset state, network/service state, companion collection code, preprocessing code, evaluation code, and an executable terminal predicate are not pinned. Version drift can therefore change whether an action remains valid, and the released episodes cannot be immutably replayed.
- **Selection and lineage limitation:** failed/error episodes, raw events, and discarded traces were removed and are not released. The exact successful/infeasible counts, serialized status field, annotator/persona IDs, review decisions, and adjudication records are unknown, so users cannot measure how filtering removed hard cases or biased the retained behavior (Datasheet §§1.1.2 and 1.1.4, pp. 2–3).
- **Split and contamination limitation:** Table 3 reports 13,604 training episodes, but official `splits.json` contains 13,603 train IDs. No near-duplicate, task-template-overlap, or downstream benchmark decontamination procedure is disclosed. The four OOD test subsets overlap, so treating their counts as disjoint would also misstate evaluation coverage.
- **Rights and participant limitation:** the README, repository root license, and paper checklist say Apache-2.0, whereas the supplementary datasheet also says the dataset is free only for non-commercial purposes. This official-source conflict, plus unclear third-party app/UI-content rights, blocks an unqualified training-reuse recommendation. The authors report informed paid contractors using non-personal devices, but wage amount, total compensation, and record-level consent audit are undisclosed (Appendix B.1, p. 14; checklist p. 25).
- **Generalization and extrapolation limitation:** only PaLM-2S is fine-tuned, online inference cost is not studied, app categories are incomplete, and OOD/long-horizon results are weak. Million-scale targets come from log-linear extrapolation and episode-independence assumptions, not observed runs; they should not be treated as a collection prescription without additional models and live-environment validation (Paper §5 and §§4.4–4.5).
