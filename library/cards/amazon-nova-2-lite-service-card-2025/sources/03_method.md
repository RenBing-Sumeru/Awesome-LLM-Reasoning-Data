At runtime, Bedrock receives a prompt, filters it, may augment it for requested services, generates a completion, filters the output, and returns the result. The Service Card describes pretraining on curated source classes, followed by SFT and RLHF to align behavior with design goals and separate tuning of safety filters. The exact architecture, checkpoints, feedback source, pair format, reward, objectives, and optimization schedule are unknown.

Development testing uses public and proprietary automated benchmarks, proxy use cases, proprietary human-evaluation prompts rated by multiple people on factors such as quality, verbosity, and formatting, automated and manual red teaming, and third-party specialist review. These are evaluation interfaces, not confirmed training records. Training filters, mixture weights, quantities, rights, splits, decontamination, sampling temperatures, rollout counts, extended-thinking budget allocation, and candidate attrition are undisclosed.
