The [official AWS Service Card PDF](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pdfs/ai/responsible-ai/nova-2-lite/nova-2-lite.pdf) directly supports the multimodal I/O, one-million-token context, three extended-thinking effort levels, redacted reasoning display, source classes, SFT/RLHF, runtime filtering, and layered evaluation methodology. The [official Bedrock model page](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/model-card-amazon-nova-2-lite.html) confirms the service identity.

The card reports more than 98% safe responses on a proprietary 6.4K-prompt harmfulness set and more than 95% on a proprietary 8.5K-prompt toxicity set. These are provider-reported, unreleased evaluation results for the end-to-end system. They do not establish the quality of training examples, feedback labels, reward calibration, decontamination, or reproducibility.
