The prior-work baseline has two main forms. Fixed datasets and benchmarks such as GSM8K, MATH, and BBH offer stable evaluation rows and automatic checking but do not expand through a disclosed generator. Narrower procedural systems cover particular logic, puzzle, or game families; framework-level efforts such as GEM also standardize LLM-facing environments. Reasoning Gym does not originate procedural content generation, programmatic rewards, curriculum learning, or GRPO (Paper §6).

What changes is the reusable construction object. More than 100 heterogeneous tasks are registered behind a shared `question`/`answer`/`metadata` interface and a task-specific `score_answer` function. Difficulty, structural, and stylistic parameters are exposed; named tasks can be mixed; some attributes can be changed by a curriculum; and the same substrate is connected to zero-shot evaluation and RLVR. This turns data identity from a fixed row into a versioned execution tuple of code, configuration, seed/index, mixture or curriculum state, scorer, and dependencies.

The important data contribution is not merely scale. The oracle metadata and executable verifier travel with generation, including for tasks with multiple valid solutions. That creates a direct feedback interface for online RL without first materializing a permanent corpus. It also makes new audit obligations visible: two users with the same task name can obtain different examples or rewards if versions, seeds, parameters, scorers, or optional dependencies differ.

Some claims should be kept below the paper's rhetoric. Procedural generation reduces exact item reuse but does not by itself eliminate memorization of templates, semantic overlap, external benchmark contamination, or verifier gaming. The paper's three design principles are targets, not task-level proofs. Breadth is partly engineering integration across many individually implemented generators, and the environment remains a one-response text contract rather than a persistent multi-turn world.

For reuse comparisons, hold fixed generator and verifier revisions, realized prompts, policy/base model, rollout budget, response limit, reward components, mixture, optimizer, and compute. Then ablate procedural refresh, difficulty control, or curriculum separately. Without those controls, a downstream delta cannot be attributed specifically to data diversity, verifier quality, or the construction recipe.
