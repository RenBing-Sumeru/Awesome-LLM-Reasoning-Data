- **Scope.** The paper covers single-turn text tasks with structured or otherwise algorithmically checkable outcomes. Knowledge-intensive, creative, unstructured-answer, persistent multi-turn, and multimodal environments remain outside the demonstrated surface (Paper §7).
- **No immutable dataset.** The artifact is a changing generator-and-scorer library. Exact replay requires the code/package version, complete configuration, seed/index, virtual size or reseeding state, mixture weights, curriculum transitions, and scorer dependencies. The exact paper-era Reasoning Gym commit is unknown.
- **Verifier soundness and completeness.** Automatic checking can still produce false positives and false negatives. The current base scorer gives partial credit when the oracle string occurs inside a longer response; task overrides may have formatting, tolerance, malformed-output, or multiple-solution edge cases. No task-level calibrated verifier audit is released.
- **Post-paper scorer drift.** Current revision `49b07130b3fcd12f2d064bba7c43869543a0e7e7` adds a cascade that can upgrade scores using LaTeX stripping, case-insensitive equality, numeric tolerance, and optional symbolic matching. Missing `math-verify` silently disables the symbolic step. This current behavior must not be projected onto the paper experiments.
- **Reward confounding.** The paper's training reward sums accuracy with a 0.2 format component, and early reward increases are partly format learning. The model can improve the plotted reward without an equal correctness gain, even though evaluation tables isolate accuracy.
- **Difficulty and mixture calibration.** Curriculum attributes exist for only a subset of tasks, and level meanings are not comparable across environments. Count Primes does not leave its initial curriculum level. Uniform task sampling also assumes an IID mixture and leaves non-stationary streams and catastrophic forgetting open.
- **Evaluation uncertainty.** Transfer experiments use three runs and 50-problem evaluation sets. Some effects are small or negative, such as +0.5 on GSM8K and -2.3 cross-domain ARC points. The experiments do not establish robustness across larger frozen sets, policies, or verifier revisions.
- **Contamination and lineage.** New surface forms do not rule out shared templates, semantic overlap, or leakage into external benchmarks. No comprehensive template/semantic overlap ledger is released, and task-level external-asset provenance needs review despite the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
- **Missing RL experience data.** The official release can regenerate prompts and scores but does not provide a canonical ledger of successful and failed policy responses, reward components, exceptions, checkpoints, and training-step decisions. A regenerated prompt stream cannot reconstruct the paper's exact online experience.
