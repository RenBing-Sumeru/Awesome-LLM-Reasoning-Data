1. **Highly specialized:** Quantum-programming tasks require domain expertise and do not directly train a general natural-language judge or ordinary code model.
2. **Simulation gap:** The simulator reproduces rules and circuit behavior but not the full noise, calibration drift, and access latency of real hardware.
3. **Baseline fairness:** Models may use long reasoning and repeated feedback, while human submissions come from different contest conditions, so success-rate comparison is not equal-budget comparison.
