Despite strong conventional coding ability, GPT-4o reaches only about 18.97% accuracy on QCoder, showing that software-only generation does not directly transfer to quantum hardware constraints. The reasoning model o3 reaches up to 78%, substantially above the 39.98% average success rate of human submissions, indicating that advanced models can exploit complex feedback, although task mix and inference budgets must be controlled. Simulator feedback is deterministic and auditable compared with an LLM judge, but it only approximates the noise and scheduling behavior of real quantum devices.
