ThinkingFree changes only the formatted input: immediately after the assistant's opening think token, it appends an empty thinking block followed by the closing think control token, so the policy generates its answer without the usual explicit long thinking segment. Ground-truth answers are unchanged. TFPI runs an ordinary RLVR objective on these transformed inputs for a short, staged initialization, then optionally switches back to standard thinking-mode RLVR.

In the reported instantiation, DAPO supplies the optimizer scaffold and a rule-based reward r(x,y) in {0,1}. Eight responses form a group for each prompt; their relative answer-level rewards supply token-shared advantages. TFPI changes the conditioning input and rollout-length schedule, not the verifier or reward function. The resulting data object includes transformed and original prompt forms, sampled responses, binary rewards, group statistics, lengths, and stage/checkpoint identifiers. Compared with direct long-context RLVR, multistage RLVR, and efficiency methods based on special length rewards, the specific contribution is an initialization phase that uses the model's existing chat-template control to collect shorter verifiable rollouts before returning to slow-thinking training.
