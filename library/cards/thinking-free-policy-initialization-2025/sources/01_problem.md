RLVR initialized from a distilled long-chain-of-thought model can begin with strong reasoning but produces long rollouts, forcing large training contexts and high collection cost. Simply starting direct RLVR at a short maximum length can truncate useful behavior and, in the paper's meta-experiment, sharply reduce accuracy. The decision problem is therefore how to obtain a cheaper initialization policy without adding a length reward or discarding the slow-thinking capability needed later.

TFPI addresses this between long-CoT distillation and standard RLVR. The underlying data object is an online batch of Polaris-53K prompts, eight policy responses per prompt, token likelihoods, rule-based binary outcome rewards, and multi-stage response-length metadata. The official ICLR 2026 paper, Tencent-Hunyuan repository, Hugging Face checkpoint collection, and TFPI-EVA evaluation dataset are confirmed. They provide code, prompts/checkpoints, and evaluation assets, but not a complete immutable release of every successful and failed rollout, reward record, and optimizer event. The card is therefore L4-ready as a reproducible training recipe with incomplete trace-level lineage.
