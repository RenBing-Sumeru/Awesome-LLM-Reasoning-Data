Under matched training compute, Table 1 reports that the three TFPI stages improve the overall six-benchmark average for DS-1.5B from 22.0 to 29.2, Qwen3-4B from 60.3 to 63.8, and DS-7B from 42.2 to 47.8, while the matched direct-RL conditions reach 25.3, 60.2, and 43.0 respectively. The paper also reports that the ThinkingFree transformation reduces output tokens by more than 70% in a meta-experiment, and that TFPI initialization lowers rollout lengths during subsequent standard long-CoT RL. The 4B result highlighted in the paper reaches 89.0% on AIME24 and 65.5% on the studied LiveCodeBench subset using under 4K H20 hours for TFPI.

These are model-training and efficiency results under the stated evaluation protocol. They do not prove that short rollouts are faithful explanations, that binary rewards are error free, or that the collected traces form a high-quality dataset. The official repository and checkpoint collection improve reproducibility, but they do not expose a complete item-level trace and reward audit trail.
