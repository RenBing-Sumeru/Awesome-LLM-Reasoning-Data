TFPI is evaluated on three Qwen-derived distilled or hybrid reasoning models and trains only on Polaris-53K mathematics prompts; transfer to other architectures, chat templates, verifier types, and non-verifiable domains remains uncertain. ThinkingFree depends on the opening and closing think-token conventions of the chat template, so it is not a model-agnostic deletion of internal reasoning. Short visible outputs also do not establish that hidden computation or reasoning faithfulness improved.

The comparisons match reported accelerator hours, but hardware efficiency, sequence utilization, and rollout mixture can still differ between short staged training and long direct RL. Benchmark subsets and multi-sample pass@1 protocols must be preserved when comparing numbers. The official repository has few commits and no tagged release on the inspected page; exact dependency and dataset revisions should be pinned. Checkpoints and evaluation sets are released, but raw successful/failed rollouts, reward logs, prompt-level sampling decisions, licenses for every inherited dataset, and decontamination records are not established as complete.
