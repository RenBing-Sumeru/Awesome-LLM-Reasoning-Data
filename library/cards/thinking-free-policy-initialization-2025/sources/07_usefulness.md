For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, TFPI identifies fields that should accompany efficient-RLVR traces: original prompt, fully rendered chat template, ThinkingFree transformation, base and old-policy checkpoint, stage and maximum length, all eight responses, truncation status, token count, binary verifier result, group reward statistics, importance ratios, optimizer step, and later thinking-mode evaluation. Separating TFPI-stage samples from subsequent standard-RL samples is essential because their conditioning distributions differ.

The official scripts and checkpoints can support controlled studies of how initialization changes rollout length, reward yield, convergence, and cross-domain evaluation. TFPI-EVA is useful for evaluation reproduction, not as evidence that training traces were released. Curators should retain failed and truncated responses rather than treating only successful short answers as the data product, and should keep efficiency metrics separate from correctness and reasoning-quality labels.
