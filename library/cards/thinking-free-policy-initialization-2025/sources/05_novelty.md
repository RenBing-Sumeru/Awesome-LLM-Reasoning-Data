Prior multistage RLVR reduces context length progressively but still rolls out ordinary long-thinking prompts; direct short-context RL can truncate the distilled policy's behavior; efficient-reasoning methods often add length rewards or mode-switching objectives. TFPI instead inserts a dedicated initialization stage whose only conceptual intervention is the ThinkingFree input transform, while retaining the same ground-truth reward and DAPO machinery. It then tests whether the initialized policy benefits later normal RLVR.

The direction signal for data curation is that prompt formatting and stage identity materially change the rollout distribution even when prompts, verifier, and optimizer are held fixed. A reusable trace corpus must therefore preserve the exact chat template and whether the sample came from TFPI or ordinary thinking mode. Appending an empty think block, DAPO, Polaris-53K, and multistage length schedules are not individually new; the contribution is their use as a pre-RLVR policy initialization and the matched-compute analysis. Performance gains do not certify the semantic quality or completeness of the released rollout data.
