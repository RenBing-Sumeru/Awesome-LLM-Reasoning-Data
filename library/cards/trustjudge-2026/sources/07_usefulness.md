Use this when an evaluation team has a judge that can expose next-token probabilities and needs a coherent score-plus-ranking contract. Input candidate responses and fixed prompts; run distributional scoring and bidirectional comparisons; output scores, pair verdicts, CR/NTR, and a final ordering. Success is lower inconsistency without loss on a target human-labeled set. Do not use it as a substitute for content, safety, or human-preference validation.
