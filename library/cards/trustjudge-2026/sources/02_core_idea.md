TrustJudge reframes reliability as consistency between two decision contracts. Distribution-sensitive scoring turns judge logits over an expanded scale into a softmax expectation, retaining entropy lost by a 1–5 integer. Likelihood-aware aggregation resolves ambiguous pairwise ties from bidirectional win/tie/lose probabilities or perplexity. The official code is available; the central artifact is the framework and inconsistency audit, not a standalone benchmark.
