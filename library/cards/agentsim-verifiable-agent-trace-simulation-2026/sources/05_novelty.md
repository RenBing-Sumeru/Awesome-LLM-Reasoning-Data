The closest work usually filters whole trajectories by success or simply concatenates existing agent data. Its novelty lies primarily in the data object, the source of negative examples, and quality control, rather than in a generic SFT, DPO, BCE, or RL loss.

The data contribution is to make feedback that was previously implicit in search, tool calls, web environments, document citations, or evaluators explicit as questions, retrieval queries, document evidence, step-by-step reasoning, verifier outcomes, model disagreement, and human recheck decisions.
