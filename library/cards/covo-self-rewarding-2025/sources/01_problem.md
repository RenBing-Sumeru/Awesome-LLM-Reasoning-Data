The official NeurIPS proceedings list this work in the NeurIPS 2025 Main Conference Track; the arXiv record was first submitted on 10 June 2025. CoVo addresses a specific bottleneck in reasoning post-training: reinforcement learning normally needs a correctness label, executable checker, reward model, or judge, yet many reasoning prompts have no dependable external verifier. The paper asks whether a policy can obtain a useful learning signal from the geometry of its own sampled reasoning paths. (Paper §1; NeurIPS proceedings)

For each prompt, the current policy produces 16 complete trajectories. A trajectory is divided into intermediate states, and each state is scored by how likely the same policy is to continue toward each distinct final answer sampled by the group. The resulting training object is not a labeled proof or a correctness-verified answer. It is an online collection of prompts, policy traces, extracted answer groups, state-to-answer distance matrices, trajectory statistics, scalar rewards, and normalized advantages. Supervision attaches as one answer-group reward shared by full trajectories, then becomes normalized advantages for Reinforce++ rather than a step-correctness label. (Paper §2–§3; Appendix C)

This boundary matters. CoVo's reward estimates internal consistency and path stability; it does not establish that an answer is true, and Math-Verify enters only during benchmark evaluation. The Card belongs to **Data Construction & Open-Release Recipes** because the paper and code expose a prompt-to-rollout-to-reward-to-policy pipeline, and to **Preference / Reward / Feedback Data** because the reusable object is a programmatic scalar feedback contract. It does not cover externally verified RLVR data. The public Hugging Face release contains prompt/reference-answer JSONL files rather than the paper-run trajectories, distance matrices, group rewards, filtered prompts, or policy checkpoints. The bilingual Card is `L4_chinese_review_ready`, while its `partial` release status still blocks training-ready reuse.
