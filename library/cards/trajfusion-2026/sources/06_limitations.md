- **Outcome-only labels:** a correct extracted answer can hide invalid intermediate reasoning, while an equivalent answer can be rejected by extraction or normalization failure.
- **Synthetic rather than grounded feedback:** the fixed reflection phrases do not identify the first error, explain its cause, or verify that the next attempt fixes it; optional certainty phrases may teach confidence boilerplate.
- **Coarse diversity proxy:** distinct wrong final answers need not correspond to distinct causal mistakes, and semantically different failures can collapse to the same answer bucket.
- **Biased retained region:** all-wrong prompts are excluded because no accepted correct trajectory can close the fused sequence; always-correct prompts reduce to ordinary RFT. The method's own limitation section says it depends on mixed-outcome sampling.
- **Selection artifacts:** choosing the shortest trajectory from a frequent wrong-answer group may favor concise but spurious or low-information failures. The paper does not disclose `alpha`, tie breaking, correct-trajectory selection, or seeds.
- **Incomplete lineage:** counts of raw candidates, malformed rejects, excluded prompts, unselected errors, and selection decisions are not reported or released, so the 15K/100K counts describe retained fused records rather than an auditable raw corpus.
- **Split and contamination uncertainty:** exact source revisions, per-source retained counts, decontamination procedures, and source-to-benchmark overlap controls are unknown despite training and evaluation both involving prominent math collections.
- **Release boundary:** no official code, constructed data, checkpoints, project page, Hugging Face artifact, artifact license, or immutable checksum was confirmed. CC BY 4.0 covers the ACL paper, not automatically the constructed data or upstream sources and models.
- **Evidence boundary:** benchmark improvements establish downstream utility under the reported setup; they are not proof of row-level data quality, step validity, licensing compatibility, or reproducibility. Small AIME test sets and undisclosed seed variance further limit interpretation.
- **Domain boundary:** evidence is concentrated in mathematical reasoning with answer-verifiable tasks. The recipe's behavior for open-ended domains, ambiguous answers, tool-using trajectories, or environments without a reliable terminal verifier is unknown.
