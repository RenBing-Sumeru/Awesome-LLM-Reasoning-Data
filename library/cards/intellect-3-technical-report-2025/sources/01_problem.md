Frontier model reports often call a training stack open even when the exact prompts, environment mixture, rollout settings, and record-level lineage are not reproducible. INTELLECT-3 is useful for testing this distinction because Prime Intellect releases the model, trainer, verifier framework, and environments while reporting a large SFT-plus-RL run.

The audit question is therefore not only how well the model performs, but which post-training data and feedback interfaces are concretely reusable and which run-level details remain undisclosed.
