The authors human-evaluate responses from six retrieval-augmented models on FACTORY and prior datasets. Roughly 40% of claims on FACTORY lack human-found support or are nonfactual, compared with about 10% on other benchmarks, showing that the hard prompts substantially increase factuality difficulty. Human filtering also reduces ambiguity and unanswerability. The benchmark is clearly more challenging, but failure to find support depends on search capability, link decay, and time and is not equivalent to permanent falsity.
