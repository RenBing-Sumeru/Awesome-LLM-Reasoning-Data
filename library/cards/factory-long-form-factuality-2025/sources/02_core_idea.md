FACTORY is a large, human-verified English long-form prompt set released in JSONL with `all`, `hard`, and `fact_checking` splits, in the 10K–100K size range. Prompt records contain questions and relevant URLs. Human fact-checking decomposes responses into claims labelled Factual, NonFactual, Inconclusive, or No Verifiable Fact and preserves evidence sources and snippets. The contribution includes difficult, answerable, non-time-sensitive prompts and claim–label–evidence objects for training or auditing factuality judges.
