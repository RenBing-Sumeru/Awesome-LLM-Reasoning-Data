Long-form factuality benchmarks often begin with automatically generated prompts that can be ambiguous, unanswerable, or too easy, while automatic judges may treat claims with unavailable evidence as false. Without human verification, benchmark defects become confounded with model hallucination and long-tail, multi-source synthesis is under-tested.

FACTORY uses a model-in-the-loop process to expand Wikipedia topics and filter difficult prompts, followed by human refinement. It also releases claim-level factuality labels, evidence URLs, and snippets for evaluated model responses.
