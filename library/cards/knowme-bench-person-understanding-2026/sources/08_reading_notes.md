1. **One-sentence positioning:** A benchmark that reconstructs autobiographical text into flashback-aware experience streams and evaluates person understanding rather than simple memory.
2. **Data takeaway:** About 4.7M tokens and 2,580 questions across factual, state-attribution, and principle-reasoning tiers.
3. **Method takeaway:** Semantic segmentation, atomic-event extraction, temporal realignment, cognitive-stream reconstruction, and expert question validation.
4. **Evidence anchor:** RAG substantially improves factual recall but not motivation, temporal causality, or principle reasoning.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is highly relevant to long-context rubric data, although higher-level labels remain inherently interpretive.
