1. **Segment text:** Autobiographical narratives are split at scene and semantic boundaries so that fixed-length chunking does not destroy causal structure.
2. **Extract atomic units:** Segments are decomposed into auditable Atomic Narrative Units marking actions, environment, perception, knowledge, and inner activity.
3. **Realign time:** Present events, memory triggers, and recalled events are identified; flashback content is moved to its original time while retaining trigger links.
4. **Reconstruct experience:** A multi-agent pipeline organizes units into a continuous personal experience stream with time, location, and five cognitive fields. Every generative module is paired with a Verify-and-Revise checker.
5. **Annotate questions:** Three-tier questions, reference answers, and evidence events are created and cross-validated by trained annotators and literary experts to ensure traceability.
