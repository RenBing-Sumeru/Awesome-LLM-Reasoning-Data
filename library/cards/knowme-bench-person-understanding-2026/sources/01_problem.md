Long-term memory benchmarks often equate person understanding with retrieving an old fact. A lifelong digital companion, however, must explain why a user made a choice, which past events shaped a current emotion, and how stable motivations and principles persist over time. KnowMe-Bench asks how autobiographical long-form text can be reconstructed into a traceable experience stream and evaluated with evidence-constrained questions that separate factual memory, subjective-state understanding, and higher-level person reasoning, preventing models from guessing personality through generic priors.
