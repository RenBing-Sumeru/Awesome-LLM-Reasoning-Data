The paper compares direct long-context prompting, RAG, external memory stores, and agentic-memory systems. Retrieval mainly improves Tier-1 factual questions and offers limited help on temporal-causal explanation, motivation attribution, and principle abstraction in Tiers 2 and 3. Even after finding relevant passages, models may misorder events or provide unsupported personality explanations. Expert scoring and judge–expert agreement analysis strengthen the reliability of open-ended evaluation. The evidence supports the claim that retrieval is not person understanding, although the substrate remains textual autobiography rather than genuine multimodal lifelogs.
