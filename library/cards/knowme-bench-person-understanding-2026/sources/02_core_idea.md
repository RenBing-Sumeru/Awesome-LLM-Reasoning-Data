KnowMe-Bench uses long-form autobiographical narratives as its substrate, producing approximately 4.7 million tokens of time-aligned experience streams and 2,580 evaluation questions. It separates memory into visual observations, auditory inputs, situational context, accessible background knowledge, and inner monologue while explicitly handling flashbacks. Evaluation has three tiers: factual extraction, subjective-state attribution, and decision/principle reasoning. Higher-level answers must cite supporting events. Open responses are graded with task-specific 0–5 rubrics and expert verification of both answers and evidence.
