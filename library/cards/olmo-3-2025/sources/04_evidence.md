The official arXiv report states that the release includes the model family's full lifecycle, including every stage, checkpoint, data point, and dependency used to build it. The AI2 project page provides a stage map from pretraining through Think/Instruct SFT, DPO, and RL to RL-Zero, with links to downloadable artifacts.

The official pre-training collection identifies a full pretraining pool, midtraining pool, long-context pool, final-stage mixes, and smaller experimental mixes. The official post-training collection lists Think SFT/DPO/RL, Instruct SFT/DPO/RL, RL-Zero data, associated checkpoints, and tool-use subsets. The 7B Instruct model card independently identifies Dolma 3 as pretraining data and Dolci as post-training data, and lists Base, SFT, DPO, and RLVR stages.

The paper reports mechanism-level evidence for data selection and feedback, such as published source-mixture tables, microanneal/integration-test procedures, task-specific verifiers, decontamination settings, and RL hyperparameters. These facts support an auditable disclosure ledger. They do not by themselves establish that an external team has reproduced every artifact, validator, generator, or result.
