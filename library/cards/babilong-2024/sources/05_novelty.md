The prior baseline is short bAbI reasoning, single-needle retrieval, and long-context QA/summarization benchmarks with fixed natural documents. BABILong changes the evaluation object by preserving synthetic answerable reasoning facts while scaling distractor length to millions of tokens, including predefined splits up to 10M tokens and reported tests up to 50M with recurrent models. The direction signal is a controllable long-context stress test where failure can be tied to fact selection and multi-fact reasoning. What is not new: bAbI tasks, exact-answer scoring, and distractor insertion are existing ideas. Reuse requires auditing generator parameters, background text rights, split/version policy, and whether public generated examples have leaked into training.
