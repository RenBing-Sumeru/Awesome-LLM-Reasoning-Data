Correctness is only correctness relative to the generated bAbI answer and the official scorer. Synthetic facts have limited vocabulary and may be easier to separate from background text after fine-tuning; the authors also note that only PG19 and Wiki backgrounds were tried. The benchmark does not validate free-form rationales, real document understanding, or whether a model used a faithful retrieval path. Public releases can contaminate future model training, and leaderboard scores can move with prompt templates, retrieval implementation, model backend changes, and generated split revisions.
