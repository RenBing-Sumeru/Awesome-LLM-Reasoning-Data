The paper reports evaluation of more than 30 long-input models and finds that many models use only about 10-20% of their advertised context, with sharp degradation as length and reasoning complexity increase. For QA1, most models work only to about 4K tokens; for QA2 and QA3, even strong systems fall below the paper's 85% satisfactory threshold when facts are embedded in distractors. The evidence is aggregate accuracy from the official generated tasks and scorer, not row-level proof of semantic reasoning. Scores are bounded by prompt choices, length bins, background corpus, model revisions, retrieval settings, and the answer-normalization policy.
