BABILong asks whether long-context LLMs can retrieve and reason over small sets of relevant facts when those facts are hidden inside very long natural-language distractor text. The primary sources are the arXiv paper, the NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks page, the official GitHub repository, and the RMT-team Hugging Face dataset/leaderboard. The evaluation surface is a generated bAbI-style QA instance: task facts, a question, PG19/Wiki-like background text inserted up to fixed length bins, and a reference answer. It belongs here because it turns long-context reasoning into a versioned benchmark with an explicit answer-level scorer; it is not a general document QA collection or a proof of real-world comprehension.
