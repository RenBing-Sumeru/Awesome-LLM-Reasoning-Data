Inputs begin with questions and reference answers from MAT-Agent and the cited 2WikiMultiHopQA and HotpotQA sources. An undisclosed initial policy samples 10 responses per question; questions with pass rate in `[0.2, 0.8]` are retained, yielding approximately 1,300 QA pairs. Item IDs, source counts, versions, mixture weights, split membership, deduplication, and the initial-policy checkpoint are unknown. (Paper §3.2 and §4.1)

During training, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct or Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct generates an episode of at most 10 interaction steps. At each step it emits natural-language Thought and executable Python Code; a controlled interpreter executes sequentially with persistent variables and returns Observation. Appendix A names `inspect_file_as_text`, `wikipedia_qa`, `web_qa`, `visit_qa`, `find_archived_url`, `local_visualizer`, and `final_answer`, but their implementations, service versions, sandbox image, package pins, resource limits, network rules, retry behavior, and exception handling are unreleased.

Each question owns a FIFO queue with `G=16` cached trajectories. A training step generates `g=8` fresh trajectories, enqueues them, and evicts the eight oldest; the method also resamples entries described as falling outside the `0.2–0.8` pass-rate band. The paper does not resolve whether that pass rate belongs to a question, queue group, or individual sample, nor does it disclose queue age, behavior-policy probabilities, off-policy correction, or replacement logs. (Paper §3.2; Table 2)

Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct assigns the answer label, while parsing and runtime success produce `R_parse` and `R_exec`; the three components form one trajectory reward. Response-masked GRPO applies that episode-level signal only across policy-generated Thought and Code tokens, excluding Observation tokens from the loss. The disclosed optimization settings are AdamW, learning rate `1e-6`, effective batch size 256, 2 epochs, maximum sequence length 2048, KL coefficient `0.001`, and eight A100 GPUs split evenly between training and online sampling. Inference temperature is `0.6`; training-rollout decoding settings, seeds, GRPO clipping, AdamW betas, and checkpoint cadence are unknown. (Paper §3.1, §3.3, §4.1)

The conceptual output is a trained 7B or 14B agent policy plus transient queued episodes and their reward fields. No public Tool-R1 checkpoint, task manifest, serialized trajectory or queue schema, raw observation log, reward log, training configuration, evaluation harness, or environment package is present in the official repository, so faithful reconstruction requires implementing and pinning every one of those components.
