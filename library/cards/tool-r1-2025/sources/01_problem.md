The official record is arXiv:2509.12867v1, submitted on 16 September 2025; no peer-reviewed venue was verified. Tool-R1 addresses the cost and instability of reinforcement learning for agents whose rollouts repeatedly call external tools: fully fresh online sampling is slow, while a useful training recipe must distinguish policy-generated reasoning and actions from environment-returned observations. (arXiv record; Paper Introduction and §3.1–3.2)

The work belongs in `environment_agent_trajectory_data` because its primary object is a complete executable agent episode used for training, not a static benchmark item. One episode begins with a question and reference answer, then alternates model-generated **Thought → Python Code** with tool-generated **Observation**, retains variables across executions, and ends through `final_answer` or the 10-step cap. It carries answer, parsing, and execution reward components plus a scalar trajectory reward, and recent episodes are held in a per-question queue.

The training pool contains approximately 1,300 moderately difficult QA pairs sourced from MAT-Agent and the cited 2WikiMultiHopQA and HotpotQA datasets. GAIA validation is only the reported evaluation surface; Tool-R1 is therefore an agent RL construction recipe rather than a benchmark or evaluation-only contribution, and the paper does not justify treating it as a generic RLVR dataset.
