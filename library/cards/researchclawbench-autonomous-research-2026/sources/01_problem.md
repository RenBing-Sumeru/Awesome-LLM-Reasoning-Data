Science-agent evaluations often check only whether code runs or a final metric resembles a paper, without testing independent problem formulation, protocols, evidence interpretation, and research artefacts. Providing the target paper also enables answer reproduction and conflates retrieval memory with autonomous research.

ResearchClawBench hides the target paper, supplies related literature and raw data, and uses expert weighted multimodal rubrics to assess rediscovery of the scientific core and experimental process.
