The benchmark contains 138 tasks over Bluecoins, Calendar, Cantook, Clock, Contacts, Maps.me, PiMusic, Settings, and Zoom: 93 operation tasks and 45 query tasks (Paper §3.2.1; Appendix A/Table 4). Formal runs begin inside the target app and use a 25-step cap, greedy decoding, and a 3-second device interval (Paper §5.1). These conditions narrow the claim to in-app task completion rather than general phone operation from launch.

Table 1 reports that instruction tuning raises average success rate from 4.59% to 21.50% for the three LLMs and from 1.93% to 13.28% for the three VLMs. Model-level SR changes are Llama-3.1-8B 2.17→23.91, Qwen2-7B 4.35→19.57, GLM-4-9B 7.25→21.01, CogVLM2 0.72→11.59, Llama-3.2-11B-Vision 1.45→10.14, and Qwen2-VL-7B 3.62→18.12 (Paper Table 1, p. 8). These are author-reported benchmark results, not independent evidence that every released trace is correct, licensed, non-overlapping, or reusable.

The authors report more than 98% completion-classification accuracy for the reward model trained on about 500 positive and negative trajectories (Appendix C.3). Because the model identity, evaluation split and size, class balance, calibration, false-positive/negative analysis, and checkpoint are undisclosed, this number supports the described collection filter but does not establish external verifier reliability.

Figure 5 shows lower SoM performance on Pixel 3a and Pixel Fold than on common-size Pixel 7/8 Pro screens, demonstrating that viewport and UI geometry materially affect results. The paper also reports hard-coded task evaluators, variable XML quality, Android-only coverage, and a fixed wait that may not suit all devices. No experiment was independently replayed for this Card, and the data and environment bundles were not hash-verified.
