The paper evaluates accuracy, average response length, and Accuracy Efficiency Score on GSM8K, MATH-500, MMLU-STEM, AIME'25, and MINERVA-MATH. In the GSM8K ablation with Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, the authors' DAPO reproduction reports 85.6 accuracy and 290 average tokens, while BFS-PO with three expansions reports 86.2 and 269. Removing best-first selection gives 86.0 and 311; using a global rather than branch-local advantage gives 85.7 and 271; removing the length penalty gives 86.1 and 275. These comparisons support the paper's narrower claim that the reported search and branch-advantage design affects the accuracy–length trade-off under its setup. They do not prove that the hidden rollout records are correct, diverse, uncontaminated, or reusable. The paper also reports an average 1.37× training speedup over its DAPO setup from shared-prefix KV-cache reuse, but no implementation or logs are available for independent audit. The official repository currently contains only a placeholder README and figure and says implementation and experimental resources will be provided later; no code, models, checkpoints, data, or releases were verified.
