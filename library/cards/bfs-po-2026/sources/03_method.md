Training examples are question–ground-truth-answer pairs from GSM8K or MATH-500. Algorithm 1 initializes a tree with a group of complete policy outputs. At each of three reported expansion iterations, it selects the minimum-length verifier-correct complete path, finds its highest-entropy unused token, and samples another group of continuations from the prefix ending at that token. Previously selected backtracking tokens are excluded to encourage different forks. The paper does not disclose a reusable per-question rollout count or training sampling temperature, and it does not release the generated trees.

For optimization, complete leaf paths receive DAPO's correctness reward plus Soft Overlong Punishment. Internal node rewards are recursively averaged from children; a branch advantage compares each branch's value with the value at its backtracking node and normalizes by the children's reward standard deviation. The clipped DAPO-style objective is applied to tree tokens, with shared prefixes allowing KV-cache reuse. Models are Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, and LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct, fine-tuned with LoRA rank 8/alpha 16 and AdamW at learning rate 1e-6 and weight decay 0.01. Maximum training budgets are 512 tokens for GSM8K and 1024 for MATH-500. The paper reports 8 NVIDIA A100 SXM4 64GB GPUs and training times of 12 and 24 hours respectively. At inference, the trained model generates one ordinary chain without the search tree.
