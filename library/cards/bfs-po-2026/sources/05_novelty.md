The paper's distinctive contribution is the combination of three elements: best-first expansion of the shortest verifier-correct complete path, maximum-entropy token backtracking without a PRM, and a branch-local advantage computed from descendant terminal rewards. This differs from static concise-CoT SFT, which supplies precomputed short targets; from plain RL length penalties, which do not organize rollout exploration around the best path; and from TreeRL as described by the authors, which uses a different uncertainty criterion and value comparison. The work does not introduce final-answer verification, DAPO's reward and clipping machinery, LoRA, token entropy as an uncertainty signal, or search trees themselves. Its atlas value is therefore a specific online trace-construction and credit-assignment recipe, not a new released dataset or evidence that shortest correct traces are intrinsically higher-quality reasoning.
