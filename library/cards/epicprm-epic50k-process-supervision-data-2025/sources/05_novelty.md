Compared with assigning a whole chain of thought a single correct-or-incorrect label, this work changes the unit or acquisition path of process data. EpicPRM introduces a data-construction framework and releases Epic50K, using precise first-error localisation to obtain cost-effective mathematical step supervision. It builds an error-propagation chain for each candidate solution: after checking the final outcome, it locates the pivotal error and the steps affected by it, avoiding the treatment of consequences as independent errors. This is why it belongs in Process and Trace Supervision Data; process rewards themselves are not new, whereas its reusable contribution is the data contract, quality control, and evaluation boundary.
