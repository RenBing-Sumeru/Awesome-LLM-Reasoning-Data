EpicPRM introduces a data-construction framework and releases Epic50K, using precise first-error localisation to obtain cost-effective mathematical step supervision. It builds an error-propagation chain for each candidate solution: after checking the final outcome, it locates the pivotal error and the steps affected by it, avoiding the treatment of consequences as independent errors. It turns the sparse feedback of whether the final answer is correct into a supervision contract that can be localized to the reasoning process. It can therefore support training while revealing the step at which a model begins to diverge.
