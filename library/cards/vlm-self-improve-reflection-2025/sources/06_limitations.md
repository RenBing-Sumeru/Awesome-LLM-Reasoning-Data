The terminal verifier cannot establish rationale faithfulness. An answer-correct completion may contain visual, symbolic, arithmetic, or logical errors, and Figure 6 shows that this is observed rather than hypothetical. Conversely, equivalent answers outside a parser's accepted form, ChartQA tolerance boundaries, malformed formatting, or incomplete environment execution can create false negatives. Self-refine targets are merely answer-correct peer completions, so they can replace one flawed path with a different lucky-answer path.

The three-candidate selector is trained on constructed sets guaranteed to contain at least one positive and mixed labels. At inference, all candidates may be wrong. The selector emits an answer rather than a calibrated correctness score and has no disclosed threshold, abstention rule, uncertainty estimate, or independent reference access. It may exploit answer frequency, formatting, or generator-specific style; no adversarial-candidate, cross-generator, calibration, or all-wrong-set audit is reported.

“Latest positive” and “latest negative” refer to iteration or list order, not independently measured rationale quality. Candidate display order is shuffled without released seeds. Exact positive, negative, malformed, duplicate, timeout, parser-failure, and derived-record counts are absent by task, model, and iteration, so selection yield and failure retention cannot be reconstructed. Other raw candidates may remain in local run files but do not survive as a published rejection ledger.

The official release is incomplete for data reuse. It publishes 40,457 direct-QA rows and 2,800 GPT-CoT warm-up rows per Qwen-VL or LLaVA-1.5 variant for three tasks, but not the frozen self-generated rollouts, terminal labels, iteration/sample IDs, selected-candidate IDs, `D_REF`, `D_SEL`, final mixed training files, model checkpoints, or run logs. Main code/data covers TabMWP, ChartQA, and CLEVR-Math; Qwen2-VL/GeoQA is separate, and equivalent complete artifacts for MiniWob and M3CoT are unavailable.

Reproducibility details remain unknown: exact base-model and tokenizer revisions, generation/data/order seeds, the precise four-versus-five-iteration stopping rule, immutable prompt/config bundles, task-level hardware/runtime budgets, and checkpoint hashes. The main and companion repositories have no immutable GitHub release or tag. The manual fidelity study also lacks its rubric, annotator count, agreement, adjudication, and sampled IDs.

Named dataset splits are reported, but immutable row manifests, exact/near/semantic-overlap checks, and pretraining-contamination analysis are not. The paper is CC BY 4.0 and the repositories declare Apache-2.0, yet no row-level license and attribution matrix covers the upstream datasets and images, GPT-4o annotations, or generated traces. Repository code licensing must not be treated as clearance to redistribute every training record. Evidence is confined to the named multimodal benchmarks and one simulated web environment, not safety-critical or adversarial open-ended reasoning.
