R3V combines three roles for the same self-generated full-solution pool: accepted completions become direct SFT targets, selected failures become context for negative-conditioned refinement, and mixed three-candidate sets train answer selection. The selector is then reused at inference, connecting training-time rollout construction with test-time compute in one MLLM rather than requiring an external reward model or verifier head.

Relative to answer-only self-training such as STaR, the distinct contribution is not simply repeated sampling. It is the conversion of both terminally positive and negative completions into three supervised interaction formats, followed by iterative regeneration from updated checkpoints. Relative to preference learning, negatives are not optimized as rejected outputs with DPO in the proposed R3V objective; they are placed in the input context for refinement and comparison.

The method is best described as outcome-labelled rollout composition, not process supervision. Its feedback is denser at the conversation-format level because a failure can be shown to the model, but the only correctness ground truth remains the parsed final answer or environment success. The paper's own fidelity audit is important precisely because it prevents the observed benchmark gains from being mistaken for proof that the retained reasoning traces are faithful.
