Table 1 reports that, averaged over the six in-domain tasks, Qwen-VL reaches 64.37 with R3V versus 59.28 with STaR and 48.47 with GPT-distilled training. LLaVA-1.5 reaches 59.03 versus 55.81 and 45.24 respectively. These are author-reported downstream model outcomes; they do not by themselves establish that individual retained rationales are correct, diverse, licensed, or reusable.

The component ablation in Table 3 reports a Qwen-VL average of 64.37 for the full recipe, 62.50 without self-refine, 60.78 without self-select, and 60.64 without iterative training. This supports the joint recipe under the tested setup, but it does not isolate the causal quality of the chosen positive or negative records. In particular, the “latest” rule is an ordering heuristic, not an independent rationale-quality score.

For out-of-domain test-time selection, Table 2 reports Qwen-VL changes from 35.63 to 38.48 on MMMU, 35.10 to 35.80 on MathVista, and 50.23 to 51.78 on VCR when three-sample selection is added to R3V. Figures 4 and 5 compare selection with Test@1 and majority voting and show improvement that eventually plateaus as candidate sets grow. Exact token, FLOP, latency, hardware, and per-point candidate budgets are not tabulated, so the scaling result should be read as method evidence rather than a normalized compute-efficiency claim.

Table 4 supplies a useful negative result: for LLaVA, STaR+DPO averages 55.90, nearly unchanged from STaR at 55.81 and below R3V at 59.03. This supports the authors' observation for their noisy-CoT DPO setup, not a general claim that preference learning is ineffective.

The strongest audit evidence comes from the paper's rationale-fidelity study. The authors manually classify 100 answer-correct Qwen-VL samples per task, yet only 8% to 70% of the multimodal samples are fully correct CoT depending on the task. The paper shows perception, symbol, calculation, and reasoning errors paired with correct final answers. This directly demonstrates rationale-level false positives in the gold-answer verifier. The paper does not disclose the full rubric, annotator count, inter-annotator agreement, adjudication procedure, or sampled record IDs.

Artifact inspection verifies the official NAACL paper, DOI, arXiv record, main code/data repository, and official Qwen2-VL companion. It also verifies the 40,457 direct-QA rows and 2,800 warm-up rows per base-model variant. No immutable release or tag, frozen generated rollout pool, per-iteration positive/negative ledger, derived `D_REF`/`D_SEL` files, final mixture, checkpoint, or replay log was found. Thus citation and recipe evidence are strong, while derived-data completeness remains partial.
