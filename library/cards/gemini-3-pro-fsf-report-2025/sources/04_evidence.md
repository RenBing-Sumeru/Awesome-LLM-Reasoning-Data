The report describes approximately 75,000 prompts in a third-party harmful-manipulation evaluation and says testers used an SME-validated classification model. It describes internal, external, and third-party evaluation layers, but does not release the prompts, evaluator identity, SME-validation procedure, classifier or rubric, split definitions, or artifacts needed to rerun the work.
