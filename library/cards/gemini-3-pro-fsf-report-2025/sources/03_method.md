The FSF report states that SFT uses thought traces generated by reasoning models. It does not identify the upstream prompts or tasks, trace-generator identities or checkpoints, trace schema, sampling settings, selection rule, human involvement, source rights, or released examples. It reports that RL on thought traces includes length penalties and that, with minor exceptions, other rewards are computed without reference to thoughts. Reward equations, reward weights, verifiers, labelers, calibration, aggregation, algorithm, optimizer, schedule, rollout generation, and update rules are unknown. The report therefore supports SFT and RL as high-level training-use claims, but not RLVR or a reproducible feedback contract.

The report also describes safety query filters, input filtering and processing, other guardrails, and red-team feedback used to improve mitigation coverage. It does not disclose the data-selection rules, thresholds, yields, or training linkage of those mechanisms. For cyber evaluation, a custom controller runs a multi-stage thinking and tool-use loop, retries parsing or validation errors up to five times, and uses model- and task-specific tools. Gemini 3 Pro is evaluated with 75 attempts and 40-step challenges; these are evaluation settings, not post-training rollout counts or a deployed inference budget.
