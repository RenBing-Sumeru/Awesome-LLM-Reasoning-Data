For Track 12, this Card supplies a careful comparison point for frontier-report disclosure. It lets readers ask whether a report names the origin and format of a post-training object, distinguishes a reward claim from a reward implementation, separates evaluation scaffolding from training infrastructure, and exposes the budgets and affordances that can affect an agentic safety result.

Use the Card to compare disclosure boundaries across frontier reports and to identify requested evidence for a data, feedback, or audit ledger. Do not use it to reproduce Gemini 3 Pro post-training, infer undisclosed data provenance or rights, label the procedure as RLVR, or convert the cyber-evaluation configuration into a training rollout or product inference claim.
