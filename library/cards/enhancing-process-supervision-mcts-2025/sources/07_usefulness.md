For Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data curation, this paper supplies a concrete schema for training-time search-generated supervision. A reusable record would preserve the problem and answer source, model and iteration, partial prefix, every expanded next step, sampling parameters, full terminal continuation, answer-check result, path, visit count, cumulative reward, relative-score components, zero-score exclusions, highest-UCB selection, stopping reason, and the tuple actually consumed by training. Training-time search cost should remain separate from any inference-time budget.

Such records could support process-supervised SFT, analysis of search-derived labels, comparisons with binary preference construction, and audits of whether improvement comes from more rollouts, the selection rule, the weighting objective, or the updated generator. They could also help test score stability across policies and quantify the loss of information caused by retaining only nonzero-score steps. These uses remain prospective because the paper does not release the records or a complete reproduction protocol.
