The closest baseline is Kvasir-VQA with approximately 58,849 mostly atomic QA pairs. Kvasir-VQA-x1 changes the reusable target by composing co-occurring clinical facts into 159,549 complexity-labeled questions, naturalizing their answers, retaining original-QA provenance, and pairing the release with reproducible perturbation scripts and expert validation. This is a direction signal because another team can directly fine-tune a VLM or build a curriculum from the public records. The underlying 6,500 images, source annotations, LLM rewriting, LoRA, standard VQA metrics, and weak image augmentations are inherited rather than individually novel.
