The contribution is not a new process verifier. It makes the normally hidden unit of RLVR data selection unusually small and inspectable: a selected prompt/label pair, a historical-variance selection heuristic, repeated on-policy rollouts, and an answer-level reward. The work also compares this minimal object with DSR-sub and a MATH training-set baseline.

Its disclosure value comes from releasing data, code, checkpoints, and experiment logs alongside the paper. That transparency makes it possible to audit a minimal RLVR recipe while still identifying what remains unavailable: source-level provenance, complete leakage controls, and exact verifier edge behavior.
