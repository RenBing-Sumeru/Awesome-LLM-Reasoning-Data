The NeurIPS 2025 proceedings report that one selected example raises Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B from 36.0% to 73.6% on MATH500 and from 17.6% to 35.7% on the average of six math benchmarks in the reported setup. The proceedings also report that two examples reach 74.8% MATH500 and 36.6% average, and that GRPO/PPO and several base models were tested.

The paper reports that policy-gradient loss is the primary contributor in its ablations and that appropriate entropy loss can improve its reported one-shot behavior. It also reports post-saturation generalization and cross-category outcomes. These are measurements and interpretations from the paper, not guarantees that the released data or code will reproduce the same values in another environment.
