For Track 12, the report gives a concrete comparison surface for multimodal post-training disclosure: source categories, data-repair operations, mixed mode tokens, stage curriculum, sample/token totals, CUA and safety coverage, and the distinction between released weights and unavailable training artifacts. It is useful when asking whether a report has supplied enough information to audit synthetic augmentation, correction, selection, feedback, and rights.

The Card should support comparisons with other frontier reports by retaining the asymmetry between disclosed structure and undisclosed instances. It does not classify the model's compute-efficient performance claims as a Track 8 result. Its scope is the evidence needed to decide what can actually be reused, reproduced, or audited from a data and feedback perspective.
