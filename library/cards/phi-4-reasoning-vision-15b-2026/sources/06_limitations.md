The full training data is not released. Public sources are listed, but their selected subsets, transformations, mixture weights, licenses after modification, and train/validation membership are not. Internal Microsoft and targeted-acquisition data are materially undisclosed. The model card refers readers to an unfilled RRRR_nnnn data-card label rather than an accessible full data card.

Data repair and verification cannot be independently audited. The report names GPT-4o and o4-mini, manual review, and occasional verification or majority voting, but does not release prompts, model versions, sampling, thresholds, correction rates, false-accept/false-reject rates, or rejected records. No contamination protocol or measurements are disclosed. The official Hugging Face model card and GitHub README also contain a faulty citation to arXiv:2511.19663, which is Fara-7B rather than this model's report; the correct primary record is arXiv:2603.03975.

Microsoft notes that the learned reasoning/direct-response switch can be suboptimal, the 20/80 mixture may not fit every domain, and detailed visual understanding remains limited. The model card further warns about English-centric performance, bias, unreliable or fabricated outputs, inappropriate content, and unsuitable high-risk uses.
