Phi-4-reasoning-vision-15B combines the Phi-4-Reasoning language backbone with a SigLIP-2 vision encoder through a mid-fusion MLP. Microsoft reports about 200B multimodal training tokens and three stages: MLP image-text alignment, full-model single-image instruction tuning, then long-context, multi-image, and responsible-AI specialization. The report gives stage sample and token totals of 2.0M/1.4B, 62.8M/188.5B, and 3.2M/12B, respectively.

The model is trained with SFT on a hybrid mixture. Reasoning examples contain a think control token, a chain of thought, and a final answer; direct-response examples begin with a nothink control token. Reasoning data is about 20% of the mixture. Data construction combines filtered and repaired public data with Microsoft internal data and targeted acquisitions, then adds regenerated captions/answers and programmatic image-text transformations. The release does not make the records, exact mixture, or complete quality-control stack available.
