This is not a data release, but it is unusually informative as a frontier construction report. It combines a stage-level multimodal SFT recipe, explicit source-family disclosure, concrete sample/token totals, qualitative data triage, model-assisted repair, image-seeded synthetic transformations, an ablation on mathematics versus computer-use data, and a disclosed 20/80 reasoning/non-reasoning mixture.

Its Track 12 contribution is the contrast between those useful interfaces and the evidence still absent for reuse. The report identifies source families and transformations, but not the actual selected rows, correction prompts, teacher outputs, source weights, rejection yields, detailed rights, contamination checks, or a full data card. It should therefore be compared as a disclosure ledger rather than treated as a complete public recipe.
