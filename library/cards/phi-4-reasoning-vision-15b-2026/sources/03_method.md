Stage 1 trains only the cross-modal MLP on clean image-captioning data while the vision encoder and language model are frozen. Stage 2 trains all components on single-image instruction data spanning VQA, mathematics and science reasoning, grounding, captioning, OCR, and computer use. Stage 3 continues full-model training on long-document, multi-image, sequential-image, and responsible-AI data. Microsoft reports AdamW, bf16 mixed precision, DeepSpeed ZeRO-1, and one epoch in every stage, but not full learning-rate values or reproducible configurations.

For quality control, reviewers classify datasets as excellent, wrong-answer or poor-caption, low-quality-question, low-quality-image, or formatting/logical-error cases. Excellent data is largely retained; some incorrect answers/captions are regenerated with GPT-4o and o4-mini, which are also used where appropriate in verification or majority-voting pipelines; high-error datasets and fundamentally flawed images are excluded. The report also describes image-seeded caption/VQA generation, detailed math/science image descriptions, scrambled/caption-matching multi-image examples, sequential screenshot changes, and prompt diversification. Exact prompts, teacher allocation, thresholds, error rates, and yields are undisclosed.
