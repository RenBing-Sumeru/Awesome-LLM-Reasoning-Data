Open-weight multimodal releases can disclose aggregate tokens, model weights, and an appealing training narrative without making their post-training data pipeline reproducible. For a reasoning model, provenance, prompt formats, generated traces, corrections, filters, verifier behavior, retained and rejected records, rights, and contamination controls determine whether a reported recipe can be independently audited or reused.

Phi-4-reasoning-vision-15B is valuable for Track 12 because Microsoft discloses substantially more stage-level construction information than a typical model announcement: a three-stage SFT curriculum, data-quality interventions, public source families, a mixed reasoning/direct-response format, and safety data. The same report retains material boundaries around internal and acquired data, source weights, generated records, verification details, and audit artifacts. This Card records both sides of that disclosure rather than treating public weights or aggregate counts as a released dataset.
