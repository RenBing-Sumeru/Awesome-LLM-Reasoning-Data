The official report directly supports the 1.5M SFT count, teacher identities at the family level, two sample formats, expert-model generation, high-temperature RL, rejection sampling, human verification for non-reasoning data, two-epoch SFT, rule/model reward split, reward rationales, GRPO, and self-feedback by voting. It also reports extensive evaluations and an ablation in which R1-distilled math/code data improves task scores while increasing response length. Those results demonstrate reported behavior and a length-quality trade-off; they do not establish that the unreleased data are unbiased, uncontaminated, or reusable. The official repository and Hugging Face page verify released model artifacts, not the training corpus.
