The most useful contribution for reasoning-data research is the intermediate expert pipeline: long R1 traces are not copied directly into the final corpus. Instead, domain experts learn R1 reflection/verification patterns alongside original responses, then produce shorter candidates that pass rejection sampling. The reward side similarly separates deterministic rule feedback from learned judgment and records rationales for preference rewards. This makes DeepSeek-V3 a concrete example of controlling teacher-trace style and length rather than treating distillation as simple imitation.
