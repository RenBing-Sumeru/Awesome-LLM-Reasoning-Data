This Card is useful for comparing direct long-CoT distillation with expert-mediated trace construction. It identifies reusable record types—original responses, R1 responses, reflection prompts, expert rollouts, rejection decisions, rule outcomes, reward rationales, and GRPO groups—and shows where an audit would require missing manifests and logs. It also cautions practitioners to treat the released checkpoint, reported training recipe, and unreleased data as three different evidence layers.
