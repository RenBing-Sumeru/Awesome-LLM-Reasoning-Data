Training uses DeepScaleR's 40K mathematics problems assembled from named AIME, AMC, Omni-Math, and STILL components. The frozen reference policy is sampled 16 times per problem to estimate its expected binary correctness. During on-policy training, mode-controlled sampling creates balanced Thinking and NoThinking groups. The reward is answer-level 0/1 mathematical correctness; the NoThinking indicator is determined by the first output control token. The constrained advantage favors direct answers only when their accuracy gap from Thinking and the reference is small enough.

The paper reports DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B and 7B bases, one epoch or 314 optimization steps in VeRL, response lengths up to 16,384 tokens, and evaluation on GSM8K, MATH500, and AIME 2024. A code repository and model collection are public. Complete raw response groups, reference estimates, rejected rollouts, and a record-level training manifest are not documented as released.
