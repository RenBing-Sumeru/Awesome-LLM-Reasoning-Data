AdaptThink is useful for constructing compute-routing records. An auditable record should retain source problem ID, reference-model samples and pass-rate estimate, forced mode, sampled response, normalized answer, correctness, importance weight, constrained advantage, chosen first token, and policy revision. Such records enable analysis of whether shorter outputs correspond to genuine easy cases or reward artifacts. Per-item rollouts, reward ledgers, and routing decisions are not completely released, so their availability remains unknown; benchmark results cannot be used to infer their record-level quality.
