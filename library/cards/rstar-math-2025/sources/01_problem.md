The authoritative publication is the ICML 2025 paper in PMLR 267, supplemented by the official Microsoft `rStar-math` branch and the two repository-linked Hugging Face releases. The paper addresses a concrete construction problem: how to turn a large pool of answer-keyed mathematics problems into step-by-step supervision and a search-guiding process model while repeatedly replacing the generator with a smaller, newly trained policy.

The reported starting pool contains 747K word problems, primarily MetaMath and competition-level NuminaMath, plus GPT-4-generated variants seeded from 7.5K MATH-train and 3.6K AMC-AIME training problems. The construction object is richer than a final answer: a problem/reference-answer pair roots an MCTS tree whose nodes contain a natural-language step embedded in Python comments, cumulative code, an execution observation, visits, a Q-value, and potentially a terminal answer. Correct root-to-leaf trajectories become SFT examples; candidates sharing a prefix become positive/negative PPM comparisons.

This work belongs in **Data Construction and Open Release Recipes** because it specifies prompt sourcing, search expansion, two programmatic feedback signals, Q-based selection, four rounds of policy/PPM retraining, and public packaging. It does not release a replayable copy of the full recipe state. The public SFT table contains 1,188,842 `query`/`response` rows, and the PPM table contains 1,407,399 rows with `prompt`, `neg`, `pos`, `neg_count`, and `pos_count`; neither table preserves the source IDs, trees, visits, Q-values, round IDs, seeds, or realized budgets used to produce it.

The complete bilingual Card reaches `L4_chinese_review_ready`. This level means that the English and Chinese review material is complete and ready for human annotation; it does not establish paper-exact reproducibility. Citation and artifact existence are verified; decontamination, upstream license reconciliation, raw-search lineage, trained checkpoints, and an immutable paper-matched run manifest remain unknown or unavailable.
