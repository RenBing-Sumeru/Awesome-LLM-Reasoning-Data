LLM judges are increasingly used instead of humans, but high correlation on one task does not establish reliability for factuality, reasoning, dialogue, creative generation, and subjective quality. Agreement can depend on data type, annotator expertise, and whether text is human- or model-generated, while model updates reduce reproducibility.

The study aggregates twenty human-labelled NLP evaluation tasks, compares eleven LLM judges under common prompting, and analyses the conditions under which they approximate humans.
