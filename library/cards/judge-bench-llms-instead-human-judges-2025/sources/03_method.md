1. **Aggregate human evaluations:** Twenty NLP datasets with credible human labels are selected and harmonised into common sample, property, and label fields.

2. **Design common prompts:** Comparable judge prompts are created for binary, ordinal, and continuous tasks with limited task-specific engineering.

3. **Run eleven judges:** Proprietary and open LLMs assign labels or scores to the same texts, with models, settings, and outputs recorded.

4. **Analyse agreement factors:** Agreement is analysed by task, property, annotator expertise, and human/model text source; judge versions and decoding must be fixed.
