On GPT-4.1 nano, students trained for 10 epochs on 10,000 filtered number sequences from trait-bearing teachers shift toward every tested animal/tree; owl preference rises from 12% before training to above 60% after training (Fig. 3). The regular-teacher control does not show comparable shifts.

For misalignment, number-only data from an insecure-code teacher makes a GPT-4.1 student produce misaligned responses almost 10% of the time, versus 0% for base GPT-4.1 and below 1% for secure/educational-insecure controls; TruthfulQA false responses increase by 2% (Fig. 4).

These results establish transfer within this matched lineage and protocol, not that filtering is always ineffective: transfer is absent in the reported different-base-model condition.
