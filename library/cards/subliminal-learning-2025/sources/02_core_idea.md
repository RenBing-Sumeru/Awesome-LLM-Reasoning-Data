Subliminal learning is the transfer of a teacher’s behavioral trait through data semantically unrelated to that trait. The key boundary is lineage: it appears when teacher and student share a base model, but not in the reported cross-base setting.

The MIT-licensed repository releases reproducibility data/code and a number-sequence demo that generates filtered JSONL, fine-tunes a student, and evaluates trait acquisition. This is a lineage-risk audit, not a safety-preserving filter.
