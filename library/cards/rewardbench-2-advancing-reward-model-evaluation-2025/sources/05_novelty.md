Closest prior work usually uses general preference pairs, final-answer correctness, or one holistic score without data specifically designed for the key failures and usage conditions of reward-model benchmarking. This paper changes the feedback object and evaluation contract by centering RewardBench 2 and placing task input, candidate response, and auditable evidence in one record, with labels produced through new human prompts, heterogeneous model responses, and multi-skill preference verification. The contribution is the combination of dataset construction, fine-grained organization, and practical use rather than merely another model or pairwise format.
