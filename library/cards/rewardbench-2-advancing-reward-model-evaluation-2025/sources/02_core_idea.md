The central artifact is RewardBench 2, containing 1,865 new prompts with chosen and rejected response pairs. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use new human prompts, heterogeneous model responses, and multi-skill preference verification for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is difficult reward-model evaluation linked to Best-of-N and PPO outcomes.
