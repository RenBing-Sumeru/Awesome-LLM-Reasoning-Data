After the first RewardBench release, performance became saturated, some items may have entered training data, and older model responses no longer represent the hardest failures of current systems. A reward model can therefore score highly yet fail on fine-grained preferences, Best-of-N selection, or PPO training.

RewardBench 2 uses newly written human prompts, responses from heterogeneous strong models, and multi-skill preference verification to restore difficulty and test the relationship between offline ranking and downstream effectiveness.
