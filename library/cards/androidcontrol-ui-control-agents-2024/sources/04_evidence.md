The repository's data card reports more than 15,000 demonstrations over 833 apps and 40 app categories, and lists the exact TFRecord fields: episode id, goal, screenshots, accessibility trees, screenshot sizes, actions, and step instructions. The public bucket and split file are the key artifact evidence for reuse. The paper reports scaling and generalization findings, but instance-level evidence is still reference-action agreement over stored demonstrations rather than real-time success in current apps. Evidence is bounded by the public GCS snapshot, split-file integrity, parser versions, and whether a result uses high-level goals, low-level step instructions, or both.
