AndroidControl asks how the amount and distribution of UI-control data affect agents that must generalize to new Android apps and tasks. The primary sources are the arXiv paper and the Google Research AndroidControl repository; the arXiv record states NeurIPS 2024 acceptance. The decision boundary is offline Android UI-control demonstrations and app/task generalization evaluation, not a live phone environment with fresh task verification. A data object is a TFRecord episode containing a high-level goal, screenshots, accessibility trees, step instructions, and JSON actions. Its atlas value is that it exposes data scale, app coverage, and out-of-distribution split design as first-class variables for GUI agents.
