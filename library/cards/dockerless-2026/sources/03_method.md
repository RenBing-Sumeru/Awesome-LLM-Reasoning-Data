Verifier construction starts from execution-labeled candidate patches drawn from SWE-Gym and Multi-SWE-RL. For each `(issue, golden patch, candidate patch)` tuple, GLM-5 runs the same three-part workflow used at inference: question generation, `K` parallel evidence-answer trajectories, and a final judgment. A sequence is retained only when the teacher verdict matches the held-out-test label. Answer traces shorter than 4 turns or longer than 30 turns, malformed exchanges, and interrupted exchanges are removed; the negative-to-positive ratio is capped at 4:1. Candidate-patch text and rendered Q&A context are each truncated to 10,000 characters. The resulting corpus covers 3.7K unique issues, but the exact number of retained Q+A+Judge sequences and the train/validation sizes are unknown.

All retained sequences train one Qwen3.5-9B backbone with next-token cross-entropy rather than separately annotated action labels. Dockerless uses AdamW, learning rate `1e-5` with cosine decay to `1e-6`, warmup ratio `0.05`, weight decay `0.01`, batch size 256, and maximum sequence length 32,768; the reported best checkpoint is selected at 150 steps on an undisclosed held-out validation split. Exact GLM-5 and Qwen3.5 revisions, teacher decoding settings, and verifier-generation temperature are unknown.

For downstream post-training, OpenHands runs SWE-Rebench-v2 tasks in `ubuntu:jammy-20260109` with the repository checked out at its base commit but without repository-specific dependencies or a test runner. SFT collection samples 16K rollouts at temperature `1.0`. Each final patch is scored in two independent Dockerless passes; failed passes are discarded, remaining scores are averaged, and the globally highest-ranked 4K are used for 3 SFT epochs. GRPO starts from Dockerless-SFT-9B, samples `G=8` rollouts per issue at temperature `1.0`, uses the same `M=2` scoring procedure, and trains for 50 steps with a 150-turn rollout cap, actor learning rate `2e-6`, batch/minibatch 64, clipping `[0.2,0.27]`, and entropy and KL coefficients both set to 0.
