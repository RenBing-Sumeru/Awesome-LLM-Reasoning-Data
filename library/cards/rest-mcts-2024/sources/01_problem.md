The official NeurIPS 2024 paper and DOI record establish ReST-MCTS* as a self-training method for mathematics and science reasoning. It targets a concrete defect in answer-filtered self-training: a trajectory can reach the reference answer while containing an invalid plan, unsupported intermediate deductions, or an accidental recovery. A terminal match is useful feedback, but it is not dense evidence about the process that produced the answer.

Direct human labeling of every reasoning step is expensive. The paper asks whether answer-keyed questions plus repeated tree search can instead yield two reusable data products without new human step annotations: verified-complete trajectories for policy SFT and scalar targets for partial solutions for process value-model training.

One construction episode contains a question, reference answer, a tree whose nodes are partial solutions with visit counts and learned values, generated next-step edges, completed candidate trajectories, terminal-answer verdicts, and selected paths. This makes the paper relevant to **Data Construction and Open Release Recipes**: its contribution is a feedback-driven pipeline for generating and selecting data, not merely an inference-time MCTS result. It does not solve independent step verification, decontamination, or general feedback for tasks without oracle answers.

The Card reaches L4 because the official paper and appendix, NeurIPS record, code repository, project page, and public Hub datasets/checkpoint were checked. The evidence boundary remains partial at the release level: public rows are flattened two-column policy or value examples, not raw searchable trees with failed branches and full verdict provenance.
