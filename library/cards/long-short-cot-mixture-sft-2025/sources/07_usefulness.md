For `instruction_demonstration_rationale_data`, this paper is a useful construction baseline: hold questions fixed, retain a long rationale, produce a short constrained counterpart, and test long-only, short-only, and mixture SFT separately. A responsible implementation should attach a stable source ID and pair ID; record the original trace, executed rewrite prompt, model revision, decoding settings, rewrite output, verifier result, and rejection reason; then release both successful and failed rewrites.

It can support three concrete studies: (1) a pair-level audit measuring answer preservation, step coverage, compression, and new-error rate; (2) a mixture-ratio and instruction-mode factorial experiment that separates data effects from inference-prompt effects; and (3) a contamination and license audit back to the original question corpus. A verifier may be a programmatic answer check where possible plus a calibrated trace-faithfulness review; neither is part of this paper's released workflow.

Reuse class: **reading and audit reference; not ready for direct training reuse without further verification**. The publicly accessible data can be inspected as a 1,984-row JSON release, but direct training use requires an independent decision on upstream rights, source contamination, pair lineage, rewrite correctness, and the intended domain. The released weights are an artifact for evaluation/inspection, not evidence that the data recipe is complete.
