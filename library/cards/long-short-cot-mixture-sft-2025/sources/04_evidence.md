- **Primary comparison.** On MATH500/AIME24/GPQA, the paper reports s1.1-32B long-only accuracy of 92.4%/53.3%/59.1% and s1-mix-32B balanced-mode accuracy of 94.6%/60.0%/61.1%. Its reported response lengths are 12,351.4/53,455.6/56,040.7 versus 8,648.7/40,251.3/21,995.7, respectively. The stated weighted average length reduction is 47.61%. These are author-reported benchmark results for one 32B student and prompt setting, not a direct measurement of released data quality. (Table 1; §3.2)
- **Construction-scale evidence.** Appendix B reports 1,000 rows and average text length 29,667.49 for s1K-1.1, versus 1,984 rows and 17,406.11 for s1K-mix. The dataset viewer independently displays one default/train split with 1.98k rows and fields `system`, `instruction`, `input`, and `output`; it currently labels the release JSON, English, and MIT. It does not publish the long-short pair map or an audit of rewrite correctness. (Appendix B; official dataset viewer)
- **Rewriting-strategy ablation.** On MATH500/AIME24/GPQA, the structure-preserved mixture scores 94.6/60.0/61.1%, compared with direct rewriting at 85.4/33.3/53.5% and ThinkTwice at 91.0/58.1/43.3%. The paper's interpretation is conditional on its rewriter, prompts, and training setup; the table does not evaluate individual rewrite faithfulness. (Table 2)
- **Mixing ablation.** Long-only is 92.4/53.3/59.1%, short-only 82.6/16.7/49.0%, and mixture 94.6/60.0/61.1% on the same three benchmarks. This supports a reported interaction in this experiment, not a general optimal mixture ratio; the authors did not vary the ratio. (Table 3; Limitations)
- **Prompt-mode ablation.** For s1-mix-32B, brief/detailed/balanced MATH500 accuracy is 81.0/92.6/94.6%, with reported lengths 2,963.9/11,162.3/8,648.7. Because this comparison changes the inference instruction, it cannot attribute balanced-mode performance solely to the mixture data. (Table 4)
