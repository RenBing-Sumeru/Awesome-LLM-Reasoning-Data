- **Unverified rewriting.** The prompt says not to add information and to preserve the answer, but no automated or human audit checks those properties. A short trace can introduce a false step, drop a necessary qualification, or preserve a wrong upstream answer. This is a curator inference from the stated feedback contract.
- **Lineage and release gap.** The public 1,984-row split lacks immutable long/short links, the identities of 16 excluded inputs, rejected outputs, source IDs, rewriter revision, sampling logs, and merge manifest. Current dataset/model pages are MIT-labelled, but the paper does not resolve upstream s1K-1.1/DeepSeek-R1 trace permissions, consent, or question provenance.
- **Reproducibility gap.** Appendix C gives several SFT hyperparameters but not epoch count, complete LlamaFactory configuration, model revision, rewriter decoding, seeds, or evaluation decoding. The linked GitHub repository and model card are marked under development and do not supply a verified training pipeline.
- **Experimental boundary.** Results cover one Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct student, 1,000 upstream examples, and MATH500, AIME24, and GPQA. The authors explicitly did not study larger scales or long/short mixture ratios. Benchmark gains therefore do not establish data quality or general transfer.
- **Confounded efficiency claim.** The balanced-mode evaluation changes the inference prompt as well as using mixture SFT. It is unsafe to attribute the reported length/accuracy trade-off solely to the short traces; a factorial study separating data and prompt effects is absent.
- **Contamination unknown.** No decontamination or benchmark-overlap procedure is reported. Since the source question manifest is undisclosed, contamination cannot be checked from the release alone.
