Under average budgets 1.5, 2.0, and 3.0, the paper reports AdaCompute-GBM outperforming fixed, random, and prompt-length heuristic baselines across all four model-dataset settings. At budget 3.0 on MATH, the reported absolute gains over Fixed-b are 5.8 points for DeepSeek-V3, 5.2 for GPT-4o-mini, and 6.4 for Qwen2.5-7B; DeepSeek-V3 on GSM8K gains 1.9 points. The learned policy reports 91-99% oracle-label imitation accuracy and task-accuracy gaps of 0.4-1.4 points from the oracle. Three-seed means and standard deviations are provided.

Classifier ablations show tree-based GBM and Random Forest using budgets more similarly to the oracle than linear, RBF-SVM, or small-MLP alternatives in the pooled setup. Difficulty analysis reports the largest self-consistency gains on level-3 MATH questions, supporting the claimed inverted-U allocation pattern.

These results evaluate allocation on small sampled benchmark subsets and an empirical utility table. They do not prove that the oracle labels transfer under model updates, API drift, new domains, or different cost metrics, and they are not evidence that a reusable trace dataset was released. The public repository supports the pipeline implementation, but the response and label artifacts underlying the tables were not confirmed in the repository tree.
