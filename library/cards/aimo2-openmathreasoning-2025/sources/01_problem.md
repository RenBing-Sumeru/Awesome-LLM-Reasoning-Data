The primary publication is the 39-page arXiv report of NVIDIA's winning AIMO-2 submission. A retitled version, *Scaling Mathematical Reasoning through Data, Tools, and Generative Selection*, appeared as a poster at the 2nd AI for Math Workshop @ ICML 2025; it was not an ICML main-conference paper. The official NVIDIA Hugging Face release, NeMo Skills recipe/documentation, competition account, and pinned code and dataset revisions provide the artifact record.

The work addresses an end-to-end construction problem: how to turn mathematical forum discussions into long-reasoning CoT supervision, Python-interleaved Tool-Integrated Reasoning (TIR), and generative solution-selection traces, then use them in large-scale answer-level SFT and test-time selection. Released records share nine fields: `expected_answer`, `problem_type`, `problem_source`, `generation_model`, `pass_rate_72b_tir`, `problem`, `generated_solution`, `inference_mode`, and `used_in_kaggle`.

The scale requires two non-interchangeable ledgers. In the paper's early pipeline, 620K AoPS discussions become 580K extracted problems, 550K after unsuitable-format removal, and **540K after LLM-based decontamination** (paper §2.1, Table 1). The official data card later states that 540K was measured near the beginning of the pipeline and overstates the final usable release. The pinned public CoT/TIR material corresponds to **306K unique solution-bearing problems**, and the release separately includes **193,170 problem-only rows**. The hosted rows total 5,678,317: 3,201,061 CoT, 1,718,466 TIR, 565,620 GenSelect, and 193,170 additional problems.

This belongs in **Data Construction and Open Release Recipes** because it discloses source extraction, multi-teacher sampling, answer judging, iterative TIR generation, GenSelect construction, SFT scaffolding, and release packaging. It is not process-supervision data: acceptance normally attaches to final-answer equivalence or correct candidate choice, even when the target string contains a long trace or code.

The Card reaches L4 bilingual-review readiness through the paper and verified official artifacts. Reuse still requires caution: a pipeline bug lost 137K proof questions; the exact original AoPS scraping implementation was internal; accepted/rejected candidates, judge and decontamination logs, structured tool execution records, and row-level forum rights are not released; hosted data and current documentation can drift.
