**Source acquisition and rights.** The exact original AoPS scraper and immutable source-post manifest are not public. Current documentation points to AoPS-Instruct as a similar input and explicitly says it was not used in the original work. The release-level CC BY 4.0 label does not supply row-level forum-post provenance, author attribution, consent, deletion status, or platform-policy analysis. These missing facts can affect lawful and ethical reuse even when NVIDIA's derived release has a permissive label.

**Answer and judge failure.** Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct equivalence is an LLM judgment, not formal answer normalization or proof checking. For converted proofs and no-answer problems, majority teacher output becomes ground truth; correlated teacher errors can therefore be promoted into acceptance targets. GenSelect retains the choice when its selected candidate has a correct answer, but does not independently verify the comparison rationale. Appendix examples demonstrate accepted code/prose inconsistencies.

**TIR observability.** Public TIR rows are interleaved strings, not normalized state/action/observation episodes. They omit sandbox version, call boundaries, exit status, exception, timeout, stdout truncation, and step-specific verifier labels. Presence of executable-looking code does not establish that the code ran in the released configuration, supported the prose, or generalized beyond the observed call.

**Count and pipeline instability.** The paper's 540K is not the released solved-problem count; the official correction is 306K. A bug loses 137K proof questions, and a fixed released manifest of their recovered solutions is unavailable. Stage-0 and later TIR use different filters. Without problem IDs and complete accepted/rejected ledgers, it is difficult to reconstruct which items survive each stage or to measure selection bias.

**Decontamination and release drift.** An LLM-based benchmark comparison exists, but matched pairs, judge outputs, thresholds, and final decisions are absent. The hosted dataset and current reconstruction docs can change after the paper; the checked code also postdates the paper and is not an immutable original-run manifest. Reuse must pin revision `d3d08664755704f422af97d43a7ff0ded4bd95df` and commit `74b8649734a6ecc2d3beca89311e1a5e02da48fa`.

**Experimental scope.** Public-leaderboard variance, limited gains for smaller models, longer outputs, and filter regressions constrain generalization. Strong benchmark or competition performance cannot establish row-level correctness, rights, decontamination, or transfer to different models and objectives.
