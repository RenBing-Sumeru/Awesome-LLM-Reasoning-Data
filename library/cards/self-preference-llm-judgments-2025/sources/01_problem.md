LLM judges may favor their own answers, but ordinary self-win-rate measurements confuse genuine response quality with bias. This can make automated evaluation and reward signals unreliable.

The paper introduces gold judgments and a Difference from Gold Bias (DBG) score, then audits model size, post-training, style, and reasoning models. It is an evaluator-bias measurement study, not a reward-model training method.

The resulting question is whether a judge favors its own answer after holding response quality as constant as the available gold panel allows.
