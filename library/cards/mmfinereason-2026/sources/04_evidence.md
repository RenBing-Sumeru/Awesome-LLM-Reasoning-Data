**Main model comparison.** Claim: the filtered corpus closes part of the multimodal reasoning gap at moderate model scale. Controlled setup: MFR-8B is obtained by SFT from the Qwen3-VL-8B family and is compared across the paper's reasoning benchmark suite with Qwen3-VL thinking baselines. MFR-8B averages 75.7, versus 72.5 for Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking and 74.5 for Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B. Boundary: the aggregate combines heterogeneous tasks, so per-task regressions should still be inspected.

**Data-efficiency comparison.** Claim: difficulty selection preserves most of the gain with much less data. Controlled setup: models use the same pipeline while training data changes from the filtered 1.8M corpus to the 123K hard subset. The 123K variant averages 73.3 while using about 7% as many examples. Boundary: difficulty is defined by Qwen3-VL-4B-Thinking and may select a different frontier for another student.
