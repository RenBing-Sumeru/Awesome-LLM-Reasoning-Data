Prior multimodal corpora often emphasize either scale, teacher-generated long CoT, or benchmark-specific hard examples. MMFineReason combines source normalization, long-CoT distillation, explicit quality filtering, and student-relative difficulty selection in one open pipeline, then measures the trade-off between a 1.8M corpus and a 123K subset. The individual tools are established; the new signal is their integrated, scale-tested data recipe.
