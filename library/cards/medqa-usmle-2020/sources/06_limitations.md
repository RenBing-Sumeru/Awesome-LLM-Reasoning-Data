Correctness is only answer-key correctness for exam items. The benchmark does not validate clinical deployment behavior, patient-specific reasoning, guideline freshness, or explanation fidelity.

The dataset is public, so contamination is a major risk for modern LLM evaluation. Results can change with option ordering, prompt format, chain-of-thought policy, auxiliary textbook retrieval, and split choice. Licensing and redistribution terms must be checked at the artifact level before using the records as training data or publishing derived subsets.
