The selection signal combines a binary tool-call correctness outcome with a penalty on the number of sentences in the optional reasoning block. The length penalty is scaled by an online solve-rate estimate, so easy contexts are penalized more for unnecessary reasoning than difficult contexts. Ada-RS-DPO converts pairwise samples into chosen/rejected examples, while Ada-RS-DAPO independently retains members of an on-policy group after standardizing rewards within that group. This is a sampling and data-selection contract; it is not evidence that every retained reasoning step is valid.
