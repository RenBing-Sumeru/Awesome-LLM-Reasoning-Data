Evidence is limited to one internal simulated e-commerce domain and Qwen3-8B, so cross-domain and scale generality is unknown. Per-step tool-call accuracy may not represent end-to-end completion of a multi-turn user goal. The paper reports that adaptive rejection sampling without the auxiliary DPO NLL stabilization can yield poor accuracy and an always-think policy. Data provenance, split construction, decontamination, rollout count, teacher identity, environment implementation, and data/environment licenses are undisclosed. The absence of released candidate groups also prevents independent replay of the acceptance decisions.
