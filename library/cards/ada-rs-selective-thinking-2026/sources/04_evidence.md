The official arXiv paper describes the training and evaluation counts, the adaptive reward, and the two optimizer integrations. The evaluation set contains 2,510 tool calls from 367 conversations across 48 tasks and eight personas. These reported counts and benchmark results document the experimental recipe, but they do not establish corpus quality, rights, or replayability. No official code, dataset, rollout trace, environment, model checkpoint, or project release was identified in the checked primary sources; artifact verification therefore remains false.
