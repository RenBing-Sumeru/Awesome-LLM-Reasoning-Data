Prior speech reward work either scores a narrow acoustic target or fine-tunes an audio judge for a single-turn task. UniSRM changes the supervised object: one model must reason over multiple dimensions and over context, then produce a structured decision across four speech-evaluation settings. RCR-GRPO further moves supervision from only the final label to agreement between each dimension-level rationale and its target. Using a Qwen audio backbone or GRPO itself is not new; the contribution is their unified data/task formulation and consistency reward.
