Under a shared evaluation, off-the-shelf LLMs achieve roughly 27% verified success in Lean, 44% in Verus or Rust, and 82% in Dafny, revealing large effects from verification automation and task distributions. Adding natural-language descriptions does not significantly improve aggregate success. The authors also report rapid progress in pure Dafny verification, from about 68% to 96% over one year. The repository exposes 55,397 experiment outcomes for checks by model, source, and language. However, the three language sets are not parallel translations of identical functions, so 82% versus 27% does not prove that Dafny is intrinsically easier, and overall statistics depend on how non-compiling `issues` are filtered.
