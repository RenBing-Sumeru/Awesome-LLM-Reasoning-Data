Previous work released DafnyBench, FVAPPS, Verina, or VerifiedCogen separately and reported results under different prompts, models, and success definitions, preventing broad comparison. The novelty is not a new verifier but a shared schema, source index, construction scripts, and result table across three formal languages, with non-compiling issues preserved rather than silently removed. It combines 6,174 new tasks with existing benchmarks to study ecosystems, natural-language assistance, and progress over time. It does not, however, align identical algorithms across all three languages.
