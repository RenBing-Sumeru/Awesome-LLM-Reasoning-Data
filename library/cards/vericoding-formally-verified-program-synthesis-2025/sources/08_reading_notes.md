1. **One-sentence position:** This benchmark aggregates 12,504 Dafny, Verus or Rust, and Lean specifications and releases verifier outcomes and construction scripts.

2. **Method takeaway:** Multi-source aggregation, cross-language translation, component normalization, native compilation checks, and 55,397 model experiments form the pipeline.

3. **Data takeaway:** It contains 3,029 Dafny, 2,334 Verus, and 7,141 Lean tasks, including 6,174 new problems; tasks and non-compiling issues must remain separate.

4. **Evidence anchor:** Verified success is about 27% in Lean, 44% in Verus, and 82% in Dafny, while natural-language descriptions add no significant aggregate gain.

5. **Reuse decision:** It suits cross-verifier research and RLVR. Unaligned subsets, weak specifications, and public-task contamination require source-aware recompilation and audits.
