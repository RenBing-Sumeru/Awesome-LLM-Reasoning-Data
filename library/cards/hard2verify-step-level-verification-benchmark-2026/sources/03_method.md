1. **Collect frontier problems:** Select recent, open-ended, difficult mathematical questions that cannot be judged by short answer rules.
2. **Generate candidate proofs:** Use frontier reasoning models to produce complete proofs, retaining successful and failed trajectories from different models and sampling settings.
3. **Review each step:** Mathematical experts assess correctness, sufficiency, and contextual support and annotate the first step that cannot be justified.
4. **Define the evaluation protocol:** Release adjudicated cases and evaluate twenty-nine generative critics and PRMs under a common setup; problem versions and proof sources must be fixed to control contamination.
