The paper evaluates twenty-nine generative critics and PRMs and finds that, with few exceptions, open-source verifiers substantially lag proprietary systems. Increasing verifier compute helps some models but does not remove failures on sufficiency in open proofs. Analysis of self-verification and generation–verification dynamics also shows that a model capable of producing strong proofs is not necessarily reliable at locating another proof’s first error. This supports benchmark difficulty, but not uniqueness of expert labels across all equivalent proofs.
