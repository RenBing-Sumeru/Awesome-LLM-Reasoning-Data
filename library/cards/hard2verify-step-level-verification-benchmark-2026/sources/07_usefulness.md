Hard2Verify can compare proof critics, generative verifiers, and PRMs on correctness, sufficiency, and earliest-error localization, including whether additional reasoning budget truly helps. Formal-math or research-proof systems can also reuse its annotation criteria as a human audit protocol and produce failure-type reports. It is not a large SFT dataset, and final-answer accuracy cannot replace step review; problem contamination should be checked before evaluating public models.
