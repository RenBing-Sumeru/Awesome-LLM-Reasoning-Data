Hard2Verify is a human-annotated step-verification benchmark for open frontier mathematics. Its value comes from recent problems, open-ended proofs, and demanding annotation rather than raw scale. Each instance preserves the problem, model proof, step boundaries, expert judgments, and earliest-error location; annotators assess not only truth but whether a step is sufficiently supported. The benchmark targets frontier proof verification, self-verification, and the effect of additional verifier compute.
