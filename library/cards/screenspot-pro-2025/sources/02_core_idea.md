The contribution is a harder GUI grounding benchmark built from authentic high-resolution professional screens. ScreenSpot-Pro spans 23 applications across five industries and three operating systems, including development, creative software, CAD/engineering, scientific/analytical tools, office software, and OS commons.

The data surface is screenshot-plus-instruction grounding. The feedback contract checks whether the predicted point or region falls inside the annotated target box. The paper also proposes ScreenSeekeR, a search-based grounding method, but the reusable benchmark object is the annotated GUI target set. Closest comparisons are ScreenSpot, SeeClick, OSWorld grounding subtasks, and other GUI grounding datasets. The direction label is high-resolution professional GUI localization.
