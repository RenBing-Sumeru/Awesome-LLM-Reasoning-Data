The paper reports that existing GUI grounding models perform poorly on professional high-resolution screens: the best evaluated model reaches only 18.9% on ScreenSpot-Pro. The proposed ScreenSeekeR search method reaches 48.1% without additional training, showing that narrowing the search space helps but still leaves much unsolved.

Instance-level evidence is the screenshot, instruction, annotated target box, predicted coordinate or box, and localization-success check. The dataset spans 23 applications, five industries, and three operating systems, which supports the claim that the benchmark is not just mobile or web UI grounding. Evidence boundaries include dataset revision, target-box precision, language variant, leaderboard model versions, and whether a method uses extra search calls or planner assistance.
