Correctness is only relative to the stated contract: Offline action/navigation matching evaluates whether the model predicts the expert next action or trajectory step under the benchmark split. Offline matching rewards imitation of recorded behavior, not necessarily live task completion. WebLINX 1.0, 1.1, and BrowserGym-derived variants must be labeled separately.

The paper should not be read as unrestricted real-world reliability. Public artifacts can become training data, service-backed environments can drift, and judge/evaluator implementations can change across releases.
