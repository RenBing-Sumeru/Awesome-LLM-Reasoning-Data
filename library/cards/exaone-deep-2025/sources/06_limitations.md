The data layer is largely undisclosed. There is no source-dataset list, prompt inventory, trace generator or teacher identity, annotator account, preference-label protocol, per-domain count, language mixture, filtering rule, retention rate, split manifest, curriculum, contamination analysis, or item-level lineage. The broad Math/Code/Science/Others labels and aggregate counts cannot substitute for those records. The official model license also cannot be extended to infer rights to the hidden corpus.

The feedback layer is even less specified. SimPER identifies the DPO optimization algorithm, not the origin or correctness of the preference pairs. The designed GRPO variant identifies an RL family, not its reward. No reward model, judge, verifier, executable checker, environment, calibration procedure, rollout count, sampling policy, aggregation method, or terminal predicate is disclosed. The Online-RL stage therefore cannot be audited as reproducible RLVR from these materials.

The released models carry EXAONE AI Model License Agreement 1.1 - NC rather than a permissive open-source license. Its research-only, non-commercial, and revocable terms matter for reuse. LG also warns that the models may produce inappropriate, biased, incorrect, stale, false, or contradictory responses, and the report recommends EXAONE 3.5 Instruct rather than EXAONE Deep for a wider range of practical applications. Reported benchmark gains do not prove the quality, coverage, legality, or cleanliness of the undisclosed training data.
