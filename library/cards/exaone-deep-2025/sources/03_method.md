The base lineage is explicit: each EXAONE Deep size starts from the corresponding instruction-tuned EXAONE 3.5 Instruct model. SFT and DPO records are placed in a shared template with a thought-tag reasoning section and a final answer. The SFT corpus contains about 12B tokens across 1.6M instances. The report's length-distribution figure separates Math, Code, Science, and Others and notes that Code records are longer on average, but it does not provide per-group counts or named sources.

Alongside SFT, the report says DPO uses SimPER on 20K preference instances and Online RL uses a designed GRPO variant on 10K instances; the exact stage schedule or branching is not described. No official artifact checked here specifies whether the preferences are human, model, rule, or outcome generated; how chosen and rejected responses were sampled; or whether SFT and DPO traces share generators. The Online-RL account omits the reward function, judge or verifier, task environment, rollout count, generation settings, calibration, aggregation, and terminal predicate.

The report provides benchmark inference settings—up to 32K generated tokens, temperature 0.6, top_p 0.95, and benchmark-specific repeated sampling—but these are evaluation settings. They should not be copied into `generator`, `rollout_count`, or `temperature` as if they described training-data construction. Training optimizer settings, retention rules, curricula, stage-level splits, checkpoints, and data lineage are also undisclosed.
