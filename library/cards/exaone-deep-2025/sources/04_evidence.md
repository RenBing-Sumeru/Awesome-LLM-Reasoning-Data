The official [arXiv report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12524) supports the EXAONE 3.5 Instruct base lineage, three model sizes, the 1.6M/20K/10K stage counts, about 12B SFT tokens, the thought-tag-plus-final-answer format, SimPER for DPO, and a designed GRPO variant for Online RL. It also states that the models are available for research purposes. The report does not name the training datasets, trace generators, preference labelers, filters, split procedure, or Online-RL reward.

The official [EXAONE Deep repository](https://github.com/LG-AI-EXAONE/EXAONE-Deep) announces the 2.4B, 7.8B, and 32B release and links LG's [official Hugging Face collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/LGAI-EXAONE/exaone-deep). The official [32B model card](https://huggingface.co/LGAI-EXAONE/EXAONE-Deep-32B) exposes downloadable BF16 weights, the EXAONE 3.5 base-model link, inference examples, and LG-provided quantization links. The [LG release article](https://www.lgresearch.ai/blog/view?seq=543) confirms the public model launch.

The paper and model card apply EXAONE AI Model License Agreement 1.1 - NC to the model material. The agreement limits use to research, prohibits commercial use without a separate agreement, and is revocable under its terms. No checked official page presents a downloadable SFT/DPO/RL dataset or licenses those undisclosed records. Benchmark results and model downloadability do not close that evidence gap.
