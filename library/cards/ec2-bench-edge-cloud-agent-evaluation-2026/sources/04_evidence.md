The paper repeats every task-configuration pair three times and reports means plus Pass3, using completion >0.7 as the per-run success threshold. For privacy judgment, it reports 96.9% F1 against adjudicated human labels on 200 sensitivity-unit/context pairs. That is evidence for agreement on the audited sample, not proof of perfect leakage detection or formal privacy protection. Privacy results apply to the 100 annotated tasks, not all 128 without qualification.
