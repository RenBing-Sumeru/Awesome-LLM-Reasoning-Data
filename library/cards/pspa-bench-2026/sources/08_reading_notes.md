Keep three things separate: generic task completion, preference-sensitive progress, and long-term improvement. APR is not PPR; PPR is the metric that directly targets flexible preference nodes.

Read the TDG section and evaluation appendix before comparing model rankings. The ranking only makes sense after understanding how a trace is aligned to a graph and how ties between paths are broken.

The artifact status matters. A paper claim that code/data are available through an anonymous status page is not the same as a durable, versioned dataset with audited license and reproducible runtime.
