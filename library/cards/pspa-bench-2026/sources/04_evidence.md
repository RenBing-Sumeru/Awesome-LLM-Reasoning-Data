The paper reports PSPA-Bench scale as 12,855 personalized instructions over 10 scenarios, 22 apps, and 100 user personas, and evaluates 11 GUI-agent methods. Immediate-objective results show a large gap between no-GUI-context and GUI-aware agents: LLM-Base has APR 0.002 and PPR 0 at low complexity, while +A11y reaches APR 0.625 and PPR 0.586.

The best immediate completion figures are still limited. Mobile-Agent E reaches APR 0.695 and PPR 0.621 at low complexity, and at high complexity has APR 0.468 and PPR 0.435 with CT 716.7 and CPT 1.692. The paper interprets this as an accuracy-efficiency tradeoff. For long-term adaptation, Mobile-Agent E reports positive deltas such as +6.72 APR and +3.68 PPR at medium complexity, while memoryless baselines show little or inconsistent gain.

Instance-level evidence is the trace-to-TDG checklist decision: which fixed and flexible unit instructions were completed. The paper validates LLM-based alignment on 100 traces from four scenarios with three annotators; inter-annotator Fleiss kappa is 0.87, and LLM alignment has overall accuracy 0.93 and Cohen kappa 0.86 against human labels. Evidence boundary: these results depend on synthetic personas, manually built TDGs, the LLM evaluator, app/runtime stability, and the availability of the anonymous artifact.
