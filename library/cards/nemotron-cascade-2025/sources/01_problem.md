
Nemotron-Cascade addresses a concrete systems problem in general-purpose reasoning post-training: math, instruction following, competitive programming, and software repair differ sharply in response length, reward latency, verifier cost, and failure modes. A single blended RL batch therefore couples infrastructure and hyperparameters that are appropriate for different domains. The report proposes handling that heterogeneity by training one domain at a time.

The primary source is arXiv:2512.13607, first submitted on 15 December 2025 and revised as v2 on 27 March 2026. The paper starts from Qwen3-8B-Base or Qwen3-14B-Base, performs two-stage SFT, then applies RLHF, instruction-following RL, Math RL, Code RL, and SWE RL in sequence. The core data object is consequently not one dataset: it is a chain of supervised conversations, preference pairs, RL prompt sets, expected answers or patches, online policy responses, and stage-specific reward signals.

For the atlas, the decision boundary is disclosure rather than leaderboard position. NVIDIA releases final weights, 8B intermediate checkpoints, a 72B reward model, and several SFT/RL datasets in an official collection. This Card asks which artifacts correspond to reported stages, what each feedback contract verifies, and which links remain unknown. Benchmark performance is kept as model evidence and is not used to infer data quality or full reproducibility.
