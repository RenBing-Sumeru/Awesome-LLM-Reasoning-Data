The distinctive contribution is the coupling of synthetic question generation, round-trip formalisation checks, stepwise theorem-prover execution, error-driven retries, and outcome-dependent training allocation. Lean is not used only to score a final answer: its diagnostics help repair formalisation, and the joint result of two candidate answers determines whether the data feeds SFT, DPO, or neither.

Compared with an opaque scalar model judge, the feedback is executable and exposes Verified, False, and Error as different states. Compared with simple verifier filtering, the recipe also retains a verified-versus-false pair as preference supervision. The novelty is procedural; it should not be read as evidence that every Lean-accepted trace is semantically faithful or that the unavailable SIMQA artifact is ready for reuse.
