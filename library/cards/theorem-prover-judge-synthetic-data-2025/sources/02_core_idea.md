TP-as-a-Judge turns theorem-prover outcomes into a data-allocation contract. A question is converted to a CoMAT-style symbolic representation, formalised in Lean, translated back to natural language, and checked for alignment by a second GPT-4o call. Only aligned questions proceed. Two generated solutions are then formalised step by step. Lean returns Verified, False, or Error; Error carries diagnostics into an iterative repair loop.

The pair of final outcomes determines use: Verified/Verified supplies SFT examples, Verified/False supplies a DPO preference pair, False/False is disposed, and unresolved Error cases are excluded. This makes the verifier useful both as a filter and as feedback for repairing the formalisation process. It does not, by itself, prove that the formal statement faithfully represents the original natural-language task.
