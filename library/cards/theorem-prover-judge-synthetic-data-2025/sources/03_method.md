The source prompts are few-shot solutions from GSM8K and MATH. Reverse question answering generates synthetic questions in arithmetic problem solving, algebra, and counting and probability. The pipeline uses the appendix-named snapshots gpt-4o-2024-08-06, gpt-4o-2024-11-20, and the latest GPT-4o chat snapshot; two candidate natural-language solutions are generated for each retained question.

Question processing follows a round trip: original question → CoMAT symbolic form → GPT-4o Lean formalisation → GPT-4o auto-informalisation → a second GPT-4o alignment decision. A failed alignment decision removes the question before answer formalisation. For each remaining answer, the system formalises the natural-language reasoning step by step and executes it in Lean. A refuted step yields False; a non-executable formalisation yields Error and is revised with Lean diagnostics; Algorithm 1 caps this loop at five retries. The prose defines Verified as all steps verifying, although the printed pseudocode appears to break after the first verified step; the unavailable implementation prevents resolution of that discrepancy.

Generation uses temperature 0 and a 3,500-token maximum. Question generation is few-shot, answer generation is zero-shot, and formalisation prompts use 10 examples for GSM8K, 8 for MATH algebra, and 6 for MATH counting and probability. SIMQA contains 3,508 response records: 1,876 GSM8K-derived and 1,632 MATH-derived. Table 8 partitions these into 2,562 SFT responses and 473 DPO pairs, or 946 response records. Domain counts are 1,500/188 SFT/DPO-pair for GSM8K, 542/185 for algebra, 512/86 for counting and probability, and 8/14 for Others.

LoRA training covers Llama-2-7B-chat-hf, Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct, and Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3. SFT runs for one epoch; DPO runs for three. Both use effective batch size 64, learning rate 3e-5, RMSprop, and seed 42; DPO uses beta 0.1. Training and evaluation use two 80 GB A100 GPUs. The paper does not disclose the exact Lean/mathlib revision, complete prompts, item-level lineage, or a released schema.
