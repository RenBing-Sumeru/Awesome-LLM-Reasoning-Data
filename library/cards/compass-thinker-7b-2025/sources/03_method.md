Prompts are collected from multiple open-source mathematics datasets, explicitly including NuminaMATH. The pipeline requires verifiable ground truth and removes prompts containing inaccessible web or image links. Because the authors found performance issues and bugs in Math-Verify, they built a separate mathematical verification program and removed items that it could not recognize or verify.

Ungraded prompts are evaluated with Llama-8B and assigned difficulty levels 1–5 by accuracy percentile. The report varies difficulty distributions across RL stages, although it does not publish the exact stage mixtures. Training starts from Qwen2.5-Math-7B and uses verl. For each prompt, the old policy samples a response group and the custom verifier supplies rule-based final-answer rewards. The GRPO variant removes KL loss, drops groups whose accuracy is 0 or 1, and increases the upper clipping bound. Reported settings are training batch 128, actor minibatch 32, 16 gradient updates per iteration, learning rate `1e-6`, maximum sequence length 4096, temperature 1.0, and top-p 1.0.
