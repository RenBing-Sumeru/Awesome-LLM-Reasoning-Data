The evidence supports the following reconstructable pipeline, with historical-release facts separated from current reproduction commands.

1. **Inputs.** Start from the GSM8K and MATH training splits. Each final row is labeled as original or augmented through `problem_source`, but the public schema does not preserve the upstream seed ID or source revision.
2. **Solution augmentation.** Prompt Llama-3.1-405B-Instruct to produce OpenMath CoT for each original problem. The current Skills recipe requests 64 random-seed solutions per GSM8K item and 512 per MATH item. Paper post-processing removes multiple-`\boxed` outputs, strips “My Solution:”, truncates at the first sentence containing `\boxed`, repairs/removes specified arithmetic patterns, rejects solutions over 1024 Llama-3.1 tokens, and rejects those under 200 characters (Appendix A.2).
3. **Question augmentation.** Use five-shot prompts over source examples to generate related problems. The current recipe requests 10 new questions per GSM8K seed and 80 per MATH seed. Apply syntax filtering, then decontaminate new questions before solution generation (Section 3; Appendix D.2).
4. **Decontamination.** Compare each synthetic question with GSM8K, MATH, AMC 2023, and AIME 2024 test questions. Retrieve the top five neighbors using `multi-qa-MiniLM-L6-cos-v1`, form both pair orders, and ask Llama-3.1-405B-Instruct whether each pair is a paraphrase. Any positive result among the ten judgments removes the synthetic question. About 50K of 569K MATH-derived candidates are removed, leaving about 519K (Section 3.1; Appendix C.2).
5. **Solutions for augmented questions.** Sample 32 Llama-3.1-405B-Instruct solutions per new question at temperature 0.7. Extract final answers, ignore null extractions, and use the most frequent remaining surface form as `expected_answer`. The authors test minimum-vote thresholds 0, 8, 16, and 24 and select **0**, so there is no fixed minimum agreement requirement (Appendix C.1, Table 9).
6. **Answer/format retention.** Original-question solutions are checked against source ground truth. Augmented-question solutions use the constructed proxy answer. Format and length filters are applied, but released rows do not expose parser output, vote count, candidate set, filter decisions, or rejection reason.
7. **Packaging.** Merge four rounded paper components: GSM8K solution augmentation 7.4K questions/0.46M pairs; GSM8K question-solution augmentation 73.6K/2.11M; MATH solution augmentation 7.4K/2.46M; and MATH question-solution augmentation 519.1K/8.94M. The paper totals 607.3K rounded unique questions and 13.97M pairs; the HF manifest supplies the exact row count of 13,972,791.
8. **Downsampling and SFT.** Publish the full train split plus overlapping fair subsets of exactly 1M, 2M, and 5M rows. Fine-tune Llama-3.1-8B-Base on full/1M/2M/5M data and Llama-3.1-70B-Base on 5M. The paper uses batch 512, AdamW, weight decay 1e-2, two epochs, learning rates 2e-5 for 8B and 1e-5 for 70B, and averages six equally spaced checkpoints (Section 4; Appendix A.4).
9. **Evaluation.** The paper uses greedy decoding and majority@256 at temperature 0.7, with GPT-4o judging predicted versus reference final answers. This evaluation contract is separate from the released training-row selection contract.

Generation settings are stage-specific. Section 2 ablations use temperature 1.0 and top-p 0.95; the 32-solution augmented-question stage uses temperature 0.7. Exact historical settings for every released stage, retries, seeds, and total GPU-hours are unknown.
