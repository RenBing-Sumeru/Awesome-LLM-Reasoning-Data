- **Threshold-zero majority is a weak correctness contract.** Augmented questions receive a proxy answer from the most common non-null surface form among 32 solutions, but the selected threshold of zero requires no fixed minimum consensus. Equivalent answers can split across forms, ties and no-valid-answer cases are not visible in public rows, and a repeated teacher error can become the target.
- **Final-answer agreement does not validate reasoning.** The paper finds examples with wrong intermediate steps and correct final answers; about 60% of 20 manually inspected flagged examples were judged incorrect. Neither source-answer matching nor majority voting supplies step-level supervision or faithfulness evidence.
- **Votes and rejections are missing.** Public rows omit `majority_votes`, `total_votes`, all 32 candidates, extraction failures, parser diagnostics, judge/reward-model labels, filter reasons, and rejected solutions/questions. The release cannot support a row-level false-accept/false-reject audit.
- **Decontamination has a bounded benchmark list.** Top-5 embedding retrieval plus bidirectional 405B paraphrase judgments can false-accept or false-reject. The original process covers GSM8K, MATH, AMC 2023, and AIME 2024 only. Omni-MATH arrived later, was not filtered, and the authors report about 1.4% test overlap with training.
- **Exact question accounting is unavailable.** The release manifest verifies 13,972,791 rows, while Appendix Table 5 reports 607.3K rounded unique questions. Exact integer unique-question and per-component accepted/rejected counts are unknown.
- **Subsets overlap.** `train_1M`, `train_2M`, and `train_5M` are fair downsamplings of the full train split, not independent splits. Adding their counts to `train` or using them as clean evaluation data would be incorrect.
- **Recipe/release drift is demonstrated.** The released data retains 564 questions longer than 1024 Llama tokens and recommends filtering them; current Skills conversion commands remove overlong questions and solutions. Running current docs does not necessarily recreate the released bytes.
- **Historical replay is incomplete.** Dataset HEAD is pinned to a 2024 commit, while the inspected Skills main is a 2026 commit. No OpenMathInstruct-2-specific historical tag, exact teacher revision, source revision, prompt/config hash, random seed ledger, container image, retry record, or stage yield manifest was confirmed.
- **Rights are release-level, not row-level.** The dataset declares CC BY 4.0 and Skills code Apache-2.0. Rows do not attach upstream source license/attribution, source revision, transformation provenance, or teacher-model terms, so the labels alone do not complete the rights chain for every record and use case.
- **Ablations do not transfer automatically.** The 20% noise tolerance is measured at particular scales and final-answer perturbations; it does not establish that arbitrary rationale noise is harmless. The authors also report weaker transfer of 8B-tuned design choices to the 70B student.
- **Benchmark scores are not data certification.** Reported model gains combine the release, optimizer, checkpoint averaging, decoding, majority@256, and GPT-4o answer judging. They do not certify row-level correctness, process validity, contamination absence, rights completeness, or independent reproducibility.
