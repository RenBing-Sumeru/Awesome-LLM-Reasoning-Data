The paper reports Elo ratings for 33 models; o1-mini and QwQ-32B-Preview are highlighted at 1578 and 1261, and many models remain in the lowest human-performance band. The paper also reports that testing across 54 contests reduces overall rating standard deviation to about 50. Instance-level evidence is the Codeforces verdict returned by the official platform, including cases that need hidden tests or special judges. Evidence is bounded by virtual participation rules, eight-submission cap, contest set, language choice, and platform availability.
