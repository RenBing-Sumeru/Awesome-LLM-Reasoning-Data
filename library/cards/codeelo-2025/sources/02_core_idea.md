The contribution is a standardized Codeforces-based benchmark that submits model solutions to the official platform and converts contest performance into human-comparable Elo ratings. The core mechanism avoids reimplementing hidden tests or special judges: the platform judge supplies accepted/rejected feedback. Closest comparisons are LiveCodeBench, USACO-style benchmarks, APPS, CodeContests, and AlphaCode's Codeforces simulation. The feedback contract is Codeforces judgment plus the authors' Elo calculation; the direction label is live hidden-contamination-resistant evaluation with an external judge.
