Earlier LLM-as-a-judge work often measures agreement on benign examples or relies on proprietary evaluators. YESciEval changes the evaluation object: each science answer is paired with a nine-rubric contract and controlled subtle/extreme failures, so a judge is tested for sensitivity to a specific defect. SFT and CPO are established techniques; the contribution is their combination with rubric-conditioned adversarial science-Q&A evaluation, not a new preference objective.
