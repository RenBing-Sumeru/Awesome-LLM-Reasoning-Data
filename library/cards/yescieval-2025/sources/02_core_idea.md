YESciEval turns science-answer judgment into nine explicit rubric scores and rationales, then trains an open LLaMA-3.1-8B judge with SFT followed by contrastive preference optimization on benign-versus-adversarial examples. Its released corpus covers ORKGSyn and BioASQ answer records, including 33 disciplines and biomedical Q&A, with 1--5 rubric scores from multiple LLMs. The data are released as CSV under CC BY 4.0 through the YESciEval Corpus repository; the framework supports evaluation and judge/reward-model research rather than a verified ground-truth reward.
