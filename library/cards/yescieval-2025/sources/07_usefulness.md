For a team evaluating retrieval-grounded science answers, reuse the nine-rubric prompt and the released CSV records to build a fixed judge audit set; report per-rubric score drops on benign and perturbed pairs. For training, use score/rationale records only after checking the CC BY 4.0 dataset terms and validating domain fit.

Do not copy its acceptance signal to tasks without paper-grounded Q&A context or without a way to audit natural errors. Success is a reproducible per-rubric discrimination result plus a sampled expert check, not a higher mean score alone.
