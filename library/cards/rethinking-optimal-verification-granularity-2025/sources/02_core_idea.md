Variable Granularity Search (VG-Search) introduces a granularity parameter g: the proposer generates g reasoning steps before the verifier scores each partial or complete candidate. With beam width B1 and branch factor B2, every cycle scores B1 × B2 candidates, retains B1, extends them, and repeats. g = 1 approaches verifier-guided beam search; a very large g approaches Best-of-N.

The paper adds two validation-tuned policies. CM-g selects the largest g that stays within an accuracy tolerance of g = 1, targeting lower compute. AM-g selects the g with maximum validation accuracy under a budget. The chosen g can vary by model, task difficulty group, and number of generations, but remains fixed within a single problem trajectory; it is not step-by-step adaptive verification.
