The evaluation spans thirteen instruction-tuned models: Qwen2.5-VL from 7B to 72B, Qwen3-VL from 2B to 32B, InternVL3.5 from 2B to 38B, and Molmo2 at 4B and 8B. It uses six benchmarks covering general multimodal ability, spatial perception, hallucination, visual mathematics, logic, and visual question answering. Generations are capped at 1,024 tokens, and runtime is measured on H200 GPUs rather than inferred only from token counts.

For each model and task, the authors compare the unscaled baseline with nine inference policies: CoT, structured CoT, plan-and-solve, self-consistency, self-aggregation, self-refinement, describe-answer, compositional CoT, and prompt repetition. They report benchmark accuracy and average runtime, construct accuracy-compute frontiers for selected model pairs, and analyze both attention to image tokens and the sufficiency of reasoning chains through LLM judging and human evaluation.
