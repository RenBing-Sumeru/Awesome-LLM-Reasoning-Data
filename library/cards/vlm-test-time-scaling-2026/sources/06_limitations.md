The study evaluates public models and benchmarks at a fixed maximum generation length and on one hardware setup, so its Pareto conclusions need not transfer to proprietary models, video tasks, interactive agents, or different latency targets. It reports no official code release, which makes exact prompt and sampling reproduction depend on the paper and supplement. Attention decay is descriptive evidence rather than a proof that later reasoning has lost visual grounding, and benchmark accuracy cannot fully measure hallucination or real-world safety.
