Prior-work baseline and change: Earlier contamination checks commonly relied on membership-style signals or changes in downstream scores. This work instead frames contamination as a measurable representation change under a known exposure intervention, allowing the audit target to be calibrated against controlled amounts of benchmark access. It thereby separates a method for estimating a benchmark-level leakage signal from a claim that any particular model is known to have trained on an item. It does not establish the unknown training corpus of a deployed frontier model, and its conclusions depend on the selected model, embedding procedure, and intervention design.
