Primary source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/choi25b.html. How Contaminated Is Your Benchmark? Measuring Dataset Leakage in Large Language Models with Kernel Divergence introduces a benchmark-leakage measurement method. The evaluation surface is a benchmark sample set, embeddings before and after benchmark fine-tuning, and a benchmark-level Kernel Divergence Score. Use it to study benchmark leakage.
