Earlier MGT benchmarks commonly emphasize detection under fixed generators or adversarial edits. OpenTuringBench changes the evaluation object by unifying binary detection and generator attribution over open LLM outputs, then adding controlled temperature, scale, rewrite, mixing, domain-shift, and unseen-generator conditions. OTBDetector itself extends a prior contrastive approach with Longformer for texts often longer than 1,000 tokens; the main novelty is the benchmark’s stress-test contract and public scale.
