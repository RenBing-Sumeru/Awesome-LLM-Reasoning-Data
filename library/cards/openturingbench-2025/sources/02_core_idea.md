The paper contributes OpenTuringBench, an open-model machine-generated-text benchmark that pairs human news with outputs from seven open LLMs and tests detection and attribution under seven scenarios. The official dataset contains 543,091 English texts (41,426 human and 501,665 machine) and supports TT binary classification and AA multi-class attribution. OTBDetector uses Longformer representations, triplet contrastive learning, and nearest-centroid classification; the public Hugging Face release is the official reusable dataset.
