The arXiv abstract states that APPS contains 10,000 problems ranging from simple one-line solutions to substantial algorithmic challenges, and that models are evaluated by checking generated code on test cases. It reports that GPT-Neo-style models could pass about 20% of the test cases on introductory problems at the time. The decisive instance-level evidence is a candidate program's execution result on the relevant tests. Evidence boundaries include public-test leakage, timeout policy, Python dependency differences, sampling budget, and whether the metric is per-test or per-problem.
