APPS contributes a large programming-challenge benchmark where language models must generate full Python programs from natural-language specifications. The mechanism is simple but strict: collect coding problems of varying difficulty, provide problem statements and tests, and score generated programs by execution. The closest comparisons are HumanEval/MBPP-style function tasks and competitive-programming datasets such as CodeContests. The direction label is executable benchmark surface: success is a test outcome, not a judge preference or prose similarity score.
