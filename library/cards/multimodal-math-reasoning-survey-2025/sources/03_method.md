Its method is a review of more than 200 studies published since 2021, according to the official abstract. The inputs are published studies on Math-LLMs and multimodal settings; the outputs are a field overview, a discussion of multimodal reasoning pipelines, and five major challenges. It does not provide a common training protocol, verifier, reward, or new artifact, so those properties remain study-specific.

For each cited primary work, record the input modalities, mathematical objective, model role, benchmark, and scoring rule. This produces a compact record that matches the survey's pipeline-oriented account. It also keeps the survey's field synthesis separate from implementation details such as data preprocessing, prompting, and component interfaces, which are not standardized across the reviewed studies.
