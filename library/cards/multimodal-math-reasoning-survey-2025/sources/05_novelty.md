The authors describe this as the first comprehensive analysis of mathematical reasoning in the era of multimodal large language models. The contribution is a synthesis spanning benchmarks, methods, pipelines, and five stated challenges, rather than a newly trained system. Its value is strongest when comparing research designs that otherwise mix perception, representation, and mathematical inference.

The new value is an integrated vocabulary for a field whose papers can otherwise appear comparable merely because they contain mathematics and multiple modalities. It clarifies that changing an image representation, a reasoning procedure, and an answer evaluator are different interventions. Readers can use this distinction to state more precisely what a new system contributes and what remains inherited from its setting.
