The underlying literature spans different models, modalities, tasks, and benchmark conventions, so individual results may not be directly comparable. In a multimodal task, an error can arise before symbolic or numerical reasoning begins, making a final wrong answer hard to attribute to one component. The survey names major challenges but does not itself establish a shared measurement protocol for resolving them.

Do not infer a mathematical-reasoning failure from answer accuracy alone when the system also has to interpret non-textual input. Conversely, a correct answer does not identify which component carried the relevant information. The survey highlights these attribution difficulties; primary papers must provide the task construction and analysis needed to decide whether a comparison isolates the intended capability.
