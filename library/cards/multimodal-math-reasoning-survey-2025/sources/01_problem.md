Multimodal mathematical reasoning requires a model to work with mathematical content that may include more than text. The authors note that mathematical reasoning is important from education to scientific work and survey how multimodal large language models are being used for it. This Findings ACL 2025 paper reviews benchmarks, methods, and challenges rather than proposing a new model or benchmark.

The problem is broader than obtaining a final numerical answer. A system must interpret the supplied modality and connect it to the mathematical task before a reasoning result can be meaningful. This survey is useful for deciding whether a paper studies perception, mathematical inference, or their combination; it does not supply one new dataset or a common experimental solution.
