The report does not release the pre-training corpora, Thinker instruction records, reference-speech data, DPO preference records, rejected outputs, source manifests, or a complete record schema. The Phase-2 token totals do not identify source composition or provenance, and the repository’s Apache-2.0 licence should not be interpreted as a licence for unreleased training data.

The Talker feedback contract cannot be independently audited. WER and punctuation-pause error are named as score-associated signals, but their exact calculation, weights, aggregation, thresholds, candidate-generation process, calibration, false-ranking rates, and human validation are not reported. Splits and decontamination are also not reported.

The report itself notes that label noise and pronunciation errors in pre-training data can lead to hallucinations. It does not quantify this risk or disclose whether the preference construction eliminates it. Therefore, the Card remains a partial report-level disclosure record.
