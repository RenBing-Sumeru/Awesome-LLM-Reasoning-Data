The report describes three pre-training stages. It first locks the LLM while training vision and audio encoders, then unfreezes all parameters for wider multimodal training, and finally trains with 32k-sequence data. It says the LLM is initialized from Qwen2.5, the vision encoder from Qwen2.5-VL, and the audio encoder from Whisper-large-v3. It reports a maximum length of 8,192 in earlier stages and 32,768 in the final stage. Source names, mixture weights, item schemas, licences, and filtering procedures are not supplied.

For Thinker, the report says ChatML instruction-finetuning includes pure-text dialogue, visual-modality conversation, audio-modality conversation, and mixed-modality conversation. The displayed format shows a user multimodal context and an assistant response, but not data origin, prompt construction, counts, filters, or release location.

For Talker, the report names context continuation, DPO, and multi-speaker instruction fine-tuning. It states that a DPO dataset contains input text x, good and bad generated speech y_w and y_l, and reference speech; samples are ranked with reward scores associated with word error rate and punctuation-pause error. The objective is shown, but the reward computation, pair-generation model, reference-speech provenance, score weights, thresholds, calibration, pair count, decoding settings, and retention yield are unknown.
