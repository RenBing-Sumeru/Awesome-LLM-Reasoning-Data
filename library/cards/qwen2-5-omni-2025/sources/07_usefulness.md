For Track 12, Qwen2.5-Omni supplies a compact comparison point for multimodal disclosure. A curator can record what is actually named: modality-level pre-training categories and selected totals, Thinker instruction-data modality groups, a DPO tuple shape, and WER/punctuation-pause-associated ranking. The same record must preserve that sources, records, score implementation, and audit controls are unknown.

It is relevant to post-training reasoning-data work because it shows an answer-level, pairwise speech-feedback interface rather than merely model output performance. It can inform questions about how speech data or preference pairs are constructed, but it must not be used as evidence that a released DPO dataset, reward model, or reproducible multimodal training recipe exists.
