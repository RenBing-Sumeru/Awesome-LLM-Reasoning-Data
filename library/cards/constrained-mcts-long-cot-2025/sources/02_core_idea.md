CMCTS constrains expansion to predefined prompts grouped into four disjoint action sets. Understanding extracts and structures the problem; reflection checks prior reasoning; coding invokes computational assistance such as SymPy; summary reviews the path and emits the boxed final answer. Initialization starts with understanding, summary terminates a path or is forced at the depth limit, and partial-order rules discourage consecutive actions of the same type, require reflection, and restrict actions according to depth and prior steps.

Selection uses UCT. During expansion and simulation, candidate state-action pairs receive Q estimates and states receive V estimates from a pretrained Qwen2.5-Math-PRM by normalizing positive and negative logits. Node rewards combine Q and V and are back-propagated. Multiple MCTS iterations produce candidate trajectories; answer frequency selects the final answer, with terminal reward breaking frequency ties. CMCTS-RULE omits the PRM, CMCTS-PRM omits partial-order rules, and full CMCTS combines both.

Relative to Native-MCTS, the main contribution is to make the action space structured and to add explicit transition constraints alongside learned process feedback. The category fit is Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data because the reusable object is the whole tree and its scored alternatives. The final chosen chain cannot reveal which actions were unavailable, which branches were pruned, or how PRM and rules interacted.
