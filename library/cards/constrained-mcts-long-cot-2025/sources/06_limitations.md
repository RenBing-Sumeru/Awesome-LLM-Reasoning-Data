The four action sets are hand-designed and not proven complete; they may exclude useful domain-specific strategies or force unnatural transitions. Partial-order rules encode assumptions about good reasoning, and the PRM can mis-rank correct but unfamiliar steps. Q/V score calibration, prompt sensitivity, UCT settings, tie-breaking, and answer parsing can all change the selected path. Tool-generated outputs also require environment and dependency capture.

The paper evaluates mathematics only. Search cost, latency, and oracle cost are not fully characterized against every baseline, and benchmark accuracy does not verify intermediate reasoning. The repository linked by the paper has no tagged release or confirmed license, and no complete tree/score corpus is identified. Decontamination, dataset versions, seeds, and rejected-branch retention remain unknown.
