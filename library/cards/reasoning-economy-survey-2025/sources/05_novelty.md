Earlier efficiency work often treated language-model serving as the main target. This survey focuses on a different bottleneck: the variable, explicit reasoning trajectory of recent reasoning models. It connects behavior such as repetition and over-analysis to choices about training signals and test-time allocation. The new contribution is a vocabulary for judging trade-offs. It does not show that all long reasoning is wasteful; on hard tasks, a longer search may be exactly the evidence a correct answer requires.
