Long reasoning can solve difficult tasks, but it can also repeat definitions, explore useless branches, and spend thousands of tokens on an easy question. This survey studies that tension. It asks how to preserve the useful part of deliberate reasoning while reducing avoidable computation. The data object is usually a prompt, an explicit reasoning trace, a final answer, and measurements such as length, latency, or cost. The important boundary is that a shorter answer is not automatically a better reasoning process.
