Use this survey when you have a trace-producing model and a real budget. First define what must not get worse: final correctness, verified intermediate steps, robustness, or user-visible latency. Then decide whether an input should receive a fixed budget, an adaptive budget, or a second-pass check. The survey helps students reject a common mistake: optimizing the mean trace length before measuring whether the removed steps occur on easy repetition or on the rare reasoning move that prevents an error.
