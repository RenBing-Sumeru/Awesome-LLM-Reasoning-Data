The prior-work baseline is agent-environment, tool-use, and benchmark-harness papers in the same atlas category. The change introduced by R2E-Gym: Procedural Environments and Hybrid Verifiers for Scaling Open-Weights SWE Agents is the particular task object and feedback contract: repository-level SWE tasks with natural-language task descriptions, executable environments, unit tests, ground-truth patches or commits, agent trajectories, output patches, reward signals, and final SWE-Bench-style outcomes. scored by automated unit-test reward inside R2E-Gym plus hybrid execution-based and execution-free verifiers for reranking/scaling; SWE-Bench Verified final scores use the official SWE-Bench harness. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07164; project: https://r2e-gym.github.io/; code: https://github.com/R2E-Gym/R2E-Gym; data: https://huggingface.co/R2E-Gym; huggingface: https://huggingface.co/R2E-Gym; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.07164), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
