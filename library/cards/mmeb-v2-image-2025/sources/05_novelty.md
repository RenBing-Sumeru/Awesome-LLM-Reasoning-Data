The prior-work baseline is document, chart, multimodal QA, and visual evaluation benchmarks. The change introduced by VLM2Vec-V2: Advancing Multimodal Embedding for Videos, Images, and Visual Documents is the particular task object and feedback contract: query input, candidate corpus item, modality/task metadata, embedding similarity score, ranked retrieval list, and task metric. scored by retrieval/classification metrics computed from labeled query-candidate pairs and ranked outputs. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04590; project: https://tiger-ai-lab.github.io/VLM2Vec/; code: https://github.com/TIGER-AI-Lab/VLM2Vec; data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/MMEB-V2; huggingface: https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/MMEB-V2; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.04590), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
