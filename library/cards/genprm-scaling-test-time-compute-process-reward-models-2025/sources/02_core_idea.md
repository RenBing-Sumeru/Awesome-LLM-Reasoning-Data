The paper constructs about 23K MATH process-supervision records containing problems, prefixes, current steps, judgments, and rationales. Relative Progress Estimation measures whether a step improves future solvability, executable claims are checked with Python, and a strong model synthesizes the final rationale. The data support generative SFT, step judging, and repeated test-time sampling.

Each record should reconstruct the input, process state, judgment evidence, and acceptance result. The work organizes records around MATH trajectories, relative-progress estimates, code execution, and generative judgment rationales, making the verifier, judge, environment, or execution source traceable for ProcessBench and mathematical answer correction. Undisclosed licensing, audit coverage, format, and decontamination should remain unconfirmed.
