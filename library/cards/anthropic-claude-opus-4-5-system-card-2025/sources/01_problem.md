Frontier system cards can disclose broad data categories and alignment methods without exposing the records, feedback contract, or audit artifacts needed to inspect a post-training pipeline. That gap matters for reasoning systems because source provenance, trace generation, reward boundaries, contamination controls, and agentic environments determine whether a reported mechanism can be independently assessed.

Claude Opus 4.5 is a high-value Track 12 item because Anthropic's official system card is unusually concrete on several interfaces: source categories, RLHF and reinforcement learning from AI feedback, earlier supervised learning with reasoning text produced by prior models, the absence of RL rewards or penalties based on reasoning-text content, selected reward-hacking mitigations, internal transcript review, and evaluation decontamination. It still withholds the record-level data, rewards, environments, and reproducibility artifacts. This Card records both sides of that boundary rather than treating the card as a released post-training dataset or recipe.
