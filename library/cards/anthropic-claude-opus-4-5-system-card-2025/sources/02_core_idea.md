The official system card describes a proprietary training mixture at category level: public internet information through May 2025, non-public third-party data, data-labeling services and paid contractors, opted-in Claude-user data, and internally generated Anthropic data. It reports cleaning and filtering, including deduplication and classification. These are disclosure-level source claims; no source manifest, mixture weights, record counts, versions, rights map, or item-level lineage is released.

For post-training, Anthropic reports substantial RLHF and reinforcement learning from AI feedback. It makes one important reasoning-text boundary explicit: RL training used no rewards or penalties based on the content of the model's reasoning text. Separately, some earlier supervised-learning data included reasoning text produced by prior models. The combination exposes a real distinction between supervised trace use and RL reward handling, but does not identify teachers, prompts, traces, preferences, reward models, verifiers, or objectives.
