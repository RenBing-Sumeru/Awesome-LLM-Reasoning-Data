The central contribution is a shared forum-processing substrate whose selection contract changes between training and evaluation. For AoPS-Instruct, Qwen2.5-14B classifies whether the first post is a concrete math question; Llama-3.1-70B extracts that question and every answer-bearing later post, including answer user and post number; Qwen2.5-72B rewrites each selected answer into formal steps; and exact 10-gram matching removes overlaps with listed math test sets. Multiple valid replies to one topic can become multiple SFT pairs (Paper Figure 2, §3.1, Appendix G).

LiveAoPSBench keeps recent topics but adds an answerability contract. It excludes proof questions and candidates without a boxed final answer, applies stricter 8-gram matching, and rewrites each item independently with Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct. Final answers must agree under text equality, numeric equality, or SymPy equivalence; disagreements are removed and remaining answers are deduplicated. The stack can observe timestamps, extractable terminal answers, and cross-model agreement. It cannot establish that an intermediate derivation is valid, that two models did not share an error, or that a paraphrased older problem is uncontaminated.

Three artifacts must remain distinct. The paper's AoPS-Instruct is a 647,255-pair pre-2024 training corpus; the paper's LiveAoPSBench-0824 is a 3,863-question January-August 2024 evaluation snapshot; and the checked full-year LiveAoPSBench-2024 release has 5,328 rows at HF revision `3ff6280f4c1d75a035698524c26ee91c70e4e2b5`. The linked AoPS-Instruct download is a third-party reproduction at a separate HF revision. Matching row counts do not make those releases one verified dataset.

Numina and Omni-MATH already used AoPS-derived material, while LiveCodeBench supplied the timestamped-evaluation precedent in code. The concrete difference here is to crawl the broader forum rather than only contest collections, preserve posting time as a selection variable, and use the same incoming stream for SFT and a stricter live evaluation protocol. The `live` property is therefore a versioned procedure, not a permanent guarantee: once a snapshot is public, future post-training can absorb it.
