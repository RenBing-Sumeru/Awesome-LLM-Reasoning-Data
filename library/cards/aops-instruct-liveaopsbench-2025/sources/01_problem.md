The official ICML 2025 paper addresses two coupled bottlenecks in Olympiad-level mathematics. Large supervised fine-tuning corpora with worked solutions are expensive to author, while static public test sets may already be present in model training. The Art of Problem Solving (AoPS) forum supplies a continually growing stream of questions and community answers, but a forum topic is not a ready training or evaluation record: the first post may not ask a concrete problem, later posts may be irrelevant or incomplete, and different users may provide conflicting or terse solutions.

The paper turns the same source stream into two objects. AoPS-Instruct uses topics posted through 2023-12 and represents a retained example as a question paired with an LLM-rewritten community solution for SFT. LiveAoPSBench uses newer timestamps and represents an evaluation item as a question, rewritten solution, extracted final answer, and posting month. The paper starts from 1,076,712 topics, obtains 652K QA pairs after its general pipeline, and identifies 647,255 pre-2024-01 pairs as AoPS-Instruct; its main LiveAoPSBench-0824 snapshot contains 3,863 questions from 2024-01 through 2024-08 (Paper §3.1-§3.2, Appendix A.1).

This belongs in Data Construction and Open Release Recipes because the research object is the transformation from community discussions to selected, model-ready records: source roles, extraction, rewriting, overlap filtering, answer checks, timestamp splits, and release packaging all determine the final data. The Card does not treat downstream benchmark gains as proof that every rewritten rationale is correct, nor does it classify the final-answer checker as formal verification.

The release boundary is material. The authors release pipeline and evaluation code and link a 5,328-row full-2024 LiveAoPSBench snapshot. AoPS-Instruct is linked only as a community reproduction for which the authors disclaim liability. The default public training view contains two-message chats but no canonical topic/post identifiers; the benchmark view exposes five model-ready columns but no source URL. The paper therefore supports an auditable construction recipe and partial artifacts, not a complete author-maintained training release with resolved lineage and rights.
