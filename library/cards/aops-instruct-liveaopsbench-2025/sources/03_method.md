Inputs are complete AoPS topics with a first-post timestamp and ordered user posts. The crawl contains 1,076,712 topics. Qwen2.5-14B classifies the first post with a few-shot binary prompt, leaving 478,337 math-question topics after 598,375 are pruned. Llama-3.1-70B then reads the numbered conversation and returns the first-post question plus every answer user's identity and post number. A repository sanity check confirms that the returned user matches the selected post. Qwen2.5-72B receives the question and raw answer and is instructed to formalize only the supplied solution without adding information. Exact 10-gram matching against listed evaluation sets produces 652K QA pairs, 647,255 of them dated before January 2024 (Paper §3.1; Appendix G; official repository `parse_aops.py`).

For LiveAoPSBench-0824, the timestamp window initially contains 14,158 QA pairs. 8-gram matching against MATH and GSM8K training sets removes 664. Excluding proof questions and non-boxed solutions leaves 7,173 pairs over 5,416 unique questions. Independent Llama and Qwen rewrites then undergo final-answer agreement checks; 1,553 inconsistent questions are removed, yielding 3,863. The same procedure yields 5,216 questions for the 2023 retrospective split. The full-2024 release later grows to 5,328 rows (Paper Appendix A.1 and B.1).

The supervision attaches to the whole answer. For SFT, the question is the instruction and the Qwen-rewritten solution is the response under each model's chat template. The paper trains DeepSeekMath-7B-Instruct, Mathstral-7B, Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, and Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct for three epochs on full AoPS-Instruct, Numina, or their mixture, then evaluates on LiveAoPSBench, MATH, a 675-item text/final-answer OlympiadBench subset, and 4,428 Omni-MATH problems. It does not construct step labels, a process reward, or an RLVR objective. Optimizer, batch size, learning rate, seeds, and exact model revisions are undisclosed.

The checked public benchmark row has `question`, month-level `post_time`, rewritten `solution`, `answer`, and `idx`. The third-party AoPS-Instruct default row is a two-message `messages` list. Neither default model-ready view exposes the internal topic ID, answer post number, or canonical source URL described by the extraction prompts. The third-party `2024_not_decontaminated` configuration preserves original/rewrite fields and hashed user metadata, but it is not the pre-2024 decontaminated training set.
