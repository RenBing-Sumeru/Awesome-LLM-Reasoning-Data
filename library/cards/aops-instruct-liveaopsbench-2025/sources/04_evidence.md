The strongest direct quality check is narrow but concrete. Ten graduate students annotate a random 10% of LiveAoPSBench-0824 (386 questions), with two annotators per question referring to the raw post. Across annotations, 92% are marked correct, 5% incorrect, and 3% no-answer. Appendix A.2 shows that even human annotators disagree when the raw post states an answer indirectly. This supports final-answer utility for the sampled benchmark subset; it does not validate every rationale, the 647,255 training pairs, or the release's source rights (Paper §3.2, §4.5, Appendix A.2).

The SFT experiments show that the constructed records change tested models. On LiveAoPSBench/MATH/OlympiadBench/Omni-MATH, DeepSeekMath-7B-Instruct moves from 11.7/47.1/14.5/12.3 without SFT to 19.0/58.8/24.3/17.8 with AoPS-Instruct. Mathstral-7B moves from 15.4/56.3/21.2/15.9 to 23.6/60.8/27.1/19.9. Llama-3.2-3B and 1B also improve in the reported AoPS-only rows. These are author-reported, three-epoch SFT results, not an independent reproduction and not a record-level correctness audit (Paper Table 3).

The result is not uniformly monotonic. Adding Numina to AoPS improves many rows, but Mathstral's MATH score is 59.6 for the mixture versus 60.8 for AoPS alone, and Appendix Table 8 includes several AIME24/AMC23 ties or regressions. In the rewrite ablation, Qwen rewriting is strongest on MATH, AMC23, and OlympiadBench, but its GSM8K score is 79.2 versus 81.1 for Llama rewriting. This makes the evidence conditional on model, benchmark, mixture, and rewrite policy rather than a general data-quality guarantee (Paper Figure 5b, Tables 3 and 8).

Timestamp evidence is also correlational. Exact 10-gram overlap with Numina decreases across the five four-month windows from 13.24% to 11.65%, 12.82%, 9.92%, and 6.88%, and evaluated models score lower on 2024 than 2023 questions. Newer timestamps reduce direct exposure opportunities, but changing difficulty, topic mix, answer type, and model cutoff can also affect scores; substring matching misses paraphrases (Paper Table 2, Figure 1).

Artifacts corroborate object shape and size: the checked LiveAoPSBench release has 5,328 test rows with five fields, and the third-party AoPS-Instruct default configuration has exactly 647,255 train rows with a two-message schema. The paper's own Figures 16 and 17 document an incomplete rewrite and a counting error. Release counts and downstream gains therefore support the pipeline's usefulness, while the failure examples prevent interpreting the benchmark results as proof that every selected reasoning trace is correct.
