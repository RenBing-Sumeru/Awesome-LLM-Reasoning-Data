The arXiv source table reports five in-distribution datasets with train/dev/test splits: SeqATIS 11,670/694/774, SeqSNIPS 39,750/2,198/2,199, SeqSGD 6,782/1,092/1,567, SeqMultiWOZ 6,816/485/983, and SeqTopV2 94,458/13,477/6,095. OOD test surfaces include SeqToolQA with 358 examples and ToolBench HomeSearch/Booking with 100 and 120 examples, plus ToolLLM, API-Bank, and ToolAlpaca evaluations. Reported evidence shows fine-tuned models reach high in-distribution API-F1 while parameter prediction remains harder, and combined-data fine-tuning improves OOD generalization over ToolLLaMA-2-7B and Lynx-7B under the paper's metrics. Row-level evidence is exact comparison to gold API names, parameter names/values, and sequence order.
