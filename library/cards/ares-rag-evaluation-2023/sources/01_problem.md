ARES addresses the cost of evaluating retrieval-augmented generation systems when each candidate pipeline needs domain-specific labels for retrieved passages and generated answers. The primary source is the arXiv paper, later published at NAACL 2024, plus the official Stanford Future Data repository and package. The evaluation surface is a query, retrieved passage or document context, generated answer, and one of three binary criteria: context relevance, answer faithfulness, or answer relevance. It belongs in the atlas as a RAG evaluation and judge-feedback contract, not as a new QA dataset, retriever, or general-purpose reward model. Its value is the combination of synthetic judge training, a small human preference validation set, and prediction-powered inference confidence intervals for comparing RAG configurations.
