The paper reports that ARES improves context relevance and answer relevance evaluation accuracy over RAGAS by about 59.9 and 14.4 percentage points in the KILT/SuperGLUE pseudo-RAG setting, and that PPI improves ranking accuracy over the judge alone. On AIS attribution data, ARES estimates answer hallucination rates within 2.5 percentage points of ground truth while using 200 validation labels. For existing RAG systems on NQ, WoW, and FEVER, it reports average Kendall tau of 0.91 for context relevance and 0.97 for answer relevance. The decisive row-level evidence is the criterion label assigned to each triple plus the validation-set labels used for PPI; aggregate rankings alone are not reusable proof. Evidence is bounded to English text RAG settings and the tested model, prompt, corpus, and label budgets.
