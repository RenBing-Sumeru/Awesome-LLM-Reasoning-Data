Correctness is relative to the criterion definitions, human validation labels, synthetic data quality, and PPI assumptions. The paper itself notes weak generalization under drastic shifts such as English to other languages, text to code, and text retrieval to entity/web/citation extraction. Specialized domains may require expert annotators for the validation set. GPU and storage requirements are nontrivial because the reported setup uses large synthetic generators and DeBERTa-v3-Large judges. Public example datasets and prompts can be useful for replication but do not make every downstream RAG score comparable without pinning corpus, output triples, prompts, labels, and judge versions.
