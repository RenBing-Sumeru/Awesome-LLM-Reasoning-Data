Keep three distinctions separate: criterion-level binary labels versus aggregate RAG-system scores; judge predictions versus PPI-corrected estimates; evaluation feedback versus training reward. Read the method before the result tables, because the reported gains depend on the validation-label budget and synthetic judge setup. Do not treat a confidence interval that captures an average score as proof that every generated answer is faithful. For reuse, the most fragile boundary is domain shift: in-domain passages and few-shot examples are required to rebuild the judge contract.
