Prior math benchmarks are often public, competition-derived, or easier to contaminate. FrontierMath changes the construction by commissioning original advanced problems and controlling access while aiming for answer verification. The direction signal is that hardest-math evaluation must record provenance, release policy, and verifier coverage together. What is not new is answer-level math scoring. Before reuse, inspect problem errors, version changes, hidden/public split, checker coverage, and whether tool-assisted solving is allowed.
