The contribution is a benchmark of hundreds of original expert-vetted advanced mathematics problems across major branches, intended to remain difficult for frontier models and reduce contamination. The mechanism is expert problem writing and review, controlled release, difficulty tiering, and answer-level verification/checking. The feedback contract is programmatic or rule-based answer acceptance where available, under access-controlled benchmark policy. Closest comparisons are MATH, OlympiadBench, GPQA-style expert benchmarks, and formal proof benchmarks. The direction label is high-difficulty hidden math evaluation with verifier coverage as an audit field.
