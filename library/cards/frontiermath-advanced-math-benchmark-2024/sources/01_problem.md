FrontierMath asks whether frontier AI systems can solve original expert-written advanced mathematics problems that are hard to contaminate and often require concise verifiable answers. The primary sources are arXiv:2411.04872 and the Epoch AI project page. It belongs here as a controlled-access advanced-math benchmark, not as a public training corpus or proof dataset. A problem includes a statement, domain/difficulty metadata, expected answer, and an answer-checking or verifier policy where available. Its atlas value is the explicit tension among expert authorship, hidden access, answer verification, and reproducibility.
