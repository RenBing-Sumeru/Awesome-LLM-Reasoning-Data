1. Inputs: expert-authored math problems, expected answers, domain/tier metadata, and answer-checking rules. 2. Pipeline: write and review original problems, select for difficulty and novelty, keep controlled-access tiers, evaluate model answers against expected answers or verifiers, and report aggregate solve rates. 3. Outputs: benchmark tiers, model scores, selected public/open-problem material, and access-policy notes. 4. Feedback: an answer is accepted when the benchmark's checker or adjudication rule accepts it for that problem version. 5. Reuse requires pinning benchmark version, tier, access policy, verifier coverage, answer normalizers, and whether Open Problems are being mixed with Tiers 1-4.
