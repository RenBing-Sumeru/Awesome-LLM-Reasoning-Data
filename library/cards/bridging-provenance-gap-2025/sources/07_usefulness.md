For `data_construction_open_release_recipes`, this work supplies a pre-ingestion audit layer. A reasoning-data builder can query upstream sources, parent datasets, generating models, human annotation, creator organizations, languages, tasks, licenses, and source restrictions before combining SFT, preference, tool-use, or multimodal components. Conflicts can become an explicit rights-review queue instead of disappearing beneath a mixture-level license.

An auditable reuse workflow should pin a repository commit, retain the audit's stable dataset identifier, and store the exact source-document URL, retrieval time, evidence snapshot or hash, reviewer, taxonomy version, and unresolved fields. Mixture records should preserve both the dataset license and every upstream source/model term rather than collapsing them into a single permissive label. Corrections should be versioned at field level.

The catalogue can also help stratify audits: identify model-generated datasets, trace parent collections, compare collection- and dataset-level restrictions, find missing licenses, or analyze which reasoning sources come from a narrow geography or language distribution. Its downloader/filter tooling may assist construction, but users should separate provenance metadata from the downloaded training records and revalidate live access terms.

It cannot certify data quality, correctness, safety, or legal use. It contains no reasoning traces, process labels, rewards, terminal outcomes, or sample-level verifier decisions. Benchmark performance would not repair those omissions. High-stakes reuse still needs record-level provenance, privacy/consent review, contamination analysis, content inspection, and counsel appropriate to the relevant jurisdiction.

As an open-release case study, it demonstrates why a paper, a living GitHub repository, a tagged release, an explorer, a supplemental Attribution Card, and an HF organization are distinct artifacts. Downstream cards and model reports should name the exact revision used and disclose which of those layers were actually consulted.
