Mobile-task and recovery-trajectory data usually retain only final success or a completed answer, so they cannot reveal which step in planning, retrieval, tool selection, environment execution, or evidence synthesis creates value or introduces an error.

Rather than merely adding complete successful trajectories, this work records mobile-app environment memories, synthesized tasks, screenshots and action sequences, learner/expert switch points, erroneous steps, and recovery trajectories, allowing each local behavior to be accepted, rejected, or corrected for an explicit reason.
