1. **Position:**  DoLoMiTes discovers and instantiates methodical long-form tasks from expert work.
2. **Method:**  Task elicitation, four-part typology, model drafts, and expert revision.
3. **Data:**  Twenty-five fields, 519 task specifications, and 1,857 before–after instances.
4. **Evidence:**  Models mainly fail on procedural completeness and professional inference.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for expert-revision learning; expand target tasks and expert consensus.
