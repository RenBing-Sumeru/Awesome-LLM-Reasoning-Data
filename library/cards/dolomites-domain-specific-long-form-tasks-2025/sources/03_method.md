1. **Elicit task specifications:** Hundreds of experts describe routine methodical writing through objective, procedure, input, and output.

2. **Build typology:** The authors organise 519 tasks across twenty-five fields, normalise terminology, and check methodological reasoning requirements.

3. **Generate instances:** Models generate long-form drafts from expert specifications and inputs, with up to ten samples per task.

4. **Expert revision:** Domain experts correct facts, procedures, structure, and omissions, producing 1,857 before–after examples; models and prompts must be fixed.
