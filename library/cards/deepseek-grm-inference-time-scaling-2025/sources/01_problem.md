The official arXiv preprint asks whether a generalist reward model can improve its judgments by spending more inference compute, especially on queries for which correctness is not available from an executable verifier. Scalar reward models are efficient but emit a narrow score, while ordinary LLM-as-a-Judge or critique models do not necessarily improve when repeatedly sampled. The paper therefore studies both a learning recipe for scalable reward generation and an aggregation procedure at test time.

The auditable object is a reward-generation trajectory over one query and one or more candidate responses: generated principles, textual critiques, pointwise scores, correctness labels used during training, repeated reward samples, and optional Meta RM weights. DeepSeek-GRM is verifier infrastructure, not a released rollout corpus. Official 16B, 27B, and MetaRM model checkpoints are listed in the linked Hugging Face collection, but the mixed internal/open training manifest, raw principles and critiques, and item-level lineage were not confirmed as released.
