For the 27B recipe, the paper reports 1,256K Rejective Fine-Tuning examples: 1,070K in-house general-instruction examples and 186K rejectively sampled reward-model examples, plus 237K examples for rule-based RL. Reward-model data mix internal sources with MATH, UltraFeedback, OffsetBias, Skywork-Reward-Preference-80K-v0.2, and HelpSteer2-Preference. Some UltraFeedback preferences are re-tagged; MATH trajectories are filtered by ground-truth matching; single-response verifiable items receive binary correctness rewards. The complete source manifest, item provenance, and licenses are not disclosed.

DeepSeek-v2.5-0905 samples principles and critiques three times per reward-model item for the cold start. Incorrect trajectories and items for which all samples are already correct are rejected; hinted sampling supplies the labeled best response for difficult cases, while non-hinted sampling preserves unguided trajectories. SPCT then applies GRPO with a +1 or −1 outcome reward according to whether extracted pointwise scores match the single-response label or correctly rank the unique best response. The 27B model uses a KL coefficient selected for stability, group size 4, and 900 steps each for RFT and RL; the paper reports 128 A100 GPUs for training.

For test-time scaling, the model samples reward trajectories at temperature 0.5, shuffles candidate order, and aggregates up to 32 samples by summed scores. A Gemma-2-27B-based Meta RM is trained with binary cross-entropy on labeled correct/incorrect principle–critique trajectories from cold-start and on-policy data, then guides voting; the default Meta RM sample budget is half the reward-sample count. The official model collection exposes checkpoints, but no confirmed code, raw RFT/RL records, generated critique logs, or exact inference harness was found.
