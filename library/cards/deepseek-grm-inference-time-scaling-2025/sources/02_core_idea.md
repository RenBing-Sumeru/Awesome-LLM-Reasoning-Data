Self-Principled Critique Tuning (SPCT) moves principles inside reward generation: a pointwise generative reward model produces input-adaptive principles, critiques candidate responses under those principles, and emits scores in one language-model format for single, paired, or multiple responses. Rejective Fine-Tuning supplies the cold start; rule-based online RL then rewards outputs whose extracted score ordering agrees with the available ground-truth preference or correctness label.

At inference, DeepSeek-GRM samples several principle–critique–score trajectories after shuffling response order. Plain voting sums pointwise scores, expanding the aggregate score range. Meta RM guided voting additionally learns whether a generated principle/critique trajectory is likely correct and keeps the top kmeta trajectories before aggregation. The feedback contract is mixed: rule-based accuracy against labels trains SPCT and Meta RM, but deployment-time judgments remain model-generated. Compared with scalar RMs, semi-scalar critique models, and ordinary LLM-as-a-Judge, the contribution is the coupling of self-generated criteria, online RL, and explicit inference-time reward scaling.
