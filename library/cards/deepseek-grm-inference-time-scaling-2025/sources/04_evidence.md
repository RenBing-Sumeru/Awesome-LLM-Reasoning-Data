The paper evaluates Reward Bench, PPE Preference, PPE Correctness, and RMB, reporting both greedy decoding and inference-time scaling. In its aggregate Table 2, DeepSeek-GRM-27B scores 69.9 overall under greedy decoding; summed-score voting with 32 samples is reported at 71.0, and Meta RM guided voting at 72.8. Ablations remove principle generation, rejective sampling, general instruction data, and hinted or non-hinted sampling. The paper also reports that some verifiable-task settings remain weaker than scalar reward models.

These results are evidence about reward-model benchmark behavior and how the proposed aggregation responds to added sampling. They do not validate every generated principle or critique, prove that the mixed training data are high quality, or establish that the Meta RM is unbiased. The official arXiv v3 record and Hugging Face collection were verified; the collection lists DeepSeek-GRM-16B, DeepSeek-GRM-27B, and DeepSeek-GRM-27B-MetaRM. No official code or raw training/trajectory dataset was confirmed in the accepted metadata.
