Within the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track, DeepSeek-GRM is a verifier-side scaling study. It is useful for analyzing how extra compute is allocated not to solution generation but to repeated judgments, and how a second verifier selects among those judgments. This distinction matters in post-training pipelines: a policy improvement attributed to better data may instead come from a larger reward-sampling budget or a Meta RM.

A reusable record should preserve the source item and license, query, ordered candidate responses, ground-truth preference or correctness label, shuffled order, hinted/non-hinted flag, every generated principle and critique, extracted pointwise scores, rejection reason, GRPO reward, checkpoint, temperature, sample count, Meta RM probability, selected samples, aggregate score, and benchmark outcome. Reward-model training records and inference-time judging traces should remain separate. The released model collection supports evaluation use, but absence of raw lineage prevents treating the work as an open feedback-data release.
