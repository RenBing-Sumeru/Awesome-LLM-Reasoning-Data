The direction signal is often summarized as “data is the algorithm” for reward modeling: quality and distribution can dominate raw pair count. Skywork-Reward differs from work that simply scales preference data because it treats filtering and mixture composition as first-class design choices. It does not introduce a novel reward loss, and it should not be interpreted as evidence that 80K is a universal optimum; the effective data size depends on task diversity, annotation quality, and model capacity.
