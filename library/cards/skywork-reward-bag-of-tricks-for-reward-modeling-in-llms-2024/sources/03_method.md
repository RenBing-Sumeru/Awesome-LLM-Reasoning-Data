The training pipeline starts from open preference data, applies selection and filtering, then fits reward models on the resulting collection. A reproducible release must specify every upstream dataset revision, licensing constraint, normalization/format conversion, quality scorer or heuristic, threshold, deduplication rule, source mixture weight, and final item ID. It must also record base model, tokenizer/chat template, loss, batch/learning-rate schedule, sequence length, and checkpoint selection. Without this lineage, “Skywork-Reward data” is a label rather than a reproducible artifact.
