At release, Skywork-Reward-Gemma-27B is reported at the top of the RewardBench leaderboard, and the authors report that their techniques directly enhance several highly ranked models. These are time-stamped benchmark claims. They demonstrate that a small selected collection can be competitive under the paper’s evaluation, but do not prove that every excluded example is harmful or that the same filters will help a different base model, language, or reward objective.
