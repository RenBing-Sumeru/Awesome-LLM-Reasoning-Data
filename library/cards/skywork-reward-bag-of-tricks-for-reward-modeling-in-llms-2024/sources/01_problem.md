Skywork-Reward frames reward modeling as a data-selection problem. Open preference collections can be large yet contain near duplicates, weak contrasts, inconsistent labels, or samples that teach an RM generator-specific style instead of preference. The paper asks whether a carefully selected and filtered set can train a stronger RM than a much larger indiscriminate mixture. Its central data object is not simply “80K pairs”; it is a curated subset whose inclusion rule is part of the algorithm.
