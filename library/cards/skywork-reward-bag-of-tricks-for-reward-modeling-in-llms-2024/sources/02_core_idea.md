The reported recipe produces a Skywork-Reward collection of 80K preference pairs and trains Skywork-Reward-Gemma-27B and Skywork-Reward-Llama-3.1-8B. The paper’s bag of tricks is data-centric: it discusses selecting useful sources and filtering for quality. This means a pair’s provenance, quality signal, duplication status, and source distribution matter as much as its chosen/rejected fields. A reuse that copies only the final 80K rows but not the curation logic cannot test the paper’s main hypothesis.
