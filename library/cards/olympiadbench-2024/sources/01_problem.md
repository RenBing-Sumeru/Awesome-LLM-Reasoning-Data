OlympiadBench asks whether LLMs and LMMs can solve bilingual, multimodal olympiad-level mathematics and physics problems rather than easier school-exam or general STEM QA items. The primary source is the arXiv paper submitted on 2024-02-21, revised on 2024-06-06, and marked as accepted to ACL 2024 main; the official OpenBMB repository provides the evaluation code and public release notes.

The evaluation surface is a problem record drawn from International Olympiads, Chinese Olympiads, or difficult GaoKao segments. A record can contain a problem statement, optional image material, context for progressive physics problems, solution annotation, subfield labels, answer type labels, and a reference target. The benchmark includes 8,476 problems, with math and physics, English and Chinese, text-only and multimodal slices, and both open-ended and theorem-proving tasks.

The atlas boundary is evaluation and audit, not a training recipe or a proof assistant dataset. Its value is that it turns high-difficulty contest reasoning into a reusable benchmark surface with an explicit answer-level scoring contract for many open-ended items, while leaving proof and text-description cases as a manual or non-programmatic boundary.
