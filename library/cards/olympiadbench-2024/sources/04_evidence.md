The main source evidence is the released arXiv paper, the OpenBMB repository, and the Hugging Face dataset. The paper reports 8,476 total problems, 4,869 with images, 8,476 with solutions, 6,728 open-ended questions, 1,748 theorem-proving questions, 6,142 math problems, and 2,334 physics problems. The official repository reports full-benchmark scores including GPT-4o at 25.89 average, GPT-4V at 17.97 average, and a text-only GPT-4 score of 29.93.

The decisive row-level evidence for automatic-score items is not the aggregate leaderboard but the evaluator's comparison between extracted model final answer and reference target. Numeric, expression, equation, interval, and tuple cases have a programmatic contract; proof and categorical text cases do not have the same machine-checkable guarantee.

The evidence boundary is therefore narrow. Reported percentages depend on the public dataset version, prompt format, model snapshot, image handling, answer extraction, tolerance choices, and evaluator implementation. The paper itself documents failure modes where a correct physics answer can be marked wrong because the tolerance or expression-equivalence policy is insufficient.
