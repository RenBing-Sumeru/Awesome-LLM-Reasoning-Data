The contribution is a curated bilingual multimodal olympiad benchmark for math and physics, paired with annotations and a scoring pipeline that can evaluate many free-form numerical or symbolic final answers. The core mechanism is not a new solver: it sources contest PDFs, parses them with OCR, cleans and deduplicates records, annotates question type, subfield, and answer type, and evaluates model outputs against normalized reference answers when the answer type is programmatically checkable.

The feedback contract is answer-level. Numeric values are compared with tolerances, symbolic expressions and equations are checked with symbolic computation, intervals and tuples are decomposed into comparable elements, and theorem-proving or categorical textual answers are outside the full automatic scoring contract. This makes the benchmark closest to hard scientific reasoning benchmarks such as SciBench, ScienceQA, MMMU, MathVista, and GaoKao/AGIEval-style exam suites, but OlympiadBench narrows the scope to olympiad-level math and physics with many image-bearing problems.

The direction label is high-difficulty domain-expert evaluation. Reuse should preserve the distinction between automatic-score open-ended items and proof/textual items, because the benchmark's strongest evidence comes from the answer-level verifier rather than from a universal reasoning-step judge.
