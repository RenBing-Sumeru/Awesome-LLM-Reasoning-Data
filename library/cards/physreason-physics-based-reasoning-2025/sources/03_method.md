1. **Build the set:** Collect 1,200 physics problems across topics and difficulty levels.

2. **Write references:** Create reference solutions averaging 8.1 steps, including modeling, formulas, and calculations.

3. **Score steps:** PSAS compares model solutions with reference processes to judge step correctness and locate the earliest error.

4. **Evaluate causes:** Measure modeling, formula, and calculation errors; reproduction must fix references, the scorer, and step-matching rules.
