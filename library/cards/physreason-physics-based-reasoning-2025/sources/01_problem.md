A physics problem may end with the correct number even when a model uses the wrong physical model, applies an incorrect formula that later cancels out, or makes unit and arithmetic errors. Final-answer checking cannot separate conceptual modeling, formula selection, and algebraic propagation, while general mathematical PRMs lack physical laws and dimensional information.

PhysReason provides 1,200 physics problems with reference solutions averaging 8.1 steps and uses PSAS to align model solutions, locate the earliest error, and categorize physics-specific failures.
