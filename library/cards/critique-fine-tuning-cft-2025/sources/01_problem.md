Supervision from correct answers teaches a model to imitate ideal outputs but not to locate problems in an existing flawed reasoning trace. The model may simply rewrite the answer and ignore the earliest error, preventing it from serving as a critic for filtering, feedback, or self-correction.

Critique Fine-Tuning generates incorrect or low-quality responses, asks GPT-4o to identify concrete defects and write natural-language critiques, and trains the model to read and diagnose flawed solutions rather than merely imitate answers.
