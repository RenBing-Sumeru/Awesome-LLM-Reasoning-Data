Prior methods rely on final answers, generic step labels, or a single LLM judge, leaving a mismatch between feedback and the target. This work centers supervision on questions, noisy responses, and natural-language critiques, uses creating incorrect or low-quality responses and asking GPT-4o to locate errors and explain improvements to generate and filter records, and releases WebInstruct-CFT. The novelty lies in the data object and acceptance rule rather than standard training algorithms. The central change is shifting the objective from answer imitation to critique learning.
