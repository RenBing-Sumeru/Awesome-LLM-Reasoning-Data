Construction has four auditable stages. First, a virtual Android emulator or web environment is explored, and each valid interaction records pre-state, action, and post-state screenshots. The paper also uses accessibility trees; for training they are filtered to retain visible elements' position or index information. Second, GPT-4o reverse-synthesizes a low-level instruction and then a high-level, task- or question-oriented instruction from each observed transition.

Third, synthesized tasks seed model execution in the environment. Full episodes contain the high-level goal, low-level intentions, action history, GUI actions, screenshots, and accessibility observations. The paper says the synthesis pipeline requires no human intervention, although later TRM validation uses human annotations. The public repository separately offers construction scripts, raw mobile/web triples with screenshots and JSON, derived training data, evaluation code, and checkpoints.

Fourth, GPT-4o serves as TRM. It receives the high-level instruction, the full low-level-instruction history, and screenshots from the final three timesteps, then scores completion and coherence from 1 to 5. Reward-proportional sampling feeds two SFT objectives: planning predicts a low-level instruction and action from state, high-level goal, and history; action training predicts the action from state, history, and low-level instruction. Reported backbones are InternVL2-4B, InternVL2-8B, and Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct, fully fine-tuned on eight A100 80GB GPUs. Exact optimizer, schedule, epochs, temperature, rollout budget, and seed are undisclosed.

Reset and replay are under-specified. Evaluation uses AndroidWorld system-state checks and WebArena functional evaluators, but the paper does not provide a pinned app/site snapshot, deterministic reset recipe, or guarantee that released episodes can be replayed against the original environment state.
