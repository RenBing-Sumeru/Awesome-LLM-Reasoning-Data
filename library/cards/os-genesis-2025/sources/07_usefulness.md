For the `environment_agent_trajectory_data` track, OS-Genesis is a compact example of how environment interaction becomes post-training supervision. It exposes the chain from state transition, to task language, to rollout, to graded feedback, to SFT sampling. The raw triples are useful for studying task synthesis independently of execution; full trajectories support planning/action training; derived mobile rows illustrate how an episode is flattened into multimodal conversations.

The Card is especially useful when comparing feedback contracts. Environment success checks in AndroidWorld and WebArena are terminal evaluators, whereas construction-time TRM is a probabilistic judge over completion and coherence. Keeping both layers separate helps prevent benchmark success from being mistaken for intrinsic data correctness.

Practical uses include reproducing reverse task synthesis, training GUI agents with planning and action objectives, analyzing how failed rollouts are weighted, and auditing screenshot/accessibility-tree supervision. Before combining the release with other trajectory corpora, users should reconstruct episode boundaries, identify duplicated screenshots or paired objectives, pin revisions, establish app/task splits, and review rights, privacy, and unsafe-action exposure.
