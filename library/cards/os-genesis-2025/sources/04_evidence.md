In the main controlled setting, OS-Genesis and the task-driven baseline each use 1,000 trajectories; self-instruction uses 1,500, and average trajectory length is 6.4 steps. On AndroidWorld, Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct improves from 9.82% success with the self-instruction baseline to 17.41% with OS-Genesis. InternVL2-4B reaches 15.18% and InternVL2-8B reaches 16.96% under OS-Genesis. On WebArena, overall success is 7.88%, 9.96%, and 10.79% for those three backbones, respectively.

The TRM evidence is more directly relevant to the feedback contract. On 200 sampled web and mobile trajectories, GPT-4o scores correlate with Qwen2.5-VL-72B scores at Spearman 0.788 for web and 0.729 for mobile. On 100 sampled trajectories, GPT-4o TRM scores correlate with human scores at 0.798 for web and 0.813 for mobile. An ablation compares equal sampling, binary labeler filtering, and TRM sampling; the paper reports stronger high-level-task performance for TRM, while binary filtering can slightly favor high-level success but reduce low-level results.

These results support the usefulness of the pipeline and graded sampler under the reported setup. They do not establish that the released data are error-free, that gains transfer to other environment versions, or that benchmark overlap has been excluded. The public mobile dataset page independently confirms approximately 51.1K SFT rows in one train split, MIT metadata, and `id`/`image`/`conversations` fields.
