The prior baseline was broad multitask knowledge benchmarks and exam subsets whose human-evaluation origin was not the organizing principle. AGIEval changes the collection rule: tasks are selected because they are official human exams with high stakes or high standards. The quality signal is answer-key scoring over real exams rather than generated questions, and the direction signal is a compact coordinate for human-centric standardized-test reasoning. What is not new is exact-match QA scoring, prompt-based evaluation, or multiple-choice benchmarking. Before reuse, inspect whether the source exams can be redistributed, whether task files match the paper version, and whether scores are being compared under the same prompt and extraction rules.
