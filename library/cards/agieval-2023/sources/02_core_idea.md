The contribution is a human-centric benchmark built from 20 official, public, high-standard exams, totaling 8,062 questions in the paper. The mechanism is curation of real exam problems, task-specific prompt templates, answer extraction, and script-level scoring. The closest comparison is MMLU-style multitask QA: AGIEval narrows the surface to exams explicitly written for human evaluation and includes fields such as law, math, college entrance, and civil-service testing. The direction label is static reasoning benchmark with answer-key feedback. Its reuse boundary is the public-release contamination risk, exact task subset, prompt template, answer parser, and license of the underlying exam material.
