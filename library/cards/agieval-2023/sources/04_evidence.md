The evidence for the benchmark is the released task/data/evaluation repository plus the paper's reported 8,062-question, 20-task exam collection. The paper reports that contemporary systems, including GPT-4, remained below average human performance on several exam-style tasks, while chain-of-thought and few-shot prompting changed scores substantially. Instance-level evidence is the answer key and scorer output for each question; aggregate accuracy alone does not certify reasoning quality. The evidence boundary is static public data, task-specific answer parsing, prompt sensitivity, and possible overlap with model pretraining or benchmark practice sets.
