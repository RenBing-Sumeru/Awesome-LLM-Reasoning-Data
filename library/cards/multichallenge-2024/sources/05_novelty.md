The prior-work baseline is nearby benchmark and evaluation-surface datasets that share the same feedback contract. The change introduced by MultiChallenge: A Realistic Multi-Turn Conversation Evaluation Benchmark Challenging to Frontier LLMs is the particular task object and feedback contract: task prompt, model answer, judge/checker output, and score. scored by mixed exact, rule, or judge scoring. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17399), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
