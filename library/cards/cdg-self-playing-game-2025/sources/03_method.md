The main self-play pool combines all 7,473 GSM8K training questions with 12,000 questions from the MATH training set. For each question, the Prover samples four initial solutions. Each incorrect question–solution pair receives eight Helpful-Critic samples; each correct pair receives four Misleading-Critic samples. For every question–solution–critique triplet, the Prover samples four revisions. Rollout decoding uses temperature 0.95, top-p 0.95, top-k 5, and maximum length 4,096.

The paper sets Prover, Misleading-Critic, and Helpful-Critic selection thresholds to 0.5, 0.75, and 0.5. Concretely, a helpful critique is retained if at least two of four revisions become correct. A misleading critique is retained if at least three of four revisions fail to emit `\boxed{This critic is not critical.}` and turn a correct answer into an incorrect one. The Prover set balances 10,000 initially correct, 10,000 misleading-resistant, and 10,000 helpful-corrected examples.

Selected Prover records become multi-turn conversations containing the question, initial answer, critique, and revision; critic records become instruction/output pairs. Official scripts use intermediate fields including `prompt`, `responses`, `response`, `metadata`, `round1_response`, `critic`, `conversations`, `instruction`, `input`, `output`, `system`, and `mask_history`. The repository's `data/train.jsonl` contains 19,473 raw source questions with answer and provenance-like metadata, not the selected rollout release linked on Google Drive.

The paper describes two self-play/training iterations and reports learning rates of 5e-6 then 1e-6, batch sizes 32 then 256, one epoch for the Prover and Misleading Critic, and two epochs for the Helpful Critic. The checked `main.sh` instead defaults to three loops, updates role checkpoints across loops, leaves environment-specific paths unconfigured, and does not constitute a pinned reproduction manifest. This paper–script gap should be resolved before claiming exact replication.
