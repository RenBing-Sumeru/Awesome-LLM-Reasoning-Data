Prompt synthesis has five reported stages: remove constraints from seed instructions; generate additional unconstrained prompts in batches of 20; remove semantic duplicates; sample a compatible constraint set and its keyword arguments; and rewrite the prompt so the constraints appear naturally. Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct performs every model-based generation step. Some keyword arguments are randomly sampled when they do not depend on the prompt, while context-sensitive arguments are generated by the 70B model. The paper names all-mpnet-base-v2 and dot-product similarity for deduplication but not the similarity threshold or reject manifest.

In the main RS setup, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct generates N=64 complete responses independently for each prompt at temperature 1.0. The verifier returns `R = |C|^-1 sum V(response, prompt, constraint)` and the curator extracts every non-overlapping pair whose chosen score equals c and rejected score equals r. "Non-overlapping responses" is not operationally defined, and the paper does not state whether one response can participate in multiple retained pairs.

MCTS operates at token-sequence level. A node is a partial response, and an action is a bounded token sequence from one state to another. Selection uses PUCT over Q-values, visit counts, and length-normalized policy scores. Expansion proposes K=4 actions and runs M=4 rollouts per action. The rollout term averages programmatic constraint satisfaction; a policy self-evaluation asks whether the partial response still follows content-based conditions and averages multiple yes/no probability judgments over L generations. The search reward weights verifier rollouts by 0.8 and self-evaluation by 0.2. Rewards are backpropagated through visits and Q-values. The reported maximum depth is 5 and c_puct is 1.0, but tree-iteration count, L, action-token limit, and detailed terminal conditions are unknown.

Final MCTS pairs come from eligible sibling nodes, after sampling complete rollouts, and are filtered only with verifier V. DPO then fine-tunes Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct for one epoch, maximum sequence length 2,048, learning rate 5e-7, linear scheduling, and four gradient-accumulation steps. The SFT comparison trains on chosen responses for three epochs in bf16 with learning rate 2e-6, total batch size 32, and the same maximum length and accumulation count. DPO batch size, beta, full optimizer configuration, hardware, seeds, pair count, and unique-prompt count are not reported.
