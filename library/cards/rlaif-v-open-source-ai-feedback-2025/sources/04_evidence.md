Claim: decomposed open-source feedback improves visual trustworthiness. Across six reported benchmarks, RLAIF-V 7B reduces object hallucination by 80.7% and overall hallucination by 33.7%; the paper also reports that its 12B model outperforms GPT-4V on four named trustworthiness benchmarks. These are paper-specific benchmark comparisons, not evidence that its synthetic labels equal human feedback or eliminate hallucination.
