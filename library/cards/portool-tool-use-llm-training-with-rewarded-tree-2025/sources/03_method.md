The pipeline starts with 3,607 de-identified production queries in 8 domains and splits them into 3,072 training and 535 evaluation items. The private sandbox exposes 21 production-style search, context, calculation, conversion, sorting, and terminal tools. Runtime outputs or explicit errors are appended to the policy context. Time-sensitive evaluation fixes location to Cupertino, California and records timestamps at second precision.

Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct and Qwen-3-1.7B receive 5 epochs of supervised fine-tuning, followed by 15 epochs of PORTool RL, reported as 90 update steps. For each training query, the policy generates 8 stochastic rollouts with at most 6 tool-call steps; each reasoning step is capped at 2,048 tokens. Training decoding uses temperature 1.0, top-p 1.0, and top-k -1. The implementation uses VeRL, VLLM, LoRA rank 16, batch size 512, PPO-style minibatch 128, learning rate 1e-6, no KL loss, maximum prompt length 30,720, maximum per-response length 1,024, and 8 H100 GPUs.

At a fork, PORTool derives provisional child values from discounted descendant terminal outcomes and bounded format/execution feedback. If siblings are discriminative, adaptive aggregation emphasizes the best branch; if their best cases tie, it averages descendants to prefer reliable rather than lucky children. The resulting step importance is normalized into fork-relative advantage and scaled before being mixed with trajectory-relative advantage.

Reproduction requires the exact query, SFT initialization, model checkpoint, decoding seed, complete 8-branch tree, tool schemas and backend versions, runtime responses, time/location context, all 5 raw judge votes, and optimizer state. The public appendix provides schemas, prompts, settings, and examples, but not this executable bundle.
