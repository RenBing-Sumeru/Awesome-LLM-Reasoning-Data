The formal title is CriticEval; the candidate-list title “CriticBench: Evaluating Large Language Models as Critic” is inaccurate. CriticEval divides critic ability into feedback, comparison, refinement, and meta-feedback across nine scenarios including translation, dialogue, question answering, safety, summarization, mathematics, and code. It contains both scalar judgments and textual critiques, responses at multiple quality levels, human reference feedback, and an automated scoring pipeline for comparing open and proprietary LLMs.
