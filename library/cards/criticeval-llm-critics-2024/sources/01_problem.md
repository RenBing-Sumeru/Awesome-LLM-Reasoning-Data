LLMs are widely used to critique responses, compare candidates, refine erroneous answers, and evaluate other critiques, yet existing benchmarks usually test only one capability with disconnected tasks and protocols. A model may identify a mathematical error but fail to propose an actionable revision, or compare answers without judging whether feedback itself is sound. The paper asks how to build a unified critic evaluation across tasks, output forms, and difficulty levels and whether open-ended critiques can be scored reliably.
