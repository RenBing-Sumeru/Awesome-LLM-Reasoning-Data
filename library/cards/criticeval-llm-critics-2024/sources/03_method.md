1. **Define abilities:** Critique is decomposed into direct feedback, response comparison, feedback-guided refinement, and meta-feedback.
2. **Collect scenarios:** Nine subsets are built from translation, chat, QA, harmlessness, summarization, mathematical CoT and PoT, and executable and non-executable code.
3. **Create quality gradients:** Responses from different models and controlled errors cover obvious failures, subtle defects, and strong answers.
4. **Build references:** Human critiques are written or reviewed for key examples, together with scores, preferences, and revised outputs.
5. **Unified scoring:** Objective tasks use answers or execution signals, while open critiques are evaluated with validated strong-model judgments and human comparisons. Data and scripts are released in the repository.
