The authors first validate CriticEval’s automated scoring against human judgments and then evaluate multiple open and proprietary models. Critic ability does not scale proportionally with general generation quality: strong models often perform well on comparison but still lag in feedback completeness, actual refinement gains, and meta-feedback, with rankings changing across tasks. This supports the diagnostic value of the four-way decomposition. However, open-ended text still relies partly on strong-model judges, whose errors may interact with the style of evaluated systems.
