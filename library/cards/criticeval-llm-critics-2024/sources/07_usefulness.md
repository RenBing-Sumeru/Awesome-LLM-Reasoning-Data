CriticEval is useful for evaluating critique models, self-refinement agents, and automatic data filters. Reporting the four dimensions separately reveals whether a system fails to detect an issue, provide actionable feedback, or produce an effective revision. Quality-graded examples can also train critics or meta-critics. Training must remain separate from public tests and prioritize human critiques and objective verifiers. In deployment, fluent feedback should not be trusted unless the revised answer actually improves.
