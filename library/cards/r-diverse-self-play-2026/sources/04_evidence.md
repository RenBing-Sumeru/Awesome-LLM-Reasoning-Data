Across seven mathematical and three general-reasoning benchmarks, Table 1 reports that five-iteration R-Diverse raises Math AVG from 42.58 to 52.59 for Qwen3-4B-Base and from 49.18 to 56.46 for Qwen3-8B-Base. Three-iteration variants score 50.68 and 55.40. Overall AVG moves from 27.10 to 36.68 for 4B and from 34.49 to 40.75 for 8B. Evaluation is greedy pass@1 except AMC and AIME, which use mean@32. These results measure trained models under the authors' protocol; they do not verify every generated question, pseudo-label, code abstraction, or replay record. (Paper Table 1, §4.1.)

The Qwen3-4B ablation reports Math AVG 52.59 for the full method, 49.62 without MAP, 51.18 without memory replay, and 50.50 without SAM. Removing the max and mean MAP terms together gives 49.88; removing representation abstraction gives 50.79, while replacing embedding-based similarity gives 51.05. These comparisons support that the named components affect the reported outcome, but do not separate record quality from optimizer dynamics or establish statistical reliability because seeds and repeated-run variance are absent. (Paper Table 2.)

The paper also measures the proposed failure mode. For R-Diverse, the GPT-4o duplicate ratio over top-3 retrieved historical neighbors falls from 59% to 53%, while Challenger entropy rises from 0.64 to 0.94; R-Zero's duplicate ratio rises from 71% to 84%. Five 200-question iteration sets receive GPT-4o ground-truth labels, and the preceding Solver's pass rate on its target set stays near the intended 50% region, from 47.5% to 50.5% across the diagonal. These are mechanism-aligned analyses, but SAM embeddings and GPT-4o judgments remain proxies, not released record-level correctness evidence. (Paper §5.3–§5.4, Figure 4, Table 3.)

Artifact evidence is much weaker. The official GitHub repository was rechecked on 2026-07-22: it shows one commit, a README, and figures, with no implementation, generated data, model weights, memory bank, LICENSE, tags, or releases. The README's release plan is prospective. Consequently the accepted metadata remains `status: partial` and `artifact_verified: false` despite the paper-level experimental evidence.
