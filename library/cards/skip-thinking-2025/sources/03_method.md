The pipeline begins with task examples containing a question and answer. Following the setup attributed to Ho et al., text-davinci-002 generates rationales with few-shot exemplars whose rationale and answer positions are swapped, a maximum sequence length of 128, and temperature 0.7. The experiments use seven reasoning benchmarks spanning arithmetic, symbolic, commonsense, and shuffled-object reasoning, with GPT-2 and T5 variants as students. The paper follows the earlier work's train/test partitioning; a released item-level manifest and decontamination record were not verified.

CWT first partitions each rationale into a fixed number of chunks. Average Chunking is the starting comparison, while SBC merges adjacent material and searches possible cut positions, accepting a boundary change when the student's loss improves beyond a threshold. The resulting examples use stage markers and prefixes so that each chunk, followed by the answer stage, becomes a separate target. The boundary search is updated with the student during training rather than being a one-time semantic annotation.

For STT, the skip data generator removes a candidate chunk, supplies the preceding context and stage marker to the CWT-trained student, and checks the predicted answer. An incorrect answer means the chunk must remain explicit; a correct answer makes it eligible for internalization. Training uses the resulting skip examples, and inference uses the paper's special skip control to emit only retained reasoning chunks before the answer. The verifier is the benchmark answer predicate, not a step verifier. Code, processed chunk data, per-removal logs, seeds, and exact replay environment remain unavailable in the confirmed artifacts.
