For the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track, Skip-Thinking is useful as a training-time trace-transformation case rather than a test-time search corpus. It shows that a shortened rationale has a hidden lineage: multiple possible boundaries and deletion trials precede the visible output. That lineage is necessary when comparing long-to-short distillation recipes or studying whether compression removes redundancy, uncertainty, or essential dependencies.

A reusable schema should store the task item and split, teacher prompt and version, original rationale and answer, initial and revised chunk boundaries, stage markers, student checkpoint, per-candidate loss, threshold decision, removal order, every predicted answer and correctness check, retained/skipped label, final compressed trace, and downstream model setting. Training-time construction signals must remain separate from evaluation accuracy. Without those records, the paper is most useful as a recipe and audit checklist, not as an open dataset source.
