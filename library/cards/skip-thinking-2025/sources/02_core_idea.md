Skip-Thinking separates the recipe into Chunk-Wise Training (CWT) and Skip-Thinking Training (STT). CWT converts one long rationale into staged prefix-to-next-chunk examples so the student learns one chunk at a time. Its Search-Based Chunking (SBC) starts from chunk boundaries and greedily adjusts them using the current student's loss as a heuristic for internally coherent, learnable segments. STT then removes chunks one by one, runs the CWT-trained student toward the answer, and labels a removed chunk as skippable only when answer correctness is preserved.

The data object is therefore a teacher rationale plus evolving chunk boundaries, per-chunk loss comparisons, deletion trials, answer-correctness outcomes, and the final retained/skipped sequence. The feedback contract is programmatic at the answer level but only a proxy at the chunk level. Unlike full-rationale CoT distillation, the method changes the unit of supervision; unlike fully latent reasoning, it retains selected explicit chunks. Teacher-generated CoT, chunking, and rationale internalization are not individually new—the contribution is their loss-guided and correctness-gated combination.
