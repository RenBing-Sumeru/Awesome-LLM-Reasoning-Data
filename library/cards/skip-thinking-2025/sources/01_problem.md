The official EMNLP 2025 paper studies a specific weakness of chain-of-thought distillation: a small language model is usually trained against an entire teacher rationale in one update, so many transitional or stylistic tokens can dilute the gradient contribution of the tokens that carry the next reasoning dependency. The resulting model must also emit the full rationale before its answer, increasing response time. The paper tests whether the rationale can be divided and selectively shortened without abandoning explicit reasoning altogether.

For this atlas, the relevant object is not merely the final student model. It is the transformation from a question, answer, and text-davinci-002 rationale into chunked training examples and retained or skipped chunks. The decision boundary is answer correctness after removing a chunk: a chunk whose removal preserves the answer is treated as non-essential for the Skip-Thinking training recipe. This is an algorithmic construction signal, not proof that the removed text is causally irrelevant, and no reusable compressed-trace release was verified.
