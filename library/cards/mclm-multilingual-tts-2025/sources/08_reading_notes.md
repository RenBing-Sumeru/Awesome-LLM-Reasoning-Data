Read the paper as an evaluation correction to optimistic scaling claims. Start with its FLOPs accounting, because the comparison among outcome rewards, process rewards, and budget forcing is meaningful only after verifier and generator cost are both counted. Then contrast English gains with per-language gains and consistency. The key lesson is not that one strategy wins, but that method selection and token allocation must be evaluated on the language distribution that will actually be served. The released model and benchmark support that check, while the multilingual failure cases are as informative as the average score.
