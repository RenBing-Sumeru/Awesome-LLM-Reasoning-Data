Use this work when deciding whether a test-time budget policy is safe to ship in several languages. Report language-stratified accuracy, variance, consistency, candidate count, branch width and depth, reasoning tokens, generator and verifier FLOPs, answer extraction success, wall-clock latency, and price. Compare strategies at matched total cost and inspect every language rather than only a macro average. MCLM is particularly useful as a regression suite for a policy that is developed in English but expected to serve multilingual mathematics, because it makes a lack of transfer visible before the policy is generalized by assumption.
