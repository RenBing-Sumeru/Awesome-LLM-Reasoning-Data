The study focuses on mathematical reasoning and primarily smaller models, so its language-generalization diagnosis may not transfer unchanged to code, agents, or larger proprietary systems. MCLM translations and answer parsing can introduce language-specific artifacts, while FLOPs matching does not capture every practical cost of different generators, reward models, or tokenizers. The findings also depend on the tested 55-language distribution and selected methods. A deployment should therefore test its own language mix, verifier reliability, script and formatting effects, and per-language latency before treating an English scaling curve as evidence of multilingual robustness.
