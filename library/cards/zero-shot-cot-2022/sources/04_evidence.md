**Main comparison (reported arithmetic results).** Claim: one generic cue plus answer extraction materially improves zero-shot multi-step solving. Controlled setup: the same text-davinci-002 model and benchmark questions are used; standard zero-shot asks directly for the answer, whereas Zero-shot-CoT adds the rationale call and second answer-extraction call, increasing inference cost. Accuracy rises from 17.7% to 78.7% on MultiArith and from 10.4% to 40.7% on GSM8K. These results support an elicitation effect for the tested model/tasks, but do not validate rationale steps, isolate the exact cue wording from the two-call design, or show that the traces are safe training labels.
