RLHF and preference modeling predate this work. Its contribution is a current taxonomy that makes the feedback-to-model-to-optimizer chain explicit and includes benchmark evaluation. That update matters because recent systems use human feedback, AI feedback, process signals, and judge-like evaluators in overlapping roles; the taxonomy makes the overlap inspectable rather than collapsing it into “RLHF data.” It should not be read as evidence that a judge’s output is ground truth or that a reward-model score transfers unchanged across prompts, policies, and optimization settings.
