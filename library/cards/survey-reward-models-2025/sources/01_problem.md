Terms such as preference data, reward model, judge, and verifier are routinely conflated in post-training discussions. This 2025 survey at https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12328 maps reward models from preference collection through modeling and use to evaluation. It establishes the data lineage before readers enter feedback-data, reward, or judge-specific tracks. In particular, a preference pair, the policy that produced it, an annotator decision, and a learned score are different records with different failure modes. Treating them as one object makes provenance and reliability claims hard to interpret.
