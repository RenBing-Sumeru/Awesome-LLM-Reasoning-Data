1. **Positioning:** STELAR-VISION addresses the mismatch between one-size-fits-all chain-of-thought and visual problems requiring different reasoning structures.
2. **Method handle:** Multi-topology generation, final-answer labeling, topology scoring, outcome-reward filtering, SimPO, and short-correct preference construction determine performance.
3. **Artifact handle:** The released dataset contains approximately 123K visual-reasoning preference records covering chain, tree, and graph structures.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Overall in-distribution accuracy rises from 18.3% for the base model and 23.7% for Chain-Only to 28.0%; Frugal Learning reduces length by 18.1%.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is suitable for verifiable visual reasoning, but topology-format shortcuts and incorrect intermediate steps must be audited before reuse.
