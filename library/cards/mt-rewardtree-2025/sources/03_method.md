Inputs are source sentences from WMT17–WMT20 test sets and FLORES development/test sets in DE–EN, EN–DE, RU–EN, EN–RU, ZH–EN, and EN–ZH. The exact upstream versions, record IDs, and allocation manifests are undisclosed. TowerInstruct-7B-v0.2 is the generator, with temperature 0.95. The paper models translation as a deterministic token MDP: a generated prefix is the state, a token is the action, and EOS terminates a rollout.

For each selected prefix, the approximate MCTS pipeline expands the top-2 next tokens by model logits. From each sibling it samples three complete translations to EOS. COMETKiwi scores each completion, and the mean of the three scores becomes that sibling node’s value. The higher-valued sibling supplies the prefix for the next cycle. For data retention, the pipeline selects one rollout from the superior sibling and one from the inferior sibling and keeps the pair only when their COMETKiwi score gap is between 0.04 and 0.4. The number and identities of filtered-out candidates, search-depth distribution, maximum length, top-p, seeds, retry policy, and total compute are unknown (paper §3.1; §4.1; Figure 2).

The resulting training release has 8,652 records across the six directions. The prefixed and arbitrary repositories each expose one split named `train` with `instruction`, `input`, `chosen`, `rejected`, `chosen_score`, and `rejected_score`. MT-PRMBench has 1,200 records—200 per direction—and its prefixed and arbitrary repositories also expose a sole split named `train`, with the same fields except `input`. The public schema does not state whether the two scalar scores are retained-rollout COMETKiwi scores, three-rollout node means, or another export (Table 2; official Hugging Face viewers).

The authors train implicit PRMs with DPO or KTO and `beta=0.1`, using LLaMA-3.2-3B-Instruct and Qwen-2.5-3B-Instruct backbones. Their equations derive per-token process rewards from policy/reference log-probability ratios and aggregate weighted token rewards for sequence scoring. Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct is later paired with the trained PRMs for reward-guided test-time decoding; this is an inference-time application, not evidence of RL integration (§3.2; §4.1; §5.3).

Reproduction requires pinning the generator, PRM backbones, COMETKiwi checkpoint, paper/code/data/model revisions, and WMT/FLORES versions, then recovering the missing decoding, optimizer, split, and decontamination settings. The officially linked GitHub repository was identified, but its contents and license were not verified in the curation environment, so the Card does not claim an executable end-to-end replay.
