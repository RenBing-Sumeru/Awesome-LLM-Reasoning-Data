Hardware-code benchmarks mostly generate isolated HDL modules from specifications and do not test agents on historical bugs spanning RTL, configuration, firmware, and verification files in real SoC repositories. HWE-Bench builds repository-level repair tasks from real pull requests. Agents read issue descriptions and full codebases, submit patches, and are judged by native simulation or regression flows. The goal is hardware-semantic reasoning and cross-artifact coordination, not merely Verilog syntax.
