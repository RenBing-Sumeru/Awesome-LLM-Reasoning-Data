The contribution is a standardized long-context evaluation suite plus metric guidance for closed and open-ended tasks. The core mechanism collects 20 subtasks, 508 long documents, and more than 2,000 human-labeled query-response pairs across domains and input lengths from about 3K to 200K tokens. Each sample exposes `instructions`, `outputs`, `input`, `source`, and `evaluation` fields. The feedback contract is mixed: exact/exam/F1/ROUGE metrics for closed or QA-style tasks, LIE-adjusted open-ended evaluation, GPT-4/GPT-3.5 judge battles against baselines such as Turbo-16k, and human evaluation subsets. Closest comparisons are LongBench, ZeroSCROLLS, SCROLLS, NarrativeQA, Qasper, GovReport, and other long-document QA/summarization suites; L-Eval's direction label is standardized long-context benchmark with metric-bias audit.
