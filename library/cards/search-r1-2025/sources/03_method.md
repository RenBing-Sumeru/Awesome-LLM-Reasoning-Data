Training prompts merge the NQ and HotpotQA training sets. Rollout uses Qwen2.5 3B or 7B base/instruct policies, a 2018 Wikipedia corpus, E5 retrieval, and three returned passages per call. Generation continues until an <answer> span is produced or the maximum action budget is reached; malformed actions trigger a fixed rethink message. The paper evaluates PPO and GRPO. Its rejection-sampling baseline generates five candidates per prompt and keeps answer-correct trajectories, but that baseline is distinct from the Search-R1 RL data contract.
