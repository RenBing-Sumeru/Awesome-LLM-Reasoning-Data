All model scores are author-reported and were not independently reproduced. Under the common protocol of four agents, two runs per task, and a 15-step horizon, Table 3 reports overall Success Rate of **21.14% for UI-TARS-1.5 7B, 11.40% for Qwen3-VL 2B, 24.23% for Qwen3-VL 4B, and 31.83% for OpenAI Computer Use Preview** (Paper §4 and Table 3, p. 6). These results compare evaluated agents under the paper's environment and maximum-of-two aggregation; they do not establish evaluator correctness or trajectory-data quality.

The source-subset result is a useful warning about familiarity and contamination. UI-TARS leads OpenAI CUA on the OSWorld subset, **21.27 versus 16.74**, but trails it on the MacArena-specific subset, **10.20 versus 36.73**, a 26.53-point reversal (Paper Table 3 and §4.2, pp. 6–7). The authors hypothesize task-distribution or platform familiarity, but no training-exposure audit is supplied. The reversal is therefore evidence for auditing lineage, not proof of a particular causal mechanism.

Official artifact counts corroborate the evaluation surface. The repository tree contains exactly **421 task JSONs = 221 OSWorld-derived + 151 macOSWorld-derived + 49 custom**, and every custom JSON has one evaluator with weight 100. The official Hugging Face bucket contains nine files totaling **121,038,630,504 bytes**, including the two `.utm` folders; these are executable VM assets, not a rollout dataset. Humans reportedly reviewed all tasks, but no measured human-performance baseline, annotator agreement, evaluator test coverage, or rejection statistics are reported.

Artifact inspection also provides direct negative evidence. Task `450f6f33-bf2c-43bf-a349-4363e9b75740` instructs merging Contacts entries, while its evaluator checks a Session Setapp website-blocker/YouTube state. This instruction/evaluator mismatch contradicts any blanket assumption that every released score measures its stated task. Separately, code inspection shows that the second of two runs closes applications but does not reclone the VM, so maximum-of-two results may combine runs with different residual-state conditions.

The runner's output schema demonstrates the ability to retain successes, zero scores, and errors locally: screenshot PNGs, action/reward/done/info entries, response history, result, log, video, and exception objects. However, no canonical public rollout bundle for the paper experiments was found. Evidence of logging capability must not be rewritten as evidence that the normalized reported trajectories were released.
