- **Verifier validity:** Final-state scripts can produce false positives or false negatives under application, filesystem, accessibility, shell, window-title, or version drift. The released Contacts/Session task mismatch is a confirmed semantic defect, not a hypothetical risk. No evaluator error-rate study or comprehensive unit-test ledger is reported.
- **Reset discrepancy:** The paper says every episode uses a clean copy-on-use VM. The audited two-run helper fully resets only run 1; run 2 closes applications and runs with `reset_env=False`. Residual files, settings, background processes, caches, or external-service state can affect the second score and the maximum-of-two aggregate.
- **Construction provenance:** The paper says tasks were manually created and reviewed, while all 49 custom JSONs say `source: synth_generation`. No model, prompt, sampling protocol, annotator workflow, rejection count, agreement measure, or adjudication explains the discrepancy. An LLM generator must not be inferred from the label alone.
- **Release boundary:** The runner can create rich local screenshot/action/reward/result/video records, but no canonical public success/failure/timeout rollout corpus or immutable manifest of the reported experiments was confirmed. Task and VM availability is not equivalent to trajectory availability or deterministic replay.
- **Split and contamination:** No train/dev/test split, hidden-test policy, duplicate audit, or formal model-exposure/decontamination procedure is disclosed. All prompts/evaluators are public and 372 tasks inherit OSWorld/macOSWorld lineage, enabling leakage and grader gaming. The observed ranking reversal is not a contamination audit.
- **Privacy and safety:** Annotator demographics, compensation, consent, and a privacy/redaction audit are unknown. Local outputs contain desktop screenshots, response histories, logs, and videos; the 121 GB VM bundle was not exhaustively inspected for embedded state or credentials. Some setup scripts access live URLs, adding side-effect, content-drift, and sandboxing risks.
- **Replay and systems cost:** Reproduction requires Apple Silicon, UTM, licensed macOS, large VM downloads, third-party applications, and mutable external resources. A host supports at most two guest VMs. Seeds, exact model/API snapshots, decoding settings, paper-bound VM/task hashes, and a deterministic replay ledger are unknown.
- **Licensing:** MacPaw code/framework/custom tasks are stated as MIT, OSWorld tasks as Apache-2.0, and macOSWorld tasks as CC BY-NC 4.0; Apple EULA and third-party application terms remain separate. Referenced component paths/licenses are not fully aligned with the audited tree, so redistribution and commercial-use rights require record/component-level review.
- **Evaluation scope:** The paper reports no human-performance baseline. Four-agent benchmark scores do not establish production reliability, evaluator correctness, safety, or fitness as training data. The accepted `training_use` is therefore evaluation and audit only.
