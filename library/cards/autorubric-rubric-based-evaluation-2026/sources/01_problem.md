Rubric evaluation implementations are fragmented: some support only binary criteria, others ask a judge to score many dimensions at once, and calibration, weighting, voting, and psychometric diagnosis are often absent. The same rubric therefore yields incomparable scores across codebases, and judge errors cannot be traced to criterion definition, scale, or aggregation.

Autorubric provides a unified framework for multiple criterion types, single or multiple judges, calibration, voting, and statistical analysis, validated on CHARM-100 and other tasks.
