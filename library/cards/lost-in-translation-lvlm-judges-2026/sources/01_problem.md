An English LVLM judge may change its decision on translated image-text tasks because of wording, cultural expression, or chart terminology even when the image and answer meaning remain unchanged. Strong English performance therefore does not establish stable cross-lingual multimodal judging.

MM-JudgeBench localizes image-text tasks into 25 languages, verifies translation meaning, chart understanding, and preference labels, and measures language transfer and cross-cultural bias.
