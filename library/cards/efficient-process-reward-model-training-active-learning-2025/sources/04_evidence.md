The most important controlled experiment starts from the same 100,000 decontaminated NuminaMath trajectories, uses the same Qwen2.5-Math-7B-Instruct backbone and QwQ-32B labels, and changes only active selection versus random selection. With 50% of the annotation budget, ActPRM reaches 0.673 average F1 on ProcessBench, matching full-data training and outperforming random selection by 3.3 points at the same budget. At 62.5% budget, it reaches 0.680. After constructing ActPRMData from a million-scale pool, the resulting model obtains 75.0% on ProcessBench and 65.5% on PRMBench. These results support higher value per annotation, although the final labels are generated by QwQ-32B rather than verified individually by humans.
