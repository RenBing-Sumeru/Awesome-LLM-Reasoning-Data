First, ActPRM assumes that the current model’s uncertainty represents sample value. A confidently wrong PRM may discard useful trajectories, creating an active-learning blind spot. Reuse should retain a random sample of low-uncertainty records to estimate the miss rate. Second, step labels depend entirely on QwQ-32B’s first-error judgments, so systematic mathematical mistakes can enter the dataset; human audits should be stratified by difficulty and label pattern. Third, only steps up to the first error are labeled, making the data unsuitable for studying recovery after an error or the quality of later reasoning.
