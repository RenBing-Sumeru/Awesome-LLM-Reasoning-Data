Researchers with large mathematical problem and trajectory pools but limited labeling budgets can first train an initial PRM, apply ActPRM’s two uncertainty criteria, and send only selected trajectories to a strong LLM or human expert, producing a dataset with first-error labels. ActPRMData can also be used directly to train a discriminative PRM and evaluate first-error F1 on ProcessBench and PRMBench. Its `stds` field supports analysis of model disagreement and labeling errors. The procedure is unsuitable for reasoning text without clear step boundaries and should not be used without a reasonably reliable initial PRM and step-level judge.
