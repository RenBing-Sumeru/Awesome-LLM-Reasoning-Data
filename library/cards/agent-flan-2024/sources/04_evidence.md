The main comparison uses Llama2-7B and the same held-in/held-out evaluation suite for a reimplemented AgentTuning and Agent-FLAN. Overall score rises from 38.2 to 41.7; T-Eval rises from 61.8 to 66.0 and WebArena from 2.71 to 4.68 (Table 1), while the training-token count falls from the 37.3M all-aligned mixture to 18.1M after capability weighting (Table 2). This supports the redesigned mixture under this backbone, but format conversion and changed data weights move together.

The negative-sample ablation holds the Agent-FLAN setup and removes its negative examples. Agent-H falls from 89.1 to 84.5 without negatives, with ReAct-format hallucination increasing from 9.9 to 15.6, while T-Eval stays 66.0 versus 66.3 (Table 3). This isolates the value of explicit non-tool cases for hallucination behavior, but Agent-H checks output mode and does not establish semantic safety for arbitrary tools.
