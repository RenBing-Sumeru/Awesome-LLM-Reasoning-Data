Performance depends on evidence retrieval, the definition of claim complexity, and the verifier’s ability to distinguish plausible but wrong paths; an error at any of these stages can misallocate compute or select a polished hallucination. Complexity derived from latent states may not transfer across base models or changing claim distributions. The paper evaluates reported fact-checking datasets and labels, so its gains should not be read as a guarantee for time-sensitive news, adversarial misinformation, or claims lacking retrievable evidence. The extra paths and verifier also add latency that must be measured in a deployed pipeline.
