Audit the router and verifier separately. First measure whether the latent complexity classifier sends genuinely difficult claims to the expensive path; then measure whether the verifier selects a better trajectory than majority voting from the identical candidate pool. Retrieval must be frozen when comparing the selection policy, otherwise improved evidence can be mistaken for improved test-time scaling. A practical replication should report per-complexity-bin accuracy, routing rate, verifier calibration, path count, evidence coverage, and end-to-end cost. This reveals whether selective scaling saves compute without silently denying hard cases the needed exploration.
