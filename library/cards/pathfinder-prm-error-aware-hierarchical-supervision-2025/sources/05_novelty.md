Compared with assigning a whole chain of thought one correct-or-incorrect label, this work changes the unit or acquisition path of process evidence. PathFinder-PRM constructs about 400,000 hierarchical error-aware process examples that distinguish first-error steps from error propagation. It combines PRM800K with model traces and labels steps as correct, first-error, or contaminated subsequent steps. This is its direction signal; the reusable contribution is the data contract, quality control, and evaluation boundary.
