TGPO first appeared as arXiv:2509.14172 on 17 September 2025; this Card reads v2 dated 19 September 2025. The same title and author list later entered the ICASSP 2026 proceedings under DOI 10.1109/ICASSP55912.2026.11464574 and was scheduled in the official program for 5 May 2026. The Card therefore preserves the assigned 2025 first-publication year while recording the current formal venue as ICASSP 2026.

The paper addresses a supervision-allocation problem in offline web-agent learning. A complete run usually has only a success/failure label. Assigning that terminal label to every action makes good decisions inside failed runs look negative and poor decisions inside successful runs look positive; manual step annotation is expensive. Sparse terminal feedback also provides little direct pressure against redundant or cyclic interactions.

The source episode is a user instruction followed by web states and actions until success or failure. A state is represented as a screenshot or DOM tree, and actions include click, type, and scroll. The reported task pools contain 300 Online-Mind2Web tasks from 136 real websites and 50 self-constructed C-WebShop Chinese e-commerce tasks from Taobao. Multiple executions of a task are the input to TGPO, but the paper does not disclose the execution count, generator policy, retained episode count, or a serialized row schema.

TGPO belongs in `environment_agent_trajectory_data` because the trainable object retains instruction, environment state, action, transition, terminal outcome, and a derived state-transition tree. The environment is part of the feedback contract rather than a presentation layer. The paper is not a general release of web trajectories or a replayable browser environment: no raw episodes, merged trees, task manifest, environment snapshot, or terminal-check implementation is available.

Official paper and venue records, the full v2 method, equations, models, task counts, and reported results support L4-depth screening of the mechanism and risks. They do not support an upgrade of the accepted `L3_summary_ready` metadata level or a claim of reusable data. Code, data, model checkpoints, project assets, environment package, and license remain unverified.
