The state merge is a consequential heuristic rather than a validated equivalence relation. Standardized URLs, post-change effective action sequences, and image hashes can falsely merge pages that look similar but differ in hidden state, user account, cart contents, timing, or available controls; they can also fail to merge semantically equivalent pages. Such errors propagate into conflict detection, cycles, shortest paths, all process rewards, and chosen/rejected labels. This is a curator inference grounded in the disclosed merge rule; the paper provides no merge-quality evaluation.

The action-accuracy component depends on an undisclosed VLM. Its model, revision, prompt, expected-interface-change representation, calibration set, adjudication, and error analysis are unknown. A false positive or negative can be amplified when the resulting branch has a large reward gap. Format reward can favor syntactic validity without usefulness, and the cycle penalty can punish intentional revisiting or recovery. Shortest-path progress may penalize necessary verification, accessibility, or safety steps. These are curator-inferred failure modes; no reward-attack or sensitivity study is reported.

The preference objective has unresolved numerical and selection behavior. The paper does not disclose beta, pair counts, tie rules, minimum gaps, duplicate handling, balancing, or safeguards when `sigma(R_s)` is zero or very small. Alpha is only bounded to 2–5 rather than fixed per experiment. Optimizer, batch size, scheduler, sequence limit, seeds, checkpoint selection, and the preceding SFT recipe are also unknown.

Data and evaluation lineage cannot be audited. No task IDs or train/dev/test map establish whether the same or near-duplicate tasks enter SFT, tree construction, preference optimization, model selection, and final evaluation. Raw trajectory counts, success/failure balance, dropped records, tree sizes, pair counts, and record-level provenance are absent. The reported 38.71% and 26.95% conflict rates therefore cannot be traced back to released records.

Exact replay is blocked by missing artifacts: no code, task files, raw or processed trajectories, trees, verifier decisions, chosen/rejected pairs, model checkpoints, browser or environment image, terminal-check implementation, configuration, or release tag was verified. Browser-use and SeeAct versions, website snapshots, cookies, authentication, reset semantics, and browser state are unknown, while live websites and Taobao can drift independently of the method.

Rights and privacy are unresolved. No code, data, model, environment, or C-WebShop license was verified; paper access does not grant reuse rights for screenshots, DOM content, Taobao tasks, generated trajectories, or model outputs. The paper does not discuss credentials, personal-data handling, consent, terms of service, or redaction. Results from two stacks and 350 reported tasks also do not establish robustness across websites, agents, models, or time, and no independent reproduction was found in the confirmed artifacts.
