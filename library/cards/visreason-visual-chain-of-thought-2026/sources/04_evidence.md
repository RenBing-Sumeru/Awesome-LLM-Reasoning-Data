The authors fine-tune the same Qwen2.5-VL base on VisReason and VisReason-Pro and evaluate step accuracy, interpretability, and cross-dataset transfer on multiple visual reasoning benchmarks. Both datasets substantially outperform the base model, while the Pro version is stronger on spatial and compositional tasks. The results support scale and grounding, although gains are also influenced by the stronger annotation model.

The authors also report domain- and task-specific results so aggregate gains are not driven by one subset.
