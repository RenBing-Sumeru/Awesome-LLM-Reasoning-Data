VisReason contains about 489K annotated examples across four visual domains. Each example includes an image, question, multi-round human-like rationale, answer, and region-of-interest grounding. The authors also build the 165K-example VisReason-Pro with stronger expert-level GPT annotations and depth-informed 3D spatial grounding. Both resources support visual-CoT SFT, step supervision, and multimodal PRMs.

Records also preserve alignment between image regions and current steps, allowing visual verifiers to consume the process labels directly.
