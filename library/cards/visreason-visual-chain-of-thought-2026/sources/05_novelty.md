Earlier visual-CoT data mainly provides single explanations or small domain-specific sets, with spatial evidence implicit in text. VisReason scales to nearly 489K examples and decomposes reasoning into multiple rounds of visual attention and inference, while the Pro version adds depth-based grounding. The novelty is the combination of scale, step structure, and regional grounding rather than ordinary teacher distillation.

Regional evidence is therefore no longer merely implicit context in explanatory text.
