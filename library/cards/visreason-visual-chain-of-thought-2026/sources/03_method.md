1. **Collect visual tasks:** Organize questions, answers, and localizable visual objects from four image domains.
2. **Generate multi-round rationales:** Split visual reasoning into observation, relation judgment, and synthesis steps.
3. **Annotate spatial grounding:** Add areas of interest to steps; VisReason-Pro further introduces depth-informed 3D grounding.
4. **Clean and train:** Filter image-text inconsistencies and wrong answers, release the 489K/165K datasets, and fine-tune Qwen2.5-VL. The release is also divided by source visual domain to support cross-domain transfer analysis.
