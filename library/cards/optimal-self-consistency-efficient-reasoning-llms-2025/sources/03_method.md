Inputs are evaluation questions from GSM8K, MATH, MMLU, and GPQA-Diamond and generations from Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen2.5-MATH-7B, and Qwen2.5-32B at temperatures 0.4-1.0. To control cost, the experiments first draw 100 generations per question and treat the resulting empirical answer distribution as the simulation distribution; SC variants then resample from that multinomial rather than replaying fresh online inference. Analysis focuses mainly on aligned questions, where the correct answer is the distributional mode.

For dynamic allocation, the record for each question needs its sampled final answers, unique-answer count K, top-two counts n1 and n2, ASC confidence, PPR-1v1 confidence, blended rank, exclusion flag, and each allocation step. Blend-ASC repeatedly samples the lowest-ranked question until global budget T is reached, then outputs empirical modes. The paper evaluates the average samples needed to match SC@64 or SC@128 reducible error and reports batching variants for prompts and incoming query groups.

There is no executable environment or correctness reward in the loop; plurality and beta/count-based confidence are the selector. Fixed-allocation experiments use a smoothed convex approximation with oracle access to empirical margins. Reproduction still requires answer normalization, dataset versions, seeds, the exact aligned-question filter, cached 100-sample distributions, and code, none of which was confirmed as an official release.
