For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, the work suggests a compact schema for allocation-aware self-consistency: question ID and benchmark revision; prompt and decoding configuration; every candidate trace and normalized final answer; answer-frequency table; top-two margin; ASC, PPR, and blended confidence at each step; selected question; budget spent; exclusion or stopping reason; empirical mode; correctness label kept separately from consensus; and latency.

Such records could support research on schedulers, early stopping, calibration, selector training, or auditing whether compute is concentrated on genuinely ambiguous items. They should not be repurposed as positive demonstrations merely because they belong to the majority. The aligned/misaligned flag, external answer correctness, and consensus stability must remain distinct fields. Without the cached generations and allocation logs, current reuse is limited to reimplementing the recipe and testing it on independently generated traces.
