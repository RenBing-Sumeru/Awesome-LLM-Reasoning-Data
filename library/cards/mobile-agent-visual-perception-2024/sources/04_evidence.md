The paper reports Mobile-Eval results over simple, moderate, and difficult task groups. For Mobile-Agent, the reported success rates are 0.91, 0.82, and 0.82, with completion rates of 98.2, 90.9, and 91.3 in the same groups.

The row-level evidence is an executed mobile trajectory judged against task completion and progress/efficiency metrics. This is stronger than a static answer comparison because the action sequence must interact with the mobile UI, but it is weaker than a deterministic verifier because app state, UI versions, and completion judgments can drift.

The evidence boundary is the 2024 Mobile-Eval setup and its chosen apps, difficulty groups, visual tools, and model backend. Results should not be transferred to other phones, app versions, or post-2024 app UIs without rerunning the environment.
