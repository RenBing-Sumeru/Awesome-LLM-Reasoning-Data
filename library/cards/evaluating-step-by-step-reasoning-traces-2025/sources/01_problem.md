Step-by-step reasoning is widely used, but a correct answer does not show whether its intermediate trace is factual, valid, coherent, or useful. This Findings of EMNLP 2025 survey is available from ACL at https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.94/. It addresses inconsistent trace-evaluation practice by mapping the criteria, datasets, and evaluator implementations. A trace evaluator can make different claims depending on the unit it labels, the reference evidence it can see, and whether its result is aggregated over steps, traces, or answers. Its role is to establish that vocabulary before readers consume process-label or trace datasets.
