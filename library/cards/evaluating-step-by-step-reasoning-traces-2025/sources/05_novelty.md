Reasoning evaluation existed before this paper; its contribution is a unified criterion-level map that exposes why evaluator and benchmark results can conflict. It combines the criteria with a review of datasets and evaluator implementations, so a reader can see whether a claimed process score answers the question they care about. The key point is that “process quality” has multiple targets, including semantic correctness, logical validity, readability, and task usefulness. It does not validate every reviewed evaluator or supply a universal scorer.
