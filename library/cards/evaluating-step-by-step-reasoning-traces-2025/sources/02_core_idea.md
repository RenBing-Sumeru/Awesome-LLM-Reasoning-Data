The paper proposes four top-level criteria: factuality, validity, coherence, and utility. Each criterion asks a different question: whether statements are supported, whether reasoning follows, whether the explanation hangs together, and whether it serves a task or reader. Each therefore needs a different data or evaluator contract, so no single outcome score can stand in for all of them. The taxonomy connects trace datasets, evaluator implementations, and evaluation findings without claiming they are interchangeable or that final-answer accuracy validates intermediate reasoning.
