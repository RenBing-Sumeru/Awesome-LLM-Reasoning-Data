Correctness is only relative to the official answer key and evaluator. A correct option does not certify that a model can safely design or run a biological experiment, and a wrong option may reflect formatting, parser, or tool-use constraints rather than absence of domain knowledge.

The public release is not the whole evaluation set, and later dataset updates can alter row-level contents without changing the paper title. Figure/table tasks may depend on source article licensing and rendering; sequence/database tasks depend on database snapshots and construction rules.

Legal reuse risk is lower than unknown because the Hugging Face card lists CC-BY-SA-4.0, but task-level source licenses and share-alike obligations still need to be preserved. Contamination risk is explicit: public questions can enter future training data, and private-test claims require the retained split.
