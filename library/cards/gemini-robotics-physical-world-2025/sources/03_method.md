The generalist pipeline begins with expert teleoperation on an ALOHA 2 fleet. Over 12 months, experts collect thousands of hours across thousands of tasks with varied objects, skills, difficulty, horizon, and dexterity. The report does not publish episode counts, cameras, proprioception, action dimensions, operator/site metadata, failure retention, or a task manifest. Action-labelled episodes are mixed with web, code, image/audio/video, embodied-reasoning, and VQA data; sampling weights, augmentation, filters, and licenses are unknown.

At model input time, a record supplies current-scene images and a natural-language task instruction. A distilled Gemini Robotics-ER cloud backbone processes the multimodal context, and an onboard local decoder predicts chunks of continuous low-level actions. Backbone latency is under 160 ms, observation-to-action-chunk latency is about 250 ms, and chunking supports an effective 50 Hz control rate. These are inference measurements, not training compute or rollout budgets.

Task specialization starts from the generalist Gemini Robotics checkpoint and fine-tunes separately on 2,000–5,000 curated high-quality demonstration episodes per long-horizon task. The quality rule, rejects, and yield are not disclosed. Reasoning specialization instead uses a re-labelled action dataset: the model predicts left- and right-arm trajectories for roughly the next one second as an intermediate, then decodes those trajectories to actions. Fast adaptation uses eight short-horizon segments and compares 5, 20, and 100 demonstration episodes.

Evaluation uses task-specific binary success and 0-to-1 progress, repeated physical trials, randomized back-to-back A/B order, and pairwise t-tests. Long-horizon tasks generally receive 20 trials, except the spelling game with 12; each fast-adaptation task/data point receives 10 trials. These success/progress values are evaluation judgments, not a disclosed training reward.

Gemini Robotics batch size, loss, optimizer, training steps, schedules, checkpoint selection, and compute are unknown. Appendix values of 2M and 1M steps belong to multi-task or single-task diffusion baselines, not Gemini Robotics. Reproduction also requires robot calibration, controller and tool versions, cloud/local model checkpoints, distillation targets, relabelling code, complete split membership, and raw trials.
