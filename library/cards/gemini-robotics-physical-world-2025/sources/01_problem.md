Gemini Robotics is a 2025 Google DeepMind report that asks how a Gemini-derived multimodal model can move from embodied understanding to executable physical action. For the Atlas, the important object is not a benchmark score alone: current-scene images and a natural-language task instruction are mapped to chunks of continuous low-level robot actions, using expert robot demonstrations and a distilled vision-language backbone.

The main action source is a fleet of ALOHA 2 robots teleoperated by experts over 12 months, producing thousands of hours of demonstrations across thousands of tasks. The wider training mixture also names web documents, code, image/audio/video content, embodied-reasoning data, and VQA data. Exact records, proportions, splits, rights, failure episodes, and selection rules are unavailable.

This report belongs in the disclosure ledger because it gives unusually concrete data-scale, specialization, trajectory-intermediate, and real-world evaluation interfaces while withholding the proprietary corpus, Gemini training steps, optimizer, weights, and trainer. At L4, the Card supports mechanism screening and audit; it does not make the robot-action data reusable. The separately released 400-item ERQA benchmark is an evaluation artifact, not the proprietary action corpus.
