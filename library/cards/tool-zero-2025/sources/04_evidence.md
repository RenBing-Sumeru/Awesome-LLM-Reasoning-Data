The reported ablation says GG-GRPO adds `5.26` points over vanilla GRPO and `6.8` over SFT on BFCL, and midpoint `25` performs best among the tested switch points. (Paper §5.3, Figures 4–5, Table 5)

Several official-paper numbers conflict. The prose beside Table 5 says `32.28`, not the table's `32.38`. Table 9 changes Tool-Alpaca/average values from Table 4: 7B `65.71/77.65` versus `63.71/77.32`, and 32B `67.38/79.32` versus `64.38/78.99`. The introduction's claimed `7.14%/7.18%` gains also do not match Table 3's absolute gaps of `6.80/6.84`. These discrepancies remain unresolved rather than being normalized by the Card.

The public ToolACE artifact currently exposes 11,300 rows and labels itself a subset, while Table 2 reports 99,266 retained ToolACE records. The difference is evidence of a release gap, not evidence that the public subset is low quality or that the larger corpus is available. Likewise, benchmark scores do not certify record lineage, reward correctness, contamination control, or training-data quality; they only show the behavior of the studied end-to-end configurations on the reported evaluations.
