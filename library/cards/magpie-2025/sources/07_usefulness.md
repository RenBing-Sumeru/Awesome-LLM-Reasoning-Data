Dataset builder: Given an aligned open-weight teacher and its exact chat template, run the two-stage prefix-to-instruction and instruction-to-response recipe, store role-tagged records, then apply a declared filter configuration. The output is an SFT corpus; success requires diversity and duplication audits plus a matched-base-model utility comparison. Do not use this recipe when the teacher license forbids derivative data or the domain needs expert-verified correctness that learned filters cannot supply.

Data auditor: Given a Magpie release, inspect UUID/conversation fields, teacher and template revisions, reward and quality thresholds, response-length distribution, safety labels, and nearest-neighbor distances. The output is a reuse decision and filter ledger; success means every retained subset is reproducible and its terms are compatible with the target model. Do not infer source consent, factuality, or decontamination merely from the dataset being public.
