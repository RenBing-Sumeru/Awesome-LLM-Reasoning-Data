Self-Instruct and related synthetic-data methods start from seed tasks or handcrafted prompts and expand within that initial scope. Magpie changes the prompt-source decision by giving an aligned model only its user-role chat-template prefix, so the teacher invents instructions from the distribution encoded in its weights before answering them. The direction signal is that an aligned checkpoint can act as both an implicit task generator and response teacher at million-record scale. Chat templates, teacher sampling, reward filtering, SFT, and DPO are not themselves new.
