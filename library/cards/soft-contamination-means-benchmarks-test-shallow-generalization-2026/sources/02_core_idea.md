Soft contamination is semantic task overlap, not text overlap. The authors embed sampled Olmo3 pretraining data and all three instruction datasets, label high-similarity pairs, and fine-tune Olmo3 on exact or synthetic semantic duplicates. The central result is that duplicate exposure can improve held-out items from the same benchmark without usually transferring to related benchmarks. Thus a benchmark gain can be shallow within-benchmark generalization. The contribution is an audit method and code, not a released training dataset.
