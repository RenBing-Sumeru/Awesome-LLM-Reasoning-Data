The key causal test mixes 500 level-2/3 MuSR semantic duplicates into a 10,000-example SFT set and compares it with the same clean set. Table 5 reports Olmo3 contaminated fine-tuning at 66.4 on seen MuSR items and 54.4 on unseen items, versus clean fine-tuning at 50.0 and 48.8; TrueDetective remains 28.0. This supports benchmark-specific transfer from realistic duplicate exposure, but the unseen gain is modest and Qwen3 replication is noisy. Corpus analysis finds semantic duplicates for 78% of CodeForces tasks and exact duplicates for 50% of ZebraLogic tasks; those rates apply to this inspected Olmo3 corpus, not all models.
