The paper likely underestimates soft contamination: semantic retrieval has false negatives, Dolma contains relatively few synthetic rephrasings, and RL-environment data is not covered. It studies an open-data model, whereas frontier corpora are larger and unavailable; their duplicate rate cannot be inferred from Olmo3. The realistic-dose MuSR result strongly supports seen-item effects but gives mixed evidence for held-out transfer, and Qwen3 is noisy. Report retrieval recall, labeling error, corpus snapshot, and the clean-versus-contaminated split rather than treating a cosine score or one model as proof of ecosystem-wide contamination.
