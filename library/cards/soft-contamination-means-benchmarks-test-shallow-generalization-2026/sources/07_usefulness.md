Use this work to audit an open training corpus before trusting a reasoning benchmark. Given a benchmark, corpus snapshot, embedding model, and annotator budget, retrieve high-similarity pairs, label task equivalence, and report exact/semantic overlap with uncertainty. To test impact, construct validated duplicates of one benchmark split and compare seen, held-out, and external items with a matched clean fine-tune. Do not use cosine similarity alone as a filter or treat n-gram removal as proof of OOD evaluation.
