Read this as a joint policy–verifier design. The decisive checks are whether the process head improves selection over outcome scoring, whether its gain survives a matched-cost separate verifier, and how the gain changes with the number and length of sampled trajectories. Keep the authors’ reported “reasoning effort” separate from total compute: longer internal chains, more candidate chains, and scorer passes are different levers. An apparent scaling curve is meaningful only after all three are logged.
