Use this paper when a system needs both long-form reasoning and a process-level selector but cannot afford an entirely separate large reward model. Evaluate the policy-only, shared-head, and separate-verifier variants under the same candidate count and total inference cost. Log the effort mode and selected trajectory, not just the final benchmark score. The method is most relevant to tasks with outcome checks that can supervise learning; it is less directly applicable when correctness is subjective or no final reward is available.
