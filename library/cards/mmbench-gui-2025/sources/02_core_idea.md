The contribution is a unified multi-platform GUI-agent benchmark with a hierarchy that separates perception, grounding, execution, and collaboration. The mechanism is to score agents at progressively more interactive levels rather than collapsing all GUI ability into a single success rate.

Closest comparisons are ScreenSpot/GUI grounding datasets, mobile-agent benchmarks, OSWorld-like desktop tasks, and web-agent benchmarks. The feedback contract is mixed: static QA/grounding scores for lower levels and environment or evaluator success/efficiency scores for higher levels.
