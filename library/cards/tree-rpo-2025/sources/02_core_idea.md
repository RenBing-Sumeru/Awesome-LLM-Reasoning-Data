The central feedback contract is programmatic. Operational verifier, reward, or judge: A verifiable reward function evaluates complete leaf paths. TreeRPO does not use a separate process reward model; it estimates step values from descendant leaf rewards. Terminal predicate: A branch terminates on a stop token or when the maximum tree depth is reached. The trace supervision granularity is step_level; process_reward; trajectory_value. This is relevant to rollout/search/test-time data because It makes a concrete test-time trace-to-training contract auditable: leaf correctness signals are transformed into intermediate rewards and filtered sibling groups, while the lack of released raw trees limits independent trace analysis.
