LLaVA-Critic builds critic instruction data spanning diverse tasks and criteria and trains an open generalist multimodal evaluator that produces scores and rationales, performs pairwise comparisons, and supplies reward signals for preference learning. LLaVA-Critic-113K contains about 46K images and 113K critic-instruction examples spanning scoring, pairwise preference, evaluation rationales, and diverse visual tasks. A record typically includes an image, task or question, one or two candidate responses, evaluation criteria, a score or preference, and a rationale. The official Hugging Face release supports critic SFT and multimodal preference learning.
