LLaVA-Critic matches or exceeds GPT models on several LMM-as-a-Judge evaluations and supplies useful rewards in preference-learning experiments, improving aligned multimodal models. The results show that an open 7B-scale critic can serve as a general visual evaluator, although performance remains tied to the quality of synthetic judge labels. These results support the central claim only under the released data, model scales, candidate budgets, and evaluation protocols; cross-domain use requires new validation.
