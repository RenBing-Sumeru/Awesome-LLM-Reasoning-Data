Pretraining has three disclosed stages. Encoder alignment freezes the language model while adapting vision and audio encoders. A general 32,768-token stage trains all parameters on an approximately 4T-token mixture spanning text, audio, image, video, and video-audio records. A 262,144-token stage increases long audio/video exposure. Separately, AuT is trained from scratch on 40M hours of audio-text pairs generated by Qwen3-ASR, with a reported Chinese:English:multilingual ratio of 3.5:3.5:3.

Thinker post-training uses ChatML-formatted text, vision, audio, and mixed-modality conversations. Specialist teachers trained independently with SFT and RL generate domain data; on-policy distillation pairs an audio query with its text form and transfers the stronger text-conditioned response; interaction-aligned RL uses multi-turn trajectories and rewards language consistency, persona stability, and sustained instruction following.

Talker has a separate four-stage path: more than 20M hours of multilingual speech with multimodal context, quality-stratified 64K continual pretraining augmented by a captioner, DPO on human multilingual preference pairs plus GSPO with rule rewards, and lightweight speaker fine-tuning. These descriptions support mixed feedback and multiple supervision granularities, but exact record counts, reward formulas, and released artifacts remain unknown.
