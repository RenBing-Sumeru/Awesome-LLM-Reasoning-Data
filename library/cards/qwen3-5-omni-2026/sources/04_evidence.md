The official arXiv v2 report supports the three pretraining stages, aggregate token/hour counts, specialist-teacher construction, paired-query OPD, interaction objectives, human preference training, rule-reward GSPO, and Talker stages. It also states that the model is available through an API. No official downloadable training set, preference set, teacher-output collection, interaction-trajectory set, reward log, source inventory, or stage manifest is linked in the checked Card artifacts.

The report contains extensive evaluation across text, vision, audio, audio-visual, speech, long-context, reasoning, and agent tasks. Those results are evidence about model behavior under the stated evaluation protocols. They do not validate individual training records, reveal source rights, establish consent for voice material, or show that the post-training feedback stack is reproducible. API access verifies service availability, not training-pipeline availability.
