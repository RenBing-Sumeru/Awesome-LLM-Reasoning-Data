Qwen3.5-Omni aligns text, image, audio, video, speech generation, tool use, and long-context interaction while trying to avoid cross-modal degradation. This is not one homogeneous data problem: encoder alignment, broad multimodal pretraining, long-context extension, specialist distillation, audio-to-text behavior transfer, interaction trajectories, multilingual speech preferences, and speaker adaptation use different record types and feedback contracts.

The report exposes stage structure and several aggregate token/hour counts, but it does not publish the underlying corpora or stage manifests. More than 100 million hours of audio-visual content, 40 million hours used for the audio encoder, and more than 20 million hours used for Talker training are author-reported aggregates whose overlap and source membership are not fully specified. Benchmark coverage and API availability do not establish provenance, consent, licensing, decontamination, or item-level quality.
