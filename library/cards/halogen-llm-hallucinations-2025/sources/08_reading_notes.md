1. **Position:**  HALoGEN verifies atomic generated facts at scale with nine task-specific verifiers.
2. **Method:**  10,923 prompts, atomisation, authoritative sources, and automatic checks.
3. **Data:**  About 150K outputs from fourteen models with three source categories.
4. **Evidence:**  Up to 86% of atomic facts hallucinate in some domains, and rankings vary.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for high-precision audits; report verifier coverage and snapshots.
