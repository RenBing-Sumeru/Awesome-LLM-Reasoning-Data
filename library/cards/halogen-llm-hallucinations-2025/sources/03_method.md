1. **Design nine tasks:** Generation tasks such as programming, scientific attribution, and summarisation are selected where high-quality knowledge sources exist.

2. **Atomise outputs:** Each task decomposes responses into functions, citations, factual statements, summary claims, or other verifiable units.

3. **Build domain verifiers:** Execution, database lookup, and evidence matching verify atomic facts with an emphasis on high precision.

4. **Measure and classify:** Fourteen models produce about 150K outputs, whose hallucinations are measured and classified; knowledge-source versions must be fixed.
