Begin with the rule-based versus rule-less distinction. For a cited benchmark, write down the puzzle input, the permitted outputs, and the correctness criterion before comparing model scores. Then compare prompting, neuro-symbolic, and fine-tuned approaches only within puzzle families where the evaluation conditions are comparable. When a paper reports a solution trace, check whether the trace establishes each required constraint or merely narrates a plausible path. Note whether the benchmark checks exact answers, satisfaction of conditions, or another measure. The survey is most useful when it turns a broad puzzle label into a precise description of the inference and verification burden faced by the system.
