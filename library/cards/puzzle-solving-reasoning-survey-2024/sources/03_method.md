This is a literature synthesis. It maps puzzle families to methods, then reviews relevant datasets and benchmarks. A typical primary task presents a puzzle statement and clues or constraints, asks for a solution, and checks whether that solution satisfies the task conditions. The surveyed approaches include prompting, neuro-symbolic systems, and fine-tuning. Use this recurring task schema when consulting the cited studies: record the input puzzle, the permitted answer form, the constraints that must hold, and the scoring rule. The survey does not prescribe one solver. Its method is to relate solver choices to puzzle structure and to show where a dataset or benchmark supplies enough information for a meaningful comparison.
