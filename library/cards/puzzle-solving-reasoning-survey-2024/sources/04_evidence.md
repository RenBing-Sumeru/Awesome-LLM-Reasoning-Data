The survey critically reviews datasets and benchmarks and reports that complex puzzle settings remain challenging, especially when advanced logical inference is required. This is not a single head-to-head experiment, so it should not be read as a universal ranking of methods. The relevant evidence in each cited study depends on its puzzle family, scoring rule, and model setup. A high aggregate score is weak evidence if the benchmark permits shortcuts or accepts answers without checking every constraint. Conversely, an error can reveal a useful limit only when the required inference is clear. Readers should preserve the link between result, puzzle definition, and correctness test instead of carrying a performance claim into another puzzle family.
