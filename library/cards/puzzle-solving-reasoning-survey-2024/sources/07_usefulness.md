Use this survey to choose a puzzle evaluation that matches the capability you want to inspect. It is useful for deciding whether explicit constraints, symbolic components, richer training data, or a different benchmark are needed. It also helps turn a vague claim of “good reasoning” into a checkable question about which constraints a solution must obey. Start by selecting the puzzle family, then write down the expected output and the validity test. Compare prompting, neuro-symbolic systems, and fine-tuning only after that specification is fixed. For a new evaluation, use the survey to ask whether wrong answers can be diagnosed, whether all necessary clues are available, and whether the scoring rule rewards a correct solution rather than a persuasive narrative.
