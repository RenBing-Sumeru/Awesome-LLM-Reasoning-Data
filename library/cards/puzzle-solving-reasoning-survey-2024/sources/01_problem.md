Puzzles expose whether a model can respect constraints and draw non-obvious conclusions, rather than merely produce a fluent explanation. This EMNLP 2024 survey studies language-model puzzle solving through rule-based and rule-less puzzle categories, the methods used on them, and the datasets and benchmarks that measure them. It summarizes existing work and releases no new benchmark. The reader-facing question is not simply whether a model returns an appealing answer. It is whether the answer satisfies the puzzle's stated conditions, and whether the evaluation can distinguish genuine constraint handling from a familiar pattern. That distinction makes puzzles a useful but demanding surface for studying reasoning.
