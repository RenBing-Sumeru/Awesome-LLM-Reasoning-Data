The decisive experiment fine-tunes LLaMA-65B on the same 1,000-example LIMA set and compares its held-out answers with three much larger-data assistants under one human pairwise-preference protocol. LIMA is preferred or tied in 43% of comparisons with GPT-4, 58% with Bard, and 65% with DaVinci003. These results support the claim that a small curated set can teach a competitive response style to this capable backbone; they do not show that LIMA is more factually correct, or that the same sample count works for weaker models.
