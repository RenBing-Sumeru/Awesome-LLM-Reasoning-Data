The primary source is xAI's six-page November 17, 2025 Grok 4.1 Model Card, read with xAI's release announcement. This Card asks what those official materials disclose about post-training, safety, and deployment—not whether the closed model's claimed capabilities make its data reusable.

Grok 4.1 has Thinking and Non-Thinking configurations. The card says both are evaluated with a production system-message instruction and deployed with safeguards, including an input filter. The announcement separately says xAI used large-scale RL infrastructure and production-traffic evaluation. Those are material evidence for a frontier disclosure ledger, but not a released construction recipe.

The card belongs in the frontier-reports disclosure category. Its object is a report-level account of closed pipelines: broad source categories, training stages, feedback families, safety mitigations, and evaluation surfaces. It does not publish training records, reward/verifier schemas, filter models, system-message instruction text, agent trajectories, or a reproducible environment.

The decision boundary matters: safety benchmark scores and production A/B-style evaluation say something about behavior under stated configurations; they do not establish data provenance, production-data use in training, rights, label quality, or a training-data release.
