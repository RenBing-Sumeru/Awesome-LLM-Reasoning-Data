Primary source: Xi et al., "AgentGym: Evaluating and Training Large Language Model-based Agents across Diverse Environments," ACL 2025 Long Papers, pages 27914-27961. The official ACL Anthology page identifies AgentGym as a released framework for generally capable LLM agents, with code, dataset, benchmark, and checkpoints linked through the project page.

The problem is that LLM-agent work lacks a unified interactive framework that can both evaluate agents across diverse environments and support exploration or learning from multi-turn feedback. AgentGym is collected as an environment-backed evaluation and training surface, not as a static instruction corpus or a single leaderboard result. A reusable record is a ReAct-style episode: task instruction, environment id, observation, available actions, thought/action output, environment response, reward or success signal, and trajectory metadata.

The decision boundary is the feedback contract. AgentGym features 7 real-world scenarios, 14 environments, and 89 tasks; its samples are useful for the atlas because they expose executable environment feedback and split the work into AgentEval benchmark records, AgentTraj/AgentTraj-L trajectories, and AgentEvol training or self-improvement runs.
