For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, DeepDiver provides a concrete schema for an adaptive web-search episode. A reusable record should preserve the original question and source class; reference answer and checklist; difficulty-tagging attempts and grader outputs; model/checkpoint; every reasoning span, query list, engine, timestamp, retrieved URL/snippet/rank, and action; final answer and stopping reason; loose/strict grader prompt and raw decisions; component rewards; group membership; and GRPO sampling parameters. That structure supports SFT trajectory study, agent-training comparisons, reward/judge audits, search-budget analysis, and evaluation of stopping policies. Reuse is presently methodological rather than artifact-level: without an official dataset, rollout release, license, and retrieval snapshot, a reader can reproduce the design concept but cannot assume the authors' records are available or legally reusable.
