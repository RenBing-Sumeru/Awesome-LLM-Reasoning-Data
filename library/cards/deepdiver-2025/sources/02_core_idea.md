DeepDiver combines a live-web question set with an SFT-to-RL curriculum. The policy alternates reasoning with a search action that emits one to five queries, receives the top two results per query from Bocha or LangSearch, and either continues or emits an answer. During GRPO, Qwen-Turbo judges compare the final answer with a reference answer and checklist: an early loose judge assigns 1–10 and treats scores at least 6 as correct, while a later strict judge takes a two-of-three majority over binary judgments. Format and accuracy rewards are supplemented only when no search-free rollout succeeds but at least one search-enabled rollout does; successful search-enabled rollouts then receive +1.0. This is a judgment-based environmental feedback contract, not a programmatic proof, and the reported benchmark accuracy does not certify every query, document, reasoning step, or reward label.
