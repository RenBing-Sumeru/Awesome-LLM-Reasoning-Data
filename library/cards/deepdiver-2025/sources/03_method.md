WebPuzzle candidates come from Wiki-corpus pages and real-user queries with retrieved webpages from a deployed smart-assistant service. LLMs create open-web Cross-Page QA items by extracting facts from multiple pages and generating an inverted question, answer, and checklist; they create Open Riddle and Wiki Riddle items by selecting entity attributes and obfuscating or generalizing them while retaining the entity as label. An LLM filter removes offensive, politically sensitive, ethically concerning, NSFW, ambiguous, controversial, multiple-choice, boolean, and unsolvable items. DeepSeek-R1 answers each remaining problem four times to assign pass@4-based easy, medium, hard, or outlier tags, and five experts reduce 500 test seeds to 275 items under uniqueness, search necessity, multi-search reasoning, and solvability criteria.

Cold-start SFT distills DeepSeek-R1 responses over 2,000 selected WebPuzzle items, 300 deployed-assistant questions, 2,200 general-reasoning problems, and 1,000 real-user queries concatenated with retrieved documents. A separate 5,000 WebPuzzle items feed RL. Each RL prompt receives 14 rollouts at temperature 0.9; the maximum is seven tool-call rounds, the reported sequence limit is 20k tokens, and retrieved tokens are masked from the policy loss. Training uses one epoch of iterative-RAG GRPO with batch size 32, learning rate 1e-6, and KL coefficient 0.001. The paper discloses prompts and aggregate settings, but no official code, model, dataset package, rollout corpus, retrieval snapshot, or complete rejection log was verified.
