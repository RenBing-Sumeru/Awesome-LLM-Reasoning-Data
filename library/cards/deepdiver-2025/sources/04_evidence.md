The strongest evidence separates model outcomes from data quality. On WebPuzzle, DeepDiver-Qwen2.5-7B reports 37.6 average accuracy over three runs versus 27.9 for its cold-start SFT model and 29.8 for R1-Distill; its average search-call rounds rise to 2.51 from 1.85 and 1.75. DeepDiver-Pangu-7B reports 38.1 versus 30.3 and 30.7. In the reward-schedule analysis, switching from the loose to the strict judge after step 80 is associated with a WebPuzzle increase from 29.1 to 37.6, while continuing the loose judge gives little improvement. On ProxyQA, the Qwen variant reports 32.72%, 9.47 points above R1-Distill. These experiments support the claim that the studied RL recipe changes search behavior and task scores in the reported environment. They do not show that WebPuzzle labels are error-free, that the LLM judge is unbiased, that live results are reproducible, or that an unreleased trajectory corpus is high quality.
