The paper's direction signal is not GRPO, iterative RAG, distillation, or LLM-as-judge individually. Its specific change is to couple them to WebPuzzle and a live search engine so that search-call allocation is optimized inside the RL episode. Relative to prompted iterative RAG and SFT baselines, the policy can vary whether and how deeply it searches; relative to prior Wiki-based search training, the environment deliberately exposes scattered, noisy, and conflicting open-web evidence. The conditional extra-search reward is an early scaffold rather than a universal reward for more calls: it applies only when search changes a group from no successful search-free rollout to at least one successful search-enabled rollout. The result is reusable as a recipe and trajectory schema even though the authors' term Search Intensity Scaling and higher benchmark scores do not by themselves establish data quality or causality outside the reported setup.
