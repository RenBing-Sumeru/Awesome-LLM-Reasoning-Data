The environment is time-dependent: Bocha and LangSearch rankings, page contents, and availability can change, while no retrieval snapshot or exact per-episode result set was verified. Answer rewards rely on Qwen-Turbo prompts, reference answers, and checklists; loose/strict scheduling can alter both learning and evaluation, and majority judgment is not a correctness proof. Pass@4 difficulty labels inherit DeepSeek-R1 and grader errors. The paper does not disclose decontamination controls, complete source URLs, user-query consent, webpage redistribution rights, or a dataset license. Its own scope is limited to 7B models, 20k-token sequences, and search engines; it reports over-searching even on simple tasks. The stated post-review WebPuzzle release, code, trained models, rollouts, rejected candidates, seeds, and retrieval snapshots were not verified as reusable artifacts. Benchmark improvements must therefore remain separate from claims about provenance, label accuracy, release completeness, or reproducibility.
