The report presents Kimi-VL as a MoE VLM with an instruction model and a thinking variant. Its reasoning-data contribution is a disclosed high-level recipe: mixed text and multimodal corpora; human-seeded and model-expanded instruction data; Kimi k1.5-generated long-CoT traces; reward-model and rule-based filtering; and RL driven by binary correctness relative to ground truth plus a length penalty.

The feedback contract is mixed. Human annotators rank and refine candidate responses for part of SFT; rule-based and model-based verification support rejection sampling; and RL uses a scalar binary correctness signal. The report also describes agent screenshots, actions, dense grounding, and continuous trajectories, but no corresponding public environment or trajectory release.
