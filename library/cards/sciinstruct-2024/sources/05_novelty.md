# Novelty

Ordinary answer-filtered distillation discards questions when the first teacher trace fails. SciInstruct changes that curation decision by routing failures through critique, revision, and finally reference-answer hints before applying a separately trained instruction-quality filter. This exposes reflection stage and quality score as auditable selection points across science, mathematics, and formal proofs. Chain-of-thought prompting, final-answer checking, SFT, and Lean proof reuse are not individually new.
