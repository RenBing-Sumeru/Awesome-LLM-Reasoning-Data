# Method

1. **Assemble answer-bearing prompts.** Input: textbook, pedagogical, public-QA, problem-set, and LeanDojo sources. Process: normalize physics, chemistry, mathematics, and theorem-proof items and translate selected Chinese questions into English when useful. Output: 257,143 raw questions or existing proof instructions. Check / stop rule: every record needs a question, answer/solution target, and subject; the paper does not release per-record source identifiers.
2. **Generate and reflect on missing rationales.** Input: a question with its reference answer and GPT-4-0613. Process: sample a step-by-step solution, judge its outcome, then ask the same model to identify and repair errors; a third stage reveals the real answer if the second still fails. Output: answer-consistent worked traces. Check / stop rule: retain traces labeled correct; the illustrated 42,497-question subset yields 19,824 initially, 5,458 after first reflection, and 7,687 after answer-hinted reflection, totaling 32,969.
3. **Filter low-quality instructions.** Input: accepted/source solutions plus model-generated candidate solutions. Process: combine source positives with ChatGLM2, GPT-3.5, and GPT-4 negatives or judged samples, extract ChatGLM3-6B-Base features, and train a classifier with logits from -1 to 1. Output: ranked instructions and the final 254,051-record SciInstruct mixture. Check / stop rule: exclude the lowest-scoring 10% in the reported filtering ablation; correct final answers still do not certify all steps.
4. **Train and test consumers.** Input: chatbot-formatted SciInstruct and ChatGLM3 6B/32B, Llama3-8B-Instruct, or Mistral-7B MetaMath. Process: run two-epoch SFT with Transformers and DeepSpeed, using learning rate 3e-6 for ChatGLM. Output: scientific, mathematical, general-language, mixture, scale, and pass@k results. Check / stop rule: task accuracy or code pass@1 determines outcomes, with leave-one-domain-out and data-fraction runs tracing data effects.

**Reproducibility:** verify the NeurIPS paper and datasheet, three CC BY 4.0 files, line-level `content/summary/subject` schema, prompts, reflection counts, classifier composition, and training scripts. A rebuild must freeze source snapshots, OCR, translations, GPT model revisions, sampling and judgment prompts, classifier cutoff, and benchmark splits; temperature, most rollout counts, random seeds, full token cost, and per-record provenance are not disclosed.
