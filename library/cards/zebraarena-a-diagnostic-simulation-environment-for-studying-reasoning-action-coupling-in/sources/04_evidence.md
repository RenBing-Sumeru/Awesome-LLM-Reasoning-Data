On hard instances, GPT-5 and Gemini 2.5 Pro reach only about 60% accuracy. Even when correct, GPT-5 uses 70–270% more tool calls than the theoretical optimum, indicating that frontier models often compensate for weak reasoning or information selection through over-querying. Programmatically guaranteed unique solutions and optimal-query baselines support both accuracy and efficiency claims, although synthetic logic puzzles do not represent the semantic complexity of real tools.
