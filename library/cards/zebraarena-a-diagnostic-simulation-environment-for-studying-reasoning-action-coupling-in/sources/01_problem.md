When a tool agent fails, real environments often combine missing knowledge, interface noise, and dynamic state, making it hard to attribute errors to internal reasoning or external information acquisition. Public static data may also be memorized, obscuring the relationship between tool-call volume and reasoning quality.

ZebraArena creates a knowledge-minimal environment from procedurally generated Zebra logic puzzles. Critical facts are hidden behind tools, while unique solutions and theoretical minimum query counts enable controlled study of reasoning–action coupling.
