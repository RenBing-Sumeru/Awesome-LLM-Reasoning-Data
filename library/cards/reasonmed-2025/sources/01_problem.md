Medical reasoning SFT needs more than a correct multiple-choice letter: a useful record should explain clinical factors and reject distractors without adding unsupported medical claims. Single-teacher distillation can repeat one model's knowledge gaps, while exhaustive generation with the strongest proprietary model is expensive. ReasonMed asks whether complementary teachers, answer-key-conditioned judging, and difficulty-dependent repair can produce a large open reasoning corpus at lower cost.

The audit object spans several levels: 194,925 source questions, nine intended candidate paths per question, verifier verdicts and error reasons, ranked or regenerated accepted paths, summaries, and three final SFT views. These units must not be collapsed. A formatted row is neither an independently sourced medical question nor independent clinical validation, and an answer-level Correct verdict does not establish every intermediate claim.
