ReasonMed's distinctive construction choice is selective escalation. It does not spend GPT-o1 on every question: nine locally generated paths estimate difficulty, easy cases are ranked, medium cases receive verifier-guided edits, and only the difficult tail is regenerated. The paper then exposes long rationale, summary, and hybrid views to compare supervision granularity and output cost from a related content pool.

This is more explicit than single-pass medical distillation, but it is still answer-key-conditioned synthetic supervision. The verifier, ranker, refiner, and evaluator are not independent clinical authorities, and the released three-field rows discard the decisions that make the EMD recipe auditable. Novelty in routing and representation should not be read as proof of medical factuality or faithful reasoning.
