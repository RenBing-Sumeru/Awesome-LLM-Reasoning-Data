The GPT-4o Score Evaluator assigns 1,000 Medium-pipeline paths an average 7.37 before refinement and 8.17 after refinement. In cross-dataset samples it gives ReasonMed 8.45 for 1,000 rows and 8.50 for 3,000, versus 8.03 for medical-o1-reasoning-SFT and 8.18 for Medical-R1-Distill-Data. Because the evaluator is another LLM and the paper does not report blinded expert adjudication of these comparisons, these scores support the pipeline's own rubric, not clinical correctness.

Reported SFT results separate the three views. After three epochs, ResponseMed-7B, CoTMed-7B, and ReasonMed-7B reach aggregate accuracy 67.0, 69.1, and 69.6 across nine medical QA benchmarks; their average outputs are about 225, 555, and 626 tokens. ReasonMed-14B reaches 72.8 versus 69.0 for the Qwen2.5-14B baseline under the paper's evaluation. These model results show training utility, but cannot validate each rationale or distinguish scale, teacher, formatting, and contamination effects.

The 100-case board-certified-physician pilot reports structured reasoning in 87%, systematic distractor elimination in 82%, and core-medical-concept use in 78%. It simultaneously finds insufficient external citations in 68%, missing clinical specifics in 35%, and redundancy/verbosity in 21%. The paper does not disclose the number of physicians, review instructions, agreement, adjudication, specialties, or confidence intervals; its Responsible NLP Checklist marks human annotators as not used despite this pilot.

The public artifact provides direct counter-evidence to a blanket “high quality” interpretation. An early released row explains that none of four vitamin deficiencies causes Rett's syndrome, yet still suggests Biotin if forced to choose. Another says every OPV statement is true but nevertheless picks an exception. Appendix A.8 presents non-cherry-picked generations for one glomerular-capillary question whose final answer changes among options across temperatures. These are internally observable inconsistencies; no external clinical diagnosis is required to see that the records need item-level review.

Release inspection confirms a substantial artifact—three JSON files totaling about 3.07 GB and 1,111,555 rows—but also confirms missing lineage and unequal counts. Official links, counts, and executable modules establish release existence and recipe evidence; benchmark gains and LLM scores do not prove medical safety or source-license compatibility.
