ReasonMed combines three initial teachers—Qwen2.5-72B, HuatuoGPT-o1-70B, and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B. Each produces three six-part paths at temperatures 0.7, 0.9, and 1.0. Qwen2.5-72B sees the question, choices, known answer, and candidate rationale, then emits a `Correct`/`Error` verdict plus a short reason. The number of errors among nine paths defines Easy (0–4 errors), Medium (5–7), and Difficult (8–9) routing.

Easy items retain two Qwen-ranked correct paths. Medium items retain two paths and use GPT-4o-mini to edit the portions implicated by verifier feedback. Difficult items use GPT-o1 to generate a new six-part rationale. GPT-4o-mini then summarizes accepted rationales. This yields CoTMed (rationale), ReasonMed (the CoT inside `think` tags followed by its summary), and ResponseMed (summary) SFT formulations.

The public scale is not three exact copies of 370K items. Dataset Server reports 370,022 CoTMed rows, 369,983 ReasonMed rows, and 371,550 ResponseMed rows, totaling 1,111,555 in one train split. Rows contain only `instruction`, `input`, and `output`; `input` is blank in inspected examples. Without stable source or pairing IDs, one-to-one alignment among the three views cannot be confirmed.
