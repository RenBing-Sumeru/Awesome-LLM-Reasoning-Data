The source table contains MedQA train/dev (10,178/1,272), MedMCQA train (182,822), PubMedQA train/validation (450/50), and dev/validation portions of six medical MMLU subsets, for 194,925 questions. Named test splits are excluded. The generation prompt requires six parts: restate the question, identify clinical background, evaluate every choice, eliminate inconsistent choices, compare survivors, and give a final answer with a concise justification.

Nine initial paths are intended for each question. The released generator code fixes top-p at 0.9 and max output at 8,192 tokens for all three temperatures. The verifier is answer-key-conditioned and checks clinical factors, option coverage, logical connection to the supplied answer, and medical factual errors. Figure 4 records 1,291,181 Correct and 442,471 Error verdicts across 192,628 questions (1,733,652 paths), not all 194,925 listed source questions; the omitted 2,297 questions are not reconciled.

EMD routing allocates repair cost. Easy questions have five to nine Correct paths and keep the ranker's best two. Medium questions have two to four Correct paths; the Error Refiner gives GPT-4o-mini the original path, correct answer, and error reasons from other attempts, requesting targeted correction. Difficult questions have fewer than two Correct paths and are regenerated by GPT-o1. A separate Response Summarizer produces the concise view, while GPT-4o scores quality from 1 to 10 for analysis rather than supplying an executable medical truth predicate.

For downstream experiments, the paper fully fine-tunes Qwen2.5-7B for three epochs with learning rate 1e-5, cosine schedule, 10% warmup, 4,096-token context, bf16, DeepSpeed ZeRO-2, and 16 H20 GPUs; a 14B variant is also reported. Data generation takes about 122 hours, while 7B training uses 448 H20 GPU-hours. The selective EMD estimate is $4,552.47 versus $16,631 for direct GPT-o1 distillation under the paper's pricing assumptions.

The repository exposes generation, verification, ranking, refinement, summarization, and scoring scripts, but no end-to-end run manifest or source-preparation script. Current code also differs from the paper prompt in places: `quality_ranker.py` independently scores each path on 0–5 before sorting, and verifier defaults are stochastic (temperature 1.0, top-p 0.95). Training and evaluation remain marked “Stay tuned,” and no tagged release fixes the exact paper implementation.
