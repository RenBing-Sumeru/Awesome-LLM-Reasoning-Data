The main contribution is to turn intermediate states produced during proof search into reusable training data rather than treating search only as inference. LeanNavigator builds state-transition graphs over Mathlib4: every provable state can define a new theorem statement, and the shortest path to a completed proof becomes its proof. The authors release about 4.7 million theorem–proof pairs totaling roughly one billion tokens, over an order of magnitude larger than the approximately 112K theorems used by ReProver. Lean executes every tactic and its kernel confirms terminal proof states, making the corpus suitable for tactic generation, proof-search training, and formal-reasoning SFT.
