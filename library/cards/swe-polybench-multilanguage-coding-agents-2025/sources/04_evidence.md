The authors run several open coding agents on the full set and PB500. Resolve rates are uneven across languages, and simple single-function tasks are much easier than multi-file or multi-node changes. High file-retrieval recall does not necessarily translate into passing tests, showing that localization and patch generation are distinct bottlenecks. Executable replay over 2,110 tasks and 21 repositories demonstrates scale and automation, while CST metrics decompose failures. The paper does not establish strict difficulty matching across languages, and public leaderboard values will change. The well-supported conclusion is that current agents are inconsistent across ecosystems and complex tasks, not that one language is intrinsically harder based on aggregate ranks.
