The main contribution is a coupled system/data recipe. Forge decouples arbitrary black- or white-box agents from LLM, environment, reward, rollout, and training servers; asynchronous scheduling and tree-structured sample merging scale trajectory collection. CISPO then turns performance and speed feedback into a length-normalized trajectory advantage, while Forge also stores outcome and process rewards.

This extends the earlier MiniMax-M2 agentic-workspace direction with a concrete M2.5 release, 229B FP8 checkpoint, broader work environments, and speed-aware RL. The feedback system can observe tests, task completion, process quality, time, and deliverable judgments, but cannot by itself establish source rights, semantic correctness beyond the verifier, or benchmark cleanliness.
