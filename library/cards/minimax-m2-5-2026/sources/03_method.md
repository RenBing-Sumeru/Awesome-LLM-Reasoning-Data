The disclosed pipeline is:

1. **Environment construction.** MiniMax converts company tasks/workspaces into hundreds of thousands of RL environments. More than 200,000 are described as real-world software environments across at least 13 languages, full-stack repositories, Web/Android/iOS/Windows development, review, and tests. Professional finance, law, and social-science contributors define office requirements and standards. Source manifests and rights are unavailable.
2. **Trajectory interface.** Arbitrary agent scaffolds interact through Forge with LLM, environment, and reward servers. Records can hold prompt/response token IDs, reasoning, XML/JSON tool calls, tool outputs, code changes, browser traces, and office artifacts. Forge's gateway/data pool, rollout engine, and train engine exchange completions and outcome/process rewards.
3. **Scaling.** Asynchronous scheduling explicitly trades throughput against sample off-policyness. Tree-structured sample merging is reported to provide about 40× training speedup. Merge semantics, topology, stale-policy limits, correctness checks, hardware, and rollout counts are undisclosed.
4. **CISPO optimization.** M2.5 continues CISPO for MoE RL. The published equation length-normalizes token log-probability terms and upper-clips the importance ratio. Advantage is the future sum of performance and speed rewards minus a baseline. Forge also describes end-to-end process-quality monitoring. Reward definitions, scales, weights, units, baseline, clipping epsilon, KL, optimizer, group size, and update count are unknown.
5. **Evaluation contracts.** Software uses repository/task tests; VIBE-Pro combines interaction and visual checks; MEWC compares recalculated spreadsheet cells; office tasks use experts and pairwise LLM judging of deliverables plus trajectories. Search uses WebExplorer/Playwright. These evaluation judges are not established as identical to RL rewards.
6. **Released model.** The 229B MoE configuration has 62 layers, hidden size 3,072, 48 attention heads, 8 KV heads, 256 local experts with 8 selected per token, vocabulary 200,064, maximum positions 196,608, multi-token prediction, and block-FP8 metadata. Pretraining, SFT, active parameters, and M2.1-to-M2.5 lineage are undisclosed.

Recommended inference is temperature 1.0, top-p 0.95, and top-k 40. SWE-Bench Verified reportedly consumes 3.52M tokens and 22.8 minutes per task on average; Terminal Bench 2 allows 7,200 seconds, while BrowseComp discards all history after 30% of maximum context. These harness-specific policies must be fixed for reproduction.
