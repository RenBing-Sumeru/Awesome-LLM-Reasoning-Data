Chat-oriented LLMs can give direct answers instead of forcing people to search and combine sources, but a direct, fluent answer can still contain unsupported or incorrect claims. This is especially consequential when a user has no easy way to inspect the model's basis for an answer. This EMNLP 2024 survey studies factuality as a reliability problem in open-ended generation: it discusses the challenges and causes behind incorrect content, the kinds of approaches researchers use to improve factuality, and the difficulty of automatically judging generated text whose wording and claims are not fixed in advance. It synthesizes prior literature rather than introducing a new factuality dataset, model, or single evaluation protocol.
