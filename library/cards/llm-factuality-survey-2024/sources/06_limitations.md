The survey does not establish one universal factuality metric, one shared evidence source, or a guarantee that a proposed method transfers across domains and response formats. Its coverage is bounded by the literature available when it was written. More importantly, the paper explicitly highlights the difficulty of automated assessment for open-ended generation: a system must handle varied wording, potentially multiple claims, and task-dependent notions of supporting information. The survey is therefore best used as a map for posing careful questions, not as a substitute for checking a method's primary evaluation setup. Claims about a system's factuality should retain the limits of the evidence on which they depend.
