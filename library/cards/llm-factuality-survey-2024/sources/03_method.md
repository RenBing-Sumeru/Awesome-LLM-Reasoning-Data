This paper is a critical literature survey, not a new model recipe or a shared benchmark. Its method is to collect and synthesize work concerned with evaluating and improving LLM factuality, then organize that work around challenges, causes, solution directions, and unresolved evaluation barriers. The synthesis deliberately distinguishes work that changes how a model generates from work that changes how factuality is judged, because those are different contributions. It uses this literature map to discuss future research directions. Readers who need exact model inputs, evidence sources, metrics, or experimental results should follow the survey's cited primary papers rather than infer those details from the survey alone.
