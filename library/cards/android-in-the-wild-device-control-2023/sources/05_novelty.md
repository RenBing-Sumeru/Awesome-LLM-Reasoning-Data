The prior-work baseline is smaller UI-control data or web/simulated GUI environments. AITW changes the scale and substrate by collecting real Android-device episodes with natural-language goals and stepwise low-level actions. The direction signal is a public trajectory schema for mobile-control agents: instruction, screen observation, action, and next-state context. What is not new is behavioral cloning, action matching, screenshot-based UI perception, or human demonstration collection. Before reuse, inspect data license and terms, bucket availability, subset definitions, duplicate or leaked tasks, action-coordinate normalization, app/version drift, and whether benchmark scores are reported on the same data conversion.
