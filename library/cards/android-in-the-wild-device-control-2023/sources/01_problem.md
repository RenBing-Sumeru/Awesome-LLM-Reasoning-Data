Android in the Wild asks how to evaluate and train agents for real Android device control from natural-language instructions and human demonstrations. The primary sources are the NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks proceedings paper, the arXiv version, and the Google Research repository. The decision boundary is offline mobile-device control demonstrations and action prediction, not a live phone benchmark with guaranteed replayable app states. A data object is an episode with an instruction, screenshots or accessibility-derived UI observations, low-level Android actions, and a sequence of states. Its atlas value is that it made large-scale real-device mobile control a concrete trajectory surface instead of a toy simulator-only task.
