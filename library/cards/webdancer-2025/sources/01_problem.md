The primary sources are the NeurIPS 2025 proceedings record and 29-page paper, arXiv:2505.22648, and the Alibaba-NLP DeepResearch/WebAgent repository. The formal proceedings list fourteen authors and use “Robert Tang”; the PDF prints “Xiangru Tang,” while the arXiv and repository citation use a shorter author list. This Card follows the venue metadata and preserves the discrepancy as an audit item.

WebDancer addresses a data and training gap in deep information seeking. Many existing QA tasks can be solved in two or three searches, while a useful autonomous search agent must decompose a question, alternate reasoning with tool calls, absorb changing web observations, and decide when enough evidence supports a concise answer. The work therefore treats the web environment and the full behavior trace—not only the final answer—as post-training inputs.

The construction begins with 60,000 CRAWLQA and 40,000 E2HQA question-answer pairs. These tasks seed GPT-4o or QwQ-Plus ReAct trajectories consisting of Thought, structured search/visit Action, Observation, and final Answer. Selected trajectories provide cold-start SFT supervision; QA pairs not used by SFT can seed on-policy DAPO episodes with terminal format and answer rewards. This makes the paper directly relevant to `environment_agent_trajectory_data`.

The scope is narrower than a general browser agent: the reported environment exposes search and visit, the main experiments emphasize short-answer information seeking, and live pages are not frozen for replay. The repository releases a WebDancer-32B checkpoint, demo/inference code, 200 QA examples, and 200 cold-start trajectory examples, not the complete 100,000 QA pool, 14,228 reported SFT trajectories, or RL rollout logs. The Card therefore remains L3 and `card: null` pending human review of the release and replay boundary. (Paper §§1–3; Appendix D; official repository datasets and issue #35.)
