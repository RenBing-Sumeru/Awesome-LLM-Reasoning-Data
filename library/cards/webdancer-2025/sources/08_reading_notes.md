- The pipeline has four evidence-distinct stages: synthesize 60,000 CRAWLQA and 40,000 E2HQA questions; sample trajectories; retain 7,678 Short-CoT plus 6,550 Long-CoT trajectories for SFT; and run outcome-reward RL.
- The released trajectory sample preserves ChatML-like messages, tool calls, and observations. During SFT, observation tokens are masked, so the supervised target is the agent's reasoning/action behavior rather than copied web content.
- Rejection sampling uses \(N=5\) candidates. RL samples 16 rollouts per question and uses \(0.1R_{format}+0.9R_{answer}\), where both components are binary and the answer is judged by Qwen-72B-Instruct.
- Table 4 reports Short-CoT versus Long-CoT averages of 4.56 versus 2.31 actions and 510.03 versus 1,599.39 thought tokens. Table 3 shows that Long-CoT raises QwQ-32B Pass@3 from 44.66 to 58.25 but also raises invalid responses from 0.97% to 13.27%.
- Public release is sample-only: 200 QA records and 200 trajectory records. Full data, failed/rejected trajectories, RL traces, exact split/decontamination metadata, and stable web snapshots are unavailable; the formal proceedings' 14-author list also differs from the PDF and shorter arXiv/repository metadata.
