The main artifact is Plan-RewardBench, containing 1,171 trajectory preference comparisons across seven splits. Each record organizes a task input, visual or environmental state, candidate response or trajectory, evaluation evidence, and a preference, score, success, or critique label, together with task provenance and capability categories. Quality control relies on collecting successful and failed tool-use trajectories and constructing preferences for planning, recovery, safety refusal, and irrelevant-tool detection, making the release suitable for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than ordinary question answering.
