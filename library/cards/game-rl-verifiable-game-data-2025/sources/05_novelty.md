Closest prior work uses general preference pairs, terminal success, or step accuracy without representing the domain-specific states and failure modes of multimodal verifiable reasoning. This paper changes the record structure and feedback contract by centering GameQA-140K and combining inputs, candidates, environmental or evidential state, and labels in one auditable example through generating screenshots, questions, reasoning steps, and answers from game-engine states and recomputing labels with code. The contribution is task-specific data construction, capability decomposition, and practical evaluation rather than sample count alone.
