Prior instruction-following data usually asks humans or LLM judges to score complete responses, with formatting treated as one ambiguous dimension. Fixed format benchmarks can evaluate models but are difficult to scale into training data. VFF changes both feedback granularity and generation: every format requirement includes a parameterized template and deterministic verifier, allowing compositional difficulty and direct creation of SFT and preference labels. The novelty is not DPO itself, but converting one class of instruction-following objectives into automatically judged, programmatically instantiable supervision.
