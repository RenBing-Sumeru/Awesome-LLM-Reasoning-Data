1. **Aggregate open reviews:** Papers, decisions, review reports, scores, and discussion records are collected from four open-review sources and normalized.
2. **Link profiles:** Reviewer identities are connected to scholarly metadata to derive topics, seniority, and potential expertise-match signals.
3. **Compute features:** Review length, structure, citations, sentiment, specificity, and reviewer-dependent metrics are calculated.
4. **Model evaluation:** LLMs receive structured prompts to judge clarity, constructiveness, completeness, and related qualities, preserving explanations and scores.
5. **Expert annotation:** Human quality labels are collected for 753 reviews and compared with LLM, textual, reviewer, and decision signals.
