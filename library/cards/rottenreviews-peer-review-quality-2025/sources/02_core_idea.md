RottenReviews collects more than 15K submissions, reviews, and metadata from ICLR 2024, NeurIPS 2023, F1000Research, and the Semantic Web Journal, linked to over 9K reviewer scholarly profiles. A human-annotated subset contains 753 review comments sampled across venues. The resource includes review-dependent features such as length, specificity, and citations, reviewer-dependent signals, and structured LLM judgments, enabling comparisons among heuristic metrics, model judges, and experts.
