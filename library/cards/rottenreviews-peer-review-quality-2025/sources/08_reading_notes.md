1. **One-sentence positioning:** A peer-review quality benchmark combining human, LLM, textual, and reviewer-profile signals.
2. **Method takeaway:** Four open-review sources are normalized and enriched with computed features, LLM ratings, and an expert subset.
3. **Data takeaway:** It contains over 15K submission resources, over 9K reviewer profiles, and 753 human-annotated comments.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Review length, scores, or acceptance outcomes alone are inadequate proxies for quality.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is useful for review-judge research but requires control of venue bias and identity-linking errors.
