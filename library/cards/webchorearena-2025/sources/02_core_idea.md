The contribution is a controlled extension of WebArena that concentrates difficulty in four task types: 222 Long-Term Memory, 168 Massive Memory, 100 Calculation, and 42 Others tasks in the pinned 532-row release. Rather than merely lengthening navigation, the benchmark requires agents to aggregate page content, preserve constraints over many interactions, and sometimes combine information across sites. Each task also declares its required observation: 451 are marked `any`, 69 require text/accessibility-tree information, and 12 require screenshots.

Its feedback contract is mixed and terminal. Across the released configurations, 423 tasks invoke `string_match`, 42 invoke `url_match`, and 99 invoke `program_html`; a task can combine evaluators. String evaluation uses exact equality, required-substring inclusion, or GPT-4o fuzzy semantic matching. URL evaluation checks the final displayed URL. `program_html` locates designated elements in the final page and compares their contents or attributes with task references. `EvaluatorComb` multiplies component scores, so one failed component can collapse the final score.

The verifier can observe the submitted answer, final URL, and configured DOM evidence, but it does not label the correctness of each intermediate action or directly expose all hidden site state and side effects. Its supervision is therefore answer-level and full-episode terminal scoring, not step-level process supervision. Relative to WebArena, the concrete change is a new set of harder memory- and calculation-centered tasks, explicit observation requirements, clearer answer-format instructions, and repeated author cross-checks. The environment family and evaluator mechanisms are adapted rather than newly invented.
