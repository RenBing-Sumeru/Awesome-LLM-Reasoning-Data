Orca 1 and other explanation-tuning methods expose the teacher's reasoning instruction together with its answer. Orca 2 changes the training record by selecting a task-appropriate strategy during generation and hiding that strategy prompt from the student. The model must infer when to use the observed behavior from the task itself, making strategy choice part of the learned signal. Teacher-generated explanations and progressive SFT are established; Prompt Erasing plus task-specific strategy mapping is the distinct change.
