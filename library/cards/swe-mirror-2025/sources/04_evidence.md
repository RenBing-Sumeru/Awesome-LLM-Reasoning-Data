Construction evidence is reported at three levels. On a balanced manually selected set of 100 issues, the LM high-quality/mirrorability filter has 84.3% precision and 86.0% recall (Table 2). On 100 manually selected candidates for each of four languages, end-to-end mirroring succeeds on 46.0% overall: Python 68.0%, JavaScript 52.0%, Go 36.0%, and Rust 28.0%. Compile or syntax errors account for 2.0%, 6.0%, 28.0%, and 36.0% respectively, showing that the pipeline's yield is not language-neutral (Table 3).

Three annotators audit the 184 successfully generated tasks from that study against their source issue and pull request. Of 177 tasks with a majority label, 115 are rated high consistency and 41 moderate consistency, totaling 156/177 or 88.1%; 21 are inconsistent and 7 have no majority. This is useful direct evidence about semantic transfer, but it is a selected 184-task audit rather than an estimate over all 60,671 retained instances (Table 4).

The final task counts are 46,820 Python instances from 31 repositories, 7,183 Rust from 6, 4,056 Go from 2, and 2,612 JavaScript from 1, totaling 60,671 tasks across 40 repositories (Table 5). Table 1 reports about 100 GB of environment storage. These numbers support environment amortization; they do not establish that every generated problem is realistic, independent, legally reusable, or uncontaminated.

For the reported 12,456-episode SFT mixture, SWE-Mirror-LM-7B reaches 22.8% on SWE-bench Verified versus 1.0% for the Qwen2.5-Coder-Instruct-7B base, while the 32B model reaches 52.2% versus 6.2%. On Multi-SWE-Bench-Flash the two models score 6.33% and 21.33% (Table 6). Main evaluation uses temperature 0, 131,072-token context with YaRN, and at most 100 interaction rounds. These are author-reported resolve rates under the OpenHands/MOpenHands scaffold, not independent replication.

Ablations at 4,096 SWE-Mirror trajectories show the 32B Error Masking model at 35.6%, compared with 33.6% for Response Only and 29.2% for Error Pruning; the untrained baseline is 6.2% (Figure 2). With 512 non-Python trajectories, the 7B model scores 10.2% after Go training, 11.3% after Rust, and 9.4% after JavaScript on Python-only SWE-bench Verified, versus 1.0% for the base (Table 7). These comparisons support the paper's training-utility claim within the specified budgets, but they do not isolate mirroring quality from teacher, scaffold, context, or mixed-source trajectory effects.
