The official scripts can generate multiple solutions for proprietary math problems, select high-value prefixes through uncertainty, and output `prompt-answer-completions-label` training records. After training, step classification can be tested on ProcessBench and majority, PRM, and hybrid aggregation compared on a fixed candidate pool. When terminal answers cannot be verified reliably, continuation success must not be treated as step-level ground truth.

The final artifact can be either process-training data or a candidate-answer ranking report.
