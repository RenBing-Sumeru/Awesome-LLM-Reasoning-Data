The authors evaluate step discrimination on ProcessBench and compare majority vote, PRMs, and two hybrid aggregation methods on MATH and GSMPlus. Uncertainty-driven data outperforms binary or sequential labeling baselines with less search, while HMRV and WRFV further improve answer selection across multiple PRMs. The evidence supports efficiency in verifiable mathematics but does not cover open-ended reasoning.

The authors also report efficiency curves under different search budgets, showing that gains do not come only from more continuations.
