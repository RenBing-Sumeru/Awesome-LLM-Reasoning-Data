The contribution is a human-verified benchmark of 2,323 realistic charts from arXiv papers with two answer-level tasks: descriptive chart reading and reasoning over chart content. The mechanism changes the baseline from clean synthetic chart QA to paper-chart QA with curated questions and verified answers. The feedback contract is answer-level accuracy under the released evaluation pipeline, including GPT-4o-based grading for open responses. Closest comparisons are prior chart QA suites and stress tests that use simpler chart distributions. The direction label is realistic multimodal evaluation surface with contamination and judge-version risk.
