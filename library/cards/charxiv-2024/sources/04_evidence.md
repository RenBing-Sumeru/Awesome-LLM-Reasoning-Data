The paper reports 2,323 natural and diverse charts and a large gap between current MLLMs and humans: GPT-4o is reported at 47.1% accuracy, the strongest open-source model cited at 29.2%, and human performance at 80.5%. The row-level evidence is the verified reference answer for each chart question and the evaluator verdict on a submitted answer. The official GitHub, project page, Hugging Face dataset, and OpenReview/NeurIPS record support the release status. Evidence is bounded by the public benchmark version, prompt format, judge model, and the fact that aggregate accuracy does not certify that an individual chart interpretation is semantically faithful beyond the answer key.
