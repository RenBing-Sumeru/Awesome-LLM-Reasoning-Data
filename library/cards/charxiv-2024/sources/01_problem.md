CharXiv asks whether multimodal LLMs really understand realistic scientific charts, using the June 2024 arXiv paper and NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks release as the primary source. The gap is that many chart benchmarks use synthetic or simplified figures, so scores can overstate performance on charts found in papers. The data object is a chart image from an arXiv paper plus a human-curated question, answer, and metadata for descriptive or reasoning evaluation. The card belongs in the atlas as an evaluation surface, not as a training recipe or chart-generation dataset. Its value is the explicit feedback contract: compare the model answer against verified reference answers with the official evaluator and keep the public split, prompt, and judge version pinned.
