The language-model pipeline begins by training each SFT model for one epoch. The SFT policy samples 278,496 response pairs at temperature 1.0. In the main gold-model setup, Skywork-Reward-Llama-3.1-8B-v0.2 scores both responses and the higher-scored response is always selected as the winner; labels are not sampled from the Bradley-Terry probability. These fixed triples train the initial Bradley-Terry reward model.

The prompt sources are the TL;DR dataset packaged by the Huang et al. summarization-from-feedback reproduction and a modified Alpaca-Instructions dataset from Alpaca-Farm. For Alpaca, prompts longer than 512 tokens and outputs longer than 106 tokens are excluded, and the original SFT, reward-modeling, and PPO training splits are combined before SFT training and pair generation. Evaluation uses the filtered validation set, not AlpacaEval.

After a policy-training block, the implementation evaluates each fixed winning and losing response under both the current policy and the original SFT policy. Their joint sequence-probability ratio defines the correction. Reward-model retraining applies the flattened relative form with `eta=0.001` and `alpha=0.9` in all language-model experiments. Thus the raw records and labels remain constant while their contribution to the loss changes by stage.

The refreshed reward model is used for the next PPO block. The KL reference is moved from the original SFT policy to the preceding stage policy, because the refreshed reward model is intended to be accurate near that distribution. When the reward model changes, the PPO value-network parameters and AdamW optimizer state are reset. PPO rollouts use temperature 0.7; evaluation generation uses temperature 0.01.

Main OCRM runs refresh after every 100,000 generated policy-training samples and report two or three stages. A five-stage Pythia-1B TL;DR extension is reported for one seed. Pythia-1B-deduped and Qwen2.5-1.5B are the principal base models; a Pythia 6.9B experiment substitutes GRPO. These stage, KL, reset, and rollout choices are part of the released recipe, not fields newly added to the preference dataset.
