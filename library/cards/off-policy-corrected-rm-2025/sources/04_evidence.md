The strongest empirical evidence is a controlled comparison under the paper's synthetic gold-model protocol. On TL;DR with Pythia-1B, the gold-model win rate is `62.9 +/- 2.7%` for PPO, `71.4 +/- 2.9%` for OCRM with two stages, and `73.6 +/- 1.7%` with three stages. This setting uses three random seeds, each with a different SFT model and resulting fixed comparison dataset. Other main table settings use one seed.

The same table reports improvements across model and task variants. For Qwen2.5-1.5B on TL;DR, PPO scores 80.5%, versus 87.7% and 89.6% for two- and three-stage OCRM. On the short Alpaca-Farm setup with Qwen2.5-1.5B, the corresponding values are 66.1%, 68.5%, and 69.5%. Larger Pythia results are also reported, but selected baselines are omitted for computational reasons.

Ablations show that the complete scaffold matters. On a Pythia-1B TL;DR run, PPO reaches a 59.6% win rate, PPO plus value/optimizer reset 60.0%, PPO with the new stage-local KL reference 67.5%, two-stage OCRM 68.6%, and three-stage OCRM 71.7%. The paper also varies fixed preference-set size, refresh interval, `eta`, and `alpha`; all tested weighting settings outperform the displayed PPO baseline, while larger refresh blocks perform better in the tested range.

In the additional experiment where GPT 4.1 Nano supplies both labels and evaluation, the reported win rates are 57.0% for PPO, 62.9% for two-stage OCRM, and 70.4% for three-stage OCRM. The authors mitigate observed position bias during evaluation by judging each pair in both orders and treating inconsistent choices as ties.

These numbers support a training-recipe claim, not a data-quality claim. Most labels and main evaluations come from the same Skywork reward model, and the GPT 4.1 Nano experiment likewise reuses one judge for labeling and evaluation. The official MIT repository exposes dataset preparation, labeling, density-ratio, reward-training, PPO, DPO, and WPO source modules and launchers, but does not provide an immutable release of the 278,496 labeled pairs or stage-specific weight snapshots. At inspected commit `89d4d19a41bcaa187c43dac2e23eb3fafba59e45`, the launchers refer to a `summarize_from_feedback_details` directory absent from the tree, while the modules are under `ocrm`; therefore code availability is evidence of an implementation path, not an independently verified turnkey reproduction.
