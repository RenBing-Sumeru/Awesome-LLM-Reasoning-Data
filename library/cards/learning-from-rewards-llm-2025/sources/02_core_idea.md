The survey's central taxonomy joins two axes: reward models and learning strategies. It places RLHF, RLAIF, DPO, GRPO, reward-guided decoding, and post-hoc correction in a broader learning-from-rewards view, while also discussing reward-model benchmarks and applications. Its contribution to the atlas is the feedback contract: record who or what supplies a signal, whether it is a pair, score, critique, or model output, and whether it is used to train, select, or correct. Compare it with primary feedback releases, not as a replacement for their annotation protocols.
