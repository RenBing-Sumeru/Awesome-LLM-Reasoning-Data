Post-training discussions often use “RLHF” as shorthand for incompatible objects: pairwise preferences, scalar rewards, learned reward models, direct objectives, and inference-time selectors. The official Findings of EMNLP 2025 record at https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.970/ frames learning from rewards as the transition from static-data learning to dynamic feedback. This Card maps those objects before readers enter preference/reward tracks; it does not release a new reward dataset. Its evaluation surface is the reward signal's role at training, inference, or post-inference time.
