Fixed-template multimodal CoT systems such as LLaVA-CoT prescribe a small set of reasoning stages, while direct imitation collections attach curated answers without exposing how textual reasoning reconnects to pixels. R1-Onevision changes the training object to a trace built through image formalization, strong language reasoning, image-aware role-playing, and post-generation quality filtering. This makes the cross-modal handoff and its judge auditable before SFT. Dense captioning, teacher distillation, SFT, and GRPO are not individually new; the contribution is their use to construct and consume a large revisitable visual-reasoning record.
