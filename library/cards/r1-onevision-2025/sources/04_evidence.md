1. **Training-strategy ablation.** Under the same Qwen2.5-VL-7B lineage, the base scores 25.4 on MathVision, 43.6 on MathVerse, and 38.2 on MathVerse Vision Only; SFT on R1-Onevision data changes these to 26.3, 43.4, and 39.7, while SFT+RL reaches 29.9, 46.4, and 40.0 (Table 4). This supports a small direct SFT benefit and a larger combined post-training gain, but it cannot attribute the full SFT+RL difference to the 155K demonstrations alone.
2. **Model-size transfer.** On the 3B base, R1-Onevision training raises MathVision from 21.7 to 23.7, MathVerse from 34.7 to 38.6, and Vision Only from 31.2 to 35.5 (Table 6). The direction is consistent across two model sizes, but both share the Qwen2.5-VL family and the same evaluation surface.
