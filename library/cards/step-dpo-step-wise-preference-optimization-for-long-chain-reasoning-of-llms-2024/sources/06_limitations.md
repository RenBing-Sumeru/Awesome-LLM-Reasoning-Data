There are important failure modes. A trace may contain an earlier subtle error that the locator misses; a correct final answer can be reached by invalid reasoning; a sampled correction can be lucky rather than generally valid; and GPT-4/human localization has its own inconsistency. Step labels are sensitive to prompt formatting such as “Step i:” and to how algebraic equivalence is judged. Benchmark overlap, ground-truth leakage, and using a stronger teacher for localization must be declared.
