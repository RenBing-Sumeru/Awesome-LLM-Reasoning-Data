The reported experiments cover Qwen and other base/instruction models, MATH, GSM8K, and harder math tasks. With fewer than 500 Step-DPO training steps, the paper reports nearly a three-point MATH gain for models above 70B; Qwen2-72B-Instruct with Step-DPO reaches 70.8% on MATH and 94.0% on GSM8K in the stated setup. These are not simply “DPO scores”: they combine a particular SFT source, stepwise prompting format, data construction procedure, and final-answer evaluator.
