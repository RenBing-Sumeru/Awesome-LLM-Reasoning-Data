The paper’s construction pipeline has three stages. Error collection samples solutions from an initial model and keeps examples whose final answer disagrees with ground truth. Step localization verifies the chain until the first wrong step, with humans or GPT-4 available for this work. Rectification samples continuations conditioned on the valid prefix and retains a self-generated step from a trajectory that reaches the correct final answer. The resulting Step-DPO set has about 10K pairs. The paper emphasizes that self-generated in-distribution rectifications are more effective than human or GPT-4 corrections in its setting.
