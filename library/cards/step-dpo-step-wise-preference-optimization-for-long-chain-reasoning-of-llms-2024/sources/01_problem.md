Step-DPO is built around the observation that a long incorrect solution often begins correctly. Standard DPO labels the entire rejected answer as undesirable, even though its prefix may contain valid transformations, definitions, or computations. That creates a noisy gradient: increasing the chosen full answer and decreasing the rejected full answer can penalize tokens the model should retain. The paper narrows the preference decision to the first point where the reasoning diverges from a valid path.
