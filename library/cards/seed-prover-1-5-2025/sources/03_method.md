The disclosed construction and use pipeline is:

1. **Sources and cold start.** Unreleased in-house synthetic SFT data teaches Seed-Prover 1.0 tool syntax and interaction. RL mixes Big-Math, NuminaMath, CriticLean, Kimina-Prover Preview, Lean Workbook, and undisclosed in-house textbook formalizations including Graduate Texts in Mathematics.
2. **Curriculum filter.** Evaluate the SFT policy at Pass@4×8. Remove tasks solved more than three times and tasks never solved under any prompt. If a failed-trajectory summary enables a solve while a direct prompt does not, retain the task under the direct prompt. Counts and parent mappings are unavailable.
3. **Agent experience.** A formal prompt, optional natural-language proof or previous-failure summary enters a multi-turn policy. The policy emits reasoning and calls Lean/LooKeng, Mathlib semantic search, or Python; tool responses update context. Verified lemmas are cached with dependencies. On budget exhaustion, the agent writes a summary and can restart; a final Lean-verified theorem receives `+1`, otherwise `-1`.
4. **Environment and RL.** Each direct trajectory has 64K tokens and at most 28 tool calls. Search is fixed to Mathlib v4.22.0; evaluation uses Lean v4.22.0. VAPO with ReTool-like tool-integrated PPO uses only terminal outcome reward. SFT/RL records, tool environments, reward code, optimizer details, and failed experiences are closed.
5. **Sketch Rubric RL.** A Doubao-Seed-1.6-initialized natural-language prover writes a proof; a sketch model converts it to at least three Lean lemma statements admitted with `sorry` and transparent main-proof assembly. Lean supplies structural score `S_FL`; an undisclosed natural-language verifier rejects invalid atomic lemmas; an undisclosed Long-CoT LLM rubric judges alignment, decomposition, difficulty reduction, lemma value/utilization, and junk values to form `S_NL`. Reward is `+1` iff lemma count is at least 3, `S_FL≥0`, and `S_NL≥0.7`; otherwise `-1`.
6. **Hierarchical TTS.** The Lean agent proves or disproves each sketch leaf at Pass@3×3. Failed leaves are recursively decomposed; disproved leaves trigger sketch revision. A tree succeeds only when every leaf verifies. Putnam starts at depth 4 and can restart with cached lemmas for effective depth 8.
7. **Compute scale.** Agent-only light inference uses Pass@8×8. Full Putnam/FATE/Combi evaluation allows up to 10 H20-days per problem; IMO 2025 uses roughly 20; Putnam 2025 allows up to 40 H20-days per problem. Total tokens, calls, branch counts, parallel width, and utilization remain unknown.
