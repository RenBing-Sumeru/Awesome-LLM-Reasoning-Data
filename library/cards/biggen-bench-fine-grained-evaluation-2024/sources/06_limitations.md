Distributing 765 instances over 77 tasks leaves small task-level sample sizes, making rankings sensitive to a few atypical examples. Instance-specific rubrics improve precision but require expensive manual design and may encode author preferences; reference answers may also constrain valid diversity in open tasks. The five judges are strong general models that may share training data and length preferences. Public responses may later enter training corpora. Reuse should aggregate at capability level with confidence intervals, refresh instances, and test reference-free and multi-judge variants.
