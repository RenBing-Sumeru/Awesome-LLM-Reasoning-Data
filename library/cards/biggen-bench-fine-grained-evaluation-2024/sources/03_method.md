1. **Capability taxonomy:** Nine generation capabilities are defined and divided into 77 diverse tasks, reducing coverage bias toward instruction following.
2. **Instance authoring:** Open prompts and reference answers are created for each task across domains, styles, and reasoning requirements.
3. **Instance-specific rubrics:** Fine-grained criteria specify required content, prohibited behavior, and quality levels for each item instead of using one generic standard.
4. **Response collection:** Answers are generated by 103 frontier models, forming a large model-task response matrix.
5. **Multi-judge validation:** Five evaluator LLMs score the responses, and their results are compared with human judgments to analyze evaluator, task, and ranking stability.
