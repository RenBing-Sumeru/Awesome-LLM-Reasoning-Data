Benchmarks such as MT-Bench and AlpacaEval center on dialogue or instruction following and usually use fixed abstract criteria. BiGGen Bench begins with capability coverage and pushes criteria down to the instance level so the judge knows exactly what to check for each item. Compared with work such as Prometheus that trains a judge, it primarily releases a public evaluation surface for both generators and evaluators. Compared with conventional benchmarks, it also exposes responses from many models plus human and judge scores, enabling research on evaluator bias and criterion design.
