1. **One-sentence positioning:** It is a fine-grained open-generation benchmark with 765 instances, nine capabilities, and 77 tasks.
2. **Data takeaway:** Each item includes a prompt, reference, instance-specific rubric, model responses, and scores.
3. **Evaluation takeaway:** Five LLM judges evaluate 103 frontier models and are calibrated against human scores.
4. **Core value:** Concrete criteria replace abstract helpfulness while improving capability coverage and interpretability.
5. **Main risk:** Tasks have few examples, rubric design is costly, and both judges and references remain biased.
