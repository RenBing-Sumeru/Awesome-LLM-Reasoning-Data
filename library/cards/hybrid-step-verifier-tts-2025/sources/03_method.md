For a candidate solution, the method evaluates intermediate reasoning steps with process verification and identifies locations that warrant targeted work. Conditional Step-level Self-refinement then revises at that finer grain, instead of using only answer-level reranking or unconditional full-trace rewriting. The verifier output acts as an operational control signal: it determines where another generation pass is potentially worth the budget.

The study further integrates this sequential operation with parallel test-time methods, producing a hybrid procedure that can explore multiple candidates while refining selected steps within them. The paper evaluates the approach on five instruction-tuned language models ranging from 3B to 14B parameters. Its implementation record also links a public code repository, which makes the generation, verification, and hybrid scheduling decisions more inspectable than a paper-only description.
