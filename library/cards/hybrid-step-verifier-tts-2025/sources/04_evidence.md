The official EMNLP paper reports extensive experiments across five instruction-tuned model families and sizes from 3B to 14B parameters. The stated experimental result is that the fine-grained hybrid use of several training-free test-time scaling methods has substantial potential to extend reasoning performance. This is direct evidence for the paper’s central comparison: a verifier-directed allocation is tested against alternatives that spend inference computation in less targeted ways.

The evidence should be interpreted as a study of the interaction among generator, process verifier, and inference schedule. It is stronger than a single-model anecdote because several model scales are included, but it does not make verifier quality disappear as a confounder. A careful reader should inspect the per-benchmark results, the cost accounting for verification and refinement, and ablations separating the sequential component from the parallel component before extrapolating to another serving stack.
