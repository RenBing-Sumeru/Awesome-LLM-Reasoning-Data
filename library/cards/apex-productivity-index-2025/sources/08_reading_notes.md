1. **Position:**  Measure economically valuable professional deliverables through practitioner-authored tasks.
2. **Method:**  Expert recruitment, real tasks, reference artefacts, rubrics, and an LM judge.
3. **Data:**  The paper has 200 cases; the public extended subset has 100.
4. **Evidence:**  GPT-5 high reasoning leads at 64.2% but remains below experts.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for professional judges; do not interpret scores directly as job replacement.
