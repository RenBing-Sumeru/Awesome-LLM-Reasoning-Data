APEX-v1.0 evaluates 200 cases across investment banking, management consulting, big law, and primary care, with expert-authored prompts, reference deliverables, and rubrics. The public APEX-v1-extended release provides 25 downloadable tasks per occupation, 100 in total, for professional-response evaluation and judge calibration. The full paper benchmark and the public subset must be distinguished rather than treating 100 as the total scale.
