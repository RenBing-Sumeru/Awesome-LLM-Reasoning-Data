Common LLM benchmarks emphasise exams, short QA, or coding and poorly represent economically valuable work in investment banking, consulting, law, and primary care, where long-form deliverables, file handling, and professional judgment matter. Without economically grounded tasks, model progress cannot be connected clearly to real productivity.

APEX has experienced practitioners author tasks and itemised criteria from their daily work, then evaluates deliverable quality with an LM judge and expert references while comparing cost, speed, and human performance.
