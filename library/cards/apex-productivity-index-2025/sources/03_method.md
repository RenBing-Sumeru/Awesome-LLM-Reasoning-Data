1. **Recruit experts:** Bankers, consultants, lawyers, and physicians with top-tier experience define representative high-value work.

2. **Author tasks:** Experts create prompts, input files, deliverable requirements, and references from real workflows rather than knowledge recall.

3. **Design rubrics:** Client readiness, factuality, analysis, format, and professional standards are decomposed into assessable criteria.

4. **Evaluate models:** Models produce deliverables scored by an LM judge and compared with experts; public subset, judge, files, and reasoning settings must be fixed.
