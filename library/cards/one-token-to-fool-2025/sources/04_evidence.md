The main attack experiment evaluates ten content-free keys on five benchmarks across specialized and general judges. It reports up to 35% false-positive rate for a colon on GPT-4o, 60–90% for “Thought process:” on LLaMA3-70B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct, and 66.8% for a single blank on General Verifier over MATH. In the same test matrix, the augmented Master-RMs have near-zero FPR. Standard judgment did not obviously collapse: Master-RM-7B reports kappa 0.91 against GPT-4o and 0.90 against a 500-item human set. This demonstrates resistance to the tested keys and protocols, not to arbitrary adversarial outputs or real end-to-end RL failure rates.
