Prior work evaluates whether a judge agrees with labels, but that can miss a reward signal that accepts empty answers. This paper changes the test object to semantically null responses and makes their false-positive rate an explicit verifier-security metric. Its defense changes training data rather than the reward interface: truncated solution lead-ins are labeled NO. The important contribution is the minimal red-team and targeted hardening, not a general proof that the trained judge understands or verifies every answer.
