UI-Genie-RM casts both action evaluation and task completion as next-token prediction of special positive/negative tokens. Its input combines the task goal, current screenshot, candidate action, the five most recent screenshots, and a textual summary of earlier actions; task completion is represented as a special action so one model can score step and outcome examples.

Initial reward data comes from AndroidControl, AMEX, and AndroidLab. Positives are checked against action type, click/long-press coordinates inside the reference bounding box, or typing semantics. Negatives come from controlled trajectory corruption and from examples that an initial RM incorrectly accepts. The learned RM then scores 10 candidate actions per exploration step, ranks paths by cumulative reward, keeps five beams, verifies completed trajectories, and helps assign new step labels through five continuation trials.

The scale labels need qualification. Paper Table 1 reports nominal RM components of 263K AndroidControl, 170K AMEX, 24K AndroidLab, and 59K exploration examples, while the current Hub viewer exposes 472,769 RM rows. For UI-Genie-Agent-16k, the same table reports size 16K, average 7.1 steps, and 2,208 trajectories. The Hub repository contains 16,693 screenshot files organized under 2,208 trajectory directories and a 74.35 MB `ui_genie_agent_16k.jsonl`; “16k” is therefore a step/example-scale name, not evidence of 16,000 distinct trajectories.
