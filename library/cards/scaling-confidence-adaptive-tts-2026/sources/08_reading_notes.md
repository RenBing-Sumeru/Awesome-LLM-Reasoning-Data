Keep four distinctions in mind. C3RL is the training-time calibration mechanism, whereas CAS is the inference-time allocation rule. The paper evaluates accuracy, ranking-style confidence discrimination, and calibration error separately, which prevents those notions from being collapsed. Its strongest budget result is relative to a 64-sample majority-vote target, not an absolute latency guarantee. Finally, the release status matters: the paper promises artifacts, but the current primary source does not yet expose them.
