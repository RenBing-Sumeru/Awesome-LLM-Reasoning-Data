The paper reports calibration and accuracy across eight text and multimodal datasets. On the Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct multimodal average, C3RL obtains 0.533 accuracy and 0.109 expected calibration error, compared with RLVR's 0.534 accuracy and 0.405 calibration error. This is useful evidence for the narrower claim that the added objective improves confidence alignment without a large accuracy loss in that evaluated setting.

For allocation, the comparison fixes the accuracy target to 64-sample majority voting. CAS reaches matching or slightly higher accuracy while averaging 10.91 samples in-domain, 5.19 on text OOD, and 7.13 on multimodal OOD; these correspond to reported savings of 5.87, 12.33, and 8.98 times. The evidence supports an efficiency frontier under these thresholds, rather than a claim that confidence is calibrated for every model or deployment distribution.
