The training data combines filtered Numina-NLI, WebInstruct-verified, LogicNLI, and LogiQA questions. For each question, the model produces an answer and confidence. C3RL adds three reward terms: a binary correctness term, a calibration term that favors certain-correct and uncertain-incorrect responses, and a reference term derived from prior sampling behavior of the question. The last term is intended to prevent a calibration objective from sacrificing answer quality.

At inference, CAS samples sequentially up to a maximum N. It keeps answer and confidence sets, obtains the largest and second-largest confidence values, evaluates the paper's stopping probability, and halts once that probability crosses a chosen threshold. The study compares the resulting average sample count with 64-response majority voting and Adaptive Consistency on in-domain, text out-of-domain, and multimodal out-of-domain benchmarks.
