- **Unknown reward contract.** The paper optimizes a scalar `R_i` but does not disclose its components, verifier, answer-equivalence rule, scale, calibration, or code. Curator inference: reward hacking and alternative-valid-answer false negatives cannot be evaluated from public evidence.
- **Selective failure removal.** SFT uses rejection sampling, and RL removes some overlength no-answer negatives from the loss. Candidate attempts, exclusion counts, and retention policy are unknown, creating survivor and observability bias.
- **No complete trajectories.** The V2 directory contains only documentation and images. It releases neither successful SFT trajectories nor correct, incorrect, timed-out, retried, tool-failed, or overlength RL episodes.
- **Environment drift.** Google, Jina, Google Scholar, summary-model outputs, caches, backups, and page contents vary over time. No Wikipedia snapshot, service version, cache manifest, retry log, sandbox image, or replay fixture is public.
- **Mixture ambiguity.** §4.1 says SFT data is entirely synthetic from SailorFog-QA-V2, while §5.1 lists SailorFog-QA, SailorFog-QA-V2, and IterBench as training data. Stage-level counts and weights are not disclosed.
- **Contamination risk.** The pipeline mines live-web facts and is evaluated on six public benchmarks, but there is no exact, semantic, entity, source-page, temporal, or tool-observation overlap audit. The reported direct BrowseComp-test training experiment is not decontamination.
- **Scale ambiguity.** More than 30,000 refers to instruction-tuning pairs, not a verified count of complete SFT or RL trajectories. Record schema, exact count, splits, duplicates, languages, domains, and checksums are unavailable.
- **Rights and privacy.** Paper and repository licenses do not establish rights for unreleased graph records, trajectories, Wikipedia snapshots, cached web content, or tool observations. No privacy, robots-policy, source-exclusion, or takedown procedure is disclosed.
- **Version confusion.** V1 has code, a 20-row QA sample, and public WebSailor models; V2 does not. Treating V1 artifacts or the separately released Tongyi-DeepResearch model as V2 without explicit linkage would be misattribution.
- **Experimental scope.** Results are author-reported under one tool stack, without training-seed variance, independent reproduction, complete compute accounting, or a controlled decomposition of data, model, context, environment, and RL changes.
