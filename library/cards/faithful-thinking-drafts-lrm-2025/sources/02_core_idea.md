The official `faithful-thinking-draft` dataset contains six experimental configurations and tens of thousands of intervened reasoning drafts. Records include the original problem, model draft, intervention location, inserted or replaced content, subsequent continuation or rollback path, and final-answer change. The feedback is not whether a step looks correct but whether intervening on it changes later reasoning or the answer, supporting causal step influence, rollout value, and process diagnosis. Repeated outcomes at the same position allow estimation of average causal effects rather than treating one accidental flip as step value. Six configurations preserve the problem, full draft, intervention position, replacement, repeated continuations or rollbacks, and outcome, allowing mean effects and variance rather than treating one flip as step value.
