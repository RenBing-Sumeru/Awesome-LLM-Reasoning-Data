The paper compares LFS with ToT-BFS, BestFS, and LLM-guided MCTS on Countdown lengths 3, 5, and 7 and on 4x4 and 6x6 Sudoku. Each game-method pair is run five times; the paper reports average win rate, tokens per game, efficiency score, Wilson 95% confidence intervals, and aggregate performance profiles/AUP. The comparisons show that search behavior and token use differ across methods, models, and task difficulty under the studied setup. They do not establish that LLM values or explore decisions are calibrated, or that serialized traces would be suitable training data. The official repository at commit `3025bdaa3add6f41388c1d5a6d354522489d312e` contains source code, prompts, task instances, generators, and analysis tools, but no checked-in result pickles for item-level verification.
