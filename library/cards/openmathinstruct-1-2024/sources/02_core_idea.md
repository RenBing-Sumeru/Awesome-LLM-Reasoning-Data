OpenMathInstruct-1 turns an openly licensed Mixtral model into a competitive math-trace teacher by sampling hundreds of code-interpreter solutions per GSM8K/MATH problem and using masked reference solutions to expose structure without revealing intermediate or final numbers. Execution plus answer matching selects SFT targets, and fair round-robin sampling prevents easy problems with many successes from dominating; because the reusable object is the checked rationale/code trace, this is instruction/demonstration/rationale data rather than raw math pretraining or an evaluation-only benchmark.

Open dataset: yes. Dataset name: OpenMathInstruct-1. Official URL: https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/OpenMathInstruct-1. Scale: 1.8M correct problem-solution pairs and 6.6M incorrect sampled trajectories; correct coverage is 1.04M unique solutions for 7,469 GSM8K training problems and 787K for 6,978 MATH training problems. Record form: `question`, `generated_solution`, `expected_answer`, `predicted_answer`, `error_message`, `is_correct`, `dataset`, and `generation_type`. File/storage format: four JSONL files split by correct/incorrect and train/validation, totaling about 8.9 GB logical file size. Domains/languages: English grade-school and competition mathematics across GSM8K and seven MATH subjects. Construction and filtering: Mixtral-8x7B writes mixed prose/Python traces under default, subject, and masked-reference prompts; the interpreter runs code and a grading script checks the boxed answer, followed by syntax cleanup and deduplication. License/access constraints: public, ungated, commercially permissive NVIDIA License; source-dataset terms remain relevant. Intended use: mathematical reasoning SFT, distillation, verifier research with incorrect traces, and data-selection studies.
