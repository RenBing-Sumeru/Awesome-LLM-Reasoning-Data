Qwen3-4B Setter and Solver are cold-SFT initialized and then optimized with GRPO-style RL. For indefinite integrals, SymPy parses the integrand and candidate antiderivative and checks derivative equality. General math first rejects malformed, copied or degenerate outputs, then requires all GPT-5.4 rubric fields to pass. Eight rollouts train each role; ten Solver samples estimate local difficulty, and accepted pools undergo deduplication and difficulty filtering.
