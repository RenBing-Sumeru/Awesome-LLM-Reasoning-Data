The official Qwen release dated September 10, 2025 exposes a model architecture, aggregate pretraining scale, post-trained weights, and benchmark tables, but not the post-training records or feedback needed to reconstruct the reasoning pipeline. A named RL algorithm and downloadable weights are not themselves a disclosure of prompts, candidate responses, rewards, verifiers, rollout groups, or selection decisions.

Qwen3-Next belongs in the Frontier Reports and Data Disclosure Ledger as a partial-disclosure case. Its public data object is principally an aggregate statement that the base model used a uniformly sampled 15T-token subset of Qwen3's 36T-token pretraining corpus; the post-training data object remains unknown.
