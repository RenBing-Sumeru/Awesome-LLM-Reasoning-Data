The strongest evidence is conditional: under the paper's mixed-teacher distillation setup, LALP predicts and selects responses that yield better downstream point estimates than GALP. All results are author-reported and were not independently reproduced.

The within-teacher sanity check is an important negative boundary. On 8,890 filtered MATH level-3-to-5 prompts with 16 responses per prompt, higher GALP terciles yield stronger downstream results for Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct and QwQ-32B candidates (Table 1). GALP is therefore not shown to be universally invalid; its failure is tied to long responses from heterogeneous teachers with different styles and self-conditioning patterns.

The token-attribution analysis provides a proposed mechanism. In the reported critical regions, GALP mass is assigned 42.3% to discourse or filler, 31.2% to math or symbols, 12.8% to transitions, and 13.7% to other tokens. LALP shifts those shares to 18.7%, 48.6%, 22.4%, and 10.3%, respectively (Figure 4). These percentages support the authors' claim that local scoring emphasizes mathematical transitions, but the category annotation method is not a released token-label corpus.

The self-conditioning analysis shows that the global-minus-local log-probability gap increases with response position across roughly 800 responses from each of three teachers (Figure 5). Appendix C.1 also reports that LALP-selected examples can have higher global training loss yet yield better downstream results than GALP-selected examples. Together, these observations challenge “easier under full-prefix loss” as a sufficient data-quality criterion.

None of these results establishes step correctness. All candidate final answers are assumed to pass a separate matcher, and LALP only ranks them. Nor do the benchmark gains certify the selected data's provenance, license, contamination status, segmentation accuracy, statistical stability, or universal usefulness. The 817 selected responses and their candidate/score ledger are unavailable, so the relationship between any individual record and the reported improvement cannot be inspected.

Release inspection supplies a separate evidence layer: the ICML, arXiv, and OpenReview identities are official, but the paper-linked anonymous repository currently redirects to its file API and returns HTTP 401. Its contents are therefore unverifiable. No implementation, repository license, selected corpus, candidate pool, step boundary file, likelihood ledger, checkpoint, log, or immutable run manifest was verified.
