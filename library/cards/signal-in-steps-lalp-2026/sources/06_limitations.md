- **LALP is not a correctness signal.** The method operates only after candidates are declared final-answer correct. It neither checks step validity nor detects a correct answer reached through invalid reasoning. The final-answer matcher, extraction, equivalence mapping, false accepts, and false rejects are not documented.
- **Long-range consistency is unresolved.** The paper explicitly notes that an early wrong premise can make later locally coherent steps score highly. Small context may miss dependencies needed for proof validity, factual consistency, variable identity, or code state. The authors suggest adding a global coherence check, but no such mechanism is evaluated.
- **Segmentation creates an unvalidated judge dependency.** GLM-4.5-Air decides step boundaries without human agreement or a segmentation-accuracy study. Split and merge choices alter equal-step weighting. Curator inference: a teacher's formatting style can change its score even when semantic content is identical, creating segmenter-mediated bias.
- **Student likelihood is not causal influence.** A high LocalLP means a transition is familiar to one pretrained student, not that training on it causes improvement. Curator inference: LALP can prefer familiar shortcuts, inherited pretraining artifacts, or stylistic patterns and may reject novel but useful reasoning.
- **Selection is student-specific.** The same response pool can be ranked differently by different students. Although teacher ordering is shown for several Qwen students, the paper does not establish a universal selected corpus across architectures, tokenizer families, sizes, or post-training histories.
- **Window choice is incompletely mapped.** Small windows can omit necessary context; large windows converge to GALP. Integer-window peaks differ near `k=4` and `k=5` for the reported 7B and 32B students, but every headline run is not mapped to an exact `k`, so reproduction and sensitivity analysis remain incomplete.
- **Equal-step averaging is boundary-sensitive.** A short step receives the same response-level weight as a long, information-dense step. Curator inference: over-segmentation can amplify filler transitions, while under-segmentation can restore token-length domination and hide sparse reasoning moves.
- **The main scale is narrow.** The principal selected set contains 817 public LIMO prompts and 817 retained responses. Main teachers and students are heavily Qwen-centered. Math results dominate the evidence, while science and code are appendix extensions; universal data-quality claims are unsupported.
- **Candidate and failure records are absent.** The selected 817 responses, unselected correct responses, incorrectly answered candidates, parse failures, ties, segmentation failures, step boundaries, likelihoods, and row-level decisions are not released. Selection bias and failure modes cannot be audited.
- **Sampling and training mappings are ambiguous.** Slash-delimited teacher-sampling and SFT settings are not mapped cleanly to every experiment. Candidate count, temperature, top-p, top-k, `k`, seeds, checkpoint selection, retry behavior, runtime, and total compute are partly or wholly unknown.
- **The embedding mechanism is underspecified.** The .935 step-level and .760 trajectory-level coverage result lacks a disclosed embedding model, normalization, preprocessing, and exact mapping from responses to steps. It supports the reported correlation but is not independently reconstructable.
- **No contamination audit is reported.** There is no row-level lexical, semantic, or provenance comparison between LIMO, MATH, OpenCodeReasoning, LeetCode, and the evaluation suites, nor any audit of teacher or student pretraining. Public benchmark provenance does not establish contamination freedom.
- **Licensing is incomplete.** Upstream LIMO, MATH, and GLM-4.5-Air publish licenses, but no license or teacher-output provenance ledger covers generated candidates, selected responses, boundaries, scores, code, or checkpoints. The arXiv manuscript license is not a data or code license.
- **The claimed repository is unavailable for verification.** At audit time, the anonymous URL redirected to its file API and returned HTTP 401 without authentication. No implementation, repository license, data, configuration, dependency lock, commit, or release could be verified. A paper link is not evidence of a reproducible artifact.
- **Statistical stability is unknown.** Results are point estimates without independent training seeds, confidence intervals, or error bars. The claim that 200 prompts recover teacher order also lacks repeated-subsample variance.
- **Evaluation gains do not certify data quality.** Table 3 and the domain extensions combine candidate generation, correctness admission, segmentation, student-specific scoring, SFT, and benchmark evaluation. They do not establish record-level correctness, faithfulness, rights, privacy or consent, decontamination, or deployment robustness.
- **A table-level ambiguity remains.** Appendix Table 8 appears to label both model blocks Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct despite showing different selection mixtures. Without the run manifest, model assignment cannot be independently resolved.
