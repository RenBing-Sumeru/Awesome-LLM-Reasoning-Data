Most reasoning benchmarks assume complete information, whereas real user queries often omit one crucial variable. A model that guesses directly can fail even when it knows how to solve the complete problem. QuestBench formalizes information acquisition as a constraint-satisfaction problem with missing assignments and asks models to select the minimal sufficient clarification question. It covers logic, PDDL planning, human-edited GSM, and equation-based GSM tasks, testing whether models know what information is missing rather than only solving fully specified problems.
