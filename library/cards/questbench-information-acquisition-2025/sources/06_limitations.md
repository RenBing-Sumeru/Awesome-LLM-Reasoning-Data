1. **One-question limit:** The benchmark allows at most one clarification and does not cover multi-turn active exploration or question-cost tradeoffs.

2. **Option bias:** Multiple-choice options reduce natural-language generation difficulty and may expose superficial patterns.

3. **Formalization scope:** Tasks must be expressible as deterministic CSPs; ambiguous preferences, noisy replies, and user refusal are not covered. Extensions need free-form question parsing and interaction costs.
