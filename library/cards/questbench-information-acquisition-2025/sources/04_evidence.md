Frontier models perform strongly on GSM-Q and GSME-Q but usually reach only about 40–50% accuracy on Logic-Q and Planning-Q. Analysis shows that some models can solve the fully specified version yet still fail to identify which variable to ask about. In Planning-Q, models remain overconfident even when “not sure” is available. This controlled comparison supports the distinction between solving and information acquisition, although multiple-choice selection is narrower than real free-form questioning.
