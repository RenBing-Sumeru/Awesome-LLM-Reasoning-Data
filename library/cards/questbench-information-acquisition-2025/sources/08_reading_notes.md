1. **One-sentence position:** QuestBench tests whether a model can ask the minimal sufficient question when one variable is missing.
2. **Method takeaway:** Complete CSP states, key-assignment hiding, question options, and unique-solution verification.
3. **Data takeaway:** Logic-Q, Planning-Q, GSM-Q, and GSME-Q retain complete and incomplete versions.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Logic-Q and Planning-Q are around 40–50%, and models often ask incorrectly despite solving complete versions.
5. **Reuse decision:** It fits active information acquisition; multi-turn settings need a separate interaction environment.
