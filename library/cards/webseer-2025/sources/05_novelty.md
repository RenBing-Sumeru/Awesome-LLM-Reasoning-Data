Relative to outcome-only search RL such as Search-R1 and web-search agents that terminate after one answer, WebSeer's specific change is to keep an episode open after a failed submission. The same record can contain answer, textual score, reflection, additional tool use, and a later answer. In cold-start construction, verifier feedback contributes not just a label but a tool-derived evidence path, and the known answer is used to reject inconsistent verifier judgments.

The work does not introduce SFT followed by RL, rejection sampling, GRPO, DAPO clip-higher, search tools, or answer-level scoring individually. Its useful direction signal is their organization around a repeated submission-feedback-reflection trajectory and the explicit ablation of that loop. The novelty claim should not be upgraded into a claim that the verifier is calibrated or that the released traces are complete.
