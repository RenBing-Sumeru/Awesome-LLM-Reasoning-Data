**Main 304K recipe.** MuMath-70B reaches 88.3% on GSM8K and 34.5% on MATH, versus 82.3% and 26.6% for the paper's MetaMath comparison, gains of 6.0 and 7.9 points. With a 7B consumer, combining D1-D4 reaches 69.2/21.6, while the single-family reformulation and question-alteration conditions reach 59.6 on GSM8K and 17.9 on MATH respectively; this supports complementarity but does not isolate teacher cost or source overlap.

**Scale and version boundary.** Appendix C increases n from 1 to 6, moving the corpus from 141K to 751K. The 13B consumer rises monotonically from 81.6/31.2 at 527K to 82.1/32.8 at 643K and 83.6/33.3 at 751K, while the 7B GSM8K result peaks at 81.1 for 643K then falls to 79.1 at 751K. More data is therefore not uniformly better, and these scaled results must not replace the main 304K claims.
