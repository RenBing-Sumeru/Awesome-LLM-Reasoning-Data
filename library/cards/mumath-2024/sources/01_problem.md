MuMath asks whether a tool-free model can gain mathematical accuracy without hiding its calculation process, by broadening augmentation beyond simple rephrasing or unconstrained difficulty increases. The primary source is Findings of NAACL 2024, and the decision boundary is its natural-language question-solution corpus rather than the later executable MuMath-Code traces.

The relevant data object starts from GSM8K and MATH training questions and creates reformulations, backward constructions, altered questions, and solutions augmented with outlines or plans. Known answers, GPT-4 judgments, and majority answers select demonstrations, while LLaMA-2 consumers are evaluated on GSM8K, MATH, and transfer sets; this static supervision object belongs directly to instruction, demonstration, and rationale data.
