The contribution is a principled free-form generation benchmark with fine-grained, instance-specific criteria across 9 capabilities and 77 tasks. The core mechanism is to attach a scoring rubric to every instance and then evaluate model outputs with multiple evaluator LMs and human-aligned checks. Closest comparisons are instruction-following or preference benchmarks with broad global criteria; BiGGen changes the unit of assessment to per-instance requirements. Direction label: LLM-as-judge meta-evaluation and rubric-bearing open-generation benchmark.
