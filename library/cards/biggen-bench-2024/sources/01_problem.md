BiGGen Bench was submitted to arXiv in June 2024 and listed as a NAACL 2025 Main Conference paper. It addresses the gap between broad open-generation behavior and coarse evaluation labels such as helpfulness or harmlessness. The evaluation object is a free-form generation instance with capability/task metadata, instruction context, input, reference answer, and an instance-specific 1-5 rubric. The feedback contract is judgment by human or LLM evaluators against that rubric, so this card belongs as a judge/rubric-based evaluation surface, not as a deterministic verifier or model-training recipe.
