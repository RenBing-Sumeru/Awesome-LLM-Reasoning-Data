Use this paper to audit a verifier-guided inference pipeline before increasing its sample count. Instrument the pipeline to estimate candidate diversity and success coverage, verifier agreement with trusted outcomes, the difference between sequential and batched selection, total verifier calls, and wall-clock cost. Those measurements turn the paper’s three-way diagnosis into a practical choice: invest in a broader generator, a better-calibrated verifier, or a less sub-optimal sampling rule. It is especially valuable when a pass-at-k improvement has stopped translating into selected-answer quality.
