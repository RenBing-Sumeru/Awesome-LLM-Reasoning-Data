The analysis relies on modeling choices about the proposal distribution, target behavior, and verifier approximation that may be difficult to estimate for a production system. Its empirical demonstrations use the reported models and tasks, so open-ended generation, tool-mediated episodes, and nonstationary online verifiers can create effects not represented by the stated regimes. Moreover, a theoretically informative decomposition does not automatically give a cheap online estimator for coverage or the verifier’s region of convergence; practitioners still need measurements, calibration studies, and cost-aware stopping rules.
