Prior work studies metric validity or individual human-evaluation practices, often for a task or period. This paper changes the unit of analysis to a seven-decade ACL corpus and jointly traces modality, criteria, task, and annotation-method signals, including the recent LLM-judge transition. Its contribution is not a validated per-paper classifier: the patterns measure terminology and reported practice, and the authors explicitly retain precision over recall. The reusable novelty is the transparent audit protocol and longitudinal baseline.
