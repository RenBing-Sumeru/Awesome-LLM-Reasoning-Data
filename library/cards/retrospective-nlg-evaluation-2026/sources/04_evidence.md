Claim: LLM judges are growing quickly but mostly complement, rather than replace, human evaluation. In the 24,291-paper corpus, 109 papers used LLM judges in 2024 and 224 in 2025, versus 19 in 2023 (Results, §3). Of instruction-tuned-judge papers, 61.4% also reported human evaluation and 86.2% of that subset mentioned validation/manual checking; therefore 38.6% used a judge without reported human evaluation. This supports a calibration gap in the audited literature, not an estimate of true judge accuracy or a causal claim about all NLG research.
