1. Position: a longitudinal audit, not an evaluator, of how NLG papers report evaluation.
2. Lever: corpus filtering followed by precision-oriented regex extraction; extraction quality, not a model score, governs the evidence.
3. Artifact: public repository releases IDs, metadata, and extracted signals; raw full text is excluded for copyright.
4. Anchor: 224 LLM-judge papers in 2025; 38.6% lacked reported human evaluation, a reporting-calibration gap.
5. Reuse: audit local practice and mandate human calibration; first validate patterns on a sampled target corpus.
