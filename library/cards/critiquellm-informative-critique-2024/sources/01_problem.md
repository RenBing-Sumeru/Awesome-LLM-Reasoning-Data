General LLM judges often output only scores or brief rationales, offering little actionable feedback for model improvement, while training specialized critique models requires large-scale data covering diverse tasks, response qualities, and reference-based or reference-free settings. CRITIQUELLM studies how to construct high-quality critique supervision so open models can score responses, identify strengths and weaknesses, cite evidence, and suggest improvements while aligning with human and GPT-4 judgments.
