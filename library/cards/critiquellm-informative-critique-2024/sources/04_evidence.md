CritiqueLLM achieves strong system-level agreement with GPT-4 and human judgments. The largest model is competitive across reference-based and reference-free tasks and surpasses GPT-4 on three of eight reference-free evaluations. Feeding generated critiques to an OpenAI conversational baseline also improves responses, suggesting that the feedback is actionable. Data ablations support the importance of diverse queries and responses of varying quality, although labels are primarily generated by GPT-4 and may transmit its scoring biases and stylistic patterns.
