Eval-Instruct provides a reusable recipe for specialized judge data: expand tasks, sample responses of varied quality, and generate structured critiques. The released data can train generative reward models, feedback models, and data filters, or support critique-conditioned SFT. Reuse should add human audits, factual tools, and multiple teachers, while separately evaluating score agreement, error localization, and downstream correction gains rather than merely imitating GPT-4 style.
