1. **One-sentence positioning:** An open judge model trained on large-scale synthetic data to generate detailed critiques.
2. **Method takeaway:** Seed expansion, multi-stage filtering, responses from ten models, and two forms of GPT-4 critique.
3. **Data takeaway:** About 1,000 final queries and 8,865 training examples, plus validation and AlignBench tests.
4. **Evidence anchor:** System rankings approach GPT-4, some reference-free tasks exceed it, and critiques can improve revisions.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is useful for generative-judge data construction, with teacher bias and factual errors requiring control.
