The paper proposes the Eval-Instruct construction pipeline and trains CritiqueLLM models at 6B, 12B, and 66B scales. Starting from 706 seeds, the pipeline expands to about 260K candidates, filters them into 4,223 diverse and 3,351 high-quality questions, and selects a balanced set of 1,000 queries. Responses from ten models are paired with GPT-4-generated reference-based and reference-free critiques, producing 8,865 training examples. The models generate both scores and fine-grained feedback, matching or surpassing GPT-4 judges on several tasks.
