Earlier open evaluators often predict only scalar scores or depend on fixed tasks and small human rubrics. CRITIQUELLM combines instruction synthesis, cross-model response sampling, and detailed critique distillation to support both reference-based and reference-free evaluation. Its main novelty lies in data construction and the output contract: judges provide verdicts, explanations, and revision suggestions, while large-scale candidate generation and multi-stage filtering expand task coverage rather than simply copying existing benchmarks.
