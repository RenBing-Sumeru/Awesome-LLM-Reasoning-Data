The method extracts text between think tags, segments steps at double-newline boundaries, and feeds each step-end hidden state to the scorer. Trace-level correctness is propagated to all of its steps as pseudo-labels; balanced correct and incorrect traces are used, with weighted binary cross entropy compensating for longer incorrect traces. The trace score is the average of observed step scores, so it can be updated without a separate model call.

During parallel generation, a KV-cache monitor detects memory saturation. STEP then prunes the lowest-scoring active trace rather than allowing the engine to preempt it into a waiting queue. The paper evaluates Qwen3-4B-Thinking, DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B, and Phi-4-reasoning-plus on AIME-25, HMMT-24/25, GPQA Diamond, EquiBench, and DivLogicEval, comparing with self-consistency, Slim-SC, and DeepConf.
