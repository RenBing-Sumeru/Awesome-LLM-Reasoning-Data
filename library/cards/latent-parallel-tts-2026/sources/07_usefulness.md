Use this work when a latent-reasoning model is compact but its deterministic trajectory leaves accuracy on the table. Begin with a small parallel budget and calibrate dropout or noise on held-out coverage, then compare LatentRM best-of-N with a matched-cost beam search and a nonparametric vote. Keep explicit answers and execution traces for downstream auditing: the latent selector can be efficient, but it should not be the only safeguard where a final answer must be externally checked.
