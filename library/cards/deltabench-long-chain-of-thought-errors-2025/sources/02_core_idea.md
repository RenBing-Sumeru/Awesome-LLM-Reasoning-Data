DeltaBench contains 1,236 long reasoning trajectories from several o1-like models across mathematics, code, and general reasoning. Records include segment correctness and earliest errors as well as strategy transitions, reflection behavior, and step validity, allowing researchers to separate genuine correction, unproductive detours, and continued reasoning after failure. The benchmark analyzes both long-CoT quality and efficiency and measures how existing process verifiers degrade with increasing length and structural complexity.
