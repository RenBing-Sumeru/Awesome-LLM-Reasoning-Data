1. **Sample long chains:** Collect complete long chains of thought from reasoning models such as QwQ and DeepSeek-R1 on mathematical, coding, and general tasks.
2. **Structure the trajectories:** Segment chains by semantic phase and explicitly record strategy shifts, backtracking, reflection, and final answering.
3. **Annotate the process:** Judge correctness and usefulness for each segment, identify the earliest error, and distinguish valid reflection from faulty correction.
4. **Build the diagnostic benchmark:** Package 1,236 trajectories and evaluate PRMs and LLM critics by length, task, and structure; segmentation rules and context length must remain fixed.
