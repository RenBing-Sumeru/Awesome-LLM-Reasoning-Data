The arXiv abstract reports comprehensive experiments showing substantial degradation in state-of-the-art GUI agents under anomaly-rich environments. The strongest evidence pattern is paired evaluation: compare the same or comparable Android GUI tasks under idealized conditions and under injected anomalies. Instance-level evidence comes from task predicates over app state and action trajectories, not from subjective natural-language grading. The evidence boundary is that the public abstract does not by itself expose every task, split, predicate, or anomaly template; score reuse should pin the official repository version and evaluator before treating aggregate degradation as comparable across studies.
