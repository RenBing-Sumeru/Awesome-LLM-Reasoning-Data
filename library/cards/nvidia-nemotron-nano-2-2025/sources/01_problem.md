Efficient reasoning models must preserve long traces and tool behavior while reducing inference cost, and their training claims are difficult to audit when alignment and compression are reported separately. Nemotron Nano 2 addresses both problems: it documents the data and feedback used to align a 12B hybrid model, then shows how pruning, forward-KL distillation, targeted RL, preference optimization, and checkpoint merging produce a 9B model. The audit question is how much of that pipeline is pinned by released records and where “majority of the data” still leaves gaps.
