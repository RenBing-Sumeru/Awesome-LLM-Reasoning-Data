The paper directly reports the domain counts, teachers, filters, three SFT stages, IFEval verifier, WorkBench state predicate, Qwen reward model, merge sweep, and full compression/distillation schedule. It evaluates the aligned and compressed models on math, code, science, instruction following, tool use, long context, and chat, and reports 3–6× higher throughput than Qwen3-8B in generation-heavy settings while retaining competitive accuracy. These results support the efficiency claim, not the absence of data bias. Official Hugging Face pages independently expose the 9B/12B checkpoints and Nemotron-Post-Training-Dataset-v2; the dataset card provides category counts, generator models, prompt/response construction, and per-sample license metadata.
