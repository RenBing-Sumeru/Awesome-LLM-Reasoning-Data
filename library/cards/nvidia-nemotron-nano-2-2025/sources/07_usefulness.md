This Card is a practical blueprint for representing reasoning post-training as data transformations rather than a list of algorithms. It connects each record type—reasoning trace, reasoning-off answer, truncated trace, tool trajectory, preference pair, reward-scored rollout—to its generator, filter, feedback contract, optimizer, and downstream checkpoint. It is especially useful for studying controllable thinking budgets, verifiable tool-use preferences, staged distillation, and release-to-training audit gaps.
