The paper says it releases the majority—not all—of its data, so exact train-to-release correspondence is unproven. Rollout counts, sampling temperatures, reward calibration, DPO/RLHF pair volumes, human-feedback protocol, and post-training benchmark decontamination are not fully specified. Synthetic traces inherit DeepSeek/Qwen biases; the Qwen-based reward model may favor its own style. The official dataset mixes CC-BY-4.0 with smaller ODC-BY and CC-BY-SA subsets and warns that Qwen/DeepSeek terms may apply. Targeted GRPO and RLHF also caused benchmark regressions that required KD and model merging.
