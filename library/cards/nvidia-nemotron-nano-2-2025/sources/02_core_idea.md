The recipe starts from Nemotron-Nano-12B-v2-Base, pretrained on 20T tokens and extended to 128K. Alignment uses about 80B tokens of prompt-response SFT data across reasoning, tool use, conversation, multilingual, and safety domains, followed by rule-verifier RL, on-policy DPO, Qwen-reward-model GRPO, RLHF, and checkpoint interpolation. The aligned 12B parent is then depth/width pruned and recovered with staged logit distillation at increasing sequence lengths. The final 9B model, sibling/base checkpoints, and most pre- and post-training data are released.
