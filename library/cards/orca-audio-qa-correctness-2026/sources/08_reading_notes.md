1. **One-sentence positioning:** An evaluator predicting a correctness distribution rather than a single score for open-ended audio QA.
2. **Method takeaway:** Synthetic pretraining, LLM-judge distillation, human calibration, and Beta-distribution modeling.
3. **Data takeaway:** 3,580 QA pairs from 15 models with 11,721 human ratings, plus large-scale weak supervision.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Human agreement reaches α=0.82 and correlation with mean human scores reaches Spearman 0.91.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is useful for audio judges and uncertainty research, with text-teacher bias requiring audits.
