The paper develops a three-stage data and training pipeline and releases human and synthetic resources for open-ended audio-QA evaluation. The core benchmark contains 3,580 question-answer pairs from 15 large audio-language models on two audio-QA datasets, with 11,721 human ratings and Krippendorff’s α of 0.82. ORCA predicts a Beta distribution over correctness rather than a single discrete label, providing both expected score and uncertainty, and reaches a Spearman correlation of 0.91 with mean human judgments.
