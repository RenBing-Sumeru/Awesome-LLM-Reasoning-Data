The official methodology page reports the v4.1 component list and weights, and states a 95% confidence interval below about plus/minus 1% for the aggregate index based on repeated experiments on selected models. It also discloses general testing parameters, including different temperature treatment for reasoning and non-reasoning models.

The strongest evidence is the component-level score under each verifier, not the final weighted number alone. A Terminal-Bench result has a different evidentiary meaning from a judge-panel GDPval-AA result or an equality-checker open-answer result.

The evidence boundary is that internal dataset copies, some AA-specific variants, and exact runner state were not verified as downloadable artifacts.
