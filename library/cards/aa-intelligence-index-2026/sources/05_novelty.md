The prior baseline is a multi-benchmark leaderboard that leaves readers to mentally combine scores. Artificial Analysis changes that by publishing one weighted score and the weights behind it.

The quality signal is the explicit table of categories, task counts, repeats, response types, scoring rules, and tool-usage flags. What is not new is any single component benchmark or verifier type.

Before reuse, inspect whether the aggregate weighting matches the intended use case, whether private/internal component copies are acceptable, and whether component-level scores tell a different story from the final index.
