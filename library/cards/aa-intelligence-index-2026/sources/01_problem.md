Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index asks how to summarize broad language-model capability into one maintained comparison score. The primary source is the official Artificial Analysis Intelligence Benchmarking methodology page, currently describing Intelligence Index v4.1.

The decision boundary is that this is an aggregate benchmark methodology, not a primary dataset, training recipe, or proof that one model is universally better. The evaluation surface is a weighted suite of component tasks across agents, coding, scientific reasoning, and general knowledge.

Its value is audit-oriented: it exposes how a popular public index combines heterogeneous verifier contracts, from test suites and official grading servers to judge panels and equality-checker models.
