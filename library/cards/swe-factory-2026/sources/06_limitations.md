The open objects do not reconcile. The repository has 671 raw SweSetupBench rows, the paper describes 2,877 constructed and 2,809 prepared Python environments, the primary trajectory dataset has 2,809 `messages`-only rows, and SWE-Factory-Gym has 430 task/environment rows. No stable IDs or manifest map these sets to training subsets, five checkpoints, or evaluation predictions. Success, verifier output, reward, and rejected records are absent from the primary trajectory schema.

The verifier can be both false-negative and semantically over-permissive. Marker typos and `set -e` caused 16 reported false negatives. Conversely, any nonzero pre-patch exit can satisfy the first half of the predicate, including crashes, missing dependencies, and timeouts; the reported parser confusion matrix does not prove that every nonzero represents the intended failing test. Because the gold patch is used for acceptance, source linkage and gold-label errors are not independently caught.

Environment reproduction is incomplete. Dockerfiles and evaluation scripts are recipes, not immutable environments. Base-image tags can move, and the release lacks built images, digest-pinned bases, package locks, binary-resource hashes, SBOMs, build logs, and a task-wide mapping from every episode to its exact container. The missing `Appendix.pdf`, absent paper-run tag, and drift between accepted settings and current examples further weaken reconstruction.

Identity and citation surfaces drift. The accepted paper is **SWE Data Construction, Automatically!** and lists Haoyu Song; older arXiv/repository citation metadata uses the longer SWE-Factory title and Yingtian Zou. The accepted PDF still contains placeholder 2018 ACM metadata, a dummy DOI, and `Trovato et al.` headers. Until an official proceedings record is available, an ACM URL, DOI, page range, and final BibTeX remain unknown.

Release rights are unresolved. The code repository's custom notice applies AGPL-3.0 terms to non-commercial/academic use with separate commercial licensing, while the Hugging Face dataset cards declare MIT without analyzing upstream licenses for issues, repository code, patches, tests, logs, or derived trajectories. The five model repositories have neither model cards nor declared licenses. Privacy, consent, secret/PII scanning, malicious-code containment, and takedown handling are also not disclosed.

Contamination controls stop at an author-reported repository split: ten training repositories are said to differ from SWE-bench repositories, but the missing appendix prevents membership audit. No issue-, commit-, patch-, semantic-, clone-, solution-, generator-pretraining-, or base-model-pretraining overlap study is released. Four languages and 12 benchmark repositories, plus training models no larger than 14B, bound the experimental generalization.
