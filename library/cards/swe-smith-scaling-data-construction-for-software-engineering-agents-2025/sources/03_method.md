Repository selection starts from the 5,000 most-downloaded PyPI packages as of 2024-11-18. The authors sort associated repositories by GitHub stars, remove repositories below 1,000 stars, and exclude all 12 SWE-bench test repositories. For each retained repository they use one commit and one verified image. The paper reports 125 unique repository-commit images occupying 290.54 GB for the 128-repository collection; immutable image digests are not published in the task rows.

Candidate generation produces `.diff` files. Execution validation compares tests before and after the bug patch and records target failures and preservation tests. The paper reports 50,137 accepted instances: 17,887 LM Modify, 4,173 LM Rewrite, 15,641 Procedural, 10,092 combined, and 2,344 PR Mirror. The main Hugging Face task schema at revision `ea6d7173829c7ec8fa16c22055699ff2e9188091` contains `instance_id`, the bug-inducing `patch`, `FAIL_TO_PASS`, `PASS_TO_PASS`, `image_name`, `repo`, and `problem_statement`. The patch field must not be interpreted as the agent's answer.

For trajectory collection, SWE-agent exposes shell execution, file viewing/editing, and submission actions. Expert runs are limited to 75 steps and USD 2.00. The paper reports 17,906 attempts over 8,686 unique tasks, producing a pool of 6,457 successful episodes. To reduce repeated easy examples, at most three successes per task are kept, yielding 5,016 fine-tuning rows. Teacher action records are converted from function-call form to the XML-style representation used by the student. Most rows use Claude 3.7 Sonnet; smaller comparison subsets use Claude 3.5 Sonnet and GPT-4o.

Release history matters. The paper-time trajectory revision `470d6f28b7219c95f43c47843cc7c69f63e077f0` has 5,016 rows with `messages` and `instance_id`. A later revision, `08e109b4a59eaeebf80e4675cd125d42e7ac99a4`, exposes `tool`, `xml`, and `ticks` splits with `resolved`, `model`, `traj_id`, and submitted `patch` fields and includes at least some unresolved rows. These split counts are alternative release views and must not be summed as independent trajectories; whether every failed paper attempt is retained remains unknown.
