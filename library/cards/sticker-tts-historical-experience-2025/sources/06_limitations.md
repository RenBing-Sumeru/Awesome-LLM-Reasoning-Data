The approach requires three specialized model calls per refinement round and still relies on long reasoning generation in the modifier and utilizer, so practical latency, serving cost, and orchestration failures may matter even when the long-solution count is favorable. A corrupted sticker can carry a wrong abstraction or correction into later rounds, and majority voting among sequentially related answers may overstate the amount of independent evidence. The reported results are concentrated on exact-answer mathematics, while the calibration of stickers, the stopping rule, and the value of history reuse remain to be established for open-ended, tool-mediated, or non-verifiable tasks.
