The novelty is not simply using one model to critique another or iterating on an answer. Sticker-TTS assigns historical reasoning a deliberately bounded, structured representation between full trajectories and final answers. That representation contains the conditions and strategy judged worth carrying forward, then becomes an object of explicit error correction before it steers the next computation. This directly addresses the information-management problem created by long multi-round histories.

It also couples this inference design to a tailored training recipe. The three role-specific models are first taught through distilled examples and then improved using their own filtered iterative trajectories, including both repaired failures and valid refinements. Compared with independent best-of-N sampling, the system's added computation is sequentially dependent: later tokens are spent after examining a compact account of what earlier computation established or got wrong. This makes it a concrete proposal for progressive, history-aware allocation of test-time budget.
