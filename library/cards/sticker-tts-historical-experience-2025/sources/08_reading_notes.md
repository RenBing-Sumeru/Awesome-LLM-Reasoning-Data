When reading or reproducing the work, inspect the sticker schema and prompts before focusing on headline accuracy. Check how an extractor distinguishes an indispensable condition from decorative reasoning, how the modifier is prevented from confidently inventing a correction, and how much of the improvement survives when the same total tokens are allocated to independent samples. The most informative replication reports per-round answer accuracy, sticker factuality, transition type, total generated tokens, latency, and the correlation among final candidate answers. It should also separate improvements from the two-stage training recipe from improvements caused by the recursive inference loop itself.
