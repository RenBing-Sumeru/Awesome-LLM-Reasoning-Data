Sticker-TTS is useful when a reasoning service can afford several sequential attempts but wants each new attempt to react to earlier evidence rather than restart independently. A practical implementation should log the question, every trace and answer, extracted and revised stickers, role prompts, iteration count, token and wall-clock cost, and final aggregation. Those records make it possible to test whether a gain comes from genuine correction, merely more generated text, or a particular role prompt; they also support replacing individual roles with smaller or stronger models while preserving the same history interface.
