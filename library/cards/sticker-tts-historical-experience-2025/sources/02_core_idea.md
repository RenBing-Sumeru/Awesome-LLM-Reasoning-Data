Sticker-TTS represents an earlier solution attempt through a short structured object called a sticker. Rather than retaining the whole trace as undifferentiated context, an extractor summarizes key reasoning steps and global strategy into conditions and a question summary. A separate modifier then inspects that object for computational or methodological errors, so the next attempt receives a revised cue rather than an unexamined memory.

The central design is a division of labor among three language-model roles: extraction preserves the important content, modification tests and repairs it, and utilization combines the repaired sticker with the question and previous answer to write a new trace. A sticker is therefore neither a mere answer nor a passive summary. It is an editable intermediate representation that makes historical experience operational in the choice of the next reasoning path.
