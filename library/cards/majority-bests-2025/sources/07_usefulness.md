For the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track, this release supports controlled offline comparison of selection rules without regenerating outputs. A reusable record should retain the benchmark and doc_id, exact prompt, generator and decoding settings, all candidate completions, extracted-answer normalization, benchmark correctness, reward-model identity and version, raw scalar rewards, selected budget N, subsample size m or adaptive rule, estimated answer masses, tie handling, selected candidate index, and final answer. Keeping generation_score separate from reward scores prevents benchmark correctness from being mistaken for the online selection signal. The pools can be used to audit BoN, self-consistency, Weighted BoN, and MoB under equal candidate budgets, study selector sensitivity to reward models, or prototype stopping rules. Reuse should remain conditional on upstream licenses, contamination checks, and the fixed-pool nature of the release.
