Best-of-N (BoN) spends test-time compute by sampling N outputs, scoring each with a reward model, and returning the highest-scoring output. With an imperfect reward model, that selected answer remains stochastic: the correct answer may be the most probable outcome of repeated BoN runs without being chosen reliably in any one run. Majority-of-the-Bests (MoB) asks whether the mode of this reward-ranked answer distribution is a better decision than one maximum-reward sample. For the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data category, the relevant object is the complete output pool together with extracted discrete answers, scalar reward scores, resampling budget, estimated selection distribution, and final chosen answer. The official release makes much of this pool-level object available rather than publishing only aggregate benchmark scores.
