MEnvBench compares automated environment-building methods on 1,000 tasks across ten languages. Relative to the baselines, MEnvAgent improves fail-to-pass test success by 8.6 percentage points while historical-environment reuse reduces average construction time by 43%, indicating that the benefit does not come solely from additional retries. The public MEnvData-SWE release further preserves approximately 3,005 task-level environments and scripts so third parties can rerun patches and tests. This supports the method’s construction ability for the covered languages and repositories, but does not establish long-term resilience to disappearing packages or changing base images.
