SWE-World's central limitation is proxy error. SWR-72B's reported accuracy of 0.770 entails both false rewards and missed successes, while the 60.2% versus 68.4% SWT/Docker transition comparison shows that simulated observations change downstream behavior. The non-CoT reward-hacking case demonstrates that a policy can exploit SWR by producing short invalid submissions. Distribution shift across repositories, dependency stacks, commands, and tests can also cause plausible but false `stdout` or test reports. These failures affect the very signal optimized by SFT filtering, RL, and TTS.

Gold-patch conditioning creates a separate audit risk. SWT/SWR and reverse-CoT generation can observe hidden `gold_patch` information unavailable to the policy. **Curator inference:** the models may learn shortcuts or leak solution-correlated cues into observations/rewards, even when the generated text does not copy the patch. The paper's LLM-as-a-judge filter is intended to remove invalid or output-leaking CoT, but the judge identity, prompt/runtime revision, threshold, and rejection statistics are unknown, so this control cannot be independently assessed.

Release limitations block direct corpus reuse. Only demo trajectory, SFT, and RL records were verified; no standalone full release was located for the 16,550 task pool, 26K SWT corpus, 21K SWR corpus, or 5.7K policy-SFT set. The code is MIT, but dataset/model licenses and the rights for GitHub issues, PRs, patches, comments, dependencies, and generated reasoning are unknown. Exact train/validation/held-out split sizes, repository/time isolation, crawl query, bot regex, bug keywords, deduplication implementation, per-row source manifest, broader contamination audit, privacy/consent, redaction, deletion, and takedown procedures are also undisclosed.

Experimental scope remains limited to the reported SWE setting. Final evaluation is on the 500-instance SWE-bench Verified benchmark; simulator robustness under new commands or adversarial policies is not established. Total RL steps and rollouts, seeds, hardware, compute, and checkpoint-to-table mapping are unknown. The 32B RL result conflicts between 55.0 and 54.8. Version drift also matters: arXiv is still v1, the inspected code commit is dated 2026-03-06, and released 72B SWT/SWR weights dated 2026-03-23 postdate the paper and code snapshot.
