The pipeline has three connected but non-identical data flows.

1. **World-model supervision.** Tasks come from SWE-Gym, SWE-rebench, and R2E-Gym. GLM-4.6 and MinMax-M2 act at temperature 0.7 for at most 100 turns in real Docker environments. Corrupted environments/logs are removed, but valid reward-0 and reward-1 rollouts are retained. Per-execution records supervise SWT; terminal test records supervise SWR, whose labels are subsampled to a 1:1 reward balance. Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking adds reverse CoT conditioned on Docker outputs, followed by LLM-judge filtering. The paper reports 26K non-CoT plus 26K CoT SWT examples and 21K non-CoT plus 21K CoT SWR examples. SWT/SWR are SFT-tuned from Qwen2.5-Instruct-32B/72B; judge identity, judge sampling budget, acceptance rate, and complete example releases are unknown. (Paper §4.2; Appendix B.)

2. **Docker-free policy data.** A deterministic sandbox handles navigation and edits, SWT answers execution actions, and SWR evaluates submit actions. The new task pool filters roughly 627K GitHub Archive records from 2010–2025 to 16,550 Python Issue-PR tasks across 3,763 repositories using bot, bug-keyword, text-length, comment, changed-file, churn, patch-length, and open-SWE-dataset deduplication rules. GLM-4.6/MinMax-M2 roll out in SWE-World. Invalid tools, timeouts, context/turn overflow, and no-submit episodes are removed; SWR then keeps only reward-1 episodes. The final SWE-World-only policy-SFT set contains 5.7K successful trajectories, so unlike world-model supervision it intentionally drops failures and near misses. (Paper §§5.1–5.2; Appendices C and F.)

3. **Policy optimization and selection.** Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct and Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 are SFT-trained for five epochs with 80K maximum context, batch 256, AdamW/cosine scheduling, and learning rate 5e-5 to 5e-6. RL initializes from those checkpoints and uses SWT-32B step feedback plus SWR-32B terminal reward under GRPO++: 32 problems per update, four rollouts per problem, policy temperature 1.0, 150-turn and 108K-context caps, 5,400-second trajectory timeout, and learning rate 1e-6. For TTS@8, the system samples eight candidate episodes, queries SWR three times per candidate, and selects the highest mean reward. Total RL steps/rollouts, seeds, hardware, compute, and tie handling are not disclosed. (Paper §§5.3–5.4; Appendix D.)

The repository exposes data-preparation, inference, SFT, RL, and TTS scripts plus small demos, but its training instructions note that internal platform packages were preinstalled and the public setup only approximates the original environment. No full immutable data manifests, exact split sizes, or complete overlap audit were verified.
