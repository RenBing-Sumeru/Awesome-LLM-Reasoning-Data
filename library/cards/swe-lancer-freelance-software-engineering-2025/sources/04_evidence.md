The benchmark’s strongest evidence is authentic transaction provenance and independent evaluation contracts. More than 1,400 jobs account for about USD 1 million in payouts, with task values ranging from USD 50 to USD 32,000. End-to-end tests for independent tasks are triple-verified by engineers, and managerial labels come from actual project-manager choices. Experiments with frontier models show that no model solves the majority of tasks and that dollar-denominated completion does not mirror short-code benchmark scores. This supports the claim that current models cannot replace most freelance engineering work, but Diamond is only a public subset and results depend on tools, time, and inference budgets.
