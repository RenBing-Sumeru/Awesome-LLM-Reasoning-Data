1. **Public-release boundary:** Client code, contracts, and privacy constraints prevent release of all 1,400-plus tasks, and Diamond may not match the full set’s domain or difficulty distribution. Researchers must state the split used.

2. **Payout is not pure difficulty:** Prices also reflect negotiation, geography, timing, and client budgets, so dollars cannot be treated directly as technical difficulty or productivity. Task count, type, and value should be reported together.

3. **Evaluation proxies:** Triple verification improves reliability, but complex requirements may remain untested. Agreement with the original manager also does not prove that a proposal is objectively optimal. High-value conclusions need human review.
