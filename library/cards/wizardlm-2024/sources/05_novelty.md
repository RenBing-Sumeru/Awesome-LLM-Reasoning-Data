Self-Instruct is the closest baseline: it expands seeds but does not explicitly steer difficulty. Evol-Instruct adds named depth and breadth operators, making the transformation from seed to training request an inspectable decision. Teacher generation and SFT are established techniques; the new object is the controlled evolution lineage at 250K-candidate scale. The method signals that instruction complexity can be designed before response generation, although it does not verify the resulting answers.
