Use Evol-Instruct when a seed instruction set is too easy or narrow for the desired SFT model. Supply versioned seeds, depth/breadth prompts, a pinned teacher, and filters; output instruction-response JSON with seed, operator, round, answer, and acceptance metadata. Success means human or task-specific checks confirm increased difficulty and a matched student improves on the target distribution. Do not use it alone for preference learning, multi-turn context, or domains where teacher answers require formal correctness.
