**Matched 13B comparison (Table 1).** WizardLM, Vicuna, and the expanded Alpaca baseline use LLaMA 13B and 70K instruction examples, but differ in construction: Evol-Instruct synthetic data, user-shared conversations, and Self-Instruct data respectively. Across MMLU, ARC, HellaSwag, TruthfulQA, HumanEval, GSM8K, AlpacaEval, MT-Bench, and WizardEval, WizardLM averages `58.96`, versus `54.60` for Vicuna and `43.44` for Alpaca. On the 218-instruction WizardEval surface it scores `89.1`, versus Vicuna's `86.9`. This supports the complete evolution-and-answer pipeline under a matched base/size, but not correctness of every generated item or the contribution of any single operator.
