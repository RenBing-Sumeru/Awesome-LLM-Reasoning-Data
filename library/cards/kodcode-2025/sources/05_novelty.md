Earlier synthetic code datasets often release instructions and model answers without executable tests, or reject a question after one failed solution. KodCode changes the auditable record to retain a question, solution, unit tests, branch coverage, up to ten fresh trials, pass-derived difficulty, and a separately verified R1 conversation. ACL Findings acceptance, external-test checks, public failure splits, and strong download/use signals are quality evidence. Synthetic instructions, unit-test generation, and reject sampling are not individually new; the direction signal is combining them into a difficulty-preserving, inspectable post-training dataset. Reuse checks should target shared solution/test errors, seed licenses, benchmark overlap, and the non-commercial data license.
