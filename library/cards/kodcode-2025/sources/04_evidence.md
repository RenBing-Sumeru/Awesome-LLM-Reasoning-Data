2. The verification and selection analyses make the mechanism testable. On externally written tests, 78/80 retained MBPP solutions and 189/190 retained LiveCodeBench-V5 solutions pass. Under matched 10K SFT sizes, the hard subset reaches 39.9 on BigCodeBench-Complete Hard versus 38.5 for random sampling, and removing style-converted data falls to 35.1. Co-generated tests are therefore useful but not infallible, and difficulty/style both affect results.
