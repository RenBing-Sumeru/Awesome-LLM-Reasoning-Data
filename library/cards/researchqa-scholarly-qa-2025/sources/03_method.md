1. **Select surveys:** Systematic survey articles across 75 fields are collected with section structure and references.

2. **Jointly derive questions and rubrics:** Each section yields research questions and required papers, explanations, comparisons, and limitations.

3. **Expert validation:** Thirty-one PhD annotators in eight fields assess query usefulness and rubric necessity.

4. **Build automatic judge:** System answers are compared against rubrics through pairwise judging; survey versions, citation parsing, and judges must be fixed.
