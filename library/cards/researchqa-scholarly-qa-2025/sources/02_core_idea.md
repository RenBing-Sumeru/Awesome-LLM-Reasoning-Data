ResearchQA contains about 21K scholarly queries and 160K query-specific rubric items across 75 fields. Questions and criteria are jointly mined from survey sections and require citing papers, explaining methods, comparing work, and discussing limitations. Thirty-one PhD annotators in eight fields find that 96% of queries support PhD information needs and 87% of rubric items merit substantive coverage. The data support scholarly QA, RAG, and citation judges.
