Closest prior work uses general preference pairs, terminal success, or step accuracy without representing the domain-specific states and failure modes of multimodal reward-model evaluation. This paper changes the record structure and feedback contract by centering Multimodal RewardBench and combining inputs, candidates, environmental or evidential state, and labels in one auditable example through constructing verifiable preference pairs from images, questions, and multi-model answers across perception, knowledge, reasoning, safety, VQA, and style. The contribution is task-specific data construction, capability decomposition, and practical evaluation rather than sample count alone.
