The paper evaluates its claim through controlled comparisons with reward models, LLM or LVLM judges, rule-based verifiers, or strong baselines. The representative result is that current multimodal reward models are uneven across capabilities, and text-only reward models transfer unreliably. Category-level analysis indicates that the gap reflects structural weaknesses rather than a few easy examples. Because results still depend on candidate generators, base models, environments, and judges, the evidence supports utility under the tested setting rather than universal deployment performance.
