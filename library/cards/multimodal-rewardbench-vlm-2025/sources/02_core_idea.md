The main artifact is Multimodal RewardBench, containing 5,211 image–prompt–preferred–rejected triplets across six feedback dimensions. Each record organizes a task input, visual or environmental state, candidate response or trajectory, evaluation evidence, and a preference, score, success, or critique label, together with task provenance and capability categories. Quality control relies on constructing verifiable preference pairs from images, questions, and multi-model answers across perception, knowledge, reasoning, safety, VQA, and style, making the release suitable for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than ordinary question answering.
