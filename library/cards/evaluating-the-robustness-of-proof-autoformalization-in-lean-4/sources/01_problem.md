Proof-autoformalization models are usually tested on polished, error-free informal proofs, so evaluations cannot determine whether they faithfully understand input. Models may produce inconsistent results after paraphrases or ignore local changes to values, symbols, or proof steps and revert to a memorized original statement.

This work constructs a robustness benchmark with global rewrites and local counterfactual perturbations, using Lean compilation and structured comparison to evaluate correctness stability and semantic faithfulness under changed wording.
