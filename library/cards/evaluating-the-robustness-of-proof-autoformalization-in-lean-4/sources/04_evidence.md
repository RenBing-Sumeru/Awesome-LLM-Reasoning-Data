The paper evaluates seven recent models. All are clearly sensitive to global rewrites, and most fail to faithfully reflect local perturbations: some reproduce the original formalization, while others silently repair counterfactual input. Lean compilation provides a reproducible correctness oracle, and local comparison directly checks whether edits enter the formal statement. The evidence shows current autoformalizers are not stable translators. The abstract reports neither a consolidated dataset size nor one best score, so inferred numbers should not be added.
