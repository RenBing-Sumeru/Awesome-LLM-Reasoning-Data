The benchmark derives two data types from informal proofs in miniF2F and MATH-500. Global perturbations change style, wording, or organization while preserving meaning. Local perturbations alter a value, symbol, or proof step and require the formalization to reflect that change, even when the resulting proof is counterfactual. Records include original and perturbed proofs, expected changes, model Lean outputs, compilation results, and consistency or faithfulness labels. The release supports robustness diagnosis rather than large-scale proof SFT.
