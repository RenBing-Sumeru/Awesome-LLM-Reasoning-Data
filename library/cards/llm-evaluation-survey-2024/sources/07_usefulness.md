Use this survey to turn a vague evaluation plan into a testable one. Instead of writing “evaluate reasoning,” write the target behavior, the scenario, the allowed resources, and the metric. Then choose at least one diagnostic slice that can reveal a known failure, such as evidence absence, longer contexts, adversarial wording, or changed tool access. Students can reuse this structure for a data card: each row should say what the item tests and why the chosen score is adequate.
