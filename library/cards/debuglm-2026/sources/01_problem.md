The official arXiv record identifies this work as arXiv:2603.17884v1, submitted on 18 March 2026. DebugLM addresses a provenance gap in heterogeneous, multi-stage post-training: after a model exhibits an unwanted behavior, a developer may observe the output but cannot directly identify which source dataset or training stage taught the behavior. The paper therefore treats debugging as source diagnosis and source-level quarantine, not as iterative program repair. (Paper §1; arXiv record)

The data unit is an instruction-response record paired with a known dataset identity. It is not a multi-step debugging episode: there are no action-observation turns, tool calls, execution traces, environment states, or persisted failure-and-retry sequence. The proposed output is a learned source tag attached to a response, and the proposed intervention is a standardized refusal conditioned on a selected source tag.

This scope is operationally useful but narrower than causal attribution. A tag records the source label used during construction and tests whether the trained model can reproduce that label. It does not independently prove that one particular training record caused one particular generation. The work belongs to **Data Construction & Open-Release Recipes** because provenance, mode, and quarantine controls are added while task records pass through sequential SFT and GRPO. The bilingual Card is `L4_chinese_review_ready`, while its release remains `partial`: the paper is sufficient for mechanism and evidence review, but no official code, transformed records, checkpoints, or immutable stage manifest were confirmed.
