Construction begins from five existing datasets. Stage 1 instruction-tunes Llama-3-8B and Qwen-3-8B on a mixture of TOFU, ChatDoctor, TruthfulQA, and Beavertails. Stage 2 then applies GRPO on WMDP. Dataset identity supplies the provenance label; the paper adds ordinary, diagnostic-tagged, and control-tagged targets around the existing instruction-response content. The ordinary-to-diagnostic mixture, control-tag sampling distribution, filtering rule, tracing-loss weight, optimizer settings, batch sizes, epochs, and Stage 2 reward implementation are unknown.

The evaluation verifier is explicit. Tracing Success Rate is exact-match accuracy against the known source tag on held-out sets. The remediation intervention matrix checks whether the model refuses inputs from a requested quarantine source while continuing to answer other sources. Free-form utility is judged by Llama-3-70B-Instruct; WMDP and standard academic benchmarks use exact match. These are response-level checks, not execution feedback from a stateful environment.

The main diagnostic call has a one-forward-pass endpoint: produce the answer and its tag. A separate QuoteSum experiment builds 1,254 cases by using GPT-4o-mini to derive isolated single-source QA pairs, then evaluates comprehensive questions that require two sources. Training still asks for one tag per sequence; evaluation draws ten outputs at temperature 1.0 and top-p 0.95 and declares success only if every relevant tag appears at least once. This is a sampled aggregation rule, not a stored multi-step episode.

The paper also tests finer labels by replacing dataset tags with 199 TOFU author tags and 14 Beavertails toxicity-subcategory tags. A semantic-perturbation evaluation uses Llama-3.1-70B to extract atomic facts and synthesize new QA records from 576 held-out TOFU development instances. These teacher-generated records belong to separate evaluation studies and should not be conflated with the main five-dataset training mixture.
