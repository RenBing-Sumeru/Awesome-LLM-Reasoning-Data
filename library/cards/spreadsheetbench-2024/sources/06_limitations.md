Correctness is only correctness relative to the official answer workbook, answer position, and evaluator implementation. An agent can pass a checked range while damaging unchecked cells, and the public evaluator details matter for numeric rounding, date handling, cached formula values, and formatting-sensitive cases.

The release should be treated as an evaluation benchmark, not a disclosed training dataset with a standard train/dev/test split. License and source-lineage review are important because tasks are derived from public spreadsheet forums and blogs. Public benchmark files may become contaminated training data. Results can also drift with spreadsheet engines, LibreOffice versus Excel recalculation behavior, OpenPyXL versions, execution feedback scaffolds, and product-specific manual evaluation protocols.
