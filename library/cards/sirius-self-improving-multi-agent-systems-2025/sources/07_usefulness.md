For the construction/open-release track, SiriuS is a reusable blueprint for outcome-selected role data, privileged critique, regeneration, downstream replay, and iterative role SFT across collaborative and competitive graphs.

A reproducible release should publish every episode and role record with source ID/revision, graph/prompt version, predecessor context, outcome, selected repair role, critic feedback, pre/post-rephrase messages, successor replay, reward, iteration, rejection reason, and fine-tune binding. Failed and unrepaired trajectories should remain auditable.

Until those artifacts exist, the repository supports recipe study and code inspection, not reuse of the claimed experience library. Reported gains do not certify intermediate reasoning, contamination absence, licensing, or reproducibility.
