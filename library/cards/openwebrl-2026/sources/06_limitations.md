- **Success-only SFT:** All 3,085 public turn rows come from 412 successful completed trajectories with reward 1. Failed demonstrations and recovery behavior are absent; treating rows independently can also obscure trajectory boundaries and duplicate history context.
- **Online rollout release gap:** The approximately 54K main-policy trajectories are not confirmed public. Identical-reward groups are discarded, judge/infrastructure failures can be loss-masked, and no immutable ledger connects generated episodes to rewards, masks, optimizer steps, or checkpoints. Full success/failure/abort retention is unknown.
- **Task membership drift:** The public RL JSONL contains 2,198 tasks, whereas default code uses a 2,102-task parquet. A paper-bound manifest is needed to know which tasks, filters, evaluator references, and validation rows produced the reported model.
- **Judge validity:** The VLM judge can be gamed and is trained against GPT-4.1 labels rather than independent human truth. Format gating, parsing, screenshots limited to the recent three, action/history truncation, API drift, and failure masking can create false positives or negatives. Judge-13K class balance and full indexed count are unknown.
- **Live-web replay:** Website content, availability, locale, region, time, cookies, personalization, login state, CAPTCHA/anti-bot behavior, redirects, and paid Browser-Use service behavior change observations and outcomes. Kubernetes isolation reduces cross-rollout contamination but does not provide deterministic site snapshots or replay capsules.
- **Decontamination and lineage:** Evaluation-overlap filtering and embedding deduplication are reported, but no immutable task-level overlap hashes, rejection ledger, exact benchmark snapshots, or base-model pretraining exposure audit is released. The full chain from 292K source tasks to 15,601 SFT candidates, 412 traces, 2,198/2,102 RL tasks, and online rollouts is incomplete.
- **Licensing:** Code, SFT Trajectories, and RL Tasks specify Apache-2.0. Judge-13K and individual policy/SFT/judge model APIs expose no confirmed license/model-card metadata. Website content, screenshots, upstream benchmarks, and third-party services may carry separate rights; they cannot inherit Apache-2.0 by assumption.
- **Privacy and safety:** SFT records contain screenshots, URLs, histories, and responses from live sites. Cards say private data is not intended, but no systematic PII/copyright scan, redaction, consent, data-subject, or takedown audit is documented. Risks include scraping, bot-detection bypass, inauthentic engagement, purchases/forms errors, and traffic imposed on third parties.
- **Systems and metrics:** Official scoring depends on a paid Stealth Browser service and recommended judges. Success excluding aborted infrastructure failures is a distinct metric. Exact code/container/browser/model/API pins, seeds, dependencies, reward constants, and a normalized 90-iteration/30-step-stage launch manifest are unavailable.
