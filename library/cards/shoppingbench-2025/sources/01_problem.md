ShoppingBench asks how shopping agents should be evaluated when the user's intent is grounded in realistic e-commerce constraints such as product attributes, long-tail knowledge, same-shop multi-product buying, coupons, and budget limits. The primary source is arXiv:2508.04266; the current arXiv API record is v4, updated 2026-06-18, with the title "ShoppingBench: A Real-World Intent-Grounded Shopping Benchmark for LLM-based Agents" and an "Accepted for oral presentation at AAAI 2026" note.

The decision boundary for this Card is benchmark, simulated shopping environment, and trajectory data. The data object is not a single product-search question. It includes user instructions, sampled real-world products, an interactive sandbox/search environment, tool calls, observations, final selected products or answers, and task-level metrics. The paper reports a sandbox with more than 2.5 million real-world products; the HTML paper gives 2,746,368 unique products.

Atlas relevance: ShoppingBench is a browser/tool-agent evaluation surface where correctness depends on stateful interaction and constraint satisfaction rather than text-only QA.
