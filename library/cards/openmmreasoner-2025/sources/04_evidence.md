The artifact evidence is concrete. The official collection links two 8B checkpoints, SFT-874K, RL-74K, the project page, and arXiv. The SFT Hub API exposes five train configurations totaling 874,357 rows: OpenVLThinker-sft-iter3 22,229, WeMath 6,944, llava_cot 553,806, m1_sft 91,953, and mmr1 199,425. Its schema verifies `id` and nested multimodal `messages`. The RL viewer exposes source-specific schemas around a common task interface and shows `reward_model` as a structure with `ground_truth` and `style`; checked first rows use `style: rule`.

The RL release boundary is also directly visible. Seven configurations have train splits: algopuzzle, mmk12, thinklite_vl_hard, tqa_train, virl39k, wemath_pro, and wemath_standard. Their API counts total 72,971 rows. The eighth configuration, `validation`, has a 1,724-row `val` split, includes `extra_info`, and lacks `avg_reward`; together these produce the collection's 74,695-row display total. No released field contains an online response, rollout ID, extracted answer, judge output, group reward, or policy-update reference.

The paper reports downstream evidence at three levels. Expanding verified SFT answer sampling from one to eight traces per source question raises the three-benchmark average from 50.5 to 55.2 (paper Table 3; supplement Table 2). Mixing both image- and text-math data then raises the same average from 55.2 to 56.3 (paper Table 5). For the selected cross-domain cold start, GSPO with 16 rollouts reaches a 54.3 average versus the 49.4 no-RL baseline and is reported as more stable than GRPO and DAPO (paper Table 7 and Figure 4). The final model is reported to improve 11.6% over Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct across nine multimodal benchmarks; on text-only evaluation, the baseline/cold-start/RL averages are 15.1/22.2/29.4 over AIME24, AIME25, and GPQA-Diamond (paper Table 8).

These results support the recipe as a training intervention under the authors' setup. They do not show that every SFT rationale is faithful, every `avg_reward` is reproducible, every ground truth is correct, or every upstream row is uncontaminated and license-compatible. The paper's qualitative traces and rollout word cloud are selected analyses, not validation of the released data at record level.
