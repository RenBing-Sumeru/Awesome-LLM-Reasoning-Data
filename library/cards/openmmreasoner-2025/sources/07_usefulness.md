For `data_construction_open_release_recipes`, OpenMMReasoner is most useful as a worked example of how one public project packages different stages of a reasoning-data lifecycle. It lets readers map source questions to teacher traces, retained SFT conversations, RL task specifications, online policy responses, terminal rewards, optimizer updates, and final checkpoints without collapsing those stages into one dataset.

The SFT release supports mixture analysis, formatting studies, alternative sampling or filtering experiments, and cold-start replication. The RL release supports prompt-pool analysis, reward-parser audits, alternative deduplication, curriculum design, and reconstruction of online GSPO. The checkpoints allow controlled comparisons between base, cold-start, and RL stages. Reusers should not treat `avg_reward` as a portable quality label until its generating policy, rollout count, sampling configuration, and seed are identified.

An auditable reconstruction should preserve three ledgers. The source ledger records upstream dataset and row ID, image provenance, license, split, and deduplication neighbors. The SFT decision ledger records teacher revision, rendered input, sampling index and seed, full candidate response, rule result, learned-judge request and result, retained/rejected status, and reason. The RL ledger records prompt revision, current-policy checkpoint, all 16 responses, extracted answers, accuracy and format components, judge fallback, group statistics, GSPO update, and any overlength or zero-variance filtering.

The recipe also enables controlled questions important to the track: whether answer diversity helps after matching compute, whether no-filter SFT remains robust under independent process checks, how image/text deduplication changes source balance, when learned-judge fallback disagrees with rules, and whether 8 versus 16 rollouts changes stability after matching sampled tokens. These studies require reconstructing the missing lineage rather than reading benchmark tables as data-quality certificates.
