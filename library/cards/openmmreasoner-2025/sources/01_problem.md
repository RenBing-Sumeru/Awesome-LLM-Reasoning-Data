Multimodal reasoning post-training has at least four different data objects: supervised conversations used for a cold start, prompts used to initiate reinforcement learning, responses sampled online by the current policy, and the rewards or decisions applied to those responses. Calling all four "the dataset" hides which object is actually released and which feedback contract created it. OpenMMReasoner addresses the operational gap by presenting one SFT-to-RL recipe around Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct and releasing data, checkpoints, and training code.

The official paper is arXiv:2511.16334v4, first submitted in 2025 and supported as CVPR 2026 by the official CVF supplemental record. The paper studies image-conditioned general reasoning, visual and textual mathematics, science, charts, puzzles, and transfer to text-only reasoning. It does not study video or audio, and its training experiments remain within the Qwen2.5-VL-Instruct family.

The public objects must be separated. `OpenMMReasoner-SFT-874K` is an approximately 874K-row conversation mixture with assistant reasoning traces. `OpenMMReasoner-RL-74K` is a prompt and reward-metadata package: its train rows carry prompts, images, ground truth, a reward style, token counts, and an average-reward field, but not the 16 current-policy responses generated during GSPO. The collection also includes a 1,724-row validation configuration, so the live release is about 73K training prompts plus validation rather than 74.7K training prompts.

For the atlas, the decision boundary is whether the release makes construction and feedback reusable enough to audit. OpenMMReasoner exposes more of the stack than a checkpoint-only report, but it does not expose complete SFT rejection lineage, online RL trajectories, judge transcripts, or an immutable paper-run manifest. Those absences are part of the data object, not minor documentation details.
