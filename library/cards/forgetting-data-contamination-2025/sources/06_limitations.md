The authors limit the claim to benchmark-question leakage during pre-training; it does not directly address privacy leakage, semantic paraphrases, fine-tuning, or deployment-time contamination. Treating the result as a general contamination clearance would therefore erase the paper’s central condition.

The controlled setting needs known insertions and filtered near duplicates, unlike most proprietary corpora. Before applying its estimates, audit exposure timing and repetition and verify that the assumed AdamW schedule and weight decay are actually known.
