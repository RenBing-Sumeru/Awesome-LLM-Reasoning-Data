The official evidence base is unusually concrete: the paper and OpenReview page report NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks Track poster status; the project and GitHub pages expose code, data loading, inference, evaluation, annotation, and submission instructions; the Hugging Face dataset exposes seven subject subsets, validation/test splits, fields, modalities, languages, and a CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 dataset license.

The paper reports 11,163 problems from 62 competitions, 13 answer types, 7 disciplines and 34 subfields, 7,904 problems with solutions, 7,571 total images, and 4,960 image-bearing problems. Main-result evidence includes GPT-4o reaching 39.97% overall as an LMM, with 28.67% math and 29.71% physics, while many open-source systems stay below 20% overall. Process-level evaluation is reported on a 96-problem sample, not the full dataset.

Row-level decisive evidence depends on the scoring path: rule-based answer matching, CS test-case pass/fail, GPT-4V model-based judgment for unsupported answer types, or sampled step scoring. The evidence boundary includes hidden test answers, model-based evaluator reliability, prompt and image-input choices, CS sampling budget, and possible contamination from public olympiad materials.
