OlympicArena asks how well LLMs and LMMs handle multi-discipline olympiad-level cognitive reasoning across science and computer science, including text-only and interleaved text-image problems. The primary sources are arXiv:2406.12753, the NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks Track OpenReview/proceedings page, the official project page, the GAIR-NLP GitHub repository, and the GAIR Hugging Face dataset.

The evaluation surface is a problem record from 62 olympic competitions across seven disciplines: mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, geography, astronomy, and computer science. A record can include problem text, a paper prompt, image URLs, answer, answer type, unit, answer ordering metadata, test cases for CS tasks, subject, language, and modality. The paper reports 11,163 bilingual problems, 13 answer types, 7,571 images, and 4,960 image-bearing problems.

The decision boundary is benchmark/evaluation surface, not a training recipe. It belongs in the atlas because it expands olympiad-style reasoning beyond math/physics into a broader multi-science and code-generation setting, while using explicit answer-level and sampled process-level feedback contracts.
