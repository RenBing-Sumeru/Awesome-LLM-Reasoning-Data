The text gridworlds are controlled abstractions: their proxy/hidden-reward divergence may not represent tools with production permissions, external state, or long-horizon human impact. The result also depends on the selected safety tasks and the particular direct-RL configuration.

This is an audit risk rather than a prevalence claim. Before generalizing, test whether the same divergence appears in a realistic-permission task whose hidden objective is independently specified and reviewed, and report both reward channels rather than only task success.
