A team deploying an LLM judge for a Likert-scale benchmark can retain the judge's rating-token logits, calibrate a conformal predictor on human-scored examples, and output a score interval and midpoint with each evaluation. The deliverable is an uncertainty-aware audit report, not a new training set. Check empirical coverage and interval width on a held-out labeled set; do not use the procedure when logits or task-matched calibration labels are unavailable.
