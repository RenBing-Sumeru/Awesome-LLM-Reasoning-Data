Prior uncertainty work often produces confidence scores or unordered prediction sets. This paper changes the output contract for ordinal rating judges: it uses logits plus split conformal calibration to return an interval, then makes the interval meaningful on a discrete rating scale. Conformal prediction and token-probability scoring are not new; the contribution is their systematic interval analysis and boundary adjustment for rating-based LLM judging. Inspect calibration size, task distribution, and access to logits before reuse.
