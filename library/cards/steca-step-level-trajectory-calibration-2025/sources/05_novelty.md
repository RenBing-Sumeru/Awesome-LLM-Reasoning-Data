Instead of comparing only complete successful and failed trajectories, STeCa intervenes at a suboptimal step and stores the repair process itself: original action, error diagnosis, reflection, corrected action, and recovery. The main contribution is therefore the construction of original exploration trajectories, step-level reward comparisons, suboptimal actions, reflection rationales, calibrated actions, and recovered successful trajectories as a learnable and auditable record, not a generic SFT, DPO, BCE, or RL objective.
