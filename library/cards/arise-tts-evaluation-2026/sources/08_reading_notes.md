When reading the paper, audit the transition table rather than only the final ARISE value. Check that token budgets strictly increase, that samples are paired across iterations, and that a correct-to-incorrect transition is not hidden by later recovery. Reproduce the metric with fixed model outputs before enabling adaptive sampling, then vary the sampling threshold to quantify its effect on confidence and cost. For a real service, add wall-clock latency, verifier calls, and tool executions beside token counts; otherwise a favorable ARISE score may miss the actual bottleneck.
