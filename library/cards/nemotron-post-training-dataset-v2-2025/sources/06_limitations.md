- **Access and versioning:** raw Parquet files are contact-gated. A reproducible snapshot needs exact repository revision, file names, row counts, hashes, and configuration manifests; these were not verifiable without access.
- **Accounting mismatch:** the nine release splits total 6,341,414 rows, whereas the report's Table 7 and 80B-token statement describe a larger SFT-stage accounting surface. No record-level reconciliation is published.
- **Provenance and contamination:** rows lack universal upstream source IDs/revisions and transformation histories. Exact deduplication and benchmark-overlap procedures, matches, removals, and false-negative analysis are unknown.
- **Selection observability:** the visible schema has no verifier identity/version, score, threshold, failure code, or rejected candidate. Release-level checks are not per-item correctness certificates.
- **Generator ambiguity:** generator totals overlap because multiple models may contribute to one record. Without candidate and transformation logs, `generator` cannot recover a unique teacher or selection path.
- **Multilingual asymmetry:** target-language prompts and responses retain English reasoning traces. This can introduce translation artifacts, code-switching, hidden English-teacher style, and unequal supervision quality across languages; this risk is a curator inference, not a measured failure rate.
- **Rights chain:** repository-level CC-BY-4.0 coexists with per-row ODC-BY or CC-BY-SA labels and Qwen/DeepSeek model terms. A label alone does not supply upstream URLs, revisions, required attribution, or a compatibility decision for downstream distribution.
- **RL over-reading:** the report describes IFEval RL, WorkBench DPO, and GRPO/RLHF, but the release lacks stage membership, rollout groups, rewards, log probabilities, preference pairs, and optimizer state. Treating visible rows as replayable RL data would be unsupported.
- **Verifier failure risk:** rule checks may false-accept superficially compliant but wrong outputs; model judges and guards may carry bias or false rejections; database-state success may miss undesirable action paths. No calibration or retained-failure audit is released.
- **Evaluation scope:** benchmark improvements measure checkpoint behavior under the paper's settings. They do not establish data correctness, safety, provenance, contamination control, license compatibility, or independent reproducibility.
