The evidence supports the following reconstructable pipeline, with an explicit boundary between the released dataset and the broader model-training report.

1. **Inputs.** Extract prompts from public/open corpora or synthesize new prompts. The dataset card says quality and complexity are required, but does not give a universal per-row source ID, source revision, or prompt-generation specification.
2. **Prompt filtering.** Remove inconsistent prompts, prompts whose answers are easy to guess, and prompts with invalid syntax. Exact filter models, thresholds, ordering, and rejection counts are unknown.
3. **Response generation.** Generate synthetic responses with DeepSeek-R1-0528, Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct-AWQ, Qwen3-30B-A3B, or Qwen3-235B-A22B. The official generator totals overlap because multiple models may contribute to one record; decoding temperature, seeds, retry policy, and candidate budget are unknown.
4. **Multilingual construction.** Translate existing English post-training data line by line, skip non-translatable material such as code, enforce a bracketed extraction format, and reject off-target outputs with language identification. Prompts and responses target Japanese, German, Italian, Spanish, or French, while reasoning traces remain English (report Section 3.1, PDF p.14).
5. **Other report-level checks.** Tool-use simulations assign Qwen3-235B-A22B the User-Agent, Assistant-Agent, and API-Server-Agent roles; parameter checks and a lightweight rule layer retain only successful trajectories. Safety data use guard filtering. These mechanisms are described in the report but are not attached as row-level fields in this release.
6. **Outputs.** Write Parquet rows with `uuid`, `license`, `generator`, `version`, `category`, `reasoning`, and `messages`, organized into nine splits under an SFT configuration.
7. **Training use.** The public object is suitable for SFT. The report separately describes three SFT stages: a full mixture packed to about 128k tokens plus roughly 10% reasoning-stripped examples; a non-concatenated tool-calling stage; and a long-context/truncated-reasoning stage. It then describes IFEval RL, on-policy WorkBench DPO pairs, and HelpSteer3 GRPO/RLHF (report Section 3.2, PDF p.15). The release provides no row-to-stage or row-to-run crosswalk.

Reproduction must therefore pin dataset revision, file hashes, all subset manifests, source and generator terms, and the exact report/model version. It must also supply the undisclosed prompt revisions, decoding settings, filter implementations, rejected candidates, and training-stage mixture; the official page alone cannot replay those decisions.
