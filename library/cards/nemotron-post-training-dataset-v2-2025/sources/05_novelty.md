Compared with a mixture described only by aggregate domain totals, this release makes a useful operational change: one versioned artifact spans technical reasoning, chat, and five target languages while retaining `uuid`, `license`, `generator`, `version`, `category`, `reasoning`, and `messages`. Those fields permit category filtering, reasoning-mode analysis, generator stratification, multilingual audits, and initial rights triage without parsing a model report.

Relative to Nemotron-Post-Training-Dataset-v1, the linked report and v2 card emphasize the five-language extension and English-only reasoning traces. That language-asymmetric object is a concrete design choice that reusers can test rather than an undocumented side effect.

The release does not introduce SFT, synthetic response generation, translation, quality filtering, syntax checking, tool simulation, DPO, GRPO, or reward modeling. It also does not expose a complete preference or RL record: rollout groups, paired choices, rewards, log probabilities, rejected alternatives, environment states, optimizer state, and stage-to-row lineage are absent.

Its direction signal for reasoning-data research is therefore not a claim of a new algorithm. It makes visible the gap between a richly described report-level pipeline and a much thinner reusable row contract. Before reuse, researchers must verify whether the published rows correspond to the report's stage counts, whether `generator` is complete and interpretable, whether language-asymmetric traces transfer safely, and whether source and license metadata are sufficient for the intended use.
