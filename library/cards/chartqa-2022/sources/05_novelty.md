The prior-work baseline is document, chart, multimodal QA, and visual evaluation benchmarks. The change introduced by ChartQA: A Benchmark for Question Answering about Charts with Visual and Logical Reasoning is the particular task object and feedback contract: chart image, natural-language question, answer string or number, source split, chart table, and optional visual annotations in the full release. scored by answer-level match or normalized comparison to the gold text/numeric answer under the evaluation script. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.10244; venue: https://aclanthology.org/2022.findings-acl.177/; acl: https://aclanthology.org/2022.findings-acl.177/; code: https://github.com/vis-nlp/ChartQA; data: https://github.com/vis-nlp/ChartQA; huggingface: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ahmed-masry/ChartQA; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.10244), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
