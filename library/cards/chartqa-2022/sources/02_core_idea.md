The contribution is to provide 9.6K human-written questions and 23.1K machine-generated questions reported by the paper; repository documents chart images, tables, splits, and annotations. for chart-understanding, visual-question-answering, logical-reasoning under a reusable evaluation surface. The core mechanism is the pairing of task inputs and model outputs with the following feedback contract: answer-level match or normalized comparison to the gold text/numeric answer under the evaluation script.

The data object or environment is static chart question-answering benchmark over chart images and underlying tables. The closest comparisons are document, chart, multimodal QA, and visual evaluation benchmarks. The direction label is benchmarks_evaluation_surfaces with a programmatic feedback contract. The main thing to preserve is not only the headline score, but the task object, evaluator, split/version, and artifact lineage.
