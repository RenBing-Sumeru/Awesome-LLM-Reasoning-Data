The collection pool covers GSM8K, MATH, AIME2024, BBH, GaokaoBench, HLE, KorBench, GPQA, SimpleQA, ChineseSimpleQA, MMLU-Pro, ARC, OlympiadBench, TheoremQA, NuminaMath, and DROP. Fifty-three commercial, open, and reasoning model variants generate responses through dataset-specific OpenCompass configurations. The paper reports more than 1,325,293 source triples; exact prompts, decoding parameters, seeds, API revisions, and collection dates are not consolidated for all generators.

Construction has three stages. Qwen2.5-7B/14B/32B-Instruct judge without chain-of-thought, with Math-Verify added for MATH, GSM8K, and AIME. First-stage consensus is treated as easy and removed from the benchmark. DeepSeek-V3 applies multiple chain-of-thought prompts to disputes; its consensus cases enter the base training pool, while selected datasets receive an additional domain-specific prompt. Remaining disputes are string-deduplicated, screened by DeepSeek-V3 for open-ended, proof-based, or incomplete-reference cases, and human-annotated. Similarity filtering reduces redundant Incorrect cases. The paper states that test and training sets do not overlap.

VerifierBench has 2,817 rows: 1,092 A/Correct, 1,526 B/Incorrect, and 199 C/Invalid. Its four domains contain 1,151 general-reasoning, 900 mathematics, 387 knowledge, and 379 science rows; seven answer types range from multiple choice and short text to formulas, multi-subproblems, sequences, and Boolean answers. The public schema contains seven fields only. Annotation rationales, model votes, filter decisions, rejected rows, and meta-error assignments discussed by the paper are not released in the row schema.

The reported 96,832-example training mixture comprises 54,420 base-consensus rows, 24,294 error-driven adversarial rows, and 18,118 complex-formula rows. DeepSeek-V3 generates one to three formula variants, checked with a symbolic algebra engine and spot-checked by experts. Error rationales from 5,000 human-reviewed cases are clustered into high-impact categories and instantiated through 34 Meta-Judge templates. Generalization augmentation rewrites prompts and truncates, retags, shuffles, or mixes long responses while preserving the judgment.

CompassVerifier-3B/7B/32B are Qwen2.5-series models fully fine-tuned for one epoch with learning rate 2e-5, batch size 32, maximum sequence length 32,768, epoch-decay gamma 0.85, and eight 80 GB A100 GPUs. Evaluation uses temperature 0; synthesis uses temperature 1. A separate reward experiment trains Qwen3-4B-Base for two GRPO epochs on Open-S1 after excluding integer-answer problems. It uses binary reward 1/0, group size 8, no KL penalty, batch 256, learning rate 1e-6, 4,096-token prompts, 12,288-token responses, and eight 80 GB A800 GPUs.
