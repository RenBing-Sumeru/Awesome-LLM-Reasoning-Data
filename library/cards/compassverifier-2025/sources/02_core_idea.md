The central idea is to make answer verification a trainable, benchmarked component rather than a per-dataset regex or prompt. First-stage Qwen2.5-Instruct judges and Math-Verify remove easy consensus cases. DeepSeek-V3 then judges disputed cases with multiple chain-of-thought prompts and selected dataset-specific prompts. Remaining disagreements are labeled by domain experts, balanced, and assigned to the test set; moderate consensus cases supply base training data.

CompassVerifier is strengthened with error-driven adversarial synthesis, complex-formula equivalence variants, and prompt/long-context perturbations. At inference it compares a question, reference answer, and candidate response and emits A, B, or C. This ternary contract distinguishes a wrong answer from an invalid question/reference/response, although the paper's binary evaluation and GRPO reward merge B and C into reward 0.
