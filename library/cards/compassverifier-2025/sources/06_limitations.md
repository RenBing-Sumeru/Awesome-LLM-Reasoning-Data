The gold contract is policy-laden. Whether equivalent formulas, formatting deviations, rounding, partial multi-answer coverage, or late self-correction count as correct depends on the question and annotation policy. The paper notes divergent penalty thresholds, but does not disclose annotator counts, assignment, agreement, or an immutable adjudication log. Consensus among related model judges can also preserve correlated errors before human review.

The benchmark is intentionally selective. Proof-based, open-ended, incomplete-reference, and ambiguous numerical-threshold problems are filtered out. String deduplication mainly removes Invalid responses, and similarity filtering reduces Incorrect responses, so the final 1,092/1,526/199 label distribution is constructed rather than natural. Binary scoring and RL collapse Incorrect and Invalid, losing the diagnostic distinction the benchmark introduces and potentially rewarding responses against flawed questions or references incorrectly.

The construction description has unresolved version/count boundaries. The paper mentions over 30 meta error patterns in one analysis, over 40 high-impact categories in another, and 34 synthesis templates, without releasing their row-level mapping. Appendix A.8 states 224,294 error-driven examples in prose, but Table 13 states 24,294 and only the latter sums with 54,420 and 18,118 to the reported total of 96,832. The card treats 224,294 as an unresolved likely typo, not as a second corpus size.

Release completeness is partial. Hugging Face exposes the 2,817-row test split and three checkpoints, but not the 96,832 training examples, votes, rationales, filter decisions, rejected records, synthesis prompts, or item-level lineage. At checked commit `2d7cba6df0b21f9c6121786ac1e5770c68473598`, the GitHub repository contains inference prompts and an evaluation notebook but no construction/training/GRPO code and no license file, despite an Apache 2.0 badge. Dataset and model pages declare licenses, yet inherited rights across 16 upstream benchmarks and generated outputs are not audited. No semantic decontamination, external overlap analysis, confidence intervals, or independent reproduction is reported.
