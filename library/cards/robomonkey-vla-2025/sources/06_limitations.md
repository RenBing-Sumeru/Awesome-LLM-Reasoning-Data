RMSE to one recorded expert action is an imitation proxy. Multiple actions may be valid, and a numerically close action may still collide, violate constraints, or fail a long-horizon task. The verifier can inherit bias from the base policy, demonstration distribution, image encoder, action discretization, and correlated synthetic labels. Test-time sampling may also propose unsafe actions before filtering, while the highest verifier score has no formal safety guarantee.

The released preference dataset lacks a dataset card; exact fields, splits, cluster settings, sampling temperatures, rejection policy, source identifiers, seeds, record-level licenses, and decontamination are unknown from the endpoint. The model card is empty. Repository/model licenses do not automatically establish redistribution rights for Bridge V2 derivatives. Benchmark gains and synthetic-data scaling are not record-level quality audits.
