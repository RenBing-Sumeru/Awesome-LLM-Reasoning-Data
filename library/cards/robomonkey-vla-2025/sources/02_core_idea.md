The training pipeline samples actions from a reference VLA at states drawn from expert demonstrations, clusters them into representatives, and labels pairwise preferences by which action has lower RMSE to the recorded expert action. A LLaVA-7B-based verifier learns these comparisons with a margin-augmented Bradley–Terry objective. At deployment, a few VLA samples define Gaussian proposal distributions for translation and rotation, majority voting fixes the gripper bit, and the verifier selects one augmented action for execution.
