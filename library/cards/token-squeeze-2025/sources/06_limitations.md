**Feedback limits.** merged_verify judges only an extracted final answer. It can reject equivalent formatting, accept an answer with invalid reasoning, or be gamed by formatting; its normalization and error rates are unavailable. The KL gate uses one sampled continuation truncated at 512 future tokens (Appendix B). Passing it does not prove that a rewrite preserves all later reasoning, facts, or faithfulness. These are curator inferences from the stated contract.

**Selection/objective limits.** p is a checkpoint- and sampling-policy-dependent difficulty proxy, not an external label. Pairs entangle correctness, length, and candidate availability. The paper reports that longer correct traces used as negatives reduce accuracy, and extreme alpha values degrade the trade-off, demonstrating sensitivity to pair construction (paper §4.3.1). Authors say epsilon was selected heuristically: low restricts compression, high allows semantic drift (paper §5).

**Release/audit limits.** Code plus one input JSONL make the recipe partially inspectable, but completions, extraction outputs, pairs, rewrites, KL values, final corpus, hashes, decisions, mappings, splits, and license remain unavailable. No construction decontamination or evaluation-overlap audit was verified. The LiveCodeBench date restriction is not evidence that training data are clean.

**Generality/reproducibility limits.** Main evidence is two DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen scales, three math benchmarks, and one date-bounded code benchmark. Results average 16 runs, but seeds, full generation/rewrite compute, and independent reproduction are unavailable. The authors call it offline-only, so it cannot update policy from new feedback under distribution shift (paper §5).
