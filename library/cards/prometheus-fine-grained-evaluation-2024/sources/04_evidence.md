On human evaluation with 45 customized rubrics, Prometheus reaches a Pearson correlation of 0.897 with humans, above GPT-4 at 0.882 and far above the OpenAI chat baseline at 0.392. The authors also compare it with GPT-4 across 1,222 rubrics in four benchmarks and show strong results on preference tasks such as HHH Alignment and MT-Bench Human Judgment relative to open reward models. The evidence supports structured rubrics and references, although GPT-4-generated labels make high agreement with the teacher partly expected.
