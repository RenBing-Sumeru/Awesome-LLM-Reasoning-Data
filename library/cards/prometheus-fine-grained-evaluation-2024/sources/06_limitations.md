1. **Teacher bias:** The 100K feedback records are generated by GPT-4, inheriting its verbosity, cultural preferences, and factual blind spots.
2. **Reference dependence:** Prometheus is strongest with a reference answer, which many open-ended tasks lack.
3. **Score calibration:** High correlation does not make absolute 1–5 scores comparable across rubrics, whose boundaries may drift.
4. **Synthetic distribution:** Response quality levels and instruction distributions are controlled by generation and differ from real user traffic.
