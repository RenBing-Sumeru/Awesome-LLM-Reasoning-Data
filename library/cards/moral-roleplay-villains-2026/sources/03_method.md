1. **Collect characters:** Diverse fictional characters are gathered from public resources, deduplicated, and represented with background and persona descriptions sufficient for role-play.
2. **Assign moral levels:** A four-level moral scale is defined from character goals, behavior, and core traits, with 200 characters sampled per level to correct the natural scarcity of villains.
3. **Construct prompts:** Actor-framed zero-shot prompts ask models to inhabit the character in a narrative or response without explicitly inducing real-world harm.
4. **Score performance:** Character fidelity is assessed through knowledge, linguistic style, motivation, and moral traits, with detailed analysis of failures on specific personality descriptors.
5. **Compare models:** LLMs with different general capabilities and safety-alignment profiles are tested for a monotonic relationship between lower character morality and poorer fidelity.
