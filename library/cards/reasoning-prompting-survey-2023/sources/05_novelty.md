This survey does not claim a new prompting algorithm, a universal evaluation, or a single explanation of reasoning. Its distinctive value is to make a rapidly growing literature readable through a common comparison-oriented account, while also collecting systematic resources and highlighting possible explanations and future work. Earlier primary papers usually establish the individual prompting results; this paper makes their assumptions and contrasts easier to inspect together. Its novelty is therefore organizational and interpretive. That helps separate a memorable method name from the task, comparison, and evidence behind it, and it helps a reader notice when two similarly named methods are evaluated under materially different conditions.
