Complex problems often require more than a direct answer, yet prompting studies use different tasks, prompt formats, model families, and evaluation choices. Those differences make it difficult to tell whether two papers are making the same claim when both say that a prompt improves reasoning. This ACL 2023 survey collects cutting-edge work on reasoning with language-model prompting so a reader can see what was compared, what task each comparison serves, and why the question was studied. The practical problem is not merely finding a named prompting method; it is judging the evidence attached to that method. The paper is a survey and resource overview, not a new model, a new prompting algorithm, or a dataset release.
