Because it summarizes many studies, the survey cannot make their task settings identical or turn their results into one common leaderboard. A reported improvement may depend on a particular prompt, model, set of demonstrations, or task formulation, and the survey itself does not supply one shared experimental protocol. It also cannot replace the detailed methods and evaluation sections of every work it cites. Use it to identify relevant work and comparison questions, then inspect primary sources for exact inputs, output requirements, model versions, and measurements.
