The source object is the 1,000-example s1K-1.1 mathematics set. The official loader reads each question, its long `deepseek_thinking_trajectory`, and its `deepseek_attempt` solution, then formats them as a user–assistant conversation with separate thought and solution fields. The upstream trace generator, source mixture, and audit status remain properties of s1K-1.1 rather than newly established facts in A*-Thought.

For step-level scoring, the long thought is segmented at double newlines. A compact GPT-2 scorer estimates a bidirectional importance score from each step’s relationship to the question and to the known solution; `alpha` balances the two directions. Steps are sorted by this score. A search node contains a span centered on a selected step together with its immediate neighbors, rather than an isolated sentence, to reduce discontinuities caused by compression.

A* search starts from the highest-ranked span and expands candidate paths according to a cost that combines current-path quality with the estimated future cost of reaching the fixed solution. The latter is expressed through the conditional self-information of the solution given the question and current path. Once the search reaches the minimum depth, s1.1-32B checks whether the partial path can lead to the target solution. A passing path terminates the search; if no path passes before the maximum depth, the current path is returned at that limit. The official script defaults to `alpha=0.5`, `beta=0.1`, expansion size 2, minimum depth 5, and maximum depth 20.

Construction preserves the original solution and replaces only the thought with the selected spans. Released rows contain `system`, two-message `conversations`, compression `rate`, and `search_steps`. The compact traces are used for three-epoch full SFT through LLaMA-Factory on QwQ-32B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B, and s1.1-32B. The paper evaluates the trained models under 512-, 1024-, 2048-, and 4096-token output budgets; these budgets describe evaluation, not separate dataset splits.
