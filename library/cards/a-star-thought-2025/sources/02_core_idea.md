A*-Thought combines step-level ranking with path-level search. A bidirectional importance score ranks each thought step by its relationship to both the question and the known solution. An A* procedure then assembles high-ranked steps and their local neighbors into candidate paths, using s1.1-32B to judge current-path quality and a solution-conditioned future-cost estimate to guide expansion. The accepted compressed thought is paired with the original solution to form an SFT example; the public AStar-Thought-1k release contains the resulting compact conversations and search records.
