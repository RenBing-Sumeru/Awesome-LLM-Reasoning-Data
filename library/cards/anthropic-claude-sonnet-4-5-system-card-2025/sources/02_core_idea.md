The system card names a proprietary source mixture at category level: public internet information through July 2025, non-public third-party data, data-labeling services and paid contractors, opted-in Claude-user data, and Anthropic internally generated data. It reports cleaning and filtering including deduplication and classification, plus substantial post-training and fine-tuning with RLHF and reinforcement learning from AI feedback. No source manifest, record count, rights map, post-training example, preference record, reward model, or optimizer artifact is released.

The core Track 12 contribution is an audit boundary across two separate evaluation practices. The reward-hacking material concerns updated coding stress tests, hidden fuzzed tests, environments, reward structures, and monitoring; it is not the RLHF/RLAIF reward contract or a real-world hacking-rate estimate. The white-box material concerns pre-release model-diff, activation-steering, and evaluation-awareness experiments; it is not a release of model internals or evidence of an accessible post-training dataset. The report itself warns that evaluation awareness can bias behavioral evaluations and systematically underrate deployment risk.
