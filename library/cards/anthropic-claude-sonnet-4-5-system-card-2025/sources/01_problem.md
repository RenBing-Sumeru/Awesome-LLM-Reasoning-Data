Frontier system cards can describe post-training, safety updates, reward-hacking tests, and interpretability work without releasing the data, feedback, model-internal, or environment artifacts needed to audit those claims. The distinction is essential for reasoning and agentic systems: a stress-test result or a white-box audit may inform a safety assessment, but neither establishes a disclosed training recipe or a reproducible data and reward contract.

Claude Sonnet 4.5 is valuable to Track 12 because Anthropic's official card makes this distinction unusually visible. It reports broad source categories, RLHF and reinforcement learning from AI feedback, a redesigned harmlessness pipeline, reward-hacking stress tests and monitoring, and a pre-release white-box evaluation-awareness investigation. Yet it withholds the associated records, reward functions, task artifacts, prompts, model snapshots, vectors, code, and full audit outputs. This Card preserves those separate disclosure boundaries.
