For **Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data**, URSA is a useful schema for separating five signals that are often conflated: image evidence, an answer outcome, a continuation-based process label, a learned PRM score, and an RL reward. A reconstruction should persist image bytes or immutable image references; question and answer; generator and judge revisions; every continuation; BEL/MIE route; step labels; PRM sequence; detected drop; outcome reward; and selected/rejected rollout identity.

The released MMathCoT/DualMath tables can support controlled SFT or PRM-format studies after verifying image access and license compatibility. They should not be treated as directly reusable, independently validated multimodal process truth. The repository and model releases support inference and scoring studies, while a faithful construction or RL replication is blocked pending missing code, records, and manifest.

Practical experiments include: compare BEL-only, MIE-only, and union PRMs; test score aggregation at matched Best-of-N budgets; measure recovery-after-error labels; and ablate gamma/rho with retained complete rollout logs. Do not replace these audits with benchmark performance.
