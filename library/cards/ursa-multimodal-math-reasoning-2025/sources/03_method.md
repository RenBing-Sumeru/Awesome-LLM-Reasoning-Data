**Stage I — CoT inputs.** URSA-Alignment-860K filters MultiMath, MAVIS, and Geo170K. MMathCoT starts from 1.43M examples from MathV360K, MultiMath, MAVIS, Geo170K, and VarsityTutors. Gemini-1.5-Flash-002 expands answer-only examples, rewrites analysis-style solutions, or unifies existing CoT. Outputs are rejected when they alter, question, or force the supplied answer with new assumptions. The checked release has 1,019,059 train rows with `image_url`, `instruction`, and `output`.

**Stage II — labels.** URSA-8B produces about 553K incorrect solutions. BEL computes a prefix's Monte Carlo value as correct continuations divided by total continuations; around 180K correct solutions with all-positive steps are added to form a 773K logical set. MIE generates about 302K visual-misinterpretation records and marks every post-insertion step negative. Their union is approximately 1.1M; the checked DualMath train split has 1,100,779 rows and encodes labels in `output`, not a separate verified route or label column.

**Stage III — RL.** URSA uses SAM-B plus SigLIP-L vision encoders, Qwen2.5-Math-Instruct, and a two-layer MLP. It trains URSA-8B-RM from URSA-8B. PS-GRPO uses 15K MMathCoT prompts, eight rollouts per prompt, 2 epochs, learning rate 2e-6, temperature 1.0, prompt/output maxima 6048/3072, batch 512, and KL 0.003. The repository supplies inference and PRM scoring, but not verified construction/training scripts, seeds, terminal checks, or immutable paper-run configuration.
