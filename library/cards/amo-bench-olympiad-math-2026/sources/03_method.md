1. **Author original problems:** Mathematics experts design new problems rather than reproducing historical contests and ensure concise unique answers.

2. **Cross-validate difficulty:** Independent experts solve and confirm at least IMO difficulty, revising ambiguity, flaws, or easy cases.

3. **Check contamination:** Corpus search, semantic similarity, and LLM review screen for substantial overlap with public problems.

4. **Grade and maintain:** Parsers or judges grade final answers and repository revisions are tracked; problem version, sampling budget, and grader must be fixed.
