Open-ended multimodal responses are difficult to evaluate with exact matching, leading researchers to use MLLMs as judges that inspect images, questions, and candidate answers. However, it is unclear whether visual judges truly ground decisions in images, remain consistent across scoring, pairwise comparison, and batch ranking, or resist position, verbosity, and hallucination biases. The paper therefore studies how well MLLMs align with human preferences under different judging protocols and identifies their characteristic failures.
