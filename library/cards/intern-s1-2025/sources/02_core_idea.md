The report couples two construction systems. For pretraining, page-level PDF parsing routes most pages through MinerU and sends equation- or symbol-heavy pages to higher-cost VLM parsers; a domain-centric web pipeline uses LLM agents to discard, rewrite, or retain groups of pages; and scientific recall uses strong-LLM silver labels to train cheaper fastText or 1.5B classifiers. This produces a science-heavy CPT mixture rather than a named reusable dataset.

For post-training, the authors call best-of-N instruction tuning "offline reinforcement learning" because every retained response has already passed criteria such as accuracy, fluency, or safety. Online RL then uses Mixture-of-Rewards to map different task feedback into one scalar: verifiable tasks can use CompassVerifier, rules, or environmental feedback, while open-ended dialogue uses POLAR-7B. The resulting object is a prompt-response or multimodal trajectory with scalar reward, sometimes accompanied by binary correctness.

The classification is therefore mixed-verification SFT and RLVR inside a frontier pipeline. The release supplies model weights and inference tooling, not the training corpus, complete Internbootcamp task collection, or a replayable reward layer.
