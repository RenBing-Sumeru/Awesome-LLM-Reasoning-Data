The official [arXiv v2 paper](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2508.15763v2) supports the disclosed CPT volumes, PDF/web construction pipelines, instruction filtering and labeling, Internbootcamp task generation, Mixture-of-Rewards routing, 8-rollout filtering, and optimizer settings. It also reports ablations or training curves for domain recall, hybrid RL filtering, and entropy control, plus evaluations across general and scientific benchmarks. Those experiments demonstrate behavior under the authors' protocols; they do not establish that the underlying records are accurate, uncontaminated, legally reusable, or released.

The official [Intern-S1 repository](https://github.com/InternLM/Intern-S1) and [Hugging Face model card](https://huggingface.co/internlm/Intern-S1) verify release of Intern-S1 weights, configuration and inference examples under Apache-2.0. They also identify the Qwen3 235B MoE and InternViT-6B starting components. The repository says training infrastructure will be released through XTuner, but the checked release does not expose the 5T-token corpus, offline instruction records, Internbootcamp's full task/sample inventory, online trajectories, Mixture-of-Rewards implementation, or complete verifier mapping.

The strongest supported conclusion is therefore asymmetric: the model artifact is reusable for inference, while the data and feedback pipeline is only documented at recipe level. Benchmark performance cannot close that audit gap.
