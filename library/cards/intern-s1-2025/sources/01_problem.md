Intern-S1 addresses a specific scaling problem: scientific reasoning must cover low-resource modalities and specialized domains without building a separate expert pipeline for every task. For this atlas, the relevant question is not whether the resulting model leads a benchmark table. It is whether the report exposes the training records, construction decisions, and feedback contracts well enough to audit how scientific capability was produced.

The report spans three data-bearing layers. Continual pretraining uses 5T text tokens, including more than 2.5T scientific tokens, plus a later image-text stage of about 250B tokens. The offline post-training stage selects best-of-N responses from curated text and multimodal instructions. The online stage uses more than 1,000 Internbootcamp task types and heterogeneous rewards. These layers are described in meaningful detail, but their records, complete source manifests, and reward implementations are not released.
