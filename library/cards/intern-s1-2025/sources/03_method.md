Scientific CPT draws from archived and web PDFs, open pretraining corpora, Common Crawl, InternVL3 multimodal data, and scientific multimodal sources. PDF pages undergo hybrid OCR/VLM parsing, parser-specific cleanup, garble detection, and page-level graph deduplication. The report says about 20% of archived-library tokens are removed; web PDFs retain about 50% after additional education-level scoring. Domain-level LLM agents inspect sampled pages from a URL domain and choose discard, rewrite, or retain actions. A separate taxonomy covers mathematics, physics, chemistry, life science, earth science, and materials science; strong-LLM labels train lightweight classifiers, and manual evaluation reports target-domain purity rising from about 2% to 50%.

Offline instruction curation filters repetitive, truncated, or hallucinated records, labels category and difficulty, stratifies sampling, and reconstructs or synthesizes underrepresented areas. Ground-truth items receive difficulty estimates from small-model rollout pass rates. Vision-language instruction data begins from InternVL3 and extends SOPHIA with rejection sampling, deduplication, length/format constraints, self-consistency, and programmatic verification when available. Best-of-N produces each retained response, but N, generator identities, sampling settings, thresholds, record counts, and final mixture ratios are unknown.

Online RL combines several sources. Internbootcamp covers more than 1,000 procedurally generated task types; the report says its generator makes more than 100,000 candidates per task and heuristic downsampling leaves more than 20,000 samples for mixed RL. Instruction-following prompts are selected at passrate@64 in [0.2, 0.8]. Math sources include OREAL-RL-Prompts, DAPO-Math-17k, Skywork-OR1-RL-Data, and internal problems. Multimodal sources include MMPR, MMK12, and private collections; some text questions are rendered as images and some multiple-choice questions are converted to fill-in-the-blank form. Open-ended prompts come mainly from UltraFeedback, HH-RLHF, and anonymized user queries, with reference trajectories from randomly selected unnamed SOTA LLMs.

Mixture-of-Rewards routes ground-truth tasks through CompassVerifier, rules, or environment feedback and routes open-ended dialogue through POLAR-7B. Hybrid filtering uses 8 rollouts: it removes overly easy/noisy items offline, all-correct or all-wrong groups online, and garbled or infinitely repetitive outputs. Training adapts OREAL without its token-level reward model, adds KL-Cov entropy control, generates 8 responses per prompt, uses batch size 4096 for 600 steps with AdamW at 5e-7, freezes the ViT and MoE router, drops about 3% of high-gradient-norm samples, and averages selected checkpoints.
