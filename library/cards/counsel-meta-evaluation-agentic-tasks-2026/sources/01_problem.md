Agent critics often produce fluent and well-structured critiques, yet they may identify the wrong step, omit the critical cause, or recommend an impossible repair. Asking another LLM to judge these critiques can mistake writing quality for diagnostic quality.

Counsel treats the critic output itself as the evaluation object. Humans annotate the underlying agent error and assess whether each critique is correct, complete, specific, and actionable, creating meta-evaluation data for process criticism.
