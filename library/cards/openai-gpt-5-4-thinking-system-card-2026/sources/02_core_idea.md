The one-sentence contribution is: GPT-5.4 Thinking expands frontier disclosure from coarse reasoning RL to concrete long-rollout preservation, confirmation-policy, prompt-injection, and cyber-safety interventions, while separately exposing dynamic safety trajectories, production-like resampling, CoT monitoring, programmatic agent tests, and two-tier deployment oversight.

Training remains partially specified. The model should revert its own long-rollout changes without destroying implicit simulated user work; computer-use behavior should follow platform policy plus developer-supplied confirmation policy; prompt-injection and cyber training should produce policy-compliant, maximally helpful dual-use behavior while refusing or de-escalating harmful action. These are objectives, not disclosed reward equations: detector, labels, scale, aggregation, calibration, rollout distribution, and optimizer are unknown.

Evaluation has several different data objects. Dynamic mental-health, emotional-reliance, and self-harm evaluations use adversarial user simulators to create varied multi-turn trajectories and check every assistant message. The production-like pilot takes deidentified conversations representative of recent GPT-5.2 Thinking traffic, resamples only the final assistant turn with GPT-5.4 Thinking, and automatically labels the new completion. Connector and function-call prompt-injection evaluations are explicitly splits of training data. Software and cyber environments use hidden tests, secret flags, pass/fail, pass@1, medal thresholds, or expert rubrics.

The monitorability suite is another layer. GPT-5 Thinking observes either CoT only, action/final output only, or all messages and predicts task-specific outcomes; g-mean-squared and cross-fit filtering aggregate performance. The answer labels can come from task-specific graders rather than the CoT monitor, creating mismatch. The deployed cyber stack is separate again: a topical classifier gates a safety-reasoner monitor that maps content to a threat taxonomy and can trigger message or actor controls.

Compared with GPT-5.2, GPT-5.4 adds a concrete long-rollout self-reversion intervention, configurable developer confirmation policy, GPT-5.4-specific dynamic and resampled evaluation, and first-general-purpose-model High-cyber deployment mitigations.
