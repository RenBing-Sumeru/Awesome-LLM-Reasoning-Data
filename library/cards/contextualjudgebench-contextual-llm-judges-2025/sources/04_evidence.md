Across 18 evaluated models, even o1 achieves only about 55% consistent accuracy, indicating that the contextual hard pairs are not saturated. Many judges drop sharply when context is removed, response order is swapped, or criteria change, directly showing that context-free judge scores cannot substitute for conditional RAG or summarization evaluation. These results support the central claim only under the released data, model scales, candidate budgets, and evaluation protocols; cross-domain use requires new validation.
