The core contribution is an audit surface for LLM-as-a-Judge: repetition stability (RS), position consistency (PC), and preference fairness (PF) jointly distinguish random variation, order sensitivity, and systematic primacy/recency preference. Rather than releasing a new training dataset, the paper applies this measurement protocol to MTBench and DevBench and uses agreement patterns to identify hard-to-judge instances. No official downloadable dataset was located; the paper uses public benchmarks and reports an anonymous reproducibility repository in its appendix.
