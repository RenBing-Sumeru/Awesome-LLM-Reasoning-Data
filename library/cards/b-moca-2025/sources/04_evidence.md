The CoLLAs 2025 paper states that B-MoCA defines 131 daily tasks and creates 45 unique environments, divided into 35 training and 10 testing environments. The official project page documents the version history: the first public release had 70 tasks, later versions added tasks, and version 2 removed 7 high-stochasticity tasks to reach 131 tasks; version 3 updates action parsing. The benchmark compares GPT-4o, Gemini-1.5-pro, Llama-3, MLLM agents, and custom imitation-learning agents. Reported findings emphasize that foundation-model agents handle straightforward tasks but degrade on long-horizon or randomized settings, while custom agents can imitate demonstrations but generalize poorly to unseen configurations. Row-level evidence is detector success inside the emulator; aggregate success rates are meaningful only with the exact environment IDs and release version.
