B-MoCA addresses evaluation of mobile device-control agents under diverse Android device configurations. The primary sources are the CoLLAs 2025 paper/arXiv PDF, official project page, and release repository. The evaluation surface is an Android emulator episode: task instruction, observation from screen image or Android view hierarchy, action, environment transition, and rule-based success signal. It belongs in the atlas as an interactive mobile-agent benchmark, not as a static screenshot dataset or a model-training recipe alone. Its value is the randomization of device settings, language, icon positions, wallpaper, and form factor to test generalization rather than only task memorization.
