The official record presents gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b as mixture-of-experts reasoning models trained with large-scale distillation and reinforcement learning. Its most reusable disclosure is an interface-level description: Harmony conversations distinguish System, Developer, User, Assistant, and Tool roles, with separate analysis, commentary, and final channels, and the public release includes reference inference and tool environments.

The Card's central interpretation is deliberately narrower than a model-performance claim. The report exposes enough to identify possible post-training data objects—conversation turns, reasoning text, tool calls, tool observations, and final responses—but it does not release the corresponding prompts, traces, rewards, filters, or provenance. The object of study is therefore the disclosure boundary itself.
