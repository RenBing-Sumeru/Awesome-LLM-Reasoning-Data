For this atlas, the distinctive contribution is not that gpt-oss makes a fully specified training-data release. Rather, it combines an open-weight model release with an unusually concrete public interface for reasoning and tools, a broad account of post-training and safety stages, and explicit model-card discussion of risks. That combination makes it possible to map what is disclosed and what remains opaque.

The report should not be read as evidence that its reinforcement-learning or distillation pipeline is transferable. Reward definitions, verifier coverage and error rates, trajectory selection, provenance, safety-data construction, and contamination controls are all material to such a claim and remain unavailable. The novelty recorded here is disclosure value, not a claim of reproducible methodology.
