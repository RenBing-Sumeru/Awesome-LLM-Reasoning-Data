Frontier model reports can make post-training behavior legible while leaving the provenance, construction, and auditability of the underlying data and feedback objects incomplete. For reasoning models, an open-weight release must not be conflated with an open training recipe: source records, accepted and rejected traces, reward calibration, verifier coverage, and contamination controls may remain unavailable.

gpt-oss is valuable for this distinction. Its official Model Card and release describe two open-weight reasoning models, their tool-facing conversation interface, and several training and safety choices, while withholding the material needed to reconstruct or audit the post-training data pipeline. This Card records that boundary as a Track 12 disclosure ledger; it does not treat the release as a reusable RLVR recipe.
