The project reports new or improved results across all attempted domains. Examples include up to twofold faster GPU kernels than prior art, better results on selected AtCoder heuristic contests, and denoising scores of 0.71 and 0.73 on the listed PBMC and Tabula tasks versus a 0.64 best-human reference. The paper states that mathematical and algorithmic solutions were reviewed by experts or competition organizers.

This evidence supports problem-specific discovery under continuous reward, not broad zero-shot generalization. The stated cost is only a few hundred dollars per problem, but that is still far larger than ordinary one-shot inference. A careful comparison must retain the reward evaluator, expert-review process, number of updates, and full task budget alongside the final headline solution.
