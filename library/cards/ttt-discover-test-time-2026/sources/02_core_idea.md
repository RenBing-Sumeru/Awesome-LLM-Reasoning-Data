The method shifts the test-time consumer from a frozen decoder to a problem-specific reinforcement-learning loop. It uses experience generated while solving one target to update the model toward higher continuous reward on that target. The intended outcome is not average performance across many inputs but the best solution found for the one active mathematical, algorithmic, kernel, or biology problem.

This makes test-time compute include learning. Search and optimization are designed to prioritize promising solutions, and reported results are reviewed by task experts or organizers. The resulting record contains more than a chain of thought: it contains a sequence of candidate solutions, reward outcomes, updates, and the best solution selected after problem-specific adaptation.
