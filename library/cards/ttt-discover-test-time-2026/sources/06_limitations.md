The approach needs a continuous, inexpensive-enough, and trustworthy reward for the individual problem; many natural-language tasks lack such a signal. Per-problem learning costs more than ordinary inference and can overfit to the evaluator or discover metric-specific shortcuts. Expert or organizer review is valuable but may differ across domains. Reproduction also depends on the Tinker service and the exact optimization configuration, so cost, access, and update stability must be audited before treating the results as a general-purpose inference recipe.
