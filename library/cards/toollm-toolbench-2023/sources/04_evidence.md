The arXiv abstract verifies 16,464 real-world RESTful APIs across 49 categories, closed-chat-model-generated instructions, solution-path annotation, ToolLLaMA fine-tuning, and zero-shot generalization to APIBench.

Instance-level evidence comes from the released task object and the scorer: ToolEval uses closed-model judgments of pass rate and win rate over full tool-use trajectories. Aggregate scores are meaningful only for the reported split, model setup, and harness. Public artifacts support inspection, but they do not remove contamination, dependency, or license checks.
