Earlier open text corpora and documentation frameworks established parts of the provenance problem. This work's change is to combine an 8TB public-domain/open-license corpus, source-oriented preparation code, a disclosed training mixture, and released validation-model checkpoints in a NeurIPS 2025 dataset paper. It is a direction signal because legal and provenance claims are made visible alongside scale and model validation rather than left entirely outside the release.

The work does not establish that all data used in LLM training is licensed, nor does it make source-level audit unnecessary. It is not a post-training reasoning-data recipe, a preference dataset, or a benchmark. Before reuse, inspect every source's actual rights statement and transformations, whether the hosted snapshot matches the paper, the exact release terms, deduplication and contamination evidence, and whether evaluation data overlap is addressed.
