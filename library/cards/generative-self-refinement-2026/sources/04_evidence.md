Before training, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct has a near-zero average Refinement Gap: on AIME24, pass@1 is 13.2%, maj@4 is 16.7%, and selfRef@4 is only 15.6%. After hybrid training, GSR-7B reaches 50.1% pass@1, 60.0% maj@4, and 66.0% selfRef@4 on AIME24. Across AIME24, AIME25, AMC22-23, MATH500, and OlympiadBench, its selfRef@4 average is 73.6%, compared with 70.5% for its own maj@4 and 70.9% for the strongest reported four-candidate reward-model selector.

The conditional analysis tests the claim that the model can exceed selection. With only one correct candidate out of four on AIME24, GSR selfRef@4 reaches 60.2%, while majority voting reaches 18.8%. When all four candidates are incorrect, the method still produces a correct solution 5.9% of the time, whereas selection baselines fail. The paper also reports that fine-tuned 14B and 32B models retain gains and that the candidate-conditioned skill transfers despite their training candidates having been generated by the 7B model.
