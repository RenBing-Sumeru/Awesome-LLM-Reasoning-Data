ST-BoN requires hidden-state access, so it does not apply directly to closed API models. Its theoretical argument assumes local Lipschitz continuity and bounded distance increments and yields a probabilistic bound on final latent consistency, not a correctness guarantee. A consensus in latent space can still converge on a shared wrong answer or reproduce base-model bias.

The authors also identify window-length adaptivity as unresolved: longer outputs may require a larger tau, while the main experiments use tau=c. Results are concentrated on six datasets and a small set of model families. Exact prompts, versions, seeds, decontamination, complete input provenance, rejected candidates, and score traces are not all established as released. The repository's final-output record is insufficient to replay every selection decision, and benchmark gains do not demonstrate process-record quality or safety.
