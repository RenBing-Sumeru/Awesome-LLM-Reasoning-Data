One-sentence contribution: Direct Preference Optimization turns chosen/rejected response pairs into a direct policy objective without fitting and optimizing a separate reward model in the standard RLHF pipeline. Core mechanism: DPO derives a closed-form objective that uses preference pairs and a reference policy to optimize the language model directly under the paper's reward-model parameterization.
