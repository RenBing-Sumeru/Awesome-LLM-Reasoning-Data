“When Data is the Algorithm” starts from a practical opacity in preference optimization. Public DPO corpora such as TuluDPO, ORPO, UltraFeedback, HelpSteer, and Code-Preference-Pairs are often compared only by downstream model scores, while the reason a pair was included, the kind of task it represents, and the confidence that its preference order is meaningful remain unclear. The paper treats dataset composition as an experimental variable whose metadata must be measured before it can be optimized.
