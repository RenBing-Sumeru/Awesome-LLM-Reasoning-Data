UltraMix is reported to be 30% smaller than the best-performing individual dataset while exceeding it on key benchmarks. The result supports the paper’s thesis that selection can be more consequential than raw volume. It must be read conditionally: “best-performing” depends on the base model, DPO recipe, annotation signals, mixture ratios, benchmarks, and any data/benchmark overlap controls. A smaller mixture can win by removing noise, by matching the evaluation distribution, or both.
