The authors analyze five popular open DPO corpora, identify structural and qualitative discrepancies, then curate UltraMix by selectively drawing from all five while removing noisy or redundant samples. The release includes annotations, metadata, and the curated mixture. A reproducible UltraMix record needs the original dataset and row ID, Magpie version/prompt/model, task and quality label, reward margin, filtering decision/reason, deduplication relation, mixture weight, and final split. Without this, a “cleaned mixture” cannot be independently audited.
