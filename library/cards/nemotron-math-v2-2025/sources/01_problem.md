Existing math supervision rarely combines several reasoning budgets, very long traces, community questions, and tool-integrated variants under one comparable schema.

Nemotron-Math generates six supervision modes per source family and packages expected answers and provenance for long-context distillation. The decision boundary is whether a serialized record survives answer verification, majority-answer correction metadata, source curation, and matched downstream tests; the direct output is Nemotron-Math-v2, consumed by long-context mathematical reasoning SFT rather than an evaluation-only benchmark.

L4 facts: primary source arXiv:2512.15489; venue/date arXiv preprint (2025); open data Nemotron-Math-v2 at https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Nemotron-Math-v2; scale 7.5 million solution traces over 85,000 AoPS and 262,000 Math StackExchange problems; full paper, official README, terms, schema, and one actual record checked on 2026-07-27.
