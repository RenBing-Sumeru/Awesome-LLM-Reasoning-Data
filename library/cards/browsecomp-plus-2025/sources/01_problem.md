BrowseComp-Plus asks how to evaluate deep-search agents without letting a changing live web blur retrieval quality, answer synthesis, and citation behavior into one score. Its archival record is the ACL 2026 paper *BrowseComp-Plus: A Fair and Disentangled Evaluation Benchmark for Deep Search Agents*. The stable Card ID retains 2025 from arXiv:2508.06600's first submission, while the title, year, venue, and 21-author list follow the formal ACL version.

The released benchmark unit is one of 830 filtered BrowseComp queries, paired with a short reference answer and document-level supervision over a fixed 100,195-document corpus. The query release exposes `query_id`, query, answer, evidence documents, gold documents, and negative documents; the payload other than `query_id` is reversibly obfuscated. The corpus exposes `docid`, text, and URL. Its Hugging Face split label is `train` only as a packaging label: the paper provides no evidence that this corpus or the benchmark records were used for model training.

The evaluation surface is larger than question-answer pairs. Search agents interact with released indexes, receive top-five results truncated to 512 tokens per document, may use an optional `get_document` action in the reported ablation, and terminate with an explanation, numeric document citations, a short answer, and confidence. Public episode files can include ordered tool calls, tool outputs, reasoning summaries when exposed, usage, retrieved document IDs, terminal status, and final output.

This task-plus-fixed-environment-plus-episode structure places the paper in `environment_agent_trajectory_data`. It should not be merged with `browsecomp-2025`: BrowseComp remains the parent 1,266-question source benchmark, whereas BrowseComp-Plus retains 830 questions and adds the frozen corpus, human evidence/gold qrels, hard negatives, indexes, graders, executable clients, and a partial trajectory release. The accepted metadata remains `L3_summary_ready` and `partial`; the Card is bilingual and audit-ready, but it does not pre-approve Review state or claim complete replay.
