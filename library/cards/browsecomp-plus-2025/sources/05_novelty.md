The closest baseline is BrowseComp evaluated through live-web browsing and final-answer judgment. BrowseComp-Plus does not claim a new independent 1,266-item benchmark. It retains 830 parent questions that survive evidence collection, scraping, and human review, then attaches a fixed 100,195-document substrate, evidence/gold qrels, mined negatives, released indexes, retrieval and citation metrics, executable clients, and four frontier-agent trajectory archives.

The concrete change is diagnostic separation. A run can now fail because its terminal status is incomplete, its retriever misses labeled evidence, its citations omit retrieved evidence, or its synthesized answer fails semantic-equivalence judgment. This makes the environment and feedback contract inspectable at several levels rather than treating final-answer accuracy as a complete explanation of agent behavior.

The direction signal for reasoning-data curation is that a web-agent benchmark should package the retrieval substrate, document supervision, action/observation schema, terminal predicate, grader versions, and failed-run retention together. The fixed corpus, human label protocol, aggregate construction attrition, executable clients, and partial episode release are quality signals because they expose what can be audited; high benchmark scores are not quality signals by themselves.

The components are not individually new verifier classes. Fixed corpora, BM25 and neural retrieval, human qrels, LLM semantic judges, document-ID citation checks, and tool-call traces all predate this work. The contribution is their integration around BrowseComp questions and the resulting ability to disentangle retrieval from answer synthesis under a controlled substrate.

Reuse still requires checks the release does not close: count and status-audit all four trajectory archives, bind artifact revisions in one manifest, pin proprietary model and judge snapshots plus seeds and retries, measure qrel incompleteness, preserve exception failures, and clarify licenses and source-page rights. Until then, the resource is an evaluation benchmark and audit case, not a verified training corpus.
