LFQA-HP-1M retains human pairwise choices from SHP-2, LFQA Eval, and Chatbot Arena after a high-confidence long-form-question filter. It releases 1,326,356 records from about 900K source prompts; each record includes question text, two answers, a human judgment, expertise flag, domain, language, turn, and source. The feedback contract remains the original human comparison, not the later nine-rubric evaluation features.
