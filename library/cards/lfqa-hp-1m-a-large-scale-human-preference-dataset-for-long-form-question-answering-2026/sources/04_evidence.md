Claim: a large, traceable long-form preference corpus exposes limits of judge models. The paper reports 1,326,356 retained pairs and uses LFQA-HP-Sample to compare nine-rubric logistic regression with prompted LLM judges. It reports position sensitivity, verbosity bias, transitivity violations, and degradation under TextFooler or DeepWordBug. These results diagnose those evaluated judges and perturbations, not factual correctness of every pair.
