1. Positioning: CaRR data and training protocol uses decomposing complex questions into verifiable subgoals and scoring entities, citations, and evidence-chain completeness to create specialized feedback for failures missed by general deep-search agent rewards.

2. Method handle: collect tasks and states, generate candidates, verify labels, and organize by capability; reproducible feedback is the key quality control.

3. Data handle: complex search questions with decomposed single-hop rubrics, citations, and chain-level rewards, with records centered on inputs, states, candidates, evaluation evidence, and feedback labels.

4. Evidence anchor: citation-aware rubric rewards improve retrieval correctness and evidence completeness, with conclusions bounded by model, environment, and judge configurations.

5. Reuse decision: best suited to training deep-research agents with robust evidence chains; contamination, false positives, and cross-environment stability must be checked.
