Closest prior work uses general preference pairs, terminal success, or step accuracy without representing the domain-specific states and failure modes of deep-search agent rewards. This paper changes the record structure and feedback contract by centering CaRR data and training protocol and combining inputs, candidates, environmental or evidential state, and labels in one auditable example through decomposing complex questions into verifiable subgoals and scoring entities, citations, and evidence-chain completeness. The contribution is task-specific data construction, capability decomposition, and practical evaluation rather than sample count alone.
