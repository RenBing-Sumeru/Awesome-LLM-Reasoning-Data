The main artifact is CaRR data and training protocol, containing complex search questions with decomposed single-hop rubrics, citations, and chain-level rewards. Each record organizes a task input, visual or environmental state, candidate response or trajectory, evaluation evidence, and a preference, score, success, or critique label, together with task provenance and capability categories. Quality control relies on decomposing complex questions into verifiable subgoals and scoring entities, citations, and evidence-chain completeness, making the release suitable for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than ordinary question answering.
