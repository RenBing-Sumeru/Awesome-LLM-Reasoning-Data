A deep-search agent can produce a polished report while citing irrelevant pages, omitting key entities, or making claims unsupported by its evidence chain. Rewards based only on the final answer or writing style reinforce responses that look researched but cannot be audited.

The work decomposes complex questions into subgoals and scores entity coverage, citation correctness, and evidence-chain completeness with citation-aware rubric rewards to train robust search agents.
