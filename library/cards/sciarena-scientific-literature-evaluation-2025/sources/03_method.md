1. **Deploy an open arena:** Users submit literature-grounded research questions and receive two anonymised model answers with citations.

2. **Collect researcher preferences:** Users vote for A, B, tie, or both bad, and the platform records questions, models, responses, citations, and votes.

3. **Clean and release:** Sessions are deduplicated and filtered and assigned query types and subjects; paper totals and public subsets are reported separately.

4. **Build judge meta-evaluation:** Automated judges predict preferences for the same pairs and are compared with human votes and Elo rankings; snapshots and judges must be fixed.
