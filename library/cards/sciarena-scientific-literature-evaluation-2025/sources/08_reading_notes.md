1. **Position:**  SciArena evaluates non-verifiable literature research through real researcher votes.
2. **Method:**  Anonymous battles, scientific questions, responses/citations, preferences, and judge meta-evaluation.
3. **Data:**  Over 20K platform votes; about 13,204 public records across twenty-five subjects and six types.
4. **Evidence:**  Automated judges differ from researchers, and more citations do not guarantee preference.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for scientific preference judges; distinguish platform totals from public revisions.
