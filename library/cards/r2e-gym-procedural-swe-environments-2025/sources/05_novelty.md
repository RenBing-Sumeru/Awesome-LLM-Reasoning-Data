SWE-Gym and SWE-smith primarily expand training environments, while best-of-N SWE-agent work usually selects trajectories with either tests or one reward model. R2E-Gym unifies commit-level data synthesis with verifier scaling. SYNGEN creates tasks from commits without requiring human issues or newly added tests, and inference exploits different failure modes of execution signals and learned judgment rather than assuming one verifier is fully reliable. Back-translation, test generation, and reward models are not individually new; the novelty is their organization into an open-weight recipe combining more environments with hybrid verification.
