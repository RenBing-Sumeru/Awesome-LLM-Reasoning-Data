The contribution links two artifacts: procedurally curated AgentGym/R2E-Gym environments and hybrid test-time verification. The paper reports over 8.7K tasks, while the current public R2E-Gym-V1 release contains about 7,478 records and a smaller 4,578-task training subset; scale must therefore be versioned. SYNGEN extracts candidate fixes from commits, generates or collects tests that expose the old-version behavior, and back-translates commits into issues. Successful expert-agent runs supervise 7B, 14B, and 32B models. At inference, test-based and execution-free verifiers rank candidates separately and are fused for SWE-agent SFT, RL, and best-of-N selection.
