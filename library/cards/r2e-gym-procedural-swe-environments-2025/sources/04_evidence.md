In training experiments, the 32B R2E-Gym agent reaches 34.4% Pass@1 on SWE-bench Verified, compared with about 20.6% when trained on SWE-Gym, indicating useful supervision from commit-derived environments. In test-time scaling, execution-based and execution-free verifiers individually saturate around 42–43%, while the hybrid strategy reaches 51%, supporting complementary errors. An ablation reports roughly 47.4% with regression tests alone and 51% after adding generated tests. These results depend on specific open models, expert trajectories, and a Best@26 budget; 51% is not single-run capability, and both test and learned verifiers may be biased.
