Prior work such as MMMU already made multimodal academic QA a standard benchmark. MMMU-Pro changes the benchmark object by auditing shortcut solvability, filtering or redesigning items, and adding a vision-only condition that stresses visual evidence use.

The direction signal is benchmark robustness rather than model improvement: it asks whether a high score survives when textual shortcuts and option priors are reduced. The quality signal is the official release of paper, code/evaluator, project page, and dataset card with post-release answer-fix notes.

What is not new: answer-level accuracy, multiple-choice/QA evaluation, and academic subject coverage all existed before. Before reuse, inspect dataset license, exact split/revision, answer corrections, prompt formatting, and any training contamination from public benchmark examples.
