The paper reports that a Gemma 3 12B TTI agent reaches an average 66.5% task success rate on the reported WebVoyager setting, and the project page describes this as the leading result among open agents trained on public data. On that setting, the curriculum outperforms a fixed horizon of 10 by 7.4 percentage points and a fixed horizon of 30 by 21.3 points. The authors also evaluate on full WebArena and report the strongest result among the compared open-source agents trained through self-improvement.

The evidence also distinguishes the proposed axis from prompting alone. A check-again prompt can improve most reported WebArena domains, and the project’s equal-token comparison shows interaction scaling with the steepest improvement trend relative to per-step budget forcing and best-of-n. These findings support the claim that more actions can be valuable, but they are benchmark-specific: task evaluators, browser state, model version, prompt, rollout limit, and external web behavior determine whether an apparent horizon gain transfers.
