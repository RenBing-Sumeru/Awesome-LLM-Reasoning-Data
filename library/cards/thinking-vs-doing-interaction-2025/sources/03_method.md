The paper first isolates interaction scaling at inference. A prompting-based “check-again” intervention asks an agent to reconsider after it signals completion, then the study contrasts this with forcing more reasoning at each step and with best-of-n under a total-token budget. In WebArena, the controlled setting samples 62 test tasks and reserves 750 tasks for online training; the maximum interaction horizon is 30. Each rollout records browser observations, actions, completion attempts, the number of steps, tokens, and environment success.

The training method, TTI, applies online reinforcement learning with a binary task signal over extended web trajectories. A fixed short horizon restricts exploration, whereas a fixed long horizon is difficult to optimize because early exploratory moves receive delayed and noisy credit. TTI therefore increases the maximum horizon over training and mainly uses online filtered behavior cloning. The released repository supplies WebVoyager and WebArena configurations, task files, evaluation scripts, and two model checkpoints; reproduction should also pin containers, prompts, allowed attempts, and the curriculum schedule.
