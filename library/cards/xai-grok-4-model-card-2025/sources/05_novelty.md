For this atlas, the contribution is not an open data or reward release. Its value is the combination of a model-card disclosure of broad source categories, filtering, post-training feedback families, and safety evaluations with a launch disclosure of scaled RL and native tool-use training.

That combination permits a disciplined ledger: some parts of the training and evaluation story are named, but the source mixture, record lineage, verifier/reward contract, filter specification, training scaffold, and reproducibility artifacts remain undisclosed. The Card preserves that boundary instead of upgrading a high-level report into a reusable recipe.
