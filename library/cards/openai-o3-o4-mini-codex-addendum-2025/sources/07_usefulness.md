Within the frontier reports and data-disclosure ledger track, this Card is a template for separating four objects that closed agent reports often conflate: the general coding-task RL pool, safety-training data, safety/capability evaluation sets, and deployment controls. Only the first two are training evidence; scores belong to evaluation, while containers, monitors, and diff review belong to deployment mitigation.

The addendum supports high-level labels for agent training, safety alignment, state/action-level feedback, and scalar reward. It does not support claims of an open coding dataset, executable reward completeness, released trajectories, a replayable training environment, process supervision over non-visible internal reasoning, or independently validated safety. In particular, passing tests is a product behavior until the training reward mapping is disclosed.

Reuse would require revision-pinned repositories, licensed task sources, container and tool manifests, trajectory and evidence schemas, exact tests/graders/rewards, synthetic-pipeline versions, label protocols, train/evaluation membership lists, and contamination checks. Without those artifacts, the report is valuable for audit-question design and cross-company disclosure comparison, not for reconstructing codex-1.
