The addendum does not identify the real-world coding tasks, repositories, revisions, licenses, environment images, test harnesses, or rollout records used for general RL. codex-1 is said to operate in varied environments and to iteratively run tests until they pass, but the RL algorithm, objective, test-to-reward mapping, PR-preference source, rollout count, decoding, optimizer, schedule, compute, and stage yields are undisclosed.

Malware safety training starts from existing policy and safety data, then adds a more detailed malware policy and synthetic prompts, code snippets, and environment configurations. The model is trained to refuse high-risk requests, provide contextual or defensive help, and handle ambiguous or dual-use cases without excessive refusal. For false-completion behavior, real environments are perturbed and synthetic environments are generated; RL contrasts unsupported completion claims with truthful acknowledgements of constraints. Prompt-injection safety adds new training data for attacks embedded in coding environments.

Deployment is documented more concretely than training. Each task runs in a cloud container loaded with the user's repository and configured development tools; the agent can edit files and execute tests, linters, and type checkers. At launch, internet was disabled after setup. The June 3 update allowed users to configure per-project internet access, including allowlists and denylists. These product controls reduce some harms but do not reveal the training environments or reward stack.
