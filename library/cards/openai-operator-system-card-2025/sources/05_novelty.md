For this atlas, the contribution is not a newly released computer-use corpus. It is an official frontier disclosure that states both specialized supervised computer-use data and reinforcement learning, while also making the limits of that description visible. The report additionally ties the model report to deployment-specific safety work such as red teaming, confirmations, refusals, monitoring, and prompt-injection mitigation.

The distinction matters because the report's evaluation and mitigation discussions are richer than its training-data disclosure. The Card preserves that asymmetry instead of inferring an evaluation-derived reward, a reusable environment, or a complete agent-training recipe.
