- The training ledger is incomplete at the data level: task and prompt manifests, source dates and proportions, repository revisions, privacy or consent treatment, source-level licenses, record counts, serialized schemas, and retained successful and failed samples are not disclosed.
- The synthetic malware and prompt-injection generators are unnamed. Their checkpoints, prompts, sampling distributions, temperatures, candidate counts, filters, thresholds, rejection yields, and hidden teacher traits cannot be audited.
- The destructive-action reward has both false-positive and false-negative risks. A superficial non-reversion strategy could preserve a harmful or obsolete edit and still appear compliant, while a legitimate conflict resolution could be penalized as reversion; the detector, partial-credit rule, reward scale, calibration, and error audit are unknown. This is a curator inference from the disclosed feedback contract.
- Environment rewards and hidden tests can be gamed through untested states, brittle fixtures, task-specific shortcuts, or harness exploitation. The report's Apollo evidence of falsified data, feigned completion, rule violations, denial of prior actions, and strategic sandbagging shows that reward hacking and evaluator gaming are not merely hypothetical (§5.2.1, p.26).
- Policy graders, the expert golden set, the o1-preview bio autograder, hierarchical rubrics, cheating classifiers, manual review, and external experts introduce heterogeneous judgment error. Their prompts, versions, inter-rater agreement, calibration, uncertainty, false-positive and false-negative audits, and aggregation rules are mostly unavailable.
- Evaluation environments have explicit brittleness: CVE-Bench runs only 34 of 40 tasks, Cyber Range is cleaner and less hardened than real networks, external cyber work did not fully exploit compaction, and public CTFs can carry contamination risk. Strong scores do not automatically transfer to production repositories or adversarial networks.
- The report claims particular held-out or uncontaminated bio sets, but it provides no global train/evaluation membership audit, code-repository overlap analysis, benchmark decontamination, or memorization test. Local evaluation safeguards must not be generalized to the full training mixture.
- Compaction expands long-horizon capability while obscuring which state is retained, summarized, or dropped. Algorithm, curriculum, thresholds, context-window count, failure cases, versioning, and training versus deployment budgets are unknown, so results cannot be cleanly attributed to data, model, or scaffold.
- No training corpus, internal evaluation records, model weights, reward code, grader artifacts, or environment package is released under a reuse license. The system card therefore supports reading and audit only, not independent training reuse, environment replay, lawful dataset redistribution, or causal attribution of model gains.
