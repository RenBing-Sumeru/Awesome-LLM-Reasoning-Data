The Card's one-sentence contribution is: GPT-5.1-Codex-Max exposes a small but concrete set of coding-agent post-training objects—synthetic malware scenarios, prompt-injection examples, and conflicting-edit RL episodes—along with several distinct feedback contracts, while withholding the records and implementations needed for reuse.

The core mechanism is behavior shaping through different feedback surfaces. Malware responses are conditioned by a detailed policy and checked against an internal expert-curated golden set; prompt-injection behavior is trained under Instruction Hierarchy-style data and measured by whether the injected instruction is ignored; destructive-action RL perturbs the workspace with user-model edits and rewards non-reversion. Separately, capability evaluations use executable or judgment-based predicates: Playwright tests, hidden unit tests, CTF completion, CVE exploitation, Cyber Range scenario success, hierarchical rubrics, an o1-preview autograder validated against a biosecurity expert, classifiers with manual cheating review, and external experts.

These contracts observe different things. Policy graders can observe response compliance but not whether underlying reasoning is faithful; tests can observe specified end states but miss untested regressions or reward-hacking strategies; environment success can observe task completion but depends on harness fidelity; expert and model judges can score rubric-defined quality but introduce calibration and bias risks. Positive reinforcement for preserving edits names a desired outcome but does not disclose how preservation is detected, how partial conflicts are handled, or how that reward interacts with other objectives.

The direction represented is auditable frontier-agent post-training disclosure. The closest Atlas comparison is the *GPT-5 System Card*, which gives broader source and router-signal categories, and the later *GPT-5.3-Codex System Card*, which repeats a narrower conflicting-edit RL disclosure. The distinguishing value of GPT-5.1-Codex-Max is the combination of three named safety-data interventions, compaction-enabled long-horizon operation, and richly specified evaluation environments. The key boundary is unchanged: evaluation detail cannot substitute for a released training corpus or reward implementation.
