Public mathematics benchmarks such as AIME and MATH are heavily reused for training and evaluation and are becoming saturated by strong reasoning models. Most contain only English natural-language answers and lack bilingual alignment or formally checkable tasks. Final numeric scoring can also hide guess-based reasoning.

OlymMATH manually curates difficult problems from printed competition sources and combines bilingual numeric-answer evaluation with Lean formal verification in one suite, releasing large model-trajectory data for process and language analysis.
