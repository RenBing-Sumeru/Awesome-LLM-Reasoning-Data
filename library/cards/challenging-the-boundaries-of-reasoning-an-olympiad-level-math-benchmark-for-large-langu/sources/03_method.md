1. **Curate printed problems:** Olympiad problems that are not widely digitized are selected from publications, organized by domain and difficulty, and expert-checked for statements and answers.

2. **Create bilingual versions:** Parallel Chinese and English formulations are prepared and checked for mathematical and numeric consistency rather than relying on raw machine translation.

3. **Build two verifier types:** Two hundred computational problems use answer parsing and rule matching, while 150 are formalized in Lean 4 and checked by the kernel.

4. **Collect model trajectories:** Twenty-eight models run under common sampling settings, storing full reasoning, final answers, language, domain, and scores.

5. **Analyze dimensions:** Difficulty, language, domain, pass/consistency, and whether correct answers arise from rigorous reasoning or heuristics are compared.
