After fine-tuning Qwen3-4B on CHIMERA, the model approaches or matches much larger DeepSeek-R1 and Qwen3-235B systems on GPQA-Diamond, AIME 2024/2025/2026, HMMT25, Humanity’s Last Exam, and related tasks while retaining cross-disciplinary generalization. This supports the efficiency of compact high-coverage data, but teacher, base model, and token budgets differ, and model cross-validation is not independent human ground truth.
