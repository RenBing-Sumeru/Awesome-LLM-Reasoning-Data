1. **Generate a knowledge taxonomy:** Use models to recursively build hierarchical concept trees for eight disciplines, refine them into more than one thousand topics, and remove duplicate or overly broad nodes.

2. **Synthesize by topic:** Generate multi-step scientific or mathematical problems for each leaf while controlling task form, difficulty, and topic coverage.

3. **Generate long solutions:** Use strong reasoning models to produce CoT and final answers and store question–reasoning–answer records.

4. **Cross-validate and filter:** Ask multiple strong models to independently check problem validity, answer correctness, and explanation consistency, retaining only high-consensus examples in the roughly 9K corpus.
