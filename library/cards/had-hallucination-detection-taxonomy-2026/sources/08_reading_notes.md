1. **Position:**  HAD unifies hallucination detection, localisation, and correction under one taxonomy.
2. **Method:**  Eleven types, 90K synthetic training examples, 2,248 human HADTest records, and joint generation.
3. **Data:**  Inputs, erroneous text, types, spans, and correction targets are retained.
4. **Evidence:**  It reaches SOTA on HaluEval, FactCHD, FaithBench, and other cross-dataset tests.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for unified hallucination models; guard against synthetic artefacts and unsupported correction.
