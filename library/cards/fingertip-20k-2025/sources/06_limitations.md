- **Corpus and split boundary:** the paper reports 21,437 episodes, 95 users, and 506 apps, but the official index exposes 20,000 rows, 83 users, and 482 app identifiers. The missing mapping, validation index, and reduction rationale are unknown. The suggestion test has four exact duplicate rows and shares 172 unique keys with execution; treating rows as unique tasks or the tests as disjoint would misstate evidence.
- **Feedback boundary:** `SR1` depends on a DeepSeek-V3 same-intent judgment that the released proactive script does not implement. `SR2` depends on manual final-state inspection absent from the execution script, whose `success` output is always initialized/written as 0. Judge bias, adjudicator inconsistency, surface similarity, and the 2.5× cutoff can produce false positives or false negatives relative to useful task completion.
- **Environment and replay boundary:** episodes preserve screenshots, XML, and actions, but not per-episode app/APK versions, Android builds, accounts, initial states, network/service state, reset procedures, or deterministic replay manifests. Live UI drift and grounding failures can change outcomes independently of personalization or reasoning quality; the release is therefore an offline corpus plus live-evaluation code, not a resettable environment.
- **Selection and failure boundary:** manual checks delete low-quality data, minor human noise remains, and every retained trace receives a `finish` marker. No explicit success/failure field proves the intended outcome, rejection counts and rubrics are unknown, and failed model rollouts/results are not released. Filtering may remove hard cases, and `finish` must not be interpreted as independent success verification.
- **Training and experimental boundary:** only Qwen-2.5-VL-7B is fine-tuned, and loss, optimizer, learning rate, batch size, epochs, seed, code, configurations, and checkpoints are undisclosed. The experiments support SFT and evaluation only; they do not validate RL/RLVR, preference/reward modeling, PRM use, or broad cross-model and cross-region generalization.
- **Privacy, rights, and release boundary:** all contributors are in mainland China and primarily use Chinese third-party apps. Longitudinal screenshots, locations/scenarios, usage history, and detailed profiles create re-identification and sensitive-content risks despite manual/Qwen-VL-Max filtering. Kaggle version 2 declares CC BY 4.0 for the dataset, but the code license is unknown, third-party UI/content rights are not resolved, consent text and post-release deletion policy are undisclosed, and the planned collection-app release is absent.
- **Contamination boundary:** the main split is temporal within users, so train and tests share all 83 released users and may share apps, intent classes, and habitual patterns. No corpus-wide deduplication or benchmark decontamination method is disclosed. Any gain attributed to personalization should therefore be tested against memorization, repeated intent templates, and overlap-aware baselines.
