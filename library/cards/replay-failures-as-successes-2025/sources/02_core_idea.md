HiR leaves a sampled response unchanged and rewrites only its conditioning instruction. The pseudo-instruction contains the constraints that the response was judged to satisfy and omits the unmet constraints. The same text that receives zero under the complete original instruction can therefore receive one under a strictly narrower hindsight instruction; success is conditional on the relabeled task, not retroactive success on the original task.

Selection uses constraint-level feedback together with response entropy. Only partial outcomes with a satisfied-constraint fraction strictly between zero and one are eligible, and a scheduled curriculum score balances constraint integrity with uncertainty. Original and hindsight samples then enter a Reinforce++-style update with binary rewards, creating contrast in both response space and instruction space.

The feedback contract is mixed. Hard constraints use deterministic rules or executable checks; soft constraints use DeepSeek-V3.1 in non-thinking mode as a learned judge. A replay record is therefore auditable only if it preserves the constraint type, checker/judge version, individual decisions, rewritten prompt, unchanged response, selection score, and replay flag.
