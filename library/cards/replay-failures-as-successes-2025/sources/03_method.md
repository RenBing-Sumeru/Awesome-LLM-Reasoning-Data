HIR-16K is constructed from MulDimIF, VerIF, IFTrain, Chatbot Arena, and additional programmatic constraint synthesis. The paper reports about 16K queries with more than five decomposable constraints each. The public dataset rows expose the prompt, criteria, checker metadata, source, and identifier. Exact upstream revisions, source-wise counts, deduplication across sources, and contamination checks are not disclosed in the checked Card.

During RL, the policy samples candidate responses and obtains per-constraint outcomes. Candidates whose dense satisfaction is exactly zero or one are excluded from hindsight selection; partial failures are ranked by a scheduled combination of entropy and the fraction of satisfied constraints. The chosen response is copied unchanged, unmet constraints are removed from the prompt, and a replay indicator distinguishes the relabeled sample.

Original and replayed samples share the subsequent Reinforce++-style update, but their success predicates differ. Original samples require the full constraint set; replayed samples require the retained subset. The checked public HIR-16K artifact is a prompt-and-constraint release, not a release of policy rollouts, selected/rejected replay candidates, rewritten prompts, entropy values, training-step rewards, or checkpoint-linked replay buffers.
