Claim: correlation with GPT-4 is not enough to establish grounded-QA evaluator reliability. GroUSE holds the question or reference situation fixed while injecting specific answer and document defects, then compares frameworks on 144 labeled unit tests. The paper reports that current RAG frameworks miss important failure modes even when GPT-4 is the judge; closed models perform strongly on GroUSE while state-of-the-art open judges do not generalize to the proposed criteria. Distilling GPT-4 reasoning traces into Llama-3 improves both correlation and calibration. This supports unit-test meta-evaluation, not a claim that one rubric covers all RAG deployments.
