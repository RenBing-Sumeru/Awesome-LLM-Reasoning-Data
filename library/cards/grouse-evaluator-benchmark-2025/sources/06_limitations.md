The 144 hand-curated tests operationalize a selected taxonomy, so unseen domains, document structures, languages, and interaction patterns can contain failures outside its oracle. A judge that passes controlled cases may still fail under distribution shift or adversarial prompting. The proposed pipeline also depends on a powerful closed judge and its rubric interpretation. Reuse should add task-specific counterfactuals and measure annotator agreement rather than treating GroUSE performance as a deployment certification.
