RAGAS-like approaches score broad attributes, and existing judge studies often compare model outputs with aggregate human or GPT-4 agreement. GroUSE changes the evidence object to minimal, labeled counterfactual tests that isolate failures such as superfluous facts and adversarial abstention. This makes calibration and discriminative capability auditable at the criterion level. The use of GPT-4 or Llama distillation is not new; the contribution is the grounded-QA failure taxonomy, unit-test oracle, and their use to expose misleading evaluator correlations.
