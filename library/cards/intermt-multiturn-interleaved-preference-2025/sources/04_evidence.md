Claim: present MLLM judges do not reliably recover human judgments over multi-turn multimodal interaction. On InterMT-Bench, the paper evaluates scoring, pair comparison, and crucial-step recognition; reported score-evaluation Pearson correlations are low across the listed models, while the paper reports near-human 4.38/5 performance for crucial-step recognition. This supports the need for human-annotated local/global supervision, not a claim that every MLLM judgment is unreliable.
