The largest audit gap is release scope. The repository contains reports and successful proof artifacts, not the RL corpus, model checkpoint, failed/refined trajectories, compiler traces, self-summaries, conjecture/lemma pools, judge scores, LooKeng, or Seed-Geometry data and engine. Apache-2.0 for the repository does not establish rights for hidden in-house data, third-party formalizations, generated variants, or the 230M geometry corpus.

Training/evaluation contamination is unresolved. Training draws on open and in-house formalizations; past-IMO mostly adapts Compfiles/MiniF2F, MiniF2F is separately evaluated, and proposer variants can create near duplicates without parent-child splits. MiniCTX-v2's post-November-2024 authorship date is only a partial mitigation because model cutoffs and later RL membership are unknown.

Search compute is not normalized: light is approximately Pass@64–256 over 1–2 hours, but medium outer limits and heavy total calls, tokens, hardware, concurrency, timeouts, and stopping rules are missing. Heavy's LLM relevance judge is an opaque learned selector even though final Lean proofs are formally checked. IMO 2025 also depends on human formalization and Seed1.6-Thinking priors, with 4/6—not 5/6—completed during competition.
