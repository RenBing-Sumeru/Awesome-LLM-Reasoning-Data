Seed-Prover's contribution is not merely a stronger final prover. It exposes a coherent data-and-search abstraction in which lemma decomposition, failed proof states, compiler messages, summaries, dependencies, and verified terminal outcomes can become training or inference records. Medium search turns local failures into reusable proved lemmas; heavy search turns proposer conjectures into a growing, formally filtered fact library.

The report also links formal theorem proving to large-scale synthetic geometry generation. Seed-Geometry combines a 230M-problem generator, auxiliary-action policy, and fast symbolic forward chaining, while explicitly reporting that an explored value model was removed because its errors harmed wide search. The novelty is operationally informative, although the key datasets and systems remain closed.
