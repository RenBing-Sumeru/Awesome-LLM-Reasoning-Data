This report is useful for designing RLVR records that preserve more than a scalar reward. A practical schema can retain statement, proof attempt, local lemmas, dependencies, compiler feedback, self-summary, environment version, terminal result, and search lineage. It also motivates curriculum controls based on empirical proof rate and explicit parent IDs for proposer-generated variants.

For evaluation, the paper is a warning to report budget-conditioned results: light, medium, and heavy should be separate settings with samples, refinements, tokens, hardware, wall time, and stop rules. For dataset construction, Seed-Geometry suggests a context-to-auxiliary-action corpus with symbolic verification, but the claimed corpus cannot be reused or audited until its records, generator, DSL, splits, and licenses are released.
