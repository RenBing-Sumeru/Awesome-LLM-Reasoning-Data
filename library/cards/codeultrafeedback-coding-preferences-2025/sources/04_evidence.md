The paper reports that GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 responses are generally preferred to open-weight-model responses in its dataset analysis. It also evaluates aligned CodeLlama-7B-Instruct models trained with supervised fine-tuning and RLAIF/DPO, reporting improved alignment with coding preferences and improved HumanEval+ functional correctness relative to the original instruct model. These are the paper’s reported experimental boundaries.

The official dataset viewer verifies the released structure: 10k training rows, five possible preference labels, four models, four responses, and four annotations per record. The evidence does not prove that GPT-3.5 ratings equal human coding preferences, nor that a model tuned on these instructions will be functionally correct or secure on unseen repositories, languages, or execution environments.
