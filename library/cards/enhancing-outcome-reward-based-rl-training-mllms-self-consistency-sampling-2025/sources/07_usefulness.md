Within Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data, SCS provides a concrete schema for multimodal counterfactual rollouts used during RL. A reusable item should link the original image and question to the initial trajectory, retained prefix length, every perturbed image or noise seed, continuation, extracted option, distinct-answer count, normalized consistency reward, accuracy and format rewards, optimizer group, model checkpoint, and final update.

Keeping these fields separate supports audits of whether gains come from more samples, image augmentation, the consistency formula, terminal verification, or the RL algorithm. It also prevents the final benchmark score from being copied backward as a label for intermediate reasoning. The official prompt release can seed reproduction, but the missing rollout and reward ledger limits direct reuse for verifier training or process supervision.
