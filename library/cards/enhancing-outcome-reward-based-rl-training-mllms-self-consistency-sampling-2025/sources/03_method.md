Training prompts are filtered to multiple-choice examples with associated images. Table 1 reports 7.8k M3CoT, 2.1k Geometry3K, and 6.2k retained ScienceQA items, approximately 16.1k examples in total. The official Qwen-format JSONL inspected in the Hugging Face release contains 16,180 rows, so the paper's rounded table and released file should be reconciled rather than treated as identical counts. Each released row stores a question, answer, and serialized multimodal message; it is prompt data, not a rollout/reward ledger.

During RL, the current policy samples an initial trajectory. SCS truncates it, perturbs the image independently for each continuation, resamples option answers, computes consistency from answer-set size, and adds accuracy and format rewards. For the Qwen2.5-VL-7B RLOO configuration, the appendix reports batch size 128, temperature 1, 16 samples per prompt, maximum generation length 3000, truncation ratio 0.8, and four resampled trajectories. GRPO uses ratio 0.4 and eight resamples; the two REINFORCE++ variants use ratio 0.8 with four or eight resamples. The paper evaluates Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct, and InternVL3-8B with a structured reasoning-and-answer output format.

The environment is OpenRLHF-based online multimodal RL. The terminal verifier checks option correctness and output format; SCS supplies an additional scalar from resampled answer agreement, not a step-level judge. The paper reports eight A800 GPUs and roughly 24 hours for experiments, while an appendix timing table describes an eight-A100 comparison of 12.5 hours versus 17.2 hours; hardware and run-scope differences need reconciliation. Official Apache-2.0 code, training scripts, evaluation code, and HF data are available, but exact image archive contents, data revisions, perturbation seeds, sampled rollouts, component rewards, checkpoints, and per-run manifests are not established as a complete replay package.
