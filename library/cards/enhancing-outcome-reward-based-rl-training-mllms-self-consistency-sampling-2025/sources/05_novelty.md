Plain outcome-reward RL assigns one terminal correctness signal and cannot distinguish a faithful solution from a lucky guess. Process reward models move feedback inside the trajectory but require a learned verifier and process labels. SCS occupies an intermediate point: it creates counterfactual continuations from a partial multimodal trajectory and uses answer diversity under truncation and visual perturbation as an online scalar reward.

The individual ingredients are not new: self-consistency, image augmentation, rejection/resampling, option correctness, RLOO, GRPO, and REINFORCE++ all predate the paper. The specific change is to turn stability across perturbed suffix rollouts into a training-time feedback contract for multimodal multiple-choice reasoning, while keeping the terminal outcome reward.

For this track, the direction signal is that online RL data may include a family of related trajectories rather than one response. The quality signal comes from controlled ablations and a small faithfulness audit, but benchmark gains do not validate the released prompt corpus. Reuse should separately verify prompt provenance, perturbation policy, truncation position, continuation diversity, component rewards, and optimizer configuration.
