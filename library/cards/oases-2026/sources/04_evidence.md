Under the reported common training/evaluation pipeline, OASES is compared with direct/RAG, agentic-search, and process-supervision baselines across five QA benchmarks. Main tables report EM and F1 gains, while ablations remove co-training, process rewards, or both and compare frozen evaluators, including larger models. Additional analyses vary process-reward weight, model scale, and evaluation-rollout overhead. These results support the claim that the proposed feedback placement and on-policy evaluator adaptation affect system performance.

They do not show that each retrieved document is relevant, each state answer is correct beyond the scorer, or each positive score delta identifies a causally useful search action. Benchmark gains and ablations are system evidence, not validation of an unreleased trace dataset.
