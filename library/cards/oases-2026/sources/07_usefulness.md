OASES provides a detailed schema for search-credit records. A reusable example should preserve question/reference and split, model and policy revision, every reasoning/search action, query, retrieved document ID and snapshot, prefix-state boundary, state-conditioned evaluation answer, normalization, EM/F1 score, consecutive delta, reward weight, token-level reward placement, format penalty, outcome score, GAE/advantage, and training-buffer membership. These fields enable audits of reward causality, evaluator drift, retrieval replay, and cost.

State scores and deltas should remain derived signals alongside raw retrieval and reference outcomes. The reported benchmark results motivate the co-training recipe; they do not certify trace correctness, evidence quality, or release completeness.
