RL training for search agents often scores only the final answer, leaving intermediate queries and retrieved evidence without credit assignment. Existing dense rewards may use heuristic information gain or a fixed evaluator whose judgments drift away from an evolving policy. OASES constructs a main multi-turn search trajectory plus one state-conditioned evaluation trajectory for every accumulated search state. The relevant data object links questions, queries, retrieval observations, prefix states, evaluation answers, state scores, score differences, final outcome scores, and PPO rewards. It is a training recipe, not a confirmed public rollout corpus.
