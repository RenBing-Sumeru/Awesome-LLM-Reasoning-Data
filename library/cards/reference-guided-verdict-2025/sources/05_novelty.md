Single-answer or pairwise LLM judging often lacks a gold-reference constraint and can expose one model's bias. This paper combines the input, candidate response, and reference answer in a binary judge prompt, then uses a heterogeneous three-judge majority rather than one verdict. Multi-judge aggregation and reference-guided scoring are not individually new; the contribution is their evaluation for free-form QA with human-majority comparison.
