The source is Big-Math-RL-Verified, described by the paper as more than 250,000 mathematical problems from nine source families with reference answers and 64-response Llama-3.1-8B solve rates. For the offline audit, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B generates four responses per problem at temperature 1, top-p 1, and context length 32,768. The pipeline retains responses containing a boxed answer that Prime marks incorrect.

Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct in non-thinking mode and Grok-3-Mini-High in thinking mode independently re-judge retained pairs. Real false negatives require unanimous correctness. A manual audit of 200 consensus-positive examples finds 199 correct, one false positive, and three source questions with incorrect ground truths. For synthetic augmentation, Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct generates at least five equivalent-answer variants spanning formatting, notation, approximation, and expression changes; generated candidates are re-annotated before use.

The paper reports a combined pool of 638K question/reference/model-answer/LLM-label instances, but that pool is not released. The public balanced SFT artifact has exactly 159,136 train rows: 79,568 True and 79,568 False targets, 139,432 real and 19,704 synthetic. Each row preserves a source index, question, reference, model answer, Prime-negative flag, one LLM label and rationale, synthetic flag, and a two-turn conversation. It does not preserve both Qwen and Grok judgments, disagreement cases, rejected synthetic variants, prompt revisions, or balancing seed.

TinyV fully fine-tunes Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct for two epochs with learning rate 1e-5, AdamW, cosine decay, warmup 0.1, eight devices, per-device batch 8, gradient accumulation 8, effective batch 512, and paper maximum length 4,096. The model emits True or False. In the released add-on reward code, Prime acceptance gives terminal reward 1. After Prime rejection, TinyV's parsed 0/1 output is multiplied by tinyv_weight, default 1.0, and the reward manager writes the score at the final valid response token. Invalid output or exhausted inference retries maps to 0.

For the main GRPO experiment, the paper selects 5,000 difficult Big-Math prompts from the stated solve-rate bands, uses Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen2.5-Math-7B, batch 128, eight rollouts, mini-batch 64, learning rate 1e-6, KL coefficient 0.001, and 12 epochs. The public hard-prompt dataset contains 7,009 train rows, all Prime-negative and LLM-positive, with four output scores per row; the exact experimental 5,000 IDs and random seed are absent.

HardVerify-Math releases 250 train rows with paired false-negative and true-negative outputs. Its internal field divides 125 benchmark and 125 Big-Math records. The paper describes the benchmark half as 115 Olympiad plus 10 MATH questions, while public statistics show 110 Olympiad plus 15 Math_500. This unresolved difference must be retained when binding paper claims to the artifact.
