The prior-work baseline is nearby benchmark and evaluation-surface datasets that share the same feedback contract. The change introduced by MuSiQue: Multihop Questions via Single-hop Question Composition is the particular task object and feedback contract: multi-hop question, answer, supporting paragraphs, and decomposition information; Benchmark size: 约 25K 个由单跳问题组合而成的多跳问题。 scored by answer EM/F1 and support evidence metrics under official settings. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.00573; acl: https://aclanthology.org/2022.tacl-1.31/; project: https://github.com/stonybrooknlp/musique; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.00573), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
