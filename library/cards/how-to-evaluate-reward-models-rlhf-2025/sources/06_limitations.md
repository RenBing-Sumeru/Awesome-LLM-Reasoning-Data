First, labels in PPE still depend on references, tests, humans, or model judges, so omissions and bias become reward blind spots. Second, although the release contains mappings across twelve domains, twelve proxy-metric families, and real post-RLHF human win rates, coverage of tasks, languages, model sources, and hard cases remains limited, and public release creates contamination risk. Third, offline scores do not automatically translate into RL, search, or deployment gains. Reuse should audit problem-level deduplication, agreement, false positives, unseen-model generalization, licensing, and category-level results.
