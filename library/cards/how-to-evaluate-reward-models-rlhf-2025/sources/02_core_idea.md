The central artifact is PPE, containing mappings across twelve domains, twelve proxy-metric families, and real post-RLHF human win rates. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use offline proxy evaluation, full RLHF, and crowdsourced human comparison for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is predicting the downstream training value of candidate reward models.
