Use this paper when repeated decoding is already available and the practical question is which prompts deserve more samples. Benchmark a calibration-aware policy against fixed self-consistency at the same average and tail latency, logging each prompt’s confidence, sample count, final answer, and correctness. The paper is not a recipe for solving a task with no repeatable answer signal, nor does it replace a verifier for settings where answer agreement is a poor proxy for validity. Its value is to turn an otherwise hidden allocation decision into something measurable.
