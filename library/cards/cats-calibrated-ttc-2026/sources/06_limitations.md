The approach is only as good as the relationship between agreement and correctness in its deployment distribution. A model can be confidently and consistently wrong, calibration can shift across domains or prompts, and an early-stopping policy can systematically underinvest in rare hard problems. The reported average sample budget also needs a tail analysis: a system may look efficient on average while placing most latency on a small subset of inputs. Reuse should re-estimate calibration on the intended model, task family, and decoding settings.
