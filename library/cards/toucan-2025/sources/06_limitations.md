The main limitations are audit and replay boundaries, not merely model accuracy.

- **No general success predicate.** The mixed contract checks structure, some tool-response validity, target-tool compliance, and judge-rated quality. It can retain a plausible final answer whose tool output or synthesis is factually wrong. Target-tool matching uses substrings and can collide between similar names or reward seeded-tool compliance over an alternative valid workflow.
- **Truncated failure spectrum.** Partial failures can remain when at least one call succeeds, and irrelevance examples deliberately retain zero calls. Ordinary no-tool, all-tool-failure, assistant-error-marker, empty-final, parsing, and other rejected cases are absent. No machine-readable rejection ledger or stage-wise yield is released.
- **Judge uncertainty.** Kimi-K2 and GPT-OSS-120B may encode model, style, and domain biases. A 50-example task-judge comparison with correlation 0.264 does not provide per-dimension agreement, false-positive/false-negative rates, adversarial robustness, or calibration of the response judge.
- **Version and lineage gaps.** The paper is arXiv v1 from 2025-10-01; the dataset snapshot checked is HF revision `0df3cf3` from 2025-10-04; the code snapshot is GitHub commit `a1976de` from 2025-12-16 with no tag or release. No manifest binds paper, code, dependencies, model endpoints, MCP metadata, dataset files, or SFT checkpoints. Per-row generator, framework, decoding, seed, retries, filters, server/source commit, endpoint revision, and timestamp are unknown.
- **Replay gap.** Historical messages and observations are downloadable, but 495 community MCP services, dependencies, credentials/configuration, mutable world state, reset semantics, endpoint versions, and response fixtures are not frozen. The paper says collection occurred in 2025-06, while the HF card says 2025-06 through 2025-09; per-row timing cannot resolve this discrepancy.
- **Split and contamination gaps.** Every config is train-only. No systematic decontamination, near-duplicate accounting, or overlap audit is disclosed for BFCL V3, tau-bench, tau2-bench, MCP-Universe, earlier tool-use corpora, or model pretraining data.
- **Rights and privacy gaps.** HF declares Apache-2.0 and the code repository MIT, but no per-server/tool license and attribution manifest establishes rights for community specifications, live tool outputs, embedded third-party content, or generated trajectories. The HF card reports best-effort rule-based PII replacement, without scanner rules, measured recall/precision, consent analysis, a redaction manifest, or residual-content audit.
- **Count interpretation.** HF's 1,646,546 stored rows include the 119,287-row SFT subset. Treating all stored rows as independent episodes overstates unique full trajectories by exactly that subset; the correct full total is 1,527,259.

These gaps make the release useful for SFT and historical offline study, but they prevent claims of exact environment replay, complete component-level rights clearance, population-level failure coverage, or leakage-safe benchmark evaluation.
