For the assigned **Environment and Agent Trajectory Data** track, TOUCAN is useful because it exposes the episode boundary from task and tool declarations through assistant actions, live observations, final response, selection annotations, and provenance metadata. It supports comparisons among single-server, multi-server, irrelevance, diversified, and multi-turn constructions, and it makes the distinction between downloadable historical traces and replayable environments concrete.

Its demonstrated training use is **SFT only**. The 119,287-row configuration can be used for tool-agent supervised fine-tuning or distillation-style imitation after row-count, rights, privacy, and contamination checks. The paper does not support relabeling the corpus for RLVR, online environment RL, reward-model training, preference optimization, or process-reward-model training; multi-dimensional judge annotations are filtering signals, not a disclosed scalar reward.

The richer full configurations are also useful for non-training work: analyzing tool-call sequences, partial tool failures, judge/rule disagreement, target-tool compliance, domain mixture, and the information lost in the SFT projection. These are audit and construction studies rather than additional entries in `training_use`.

A careful reuser should pin HF revision `0df3cf3`, keep the 119,287 SFT rows linked to the 1,527,259 full trajectories, explicitly preserve `subset_name`, tool schemas, messages, assessments, and MCP metadata when available, and isolate irrelevance from ordinary episodes. High-assurance reuse additionally requires a record-level lineage manifest, frozen MCP replay fixtures or a clear offline-only designation, an accepted/rejected ledger, duplicate and benchmark-overlap checks, per-component rights review, and documented PII scanning. Benchmark gains should be independently reproduced without treating them as a substitute for these data checks.
