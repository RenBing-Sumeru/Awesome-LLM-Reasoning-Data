The release evidence supports the corpus accounting. At HF revision `0df3cf37f2abefb380370cfb02eabea2a35ae782`, Kimi-K2 has 518,516 rows, OSS 457,130, and Qwen3 551,613; these full configurations sum exactly to the paper's **1,527,259 trajectories**. HF reports **1,646,546 stored rows** only because the same release also hosts the **119,287-row SFT subset** as a fourth configuration. Adding that subset to the full configurations double-counts selected rows and must not be reported as 1,646,546 unique trajectories. All four configurations expose only a `train` split.

The paper reports 567,262 multi-turn conversations. Its SFT subset comprises approximately 28.3K original, 40K irrelevance, 15.8K diversified, and 35.2K multi-turn examples, matching the hosted count after exact row accounting. The richer full schemas expose question and response assessments plus metadata; the six-column SFT schema omits those fields, so downstream users cannot recover every selection signal from the training view alone.

The task judge is only lightly calibrated: the reported Kimi-K2 selection comparison uses 50 human-annotated examples and Pearson correlation 0.264. Public code makes the rule layer auditable and the HF card clarifies that partial tool failures may remain, but neither fact measures end-task correctness. Substring-based target-tool matching is also a weaker signal than semantic or environment-state verification.

Downstream SFT results are positive but should be read narrowly. BFCL V3 overall scores change from 55.10 to 58.26 for Qwen2.5-7B, 57.69 to 65.09 for 14B, and 61.73 to 70.45 for 32B. The paper also reports average gains on tau-bench and tau2-bench and a better model-size/OSR frontier on MCP-Universe. For Qwen2.5-14B, progressive BFCL mixtures score 60.16 for single, 64.74 after irrelevance, 64.56 after diversification, and 65.09 after multi-turn, so intermediate additions are not uniformly monotonic. These author-reported evaluations support the SFT recipe's utility in tested settings; they are not proof that the released data are clean, correctly judged, uncontaminated, licensed at component level, or replayable.
