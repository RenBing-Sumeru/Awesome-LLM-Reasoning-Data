TOUCAN's direction signal is the combination of scale, real MCP execution, heterogeneous community services, explicit task and trajectory review, and a released SFT projection. It operationalizes the path from machine-readable tool specifications to full state-action-observation episodes rather than stopping at static function-call examples or simulated tool outputs. Three extensions also make abstention/irrelevance, persona/constraint variation, and multi-turn interaction part of the same construction system.

The most reusable quality signal is not the phrase “real-world” or a benchmark score. It is the visible feedback contract: Kimi-K2 task dimensions, auditable structural rules, target-tool coverage/order, GPT-OSS-120B response dimensions, and explicit thresholds for the 119,287-row SFT subset. The full configurations preserve these assessments and MCP metadata, allowing offline analysis that the reduced SFT view alone cannot support.

What is not new should remain explicit. Synthetic task generation, LLM-as-judge filtering, supervised fine-tuning, tool-call templates, and benchmark evaluation are established ingredients. Real service calls are not a novel correctness proof, and the paper does not supply a general reward or replayable environment package. The contribution is therefore best treated as a large integrated construction-and-release recipe, not as evidence that mixed heuristics solve agent verification.

Reuse checks should compare source-server and task overlap, tool-name collision behavior, successful versus rejected trajectory retention, and whether downstream claims survive fixed unique-row accounting. The 119,287 SFT rows must remain linked to, rather than counted in addition to, the 1,527,259 full trajectories.
