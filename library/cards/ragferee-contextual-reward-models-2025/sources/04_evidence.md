Claim: small, RAG-specific data can outperform much larger general reward corpora for contextual judging. On the RAGferee test set, the reported 24B Mistral-Small discriminative model reaches 81.8% consistent accuracy with 4K pairs, while removing grounding lowers it to 51.4%; the paper also reports up to +15.5 absolute improvement on ContextualJudgeBench over existing 70B+ reward models. The evidence is specific to its benchmarks and supplied contexts.
