OffsetBias supplies counterexamples in which the rejected answer has an attractive but misleading form—such as excess length, invented concrete detail, or an off-topic continuation—while the chosen answer follows the instruction. Its 8,504 instruction/good/bad triplets are constructed to teach a judge or reward model that critical errors override such cues; the contract is deliberately authored correctness within adversarial pairs, not a broad human-preference distribution.
