Claim: hard counterexamples improve judge robustness rather than merely fitting ordinary preferences. The paper trains matched LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct judges with and without OffsetBias; difficulty filtering removes about 60% of generated candidates and leaves 8,504 instances. On the separate 80-case EVALBIASBENCH and other reported benchmarks, the supplemented judge improves over the human-preference-only counterpart. The result is bounded by the six designed bias families and the paper's evaluator settings.
