The authors do not propose a remedy for the diagnosed limitations, so the paper cannot establish which data, objective, or training protocol would restore general judge behavior. Their bias audit uses LLMBar as a broad test but does not separately analyze position, verbosity, and other fine-grained biases; a deployment audit should measure the biases relevant to its own interface. They also omit human inspection from the meta-evaluation, so claims about agreement with human judgment remain indirect. The evidence covers four released judges and the stated prompt adaptations, not all model scales or evaluation domains.

Before reuse, verify the exact checkpoints, prompts, scoring thresholds, and benchmark versions, because these choices define the measured transfer boundary.
