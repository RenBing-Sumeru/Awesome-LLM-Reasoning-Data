An evaluation team considering an open judge can run the repository’s cross-scheme and benchmark tests with its own prompts, answers, and target criteria, producing a transfer and reliability report rather than relying on native-test accuracy. A researcher fine-tuning a reward or judge model can add adversarial pairs such as LLMBar and aspect-specific factuality, toxicity, and safety checks; success requires preserving accuracy when the format and criterion change. The workflow is not sufficient when the target application has no labeled or auditable evaluation set, because the paper’s diagnosis depends on controlled cross-validation and explicit test references.

For a production gate, record native and cross-format metrics separately and route a judge that fails transfer to human or stronger-model review instead of treating its single score as a universal confidence value.
