The raw sampling result establishes meaningful but unrealized scaling headroom. On GSM8K at 32 samples, COCONUT reaches a 44.96% Pass@N upper bound, yet confidence reranking remains at 31.92%, self-consistency at 34.04%, PRM hard-estimation reranking at 34.04%, and ORM at 32.68%. Thus the best listed reranker improves over one-sample accuracy only modestly and remains more than ten points below the answer-oracle bound.

The diagnostic results support the proposed bottleneck. On the constructed analysis set, PRM and ORM have F1 scores of 54.09% and 51.59%, with low precision and high expected calibration error. Correct and incorrect thoughts have nearly identical isotropy and sparsity statistics, and trajectory effects are generally small. After geometric regularization, Pass@32 rises from 44.96% to 48.22%, while PRM reranking rises from 34.04% to 34.57%; the improvement is real but also shows that better latent separability remains an open problem.
