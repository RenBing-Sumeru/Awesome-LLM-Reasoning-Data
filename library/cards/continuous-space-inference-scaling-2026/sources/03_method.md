Using COCONUT on GSM8K, the study creates diverse continuous trajectories through dropout sampling and evaluates Best-of-N from one to 32 samples. It adapts Math-Shepherd-style automatic annotation to train a step PRM and outcome reward model: five paths per training prompt, answer-based deduplication, and ten Monte Carlo completions per step yield 238,000 PRM and 324,000 ORM training records. Reranking is compared with model confidence and self-consistency on un-deduplicated test candidates.

To explain limited gains, the authors construct a 3,014-sample analysis set and evaluate reward classification, calibration, isotropy, sparsity, compactness, straightness, curvature, and local smoothness. They then perform dropout rollouts whose final answers are checked against ground truth and introduce a geometric regularization objective inspired by contrastive learning and verifiable-reward training. The intervention is evaluated by both Pass@N and PRM/ORM reranking before and after post-training.
