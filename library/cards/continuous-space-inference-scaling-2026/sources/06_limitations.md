The empirical study is centered on COCONUT and GSM8K, so it cannot establish that all latent-reasoning architectures share the same geometry or scaling limit. Automatic Math-Shepherd-style labels may be noisy when applied to non-textual thought, and the post-training intervention produces only modest reranking gains. Although code is released, reproducing the result requires substantial latent-model and reward-model training; the work diagnoses a limitation more convincingly than it supplies a universal remedy.
