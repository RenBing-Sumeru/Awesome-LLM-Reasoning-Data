Use this Card to audit what a closed frontier reasoning report actually reveals about post-training. It is useful for separating safety-evaluation feedback from a training reward, recording the disclosed filtering and alignment claims, and converting missing data, lineage, and reproducibility details into explicit follow-up requests.

It supports safety-alignment and evaluation analysis, not direct reuse of a dataset, verifier, RLVR objective, or agent-training environment. Comparisons with open releases should preserve that asymmetry: the report is evidence of a disclosure boundary, not evidence that the underlying recipe is available or independently reproducible.
