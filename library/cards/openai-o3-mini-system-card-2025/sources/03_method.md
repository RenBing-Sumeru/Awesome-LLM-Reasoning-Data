Section 2 of the System Card says that o3-mini was pre-trained on a mix of publicly available and custom in-house datasets. It reports quality and risk filtering, reduction of personal information, and use of the Moderation API and safety classifiers to prevent harmful or sensitive content. It also states that o-series reasoning models are trained with large-scale reinforcement learning and can produce a long chain of thought before answering.

The report describes deliberative alignment and says that updating refusal-policy format and generating new safety data formed part of this mitigation work. It distinguishes a near-final checkpoint from the launched checkpoint and says that the latter has small incremental post-training improvements. However, the base-model architecture, sources and quantities of safety data, trace generation, sampling, rollouts, RL objective, reward/verifier, optimizer, schedule, and retained record schema are unknown.

For evaluation rather than documented training, the report uses public and internally constructed tests, autograded refusal metrics, external red teaming, human/expert comparisons, and Preparedness evaluations. The official release also exposes low, medium, and high reasoning-effort options. Evaluation settings include browsing, code execution, prompting, custom training, and scaffolding in some tests; these settings do not disclose the post-training environment.
