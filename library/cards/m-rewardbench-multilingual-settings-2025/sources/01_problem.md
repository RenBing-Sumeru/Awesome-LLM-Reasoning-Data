Reward models trained in English may perform well on high-resource languages yet reverse their preferences because of word order, cultural expression, or semantic variation in low-resource languages. English-only accuracy cannot reveal whether the model learned response quality or merely relied on language-specific surface cues.

M-RewardBench constructs same-language and cross-language preference pairs in 23 languages, verifies them through human review and quality rules, and measures multilingual preference transfer.
