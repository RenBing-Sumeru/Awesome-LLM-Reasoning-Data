The main artifact is M-RewardBench, containing roughly 2.87K preference instances across 23 languages, including reasoning subsets. Each record organizes a task input, visual or environmental state, candidate response or trajectory, evaluation evidence, and a preference, score, success, or critique label, together with task provenance and capability categories. Quality control relies on constructing same-language or cross-language chosen–rejected pairs from multilingual instructions and responses and verifying them with human review and quality rules, making the release suitable for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than ordinary question answering.
