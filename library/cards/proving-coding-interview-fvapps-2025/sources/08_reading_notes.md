1. **One-sentence position:** FVAPPS converts 4,715 APPS problems into Lean 4 implementation-and-proof tasks, including 1,083 quality-controlled records.

2. **Method takeaway:** Python and API translation, `#eval` behavior checks, theorem generation, `sorry` holes, and Lean 4.12.0 kernel acceptance form the pipeline.

3. **Data takeaway:** Records may contain statements, function skeletons, examples, proof obligations, and quality tags; full and curated sets must be reported separately.

4. **Evidence anchor:** On 406 theorems from 100 tasks, Sonnet proves about 30% and Gemini about 18%, showing that proof completion remains difficult.

5. **Reuse decision:** It suits verified program and proof training. Weak theorems and `sorry` exploitation require counterexample audits and kernel-level scanning.
