The authors randomly sample 100 of the 4,715 tasks, containing 406 theorems, and compare proprietary models on direct proof completion. In a fixed Lean environment, Claude Sonnet proves about 30% and Gemini about 18%, showing executable but challenging tasks; failures include theorem misunderstanding, library retrieval, and non-compiling proofs. The 1,083 curated records provide a more reliable evaluation surface, but the paper does not report controlled external-benchmark gains after large-scale FVAPPS training. Evidence supports automated Lean evaluation of program proofs, not that all 4,715 theorems fully capture original APPS requirements, and 30% is proof-only rather than end-to-end program-plus-proof success.
