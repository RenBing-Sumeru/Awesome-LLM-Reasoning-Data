The authors generate chain-of-thought trajectories from a target LLM, divide them into reasoning steps, and obtain step-correctness labels either from DeepSeek-R1 or from the target model itself. The target LLM remains frozen. ReProbe extracts hidden states or attention-and-logit features from its generation, projects them, processes them with transformer layers, aggregates token features over each step, and trains a binary credibility classifier with class weighting.

At inference time the probe supplies a score for every candidate step. Those scores can replace a process reward model in step-level beam search or be aggregated to rank complete trajectories in Best-of-N selection. The paper reports training on 10.8K PRM800K prompts with three generated trajectories per prompt, and evaluates across mathematics, planning, and general-knowledge question answering, making the test-time decision record explicit: candidate step, internal signal, probe score, retained branch, and final answer.
