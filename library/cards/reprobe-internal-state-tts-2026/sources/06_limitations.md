ReProbe requires access to internal states, attention, or logits, so it is not directly applicable to closed APIs that expose only text. Its step labels may inherit systematic errors from a larger annotator or from self-annotation, and the definition of a reasoning “step” can be fragile for native thinking models or unconstrained prose. Although the probe is small, extracting and retaining the required generator features still has a serving cost. Cross-domain results are encouraging, but each new model family should be audited for calibration, feature availability, and whether probe confidence tracks true future answer quality.
