The paper converts verifiable reasoning into verifiable judging through its V2V strategy to build Libra Bench, then uses related data with rejection sampling, SFT, and rule-reward RL to train thinking-capable Libra-RM models. Libra Bench contains 3,740 English records from 204 MATH-500 Level-5, AIME 2024, and AIME 2025 problems and five reasoning models. Fields include question, response, binary label, generator model, reference, subset, and response_with_cot; the release uses the MIT licence.
