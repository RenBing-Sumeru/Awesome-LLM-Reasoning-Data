On Libra Bench, non-thinking models obtain roughly 55.1%–69.1% accuracy while thinking models reach 73.7%–78.7%, showing that the difficult responses separate judging capability. The paper also reports correlation with downstream BoN/RL selection and state-of-the-art results for Libra-RM on several RM benchmarks. These results support the central claim only under the released data, model scales, candidate budgets, and evaluation protocols; cross-domain use requires new validation.
