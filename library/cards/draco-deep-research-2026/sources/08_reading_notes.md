1. **Position:**  Build a cross-domain, international deep-research benchmark from real requests.
2. **Method:**  De-identified sampling, complexity filtering, four-dimensional rubrics, and scoring.
3. **Data:**  Ten domains, sources from forty countries, and task-specific criteria.
4. **Evidence:**  Systems trade off accuracy, completeness, objectivity, and citations.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for in-the-wild research judges; audit platform bias.
