1. **Sample real requests:** De-identified queries are sampled from large-scale deep-research usage to preserve realistic demand.

2. **Filter and augment:** Private, simple, and non-objectively evaluable queries are removed, and complexity and cross-source requirements are added.

3. **Curate rubrics:** Experts author task-specific criteria for accuracy, completeness, objective presentation, and citations.

4. **Standardised scoring:** Agent reports are judged criterion by criterion and aggregated across four dimensions; web time, judge, and search budget must be fixed.
