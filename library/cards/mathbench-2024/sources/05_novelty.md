Prior baselines such as GSM8K, MATH, and broad evaluation suites usually emphasize problem solving or aggregate math scores. MathBench changes the object by adding bilingual stage taxonomy and an explicit theory/application split.

The direction signal is diagnostic evaluation: it can identify whether a model fails at foundational knowledge, applied reasoning, language transfer, or higher-stage topics. The quality signal is the released data, leaderboard, OpenCompass integration, and semi-automated item screening.

What is not new: multiple-choice scoring, CoT prompting, and math benchmark construction are not novel by themselves. Before reuse, inspect license terms, release version, possible public-data contamination, answer-key quality, CE implementation, and whether converting open-ended items to multiple-choice changes the reasoning target.
