**Tasks and sampling.** The main Tongyi evaluation contains MedQA 1,273; MMLU-medical 544; MedMCQA 2,089; MedXpertQA Text 2,450; HLE-149 149; and Medbullets5op 298, totaling 6,803. The shortest half of MMLU-medical and MedMCQA is removed to reduce fact-recall-only items. Multiple-choice tasks use accuracy; HLE-149 follows its standard LLM-judge protocol. Commercial-agent checks use the first 100 MedQA questions, and Valyu is additionally run on 100 PubMedQA questions.

**Trajectory generation.** Tongyi-DeepResearch-30B-A3B produces fully inspectable reasoning, Search, Visit, intermediate-summary/prediction, and final-answer traces using Serper and Jina in English. Qwen3-30B-A3B and Tongyi with Search disabled establish base/agent ablations. Step, Gemini, and Valyu expose only limited predictions, summaries, and visited URLs, so their leakage analysis relies on content matching plus manual verification.

**Detection and feedback.** BML is regex-based, QCL is lexical-overlap-based, and EAL is an LLM judgment conditioned on the question, gold answer, and web content. Human validation reports EAL precision/recall of 100%/83.33% on the first 160 Medbullets5op questions; MedQA detected cases have 94.87% precision, with recall unavailable. Final correctness and event-aligned intermediate correctness supply the outcome signal; no optimization reward is introduced.

**Retention and replay.** The paper says results are released through an anonymous 4open.science URL, but that endpoint returned HTTP 401 during review. The Card therefore cannot verify trace schema, failures, blocked pages, no-prediction turns, tool errors, timestamps, or retention completeness. Replay would require archived rankings/snippets/pages, search/browser/model versions, prompts, budgets, seeds, and detector configurations; question IDs alone are insufficient.
