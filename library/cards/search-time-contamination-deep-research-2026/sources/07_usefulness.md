For the Environment & Agent Trajectory Data track, this work supplies a practical audit schema for search episodes: preserve each query, ranked URL/snippet list, visited page snapshot, extracted evidence, intermediate prediction, contamination label, final answer, and correctness. Such records support provenance checks, event-aligned prediction analysis, retrieval-policy comparison, detector benchmarking, and sandbox evaluation.

A robust collection should retain successes and failures alike: correct/incorrect/no-prediction turns, blocked or changed pages, tool errors, timeouts, unmatched detector cases, and human overrides. It should pin benchmark version and access policy, search/browser/model versions, prompts, budgets, seeds, timestamps, page hashes or licensed snapshots, detector regexes/thresholds/judge prompts, and all final-verifier outputs.

The appropriate `training_use` is audit. Training on leaked questions, answers, or detector-target artifacts and then reporting the same benchmark would compound contamination. Legitimate derivative uses include training provenance classifiers on licensed/redacted evidence, evaluating retrieval filters, and testing policies inside a controlled corpus with held-out private/dynamic questions. Raw live-web traces are not automatically licensable or privacy-safe training data.
