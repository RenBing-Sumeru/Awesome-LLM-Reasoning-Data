The contribution is a visual, vertical, verifiable benchmark of 300 hand-crafted multimodal browsing questions with public-search evidence and process-level evaluation. The core mechanism is to require cross-modal multi-hop search, annotate pivotal sub-goals and trajectories, and score both final success and sub-goal completion. Closest comparisons include BrowseComp, BrowseComp-VL, MM-BrowseComp, and MMSearch-Plus; BrowseComp-V3 adds visual evidence, public-search answerability, human-validated trajectories, and progress metrics. Direction label: multimodal agent-environment evaluation with process feedback.
