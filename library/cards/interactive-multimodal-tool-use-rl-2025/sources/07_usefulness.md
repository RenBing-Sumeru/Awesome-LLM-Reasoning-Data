For `environment_agent_trajectory_data`, the paper is a concrete recipe for representing an episode as interleaved policy and environment sequences with task provenance, tool observations, simulated-user turns, per-turn judgments, terminal state-change verification, and an aggregated training reward. A new agent-RL pipeline can use that schema as a logging checklist even before reproducing TARL.

The method supports controlled experiments on reward granularity: terminal-only RL, direct turn-local placement, trajectory aggregation, and online judge intervention can be compared while keeping the environment and rollout policy fixed. The text/math mixture and the 30-step simplified-task GRPO warm-up also provide ablation targets for curriculum design, while the text-only versus speech-text result motivates modality-retention checks.

This record shape makes judge errors, verifier blind spots, reward hacking, overlap, and dropped failures inspectable.
