Hunyuan-MT asks how a compact open-weight model can cover broad multilingual translation, low-resource directions, terminology constraints, and slow-thinking candidate fusion. Its data objects span monolingual documents, parallel segments, instruction-translation pairs, synthetic teacher outputs, human-verified examples, GRPO candidates with scalar feedback, chain-of-thought records with process and final rewards, and six-candidate fusion records.

The lifecycle is unusually broad: general multilingual pretraining, MT-oriented continued pretraining, two-stage SFT, translation GRPO, and weak-to-strong Chimera GRPO. Yet disclosure is stage-level rather than record-level. Public weights and inference code do not include corpus manifests, filter decisions, reward implementation, rollout records, immutable splits, or paper-run lineage.

Several headline quantities are internally inconsistent. The 1.3T low-resource component is introduced under general pretraining but later credited in discussion of MT-oriented pretraining. The release also alternates among 33, 36, and 38 languages or language/dialect/script entries and between 7B branding and 8B metadata. This Card preserves all versions instead of choosing one.
