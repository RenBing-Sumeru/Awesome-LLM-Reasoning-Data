The contribution is a broad, difficult, expert-written academic benchmark released with public artifacts and a maintained evaluation pipeline. The core mechanism is to solicit original or non-trivial expert questions, require unambiguous answers, test difficulty against strong models, perform multi-stage review, and score closed-ended responses.

The evaluation surface covers 2,500 questions across over a hundred subjects; formats include exact-match and multiple-choice, with about 14% requiring image understanding. The feedback contract is mixed automatic grading: multiple-choice exact match plus exact-answer checking, sometimes requiring an LLM judge for structured comparison. Closest comparisons are MMLU, GPQA, FrontierMath, and other expert benchmarks; HLE's direction label is frontier academic evaluation with versioning and contamination risk.
