Humanity's Last Exam (HLE) is a 2026 Nature article with an arXiv preprint first posted in January 2025. The problem is benchmark saturation: many frontier models exceed 90% on older benchmarks such as MMLU, so those surfaces no longer separate expert-level academic capability well.

The data object is a closed-ended expert question with subject metadata, answer specification, solution rationale, contributor provenance, and sometimes an image. HLE is included as a frontier benchmark and contamination-audit surface, not as an open-ended reasoning judge or proof of general intelligence.
