1. Inputs: expert-submitted questions, answer specifications, detailed solutions, subject/category metadata, contributor affiliations, and optional image assets.
2. Filtering: submissions must be precise, closed-ended, solvable, original or non-trivial, and resistant to quick internet lookup.
3. Difficulty check: candidate questions are tested against frontier LLMs; easily answered items are rejected or revised.
4. Review: graduate-level reviewers and organizer/expert reviewers check quality, ambiguity, and adherence to criteria; a public review period supports corrections after release.
5. Scoring: model responses are graded by exact match or multiple-choice matching; exact-answer cases may require normalized comparison or an LLM judge.

Reproducibility requires pinning dataset version, public/private split, image assets, grader revision, judge model/prompt, prompting policy, tool access, calibration scoring, and leaderboard date.
