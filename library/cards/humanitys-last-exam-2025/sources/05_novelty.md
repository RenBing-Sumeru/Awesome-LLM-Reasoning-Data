Prior expert benchmarks already tested broad knowledge or hard science reasoning, but many were saturated, narrower, or easier to search. HLE changes the object by combining expert-contributed, non-searchable, closed-ended academic questions with broad subject coverage, multimodal items, calibration analysis, and public/private governance.

The direction signal is hard frontier measurement under a closed-ended feedback contract. The quality signal is expert provenance, multi-stage review, difficulty screening against strong models, and post-release correction workflow. What is not new: benchmark scoring, multiple-choice QA, short-answer exact matching, and model leaderboard reporting. Reuse checks should inspect dataset license, image rights, private-set policy, grader version, LLM-judge prompt/model, public contamination, and whether corrected versions invalidate earlier scores.
