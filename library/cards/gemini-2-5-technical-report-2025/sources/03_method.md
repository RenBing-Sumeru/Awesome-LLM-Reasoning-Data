The reported pre-training collection spans publicly available web documents, code, images, audio, and video, with a January 2025 cutoff for Gemini 2.5. Google says it improved filtering and deduplication. Its post-training data is described as carefully collected and vetted multimodal instruction-response pairs together with human-preference and tool-use data. The report does not identify source datasets, selected records, source shares, licenses, or the authorship and synthetic proportion of these examples.

Post-training is described across SFT, reward modeling, and RL. Google reports increased RL compute, verifiable rewards, and model-based generative rewards, with RL environments that require multi-step actions and tool use. The thinking mechanism is trained through RL to use extra inference compute; the model can decide how long to think, and a user can impose a budget. Flash-size and smaller models use distillation. Reward equations, model or human preference protocols, verifier code and calibration, teacher identity, tool environments, rollouts, sampling, and stage configurations remain unknown.

For evaluation contamination, Google reports n-gram, semantic-similarity, and model-based decontamination, complemented by internal non-public benchmarks. These are useful process disclosures, but no thresholds, benchmark memberships, decisions, or reproducible audit outputs are released.
