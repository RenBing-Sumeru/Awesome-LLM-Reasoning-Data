Gemini 2.5 Pro and Flash are sparse mixture-of-experts transformers with native text, vision, and audio inputs. The report positions the family as thinking models: reinforcement learning trains them to spend additional inference-time compute before responding, while Flash exposes a controllable thinking budget. The models support long context and tool use, so the report treats reasoning, multimodality, and agentic use as one deployment family rather than separate point solutions.

For the disclosure ledger, the important contribution is the named post-training interface. Google reports supervised fine-tuning, reward modeling, and reinforcement learning; it says quality is central across those stages, and that increased RL compute, verifiable rewards, and model-based generative rewards supported more complex multi-step, tool-use environments. This specifies a mixed feedback contract but not an executable recipe.
