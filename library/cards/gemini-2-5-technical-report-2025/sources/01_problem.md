Frontier technical reports can publish an appealing capability story without exposing the data, feedback, and audit artifacts needed to reproduce or assess their post-training pipeline. For reasoning systems, a high-level reference to reinforcement learning is not enough: the data sources, record formats, reward contract, tool environments, filtering, retained outputs, and decontamination evidence govern whether the claimed recipe is independently auditable.

Gemini 2.5 is valuable for Track 12 because Google’s official technical report makes unusually concrete disclosures about its broad pre-training mixture, post-training data classes, SFT, reward modeling, reinforcement learning, verifiable and model-generated rewards, tool-use environments, and safety/privacy evaluation. At the same time, it withholds the instances, reward implementations, tool environments, mixture proportions, and reproducibility artifacts. This Card records both the disclosed interfaces and the remaining boundary; it is not a Track 8 scaling claim.
