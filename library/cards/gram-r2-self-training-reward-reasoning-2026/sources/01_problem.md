Ordinary preference data identifies which response is better but does not record the evidence, decisive differences, or how the final judgment was reached. Reward models may therefore memorize domain or formatting preferences and fail to explain or transfer their scoring criteria to new tasks.

GRAM-R² uses multi-round self-training to generate structured review traces containing feedback, comparison reasoning, and an A/B conclusion, filters them for consistency with the original preference label, and trains a generative foundation reward model.
