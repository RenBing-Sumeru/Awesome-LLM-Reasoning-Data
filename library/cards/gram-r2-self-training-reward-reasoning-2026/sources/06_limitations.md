First, the main risks are self-training bias, duplication, contamination, and unfaithful rationales; changing the model, domain, budget, or verifier may shift the labels, so reuse requires sampled human audits and agreement reporting. Second, automatic generation or rule-based verification usually covers observable outcomes and cannot guarantee that intermediate explanations are faithful, complete, and free of gaming. Third, source data may overlap with public benchmarks when deduplication and contamination procedures are incomplete.
