Prior methods rely on final answers, generic step labels, or a single LLM judge, leaving a mismatch between feedback and the target. This work centers supervision on prompts, candidate responses, feedback, comparisons, conclusions, and A/B labels, uses iteratively generating structured reviews and filtering them by preference-label consistency and format to generate and filter records, and releases GRAM-RR-TrainingData. The novelty lies in the data object and acceptance rule rather than standard training algorithms.
