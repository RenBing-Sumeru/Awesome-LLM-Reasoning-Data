C-Eval is a NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks paper and official benchmark release for evaluating foundation models in Chinese academic and professional exam settings. The gap is that English-centered benchmarks such as MMLU do not test Chinese subject knowledge, exam language, and culturally specific reasoning at comparable breadth.

The data object is a Chinese multiple-choice question with subject, difficulty level, options, and an answer key. The evaluation surface is static answer accuracy over 52 disciplines and four levels: middle school, high school, college, and professional. The decision boundary is benchmark/evaluation surface for Chinese knowledge and reasoning; it is not a process-trace dataset, a human preference dataset, or a training recipe. Its atlas value is a clean answer-level feedback contract with clear contamination risk.
