The prior-work baseline is English-heavy exam and knowledge benchmarks, especially MMLU-style multi-subject multiple choice. C-Eval changes the language context, subject taxonomy, and exam sources while preserving a simple answer-key scoring contract.

The direction signal is that foundation-model evaluation needs language- and education-system-specific benchmark surfaces, not only translations of English tasks. The quality signal is public paper, official website, GitHub evaluator, Hugging Face release, and explicit subject/level structure. What is not new: multiple-choice accuracy, prompt-based evaluation, and answer-key grading. Before reuse, inspect license, whether test labels are public, exact splits, prompt templates, answer normalization, subject imbalance, and contamination in Chinese benchmark training mixtures.
