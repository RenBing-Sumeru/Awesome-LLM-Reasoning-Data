The official System Card provides two different evidence layers. Its “Model Data and Training” section says training draws on publicly available internet information, information accessed through third parties, and information provided or generated by users, human trainers, and researchers. It describes filtering intended to maintain quality, reduce personal information, and exclude harmful or sensitive content through safety classifiers and a moderation service. Source identities, quantities, mixture weights, terms, thresholds, and filter yields remain unknown.

At the post-training level, the report says o-series models learn to reason through reinforcement learning, may refine strategies and recognize mistakes before answering, and use deliberative alignment to reason over safety specifications. The release page also describes tool use within the reasoning process. None of these statements exposes the training data object or feedback contract.

Safety and capability evaluations form a third, separate layer. Autograded refusal tests, Preparedness evaluations, external assessments, red teaming, and benchmark results measure model behavior under particular protocols. Unless the report explicitly connects one of them to optimization, it must not be recast as a training reward, verifier, or proof of training-data quality.
