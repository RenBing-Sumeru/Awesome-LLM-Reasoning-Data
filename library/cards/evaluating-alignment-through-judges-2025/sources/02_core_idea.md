AlignEval is a reusable benchmark built from an instruction, two responses, and a strong oracle’s preference label. The mechanism is to establish that models ranked by generating preferred answers are similarly ranked by agreement with the oracle as judges, then reuse filtered comparison instances to score new models without an online judge. The official repository releases code, prompts, data, and results; AlignEval has 2,671 retained instances in GPT-4o and Claude-3.7-Sonnet label variants.
