Earlier Lean provers used smaller public statement pools, private formalization sources, or reinforcement learning without releasing a comparable aligned corpus. Goedel-Prover changes the supervision pipeline by combining two formalization styles, a soft semantic screen, a hard Lean compiler predicate, and cumulative whole-proof expert iteration at million-statement scale. This is a direction signal because statement diversity and mechanically verified proof discovery become separate, auditable levers, while the public 29.7K proofs provide a concrete quality signal. Autoformalization, compiler checking, and expert iteration are not individually new; the contribution is their scale and composition, and the full 800K solved training pool described in the paper is not exposed as a single current dataset release.
