**Matched-volume scaling test.** On GSM8K and MATH subsets, the authors compare checkpoints after the same number of training steps. Numeric expansion raises Llama2 from 51.78 to 55.42 on GSM8K and from 22.78 to 23.98 on MATH; for CodeLlama it raises 62.02 to 63.38 and 30.86 to 31.32. This isolates programmatic expansion more cleanly than the full-corpus comparison, although it remains tied to two backbones and two source families.

**Numeric-consistency test.** On manually checked GSM8K+ and MATH+ variants, CodeLlama trained with InfinityMath attains 65.8% all-three-consistent among 1,022 GSM8K+ cases solved at least once and 42.8% among 1,985 MATH+ cases, versus 62.2% and 40.5% for MathInstruct(pot). The comparison directly probes stability under number changes, but all-three consistency is conditional on at least one correct response and does not measure arbitrary symbolic generalization.
