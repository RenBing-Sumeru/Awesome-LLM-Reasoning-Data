This Card treats Deep Research as a **partial disclosure ledger** for agentic browsing post-training. The report supplies a high-level task-and-feedback interface: objective auto-gradable tasks with ground-truth answers, open-ended tasks with grading rubrics, model responses, and a chain-of-thought model used to grade those responses during browsing-task RL.

That interface is not a released record schema. The card does not provide browser observations or actions, search or click logs, web snapshots, Python inputs and outputs, report/citation fields, task provenance, grader prompts, or the reward representation. The report-level disclosure supports an answer-level feedback claim, while trace, environment, and reward details remain unknown.
