For this atlas, the contribution is not an open browsing dataset or agent framework. Its value is a relatively direct frontier disclosure of the high-level post-training interface: browsing tasks, objective answers or rubrics, chain-of-thought grading during RL, and browsing-specific safety data.

The system card also makes an unusually concrete audit distinction around browsing contamination in CTF evaluation. Together, these statements allow the ledger to separate a named task-and-feedback contract from the undisclosed records, grader, reward, environment, and reproduction controls. It must not be read as a release of an RL trajectory dataset or a fully specified RLVR recipe.
