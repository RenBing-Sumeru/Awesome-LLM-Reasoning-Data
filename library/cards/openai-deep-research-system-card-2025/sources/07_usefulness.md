Use this Card to audit a concrete browsing-agent feedback boundary: which task and answer/rubric grading elements are officially named, which safety data and mitigation classes are named, and which records and controls are still unknown. It is useful for comparing frontier system-card disclosures without conflating a system's public browsing behavior with its private training data.

The Card supports Track 12 work on post-training feedback, web-browsing environments, contamination, and safety disclosure. It is not appropriate for direct reuse of data, trajectories, graders, or an RL recipe, because none of those artifacts are released or specified sufficiently for audit.
