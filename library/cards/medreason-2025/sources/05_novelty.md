HuatuoGPT-o1 and other medical CoT pipelines use a strong teacher or outcome filter to generate long explanations, but they do not explicitly anchor every sample in a medical knowledge path before writing. MedReason changes the rationale-construction input by mapping question and answer entities to PrimeKG, retrieving and pruning connecting paths, and giving those paths to the teacher. That makes factual guidance and path coverage inspectable data decisions before SFT. GPT-4o generation, shortest-path search, answer matching, and supervised fine-tuning are not individually new.
