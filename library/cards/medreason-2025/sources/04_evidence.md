The main data-utility comparison keeps each base model and SFT hyperparameters fixed while changing the training set. Llama-3.1-Instruct-8B rises from 45.8 to 51.2 average accuracy with MedReason (+5.4), versus 49.9 with Huatuo complex CoT (+4.1); Mistral-Instruct-7B rises from 36.1 to 44.7 (+8.6), versus 42.1 (+6.0) with Huatuo data (Table 2). This supports the released data as useful across two instruction backbones, but source mixture, KG grounding, teacher, and filtering change together, so the gain cannot isolate one component.

The filtering ablation uses Llama-3.1-Instruct-8B and compares generated rationales before and after answer-recovery selection. Mean accuracy over six medical benchmarks moves from 0.539 to 0.550 (+1.1 points), although MedXpert falls from 0.178 to 0.164 (Table 5). This supports answer recovery as a modest overall quality signal, not a universal improvement or proof that retained intermediate steps are clinically correct.
