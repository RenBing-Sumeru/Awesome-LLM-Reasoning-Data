No raw rollout corpus, immutable result manifest, page snapshot, document-level licence record, or trace-level attribution ledger is released. Live results change with query time, region, indexing, and page revisions. Bing/Jina failures, blocked pages, and fallback frequency are not quantified.

Reason-in-Documents can omit, distort, or synthesize claims, and the paper reports no human or citation-level audit of those blocks. Top-10 retrieval may miss necessary evidence. The 32,768-token cap and direct-reasoning fallback create distinct terminal paths that should not be conflated. Benchmark accuracy remains model-behavior evidence, not proof that intermediate data are clean or redistributable.
