The official paper evaluates five challenging reasoning surfaces—GPQA, MATH500, AMC2023, AIME2024, and an August-November 2024 LiveCodeBench slice—and six open-domain QA benchmarks: Natural Questions, TriviaQA, HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, MuSiQue, and Bamboogle. Comparisons include direct reasoning, standard RAG, an agentic RAG baseline, and Search-o1; the paper also varies retrieval depth.

These experiments support claims about the tested inference scaffold and the contribution of retrieval/refinement under stated settings. They are not record-level validation of retrieved pages or refined knowledge, and they do not establish a reusable dataset's coverage, rights, deduplication, or factual accuracy.
