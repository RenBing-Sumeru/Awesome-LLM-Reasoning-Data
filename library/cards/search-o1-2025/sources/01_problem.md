Search-o1 addresses reasoning tasks in which a long chain encounters facts absent from the model's parameters. The object relevant to this track is an inference episode, not a training corpus: task instruction and question, partial reasoning, a model-issued query, ranked web results and page content, a document-conditioned rewrite, resumed reasoning, and the final answer.

The paper asks how retrieval can be invoked at the point of uncertainty without flooding the reasoning context with long, noisy pages. The official project and repository provide a runnable scaffold; they do not establish a released, versioned collection of experimental rollouts.
