The GRM dataset contains task goals, observations or videos, actions, before/after states, progress scores, and positive or negative transitions. Successful, failed, and partial trajectories across robots and tasks are labeled through geometric progress, stage completion, rules, visual judging, or human checks. Robo-Dopamine-Bench is evaluation-only.

Each record should reconstruct the input, process state, judgment evidence, and acceptance result. The work organizes records around task goals, state transitions, actions, progress scores, and negative transitions, making the verifier, judge, environment, or execution source traceable for high-precision manipulation-trajectory selection and RL. Undisclosed licensing, audit coverage, format, and decontamination should remain unconfirmed.
