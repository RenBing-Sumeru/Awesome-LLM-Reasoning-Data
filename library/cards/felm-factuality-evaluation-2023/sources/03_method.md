1. **Sample questions:** Open-ended prompts from multiple domains are answered by several LLMs, preserving natural hallucinations and reasoning errors.
2. **Segment responses:** Answers are split into independently judgeable sentences or semantic segments so local errors are not hidden by response-level labels.
3. **Verify manually:** Annotators search for external evidence, label each segment’s factuality, and record error types and evidence URLs.
4. **Review quality:** Multiple review rounds and disagreement resolution improve consistency, producing 817 examples and 3,948 segments.
5. **Compare evaluators:** Direct prompting, retrieval augmentation, and chain-of-thought judging are tested with segment- and response-level metrics.
