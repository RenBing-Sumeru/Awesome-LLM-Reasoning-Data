Factual errors in open-ended LLM responses may occur in only one sentence or reasoning step. A single response-level correct-or-incorrect label cannot localize the problem or show whether an evaluator actually checked evidence. FELM studies how to build a fine-grained, multi-domain factuality benchmark and compares direct, retrieval-augmented, and chain-of-thought evaluation methods for segment-level error detection, classification, and evidence use.
