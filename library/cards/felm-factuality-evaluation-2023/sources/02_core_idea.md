The paper releases a factuality dataset with 817 examples and 3,948 text segments spanning world knowledge, science, mathematics, reasoning, writing, and related domains, with at least roughly 100 examples or 500 segments per domain. Annotators label factual errors at the segment level, categorize error types, and provide supporting or contradicting links. Vanilla, retrieval-augmented, and chain-of-thought evaluators are compared, showing that retrieval generally helps but even strong models remain unreliable on fine-grained factual errors.
