For the assigned **Data Construction & Open Release Recipes** track, OmniThought is useful as a worked example of turning a heterogeneous trace pool into several post-training data products while retaining some process metadata. It also shows why an open dataset can remain only partially auditable when source, verifier, and selection lineage are missing.

- **Nested-release processing:** Pin revision `274ec6417efb435421fb222319d1bb47291ea69b`, count outer questions and nested traces separately, preserve question grouping and teacher identity when flattening, and never treat the datasets-server's partial estimate as the official row total.
- **Conditional SFT/distillation:** Reconstruct row-level source and license information, rerun correctness checks, decontaminate against the target evaluation set, then compare random, RV-only, CD-only, and combined selection at matched problems and trace counts. Re-estimate `mu_CD` for the actual student rather than copying 5 or 7.
- **Preference construction:** Use within-problem pairs only with an explicit preference semantics. The paper's DPO pair means “preferred verbosity range versus maximum verbosity,” not “correct versus incorrect”; retain that label meaning in downstream schemas.
- **Reward-model/RL study:** Treat RV and CD as judge-dependent targets, validate them against humans and alternative judges, report reward weights and correlations with length/teacher/domain, and preserve rollout/verifier logs. Online RLVR requires rebuilding the executable accuracy contract.
- **Verifier audit:** Stratify samples by domain, teacher, RV, CD, and `thought_correctness_verify`; replay code tests and independently inspect math/science answers and intermediate reasoning to measure false accepts and false rejects.
- **Release engineering:** Add immutable prompt-source revisions, domain/license fields, generation job IDs, all candidates, filter decisions, raw/final RV components, selected-subset manifests, and checkpoint hashes.

Reuse class: the paper and Card are strong construction/audit references; the public dataset is conditionally suitable for SFT, distillation, preference construction, or reward modeling only after revision, rights, correctness, and contamination checks; it is not a clean evaluation set. EasyDistill can bootstrap loading, filtering, and training, but does not currently reproduce the complete construction pipeline. Benchmark gains are behavioral evidence under reported settings, not proof that every released trace is correct or reusable.
