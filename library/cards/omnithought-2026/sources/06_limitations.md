- **Source lineage:** The release schema has no prompt-source or domain field. Named source families and aggregate domains are known, but individual questions cannot be mapped back to an upstream revision or license from the released row alone.
- **Generation disclosure:** Teacher identity is retained per accepted trace, yet teacher proportions, requests per problem, sampling temperature/top-p, maximum output, seeds, retries, original candidate count, and rejected generations are unavailable.
- **Retention semantics:** The paper's validator separates reasoning validity and solution validity, but only an incorrect solution mandates removal. Consequently, answer-correct traces with locally invalid reasoning can remain. `thought_correctness_verify` is holistic and does not locate the first error.
- **Verifier replay:** Code tests, runner versions, sandbox, timeouts, math/science normalization rules, LLM-judge model settings, and per-candidate evidence are not released. False acceptance and rejection rates are unknown.
- **RV construction:** Final RV mixes a model judgment with token length. The release does not expose the raw judge component, normalized-length component, tokenizer, `L_min`, or `L_max`, so the displayed score cannot be recomputed exactly from the row.
- **CD/RV calibration:** QwQ-32B is both a central dataset annotator and the reference in the reported audit. Mean absolute differences below 2 do not provide exact agreement, per-level confusion, domain calibration, uncertainty, or evidence that the same ranges fit unrelated students.
- **Selection bias:** Randomly removing some traces below 3,000 tokens can suppress concise or easy examples. The removed fraction, seed, source distribution, and interaction with teacher style are undisclosed.
- **Release counting:** The pinned README declares 708,009 problem rows and the paper reports more than two million CoTs. The datasets-server is partial and its 552,229 estimate is not authoritative. Exact nested counts, duplicates, and per-problem multiplicities still require a complete snapshot audit.
- **Decontamination:** Normalized Indel and 10-gram methods are named, but thresholds, removed examples, normalization, generated-output screening, and residual-overlap measurements are absent.
- **Preference meaning:** DPO selects RV 3–5 and rejects maximum-RV traces. This operationalizes a verbosity preference, not human preference, factuality, or correctness; both sides may be answer-valid.
- **RL reproducibility:** The GRPO objective names format, accuracy, RV, and CD terms, but reward weights, reward-model training data details, rollout count, decoding, advantage settings, and executable verifier code are not fully reported.
- **Experimental attribution:** The 10K studies control the problem set, but main-model comparisons change model family, selected traces, capacity target, training budget, and possibly snapshot. Benchmark gains are not proof of data quality.
- **Rights and ethics:** Apache-2.0 repository metadata does not replace per-source terms. The paper also notes teacher-bias propagation and misleading reasoning in high-stakes deployment; privacy, source consent, and downstream safety require separate review.
- **Code completeness:** EasyDistill is official and supplies a ThoughtX guide, filter script, and example config, but the checked example is a 1.5B/4K scaffold and does not freeze the complete construction or reported 7B/32B runs.
- **Version boundary:** arXiv v1 describes two teachers and Qwen2.5 experiments, while the final ACL paper adds DeepSeek-R1-0528 and Qwen3 material. The ACL publication should be the primary recipe record; OmniThought-0528 remains a separate release.
