Read the assumptions before the curves. The key question is not whether a tournament sounds plausible, but whether the chosen model has a positive correct-generation probability and a pairwise comparison advantage on the target distribution. Then track the budget accounting: generation and comparison both consume calls, and league robustness normally requires more comparisons. Finally compare the method against majority vote and verifier-based best-of-N at equal end-to-end cost, because the theorems explain a specific comparison contract rather than free accuracy from extra samples.
