The guarantees depend on behavioral assumptions that can fail precisely on difficult or adversarial inputs: the base model must sometimes generate a correct solution, and its pairwise judgments must reliably favor correct candidates. Candidate outputs may be correlated, comparisons can be position-sensitive, and a large number of calls can be expensive or slow even when the call-count curve improves. Before reuse, estimate generation and comparison accuracy on representative inputs, randomize pair order, and report wall-clock, price, and call counts separately.
