Use this study as a baseline when a system can sample several candidate solutions but has no separately trained verifier. The minimal replay record is the prompt, every generated candidate, each pair and presentation order, comparison result, aggregation score, total calls, latency, final answer, and correctness. This makes it possible to audit whether improvement comes from generation coverage, comparison quality, or a hidden change in budget, and to decide whether knockout or league aggregation fits the available serving constraints.
