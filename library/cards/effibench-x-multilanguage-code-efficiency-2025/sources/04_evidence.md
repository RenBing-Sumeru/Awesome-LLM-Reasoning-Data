NeurIPS 2025 results show that models often produce correct code but remain consistently less efficient than humans; even the strongest Qwen3-32B solutions reach only about 62% of human efficiency on average. Language effects are substantial: models tend to do relatively better in Python, Ruby, and JavaScript and worse in Java, C++, and Go, with rankings changing across languages. This supports multilingual efficiency evaluation, although absolute runtime depends strongly on hardware and implementation details.
