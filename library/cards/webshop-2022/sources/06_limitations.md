Correctness is only relative to the stated contract: Programmatic product, attribute, option, and price matching computes the shopping reward for the selected item. Reward quality is bounded by product metadata and matching rules. Simulated shopping omits many live-store constraints, and public instructions or demonstrations can contaminate later training.

The paper should not be read as unrestricted real-world reliability. Public artifacts can become training data, service-backed environments can drift, and judge/evaluator implementations can change across releases.
