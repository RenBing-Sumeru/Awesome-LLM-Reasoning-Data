The method moves speculation from tokens alone to a two-level interface aligned with tree search: semantically variable-length thoughts are drafted and rejected as units, while rejected units are corrected by token-level speculative decoding. Its other distinctive element is the history-based, step-wise threshold intended to approximate the large model's expected PRM quality instead of rejecting a fixed fraction or using a fixed threshold. This differs from standard speculative decoding, which preserves a target token distribution, and from TreeBon's fixed-length sequences and preset rejection proportion. The contribution is an inference recipe and selection contract, not a new PRM, verified process labels, a trace dataset, or proof of final-answer correctness.
