The quality guarantee is conditional and narrower than the paper's empirical accuracy metric: it defines quality as expected PRM score and assumes independent normal thought-score distributions, a large-model mean no lower than the small model's, descending large-model quality across steps, bounded variance, and a threshold satisfying the stated condition. The estimator can fail when the PRM is fooled or poorly calibrated; the paper gives a GSM8K example where an incorrect fourth thought scores 0.8916015625 and later steps remain wrong. Accepted small-model thoughts also enter threshold estimation as approximate upper bounds, so evaluator error can propagate through history. Random-item manifests, seeds, decoding temperature, initial threshold, decontamination, raw trees, per-node decisions, and generated-trace licensing remain unknown; the repository exposes code but no licensed trace release or pinned experiment package.
