Tree-search reasoning expands many intermediate thoughts, and the paper reports that thought generation accounts for more than 91% of runtime in its measured beam-search and MCTS settings. Standard token-level speculative decoding can accelerate each completion, but it ignores the thought-as-node structure and still asks the large model to participate throughout search. SpecSearch addresses the narrower problem of accelerating node expansion while trying to retain the PRM-defined quality of a large-model thought generator. For this atlas, the relevant object is not a released dataset: it is the per-expansion record of candidate thoughts, PRM scores, a dynamic rejection threshold, accept-or-correct decisions, search state, and the final selected path.
