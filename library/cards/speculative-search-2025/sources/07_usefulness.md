For the rollout/search/test-time-trace track, SpecSearch supplies a concrete schema for recording accelerated search: retain the prompt and prefix, model pair, every draft, PRM identity and score, threshold before and after update, accept-or-correct flag, replacement thought, search depth and state, latency, selected path, and final-answer check. Such records could support selector audits, PRM calibration studies, latency-quality comparisons, or construction of trace-selection datasets. Reuse should preserve rejected candidates and distinguish three predicates: passing the dynamic score threshold, matching the large model's token distribution after correction, and producing a correct final answer. The paper supports test-time compute and evaluation uses only; it does not establish that unreleased traces are licensed, decontaminated, or suitable for SFT or RL training.
