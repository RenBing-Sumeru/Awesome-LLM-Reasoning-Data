The key distinction is between a score and a distribution: ZIP-RC needs the joint reward–length distribution to reason about the benefit of another sample and the cost of finishing it. Zero overhead applies to the additional prediction at inference, not to the required post-training or CPU search arithmetic. The most persuasive result is not a single accuracy number but the matched-cost Pareto behavior across three model sizes. Treat the ICLR status and public code as strong reproducibility signals, while still checking whether the reward verifier and vocabulary reservation are compatible with a new model family.
