The paper reserves a small slice of vocabulary logits and masks those tokens from normal generation. At every forward pass, the reserved logits parameterize a grid-shaped joint distribution over terminal reward and remaining generation length. The model therefore emits next-token probabilities and an auxiliary reward–cost forecast together, without a second model, an architectural branch, or an additional forward pass.

The forecast becomes a search control signal. ZIP-RC views parallel decoding as a prefix-tree meta-MDP, where a meta-action chooses which prefixes to extend, pause, prune, or duplicate. A sampling utility combines expected best reward with expected total compute and maximum sequential length; selecting the action with the best utility gives a tunable policy for accuracy, FLOPs, and wall-clock latency.
