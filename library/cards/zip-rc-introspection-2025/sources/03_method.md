For each on-policy rollout, the method labels every prefix with a verifier-derived terminal value and the number of tokens remaining to completion. It discretizes these two targets into reward and length bins, trains the reserved-logit grid with cross-entropy, and regularizes the generator toward a frozen copy of its original policy with KL divergence. The training corpus combines DeepScaleR, MATH training, and GSM8K training, producing roughly 100,000 labeled rollouts across the reported models.

At inference, the prefix-tree state stores all generated prefixes. The controller evaluates candidate multisets of prefixes with a utility based on the joint predictions, including planned per-prefix pruning horizons; it then extends only the chosen prefixes. Experiments use Qwen3-1.7B, LFM2-1.2B Math, and LFM2-350M Math on AIME 2024, AMC 2023, MATH-500, GSM8K, and a mixed-difficulty concatenation, comparing majority voting, length pruning, weighted best-of-N with external or self evaluation, and reward-only ZIP pruning.
