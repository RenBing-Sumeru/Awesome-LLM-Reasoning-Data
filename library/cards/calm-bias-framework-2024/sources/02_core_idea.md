The contribution is CALM, an automated bias-quantification framework that identifies 12 potential LLM-as-a-Judge biases and uses principle-guided modifications to test whether judge outputs stay stable when quality-relevant content should remain unchanged. The core mechanism is controlled perturbation plus before/after measurement of judge ratings or preferences.

The data object is not a final answer label; it is a paired or perturbed judge prompt with metadata describing the targeted bias. The feedback contract is the judge model's own output under each condition, transformed into a bias score by the CALM protocol. Closest comparisons include prior position-bias, length-bias, self-enhancement, and prompt-sensitivity studies of LLM judges. The direction label is benchmark-quality and reward-model audit for LLM-as-a-Judge.
