Correctness is relative to the assumption that perturbations preserve answer quality while changing only the targeted bias cue. That assumption can fail, especially for subtle wording, identity, reference, or style changes.

The framework measures judge instability, not the true social harm of a deployment setting. Bias scores depend on selected base tasks, perturbation generators, parser rules, judge temperature, API version, and aggregation formula. Closed models may change silently. Public bias probes can also become training data, reducing their value as fresh audits.
