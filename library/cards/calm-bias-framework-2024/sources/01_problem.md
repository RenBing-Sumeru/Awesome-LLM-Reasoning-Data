"Justice or Prejudice?" is an October 2024 arXiv preprint on bias in LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation. The problem is that LLM judges are used in benchmark scoring and even as reward sources, but their decisions can shift for reasons unrelated to answer quality, such as position, wording, or other presentation and identity cues.

The evaluation surface is a judge-evaluation item: task context, candidate outputs or score request, a principle-guided bias perturbation, the judge model's rating or preference, and a bias metric comparing original and perturbed conditions. The decision boundary is meta-evaluation of judge reliability; it is not a benchmark for candidate model capability and not a verified human-fairness dataset. Its atlas value is an explicit audit contract for when LLM judge scores should not be reused as reward or evaluation labels.
