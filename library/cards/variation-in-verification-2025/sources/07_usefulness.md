For Track 5, the release is useful for auditing test-time rollout and selection contracts rather than only final benchmark scores. A reuse pipeline can retain generator identity, benchmark and item ID, response index, sampling settings, candidate CoT, ground-truth provenance, verifier identity, verifier CoT, and verdict, then compare TPR, TNR, balanced accuracy, and retained-pool gain under controlled pairings.

It can support verifier diagnostics, generator-verifier allocation studies, filtering simulations, and error-taxonomy work. Any derivative training use should first resolve source rights, decontamination, label calibration, count discrepancies, and whether rejected and accepted candidates are represented as intended. The released traces should not be promoted as a training-quality dataset merely because the paper reports TTS gains.
