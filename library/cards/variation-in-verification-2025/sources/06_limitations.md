The experiment is built from benchmark subsets and model-generated responses, so its findings need not transfer to human responses, other prompts, interactive environments, or future model families. The balanced verifier pool deliberately alters natural class prevalence. Its conditional pass rate assumes uniform sampling from retained responses and falls back to the original pool when all are rejected; it is not a deployed selection rule such as Best-of-N or majority voting.

Labels can inherit errors from answer extraction, benchmark references, Math-Verify and other string matchers, or the GPT-4.1-mini/Qwen2.5-72B fallback. The verifier's own CoT is an explanation-like artifact, not evidence that its verdict was causally derived from faithful reasoning. Reported benchmark and TTS results therefore do not establish corpus or verifier quality.
