Embodied imitation data, simulator-generated tasks, rejection sampling, and reflection tuning each have precedents. The distinctive data contribution is their record-level linkage across three stages: simulator metadata creates constrained tasks and ordered key actions; GPT-4o supplies image-interleaved thought supervision; the current policy explores; an environmental checker retains successful episodes; and selected successes or failures become masked reflection/correction examples.

The recipe also separates two feedback roles that are often conflated. Stage 2 supplies an environmental acceptance contract over an entire rollout, while Stage 3 turns a synthesized first-error location into token-level supervision on a corrected suffix. Successes and failures therefore enter different downstream training sets instead of being reduced to one scalar score.

This linkage is useful but does not establish trace faithfulness. GPT-4o writes a thought while seeing the upcoming selected action, so the text can rationalize that action after the fact. Environmental success verifies task completion, not whether the written reasoning caused the action. The paper is therefore most novel as an open construction recipe connecting trace writing, verifier-based selection, and correction masking—not as proof that its chain-of-thought is causally faithful.
