The [formal ACL paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1910/) and appendix document the three stages, data counts, verifier contract, 809-case simulator evaluation, 25 ultra-long tasks, and 30-task human-mediated real-world evaluation. The [official project page](https://embodied-reasoner.github.io/) verifies the formal 14-author/9-affiliation record and reports the main results. The [official repository](https://github.com/zwq2018/embodied_reasoner) exposes task-generation and trajectory-generation code plus generic training/evaluation entry points. The [official dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zwq2018/embodied_reasoner) lists the 9,390 origin records, the matching dialogue conversion, images, 809-case test data, Apache-2.0 declaration, and a total size of 157 GB.

On the 809 simulator cases, the paper reports overall Success Rate / Search Efficiency / Task Completeness of 14.79 / 11.97 / 38.67 for the Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct base, 25.46 / 24.75 / 53.67 after Stage 1, 65.39 / 46.25 / 77.73 after Stage 2, and 80.96 / 55.07 / 86.30 after Stage 3. This ordered ablation is consistent with benefits from each training stage under the paper's setup. The final model's composite-task success is 54.29%, compared with 14.42% for GPT-4o, while its search-task success is 65.16%, below GPT-o3-mini's 78.57%; the authors attribute search weakness to over-exploration and missed nearby objects.

The reported real-world test contains 30 tasks across kitchen, bathroom, and bedroom scenes, with a human holding the camera and executing model commands. Embodied-Reasoner reaches 56.7% success versus 50.0% for GPT-o1, 44.0% for GPT-o3-mini, and 43.3% for Qwen2.5-VL-72B. These are small, human-mediated author results without uncertainty. Neither simulator nor real-world benchmark performance independently validates every synthetic thought, establishes a unique correct action path, or proves record-level release quality.
