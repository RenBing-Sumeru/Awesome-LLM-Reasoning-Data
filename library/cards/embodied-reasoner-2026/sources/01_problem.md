Embodied reasoning data must connect a task to egocentric observations, intermediate decisions, executable actions, environment transitions, and a terminal outcome. A terminal success flag alone cannot show whether the thoughts were visually grounded, whether an exploratory detour was necessary, or whether a correction label identifies the first causal error.

Embodied-Reasoner studies this construction problem in AI2-THOR. It combines GPT-4o-assisted imitation traces, policy-generated exploration accepted by simulator checks, and reflection/correction examples made from anomalous states or failed prefixes. Because the authors release the trajectory corpus and construction code, the relevant object is not just a trained model: it is an auditable chain from simulator metadata and task templates to image-text-action records, verifier decisions, and downstream SFT objectives.
