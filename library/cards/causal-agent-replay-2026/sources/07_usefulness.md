For `environment_agent_trajectory_data`, CAR is suitable for evaluation and audit only under the verified evidence. It can serve as a schema reference for recording `state_before`, typed action, observation provenance, final output, caller outcome, and root/branch lineage; as a replay audit for separating message reconstruction from action-match and live-environment fidelity; and as a controlled experiment harness for comparing contrastive point-of-commitment attribution with interaction-aware Shapley values on seeded, mocked SCMs.

A practical audit can record one factual run, verify request reconstruction, repeat deterministic policy calls to measure action-signature stability, branch each eligible step under a mocked or snapshot-safe Environment, retain every child outcome and failure, and report K, confidence intervals, abstentions, budget stops, and branch hashes. Outcome functions should be validated separately: prefer deterministic task rules where they cover the intended predicate, and calibrate or adversarially test any `JudgeOutcome`. Real tool execution should remain disabled until snapshot, reset, permission, timeout/retry, idempotency, and rollback contracts exist.

CAR is not established as safe training data. There is no released CAR corpus, split, license text, privacy policy, decontamination audit, or evidence of SFT, RLVR, reward-model, process-supervision, or policy training. Turning branches into training examples would require an immutable record manifest; task and environment versions; successful, failed, rejected, timed-out, and errored branch retention; train/evaluation separation; record-level provenance and redaction; compatible rights; and an explicit mapping from outcome effects to a training target.

For track-level comparison, pair CAR with Who&When Pro, AgenTracer, or TraceElephant to ask whether a failure label comes from static judgment, replayed terminal flips, or sampled counterfactual outcome distributions. Keep the repository-only Who&When extension as an audit example of surrogate replay under missing original policies, not as a CAR data release or proof of real-tool fidelity.
