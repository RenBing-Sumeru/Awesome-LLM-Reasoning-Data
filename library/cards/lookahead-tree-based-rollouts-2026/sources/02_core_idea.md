The contribution in one sentence is: replace eight independent stochastic RLVR rollouts with a width-eight branch–lookahead–prune generator that spends the same nominal response count on trajectories designed to remain distinct, then apply the same terminal verifier and group-relative optimizer.

LATR extends every active branch with its highest-probability token and may fork a child from a non-top token when that candidate clears an absolute probability threshold and remains close enough to the top token under a relative-gap threshold. A new branch is not accepted merely because its first token differs. The system simulates parent and child over specified windows and prunes a branch, together with its descendants, when the continuation remains insufficiently different. After the tree reaches the target width or the tree phase stops, ordinary stochastic sampling completes the surviving sequences; if too few survive, the implementation can pad the group by duplicating a survivor.

The feedback contract remains terminal and programmatic. Countdown computes \(0.1 I_{\mathrm{format}} + 0.9 I_{\mathrm{correct}}\): correctness additionally requires using every supplied number exactly once and evaluating to the target. Math uses reward 1 for an exact correct final answer and 0 otherwise after the paper’s upstream conversion to integer-answer problems. GRPO or DAPO compares these full-episode scalar rewards inside the group. The verifier can observe answer syntax and task-specific final correctness; it cannot establish that intermediate reasoning is valid, that two lexically different branches use different semantic strategies, or that a correct answer was reached faithfully.

The nearest baseline is ordinary stochastic sampling under the same policy-update method. LATR is explicitly orthogonal to GRPO and DAPO: the optimizer, terminal reward, and response-count budget remain comparable while the rollout worker changes. Random branching, removing or randomizing pruning, and token-level rather than trajectory-level lookahead are also evaluated as ablations and underperform the configured method. Relative to TreeRL-style search-to-process-supervision work, LATR retains answer-level terminal rewards and uses the tree to diversify the on-policy group rather than to derive step rewards.

The research direction is **search topology as a reasoning-data construction variable**. The paper’s score gains concern policy learning from differently constructed online groups; they do not prove that the public processed rows are high-quality trajectories, that edit distance measures reasoning diversity, or that LATR should be used for inference after training.
