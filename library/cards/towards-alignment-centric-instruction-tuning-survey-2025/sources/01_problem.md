Instruction tuning is used to align LLMs with human intentions, safety constraints, and domain requirements, but the data lineage is often compressed into a generic prompt–response table. The primary source is the arXiv preprint at https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17184, submitted in 2025 and revised on arXiv in November 2025. Its focus is instruction-data taxonomy: it maps a full instruction-tuning pipeline and routes readers to concrete demonstrations, distillation, self-improvement, and evaluation work rather than introducing one new dataset. The relevant data object is an instruction, a response whose origin must be known, and the data-construction, tuning, and evaluation context around that pair. Individual release claims still need their original source.
