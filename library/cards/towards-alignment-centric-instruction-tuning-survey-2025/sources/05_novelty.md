Instruction-tuning discussions often focus on optimization while leaving the origin of the response data implicit. This survey makes data origin a first-class alignment distinction and connects it to tuning and evaluation choices. Its direction signal is that expert demonstrations, teacher distillation, and self-improvement should not be given the same audit assumptions. It does not introduce instruction tuning, LoRA, or synthetic data. Before reuse, inspect each primary release for source provenance, filtering, safety claims, language coverage, licensing, and benchmark contamination controls.
