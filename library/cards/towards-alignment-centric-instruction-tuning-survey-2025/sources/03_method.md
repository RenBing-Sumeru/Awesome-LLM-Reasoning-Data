This is a survey rather than an implementation or a new data release. It synthesizes data collection methodologies, full-parameter and parameter-efficient fine-tuning strategies, and evaluation protocols. Within its data taxonomy, an instruction-response example may come from expert annotation, a larger-model distillation process, or self-improvement; the paper frames those sources as trade-offs rather than equivalent labels. The output is a pipeline map. Use it to classify instruction data and evaluation evidence, not as a recipe for SFT, distillation, or safety alignment. To reproduce a referenced approach, inspect the source dataset's collection protocol, response generator, filtering, split, license, and evaluation setup; the survey itself does not supply those release-level fields.
