1. **Position:**  Extract long instructions and atomic constraints from real agent applications.
2. **Method:**  Fifty applications, block extraction, cross-validation, human review, and multiple verifier types.
3. **Data:**  707 instructions and 8,415 constraints, averaging 1,723 words and 11.9 constraints.
4. **Evidence:**  Best models perfectly follow under 30%; conditional and tool constraints are hardest.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for agent-instruction judges; prefer deterministic checks for critical rules.
