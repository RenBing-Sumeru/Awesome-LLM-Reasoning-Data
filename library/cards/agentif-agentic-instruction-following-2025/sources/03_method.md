1. **Collect real applications:** System instructions, tool rules, role requirements, and workflows are extracted from open and industrial agents.

2. **Extract constraints blockwise:** Long instructions are segmented and atomic formatting, conditional, tool, prohibition, and cross-block requirements are extracted.

3. **Cross-validate and review:** Multiple extractors cross-check constraints, and humans correct omissions, duplicates, and unverifiable items.

4. **Configure verifiers:** Each criterion receives a code, LLM, or hybrid verifier; instructions, judges, tool simulations, and aggregation must be fixed.
