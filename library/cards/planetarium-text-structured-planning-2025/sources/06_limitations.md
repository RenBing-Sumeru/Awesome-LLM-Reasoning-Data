1. **Domain scope:** The current data covers only two classic domains and the STRIPS subset, excluding numeric fluents, temporal planning, and complex action effects.

2. **Template distribution:** Programmatically generated descriptions may retain learnable template cues, so high scores do not imply understanding of real user planning language.

3. **Equivalence complexity:** Object-mapping search grows with problem size, and behaviorally equivalent alternative domain encodings may not be accepted. Extensions require human semantic audits.
