Semantic similarity is not logical equivalence. A sentence encoder may ignore numbers or operators, prompting labels inherit judge-model errors, and consistency labels depend on the sampled continuation policy and thresholds. Agglomerative clustering makes irreversible grouping decisions; an incorrect merge can remove a genuinely distinct route, while an overly strict threshold leaves redundancy unchanged. Hyper-node aggregation can also conceal disagreement among member states.

TD(λ) trades variance for bias rather than producing ground truth, and averaging correlated verifiers does not remove shared errors. The reported compute metric is generated tokens, not full wall-clock or hardware cost; embedding, clustering, and parallel verifier overhead must be counted separately. Evaluation is limited to math benchmarks and specific model/search configurations. Complete rollout trees, rejected candidates, embedding supervision, reward targets, answer-normalization rules, decontamination records, licenses for derived artifacts, and reproducible per-run manifests remain unknown or incomplete.
