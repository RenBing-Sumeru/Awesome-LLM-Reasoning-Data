The embedding model can be post-trained from two kinds of state-pair supervision. A prompting route asks a general LLM whether two reasoning states are equivalent. A consistency route swaps states and compares their effect on sampled subsequent actions, then thresholds that difference into same/different labels. Standard cross-entropy training aligns cosine similarity with those labels. For GSM8K and GSM-Plus, the policy and value network are fine-tuned from Llama-3-8B on GSM8K; the value-network setup samples 16 initial-state rollouts per question at temperature 1.0. For MATH, the study uses a Mistral-7B-based policy and a PRM from prior work.

At inference, each expansion produces child states, their embeddings and verifier scores; clustering creates hyper-nodes, search chooses among hyper-nodes, and member states are expanded in verifier-score order. The paper evaluates four search algorithms on GSM8K, one sampled GSM-Plus variant per original case, and MATH500. A reproducible exported record would need the source split, prompt, full state text, parent/child edges, embedding-model revision, pair label or clustering distance, threshold, member list, raw and aggregate verifier scores, TD target provenance, expansion budget, decoding settings, and final answer. The official repository supplies code and an example JSONL, but complete trees, rejected states, state-pair training records, verifier targets, and per-run seeds are not confirmed as released.
