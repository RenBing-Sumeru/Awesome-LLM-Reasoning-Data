Verifier-guided tree search can spend substantial compute without proportionate gains. FETCH separates two failure modes: over-exploration, where independently sampled nodes express semantically equivalent reasoning and are expanded repeatedly; and under-exploration, where high-variance verifier scores cause frequent trajectory switching or trap search on a locally attractive branch. The relevant data object is a search trace containing a problem, partial reasoning states, expansions, semantic-equivalence decisions, verifier scores, cluster membership, and a final answer—not merely the benchmark question and answer. FETCH is a search framework; the paper and repository do not establish that complete rollout trees are released as a reusable corpus.
