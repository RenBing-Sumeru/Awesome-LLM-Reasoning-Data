The prior baseline is full MATH evaluation: 5,000 test problems with nontrivial grading cost and frequent reporting friction. MATH-500 changes the evaluation workflow by giving a fixed, smaller reporting subset tied to a public grader. The direction signal is practical comparability for math-verifier papers. The quality signal is its linkage to MATH plus OpenAI's released split. What is not new is the problem source, answer format, or grading philosophy. Reuse should inspect subset lineage, MATH license, grader exactness, answer extraction, contamination, and whether the reported solver used additional search.
