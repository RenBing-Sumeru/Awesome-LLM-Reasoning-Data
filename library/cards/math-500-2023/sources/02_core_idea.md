The contribution is the public identification of a 500-problem representative MATH test subset used in OpenAI's process-supervision study. The mechanism is subset selection, not new annotation: OpenAI reports choosing 500 problems uniformly at random from the MATH test remainder after excluding PRM test examples. The evaluation surface is final-answer correctness on those 500 problems. Closest comparisons are the full 5,000-problem MATH test split and other fixed math subsets. The direction label is benchmark/evaluation surface with contamination and scorer-version risk.
