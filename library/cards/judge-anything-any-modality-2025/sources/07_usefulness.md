The work can serve as a unified regression suite for omni-models and multimodal reward models: TaskAnything measures task capability, while JudgeAnything tests whether automated evaluation is trustworthy. The data can train criterion-conditioned judges that consume image, audio, and video, or analyze calibration differences between pairwise and scoring modes. Image-text products may use the relevant subset, whereas high-stakes review requires domain experts, verifiable metrics, and human arbitration.
