The paper introduces two complementary benchmarks. TaskAnything curates 1,500 queries from established datasets across 15 any-to-any modality categories to assess multimodal understanding and generation. JudgeAnything adds candidate outputs, human judgments, and detailed rubrics to evaluate five advanced MLLMs under pairwise comparison and score evaluation. The authors also release OmniArena, a unified platform for continuously evaluating omni-models and multimodal reward models.
