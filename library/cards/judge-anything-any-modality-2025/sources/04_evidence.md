Five advanced MLLMs are evaluated. On multimodal understanding, average human agreement is 66.55% for pairwise comparison and 42.79% for score evaluation; on multimodal generation, it falls to 53.37% and 30.05%, respectively. This gap shows that judging ability is substantially weaker than apparent task performance and exposes cross-modal bias and hallucination. The coverage of many modality combinations and two protocols supports the generality of the issue, although five models and 1,500 tasks cannot represent every emerging omni-model.
