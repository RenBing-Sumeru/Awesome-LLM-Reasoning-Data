Earlier MLLM-as-a-judge benchmarks mainly covered visual question answering, image captioning, or one output modality and could not compare cross-modal judging such as evaluating video from audio or text from video. This work unifies a capability benchmark, a judge benchmark, and a continual evaluation platform, allowing judges to inspect arbitrary input and output modalities. Human preferences, rubrics, and pairwise evaluation are not new; the main advance is modality coverage and a unified interface.
