1. **One-sentence positioning:** It extends MLLM-as-a-judge from image-text tasks to arbitrary input and output modalities.
2. **Method takeaway:** 1,500 tasks, 15 modality combinations, human judgments, detailed rubrics, and two evaluation protocols.
3. **Data takeaway:** TaskAnything measures task capability, JudgeAnything measures judging ability, and OmniArena supports continual evaluation.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Average judge agreement on multimodal generation is substantially lower than on understanding.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is useful for omni-modal evaluation and reward-model research, but closed-model rankings are not durable conclusions.
