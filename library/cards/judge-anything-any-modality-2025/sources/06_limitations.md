1. **Modality boundary:** The 15 categories still inherit existing benchmarks and cover streaming speech, very long video, and multi-file mixtures only weakly.
2. **Human-label cost:** Open-ended multimodal quality is subjective, and majority preference can hide cultural or use-case differences; annotator disagreement should be reported.
3. **Rapid model turnover:** Conclusions come from five models at one point in time, so the leaderboard can age quickly and needs hidden tests and continual additions.
4. **Judge accessibility:** Closed models differ in media-input restrictions, and protocol differences may be mistaken for capability differences.
