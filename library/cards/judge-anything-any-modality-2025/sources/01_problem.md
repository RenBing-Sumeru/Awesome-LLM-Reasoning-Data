Existing multimodal evaluation mainly focuses on image-text understanding and often assumes an image input with a textual output. It therefore cannot uniformly handle arbitrary combinations of audio, video, image, and text. Open-ended multimodal generation also lacks unique references, while text-only judges cannot inspect the original media and are vulnerable to cross-modal bias and hallucination. The paper asks both what current omni-models can do on any-to-any tasks and whether they can reliably judge such outputs like humans.
