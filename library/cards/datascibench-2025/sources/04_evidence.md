The arXiv abstract reports experiments on 6 API-based models, 8 open-source general models, and 9 open-source code-generation models. It reports that API-based models outperform open-source models on all metrics, and that Deepseek-Coder-33B-Instruct is the best open-source model among those evaluated. The decisive instance-level evidence is the Task-Function-Code score: an executed outcome is checked by a metric and programmatic rule tied to the task. The evidence boundary is that scores depend on execution environment, metric implementation, GT validation, prompt/scaffold, and dataset revision. Aggregate model ordering should not be reused without the official metric code and release version.
