On held-out LiveCodeBench, COBALT raises Pass@1 to 31.7 for R1-Distill 8B and 38.5 for Qwen3 8B, absolute gains of 9.0 and 6.2 over base models (Section 5). In the reward-hacking analysis, Qwen3 8B-FT-COBALT has 855 identified failures, reduced to 85 with perturbed trajectories; R1-Distill falls from 196 to 119 (Table 3). This supports robustness to the paper’s erroneous-feedback intervention, not immunity to arbitrary verifier errors.
