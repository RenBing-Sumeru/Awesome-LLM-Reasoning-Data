1. **Select papers:** Eighty-two Nature-family papers with comparable manuscripts and transparent review materials are collected across three scientific areas.
2. **Generate reviews:** Original human reviews are assembled, while systems powered by GPT-5.2, Gemini 3.0 Pro, and Claude Opus 4.5 produce AI reviews.
3. **Segment criticisms:** Long reviews are split into criticisms targeting one specific issue, anonymized, and randomized.
4. **Expert annotation:** Forty-five domain scientists rate each criticism for correctness, significance, and evidential sufficiency and record rationales.
5. **Aggregate audit:** AI and human reviewers are compared on composite quality, unique coverage, overlap, and 16 recurring AI weaknesses, followed by the release of PeerReviewBench.
