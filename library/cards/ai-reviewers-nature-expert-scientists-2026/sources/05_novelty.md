Earlier AI peer-review studies usually compare aggregate scores or acceptance decisions. This work decomposes reviews into atomic criticisms and has real domain scientists verify each one on three quality dimensions. It also measures both criticism quality and marginal coverage: a reviewer with lower average quality may still reveal important issues missed by others. Generating reviews with strong models is not new; the contribution is the high-cost expert dataset, fine-grained rubric, and human-AI complementarity analysis.
