The authors recruit 45 scientists in physical, biological, and health sciences, who spend 469 hours reviewing human- and AI-written reports for 82 Nature-family papers. They evaluate 2,960 issue-specific criticisms for correctness, significance, and sufficiency of evidence, then analyze reviewer coverage and recurring failure modes. The study also releases PeerReviewBench, turning the expert protocol into a reusable meta-evaluation resource for AI reviewing.
