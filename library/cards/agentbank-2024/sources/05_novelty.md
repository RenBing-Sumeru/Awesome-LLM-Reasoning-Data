AgentInstruct and Agent-FLAN already show that multi-turn agent traces can supervise open models. AGENT BANK changes the collection bottleneck by separating gold-action recovery from rationale writing and by reusing failures through answer forcing rather than discarding them. Its 16-task, 50k-scale release and explicit difficulty-bias analysis make it a direction signal for heterogeneous trajectory factories. GPT teachers, ReAct-style serialization, task environments, and masked causal SFT are inherited rather than new.
