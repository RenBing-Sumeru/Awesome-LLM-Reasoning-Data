The strongest evidence is a set of controlled accuracy comparisons under stated models and inference prompts. These are author-reported results; no independent reproduction or public corpus audit was verified.

- **In-domain utility.** With Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 and self-consistency inference, the unfine-tuned model scores 60.0, 38.2, 80.6, 56.2, and 80.8 on GSM8K, NumGLUE, ARC-Challenge, ReClor, and StrategyQA. ReGenesis scores 76.6, 74.7, 85.4, 70.6, and 91.3, an average absolute gain of 16.56 percentage points. STaR scores 66.0, 64.5, 84.1, 63.8, and 85.9 under the same evaluation column. (Table 2)
- **OOD utility.** For models fine-tuned separately on each of the five source datasets and evaluated on ASDiv, SVAMP, AQuA, BBH, ANLI, and OpenBookQA, the authors aggregate ReGenesis at about +6.1 percentage points relative to the unfine-tuned model; compared methods average about -4.6 points. The direction is not universal at every cell: NumGLUE-trained ReGenesis falls from 77.2 to 76.9 on ASDiv, and ReClor-trained ReGenesis falls from 77.2 to 76.4 on ASDiv, while most reported cells improve. (Paper §4.3, Table 3)
- **Component ablation.** After GSM8K training, the complete adaptation + structure + path pipeline scores 76.6 in-domain and 81.2/83.9/48.8/69.3/49.5/81.4 on the six OOD tests. Removing structure yields 66.2 and 76.1/80.3/47.6/53.4/42.1/75.6; removing adaptation yields 70.0 and 77.5/80.1/47.2/57.9/43.0/76.4. (Table 4)
- **Selection without reference answers.** Majority-filter ReGenesis scores 62.8 on GSM8K and 83.1 on StrategyQA, versus LMSI at 62.3/79.0 and the unfine-tuned model at 56.3/80.8. This is evidence that the scaffold can operate with agreement-based selection, but it covers two tasks and is below the main ground-truth-filter ReGenesis results of 76.6/91.3. (Tables 2 and 5)
- **Model transfer.** On 10,000 NumGLUE examples, Meta-Llama-3-8B-Instruct rises from 58.9 to 79.2 in-domain; STaR reaches 75.6. The authors report a 6.46-point mean gain across the six OOD tasks. (Paper §5.3, Table 6)
- **Hint placement.** When hints are given to all GSM8K training items, supplying them only at adaptation and structure stages scores 75.7 in-domain and beats supplying them at all three stages on five of six OOD tests; the respective vectors are 79.3/79.7/50.0/67.3/41.9/77.4 and 78.8/78.4/44.1/69.0/41.3/77.0. This supports withholding the answer from final path generation, but it does not isolate the main pipeline's failed-item-only retry. (Appendix Table 7)

The experiments establish downstream utility under these datasets and budgets. They do not establish that every selected path is stepwise valid, that the 25 guidelines cause transfer independently of selection and sample count, or that the unavailable generated corpus has auditable lineage.
