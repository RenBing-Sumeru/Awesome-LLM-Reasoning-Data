**Feedback limitation.** Exact final-answer agreement is an outcome check, not a process label. It can accept an invalid intermediate claim that happens to reach the expected answer and reject an equivalent answer whose formatting defeats extraction. The paper's own Figure 1 worked path says that working 10 hours past an 8-hour limit means “2 shifts,” yet continues to the correct wage; this illustrates the gap between terminal success and step validity. Majority vote replaces reference truth with sample agreement and can reinforce correlated errors.

**Hint and self-generation limitation.** The main retry withholds the answer from final path generation, but adapted guidelines and structures were generated with the answer and can carry answer-specific constraints into the path. Curator inference: this reduces direct leakage relative to STaR but does not eliminate post-hoc rationalization. Because the same model generates every intermediate object, systematic misconceptions and stylistic artifacts can propagate through the whole pipeline.

**Selection and data limitation.** Randomly retaining at most five passing paths does not explicitly optimize semantic diversity, minimality, factual correctness, or coverage. Failed paths, rejection reasons, per-path verifier outputs, and whether a record came from the hinted retry are not available in a verified release. Table 1 measures whether an instruction has at least one answer-correct path, not the fraction or quality distribution of all 25 paths.

**Experimental limitation.** The main results use five established answer-keyed training datasets and exact-match evaluation. OOD tasks remain adjacent academic reasoning benchmarks, and per-cell gains are not universal. The diversity analysis changes dataset composition within a fixed 7,000 examples but does not establish causal transfer to open-ended tasks. The strategy-clustering analysis uses Mistral as both generator-related model and judge, with no human agreement or external diversity metric.

**Reproducibility and governance limitation.** No official code, generated corpus, checkpoint, or method-specific project release was verified. Exact dataset revisions, item identifiers, decontamination checks, answer extractors, random seeds, sequence limits, total generation tokens, retry counts, and corpus license are unknown. OpenReview's CC BY 4.0 applies to the paper record, not automatically to derived data from five datasets and two model families. Benchmark performance cannot resolve these release and provenance risks.
