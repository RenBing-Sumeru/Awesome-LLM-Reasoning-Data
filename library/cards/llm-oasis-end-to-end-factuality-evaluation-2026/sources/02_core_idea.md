LLM-Oasis starts from 81,275 five-sentence Wikipedia passages, extracts 681,201 claims, and creates 81,275 factual and 81,275 unfactual texts, with several intermediate-task datasets. The public end-to-end gold split has 1,708 raw texts, and the claim-verification gold set has 2,656 examples. The suite supports claim extraction, falsification detection, and complete-text factuality classification.
