Existing factuality resources are often task-specific, small, or reduced to isolated claim verification, making them weak for training an evaluator that reads a complete text and judges end-to-end factual reliability. A few falsified claims can change a long text’s label, so shallow lexical patterns are insufficient.

LLM-Oasis extracts claims from Wikipedia, systematically falsifies some of them, generates paired long texts, and creates a human-validated gold test.
