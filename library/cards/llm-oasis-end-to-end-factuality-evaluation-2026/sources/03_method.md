1. **Extract claims:** Independent factual statements are extracted from five-sentence Wikipedia passages with page and evidence context.

2. **Falsify claims:** Entities, relations, numbers, or attributes are modified to create plausible but false claims.

3. **Generate paired texts:** Factual and unfactual long texts are generated from original and falsified claims to reduce copying shortcuts.

4. **Human validation:** Expert linguists validate pipeline quality and build blind gold sets; Wikipedia snapshot, generators, and licensing must be fixed.
