1. **Position:**  LLM-Oasis tests end-to-end factuality with paired factual and unfactual long texts.
2. **Method:**  Wikipedia claim extraction, falsification, paired generation, and human validation.
3. **Data:**  81,275 text pairs, 681,201 claims, and 1,708 end-to-end gold examples.
4. **Evidence:**  GPT-4o reaches only about 60% accuracy.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for whole-text evaluators; control synthetic artefacts and non-commercial licensing.
