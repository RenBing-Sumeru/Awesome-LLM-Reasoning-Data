Each particle represents a partial reasoning trajectory. The system extends particles with the language model, scores the new partial states with a process reward model, updates particle weights, and resamples a new population using a softmax distribution over those weights. It repeats this generation–scoring–resampling cycle until full solutions exist, then uses the reported final-answer selection protocol for comparison with other methods.

The paper also studies how the same framework distributes budget across iterations and parallel chains, including Particle Gibbs and parallel tempering variants. Partial trajectories, step rewards, normalized weights, resampling ancestry, and final answers are the operational records. Because generation and reward scoring can run across particles in parallel, the method makes the trade-off between more parallel candidates and deeper sequential refinement explicit.
