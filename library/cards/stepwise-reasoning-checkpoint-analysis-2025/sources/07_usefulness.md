Saving discarded branches is essential for diagnosing selection bias rather than only celebrating the final choice.

Compare ordinary beam/DVTS, ACS without CCA, CCA added to baseline searches, and a random-cluster control under equal N, M, PRM, prompt, and token/latency budgets. Evaluate answer accuracy, pass@k/answer-discovery, checkpoint-origin rate, cluster diversity, PRM calibration, truncation rate, and early-stop regret separately. SRCA's reported curves motivate these measurements; they do not dictate a general budget or threshold.

Treat its PRMs as external evaluated components, not as verified labels attached to an available dataset.
