The paper states two direct limitations. Precise step boundaries are difficult: Llama-style `### Step` structure is clearer than the conversational or unstructured output of some slow-thinking models. And CCA candidates often end with an incomplete reasoning path; they may still produce a correct answer, but are less interpretable and transparent than naturally completed chains. (Paper Limitations.)

The feedback layer adds further audit risks. PRM scores decide both which answer clusters survive and which truncated candidate wins. A PRM can be miscalibrated, sensitive to dialogue formatting, or favor a fluent but wrong endpoint. Using the last step score makes the PRM behave more like an outcome score; Appendix B.2 itself notes that correct paths may have imperfect intermediate reasoning. Exact equality clustering can also be brittle across equivalent numeric/expression formats. These are evidence-grounded curator inferences from the disclosed mechanism, not a measured SRCA error rate.

Reproduction and reuse remain limited. No official implementation, run configuration, checkpoint/cluster/score trace, raw result, model revision pin, or benchmark item manifest was verified. It is therefore impossible to independently check KV rollback, count checkpoint candidates, reproduce early stopping, inspect failed branches, or audit benchmark/PRM contamination from the public paper alone. Reported benchmark accuracy must not be used as proof that intermediate completions are safe process labels or suitable training data.
