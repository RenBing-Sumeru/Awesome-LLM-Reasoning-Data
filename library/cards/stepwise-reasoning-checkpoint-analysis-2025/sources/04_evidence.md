Under N=128 and M=4, Table 1 reports Llama-3.2-1B with the DeepSeek PRM at 85.60% GSM8K, 53.40% MATH500, 24.97% AIME, and 20.74% OlympiadBench for SRCA; the corresponding DVTS figures are 83.47%, 52.60%, 20.68%, and 19.40%. With the Skywork PRM, SRCA reports 85.97%, 65.20%, 39.71%, and 27.75%, versus DVTS 84.00%, 64.80%, 29.03%, and 25.82%. These are author-reported end-to-end accuracies for a fixed policy/PRM/scaffold, not isolated estimates of checkpoint quality. (Paper Table 1.)

The scale evidence is conditional on the same search setting. With DeepSeek PRM on MATH500, SRCA is reported at 51.2% with N=16 while DVTS is 49.8% with N=64. On AIME with Skywork PRM, SRCA at N=16 is 32.48%, above the best reported N=128 baseline, DVTS at 29.03%. The paper observes diminishing returns as N increases. It does not provide raw trials, uncertainty intervals, seeds, or a released trace ledger. (Paper §4.2.2, Fig. 2.)

For mechanism evidence, CCA is reported to account for about a 10% pass-rate improvement in the paper's ablation, and 19.07% of final answers originate from its expanded pool; checkpoint-answer rate ranges from 11.63% on GSM8K to 25.04% on OlympiadBench. Replacing ACS with a random cluster baseline at N=128/Skywork gives 83.78%, 65.00%, 28.62%, and 25.96%, below SRCA on the four datasets. At tau=0.95, early stopping reduces average reasoning steps by 27% while losing 0.58% accuracy in the stated experiment; low tau=0.5 loses 14% accuracy on MATH500. (Paper §5.2–§5.3, Table 3, Figs. 4–5.)
