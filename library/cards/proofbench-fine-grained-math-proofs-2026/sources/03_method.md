1. **Select proof problems:** Natural-language proof problems with authoritative references are selected from six advanced competitions.

2. **Generate model proofs:** Three strong reasoning models generate candidate proofs spanning correct, partial, and flawed solutions.

3. **Expert scoring and schemes:** Mathematics experts define key steps and points, score 435 proofs from zero to seven, and resolve disagreements.

4. **Train ProofGrader:** The judge reads the problem, reference, and marking scheme and aggregates five scores by median; sampling and aggregation must be fixed.
