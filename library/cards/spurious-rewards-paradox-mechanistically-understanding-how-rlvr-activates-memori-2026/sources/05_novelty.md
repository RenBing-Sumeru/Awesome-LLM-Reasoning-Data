Earlier work showed that spurious rewards can improve Qwen and contamination may explain it. This paper moves from black-box scores to a layerwise causal account: L18–20 trigger retrieval, L21+ adapt representations, and reset/keep-only/steering test the division.

Its novelty is a selective probe-gated counterfactual audit, not a new RLVR objective or data release: a suspicious success is flagged only if it disappears when the localized shortcut is suppressed.
