The contribution is an Anchor–Adapter account of shortcut retrieval: Qwen2.5-Math’s middle MLP layers L18–20 act as a Functional Anchor that triggers a memorized answer, while L21+ structurally adapts the residual stream. The Perplexity Paradox—falling answer perplexity with worsening prompt-side coherence—serves as its macroscopic signature.

The authors release analysis code, not a new dataset: training data and RLVR preparation are delegated to the earlier Spurious Rewards repository, and additional comparison checkpoints are announced but not yet public.
