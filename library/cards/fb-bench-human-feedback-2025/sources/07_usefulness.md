FB-Bench can evaluate feedback responsiveness in dialogue models, train revision models, or construct preference and process supervision over original answer, feedback, and revised answer. Its task, deficiency, and feedback labels support stratified sampling to identify which feedback types are most difficult. Training should preserve the original response and checklist rather than extracting only the corrected answer. Scaling the resource should mine natural feedback from real dialogue logs and use human verification to ensure that the feedback targets a verifiable deficiency.
