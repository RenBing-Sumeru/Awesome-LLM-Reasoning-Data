The final FB-Bench contains 591 Chinese real-use scenarios spanning eight task types, five types of deficiencies in the original answer, and nine forms of feedback. The data distinguishes two major interaction scenarios and provides a feedback-specific checklist for every revised response, requiring the judge to verify what must be corrected, which correct content should be retained, and which new problems must not be introduced. It evaluates feedback comprehension and revision rather than simple chosen–rejected preference, enabling diagnosis across correction, rebuttal, clarification, follow-up, and other feedback types.
