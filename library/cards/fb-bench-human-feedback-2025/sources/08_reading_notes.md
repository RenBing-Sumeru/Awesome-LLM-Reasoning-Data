1. **One-sentence positioning:** A benchmark testing whether models understand realistic Chinese feedback and effectively repair deficiencies in an earlier answer.
2. **Data takeaway:** 591 examples, eight tasks, five deficiency types, nine feedback types, and instance-level checklists.
3. **Method takeaway:** A deficient response and user feedback are fixed, and the second response is scored item by item.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Direct feedback is easier, while implicit hints, rebuttals, and feedback requiring renewed reasoning are harder.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is highly relevant to human-feedback judgment, though scale and the two-turn setting limit training coverage.
