- No paper-specific code, selected SFT/RFT corpus, online rollout data, HES score file, checkpoint, or immutable experiment manifest was verified. The paper names open-r1, verl, and upstream datasets, but does not publish the transformations that connect source records to reported subsets.
- The RFT/RL correctness contract is incomplete: answer extraction, normalization, tolerances, checker implementation, timeouts, exceptions, and per-candidate decisions are unknown. HES cannot replace this missing verifier because incorrect traces can score higher than correct traces.
- Relative HES is implementation-sensitive. Tokenizer and model revisions, vocabulary scope, precision, log base, special-token handling, percentile rounding for short responses, and tie-breaking are not specified completely. The top 0.5% rule also selects a length-dependent number of positions, and the long-trace ablation shows only a small highest-versus-lowest gap.
- The claim of zero extra online inference overhead means no extra model forward pass. Entropy is still an O(vocabulary) operation per token and may require logit retention, transfer, and aggregation. No measured wall-clock latency, memory, communication, energy, or monetary cost is reported; the proxy model's approximately 1/13 inference-cost claim is likewise not tied to a measured hardware accounting.
- Exact sampled IDs, upstream revisions, random seeds, stage-wise yields, train/evaluation overlap, and exact/near/semantic decontamination checks are undisclosed. LiveCodeBench's upstream contamination-resistant design does not audit overlap across this paper's full mixture.
- The empirical scope is dominated by mathematical reasoning, with one STEM and one code evaluation surface in the main cross-domain table. Results do not establish transfer to other tokenizers, languages, dialogue, agent trajectories, or non-verifiable domains. Reported evaluation samples multiple outputs, but training-run variance and independent seeds are not supplied.
- The paper itself leaves agentic planning untested, warns that HES may lose discriminative power when uncertainty is distributed throughout open-ended outputs, and computes HES only once before training instead of evaluating dynamic curricula or periodic rescoring.
- The paper reports upstream licenses, not a license for a paper-specific selected dataset because no such dataset was verified. OpenReview lists 8 authors, while arXiv v1 lists 9 and adds Fengbin Zhu; the records also differ in ordering. Citation and venue status must therefore be version-pinned, and the work should not be described as accepted at ICLR 2026.
