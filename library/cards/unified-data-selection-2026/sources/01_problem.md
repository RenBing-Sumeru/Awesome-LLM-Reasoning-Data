The official arXiv v1, dated 21 May 2026, and the OpenReview record for ICLR 2026 Submission 8937 are the primary sources for this Card. OpenReview still describes the work as a submission, while the later arXiv version is an under-review preprint; no acceptance record was verified.

Reasoning-data selection is commonly specialized to one training stage: an offline demonstration filter for supervised fine-tuning (SFT), a correctness gate and sampler for rejection fine-tuning (RFT), or a rollout/batch policy for reinforcement learning (RL). The paper asks whether a single model-intrinsic uncertainty statistic can rank useful full-response trajectories across all three settings without training a separate reward model or judge.

The data objects are not a newly released dataset. They are existing prompt-response demonstrations for SFT, model-generated candidate solutions for RFT, and successful/failed online rollouts for GRPO. Each response is augmented conceptually with token-distribution entropies and a response-level High-Entropy Sum (HES); RFT and RL additionally require an external answer-correctness outcome. This makes the paper relevant to `data_construction_open_release_recipes`: it specifies a selection recipe over reasoning traces, while the missing record manifests, implementation, verifier contract, and selected/rejected releases keep the Card at L3 rather than establishing an open, reproducible data release.
