Correctness is only final-answer correctness for controlled prompts. The benchmark does not prove whether a model read the whole context, used a faithful chain of reasoning, or would behave the same under different instructions. NaturalQuestions-derived QA can carry dataset-lineage and contamination risk, while generated KV instances depend on seed and key formatting. Scores can move with prompt template, decoding, context window implementation, answer normalization, and model version. Do not reuse the curves as training rewards without a separate feedback-contract audit.
