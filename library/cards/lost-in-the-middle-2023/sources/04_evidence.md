The paper reports the same qualitative failure across multi-document QA and synthetic key-value retrieval: models are strongest when relevant information appears near the beginning or end of the context and worse when it appears in the middle. The release exposes QA sweeps with 10/20/30-document contexts and KV sweeps such as 75/140/300 keys. Instance-level evidence is a known answer checked after moving the evidence position. The boundary is strict: aggregate curves diagnose position bias for the tested prompts, models, and scorer, not all uses of long-context models.
