The contribution is a position-controlled long-context benchmark that keeps the answer constant while moving the supporting evidence. The mechanism is simple but sharp: construct prompts with a relevant document or key-value pair at specified positions, run models at fixed context lengths, and score exact or normalized answer accuracy by position. The closest comparison is ordinary retrieval QA or long-context QA, which usually varies documents without isolating evidence location. The feedback contract is answer-level correctness against the known answer, so this is a benchmark/evaluation-surface card for position-bias auditing.
