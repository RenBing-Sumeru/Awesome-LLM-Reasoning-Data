Primary source: Liu et al., "Lost in the Middle," arXiv 2023 and TACL 2024. The paper asks whether long-context language models actually use a relevant fact when that fact is embedded at different positions in a long prompt. The decision boundary is diagnostic evaluation, not a training recipe and not a general long-context capability score. The evaluation surface is a controlled prompt plus a known answer: multi-document QA with one gold-bearing document among distractors, and synthetic key-value retrieval with a target key. Its atlas value is the position-bias coordinate: the same instance can be rescored after moving the evidence.
