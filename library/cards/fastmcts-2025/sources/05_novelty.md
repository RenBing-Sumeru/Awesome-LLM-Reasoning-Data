FastMCTS treats a search tree as an offline data-construction object, not merely an inference decoder. Its distinctive efficiency move is to cache every simulated continuation as step nodes, so later expansion reuses prefixes rather than regenerating full paths. Adaptive Stay and score-scaled exploration allocate the token budget across problem difficulty, while one retained tree supplies accepted SFT paths and heuristic step/branch preference pairs. The method therefore links token accounting, search topology, terminal verification, and downstream supervision. It does not introduce a new mathematical verifier, and its “step-level supervision” is derived from terminal outcomes and search coverage rather than direct step annotation.
