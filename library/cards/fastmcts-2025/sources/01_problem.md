Independent rejection sampling regenerates a complete solution for every attempt. Long shared prefixes consume tokens repeatedly, and the number of accepted paths concentrates on easy problems. A tree can reuse prefixes and redirect expansion toward uncertain or promising states, but ordinary MCTS simulation is expensive for language models and terminal success does not automatically reveal which intermediate step was sound. FastMCTS asks whether an MCTS-inspired construction substrate can produce more useful math traces under comparable generation-token budgets and turn its tree statistics into SFT and preference objects without a separately trained process reward model.
