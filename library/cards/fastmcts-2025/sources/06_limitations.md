The supervision contract is weaker than the label names suggest. The verifier prompt focuses on final-answer agreement, and the preference recipe assumes that a correct final result makes every preceding step correct. A zero-score node can be underexplored rather than wrong; shared generator/verifier family errors can survive unanimous judging; and unbounded consensus retries can bias cost and termination. Prefix reuse saves generation but may correlate trajectories and reduce diversity, which the authors identify as unresolved.

The data lineage is incomplete. Exact English proportions, Chinese web URLs and rights, item manifests, semantic decontamination, seeds, splits, and paper-run hashes are unavailable. The code repository has no license, uses unpinned dependencies, and its example settings do not reproduce the main paper configuration. No experimental trees, failed trajectories, rejected branches, synthesized SFT/preference datasets, or checkpoints are released. Finally, the evidence is math-only, the over-30% claim depends on the reported models and token budgets, and the 3.9-point headline combines FastMCTS SFT with Branch-DPO.
