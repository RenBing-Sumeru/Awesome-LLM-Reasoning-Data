The prior-work baseline is agent-environment, tool-use, and benchmark-harness papers in the same atlas category. The change introduced by Finance Agent Benchmark: Benchmarking LLMs on Real-world Financial Research Tasks is the particular task object and feedback contract: finance question, relevant filing or retrieval context, agent tool trajectory, final answer, gold answer or rubric-backed scoring result scored by expert-authored answer and official evaluator; exact v2 rubric and judge settings require page/version pinning. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00828; project: https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/fabv2), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
