1. **Task representativeness:** One hundred systems remain a research test set; high-dimensional, hybrid, noisy real control systems may be much harder.

2. **Formal-model assumption:** SMT results hold only for the specified dynamics and sets. Modeling error or unmodeled disturbances can invalidate real-world safety guarantees.

3. **Solver dependence:** Success depends on template spaces, timeouts, and solver versions. Reproduction must report encodings, seeds, retrieval corpora, and compute budgets.
