The official Polaris-Composition-1323K release contains about 1.323 million composed verifiable prompts. Each record combines source subproblems, a composition instruction, reference answers for each component, and a jointly judgeable output format. Final reward requires all subanswers to be returned under the contract and checked by their respective rules. The dataset automatically expands saturated prompt pools; its key artifacts are prompt composition and verifier composition, while model training demonstrates that the new prompts restore gradients.
