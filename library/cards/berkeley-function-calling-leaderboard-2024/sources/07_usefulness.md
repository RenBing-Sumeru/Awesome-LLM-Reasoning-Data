BFCL is useful as a schema for tool-call evaluation records: store prompt, available schemas, expected call, predicted call, parse errors, executor result, category, turn state, and evaluator version. It is also a reference for separating syntactic validity from tool choice and execution correctness. For the atlas, BFCL supplies a concrete environmental feedback contract for function-calling models and a checklist for comparing agent/tool benchmarks without flattening them into one overall score.
