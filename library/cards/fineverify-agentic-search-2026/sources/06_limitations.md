FineVerify inherits the model's ability to decompose the question and to judge each sub-question, so a missing requirement or correlated self-verification error can still select a polished but wrong trajectory. Its cost includes more than trajectory count: decomposition and per-candidate, per-sub-question verification can become expensive on long multi-claim questions. Benchmark gains may also depend on retrieval access and answer extraction, and the paper's released artifacts should be checked for the exact prompts, search environment, and evaluation terms before production reuse.
