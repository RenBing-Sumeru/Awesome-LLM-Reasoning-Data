Across four benchmarks and two models, the paper reports consistent gains over its scaling baselines. With four sampled trajectories, FineVerify improves GPT-5-mini by 8.2 accuracy points and Gemini-3-flash by 5.6 percentage points on average. Those comparisons directly test the claim that a fine-grained selector can make a small trajectory budget more useful than simple aggregation.

The strongest illustrated result is on BrowseComp-Plus: with twelve sampled trajectories, FineVerify lets GPT-5-mini exceed the reported GPT-5 reference score. The evidence is compelling for the stated benchmark and model APIs, but it is not a controlled statement about all web agents: retrieval conditions, decomposition quality, and model-judged verification can change together, so the released traces should be inspected when transferring the claim.
