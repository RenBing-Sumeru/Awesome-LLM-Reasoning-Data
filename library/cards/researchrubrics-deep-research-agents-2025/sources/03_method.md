1. **Select tasks:** Experts design open research questions across business, technology, science, and social analysis, excluding single-lookup tasks.

2. **Decompose rubrics:** Explicit requirements, implicit context, synthesis, citations, and communication are converted into 20–43 weighted criteria per prompt.

3. **Label complexity:** Tasks are graded for conceptual breadth, logical nesting, and exploration to control structural difficulty.

4. **Evaluate reports:** Humans or LLM judges assign criterion-level compliance; reproduction must fix retrieval cutoff, agent budget, judge, and web snapshot.
