The contribution is to provide a scale that is not fully pinned in local metadata and must be checked against the official release before quantitative reuse for legal-research, multi-hop-legal-qa under a reusable evaluation surface. The core mechanism is the pairing of task inputs and model outputs with the following feedback contract: legal expert answer or official Vals scoring rubric; exact judge and private-set policy are needs_audit.

The data object or environment is legal research setting with possible multi-hop source retrieval. The closest comparisons are agent-environment, tool-use, and benchmark-harness papers in the same atlas category. The direction label is benchmarks_evaluation_surfaces with a judgment_required, mixed feedback contract. The main thing to preserve is not only the headline score, but the task object, evaluator, split/version, and artifact lineage.
