Construction begins with decomposed questions from MuSiQue. GPT-4o is used to enrich them into coherent subquestion–answer pairs and to derive N candidate search queries per step. Queries are issued to M sources, with Google, Bing, and Wiki-18 given as examples; a query is retained only if it returns valid results in at least `ceil(M/2)` sources. The paper reports 60k filtered search keywords, while 19k MuSiQue-derived examples are used for RL training; the exact verified values of N and M, a complete release manifest, and record-level rejection logs remain unknown.

During rollout, Qwen2.5 3B or 7B Base/Instruct models follow a three-demonstration prompt and iterate through ``think``, ``search``, and externally supplied ``information`` blocks until producing ``answer`` or exhausting the action budget. Retrieved ``information`` tokens are masked from the optimization loss. The implementation builds on Search-R1 and verl. Training uses E5 retrieval over the synthesized corpus; evaluation augments the corpus with the 2018 Wikipedia dump and retrieves `k=3` documents. The appendix reports rollout temperature 1.0, `top_p=1.0`, KL coefficient `1e-3`, and PPO clipping ratio 0.2. The paper does not establish a frozen search-service snapshot, complete per-record reward logs, or a released replayable environment.
