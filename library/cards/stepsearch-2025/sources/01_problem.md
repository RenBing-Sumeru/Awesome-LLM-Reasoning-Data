StepSearch addresses sparse supervision in reinforcement-learning-based multi-hop search. A single terminal answer score cannot identify which intermediate query retrieved useful evidence, which repeated prior work, or where a trajectory failed. The relevant data objects are therefore two related but distinct records: a constructed MuSiQue-derived supervision record containing decomposed subquestions, subanswers, reference search keywords, and retrieved-document evidence; and an online rollout containing repeated ``think``, ``search``, and ``information`` rounds followed by ``answer``. The paper reports 60k filtered subquestion search keywords and uses 19k examples for training, but the accepted artifact record does not verify a downloadable corpus of the constructed records or raw RL rollouts. For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, the important unit is each search round together with its query, returned documents, accumulated retrieval history, step reward, and final outcome—not the benchmark score alone.
