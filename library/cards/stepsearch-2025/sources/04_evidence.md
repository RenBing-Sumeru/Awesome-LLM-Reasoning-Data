The paper evaluates Qwen2.5 3B and 7B Base/Instruct models on HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, MuSiQue, and Bamboogle using Exact Match and word-level F1, explicitly avoiding third-party LLM judges. It reports absolute improvements of 11.2 points for 3B and 4.2 points for 7B over search-RL baselines with 19k training examples. The Qwen2.5-7B-Base ablation provides more mechanism-specific evidence: removing the full step reward changes F1 from 0.493 to 0.475 on HotpotQA, 0.450 to 0.433 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, 0.324 to 0.283 on MuSiQue, and 0.573 to 0.485 on Bamboogle. Additional training curves and cases describe repetitive searches and reward collapse when only a global search-key score is used. These results support the usefulness of step-wise feedback within the reported setup; they do not verify the accuracy, completeness, provenance, or release quality of every constructed supervision record.
