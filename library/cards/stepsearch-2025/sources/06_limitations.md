The authors evaluate only text QA and Qwen2.5 models at 3B and 7B scale; behavior on multimodal tasks, other agent environments, or 14B/32B models is unknown. Training uses 19k MuSiQue-derived examples, so the paper does not establish how StepSearch compares with Search-R1 at matched data scale. Reward collapse and unstable training remain observed risks. The step reward depends on access to ground-truth documents and TF–IDF lexical similarity, which may not transfer to open-ended tasks without gold evidence and may reward surface overlap rather than factual utility. Construction also depends on GPT-4o and multiple search sources whose versions and result snapshots are not fully pinned. A downloadable trajectory corpus, rejected-query records, per-step reward logs, dataset license, decontamination procedure, and end-to-end replay protocol are not verified by the accepted artifact metadata. Public code and benchmark gains must not be treated as evidence that those data artifacts are reusable or individually correct.
