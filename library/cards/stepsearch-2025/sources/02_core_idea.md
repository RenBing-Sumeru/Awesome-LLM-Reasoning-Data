The contribution is StePPO, a PPO variant that places feedback at the search-round boundary instead of relying only on a terminal reward. Its global contract combines output-format enforcement, final-answer accuracy, and a search-key reward. For round t, the step reward is `r_step(t) = G_t - P_t`: information gain `G_t` measures the marginal improvement in TF–IDF cosine similarity between newly retrieved documents and the task’s ground-truth documents relative to the best match observed earlier, while redundancy penalty `P_t` is the fraction of current documents already present in retrieval history. The global reward is assigned at the final output position and the step reward at the final token of each search round. This is an automatically computed process signal grounded in gold-document coverage and retrieval overlap; it is not a human process annotation, learned judge, or proof that every selected query is semantically correct.
