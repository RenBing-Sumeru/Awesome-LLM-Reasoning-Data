For the rollout/search/test-time trace data track, StepSearch is useful as a schema and recipe example. A reusable record would preserve the original question, decomposed subquestions, candidate query, returned document identifiers or snapshots, retrieval-history state, information-gain term, redundancy term, total step reward, format and answer rewards, stopping reason, and final answer. Such records could support agent RL, process-reward ablations, verifier calibration, query-policy analysis, and comparisons between global and step-wise supervision. The paper supports agent training and test-time search research, but the Card does not claim that an independently reusable StepSearch trajectory dataset is currently verified.
