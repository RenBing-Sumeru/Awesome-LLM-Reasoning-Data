Relative to Search-R1-style search RL with primarily global outcome signals, StepSearch makes intermediate retrieval behavior directly optimizable: gold-document information gain and retrieval-history redundancy are converted into round-local token rewards. It also couples that reward contract to a MuSiQue-based pipeline for subquestion-aligned search keywords. For this atlas, the direction signal is the explicit linkage among a constructed reference object, an online search trajectory, a process reward, and a terminal QA outcome. PPO, MuSiQue, Qwen2.5, iterative ``search`` tool use, E5 retrieval, and final-answer rewards are reused components rather than new contributions. The claimed novelty should therefore be compared at the feedback placement and trace-field level, not inferred from final benchmark rank.
