**Release and replay.** Neither the ACL nor arXiv record links official code, data, or a project. There is no verifiable environment package, commit, checksum, reset contract, state serializer, seed manifest, or success/failure retention policy. The deterministic same-input/same-output claim does not specify cross-dialogue reset, transaction rollback, exception handling, or database mutation.

**Split and contamination.** DialogTool directly derives from SGD and MultiWOZ. Training/evaluation counts are given, but split creation, dialogue/schema overlap, near-duplicate control, and evaluated-model pretraining exposure are not audited. The paper itself has a turn-count inconsistency. Training on derived evaluation dialogues or labels would compromise later benchmark use.

**Feedback.** Component accuracy can hide error propagation: a wrong call changes state and can corrupt later responses. No autonomous retry or recovery score is defined. BLEU/ROUGE-L measure surface overlap, while GPT-4o role scores and a human study of 50 responses per model address style/quality, not transaction correctness or end-to-end goal completion.

**License, privacy, and security.** The paper is CC BY 4.0, but no dataset/environment artifact license is available. SGD, MultiWOZ, stored lookup results, and movie/TV role configurations require separate rights review. The ethics statement says public vetted sources were used and offensive/biased content was avoided, but record-level consent, PII scan, redaction, and role-rights evidence are absent. Tool creation executes model-generated Python; unavailable-library hallucinations are observed, yet sandboxing, resource limits, filesystem/network access, and side-effect controls are undocumented. VirtualMobile reduces live-service risk but also omits permissions, drift, concurrency, and real transactional failure.
