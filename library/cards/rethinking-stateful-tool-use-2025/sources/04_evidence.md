Table 3 shows that no evaluated model exceeds 80% on tool creation or any complete tool-utilization dimension. GPT-4o scores 66.7 on creation, 63.5 on awareness, 77.8 on selection, and 68.7 on execution. Llama 3 70B reaches 69.7 on creation and 68.1 on execution; Qwen1.5 72B reaches 75.5 on awareness and 71.9 on selection. Selection generally exceeds execution because execution additionally requires all argument names, values, and formats.

Longer context materially degrades GPT-4o. At 10 dialogue turns, awareness/selection/execution are 78.9/85.7/75.2; at 20 turns they fall to 59.7/69.5/51.2; at 30 turns to 56.7/60.1/38.5; and at 40 turns to 52.3/54.4/35.3. Error analysis identifies missing arguments as the dominant execution problem, plus API errors and value mismatches. GPT-4o misses 12.4% of keys, adds 0.9% extra keys, and has 24.3% value mismatch among compared cases; date and location values are frequent sources.

These findings support the claim that state and argument recovery become harder over long horizons. They do not demonstrate that DialogTool trajectories are safe or high-quality training data. Results are component-level, not an episode success rate; source dialogues are inherited; complete model outputs and failures are not released; and lexical/role metrics cannot verify that a final response reflects a correct environment transition.
