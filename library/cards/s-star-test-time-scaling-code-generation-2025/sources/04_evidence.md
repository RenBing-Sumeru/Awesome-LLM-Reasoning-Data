The paper evaluates 12 instruction and reasoning models on LiveCodeBench and CodeContests and separates coverage from selection. Results show the hybrid procedure can improve final Pass@1 relative to studied baselines; they do not establish that public tests, generated inputs, judgments, or selected programs are uniformly correct. The official EMNLP record states that code, model generations, and intermediate results are available in https://github.com/NovaSky-AI/SkyThought.
