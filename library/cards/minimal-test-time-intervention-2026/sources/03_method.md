During generation, MTI computes Shannon entropy over each next-token distribution and activates guidance only above a threshold. At those positions it mixes conditional logits with a negative-prompt branch in log-probability space. Cache reuse avoids the dual-cache implementation of ordinary LLM classifier-free guidance; the negative branch is created by inserting a short error-oriented cue immediately before the uncertain token.

The paper evaluates direct inference, full-position vanilla guidance, and MTI across threshold values on MMLU-Pro, coding, STEM, and spatial visual-language benchmarks. Main results use greedy decoding, while AIME2024 uses eight stochastic runs. DeepSeek-R1-7B, Ling-mini-2.0, and Qwen3 models of several scales test whether a token-level intervention remains useful across different reasoning capabilities and output domains.
