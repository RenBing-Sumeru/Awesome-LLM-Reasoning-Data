MTI requires access to token logits and KV-cache behavior, which may be unavailable for closed APIs or incompatible serving stacks. Its performance depends on entropy threshold, guidance weight, and prompt wording; low thresholds can disturb confident steps and high thresholds leave errors untouched. The paper tests many benchmarks but does not compare against every modern search-and-verifier pipeline, and the PDF does not expose a stable repository despite stating that code exists.
