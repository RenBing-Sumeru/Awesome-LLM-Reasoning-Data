At an entropy threshold of 0.5, DeepSeek-R1-7B reaches a 70.98% average across six reported general, coding, and STEM measurements, versus 61.70% for direct inference, while guidance activates on only 21.8% of tokens. Qwen3-14B reaches 85.33% versus 82.33% with 32.8% activation. On AIME2024 under sampling, MTI raises DeepSeek-R1-7B from 54.17% to 62.92% and Ling-mini-2.0 from 60.00% to 71.25%.

The paper also reports the efficiency trade-off directly. Across five benchmarks, Qwen3-14B at threshold 1.0 scores 80.70% with 4,551 seconds total latency, nearly equal to direct inference at 78.26% and 4,547 seconds, whereas vanilla guidance takes 12,411 seconds and scores 76.30%. In spatial VLM tests, activation is only 3.7%–4.5% of tokens while accuracy improves by 1.79 to 2.97 points, supporting the claim that useful intervention is sparse.
