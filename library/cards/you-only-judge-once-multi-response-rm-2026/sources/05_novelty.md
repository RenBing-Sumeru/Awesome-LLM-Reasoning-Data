Closest prior work uses general preference pairs, terminal success, or step accuracy without representing the domain-specific states and failure modes of multi-response reward modeling. This paper changes the record structure and feedback contract by centering MR²Bench and combining inputs, candidates, environmental or evidential state, and labels in one auditable example through sampling multiple responses for the same visual input and obtaining set-level rankings with pairwise verification. The contribution is task-specific data construction, capability decomposition, and practical evaluation rather than sample count alone.
