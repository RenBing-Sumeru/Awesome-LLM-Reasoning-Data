Using Visual-ERM for RL with Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct improves chart-to-code by 8.4 points and table and SVG parsing by 2.7 and 4.1 points on average. On VC-RewardBench, the 8B Visual-ERM substantially outperforms Qwen3-VL-235B-Instruct and approaches leading closed models. The evidence supports fine-grained visual feedback but covers only three structured-image families. These results support the central claim only under the released data, model scales, candidate budgets, and evaluation protocols; cross-domain use requires new validation.
