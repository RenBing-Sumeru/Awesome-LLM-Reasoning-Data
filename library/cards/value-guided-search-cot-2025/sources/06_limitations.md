**Feedback and modeling limits.** `math-verify` judges a parsed final answer, not the validity, relevance, or faithfulness of intermediate CoT. The token-level target repeats one terminal class over all rollout prefixes, so an early prefix before a later decisive error may receive a negative label, and a flawed path ending in the right answer may receive a positive label. Treating these values as semantic step quality would be a curator error. The qualitative score of 0.337 on a critically wrong beam also demonstrates learned-value miscalibration.

**Selection bias and missing failures.** All-failure prompt groups are removed because they provide no positive learning signal under the 1.5B rollout policy. This conditions OpenR1-VM on tasks that that policy can solve at least once and can underrepresent hard or systematically failing cases. Incorrect and incomplete traces are retained only inside surviving groups. Pre-filter rejects, the all-failure groups, full rejection reasons, and complete inference-time discarded branches are not published as a separate audit artifact.

**Distribution and generalization.** DeepSeek-VM-1.5B estimates success under one fixed rollout policy. Applying it to 7B or 14B generators changes the prefix distribution, and the reported advantage narrows at 14B. The study is limited to competition mathematics with boxed final answers; transfer to code, proof, open-ended reasoning, or different answer checkers is not demonstrated. Reward hacking through answer formatting or checker edge cases was not systematically stress-tested.

**Attribution and reproduction.** The authors report an 8-gram overlap check and use 2025 tests that postdate the underlying models/data, but comprehensive upstream OpenR1-Math decontamination is unknown. Exact allocation of 14 roll-ins across model sizes, data-generation decoding parameters, seeds, source snapshot pins, and the public identity of the 500-example value-model validation split are undisclosed. The end-to-end filtering/collection pipeline is not present in the checked repository, and the 128-H100 run and benchmark curves were not independently reproduced.

**Governance and packaging.** OpenR1-VM declares Apache-2.0, while OpenR1-Cleaned declares CC-BY-NC-4.0; the relationship between these terms and all upstream records requires review. No license was verified for the code or DeepSeek-VM-1.5B weights. The model repository lacks tokenizer files and the custom classifier source, so loading depends on the GitHub implementation and an upstream tokenizer. These gaps make direct commercial or policy-sensitive training reuse inappropriate without additional rights and version checks.
