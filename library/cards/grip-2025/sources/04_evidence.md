The construction statistics establish scale under the authors' accounting. Table 1 reports a 280-fold expansion from 7.5K seeds to 2.1M pairs and 71.8% of generated questions using concept combinations absent from the seed set. Appendix B gives the exact denominator 2,123,345 and estimates `0.000057 USD` per retained pair from 8 A100 80GB GPUs for 36 hours at a quoted rental price. These figures omit labor, unsuccessful experiments, training runs, and the cost of developing the pipeline. Embedding similarity and concept-combination novelty measure distance from seeds, not mathematical correctness. (Paper Section 3.5; Appendix B.)

The component evidence is mixed and informative. Table 4's average question-quality score falls from 0.94 for one-hop to 0.72 for two-hop and 0.62 for hub-constrained three-hop; removing the hub constraint yields 0.39 at three hops and 0.21 at four hops. Table 6 reports 0.83 with concept filtering versus 0.61 without it. Table 7 trains Mistral-7B on matched 0.2M groups: one-/two-/three-hop data scores 32.6 on MATH and 71.2 on GSM8K, compared with 29.3 and 67.6 for one-hop plus duplicated one-hop synthesis. These are author-reported ablations, not an independent data audit. (Paper Section 4.5.)

For the judge, 500 synthesized problems receive human qualified/unqualified labels. Table 5 reports 95.7% accuracy for the three-model combination and 94.3% for GPT-4.1. The paper does not disclose the human sampling frame, annotator count, instructions, agreement, or confidence intervals. Reverse concept extraction reports 88.65% full and 98.49% partial adherence, but reuses Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct and omits the sample size. (Paper Section 4.5; Appendix E.3.)

Downstream SFT supports usefulness under the tested setup. Table 2 raises Mistral-7B's five-benchmark mathematical average from 28.0 to 57.3 and Qwen2-7B from 66.5 to 70.2. Appendix Table 11 reports GRIP-MATH-mini at 43.0 versus 38.8 for an equally sized 80K MathCoder comparison on Mistral-7B. These results combine generation, filtering, scale, and training; they do not prove row-level quality or isolate one causal component.

The official subset enables a limited independent structural check. All 24,957 rows have the four documented non-empty fields. There are 24,954 unique question strings, leaving 3 exact duplicate-question groups attached to different concept labels; there are 24,946 unique solution strings. This is evidence that the sample is inspectable and not perfectly question-unique, but it cannot estimate the full corpus's correctness or duplication rate because the README supplies neither the sampling seed nor a full-population manifest.
