The reconstructable pipeline is:

1. **Seed and concept extraction.** Start from 7,500 MATH training question-solution pairs. Qwen2.5-32B extracts 1-5 atomic concepts under the published Prompt A.1.
2. **Dual concept filtering.** Qwen2.5-7B removes vague, incorrect, or over-detailed concepts. BGE-M3 similarity 0.90-1.00 triggers direct grouping; 0.70-0.90 triggers an LLM synonym decision; below 0.70 remains distinct. An LLM chooses or synthesizes a representative label, leaving roughly 10K concepts. Appendix D reports that the share of generated problems passing the authors' quality check rises from 26% with embedding-only deduplication to 45% with dual filtering.
3. **Graph and combination selection.** Co-occurring concepts receive weighted explicit edges. GRIP enumerates one-hop pairs, two-hop pairs sharing a neighbor, three-hop pairs between a hub and a concept three edges away, and fully connected communities of 3 or 4 concepts. Low-weight three-hop relations are removed, but the cutoff is undisclosed.
4. **Problem and solution generation.** Qwen2.5-32B receives concepts and Prompt A.2 without the source question. An unnamed rating model assigns difficulty. Qwen2.5-Math-7B produces low/medium solutions and Qwen2.5-Math-72B produces high-difficulty solutions. Temperature, top-p, token limits, retries, seeds, and raw candidate counts are unknown.
5. **Multi-model selection.** Three open mathematical models score problem logical/presentational completeness from 0 to 1. An undisclosed weighted average must be at least 0.85. The same models give binary solution judgments; unanimous acceptance is required. No symbolic execution, theorem prover, or process-step verifier is used.
6. **Output and release.** The paper reports 2,123,345 retained pairs from 8 A100 80GB GPUs over 36 hours with vLLM. The official supplemental JSON releases 24,957 randomly selected rows with only `KC1`, `KC2`, `question`, and `solution`.
7. **Downstream SFT.** Six base models are trained exclusively on GRIP-MATH using LLaMAFactory for 2 epochs, learning rate `5e-6`, global batch 128, sequence length 4,096, cosine scheduling, 3% warm-up, DeepSpeed ZeRO Stage 3, and FlashAttention 2. The optimizer, model revisions, random seeds, and per-run compute are not stated. (Paper Sections 3-4; Appendices A, B, and D.)

Faithful reproduction additionally requires the concept graph, branch counts, low-weight cutoff, difficulty model, exact judge weights/prompts, all generated and rejected records, split manifest, model revisions, and license. The supplemental sample supplies none of those fields, so it supports row-level inspection but not end-to-end reconstruction.
