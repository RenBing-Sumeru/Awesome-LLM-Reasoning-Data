- **Partial, underspecified release:** The official supplemental ZIP provides 24,957 rows rather than 2,123,345, and only `KC1`, `KC2`, `question`, and `solution`. It omits graph relation, difficulty, seed lineage, judge scores, model versions, split, and rejection state. The README promises later release but provides no sampling seed, license, or versioned full-data path.
- **No verified implementation:** No official code repository, project page, full dataset, model checkpoint, or Hugging Face release was verified as of 2026-07-21. Exact graph code, low-weight cutoff, difficulty model, judge weights/prompts, decoding parameters, retries, optimizer, and random seeds are unknown.
- **Model judgment is not proof:** Three related mathematical LLMs can share misconceptions, benchmark exposure, or stylistic preferences. Unanimous acceptance does not formally establish that a solution's intermediate derivation is valid. The 0.85 problem score cannot be reproduced without the unpublished weights.
- **Judge study underreported:** The 500-item human comparison omits sampling frame, annotator count, instructions, agreement, and uncertainty. The paper explicitly reports no error bars or statistical-significance analysis. A 95.7% aggregate agreement does not reveal false-acceptance rates in the 2.1M pool.
- **Graph compatibility is heuristic:** The paper's own ablation drops from 0.94 at one hop to 0.62 at hub-constrained three hops and 0.21 at unconstrained four hops. Indirect proximity can generate contrived, ambiguous, or unsolvable problems; unpublished branch rejection counts hide the resulting yield.
- **Concept and sample duplication:** Extraction and similarity clustering can merge distinct concepts or preserve synonyms. The official sample contains 3 exact duplicate-question groups among 24,957 rows, and the graph/intermediate vocabulary is not available for deeper normalization audits.
- **Limited and non-independent validation:** Reverse concept adherence reuses Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct and omits sample size. Decontamination covers only lexical n-gram overlap with MATH test, not semantic leakage, solution overlap, other evaluation sets, or contamination inherited through generator/judge pretraining.
- **Narrow domain and attribution:** The construction is tested only on English mathematical reasoning derived from MATH. Downstream gains combine scale, generator capability, filtering, teacher routing, and SFT; they neither isolate the graph's causal contribution nor prove transfer to other domains.
- **Cost boundary:** The `0.000057 USD` estimate counts 36 hours of 8 A100 80GB GPUs at one quoted rental rate for synthesis/scoring. It excludes labor, pipeline development, failed runs, full model training, storage, and future reproduction costs.
- **License and governance unknown:** The supplemental package contains no data license. MATH-derived concept rights, generated-question/solution terms, code terms, model-output redistribution, intended uses, and removal/correction procedures remain undisclosed.
