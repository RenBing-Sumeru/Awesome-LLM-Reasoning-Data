Dyve represents process verification with two learned output modes. System 1 is normally a single plus token for a straightforward correct step. System 2 contains a detailed analysis in a think-tag block and ends with a plus or minus verdict. Importantly, System 2 is not synonymous with “incorrect”: a complex but correct step may receive an explanation ending in plus. The mode is learned from the mixed training targets rather than selected by a disclosed hand-written difficulty threshold.

The construction recipe separates two examples. A fully correct trajectory interleaves each reasoning step with its positive verifier response. A divergent trajectory retains the correct prefix and the first flagged error step, then stops. DeepSeek-V3 first filters noisy OmegaPRM assessments at the rollout level; DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B then writes the stepwise fast or slow targets used to supervise the smaller verifier.
