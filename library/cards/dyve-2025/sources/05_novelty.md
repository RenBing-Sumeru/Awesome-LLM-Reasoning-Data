The central novelty is to encode adaptive verification effort directly in the supervision object. Rather than train a classifier and a separate explanation model, Dyve places a one-token verdict and a reasoned verdict in one autoregressive target space, allowing the student to learn when to answer quickly and when to deliberate. The paper also clarifies that deliberation can accompany a difficult correct step, not only an error.

As a data-construction recipe, the work stages search, consensus filtering, class rebalancing, and target synthesis. This makes the verifier refresh process inspectable as a sequence of transformations, even though several transformation records are not included in the release. The contribution is therefore a useful design pattern for process-supervision data, not proof that the resulting labels are noise-free or calibrated.
