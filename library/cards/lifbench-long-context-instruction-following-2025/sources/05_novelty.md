Long-context tests such as Needle-in-a-Haystack primarily measure retrieval, while instruction benchmarks such as IFEval use relatively short contexts. LIFBench combines the two by evaluating whether complex constraints remain satisfied in long inputs and treats stability as an independent metric. Its key methodological novelty is paired variation along length, expression, and variables, allowing performance changes to be attributed to specific factors. LIFEval compiles natural-language instructions into deterministic rubrics without human or LLM judges, making the data suitable for precise regression testing.
