Experiments with 20 models across six length ranges show that a long context window does not imply stable instruction following. Many models degrade as context grows and remain sensitive to equivalent wording or variable substitutions. LIFEval identifies whether a model violates format, count, or content conditions, providing more diagnostic information than exact match while avoiding LLM-judge stochasticity. Automatic expansion allows controlled variation of individual factors. The evidence primarily covers programmatically verifiable constraints and should not be directly generalized to open-ended writing quality or implicit-intent following.
