Deterministic checking requires relatively structured tasks and output formats, so the benchmark may overemphasize formatting while underrepresenting semantic quality in open tasks. Automatically paraphrased instructions may be unnatural or subtly change meaning, and variable expansion may not reflect real user distributions. Stability degradation may arise from attention limits, positional bias, or decoding randomness, which the scores alone cannot disentangle. Reuse should verify paraphrase equivalence, fix sampling settings, and combine the benchmark with real long-document tasks and human evaluation.
