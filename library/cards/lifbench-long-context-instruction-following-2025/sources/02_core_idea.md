LIFBench contains 2,766 instructions across three long-context scenarios and 11 task types, automatically expanded along the dimensions of length, expression, and variables. The paper also introduces LIFEval, which decomposes each complex instruction into programmatically checkable rubric items for requirements such as format, content, position, count, and conditions, producing fine-grained scores. Twenty models are evaluated over six context-length intervals, with analysis of not only average capability but also stability across variants of the same task.
