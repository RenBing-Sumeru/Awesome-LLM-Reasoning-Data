Evaluation of six leading MLLMs in a unified agentic MM-RAG pipeline shows that final-answer scores hide systematic over-retrieval, under-retrieval, and modality-misaligned planning. After Search-Align fine-tuning on MC-Search chains, open models improve step planning and retrieval fidelity while maintaining or improving answer accuracy. The evidence supports process-data utility, although the benchmark contains only 3,333 tasks.

The authors also report results for five chain structures to test whether gains come from one task pattern.
