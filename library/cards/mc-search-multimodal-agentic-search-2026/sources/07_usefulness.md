MC-Search can serve as a hidden test for multimodal search agents with separate reporting of subquestion planning, modality selection, evidence recall, and final answers. Verified chains can also support SFT for more faithful search trajectories. If the retrieval corpus changes, HAVE or human verification must be rerun. The long-chain protocol is unnecessary for one-shot VQA.

The output can be a Search-Align model or a hop-by-hop retrieval diagnostic report.
