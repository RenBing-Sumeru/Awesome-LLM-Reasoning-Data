Earlier multimodal retrieval benchmarks provide documents or label only final evidence, so an agent need not decide the next search. MC-Search records what to search, which modality to use, and what intermediate conclusion is obtained, and verifies every hop with HAVE. The novelty is executable cross-modal search trajectories and process metrics rather than multi-hop QA itself.

Errors can therefore be localized to a specific subquestion-planning or modality-selection decision.
