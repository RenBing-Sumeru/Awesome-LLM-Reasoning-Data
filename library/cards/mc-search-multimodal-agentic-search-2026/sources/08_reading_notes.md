1. **Positioning:** MC-Search tests whether agents dynamically plan cross-modal multi-hop retrieval.
2. **Method handle:** It designs chains, labels subquestions and modalities, verifies hops with HAVE, and evaluates or fine-tunes.
3. **Data handle:** 3,333 tasks average 3.7 hops across five reasoning structures.
4. **Evidence anchor:** It reveals over/under-retrieval and modality mismatch; Search-Align improves planning fidelity.
5. **Reuse decision:** It fits agentic MM-RAG; version retrieval corpora and allow alternative paths. Evaluation should also preserve per-hop evidence hits, modality switches, and unnecessary retrieval counts.
