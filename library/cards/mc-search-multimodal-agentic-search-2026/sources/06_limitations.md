First, an annotated chain represents only one valid search order, so agents using equivalent paths may be undervalued by planning metrics. Second, retrieval corpora, webpages, and image indexes change over time and require versioning. Third, HAVE depends on complete supporting-fact annotations and can misjudge omitted evidence. Reuse should allow alternative chains and replay retrieved results.

In addition, current chains are relatively short and do not cover very long open-ended research searches.
