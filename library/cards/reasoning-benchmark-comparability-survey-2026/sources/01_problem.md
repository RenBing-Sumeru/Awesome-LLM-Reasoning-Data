Reasoning benchmarks are plentiful, but their scores often look more comparable than they are. One benchmark may test symbolic deduction from a fixed prompt; another may allow retrieval, tools, images, long context, or repeated attempts. Their answer keys and success criteria can differ as well. This 2026 survey addresses that mismatch. It treats a benchmark instance as more than a question and answer: it has a capability target, a setting that shapes what the model can do, and an evaluation rule that gives the score meaning.
