The paper synthesizes benchmarks through Object, Setting, and Evaluation instead of proposing a new model. To apply it, build a short record for every candidate benchmark. State the dependency structure a solver must preserve, the context and actions it receives, and the exact success rule. Add freshness, possible overlap with training data, and the reporting protocol. This makes two hidden choices visible: whether a model may obtain extra evidence, and whether a final answer is enough when the task is supposed to test multi-step reasoning.
