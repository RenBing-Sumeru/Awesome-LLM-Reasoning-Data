The survey’s central framework has three questions. Object: what reasoning capability is being tested? Setting: which inputs, tools, budgets, and environmental conditions are available? Evaluation: what does the metric actually count as success? Put these three fields next to every benchmark score. If any field changes, an apparent improvement may reflect a different task contract rather than a stronger model. This is a direct lesson for reasoning-data work: data design and scoring cannot be separated from the behavior a benchmark claims to measure.
