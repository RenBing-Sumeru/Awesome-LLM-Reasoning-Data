The training path is deliberately staged. MiniMax-Text-01 first receives 7.5T additional non-synthetic tokens, with STEM, code, books, and reasoning raised to 70 percent and context extended from 32K to 1M. Long-CoT SFT then teaches reflective reasoning across math, code, STEM, writing, QA, and multi-turn chat; math and code are about 60 percent of that mixture, but its total size, sources, and response authors are unknown.

RL starts with tasks whose final outcomes can be checked by rules or execution. Math uses answer extraction and checking, SynLogic supplies task generators and verifiers for 41 logic families, competitive programming uses tests, and software engineering runs proposed changes in containers against relevant tests. The policy later receives 25K general prompts scored by Generative Reward Models or by combined rule and model feedback.

CISPO is the optimizer scaffold joining these feedback regimes. It clips importance-sampling weights rather than token updates, uses group-relative advantage, dynamic sampling, length shaping, and no KL term. The curriculum begins with rule-verifiable reasoning and gradually adds model-scored general tasks. For the 80K phase, the 40K model filters easy prompts, harder math and code gain weight, and repetitive synthetic reasoning is downsampled.

This composition is more informative than a weights-only release, but it remains report-level. The official artifacts contain the 40K/80K weights, model code, and deployment guides; they do not contain the training rows, reward model, rollout history, execution bundle, or exact training implementation.
