The paper tests its central claim through controlled comparison with existing reward models, general judges, rule-based verifiers, or benchmark methods. The most representative result is that relative gains reach 31% on HealthBench and 7% on GPQA-Diamond. This indicates that RaR-Science exposes capability gaps missed by ordinary evaluation or provides a more useful feedback signal. Because results still depend on base models, candidate generators, judges, and task distributions, the evidence supports utility under the tested setting rather than universal effectiveness.
