Closest prior work usually uses general preference pairs, final-answer correctness, or one holistic score without data specifically designed for the key failures and usage conditions of rubric-based reinforcement learning. This paper changes the feedback object and evaluation contract by centering RaR-Science and placing task input, candidate response, and auditable evidence in one record, with labels produced through instance-specific criterion construction, per-criterion judging, and reward aggregation. The contribution is the combination of dataset construction, fine-grained organization, and practical use rather than merely another model or pairwise format.
