Open-ended tasks such as scientific explanation or medical guidance have neither a unique answer nor an executable test, so outcome rewards are unavailable. Asking an LLM for one overall score is sensitive to wording and does not reveal which requirement was violated, leaving GRPO without a stable and auditable reward.

Rubrics as Rewards generates weighted criteria for each problem, judges candidates criterion by criterion, and aggregates the judgments into a reward so that reinforcement learning can extend beyond verifiable domains.
