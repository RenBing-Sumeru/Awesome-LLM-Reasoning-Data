The central artifact is RaR-Science, containing roughly 22.9K scientific reasoning questions with five to twelve weighted rubrics each. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use instance-specific criterion construction, per-criterion judging, and reward aggregation for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is providing GRPO rewards for open tasks without programmatic verification.
