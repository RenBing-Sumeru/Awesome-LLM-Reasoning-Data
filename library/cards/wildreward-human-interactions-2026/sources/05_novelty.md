Earlier implicit-feedback work often reduces behavior to clicks, acceptance, or binary continuation, while reward-model training typically assumes explicit chosen–rejected pairs. WildReward changes the data object to an ordinal record derived from conversational behavior and trains directly with ordinal regression. Its novelty is connecting in-the-wild multi-turn interaction patterns to a five-level reward scale and demonstrating direct use in online DPO. The underlying ordinal model is established; the new contribution is the extraction and validation pipeline.
