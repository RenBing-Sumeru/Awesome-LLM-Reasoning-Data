WildReward replaces explicit pairs with five-level satisfaction labels extracted from real WildChat follow-up behavior. WildFB contains about 186,000 filtered instances, and the reward model is trained by ordinal regression. Evidence includes benchmark comparisons, calibration, consistency, user-diversity scaling, and online-DPO gains. The main value is realistic implicit feedback; the main risk is confounding because behavior is not a unique expression of preference. Direct-rating validation on local logs is the required reuse check.
