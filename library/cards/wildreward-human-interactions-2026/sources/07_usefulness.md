WildFB can train ordinal reward models without manually producing response pairs, and its labels can be converted into pairs when DPO requires them. The pipeline is useful for product logs where follow-up behavior is available but explicit voting is sparse. Researchers can study calibration, user diversity, and satisfaction prediction from conversation context. It should not be used to infer individual mental state or to treat silence and abandonment as universally negative; local behavior semantics and privacy policies must be validated first.
