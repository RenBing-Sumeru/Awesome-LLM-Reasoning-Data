The pipeline identifies assistant turns with observable user follow-up, filters low-information or ambiguous interactions, and maps behavioral patterns to ordered satisfaction categories. The resulting prompt, response, conversation context, behavioral evidence, and ordinal label form WildFB. WildReward is trained to predict the ordered level rather than a binary preference. The authors evaluate ranking, calibration, and cross-sample consistency, study performance as user diversity increases, and use the reward model to select online DPO pairs from newly sampled responses.
