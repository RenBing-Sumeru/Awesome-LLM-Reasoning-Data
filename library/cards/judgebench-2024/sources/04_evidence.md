The arXiv abstract reports that JudgeBench is harder than previous judge benchmarks and that strong models such as GPT-4o perform only slightly above random guessing on it. The official dataset card gives concrete released evidence: two public splits, 350 GPT-4o response pairs and 270 Claude-3.5-Sonnet response pairs, with fields needed to replay pairwise judgments. The instance-level evidence is the objective label attached to each answer pair, not a free-form rationale from another judge. The evidence boundary is important: the public benchmark is small, English, and fully released under a dataset license; scores can change with prompt formatting, answer order, model/API revision, reward-model calibration, and whether the evaluator reports single-game or swapped-order decisions.
