For Track 12, this Card makes the model-report boundary usable in comparisons. A curator can record that stage one has programmatic outcome signals for mathematics and code, while stage two mixes a general reward model with rule-based verifiers. The same record preserves the crucial unknowns: which data and tests instantiate those signals, how they are aggregated, and whether they are robust or licensed.

It is useful when evaluating claims about RLVR, agent-related reasoning, or open-weight reproducibility. The proper comparison unit is not a benchmark score or parameter count, but the available evidence for prompts, traces, reward/verifier behavior, selection, audit, and rights. This Card is deliberately a Track 12 disclosure ledger, not a Track 8 scaling or test-time-compute entry.
