π² separates answer construction from reasoning-trace construction. Tables make multi-hop questions and answers executable; after verification, web pages turn the same task into realistic long-context reading, and a teacher writes a trace without seeing the hidden table. This prevents the teacher from defining its own ground truth while still producing model-compatible reasoning supervision.

Open dataset: yes. Name: π² training data and π²-Bench. Official code/project: https://github.com/vt-pi-squared/pi-squared. Official data: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ISRgLijDFvwFpHzV4tmHix6MIwIGG2gd?usp=sharing. Scale: 1,174 validated QA traces, including 922 training records; π²-Bench contains 228 human-reviewed items split into 178 test and 50 validation examples. Record form: long unstructured context, analytical question, structured reasoning trace, short answer, and construction metadata; the repository links JSONL data. Construction uses Wikipedia tables/pages, about ten web-search articles per QA, SQL/Python consensus, quality ratings, and model-family-matched back-translation. License: not confirmed. Intended use: long-context SFT, self-distillation, and evaluation.
