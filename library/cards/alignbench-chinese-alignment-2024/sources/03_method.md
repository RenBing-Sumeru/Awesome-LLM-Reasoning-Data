1. **Collect questions:** Candidate queries are gathered from real Chinese use cases across eight categories including knowledge, reasoning, writing, role-play, professional capability, and safety.
2. **Human filtering:** Ambiguous, easy, and translation-like questions are removed to preserve naturalness, evaluability, and discrimination.
3. **Create references:** Annotators write and cross-check references; knowledge questions also store trusted webpage URLs and supporting quotations.
4. **Design rubrics:** Category-specific multidimensional scoring rules calibrate the relationship between dimensions and aggregate scores.
5. **Automatic evaluation:** The judge reads the query, response, reference, and rubric, generates an analysis, and assigns scores. Human comparisons validate the protocol, and all resources are released.
