Chinese LLMs developed rapidly, but early alignment benchmarks were often translations of English tasks and poorly represented Chinese culture, expression, knowledge context, and real user needs. Simple holistic GPT-4 scoring is also vulnerable to missing references, scale drift, and limited Chinese capability. The paper asks how to build a native Chinese, multidimensional, interpretable alignment benchmark and make LLM-as-a-judge reliable on both knowledge-intensive and open-ended questions.
