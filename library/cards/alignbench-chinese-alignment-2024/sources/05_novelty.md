Earlier Chinese benchmarks emphasized knowledge QA or translated English tasks, while MT-Bench-style methods lacked systematic evidence and Chinese-specific category design. AlignBench combines native real-world queries, human references, web evidence, and multidimensional judging with category-calibrated rules. LLM-as-a-judge and chain-of-thought scoring are not new; the contribution is high-quality Chinese alignment data engineering and an auditable protocol.
