The central artifact is HelpSteer3/RLBFF, containing roughly 40.8K textual-feedback examples with principle-satisfaction labels. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use extracting binary-testable principles from free-form feedback and assigning yes/no labels for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is training reward models that judge responses under user-specified principles.
