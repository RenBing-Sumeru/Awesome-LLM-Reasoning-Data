Free-form human feedback can express specific requirements such as “factually sufficient but inappropriate in tone,” yet it is difficult to convert directly into stable rewards. A single preference label discards the underlying principle, while programmatic verification applies only to tasks with decidable answers, leaving no trainable interface between the two.

RLBFF extracts binary-testable principles from feedback and assigns per-principle yes/no labels, enabling a reward model to judge responses under user-specified criteria.
