Earlier math instruction releases often aggregate source answers or publish a single math-only mixture. AceMath changes the auditable object by releasing the exact general-before-math stages, regenerating responses into a common style, and separating broad prompt diversity from cross-model answer agreement used for high-quality subsets. Synthetic prompt evolution, SFT, and answer matching are not individually new; the direction signal is their staged composition plus controlled comparisons of synthetic quantity, filtering quality, and curriculum order. The release is therefore most novel as a reproducible data schedule, not as a new proof verifier.
