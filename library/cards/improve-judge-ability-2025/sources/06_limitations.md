The authors state that judgment precision is not consistent across domains, especially for open-ended and point-wise tasks. A deployment team should therefore validate agreement on its own task rather than treat RewardBench performance as a universal reward guarantee.

The filter defines correctness from source preferences and GPT-4o order consistency; a fluent but wrong critique can still become a target if those signals agree. Before reuse, audit retained rationales and test position, length, and reward-hacking failures on held-out responses. The downstream policy experiment is internal, so its gain cannot establish external RLAIF performance.

Limited proprietary general dialogue is mixed into training, but its size, contents, and license are not reported. External reproductions cannot exactly recreate the general-ability retention distribution and should document substitute data and resulting differences.
