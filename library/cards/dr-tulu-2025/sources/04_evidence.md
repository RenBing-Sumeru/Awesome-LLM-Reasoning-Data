The evidence bundle verifies the official arXiv record, Ai2 release post, rlrsearch repository and license, author-linked Hugging Face collection, SFT and RL dataset cards, and final model card. The paper reports 16K SFT trajectories, while the public SFT train split has 13,062 rows and explicitly omits MegaScience, HotpotQA and ScholarQA subsets. It reports roughly 9K RL prompts, approximately 5K SearchArena/OpenScholar plus 4K RaR, while the public RL train split has 4,881 SearchArena/OpenScholar rows and explicitly omits RaR. Both public data pages expose only a train split. The final release-level model tree is Qwen3-8B-Base to Qwen3-8B to DR-Tulu-SFT-8B to DR-Tulu-8B RL; that is not a per-record or per-step provenance graph.
