The feedback contract is mixed rather than a single verifiable terminal oracle. Search-derived documents seed persistent rubrics; an LM proposes positive and negative evolving natural-language rubrics from the question, sampled responses, search context and the current buffer. An LLM judge scores rubric and claim-support/relevance components. Programmatic components check tool/citation protocol, answer/citation/query format, and valid non-empty search queries capped at three. A final answer receives the composite feedback, but no released objective success predicate resolves open-ended deep-research quality. Supervision is therefore full-episode plus scalar reward, with rubric judgment decisive at the terminal stage.
