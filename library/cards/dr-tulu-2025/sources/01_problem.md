DR Tulu is curated for the Frontier Reports and Data Disclosure Ledger because it exposes a long-form research-agent record while retaining material release boundaries. A record can contain a question, source identifier, source, question type, conversations or messages, think/tool/answer/cite actions, tool observations, citation fields, and a rubric payload or rollout score. SFT mixes filtered SearchArena/OpenScholar information-seeking queries with HotpotQA, TaskCraft, WebWalker-Silver, MegaScience and PopQA-inspired prompts; RL uses filtered long-form SearchArena/OpenScholar and RaR prompts. SFT trajectories are GPT-5 simulated reasoning, tool calls, outputs and cited answers, whereas RL trajectories are on-policy policy rollouts with real tool calls. The substrate is dr-agent-lib with MCP web/paper search and browsing, asynchronous calls, caching and rate-limit management.
