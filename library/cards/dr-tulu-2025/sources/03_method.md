The reported lineage is named prompt sources, LM prompt filtering, GPT-5 SFT trajectories, an SFT checkpoint, real-tool on-policy GRPO, search-based persistent rubrics plus online evolving buffers, then a final RL checkpoint. SFT retains correctly formatted trajectories and exact short-form final answers; prompt quality is scored on a one-to-five scale. Rubric buffers remove zero-variance items and retain the top five by rollout-score standard deviation. RL uses 32 unique prompts and eight rollouts per prompt, a 16,384-token response maximum, at most ten tool calls, temperature and top-p of 1.0, KL coefficient 0.001, token-level aggregation, packed sequences and asynchronous tool calls. The public SFT and RL files are released datasets, but their rows are only a subset of the reported paper/final-run mixtures and do not establish a release of online rubric revisions, tool-result stores or full rollout traces.
