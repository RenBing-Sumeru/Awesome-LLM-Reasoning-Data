CRV compares against black-box and gray-box baselines such as MaxProb, entropy, Chain-of-Embedding, and CoT-Kinetics using AUROC, AUPR, and FPR@95, finding stronger error signals in graph structure. Analysis across arithmetic, Boolean reasoning, and GSM8K shows highly domain-specific fingerprints, and interventions on graph-identified features can correct some faulty reasoning. The conclusion depends on the chosen model and transcoders.

Results are reported by domain so that aggregate averages do not hide structural differences among arithmetic, Boolean, and word problems.
