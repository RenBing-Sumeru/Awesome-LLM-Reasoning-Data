1. **Coverage boundary:** The 1,250 examples support diagnosis rather than exhaustive visual-domain coverage; fine-grained OCR, video, and specialist imagery may be underrepresented.

2. **Feedback risk:** Pairwise labels collapse multiple error types, and a single chosen/rejected decision cannot express uncertainty when both responses are wrong or partially correct.

3. **Reproduction and use:** The public benchmark may be used for tuning or training; users should disclose exposure and retain hidden or temporal splits.
