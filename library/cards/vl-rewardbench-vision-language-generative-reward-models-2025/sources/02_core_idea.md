VL-RewardBench uses AI-assisted hard-example selection followed by human verification to curate 1,250 high-quality image–text preference instances spanning general multimodal queries, visual hallucination, and complex reasoning, and evaluates vision-language generative reward models with pairwise accuracy. VL-RewardBench contains 1,250 human-verified preference examples across general multimodal, hallucination, and complex-reasoning domains. Each record links an image and question to two candidate responses and a preference label, with source or error-type metadata for some subsets. The official release is an evaluation benchmark rather than a large training corpus.
