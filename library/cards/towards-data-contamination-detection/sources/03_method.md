The study combines eight challenging evaluation datasets, four modern LLMs, and five existing contamination-detection approaches. It applies the approaches under the paper's stated model-access and prompting conditions, then compares their outputs for robustness and agreement. The resulting evidence consists of method-specific contamination judgments and an analysis of their limitations, rather than a new post-training label set. The paper does not have a ground-truth verifier for each model's undisclosed training corpus. Its reported results remain tied to the detector, benchmark format, model-access setting, and prompt configuration used in the study.
