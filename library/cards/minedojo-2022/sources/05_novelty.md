The prior-work baseline is agent-environment, tool-use, and benchmark-harness papers in the same atlas category. The change introduced by MineDojo: Building Open-Ended Embodied Agents with Internet-Scale Knowledge is the particular task object and feedback contract: Minecraft tasks, simulator interfaces, knowledge base resources, videos/wiki data, MineCLIP signals, and agent results scored by MineDojo task success metrics, environment predicates, and benchmark scripts. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08853; openreview: https://openreview.net/forum?id=rc8o_j8I8PX; project: https://minedojo.org/; code: https://github.com/MineDojo/MineDojo; data: https://minedojo.org/knowledge_base), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
