1. **Analyse real needs:** Researchers analyse large numbers of real research queries to define a distribution across 22 fields.

2. **Author tasks:** PhD-level and senior experts write 100 open tasks and references with controlled difficulty and evaluability.

3. **Generate adaptive criteria:** RACE derives fine-grained checks from each question, reference, and report to reduce limitations of fixed rubrics.

4. **Evaluate reports and citations:** Report quality, effective citation count, and citation accuracy are scored separately; retrieval time, judge, and system budget must be fixed.
