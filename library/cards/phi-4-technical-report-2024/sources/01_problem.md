The primary sources are Microsoft Research Technical Report MSR-TR-2024-57, arXiv:2412.08905, the official `microsoft/phi-4` model repository, and its later official data summary. Microsoft identifies 27 authors and published the report in December 2024. The Hugging Face repository verifies a downloadable MIT-licensed checkpoint; it is not a training-data release.

The report asks how a 14B dense model can gain reasoning, coding, and instruction-following capability through data construction and curriculum rather than a major architecture change. Phi-4 uses synthetic data throughout pretraining and post-training, while filtered organic data supplies both direct training material and seeds for synthetic generation.

For this atlas, the audit problem is to keep four ledgers separate: what the report says about data, what model weights are downloadable, what training records and generation decisions are downloadable, and what evaluation says about model behavior. Phi-4 has substantial evidence in the first two ledgers, no official corpus in the third, and extensive benchmark evidence in the fourth. Scores cannot fill missing source, license, lineage, or verifier fields.

The disclosed data objects span several scales. The model card reports a 9.8T-token training run. The report describes about 50 broad synthetic dataset types totaling about 400B unweighted source tokens, around 8B SFT tokens, 250,297 first-round Pivotal Token Search examples, and 841,842 second-round judge-guided DPO pairs. These are report-level aggregates without released row schemas or a crosswalk into the final model. That makes Phi-4 a strong `frontier_reports_data_disclosure_ledger` entry, while its `partial` status and L4 level must remain.
