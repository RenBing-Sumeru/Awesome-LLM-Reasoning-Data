Reasoning-model releases can disclose a sequence of SFT and RL stages while leaving the prompts, traces, rollouts, verifier implementation, reward weights, and audit records unavailable. Without those artifacts, a reader cannot determine how the reported training mixture was assembled, whether task rules correctly measure reasoning, or whether released weights are reproducible evidence for the claimed pipeline.

DeepSeek-R1 is a central example. Its report describes R1-Zero, cold-start SFT, reasoning RL, rejection-sampled SFT, general SFT, final alignment RL, and dense-model distillation. It releases model weights, code, and a paper, but not the underlying cold starts, RL trajectories, 800K distillation rows, source manifests, feedback implementation, training splits, or decontamination evidence.
