R1-Zero begins from DeepSeek-V3-Base and uses GRPO on rule-evaluated reasoning tasks. The report says the reward includes accuracy and format terms. R1 then starts with thousands of cold-start examples assembled through several undisclosed methods, including readable R1-Zero outputs and human post-processing. A readable long-CoT pattern ends with a final summary or answer, but the special-token schema and records are not released.

After reasoning RL, the pipeline samples multiple responses for reasoning prompts and retains correct responses through rejection sampling. The report filters mixed-language, long-paragraph, and code-block CoT patterns. DeepSeek-V3 produces or judges parts of the later general SFT material. Later all-scenarios RL combines rule feedback and general reward models. Extractors, task environments, V3 prompts, judge versions, rule thresholds, reward coefficients, selection yields, and rollout settings are unknown.

The R1 checkpoint generates 800K samples for dense distillation, with publicly released distill model weights. The report does not release the 800K rows, cold-start set, 600K rejection-sampled reasoning data, 200K non-reasoning data, or the accepted and rejected candidate groups from which they were assembled.
