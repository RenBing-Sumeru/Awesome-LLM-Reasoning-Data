The most direct data evidence is the generation of 16,061 training tasks and 2,020 test tasks from real projects, with every stage verified by execution of corresponding unit tests rather than an LLM judge. Fine-tuning open models on this data yields substantial improvements on TDD coding over the base model and data recipes without the RDG schedule, indicating that incremental structure has training value.

This supports the claim that tests can serve as sources of requirements and development steps. However, the public abstract does not report every model’s absolute scores, so comparisons should use same-model, same-data-size settings from the paper tables. Test passing guarantees only covered behavior, and unexecuted functionality or cross-stage side effects may remain undetected.
