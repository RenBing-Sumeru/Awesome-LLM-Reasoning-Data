Use Arena-Hard as a reference for building hard open-ended judge benchmarks and for auditing judge-based evaluation. Preserve prompt ID, source/provenance, benchmark version, baseline answer, candidate answer, judge model, judge prompt, style controls, random seed or retry policy, pairwise label, confidence interval, and leaderboard date. For atlas curation it is useful because it treats benchmark quality itself as measurable through separability and agreement with human preference.
