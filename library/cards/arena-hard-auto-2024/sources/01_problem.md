Primary source: arXiv 2406.11939, submitted June 2024 and revised October 2024, plus the official lmarena repository and Hugging Face release. The problem is continuous curation of challenging open-ended LLM evaluation prompts without repeatedly paying for full human preference collection. Arena-Hard-Auto belongs here as a benchmark-building and automatic pairwise-judge evaluation surface; it is not a ground-truth QA dataset. The data object is a hard user prompt, two model answers, judge configuration, pairwise judgment, and aggregated win-rate score.
