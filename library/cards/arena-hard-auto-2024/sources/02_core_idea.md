Arena-Hard introduces BenchBuilder, an automated pipeline that selects challenging open-ended prompts from crowdsourced conversation data and evaluates models with LLM-as-a-judge. The mechanism is to curate hard prompts, generate candidate model responses, ask a configured judge to compare against a baseline or rival answer, and aggregate win rates with confidence intervals. The closest comparisons are MT-Bench, AlpacaEval-style automatic preference benchmarks, and Chatbot Arena human preference rankings. The direction label is judge/reward meta-evaluation with explicit separability and human-alignment checks.
