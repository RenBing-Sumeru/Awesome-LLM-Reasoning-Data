Prior open-ended benchmarks often relied on manually written prompts, limited prompt difficulty, or human evaluation that was expensive to refresh. Arena-Hard changes the construction layer by using crowdsourced data plus LLM filtering to build hard prompts and by evaluating the benchmark itself with separability and human-alignment metrics. What is not new: LLM-as-a-judge and pairwise preference scoring existed before. Reuse checks must inspect prompt provenance, privacy filtering, judge bias, baseline choice, style control, public leaderboard drift, and whether the benchmark version is v0.1, v2.0, or another release.
