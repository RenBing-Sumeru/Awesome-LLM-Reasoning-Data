The arXiv abstract reports Arena-Hard-Auto as 500 challenging prompts curated by BenchBuilder. It reports 3x higher separation of model performances than MT-Bench, 98.6% correlation with human preference rankings, and an evaluation cost of about $20 for the paper configuration. The row-level evidence is a judge comparison record for a prompt-answer pair, not a factual correctness proof. The boundary is the judge model, baseline, prompt version, style controls, model answer generation settings, and the human preference reference used for validation.
