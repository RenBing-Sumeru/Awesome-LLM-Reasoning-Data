Large long-CoT distillation and failure mining each exist separately. Logics-STEM connects them as a distribution-design loop: curation builds a broad first-stage proposal distribution, then verified model failures define where document-grounded synthesis should concentrate. The release also exposes large open pre- and post-downsampling variants with explicit deduplication and decontamination stages.
