**End-to-end 8B claim.** Under the paper's 64K-context evaluation, Logics-STEM-8B-RL scores 90.42 on AIME 2024, 87.08 on AIME 2025, 74.79 on HMMT 2025, 62.50 on BeyondAIME, and 73.93 on GPQA-Diamond; the paper reports a 4.68-point average lead over the next-best 8B model.
