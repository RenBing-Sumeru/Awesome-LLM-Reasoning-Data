Operation: normalize records and use Qwen3-235B-Instruct to label validity, domain, level, answer type, and other quality fields. Output: structured candidate questions. Check: remove invalid, ambiguous, and unsupported records.

Operation: apply MD5 exact matching, MinHash near-duplicate filtering, and remove identical 13-gram overlap with benchmark samples. Output: cleaner source questions. Check: retain provenance and log each removal criterion.
3. **Distill long reasoning.** Input: retained questions. Operation: Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking writes long-CoT responses; repetitive outputs are filtered and incorrect answers may be regenerated once. Output: 7.2M curated records. Check: verify final answers where possible, noting that incorrect teacher responses are not categorically removed.
4. **Sample the SFT mixture.** Input: curated records ranked by response length. Operation: keep all above the 75th percentile, 50% of the 50th-75th band, and 10% of the 20th-50th band. Output: 2.2M records, including 1.05M math and 1.14M broader-STEM examples. Check: audit domain/source balance, not only length.
5. **Target model failures.** Input: the first-stage SFT model and a target question distribution. Operation: identify verified wrong answers, retrieve related documents, and synthesize new questions and solutions around those failure regions. Output: failure-driven data mixed with original SFT records. Check: verify answers and prevent target benchmark text from becoming training leakage.
6. **Run second-stage training.** Input: the failure-driven mixture. Operation: perform continued SFT or sample its prompts for RLVR with verified reward. Output: Logics-STEM SFT/RL models. Check: compare source-specific synthetic mixtures, mixture ratios, and original-data baselines.
