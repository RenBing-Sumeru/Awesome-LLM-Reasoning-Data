The core idea is “workspace plus artifact-aligned reward.” Tasks are instantiated in executable environments, trajectories are generated or distilled under varied agent scaffolds, and acceptance is chosen for the artifact type rather than delegated to one universal judge. Repository repair uses tests; terminal tasks use generated Docker environments and scripts; search requires retrieved evidence; spreadsheets are recalculated and compared at value level; and slides combine execution, functional, layout, and rendered-image checks.

For optimization, the model is the policy and everything beyond its generation interface—including tools, memory, context rewriting, sub-agent control, and artifacts—is the environment. Forge records completion/observation pairs but propagates credit over the full episode. This permits white-box and API-only agent scaffolds to share a training system while keeping the environment boundary explicit.
