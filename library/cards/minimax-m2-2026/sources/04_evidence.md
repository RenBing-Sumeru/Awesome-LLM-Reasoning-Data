The report presents within-series gains from M2 to M2.5 and M2.7 across agentic coding, cowork, and reasoning evaluations, plus architecture and mechanism ablations. These support the claim that the combined model, data, agent scaffold, and RL system improved, but they do not isolate the quality contribution of any one corpus. Benchmark scores therefore should not be treated as item-level data validation.

Stronger evidence comes from explicit acceptance procedures: golden patches must satisfy task-specific tests; generated environments are executed and repaired; search trajectories must cite retrieved evidence; spreadsheet formulas are recalculated externally; and slide artifacts are rendered before visual scoring. The report also describes verifier edge-case analysis and cross-model disagreement checks for reasoning data. However, it publishes no corpus samples, acceptance rates, held-out verifier audits, reward-confusion matrices, or per-stage data ablations. The official repository and model page release model artifacts and deployment guidance, not Forge, the training workspaces, reward services, or trajectory datasets.
