Frontier multimodal reports combine several evidence types that are easy to conflate: aggregate descriptions of hidden training corpora, downloadable model weights, public inference or reward code, and benchmark results. GLM-V is useful for the disclosure ledger because its current technical report describes a broad pre-training, long-chain-of-thought SFT, RLHF/RLVR, and curriculum-sampled GRPO pipeline, while the official release exposes models and selected components rather than the underlying training records.

The relevant data objects change by stage. Pre-training records include image-caption pairs, interleaved pages and books, OCR renderings, grounding boxes, GUI screenshots and DOM-derived question-answer pairs, videos, documents, diagrams, and text. SFT records pair multimodal prompts with standardized long-CoT responses. RL records add task/subdomain, offline and online difficulty, one or more policy rollouts, extracted boxed answers, verifier or reward-model scores, format/style judgments, and selection for an update. The report describes these schemas conceptually but does not release a joined record ledger.

Official artifacts have a different boundary. GLM-4.1V-9B-Thinking, GLM-4.1V-9B-Base, and GLM-4.5V weights are linked from the official repository; the two instruction-model Hugging Face cards are labeled MIT. The GitHub repository is Apache-2.0 and includes deployment examples plus a domain-specific reward system. No official pre-training corpus, long-CoT SFT set, RL prompt pool, difficulty labels, preference set, rollout log, accepted/rejected curriculum records, or full training infrastructure was confirmed. Open weights and verifier code therefore must not be recorded as an open training-data release.
