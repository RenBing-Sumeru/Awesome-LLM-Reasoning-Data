1. **Pre-training source pools.** The report starts from more than 10B image-text pairs drawn from LAION, DataComp, DFN, Wukong, and web search, and also names MINT, MMC4, OmniCorpus, more than 100M digitized books, academic papers, synthetic and natural OCR, grounding, GUI, video, and text sources. These are source or initial-pool quantities, not a released final-record manifest.

2. **Source-specific construction and filtering.** Image-caption data undergo resolution, solid-color, caption-length and image deduplication rules, CLIP filtering above 0.3, concept-balanced resampling, and factual recaptioning. Interleaved web data removes irrelevant images, advertisements, QR codes, and low-information layouts. Academic PDFs are parsed; OCR pages and tables are rendered; GLIPv2 supplies natural-image boxes; Playwright captures GUI screenshots and visible DOM elements; video uses integrity checks, embedding deduplication, and human-in-the-loop annotation.

3. **Reported pre-training products.** The report names 220M OCR images, 40M natural-image grounding annotations, more than 140M GUI question-answer pairs, and a 50M instruction-tuning mixture, plus video and interleaved corpora without complete final counts. It describes manual and automated contamination review for open-source instruction data. It does not publish the final unique-record count, per-source weights, source revisions, licenses, filtering yields, or train/evaluation overlap ledger.

4. **Pre-training schedule.** GLM-4.1V-Thinking and GLM-4.5V use the shared AIMv2-Huge-based vision architecture with GLM-4-9B-0414 and GLM-4.5-Air respectively. Multimodal pre-training runs for 120,000 steps at sequence length 8,192 and global batch 1,536; long-context continuation adds video and sequences beyond 8K, running 10,000 steps at length 32,768 and the same batch. Packing and model-specific tensor/expert/pipeline/context parallelism are described.

5. **Long-CoT SFT.** Curate Chinese- and English-dominant reasoning examples across verifiable and non-verifiable domains. The pretrained model filters prompts judged too easy or too hard. Responses use a think span and answer span, with exactly one region delimited by the `begin_of_box` and `end_of_box` special tokens for verifiable answers; GLM-4.5V omits explicit answer tags. Remove format violations, inconsistent/noisy reasoning, mixed-language phrasing, and redundant thoughts, and recycle selected examples from later RL checkpoints. Full-parameter SFT uses length 32,768 and global batch 32; record count and generator lineage are unknown.

6. **RL task preparation.** Identify verifiable sub-tasks in every domain, filter or synthesize question-answer pairs, convert unique-answer multiple choice into fill-in-the-blank, validate correctness, and assign offline difficulty from multi-model pass@k plus expert labels. Pilot RL runs test whether each domain's data and reward design produce learnable gains. Counts, source snapshots, pilot outcomes, and retained manifests are not released.

7. **Reward construction.** Extract the boxed answer for RLVR and apply task-specific rule, symbolic, geometric, functional, or semantic checks; use reward models for RLHF tasks. Shared format/style checks penalize invalid box use, mixed languages, repetition, poor fluency, or instruction noncompliance. The official repository releases a configurable VLM reward-system package with symbolic, multimodal, and agent verifiers, including LLM-judge fallbacks, but not the reward-model checkpoint or full production reward logs.

8. **RLCS and GRPO.** Blend domains in predetermined but undisclosed proportions and optimize with GRPO. Combine offline tiers with online rollout pass@k and subcategory performance, reweighting difficulty every iteration. Ratio-EMA expands sampling to offset all-correct/all-incorrect groups. Other reported choices include larger batches, forced answering after truncation, removal of KL and entropy losses, a higher upper clip, top-p 1, per-sample loss, load balancing, packing, and gradient accumulation. Exact rollouts, temperature, batch, learning rate, clip, steps, tokens, and compute remain unknown.
