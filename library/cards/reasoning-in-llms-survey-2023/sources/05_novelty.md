Earlier overviews commonly list reasoning tasks and methods without preserving where an intervention acts. This survey connects fully supervised, prompting, and hybrid branches to evaluation and interpretation, so a rationale can be recognized as training data, an inference artifact, or evidence depending on context. It introduces no new reasoning algorithm or benchmark; the novelty is the boundary-aware map and its synthesis of what observed LLM behavior does and does not establish. That map became a useful snapshot of the field through roughly 2022–2023.
