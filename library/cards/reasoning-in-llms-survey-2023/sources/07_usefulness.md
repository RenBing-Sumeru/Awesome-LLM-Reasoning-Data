Use the survey when screening a reasoning paper or planning a literature review. Supply the paper's prompts, training records, generated traces, feedback, and metrics; assign each to training, inference-time elicitation, self-improvement, evaluation, or interpretation, and output a claim-evidence table. Success means the paper can be described without using “reasoning” as an unexplained umbrella term. Do not use this 2023 snapshot as a complete taxonomy of later reasoning models or as evidence for a particular score.
