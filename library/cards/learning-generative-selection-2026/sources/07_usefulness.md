For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, this paper supplies a schema for separating candidate generation from candidate selection. A reusable selection record should preserve source dataset and item ID, problem text, generator and sampling parameters, every indexed candidate, raw verifier execution or symbolic result, binary label, candidate-set resampling attempt, filtering reason, ordering, selector checkpoint, selector reasoning, parsed index, format status, chosen-candidate label, scalar reward, and rollout seed. Such records support RLVR selector training, verifier-error audits, studies of pool size and correctness ratio, cross-generator transfer, and test-time-compute accounting. Current reuse is recipe-level only: without released manifests, candidates, labels, selector rollouts, checkpoints, and licenses, the paper does not provide an artifact that can be assumed ready for training.
