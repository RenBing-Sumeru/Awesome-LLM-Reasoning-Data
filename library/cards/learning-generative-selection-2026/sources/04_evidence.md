The main table evaluates selection from eight candidates produced by Qwen3-1.7B, 4B, or 8B generators. On Qwen3-1.7B candidate sets, the math-trained 1.7B selector reports 65.00, 54.17, and 36.25 accuracy on AIME24, AIME25, and HMMT25, compared with 48.75, 38.33, and 26.67 for prompted 1.7B GenSelect. On LiveCodeBench v6, the code-trained selector reports 36.84 versus 34.72 for prompted 1.7B GenSelect; the math-trained selector reports 36.45. When candidates come from Qwen3-4B or 8B, the math-trained 1.7B selector remains competitive with larger prompted selectors, supporting transfer beyond the training generator. These results measure selection accuracy under the paper's precomputed labels and candidate distributions. They do not establish that verifier labels are error-free, that the constructed training records are released or licensed, or that higher benchmark accuracy proves higher data quality.
