CL-bench contains 500 complex real-world contexts, 1,899 tasks, and 31,607 expert-verified rubrics. New domain knowledge, rule systems, procedures, or empirical laws required for each task are fully contained in the context and designed not to depend on pretraining memory. Released in JSONL, the data support context-learning evaluation, long-context judges, and reasoning over new rules, with licensing focused on evaluation and benchmarking.
