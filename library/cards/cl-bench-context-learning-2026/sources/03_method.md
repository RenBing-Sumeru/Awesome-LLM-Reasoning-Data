1. **Build novel contexts:** Domain experts author new knowledge, rules, and procedures that cannot be solved from pretraining memory.

2. **Design dependent tasks:** Multiple tasks require understanding, combining, and applying information from each context.

3. **Author rubrics:** Correct use of rules, states, constraints, and outputs is decomposed into 31,607 expert criteria.

4. **Evaluate models:** Models answer from context and task alone and are scored criterion by criterion; context version, length, and reasoning budget must be fixed.
