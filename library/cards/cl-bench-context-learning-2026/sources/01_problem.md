Long-context benchmarks mainly test retrieval or comprehension, while in-context learning often involves simple demonstration patterns. Real tasks require models to acquire new knowledge, rules, procedures, and empirical laws absent from pretraining and use them to solve complex problems. A model may retrieve a passage without truly learning or combining its content.

CL-bench has experts author novel contexts and dependent tasks and uses fine-grained rubrics to verify whether models learn and apply the new information.
