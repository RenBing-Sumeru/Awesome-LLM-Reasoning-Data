W²Bench evaluates whether a reward model ranks responses according to explicit writing requirements; WEval creates a natural partial order by asking a generator to answer prompts after one or more requirements are removed. WRL treats the full-instruction answer as chosen and requirement-dropped answers as rejected, trains a Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct reward model with Bradley--Terry loss, then uses it for GRPO. The paper releases code and data, but the paper does not establish that its synthetic order is a universal human-preference ground truth.
