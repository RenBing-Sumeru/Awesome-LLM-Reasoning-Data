A mathematical model can reach the correct answer while using invalid transformations, correcting earlier mistakes, or adding many irrelevant steps. Final accuracy treats such traces as equivalent to rigorous and concise solutions and cannot determine whether a longer chain of thought actually improves process quality.

The work builds MR-GSM8K with human labels for step validity and redundancy and calibrates the ReasonEval evaluator for process comparison and high-quality chain-of-thought filtering.
