The central artifact is MR-GSM8K/ReasonEval, containing roughly 3,000 mathematical solutions with step-level validity and redundancy labels. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use human step segmentation, validity and redundancy labeling, and evaluator calibration for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is evaluating process quality and filtering high-quality chains of thought.
