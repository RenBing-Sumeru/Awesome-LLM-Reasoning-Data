1. Positioning: MR-GSM8K/ReasonEval uses human step segmentation, validity and redundancy labeling, and evaluator calibration to create specialized feedback for failures that general step-quality evaluation evaluation misses.

2. Method handle: collect tasks and candidates, generate feedback, verify boundary cases, and organize by capability; label review is the decisive quality control.

3. Data handle: roughly 3,000 mathematical solutions with step-level validity and redundancy labels, with records centered on inputs, candidates, evaluation evidence, and feedback labels.

4. Evidence anchor: higher final-answer accuracy does not guarantee improved process validity, with conclusions bounded by model, candidate, and judge configurations.

5. Reuse decision: best suited to evaluating process quality and filtering high-quality chains of thought; contamination, false positives, and distributional stability must be checked.
