OpenGenAlign is a 33K-pair preference dataset and benchmark for long-context QA, data-to-text, and summarization. It defines four quality dimensions—hallucination, comprehensiveness, reliability, and efficiency—and asks o3 to compare responses with greater weight on the first two. Three independent o3 judgments and majority vote form each chosen/rejected label; a 100-pair-per-task human study reports 81% agreement with those labels. The paper links an official GitHub project, but its card had no verified official dataset download at intake. The data object is a prompt, two model outputs, four-dimension evaluation, and a voted preference label.
