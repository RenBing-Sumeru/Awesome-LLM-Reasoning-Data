Correctness is relative to the authors' definition of critical failure: the earliest failure from which the agent does not recover. That label can differ from a visible first error, terminal error, or user-facing harm. The taxonomy covers three domains and may miss failures in other tool ecosystems. Constraint evidence can be weak or false-positive, and the LLM judge may override, misread, or overfit to the violation log. Public reuse is constrained by unknown dataset URL, license, privacy status of production incident traces, exact prompts, judge model version, and runtime/tool-schema versions.
