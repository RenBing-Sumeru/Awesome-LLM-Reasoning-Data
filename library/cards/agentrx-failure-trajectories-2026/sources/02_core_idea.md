The contribution is a benchmark and diagnostic framework for the first unrecoverable failure in AI-agent trajectories. The core mechanism is to normalize heterogeneous logs, synthesize global and dynamic constraints from tool schemas, policies, and trajectory prefixes, evaluate those constraints step by step, and feed the violation log to an LLM judge. The evaluation surface is 115 failed trajectories from tau-bench, Flash, and Magentic-One/Who&When, annotated with critical steps and nine root-cause categories. Compared with prior final-score or first-failure labels, the direction signal is evidence-backed localization of the decisive unrecovered failure. The contract remains bounded by the authors' taxonomy, annotator process, LLM checker, and released artifact details.
