Use AgentRx as a schema reference for agent failure cards: store trajectory id, task, step index, actor/tool, observation, violation evidence, recovery status, critical-step label, and root-cause taxonomy label. It is also a recipe for building verifier-assisted agent debugging surfaces where constraints produce evidence before a judge makes the final attribution. For atlas use, it separates evaluation, diagnosis, and training reuse: the trajectories can audit agents, but turning them into reward data requires a separate check of label quality, privacy, and license.
