The paper reports 115 failed trajectories: 29 tau-bench failures from 115 sampled runs, 42 Flash trajectories with at least one agent failure, and 44 failed Magentic-One trajectories from Who&When. The strongest evidence is row-level annotation of failure events, critical steps, rationales, and categories, plus a diagnostic log that links violated constraints to trajectory evidence. Reported aggregate gains include 23.6 percentage points in failure localization and 22.9 points in root-causing over baselines; tau-bench step accuracy rises from 32.2% to 54.0% in the reported best setting. These numbers are bounded by the three domains, GPT-5 judge setup, authors' taxonomy, and availability of the underlying trajectories.
