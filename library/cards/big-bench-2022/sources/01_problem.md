BIG-bench asks how to quantify and extrapolate language-model capabilities across a broad, community-built suite rather than a single task family. The primary sources are the arXiv/TMLR paper, OpenReview entry, and the official google/BIG-bench repository. A data object is a benchmark task written in the BIG-bench format: examples with prompts/inputs, targets or multiple-choice options, metadata, and a task-specific scorer. It belongs here as a canonical static evaluation surface for model capability auditing; it is not a training set, an environment for action, or a homogeneous reasoning benchmark.
