The contribution is a 204-task collaborative benchmark suite authored by a large research community, together with a common task format and evaluation harness. The core mechanism is to collect many task types, run models through standardized prompting/evaluation APIs, and aggregate results to study scaling behavior and task-level variation. The feedback contract is task-specific: multiple-choice accuracy, exact/regex/string matching, or a custom programmatic metric. Closest comparisons are GLUE/SuperGLUE, MMLU, HELM, and narrower reasoning benchmarks; the direction label is broad static evaluation surface.
