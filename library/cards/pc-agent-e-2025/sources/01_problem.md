The primary sources are the current arXiv v2 paper, the accepted OpenReview text, and the official ICLR 2026 poster record; the paper first appeared in 2025 and is formally published as an ICLR 2026 conference paper. The Card also relies on the author-maintained PC-Agent-E repository, project page, Hugging Face dataset and model, and the WindowsAgentArena-V2 repository. These sources establish the paper identity, release boundary, code behavior, and evaluation surface rather than merely repeating the abstract.

The concrete problem is how to obtain useful computer-use supervision without collecting thousands of full human demonstrations. The paper begins with 312 retained human-completed Windows task trajectories, augments each human action with a reconstructed thought and nine alternative Claude 3.7 Sonnet thought-action decisions, and flattens the augmented episodes into approximately 27K action-level SFT samples. A released episode contains a companion task description plus per-step screenshot, normalized action, optional element and rectangle, marked screenshot, synthetic thought, and up to nine boost responses. The release contains 312 JSONL episodes, 312 task Markdown files, and 4,503 PNG screenshots; 4,503 images and 27K derived SFT rows must not be described as independent complete trajectories.

This belongs in `environment_agent_trajectory_data` because it exposes the conversion from executed GUI episodes into state-action supervision and because WindowsAgentArena-V2 supplies the environment-side evaluation contract. The boundary is equally important: the nine Claude alternatives are not executed branches, carry no post-action observation or environment reward, and therefore are not replayable trajectories. The work does not report RL, an online data-collection policy, or a training-time environmental verifier.

The Card is L4-ready at the content level because official paper, venue, code, data, model, construction scripts, training config, benchmark repository, and license surfaces were inspected. L4 does not mean the release is safe for unrestricted training reuse: screenshot privacy and rights, per-record lineage, replay-based correctness, synthetic-branch validity, and immutable versioning remain unresolved.
