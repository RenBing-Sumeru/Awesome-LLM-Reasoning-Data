- **Training feedback is not environmental.** Nine Claude alternatives per human step are never executed and receive no next observation, task evaluator result, or reward. Syntax-valid responses can still click the wrong control, use a stale coordinate, or contradict hidden application state. Treating all retained branches as positive SFT targets can create false positives; this is a curator inference from the released pipeline.
- **Termination labels are construction-contaminated.** Failed-ending records are removed, and `refinement.py` can rewrite the final surviving action to `finish`. `finish` is therefore partly a terminal token imposed by cleaning, not evidence that the environment goal was achieved. The 50-step decline from 36.0 to 31.4 also shows that termination awareness remains brittle.
- **Failure supervision is selectively absent.** Failed human endings are dropped, ordinary failed attempts are not retained as labeled negatives, and synthetic branches have no success/failure label. This can bias the model toward plausible positive actions and hide recovery behavior; the downstream effect is not quantified.
- **Collection and lineage are incomplete.** The LLMs, prompts, seed count, and mixture weights used to expand the task pool are unknown. Per-record task ancestry, Claude temperature/top-p, random seeds, rejection rates, cost, and the semantic-similarity encoder used for decontamination are also undisclosed.
- **Release reproducibility is partial.** The dataset is one ZIP and the GitHub repository has no tagged release, immutable manifest, or changelog. GPU model, pinned runtime/container, VM images, raw evaluation logs, and evaluator revision used for reported scores are not bundled. Coordinate-based GUI actions are sensitive to resolution, scaling, application version, and initialization drift.
- **Rights and privacy remain unresolved.** Repository, dataset-card, and model-card metadata state MIT, but annotator consent, screenshot redaction, PII review, application-asset rights, and third-party benchmark licenses are unknown. The MIT label alone does not establish that all visible desktop content can be redistributed or used for training.
- **Evaluation does not certify the data.** WindowsAgentArena-V2 uses mixed hand-written, programmatic, LLM-assisted, and limited human checks and has required repairs for infeasible tasks and evaluator bugs. Benchmark gains can coexist with invalid training branches, evaluator false positives/negatives, or task-distribution overfitting. OSWorld's infeasible-task behavior further limits cross-platform interpretation.
