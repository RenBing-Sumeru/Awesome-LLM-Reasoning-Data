The prior-work baseline is agent-environment, tool-use, and benchmark-harness papers in the same atlas category. The change introduced by MyPCBench: A Benchmark for Personally Intelligent Computer-Use Agents is the particular task object and feedback contract: personal computer-use tasks, app/web states, Docker/QEMU environment, actions, rubrics, and dataset records scored by MyPCBench rubrics, judge/scorer, and environment checks. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16748; project: https://mypcbench.com/; code: https://github.com/ljang0/MyPCBench; data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ljang0/MyPCBench; huggingface: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ljang0/MyPCBench; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.16748), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
