1. **Vertical domain:** The data directly targets deepfake imagery, so its cues and error patterns may not transfer to general visual reasoning.
2. **Bootstrap bias:** Most pointwise and pairwise labels are expanded by a generator–judge process, which may amplify the seed judge’s preferences and shared blind spots.
3. **Access restriction:** Although the structure is public and the dataset is marked Apache-2.0, downloading requires agreement to academic-use and information-sharing conditions.
