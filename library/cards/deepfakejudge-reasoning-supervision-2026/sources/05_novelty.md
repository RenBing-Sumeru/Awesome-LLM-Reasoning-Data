Traditional deepfake benchmarks treat explanations as auxiliary text and evaluate only classification, while general MLLM-judge data lacks pixel-grounded authenticity evidence. DeepfakeJudge places image, authenticity label, localized evidence, candidate reasoning, and pointwise/pairwise judgment within one data lineage and explicitly meta-evaluates whether a judge detects visually ungrounded rationales. Its novelty lies in bootstrapping large-scale judge supervision from a small set of human visual cues and releasing interconnected subsets for detection, reasoning generation, and evaluator training.
