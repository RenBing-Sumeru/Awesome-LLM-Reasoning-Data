Table 1 reports that, on the shared 64-item subsets, MetaScale has the highest listed GPT-4o score on Arena-Hard, MMLU-Pro, and GSM8K, and the highest listed Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct score on Arena-Hard and MMLU-Pro. The evidence is not uniformly favorable: for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on GSM8K, adding evolution lowers the reported accuracy from 84.38 to 82.81, although both exceed the listed baselines. Figure 2 shows steeper gains than Best-of-N as the GPT-4o sampling budget rises from 1 to 128 on Arena-Hard and MMLU-Pro. On the full Arena-Hard set with style control, Table 2 reports MetaScale-128 at 80.1 and o1-mini at 79.2 with overlapping 95% confidence intervals; without style control, Table 3 reports 89.0 for MetaScale-128 and 92.0 for o1-mini. These comparisons support evaluation of the studied search policy but expose sensitivity to model, task, subset, judge, and style control. They do not demonstrate that unreleased meta-thoughts, retrieved examples, UCB logs, or reward scores are accurate, licensed, or suitable as training data.
