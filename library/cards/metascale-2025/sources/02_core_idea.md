MetaScale has three phases. Initialization asks the target LLM to self-compose task-specific meta-thoughts and also retrieves the top 8 embedding-similar tasks from WildChat, prompting the model to abstract and adapt their strategies. Selection treats each meta-thought as a multi-armed-bandit arm and chooses the largest UCB value, combining the arm's empirical mean Skywork reward with an exploration bonus based on total attempts and selection count. The selected meta-thought conditions a new response, whose scalar reward updates that arm. At every evolution interval (k), high-UCB meta-thoughts become parents and the target LLM generates refined children; the paper does not use literal text-level crossover or mutation. After (T) attempts, the system returns the response with the largest reward-model score. The feedback contract is mixed: a learned outcome reward drives search, exact answer checks evaluate GSM8K, extraction plus answer matching evaluates MMLU-Pro, and an LLM judge evaluates Arena-Hard.
