MetaScale addresses a test-time scaling problem in which a model can sample more responses but still relies on a fixed prompting or reasoning structure. The paper argues that one strategy may not transfer across mathematics, multidisciplinary questions, and open-ended instruction following, so the system should search over how to reason as well as over candidate answers. The paper's auditable unit is a query-specific trajectory containing initial meta-thoughts, selected strategies, candidate responses, scalar reward-model scores, UCB statistics, evolved strategies, and the final highest-scoring response. A meta-thought has two textual fields—a cognitive mindset and a problem-solving strategy. This fits the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track because the relevant object is the evolving pool and its selection history, not just the terminal answer. The accepted version appears in Findings of ACL 2026, pages 11828–11842.
