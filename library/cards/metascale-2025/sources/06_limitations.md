MetaScale optimizes and finally selects with the same outcome reward model, so reward-model error can be reinforced through both UCB updates and terminal selection; the paper does not provide a separate calibration or adversarial robustness study. Retrieval provenance is incomplete: WildChat is named and top-8 similarity is reported, but the dataset version, embedding model, index, retrieved IDs, and contamination checks are not disclosed. Important execution settings—UCB β, evolution interval (k), exact initial-pool size, MetaScale decoding temperature, and seeds—remain unknown. The main matched tables use only 64 random items per benchmark without released identities. Arena-Hard results change under style control, and MMLU-Pro assigns a random option after extraction failure. The authors identify English-only tasks and single-model experiments as limitations; only two target models are evaluated. No official code, meta-thought pool, reward log, UCB history, generated-response set, or sample manifest was verified. The ACL paper's CC BY 4.0 status does not establish reuse rights for WildChat-derived records or generated traces.
