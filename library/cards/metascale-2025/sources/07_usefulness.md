For the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track, MetaScale suggests a strategy-level trace schema: benchmark item and retrieval query; retrieved WildChat IDs and similarity scores; initial cognitive mindsets and problem-solving strategies; arm selection counts, empirical means, UCB values, and β; every selected meta-thought, generated response, and scalar reward; evolution interval, parent set, child strategies, and lineage; attempt budget; final argmax response; and an evaluation result kept separate from the search reward. Such records could support studies of exploration versus exploitation, retrieval influence, strategy diversity, reward hacking, and scaling efficiency. The paper supports test-time construction and evaluation use, not SFT, preference training, or RL reuse. Without a released trace set and licenses, this remains a schema inferred from the method rather than an available dataset.
