Prior test-time methods commonly sample answers under one prompt, refine a response, or search with a verifier. MetaScale changes the search variable: each arm is a textual strategy rather than an answer, and the pool itself changes during inference. Its initialization combines model-authored strategies with abstractions from retrieved WildChat examples; its selector uses accumulated arm rewards rather than scoring independent candidates only; and its LLM-mediated evolution produces new strategy arms from high-UCB parents. Genetic algorithms, UCB, retrieval, personas, meta-reasoning, and reward-model selection are not individually new. The contribution is their query-local integration into a fixed-budget strategy-search trajectory. For this atlas, that trajectory is the novelty signal: the lineage between retrieved examples, parent and child strategies, arm statistics, candidate responses, and final selection should be retained if the method is ever released as data.
