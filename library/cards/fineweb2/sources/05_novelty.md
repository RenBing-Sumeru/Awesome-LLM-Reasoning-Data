Prior open-web corpora already used filtering and deduplication. The direction signal here is the explicit attempt to make those steps adaptable across languages and to connect rebalancing and evaluation-task selection to the curation recipe. It is not a new reasoning-supervision format, a preference dataset, or a proof that its corpus is fit for every post-training use. Reuse therefore requires checking the official release artifacts, including data license, provenance, language coverage, and contamination controls.
