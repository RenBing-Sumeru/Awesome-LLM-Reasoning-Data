Open multilingual pre-training data must be filtered, deduplicated, rebalanced, and evaluated across languages, yet those choices are often described as separate implementation details. The official arXiv record at https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20920 identifies the paper as a 2025 release and reports a FineWeb-derived pipeline adapted to more than 1000 languages. This Card examines the lifecycle of a data release, not a recommendation to use any particular corpus. Its object is a curated text corpus plus the documented processing decisions that produced it.
