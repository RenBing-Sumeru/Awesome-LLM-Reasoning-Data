HotpotQA is an EMNLP 2018 benchmark paper for diverse, explainable multi-hop question answering over Wikipedia. The concrete gap was that many QA datasets could be solved from a single paragraph or did not expose the supporting evidence needed to audit multi-hop reasoning.

The data object is a crowdsourced question with an answer and sentence-level supporting facts, evaluated in distractor and full-wiki settings. This Card includes it as an evaluation surface for retrieval-plus-reading and evidence-grounded reasoning, not as a verifier for open-ended explanations or a training recipe.
