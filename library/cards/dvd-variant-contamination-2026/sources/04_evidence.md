Claim — variance of local generation behavior is a more reliable signal of variant contamination than static lexical or likelihood signals.

Controlled setup — variant-contaminated and clean conditions are constructed from Omni-MATH and SuperGPQA, with Qwen2.5 and Llama3.1 models fine-tuned at different scales. DVD is compared with perplexity, Min-k%, CDD, and embedding similarity; the controlled difference is exposure to semantic/structural variants.

Result — the paper reports DVD consistently outperforming all listed baselines across its datasets and model families, with robustness to hyperparameters. The accessible primary abstract does not provide a table value that can be safely quoted here.

Boundary — results are from simulated fine-tuning contamination and two domains. They support the detector under those constructions, not proof for unknown real pretraining corpora.
