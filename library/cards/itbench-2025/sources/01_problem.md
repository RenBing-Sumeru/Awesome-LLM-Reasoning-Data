The canonical source is the final ICML 2025 paper in PMLR 267, supplemented by the official ITBench framework, ITBench-Lite, ITBench-Trajectories, and ITBench-SRE-Agent releases at the audited revisions recorded in `paper.yaml`. The final paper contains **102 scenarios: 42 SRE, 50 CISO, and 10 FinOps**. The earlier arXiv manuscript's 94-scenario count is version history, not the final benchmark size.

ITBench addresses a specific evaluation gap: general-purpose AI agents must investigate and act in partially observed operational systems, yet static question-answer benchmarks do not expose tool errors, evolving state, hazardous actions, or the difference between choosing to stop and actually restoring the desired state. SRE scenarios are based on incidents observed in the authors' SaaS products, CISO scenarios derive from CIS benchmarks, and FinOps scenarios derive from FinOps Foundation domains and capabilities; incident-level provenance is not disclosed.

The paper's primary evaluation object is an `M/E/T/D` scenario tuple: metadata, an operational environment, triggering events, and a desired outcome. A run adds partial observations, actions and tool calls, environment responses, a structured final answer or final state, an explicit stop event, and domain-specific evaluation. Later official artifacts add two distinct data objects: ITBench-Lite supplies **65 static scenarios** (35 SRE snapshots, 15 FinOps synthetic-anomaly scenarios, and 15 CISO static scenarios), while ITBench-Trajectories supplies SRE ReAct session logs and associated outputs/judgments where present.

This Card belongs in `environment_agent_trajectory_data` because the environment, state/action history, terminal predicate, and feedback object jointly define the record. It does not treat benchmark scores as proof of data quality, and it does not infer a training recipe from evaluation logs. The sources support L4-depth bilingual content for human review, while canonical metadata remains at `L3_summary_ready` and the overall release status remains `partial`.
