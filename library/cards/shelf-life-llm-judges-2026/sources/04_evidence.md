On DeepScaleR, all weak-response-trained judges have negative FutureProof values (Fig. 3), showing degradation on strong responses. Refreshing on strong responses is positive in every setting; DPO reaches +7.6 absolute points for Mistral-24B. Strong-trained judges are comparatively backward-compatible, and continual DPO moves FutureProof closer to zero (Figs. 4–6). The result supports updating under these generators/datasets, not universal temporal generalization.
