Use this Card to audit the boundary between an official frontier disclosure and a reusable post-training artifact. It establishes source-backed anchors for four questions: which source classes are named; which high-level feedback families are named; which safety/product/evaluation mechanisms are reported; and which data, verifier, filter, and reproducibility details remain unknown.

It can support comparison across Track 12 reports without treating shared terms such as “verifiable rewards,” “model grading,” “tool-use RL,” or “test-time compute” as evidence that their underlying data objects or feedback contracts are comparable. It is suitable for disclosure analysis and interpretation of reported safety/evaluation evidence, not direct training-data reuse or recipe replication.
