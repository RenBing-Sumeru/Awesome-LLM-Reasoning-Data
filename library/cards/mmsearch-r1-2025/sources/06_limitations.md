Search-required labels are policy-relative: eight rollouts from one trained model do not establish an intrinsic property of a question, and discarding all-failure items removes hard cases. Exact match favors concise answers but can reject valid variants or reward unsupported guessing. The search penalty treats every search as the same cost and may suppress useful verification. Format compliance does not measure reasoning or evidence quality.

Live SerpApi results, webpages, Jina parsing, and Qwen3-32B summaries can drift or disappear. Cached image-search results improve partial replay but do not pin text-search content. Raw RL rollouts, rejected alternatives, query/retrieval relevance labels, reward logs, seeds, and complete environment snapshots are not confirmed as released. The dataset’s Apache-2.0 tag does not by itself resolve rights for every web image or page, and semantic decontamination remains unknown.
