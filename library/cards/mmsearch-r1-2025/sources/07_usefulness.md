MMSearch-R1 is useful both as a released prompt dataset and as a schema for multimodal search-agent trajectories. Auditable episodes should retain FVQA item/revision IDs, search-need labels and their policy dependency, every model span and action, exact query, returned URL/document IDs, thumbnails or summaries, timestamps, tool/model revisions, loss masks, terminal answer, both reward components, selected rollout, and rejected siblings. Without these fields, the method can be reproduced approximately but the original interaction corpus cannot be replayed.
