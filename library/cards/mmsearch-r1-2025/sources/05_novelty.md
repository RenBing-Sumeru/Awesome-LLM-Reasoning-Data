For reasoning-data curation, the distinctive contribution is the coupling of a search-need label with an executable multimodal episode grammar. Image identification and textual fact retrieval are separate actions, environment observations are excluded from the policy loss, and a search cost shapes when a correct-answer trajectory is preferred. The individual components—web search, GRPO, exact match, and format rewards—are established; the contribution is their integration with a released search-balanced VQA set.
