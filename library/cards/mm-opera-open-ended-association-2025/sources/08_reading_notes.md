1. **One-sentence positioning:** It is a systematic 11,497-instance benchmark for open-ended associative reasoning in vision-language models.
2. **Task takeaway:** RIA measures remote concept linking, while ICA measures inference of association rules from context.
3. **Data takeaway:** Each item requires a free-form answer and an explicit reasoning path rather than only a final label.
4. **Evaluation takeaway:** A process-reward-informed judge separately examines the association and the reasoning chain.
5. **Main risk:** The valid-answer space is incomplete, and the judge may exhibit cultural and presentation-format biases.
