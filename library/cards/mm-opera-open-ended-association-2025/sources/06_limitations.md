Open-ended association lacks a complete answer space, so a judge may reject uncommon but reasonable cultural associations or favor longer and more formally structured reasoning. Although the dataset analyzes multilingual and cultural variation, 11,497 instances cannot represent every knowledge background, and the source images and concepts may reflect internet and popular-culture biases. Explicit reasoning paths are also not guaranteed to faithfully represent internal model processes. Reuse should therefore include multiple judges or human review and should report conclusion validity, association diversity, and path quality separately rather than relying on one aggregate score.
