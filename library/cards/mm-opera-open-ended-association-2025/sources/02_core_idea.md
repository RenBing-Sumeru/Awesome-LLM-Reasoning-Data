MM-OPERA contains 11,497 instances across two open-ended tasks: Remote-Item Association and In-Context Association. The former asks models to discover a shared connection among semantically distant images or concepts, while the latter requires inferring a new association rule from context. Both tasks demand free-form answers and explicit reasoning paths. The paper also introduces process-reward-informed judging strategies that decompose evaluation into dimensions such as association validity, path coherence, and task-constraint satisfaction, followed by analyses across abilities, domains, languages, and cultures.
