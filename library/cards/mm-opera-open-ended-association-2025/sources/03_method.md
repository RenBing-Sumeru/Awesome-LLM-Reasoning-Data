1. **Ability definition:** Drawing on the psychology of association, the authors distinguish divergent association from convergent associative reasoning and instantiate them as RIA and ICA.
2. **Instance construction:** Images, conceptual items, and contextual demonstrations are combined while controlling semantic distance, explainable relations, and difficulty, yielding 11,497 open-ended instances.
3. **Response requirement:** Models must provide not only an associated conclusion but also an explicit reasoning path connecting the elements, enabling diagnosis of shallow pattern matching.
4. **Judge design:** Fine-grained evaluation requirements are generated for each task, intermediate reasoning and final answers are scored separately, and the scores are aggregated.
5. **Validity analysis:** Instance sensitivity, judge agreement, and cross-domain, cross-language, and cross-cultural analyses are used to test whether the benchmark measures the intended ability.
