MM-OPERA does not merely extend object recognition, visual question answering, or closed-form logic problems; it defines association as an independent dimension of multimodal intelligence. Its novelty lies in open-ended outputs rather than answer options, recording both answers and reasoning paths, organizing remote and contextual association through psychometric principles, and applying process-reward ideas rather than a single holistic impression score. Compared with creativity benchmarks, it emphasizes explainable connections among visual or conceptual items. Compared with traditional reasoning benchmarks, it permits multiple valid answers while requiring the association chain to remain auditable.
