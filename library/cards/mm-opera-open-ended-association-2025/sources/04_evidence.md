The paper evaluates a range of advanced vision-language models and systematically examines instance sensitivity, the validity of LLM-judge strategies, and performance differences across abilities and data groups. Experiments show that models performing strongly on conventional multimodal benchmarks still struggle with open-ended association, cross-concept linking, and explainable reasoning. Process-aware judging provides finer distinctions than scoring only final answers. The authors also analyze variation across languages, cultures, and domains, suggesting that the benchmark is not limited to one English-centric knowledge distribution, although open-ended scores remain constrained by the evaluator model’s capabilities.
