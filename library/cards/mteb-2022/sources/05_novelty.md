The prior-work baseline is nearby benchmark and evaluation-surface datasets that share the same feedback contract. The change introduced by MTEB: Massive Text Embedding Benchmark is the particular task object and feedback contract: dataset adapters, embedding model outputs, task-specific labels, and aggregate leaderboard metrics; Benchmark size: 初版覆盖 8 类 embedding 任务、58 个数据集；后续 leaderboard 持续扩展。 scored by task-specific metrics such as nDCG, accuracy, F1, Spearman correlation, and clustering scores. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.07316; code: https://github.com/embeddings-benchmark/mteb; huggingface: https://huggingface.co/spaces/mteb/leaderboard; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.07316), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
