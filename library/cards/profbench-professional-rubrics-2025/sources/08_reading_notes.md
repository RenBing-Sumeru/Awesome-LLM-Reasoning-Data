1. **Position:**  Professional criteria test both answering and judging.
2. **Method:**  Forty tasks, 15–59 rubrics per task, multiple responses, and expert labels.
3. **Data:**  More than 7K response–criterion pairs across four PhD/MBA domains.
4. **Evidence:**  GPT-5-high reaches 65.9%; judge cost drops by 2–3 orders.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for professional judges; small-sample rankings need uncertainty.
