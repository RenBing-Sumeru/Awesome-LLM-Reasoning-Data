1. **Design tasks:** Experts from four domains create tasks requiring document processing, synthesis, and report generation with context files.

2. **Author rubrics:** Experts decompose answer quality into 15–59 criteria covering facts, derivations, completeness, and deliverables.

3. **Collect labels:** Several models generate responses, and experts judge compliance criterion by criterion, producing more than 7K pairs.

4. **Calibrate judges:** Answerer and judge roles, prompts, and model combinations are adjusted to reduce self-enhancement bias; response pools and cost settings must be fixed.
