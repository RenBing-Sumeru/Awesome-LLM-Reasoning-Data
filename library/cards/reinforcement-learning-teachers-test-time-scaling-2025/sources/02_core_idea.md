A Reinforcement-Learned Teacher starts from Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and receives each question together with its known solution. It outputs tagged solution and explanation sections. For downstream SFT, the explanation tokens are extracted into the student's reasoning format and followed by the known solution, yielding a question–reasoning–answer record without extra language-model postprocessing.

The teacher's reward has two student-conditioned components. The first uses the frozen student's mean and minimum log-probability over solution tokens after seeing the explanation. The second uses KL divergence over explanation tokens to penalize logical jumps that are plausible only when the teacher can already see the solution but not from the student's question-only context. A format violation receives -1. GRPO optimizes relative reward within groups; the reward is a learnability proxy, not a formal checker of every reasoning step.
