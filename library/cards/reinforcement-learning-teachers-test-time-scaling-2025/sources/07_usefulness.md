For the Data Construction and Open Release Recipes track, RLT is a concrete example of teacher-trace generation whose feedback is defined by the downstream consumer. It helps researchers specify five linked objects: known question-solution seeds, answer-conditioned teacher explanations, frozen-student dense rewards, raw distillation records, and the resulting student checkpoint.

Use the recipe to study student-aware trace rewards, cold-start construction, teacher-size efficiency, reward ablation, and cross-domain trace generation. A responsible reproduction should pin code and upstream revisions, release the trained teacher, record every candidate and reward component, publish exact subsets, test with multiple reward students, audit answer leakage and step validity, document decontamination, and license the generated data. Do not infer trace quality solely from student benchmark gains.
