The paper shifts reinforcement learning from “solve without seeing the answer” to “teach effectively with the answer available.” The important distinction is objective alignment: a teacher is rewarded through the intended student's token probabilities rather than only through final-answer correctness. This produces a dense signal even when the small base model cannot discover a hard solution unaided.

Compared with common trace-distillation pipelines, the method trains the generator for downstream learnability and uses its outputs without a separate stylistic postprocessor. It also exposes the answer-conditioned format, two reward components, GRPO settings, and student transfer experiments. The contribution remains a recipe and code release rather than a complete data release because traces, rejected candidates, rewards, and the trained teacher are not published.
