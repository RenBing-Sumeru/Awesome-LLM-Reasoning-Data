For the rollout/search/test-time-trace track, TrajSelector is useful as a concrete schema for retaining one trajectory from a candidate set: preserve the prompt and ground truth, every candidate trace, exact step boundaries, sampler and layer identity, boundary hidden vectors, outcome-checker result, three-class step probabilities, pooled score, N, decoding parameters, and final membership decision. Publishing those fields would make selector errors and compute-quality tradeoffs auditable.

The verifier can serve two evidence-backed uses: Best-of-N answer selection at inference and preliminary offline SFT-data ranking. A reuse should not turn the predicted score into a claim of verified reasoning quality. It should calibrate scores, compare alternative segmentation and pooling, audit Math-Verify failures, retain rejected candidates, test cross-sampler transfer, report wall-clock and memory costs, and run contamination checks before treating selected traces as training data.
