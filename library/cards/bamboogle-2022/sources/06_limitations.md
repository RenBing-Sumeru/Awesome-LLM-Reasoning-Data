Bamboogle is small and public, so it is fragile as a leaderboard and high-risk for contamination. Correctness is not mechanically verified beyond short-answer matching or human judgment, and alternative valid aliases can affect scoring. Self-ask traces are elicited text, not guaranteed faithful reasoning. Search-augmented results depend on a mutable external search engine. The paper should not be read as proving general compositional reasoning; it isolates one QA-style failure mode under particular prompts and models.
