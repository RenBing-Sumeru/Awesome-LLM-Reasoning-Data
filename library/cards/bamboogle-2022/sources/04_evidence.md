The main row-level evidence is whether the model's short final answer matches the reference answer, and for gap analysis whether each needed subanswer was correct. The official Bamboogle dataset page records a 125-question adversarial set, while the paper reports that larger GPT-3-family models improve single-hop performance faster than multi-hop composition and that self-ask/self-ask-with-search narrows the gap. The evidence boundary is important: aggregate accuracy is sensitive to prompt exemplars, answer normalization, human grading, search results at evaluation time, and the exact exported version of the small public dataset.
