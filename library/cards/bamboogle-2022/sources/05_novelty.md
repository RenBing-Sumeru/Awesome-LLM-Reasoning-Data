The prior baseline is open-domain QA evaluation where success is only the final answer, plus chain-of-thought prompts that may produce unstructured rationales. This work changes the audit surface by explicitly measuring cases where subanswers are available but composition fails, and by making follow-up questions a prompt-level contract. The direction signal is that decomposed interaction can be inspected or attached to search. What is not new: multi-hop QA, short-answer grading, and search-augmented QA already existed. Before reuse, inspect the small dataset's license, exact export, grading rubrics, search snapshot, and whether Bamboogle questions are now contaminated in model training.
