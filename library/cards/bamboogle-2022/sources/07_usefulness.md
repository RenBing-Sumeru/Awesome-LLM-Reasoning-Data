The card is useful as a schema for compositional QA audits: store original question, gold answer, subquestions, intermediate answers, final answer, grading decision, prompt template, and any external search results. It is also useful when comparing direct answering, chain-of-thought, and structured decomposition prompts. For this atlas, the reusable idea is the feedback contract around subproblem success versus final composition failure, not the small public dataset alone.
