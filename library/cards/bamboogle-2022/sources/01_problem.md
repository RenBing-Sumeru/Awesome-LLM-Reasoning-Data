The paper asks why language models can answer the subquestions needed for a compositional question but still fail to compose those answers into the final answer. The primary source is the Findings of EMNLP 2023 paper, with the arXiv preprint, official self-ask repository, and official Bamboogle dataset page as artifacts. Bamboogle is the evaluation surface most relevant to this card: 125 adversarial compositional QA questions designed to be difficult for Google and to require combining facts. It belongs here as an answer-level compositional reasoning benchmark; it is not a training recipe or a verified proof environment.
