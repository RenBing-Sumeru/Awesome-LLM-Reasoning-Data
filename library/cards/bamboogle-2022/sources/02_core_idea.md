The contribution is a diagnostic definition of the compositionality gap plus a structured prompting method, self-ask, that makes the model ask and answer follow-up questions before producing the final answer. The evaluation object is a compositional question with a short reference answer and, for the gap analysis, related subquestions. The feedback contract is answer correctness for the final question and subquestion correctness when measuring the gap; grading may involve exact-match-assisted or human judgment for short answers. Closest comparisons are direct prompting, chain-of-thought prompting, and self-ask with a search engine attached.
