For Track 5, this is a useful audited example of the distinction between a full rollout trace and a selected training trace. It exposes the selector, representation change, length filter, SFT target, and later RL reward contract, allowing readers to separate construction claims from verifier claims.

Reuse as a training-data artifact remains limited: researchers would need paper-matched rows, entropy and mask records, source manifests, and reward/checker logs before they could audit or faithfully reproduce the reported data object.
