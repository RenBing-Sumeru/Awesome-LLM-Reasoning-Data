The arXiv abstract and official project page report 12,751 text-to-SQL pairs, 95 databases, 33.4 GB of database content, and 37 domains. The paper reports an early chat-model execution accuracy of 40.08% against human performance of 92.96%, showing a large gap under the official setting. GitHub documents the row schema: db_id, question, evidence, SQL, database_description, sqlite database, and gold SQL file. Evidence is decisive only when the exact database dump and evaluator are pinned; leaderboard numbers have changed as new models and cleaned splits appeared.
