1. **One-sentence positioning:** It tests whether multimodal judges can follow multiple, switchable, and conflicting criteria.
2. **Method takeaway:** Difficult response pairs, human criterion preferences, switching and conflict tests, and three dedicated metrics.
3. **Data takeaway:** It spans open generation and verifiable reasoning with both criterion-level and holistic labels.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Across 25 LMMs, holistic critic fine-tuning does not reliably transfer to pluralistic judging.
5. **Reuse decision:** It suits controllable reward/judge training, but criterion disagreement must not be collapsed into one unquestioned label.
