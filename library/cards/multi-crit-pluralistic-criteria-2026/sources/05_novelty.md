Earlier MLLM-as-a-judge datasets typically assign one holistic winner to each pair or compute multidimensional scores under a fixed rubric, without testing whether the judge follows the currently specified standard. Multi-Crit stores human preferences for the same pair under multiple criteria and explicitly constructs criterion-switching and conflict cases. Multidimensional scoring is not new; the novelty is treating the criterion as a controllable variable that separates general judging ability from judging under different value functions.
