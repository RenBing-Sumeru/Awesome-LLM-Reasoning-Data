1. **Criterion subjectivity:** Boundaries and conflicts in open-ended tasks vary by culture, use case, and annotator. Reuse should preserve disagreement rather than only majority labels.
2. **Pairwise limitation:** The benchmark mainly tests pairwise criterion following and does not fully cover absolute scoring, long-list ranking, or realistic multi-turn review.
3. **Training contamination:** Fine-tuning on public test pairs would quickly invalidate meta-evaluation, requiring strict separation and hidden or refreshed tests.
