The paper reports system-level evidence rather than record-level validation. Across the four benchmark families, RPM-MCTS improves average pass@1 over the corresponding base models by 11.90% for Qwen3-8B, 7.71% for Qwen3-235B-A22B, and 10.86% for Claude Sonnet 3.7 under the reported protocol. Removing knowledge-base rewards lowers the Qwen3-235B-A22B average by 1.05%, with a 4.67% average drop on APPS-competition and CodeContests; removing execution rewards causes the largest ablation decline. The paper also reports about 15% lower token use than the prior MCTS comparator for Qwen3-235B-A22B and Claude Sonnet 3.7. Fine-tuning Doubao-1.5-pro-32K on the 2.4K RPM-MCTS samples plus 170K foundational samples yields reported gains from 0.9 to 4.9 points across five downstream benchmarks. These results support utility under the tested search and training configurations, but they do not verify every retrieved analogy, reflected prefix, or distilled sample.
