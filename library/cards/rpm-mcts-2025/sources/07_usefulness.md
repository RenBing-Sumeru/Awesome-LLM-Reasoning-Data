For the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track, RPM-MCTS is useful as a schema and construction recipe for recording how test-time search turns candidate algorithmic steps into a selected code solution. A reusable trace should preserve the source problem and split, knowledge-base revision and retrieved record IDs, every candidate node, embeddings or similarity scores, pruning decisions, UCB and backed-up values, generated code, public-test outputs, LLM judgments, reflection text, error boundary, retained prefix, final path, token cost, and all model and sandbox revisions. Such traces could support search-policy analysis, verifier calibration, failure auditing, compute-efficiency studies, or SFT after an independent quality filter. The paper’s 2.4K experiment establishes a possible training use, not an available dataset or a general guarantee that MCTS-selected traces are suitable for post-training.
