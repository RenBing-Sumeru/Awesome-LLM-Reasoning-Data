The release contains about 103K items and reports distributions over visual types, mathematical topics, difficulty, and decontamination. Training experiments show that SFT or verifiable reinforcement on the data improves several visual-math benchmarks while retaining general capabilities, and ablations identify scale, visual diversity, and verification filtering as important factors. This supports training utility but does not prove every image semantics and reference solution correct; model and compute budgets must be controlled in comparisons.
