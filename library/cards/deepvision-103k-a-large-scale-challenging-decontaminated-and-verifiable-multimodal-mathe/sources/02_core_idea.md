DeepVision-103K releases roughly 103K visual mathematics problems. Core records combine an image, question, reference answer, and provenance or topic metadata, spanning diverse K–12 knowledge points and visual forms. Its feedback contract parses final answers into comparable objects and applies deterministic mathematical rules, enabling direct outcome rewards for RLVR. The current official dataset page is under skylenage/DeepVision-103K, so older organization paths should not be treated as the sole reference.
