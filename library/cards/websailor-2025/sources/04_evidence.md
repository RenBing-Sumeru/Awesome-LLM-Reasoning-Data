Table 1 evaluates WebSailor under the same ReAct implementation on BrowseComp-en, BrowseComp-zh, XBench-DeepSearch, and a 103-case text-only GAIA subset. At nonzero-temperature pass@1, the reported scores are 3.3/9.7/27.7/33.0 for 3B, 6.7/14.2/34.3/37.9 for 7B, 10.5/25.5/53.3/53.2 for 32B, and 12.0/30.1/55.0/55.4 for 72B. These are author-reported results, not independent reproductions.

The strongest directly comparable open-source baselines in Table 1 vary by benchmark. WebDancer-32B reports 2.5/14.1/38.7/40.7, while WebDancer-QwQ reports 3.8/18.0/39.0/51.5. Thus WebSailor-32B and 72B lead these listed open baselines across all four columns, while WebSailor-3B/7B do not dominate every larger baseline. The proprietary DeepResearch row remains much higher on BrowseComp-en (51.5 versus 12.0 for WebSailor-72B), so “closing the gap” should not be read as full parity across all settings.

Table 2 tests unfiltered SailorFog-QA difficulty. o4-mini reaches 47.3 pass@1 and DeepSeek-R1 38.9, compared with 90.2/84.4 on WebDancer-QA and 26.3/9.5 on BrowseComp-en. Figure 3 further shows a longer tool-call tail than WebDancer, with many correct pre-filter trajectories above five calls and some above twenty. Tool-call count is only a proxy for reasoning difficulty, and the authors acknowledge that ambiguity and non-unique answers also lower SailorFog-QA accuracy (Paper §5.3).

The training analysis is directionally supportive but incomplete. Figure 5 reports improvements from RL over the RFT models across all four benchmarks and larger gains on BrowseComp; Figure 6 shows that direct RL improves but converges below the cold-started 32B model, with fewer tool calls. Section 4.2 claims DUPO is approximately 2–3 times faster than DAPO-style sequential batch refilling. No full learning curves, variance across training seeds, compute budget, judge agreement, trajectory-count ablation, or independent replay is available.

The release evidence is narrower than the experimental evidence. The official repository’s only SailorFog data file contains 20 valid UTF-8 JSONL rows with `question` and `answer`; it does not contain thoughts, actions, observations, rewards, success flags, failures, splits, or provenance. Official 3B, 7B, and 32B model pages exist, but no official 72B model page was confirmed. These facts support model inference and method reading, not reconstruction of the reported training set.
