The paper reports that, on SWE-Bench Verified, Qwen-2.5-72B-Instruct rises from 9.4% PASS@1 to 22.4% after Agent-RLVR, compared with 20.8% for its rejection-sampled Agent-SFT baseline. Adding the patch reward model raises reported PASS@1 to 27.8%. Its guidance ablation reports an average of 165 successful rollouts (20.3%) with guidance versus 138 (16.9%) without, and fewer empty patches. These experiments support the claims that guidance changes rollout yield, offline preference training changes model behavior, and the same patch data can train a selector under the studied setup. They do not establish that the unreleased trajectories or guidance are complete, correctly attributed, contamination-free, licensed for reuse, or uniformly high quality. Six SWE-Bench Verified instances did not compile and were retained in the denominator. The official Scale Labs page verifies the paper and affiliation, but no official code, data, model, or trajectory release was found.
