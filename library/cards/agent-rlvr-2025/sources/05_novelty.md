Relative to ordinary RLVR on independently successful rollouts, Agent-RLVR inserts a teacher-assisted reattempt between failure and policy update, using environment information to make new successful trajectories reachable while retaining unit-test execution as the terminal verifier. Relative to rejection-sampled SFT, it constructs same-task winning/losing full-trajectory pairs for offline DPO; relative to plain test-time sampling, it reuses the collected patch data to train a pairwise reward model for selection. The direction signal for this atlas is the explicit join among environment state, failed trace, privileged guidance, reattempt, test reward, and downstream preference pair. Guidance, offline DPO, unit-test grading, and Agentless-style localization/repair are not individually new; the contribution is their particular construction loop for sparse-reward software-agent training.
