The dataset contains issue descriptions, complete repository snapshots, Docker environments, regression and focused tests, guidance, and agent trajectories. The paper says 593 problems come from SWE-Gym and 219 are self-collected with a similar scraping and annotation pipeline, although these counts sum to 812 rather than the reported 817 environments. It samples 16 trajectories per problem during guided and unguided collection, yielding 8,186 trajectories, 4,093 positive. On-policy traces come from Qwen-2.5-72B-Instruct and off-policy traces from the guidance model, claude-3-7-sonnet-20250219. A simplified Agentless scaffold retains localization and repair. Twenty percent of positive non-guided trajectories are used for SFT for five epochs at learning rate 1e-5 and sequence length 8k; the complete preference data is used for one epoch of DPO at 1e-6. Each winning trajectory passes all task tests and is paired with an incorrect same-task trajectory. The separate Qwen-2.5-Coder-32B reward model is trained for 500 steps with pairwise loss, learning rate 1e-5, and warmup ratio 0.05, then ranks k=32 patches. Collection temperature, seeds, exact task list, and the accounting between per-task samples and final trajectory count are undisclosed.
