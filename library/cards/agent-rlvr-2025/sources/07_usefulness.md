For the rollout/search/test-time trace track, Agent-RLVR is useful as a schema for preserving how exploration is changed by feedback. A reusable record should keep the issue and repository revision, Docker image, scaffold and tool calls, initial trajectory, generated or empty patch, exact test executions, binary outcome, teacher identity and prompt inputs, each guidance channel, guided reattempt, same-task winning/losing pair, SFT/DPO membership, and reward-model score. It also illustrates why three predicates must remain separate: passing environment tests, being preferred in offline training, and being selected by a learned reward model. Researchers can compare guidance-assisted rollout construction, environment rewards, preference conversion, and test-time patch selection, but the paper alone does not authorize reuse of the unreleased environments or traces.
