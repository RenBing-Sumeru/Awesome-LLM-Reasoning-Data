The disclosed pipeline is:

1. **Perception collection.** Automated crawling and human-assisted exploration collect screenshots and metadata across websites, applications, and operating systems, including nested pages. Parsers extract element type, hierarchy/depth, bounding box, and text. VLMs synthesize element descriptions, dense/state-transition captions, visual QA, and set-of-mark-style supervision.
2. **Action normalization.** Human-reviewed PC tasks and standardized traces from MM-Mind2Web, GUIAct, AITW, AITZ, AndroidControl, GUI-Odyssey, and AMEX are mapped to a common desktop/mobile schema. `Finished()` marks completion; `CallUser()` requests login/authentication intervention.
3. **Tutorial knowledge.** MINT/OmniCorpus-derived GUI tutorials pass a fastText classifier trained on curated positives and random negatives, LLM fine filtering, URL/LSH deduplication, ad/noise removal, and LLM rewriting. Counts, filters, models, and rights remain unknown.
4. **Thought augmentation.** Action Reflection conditions an undisclosed VLM on context and the target action to generate per-step thought, which risks backward rationalization. Thought bootstrapping instead samples multiple thought-action candidates without revealing the action and selects a thought whose action matches the correct target. Candidate counts, temperatures, and rejected thoughts are unavailable.
5. **Online VM flywheel.** Human/model goals drive the latest checkpoint through hundreds of virtual PCs over multiple rounds. Rules remove anomalous, redundant, or no-state-change actions; a VLM rejects low scores; humans review a subset, identify the first error, discard the suffix, and retain the valid prefix. Exact VM images, rounds, trajectories, thresholds, yields, and agreement are unknown.
6. **Reflection data.** Annotators label a corrected thought/action at the first error. Post-reflection records continue from the actual erroneous state with recovery thought/action. SFT loss applies only to corrected steps. DPO pairs corrected against erroneous actions, conditioned on instruction/history, with the SFT model as reference.
7. **Three training phases.** Roughly 50B tokens cover continual pretraining on the described corpus except reflection tuning, using a constant undisclosed learning rate; annealing on high-quality perception, grounding, action, and reflection subsets to produce UI-TARS-SFT; and DPO on human online correction pairs to produce UI-TARS-DPO. Per-stage tokens, mixtures, optimizer, LR, beta, batches, compute, and selection are unknown.

Evaluation feedback must remain separate from training feedback. OSWorld environment success uses three runs and 15/50-step budgets; AndroidWorld randomizes task parameters. System 1 emits direct actions, while System 2 emits thoughts then actions and is studied at Best-of-N 1, 16, and 64. These test-time sampling/selection settings are not evidence of the training sampler or reward.
