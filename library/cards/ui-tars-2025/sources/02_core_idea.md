The core object is a state-action record: task instruction, screenshot and interaction history, optional thought, normalized GUI action, next state, and quality/correction feedback. One unified action vocabulary spans desktop and mobile, including Click, Drag, Scroll, Type, Wait, Finished, CallUser, hotkeys, double/right click, LongPress, and navigation keys.

UI-TARS combines offline perception/demonstration construction with an online VM flywheel. Synthetic thoughts and action-correctness selection enrich reasoning; human first-error labels, corrected actions, and recovery continuations create reflective SFT targets and corrected-versus-erroneous DPO pairs. The contract is mixed because heuristics, VLM scores, humans, action matching, and environment success observe different notions of quality.
