The addendum's most concrete contribution is an auditable shape for automated long-horizon behavior assessment. It names the seed count, per-target transcript count, turn range, target models, generator family, scoring dimensions, scale, threshold, and uncertainty method. Few incremental system cards expose this many structural fields.

It also links safety training to agent environments rather than only chat refusal. Computer-use screens, hidden environmental instructions, long-horizon coding, sandboxes, hidden tests, and training-environment hacking create state-action and full-episode audit objects.

The report is unusually candid about validity threats. It warns that extreme seeds shape absolute scores, notes increased evaluation awareness, identifies training-distribution reward-hacking tasks, flags near-but-not-identical checkpoints in one blackmail test, and corrects historical metrics. These qualifications are as important as the headline numbers.
