The contribution is a 12,500-problem competition-math dataset with 7,500 training and 5,000 test problems across seven subjects and five difficulty levels. The mechanism is curated problem collection with worked solutions and final answers, plus a grading surface for model final answers. Closest comparisons are GSM8K-style grade-school word problems and smaller symbolic/math QA sets; MATH is harder, more diverse, and competition-oriented. The feedback contract is final-answer equivalence after normalization or symbolic checking, so the direction label is benchmark/evaluation surface rather than reward modeling.
