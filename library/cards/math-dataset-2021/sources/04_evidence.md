The evidence is the released 12,500-problem dataset, its 7,500/5,000 train/test split, and baseline model results showing that contemporary language models solved only a small fraction of test problems. Row-level decisive evidence is the problem statement, reference final answer, optional worked solution, model final answer, and grader or equivalence judgment. The boundary: aggregate accuracy depends on answer extraction and equivalence handling, and a correct final answer is not proof that the model's reasoning path was valid.
