The prior baseline was a mixture of easier arithmetic word-problem datasets and smaller math QA resources. MATH changes the object: contest-style problems with solution text, subject taxonomy, difficulty levels, and a hard held-out test split. The direction signal is that answer-level math evaluation can become a stable substrate for later verifiers and process-supervision work. The quality signal is scale plus metadata. What is not new is automatic final-answer grading itself. Reuse should inspect source lineage, license, exact split, answer normalization, contamination, and whether solution text is used as training data.
