Primary source: Hendrycks et al., "Measuring Mathematical Problem Solving With the MATH Dataset," NeurIPS 2021 Datasets and Benchmarks. The paper addresses the lack of a difficult, standardized competition-math benchmark for language models. The decision boundary is answer-level mathematical problem solving, not formal theorem proving and not process-label supervision. The data object is a competition-style problem with subject, difficulty, full worked solution, and final boxed answer. Its atlas value is foundational: many later math-reasoning, PRM, and verifier papers inherit MATH as the problem source or evaluation surface.
