GUI-Libra makes GUI reasoning supervision actionable by coupling each rationale to a structured executable action, filtering their alignment, emphasizing action/grounding tokens during SFT, and limiting policy drift when offline rewards cannot recognize every valid action. The primary data object is a reasoning-action step, the feedback contract mixes model-based filtering with partial step reward, and the work belongs here because those records are consumed directly by SFT and RL rather than serving only as an environment benchmark.
