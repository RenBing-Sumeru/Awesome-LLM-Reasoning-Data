1. **Clean source trajectories.** Input: seven public web/mobile GUI datasets. Operation: remove incomplete, under-3-step, over-50-step, and unsupported compound-action trajectories. Output: 19K trajectories/170K steps. Check: schema and action-space validity.
2. **Augment structured supervision.** Input: screenshot, instruction, history, annotated action, and coordinates. Operation: GPT-4.1 writes a long rationale, action description/type/target/value while retaining original coordinates. Output: reasoning-then-action JSON records. Check: parseable `<think>` and `<answer>` structure.
3. **Filter and split.** Input: augmented records. Operation: run Qwen3-VL-8B ten times and require action agreement at least 0.3; require original coordinates inside a Qwen3-VL-32B target box. Output: 81K SFT steps/9K trajectories and a domain/step-balanced 40K RL subset. Check: reject disagreement, coordinate mismatch, early-step bias, and excess mobile records.
4. **Action-aware SFT.** Input: full reasoning-action records plus direct-action variants. Operation: mix both forms and reweight action and grounding tokens (default weights 2 and 4). Output: an action-aligned policy. Check: fixed two-epoch baseline exposure versus one epoch on the doubled mixed set.
5. **Conservative RL.** Input: the SFT policy and 40K static steps. Operation: GRPO samples eight responses, applies partial step reward, KL regularization, and success-adaptive scaling of negative gradients. Output: GUI-Libra models. Check/stop: monitor offline/online performance; KL coefficients are 0.005 for 7B and 0.001 otherwise.
