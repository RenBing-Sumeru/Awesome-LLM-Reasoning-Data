The paper gives a three-part map. Reasoning-Enhanced RAG uses reasoning to improve stages of retrieval-augmented generation, such as deciding how to seek or use information. RAG-Enhanced Reasoning uses retrieved knowledge as missing premises or context for a reasoning process. Synergized systems repeatedly interleave search and thought, allowing intermediate reasoning to influence later information seeking. This distinction prevents a broad “RAG plus reasoning” label from hiding the actual mechanism. For each system, a reader can ask three concrete questions: Does reasoning mainly improve search or evidence use? Does retrieval mainly supply facts needed for a reasoning step? Or do the two components repeatedly affect each other? The answer changes what failure to diagnose, what cost to expect, and which evaluation evidence is relevant.
