Use this survey to design an evidence-aware reasoning system or to diagnose one that fails. If retrieval is weak, study Reasoning-Enhanced RAG and ask whether reasoning can improve query formation, selection, or use of retrieved content. If reasoning lacks necessary facts, study RAG-Enhanced Reasoning and ask what premises should be retrieved. If the task needs repeated information seeking, study the synergized family and make the search budget and stopping condition explicit. The taxonomy also gives a compact vocabulary for explaining what a benchmark actually tests. Before selecting a design, specify whether the main risk is missing evidence, poor evidence use, or an inability to decide what to search for next. This prevents an expensive iterative system from being chosen when a simpler evidence-supply design addresses the actual failure.
