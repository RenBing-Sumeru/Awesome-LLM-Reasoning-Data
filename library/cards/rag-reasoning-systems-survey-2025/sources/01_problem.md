Retrieval can add factual evidence to a language model, but a retrieved passage alone does not solve a question that requires several inferential steps. The system must still decide which information matters, connect it to the question, and avoid treating unsupported text as a premise. Conversely, a reasoning-only system may invent premises or fail to ground its answer in external knowledge. This EMNLP 2025 Findings survey addresses that gap by reviewing systems that combine retrieval and reasoning for knowledge-intensive problems; it is not a new corpus, retriever, or model release. For readers, the problem is not simply whether a system has access to documents. It is whether search, evidence selection, intermediate reasoning, and final answering work together in a way that can support the required conclusion.
