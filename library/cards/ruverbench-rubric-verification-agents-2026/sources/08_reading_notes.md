1. **Position:**  RuVerBench tests reliable criterion verification on long agent outputs.
2. **Method:**  Research/code outputs, criterion decomposition, human gold labels, and prompt/batch/vote controls.
3. **Data:**  2,458 output–rubric–human binary judgment instances.
4. **Evidence:**  Judges are noisy, batching costs accuracy, and voting gains diminish quickly.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for agent-judge selection; report human agreement and cost curves before training.
