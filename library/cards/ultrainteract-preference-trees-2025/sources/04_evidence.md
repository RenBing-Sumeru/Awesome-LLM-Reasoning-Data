# Evidence

**Claim:** UltraInteract's generated, checked actions add more useful reasoning supervision than either the original source answers or the general alignment mixture alone. **Controlled setup:** Table 5 evaluates Eurus-7B-SFT over averaged coding, mathematics, BBH, and IFEval results; the full recipe uses UltraInteract plus the general SFT mixture, `Ground-truth` replaces generated actions with original rationales and answers when available, `Open-source Only` removes UltraInteract, and `UltraInteract Only` removes the general mixture. **Result:** the full recipe averages 53.6, versus 44.0 for `Ground-truth`, 37.0 for `Open-source Only`, and 47.7 for `UltraInteract Only`, absolute differences of 9.6, 16.6, and 5.9 points. **Boundary:** this supports the utility of generated actions and the complementarity of the mixed recipe in the reported 7B setting, but the removal variants change data volume, the ground-truth variant falls back to UltraInteract rationales when a source has none, and the experiment does not isolate preference trees, critiques, and code actions one at a time.
