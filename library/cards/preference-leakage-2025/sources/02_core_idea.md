Preference leakage is a contamination mechanism in which related generator and judge LLMs transfer a preference to the generator-trained student. The paper operationalizes relatedness as same model, inheritance, or same family and compares judge outcomes using a preference leakage score. It releases code and data for the study, rather than proposing a new reward model; the central audit object is the generator--student--judge lineage and its resulting pairwise verdict bias.
