**Claim.** Generator--judge relatedness produces systematic favorable judgments. **Setup.** The paper evaluates related students on Arena-Hard and AlpacaEval 2.0 and reports the preference leakage score under controlled relation types. **Result.** The average score is 23.6% for the same-model condition, 19.3%/22.3% for two inheritance conditions, 8.9% for same-family same-series, and 2.8% even for different-series family models (Table 2). **Boundary.** These are measured configurations, not a universal correction factor for closed model lineages.
