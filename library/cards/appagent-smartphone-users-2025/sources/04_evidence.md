The arXiv abstract reports extensive testing over 50 tasks in 10 applications, including social media, email, maps, shopping, and image-editing tools. The evidence is task completion after UI interaction, not a hidden unit-test suite or a public row-level verifier. The important row-level evidence would be the task instruction, initial app state, action sequence, observations, final state, and completion judgment. The boundary is fragile: live apps, account state, UI redesigns, network effects, and model prompts can change the result.
