Primary source: arXiv 2312.13771, submitted in 2023 and revised in 2026 with the comment "Accepted to CHI 2025," plus the official project and Tencent repository. The problem is how to make multimodal agents operate smartphone apps through the same visible UI channel available to human users, without requiring app back-end APIs. AppAgent belongs here as an agent-environment and trajectory-style smartphone-control surface; it is not primarily a dataset release. The evaluation surface is a high-level mobile task, screenshot/state observations, touch/text actions, and a completion judgment over the app result.
