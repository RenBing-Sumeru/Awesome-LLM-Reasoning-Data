Use AppAgent as a design reference for mobile GUI-agent environments and as a checklist for trajectory fields. Preserve app name/version, task instruction, initial state, screenshots or accessibility-free observations, action vocabulary, action coordinates/text, knowledge-source provenance, final state, and completion judgment. It is useful for evaluating whether a mobile agent can act through the same channel as a user. For atlas curation, it highlights runtime-state and side-effect fields that pure text benchmarks often omit.
