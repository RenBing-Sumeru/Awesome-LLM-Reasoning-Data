Correctness is only relative to the tested app versions, device state, accounts, prompts, and task definitions. A completion judgment may miss privacy leakage, unintended side effects, or partial failures that are not visible in the final screen. The official sources do not make the 50-task evaluation a stable public benchmark with hidden tests. GUI-only control broadens applicability but makes reproduction sensitive to layout changes and service availability.
