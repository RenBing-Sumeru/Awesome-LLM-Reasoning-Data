The strongest evidence is the dataset audit itself. The verified release contains 251,122 problems—an order of magnitude larger than commonly used GSM8K or MATH collections—and includes 47,010 newly reformulated open-ended items. The official card publishes source counts, fields, and the complete filtering policy, and separates the verified release from an additional unverified subset. The authors manually inspect each filtering stage. This supports the scale-and-verifiability claim but does not prove that every reference answer or reformulation is semantically flawless.
