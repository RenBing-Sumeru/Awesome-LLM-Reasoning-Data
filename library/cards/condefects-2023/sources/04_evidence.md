The paper-level evidence is the reported AtCoder source window and paired fault-location/repair records. The repository supplies concrete commands for subset checkout, execution, and coverage collection, and documents output files such as `covMatrix.txt`, `results.txt`, and `testList.txt`. The decisive instance-level evidence is whether a specific program's released tests fail before repair and pass after the intended correction, with fault metadata matching the record. Evidence is bounded by AtCoder data lineage, test availability, selected time window, and any 2024/6 dataset update.
