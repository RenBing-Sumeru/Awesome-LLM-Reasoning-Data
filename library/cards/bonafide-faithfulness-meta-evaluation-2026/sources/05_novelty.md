Previous faithfulness studies typically propose a new metric and validate it through intuitive cases or correlation with older metrics without independent truth. BonaFide first constructs tasks where the computation actually used by the model is recoverable and then treats each metric as the object being evaluated. Its novelty is ground-truth meta-evaluation rather than another replacement score, with separate step- and trace-level faithfulness. This reversal makes metric validity falsifiable and localizes where a metric fails as chain length, task, or model changes. This directly reveals where a metric diverges from truth across tasks, models, or chain lengths. Deletion, counterfactuals, and LLM scoring are not new. The change is constructing tasks that recover the path actually used and treating the metric itself as the audited object, making metric validity falsifiable.
