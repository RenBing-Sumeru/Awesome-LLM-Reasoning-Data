The contribution is EvalBiasBench plus OffsetBias-style debiased evaluator tuning: construct cases where a judge can be pulled by non-quality cues, then use debiased data to tune evaluators. The mechanism is to name concrete bias categories, provide paired or labeled evaluation surfaces, and test whether an evaluator's judgment follows the intended quality signal rather than a spurious offset. The feedback contract is judgment-required: the score is meaningful only relative to the bias label, rubric, and evaluator prompt used. Closest comparisons are reward-model benchmarks, JudgeBench-style judge audits, and generic preference datasets. The direction label is evaluator-bias audit data for reward and judge construction.
