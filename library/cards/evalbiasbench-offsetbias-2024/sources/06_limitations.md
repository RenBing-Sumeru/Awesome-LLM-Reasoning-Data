Correctness is only relative to the listed bias categories, target labels, and judge prompt. A debiased evaluator can still fail on unseen biases, domain-specific rubrics, multilingual settings, safety cases, or adversarial formatting. Public benchmark cases can leak into later evaluator training. Dataset license, split policy, and model checkpoint terms must be checked at artifact level. OffsetBias should not be read as proof that an evaluator is globally fair; it is a controlled surface for diagnosing and tuning specific judge biases.
