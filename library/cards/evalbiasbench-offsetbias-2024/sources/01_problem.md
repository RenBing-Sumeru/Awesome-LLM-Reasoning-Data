OffsetBias asks how evaluator-tuning data can expose and reduce systematic judge biases rather than only improve average agreement. The primary sources are arXiv:2407.06551, the official NCSoft GitHub repository, and the NCSOFT Hugging Face dataset; the arXiv record reports Findings of EMNLP 2024 status. It belongs here as a judge-robustness benchmark and debiasing data release, not as a general assistant-quality leaderboard. A record is a biased evaluation case with prompt, candidate response surface, bias category or debiased target, and a judgment target. Its value for the atlas is that evaluator labels are treated as artifacts with bias modes and correction data, so the feedback contract itself becomes auditable.
