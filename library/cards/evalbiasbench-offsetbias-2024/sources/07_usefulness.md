Use OffsetBias as a schema for evaluator-bias audits. Preserve prompt, candidate answers, bias category, target judgment, annotator or generator provenance, judge prompt, judge model, decoding settings, and score. It is useful for testing whether reward models, LLM-as-judge systems, or preference pipelines are sensitive to irrelevant offsets. For atlas work, it is a reminder that a feedback contract can itself be the object under evaluation.
