The evidence is the released paper, code, and Hugging Face dataset that make the bias cases and debiasing setup inspectable. The important unit-level evidence is whether a specific judge case changes decision under a biasing cue and whether the debiased target is preserved. Aggregate improvements only support the claim under the tested evaluator models, prompts, and bias categories. They do not certify that the resulting evaluator is unbiased for all instruction-following, domain expertise, or safety judgments.
