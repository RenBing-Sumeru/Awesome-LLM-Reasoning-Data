The direction signal is the unification of content moderation and refusal quality. Earlier tools frequently score only prompt danger or response toxicity; WildGuard makes the frequent operational failure “the system refused when it should answer” measurable alongside “the system answered when it should refuse.” The work is not a universal safety policy, a formal proof of jailbreak robustness, or a substitute for human review in high-impact decisions. Its taxonomy and labels encode specific normative choices.
