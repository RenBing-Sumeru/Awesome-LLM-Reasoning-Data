WildGuard is trained from WildGuardMix and evaluated on WildGuardTest, a 5K human-labelled test set, plus ten public moderation benchmarks. The dataset construction deliberately includes ordinary prompts, harmful requests, refusal-like responses, compliant harmful responses, and jailbreak-shaped inputs so that the three labels cannot be solved merely by keyword spotting. A reproducible record should retain the prompt, response, source or generation route, risk taxonomy label, each of the three target labels, annotator protocol or synthesis method, split, language, and policy version. Input formatting matters because prompt-only, response-only, and paired judgments expose different evidence.
