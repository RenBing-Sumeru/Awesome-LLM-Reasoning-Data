WildGuard addresses a moderation gap that is often hidden by a single binary “safe” label. A deployed assistant needs to distinguish whether a user prompt expresses harmful intent, whether a generated response contains unsafe material, and whether the assistant appropriately refused. Jailbreak prompts make the distinction operationally important: a prompt may be adversarial without itself being a harmful answer, while an overbroad refusal can be safe but unhelpful. The paper frames moderation as a multi-task, policy-dependent decision problem rather than a generic toxicity classifier.
