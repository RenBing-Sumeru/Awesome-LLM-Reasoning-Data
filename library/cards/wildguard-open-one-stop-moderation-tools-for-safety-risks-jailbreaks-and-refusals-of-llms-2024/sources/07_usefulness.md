Use WildGuard as a transparent first-stage moderator, a dataset for training or evaluating multi-task safety judges, or a regression suite for refusal behavior after assistant changes. It is most useful when its scores feed a documented policy: allow, refuse safely, transform to a safe alternative, send to a higher-cost reviewer, or log for review. It should not silently become the sole arbiter for legal, medical, employment, or other high-stakes user outcomes, nor should a safety score be presented as an explanation of policy correctness.
