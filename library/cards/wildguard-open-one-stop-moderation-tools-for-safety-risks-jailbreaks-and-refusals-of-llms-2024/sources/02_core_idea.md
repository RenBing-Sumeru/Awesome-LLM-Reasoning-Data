The contribution is an open, unified moderator trained to make prompt-harmfulness, response-harmfulness, and refusal judgments with shared representations and explicit task outputs. Its central data asset, WildGuardMix, combines 92K labelled examples spanning direct requests and adversarial jailbreaks across 13 risk categories. The feedback contract is three-way: given a prompt, judge intent; given a response, judge generated risk; given the interaction, judge whether the response is a refusal. A product must still set thresholds, policies, and escalation paths outside the model.
