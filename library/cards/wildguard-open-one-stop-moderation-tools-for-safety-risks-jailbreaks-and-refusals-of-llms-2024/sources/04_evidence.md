The paper reports that WildGuard is competitive with or exceeds compared open moderators across the multi-task evaluation, including up to a 26.4% improvement in refusal detection and up to a 3.9% prompt-harmfulness improvement over GPT-4 in the reported comparison. It also studies a deployed protection setting in which the measured jailbreak success rate drops from 79.8% to 2.4%. These are evaluation-bound results, not a guarantee that every future attack is blocked: attack generator, policy, model, interface, threshold, and the definition of success must match before comparing numbers.
