The paper evaluates the constructed data and post-training methods on GSM8K, MATH/MATH500, AIME, Blocksworld, and multilingual Multi-IF. Table 4 reports that SFT on the constructed data improves several studied Llama and Qwen baselines, while Sky-T1 can underperform on the smaller models in this setup. Table 5 is also a warning rather than a monotonic success story: plain DPO sharply degrades several planning and instruction-following scores, after which cDPO, the joint loss, and masking recover different portions. On MATH, the reported best model moves from Test@1/8/32 of 51.0/70.2/79.2 to 51.0/70.8/82.8 when MCTS decoding is added. These experiments support the interaction among data path choice, preference construction, and optimizer design, but they do not prove the correctness of individual thought nodes or the completeness of the public data release.
