For the rollout/search/test-time-trace track, Marco-o1 v2 is a concrete example of turning one search tree into several downstream data products: long-path SFT targets, short-path preferences, rejected near-prefix branches, and test-time MCTS candidates. An auditable record should preserve the source prompt, node-transition template, generator model per node, prompt and decoding settings, parent-child IDs, node role and text, rule-check result, reward and backpropagation, stopping reason, all accepted and rejected paths, relative-length bucket, common-prefix mask, and final training split. This schema can support SFT, DPO, search-policy analysis, and verifier audits while keeping terminal correctness separate from model-written reflection quality.
