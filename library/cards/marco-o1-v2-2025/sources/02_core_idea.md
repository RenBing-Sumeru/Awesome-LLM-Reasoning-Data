Marco-o1 v2 defines task-specific transition templates over nodes such as Sub-Task, Thinking, Reflection, Hypothesis, Double-Check, Reclarify, and Answer, then uses MCTS to explore allowed transitions. Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct generates ordinary thought content, while Llama3.1-70B-Instruct is used for Reflection nodes; rule-based correctness at Answer nodes supplies the terminal reward and can trigger backtracking. From the completed trees, all valid answer-reaching paths are grouped by relative token length. The longest correct paths are used for SFT, short correct paths serve as chosen DPO examples, and incorrect paths sharing the shortest common prefix provide rejected examples. CoT-aware optimization adds length balance, conservative DPO, mask-based DPO on the differentiating suffix, and a joint DPO-plus-SFT loss. The feedback contract is mixed because rule-checkable terminal answers are combined with model-generated thought and reflection content.
