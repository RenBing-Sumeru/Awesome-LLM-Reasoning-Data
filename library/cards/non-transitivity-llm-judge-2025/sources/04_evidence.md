The central AlpacaEval comparison tests agreement with Chatbot Arena. Moving from the baseline method to round-robin plus Bradley--Terry raises Spearman correlation from 95.0% to 96.4% and Kendall correlation from 82.1% to 86.3% (abstract). This supports better ranking agreement in that setting, not universal judge correctness or a guarantee for other benchmarks.

The controlled contrast is primarily aggregation protocol: baseline comparisons versus the tournament and preference model, with Chatbot Arena as an external ranking reference. The absolute gains are 1.4 Spearman points and 4.2 Kendall points. These metrics assess rank agreement, not individual-verdict accuracy; the result does not identify every cause of the cycles, quantify human-label accuracy, or prove Swim reaches the same result at every budget.
