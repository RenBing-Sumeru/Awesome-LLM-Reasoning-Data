The reported gains are anchored to AlpacaEval and Chatbot Arena correlation, so they do not establish that every task, judge, or model set has the same cycles. Tournament judging costs more comparisons; Swim is intended to mitigate that cost but must be evaluated under a fixed budget. Reusers should audit order/position effects and report the exact pairing schedule.

The strongest curator risk is treating tournament ranking as ground truth: Arena correlation is an external agreement target, not proof of objective response quality. Non-transitivity may interact with prompt design, candidate order and response distribution, so a cycle rate from one release should not be copied to another. Before reuse, publish the compared pool, pairing graph, judge identity and order randomisation; otherwise the stability claim cannot be independently audited.
