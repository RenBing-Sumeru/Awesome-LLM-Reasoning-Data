Outcome reward models can accept a correct final answer even when its visual reasoning contains faulty intermediate steps. Existing process-reward benchmarks largely test text-only reasoning, leaving no controlled way to measure whether a multimodal process reward model (MPRM) can find errors in image-grounded chains.

PRMBench-V addresses that gap with a human-verified benchmark of visual reasoning chains containing controlled, localized errors. It is an evaluation resource and a calibration surface for MPRMs, rather than a claim that an automatic checker establishes semantic correctness.
