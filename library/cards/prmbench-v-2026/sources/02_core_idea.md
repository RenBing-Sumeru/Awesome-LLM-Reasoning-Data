PRMBench-V turns multimodal process evaluation into nine explicit error-detection decisions: a seed visual problem, a verified chain of thought, an injected error type and human-checked step labels. The key change from final-answer or text-only evaluation is to hold the problem and most of the chain fixed while varying a localized failure, including a visual-perception error. The release described in the paper contains 907 queries and 8,163 labeled test cases across five broad domains and 43 subcategories; the paper does not provide an official standalone data or code URL. The feedback contract is human verification layered over model-generated chains and perturbations.
