ScienceWorld: Is your Agent Smarter than a 5th Grader? defines an evaluation surface in text-game-environments, science-tasks, embodied-agents: whether model outputs or agent trajectories satisfy the task-specific feedback contract. The primary source and artifacts are paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.07540; acl: https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.775/; project: https://sciworld.apps.allenai.org; code: https://github.com/allenai/ScienceWorld; data: https://github.com/allenai/ScienceWorld; doi: https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2022.emnlp-main.775; the local metadata records the source as 2022 / EMNLP 2022 main proceedings / arXiv.

The decision boundary is environment_agent_trajectory_data with roles benchmark, agent_environment and verification contract environmental, programmatic. One benchmark instance is: science tasks, text-game states, actions, gold paths, simulator code, and task scores. This is not a model-training recipe unless the paper separately releases training data; the card treats it as evaluation, audit. It matters for the atlas because the reusable object is an evaluation/data record with a named scorer, judge, verifier, reward, or environment predicate rather than an abstract capability claim.
