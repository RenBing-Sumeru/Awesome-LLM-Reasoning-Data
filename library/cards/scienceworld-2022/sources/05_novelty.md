The prior-work baseline is agent-environment, tool-use, and benchmark-harness papers in the same atlas category. The change introduced by ScienceWorld: Is your Agent Smarter than a 5th Grader? is the particular task object and feedback contract: science tasks, text-game states, actions, gold paths, simulator code, and task scores scored by ScienceWorld simulator predicates, task scoring, and gold-path checks. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.07540; acl: https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.775/; project: https://sciworld.apps.allenai.org; code: https://github.com/allenai/ScienceWorld; data: https://github.com/allenai/ScienceWorld; doi: https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2022.emnlp-main.775), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
