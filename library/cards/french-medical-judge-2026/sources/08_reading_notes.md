1. Position: medical judge reliability is a generator-conditioned audit problem, not a generic similarity-metric ranking.
2. Mechanism: clinician labels 500 answers from five generators; the decisive check is binary semantic equivalence to a reference.
3. Artifact: the paper describes S-Édition, MediQAl, and an 184-instance alignment set, but no official downloadable card-relevant dataset or code was found.
4. Evidence: Qwen-80B reaches 77.60% accuracy; aligned Phi-3.5-mini reaches 71.40%, versus 47.00% for its base, on the held-out set.
5. Reuse: stratify by generator and retain clinicians; first verify release, license, and a richer safety-label scheme.
