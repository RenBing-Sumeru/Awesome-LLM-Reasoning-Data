DeepSeek-R1-32B and s1-32B are evaluated deterministically from 500 to 16,000 reasoning tokens in 500-token increments. When a model tries to finish early, budget forcing appends a “Wait” cue; at the assigned limit, decoding is forced to the end-of-thinking delimiter. For every prompt and budget, the study records the complete trace, final answer, correctness, and token-level log probabilities on AIME 2024/2025, difficulty-labeled MATH-500, and GPQA Diamond.

The analysis computes marginal utility as the accuracy change per 500 added tokens, then tracks each answer transition between adjacent budgets. It reports positive and negative flips, their ratio, and textual indicators such as hesitation markers, answer oscillation, and confidence trajectories. A cost-aware utility subtracts a normalized compute term weighted by a deployment sensitivity parameter, making the preferred stopping point move as compute becomes more expensive.
