On AIME, DeepSeek-R1-32B’s reported marginal utility falls from +3.2 percentage points per 500 tokens between 0.5K and 2K to −0.3 beyond 12K; accuracy peaks at 55.8% at 12K and falls to 54.9% at 16K. The corresponding s1 model turns negative earlier. Across R1 trajectories, the cumulative negative-to-positive flip ratio passes one around 7K tokens and rises to 7.55 at 16K, indicating that harmful revisions increasingly outnumber repairs.

The pattern is not confined to a single forced setup. Easy MATH-500 items cross the overthinking threshold at roughly 2K tokens while the hardest group benefits to roughly 8K, and GPQA Diamond peaks near 10K before declining. The authors report 76.3% precision at 80% recall for combined overthinking indicators, 67.5% genuine reconsideration among 80 examined negative flips, and similar decline patterns in 312 naturally generated traces longer than 8K tokens.
