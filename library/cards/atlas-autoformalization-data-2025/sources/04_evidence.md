Under the same pass@k protocol, the LoRA ATLAS Translator reaches 39.46% pass@1 on ProofNet, compared with 31.43% for Herald Translator and 23.56% for the initialization model. On MathQual it reaches 22.75%, compared with 19.01% for Kimina-Autoformalizer. Except for ProofNet pass@32, improvements over the strongest baseline are reported as statistically significant. Full-parameter training of DeepSeek-Prover-7B on ATLAS further reaches 54.99% pass@1 on ProofNet. These results support the dataset’s utility, although the stronger base model also contributes to later gains.
