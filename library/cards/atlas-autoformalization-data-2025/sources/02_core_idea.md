ATLAS’s central contribution is to “lift” data from a formal library instead of waiting for natural-language problems to translate. After ten expert-iteration rounds, the official ATLAS dataset contains 117,145 undergraduate-level natural-language–Lean parallel statements, together with 465 graduate-level MathQual evaluation problems. The data combines Mathlib concepts, teacher generation, proof and contraposition augmentation, and validation filtering for training an autoformalization translator and evaluating compilable translations on ProofNet, PutnamBench, and MathQual.
