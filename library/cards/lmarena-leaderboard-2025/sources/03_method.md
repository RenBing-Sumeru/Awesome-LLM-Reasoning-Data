The evaluation pipeline is: present the task input, run the model or agent in the specified substrate, collect the submitted artifact, execute or apply the scorer, and aggregate the result under the official leaderboard/reporting policy. The substrate is Online model comparison platform and leaderboard pipeline.

The verifier/reward component is Human pairwise preference aggregated by the official ranking methodology. Outputs are interpreted only through that contract; training or optimization use is not assumed. Reproducibility depends on pinning the task version, harness or judge version, model settings, release license, and hidden/public split policy. Artifacts to verify are paper=https://arena.ai/leaderboard, project=https://arena.ai/leaderboard, code=https://github.com/lmarena/arena-rank, data=https://huggingface.co/lmarena-ai.
