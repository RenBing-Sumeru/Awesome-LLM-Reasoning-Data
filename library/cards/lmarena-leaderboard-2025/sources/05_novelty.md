The direction signal is useful because it moves evaluation toward the artifact or workflow that users actually inspect. The quality signal is strongest when the scorer, split, and artifact version are explicit. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking models; the reusable contribution is the particular task object plus feedback contract. Reuse checks should ask: pin snapshot date, ranking package/version, prompt filters, model sampling settings, and confidence intervals; do not treat Arena rank as proof of factual or task-specific correctness.
