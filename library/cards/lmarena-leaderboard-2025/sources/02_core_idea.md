One-sentence contribution: LMArena is a continuing human-preference leaderboard infrastructure that ranks models from anonymized pairwise battles rather than fixed-answer correctness. The core mechanism is to turn a realistic task substrate into an observable input-output-scoring contract rather than only reporting model impressions.

Inputs come from User-submitted prompts in the Arena platform.; outputs are Pairwise human preference vote, tie, or abstention plus model and filter metadata.; feedback is Human pairwise preference aggregated by the official ranking methodology. It belongs under benchmark/evaluation surfaces because the central artifact is the measurement contract. Closest comparisons are other benchmark surfaces in the same domain, but this card should be read at the level of the released task object and scorer rather than as a broad model capability claim.
