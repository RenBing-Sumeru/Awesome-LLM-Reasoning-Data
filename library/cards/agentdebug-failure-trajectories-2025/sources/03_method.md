The authors analyze more than 500 failed trajectories, then curate 200 representative episodes: 100 ALFWorld, 50 WebShop, and 50 GAIA. Ten graduate annotators with NLP and LLM-agent experience review each decision step. Guidelines are refined over three pilot rounds; annotators receive training, a shared subset is independently double-annotated, and disagreements are adjudicated collectively. Module-level Cohen's kappa is 0.55.

AgentDebug has three operational stages: map each step's memory, reflection, planning, and action content to taxonomy types; identify the earliest critical error; generate feedback and re-roll out from that point, refining feedback on continued failure. The paper reports GPT-4.1 at temperature 0 for critical-error detection. The repository provides detectors, provider wrappers, vendored environment adapters, rollout collection, and step-to-episode conversion. Exact benchmark revision, generating-model mixture, sampling yields, reset state, and per-episode attempt histories remain unknown.
