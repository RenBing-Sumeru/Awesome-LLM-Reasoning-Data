The strongest data evidence is the documented annotation process and official release link: 200 selected episodes, ten trained annotators, three pilot rounds, double annotation on a shared subset, adjudication, and kappa 0.55. Appendix examples expose fields such as task, critical module and step, evidence, observation, memory, reflection, plan, and action.

The paper reports 24.3% versus 0.3% all-correct localization and 45.0% versus 28.0% step accuracy against its strongest baseline, plus up to 26% relative task-success improvement after feedback. These results test the diagnosis/recovery system. They do not show that all annotations are stable, that the 200 cases represent environment-wide failure rates, or that the data licence and replay package are complete.
