The main data contribution is causal compression of failure supervision. Earlier failure analyses often enumerate surface errors; AgentErrorBench asks which minimal early error explains the cascade, and retains evidence and actionable guidance. AgentDebug connects that structured diagnosis to an executable recovery loop.

Error taxonomies, self-reflection, and post-hoc retry are not individually new. The direction signal is the joint record contract linking module-local labels, root-cause selection, feedback, and environment re-execution. Reuse should preserve both judgment provenance and terminal outcomes rather than collapsing them into one quality score.
