The one-sentence contribution is a professional-work benchmark that moves evaluation from short answers to realistic deliverables.

The core mechanism is occupation sampling, task specification, reference materials, human deliverables, model deliverables, expert comparison, and optional public grading service. The object being scored is 1,320 full-set tasks across 44 occupations and 9 U.S. GDP sectors, with 220 open-sourced gold tasks, reference files, and deliverables such as documents, slides, diagrams, spreadsheets, and multimedia, and the feedback contract is expert blind pairwise comparison of AI and human deliverables; the public automated grader is experimental.

The closest comparisons are static QA benchmarks, office automation tasks, and agent benchmarks that lack expert deliverable comparison at this scale. Its direction label is evaluation-surface and feedback-contract curation rather than generic dataset summarization.
