Inputs are the task materials and metadata needed to form one record: 1,320 full-set tasks across 44 occupations and 9 U.S. GDP sectors, with 220 open-sourced gold tasks, reference files, and deliverables such as documents, slides, diagrams, spreadsheets, and multimedia.

Pipeline: Select economically relevant occupations and tasks; package reference files and deliverable requirements; collect human and model outputs; ask domain experts to compare outputs blindly; expose a public subset and grader for reproducibility experiments.

Outputs are scored benchmark records or evaluation summaries under this contract: expert blind pairwise comparison of AI and human deliverables; the public automated grader is experimental. Reuse must pin source version, split, scorer or judge version, prompt/scaffold policy, runtime environment where relevant, and artifact license.
