GDPval evaluates economically valuable one-shot professional deliverables with expert blind comparisons and an experimental public grader. The primary sources are OpenAI GDPval publication, arXiv 2510.04374, and evals.openai.com/gdpval.

The concrete problem is whether AI systems can produce real-world professional deliverables that match or beat human expert work across economically important occupations. The decision boundary is one-shot deliverable evaluation, not a full interactive agent-environment trajectory dataset.

The data object or evaluation surface is 1,320 full-set tasks across 44 occupations and 9 U.S. GDP sectors, with 220 open-sourced gold tasks, reference files, and deliverables such as documents, slides, diagrams, spreadsheets, and multimedia. This is useful for the atlas because it makes the feedback contract explicit: expert blind pairwise comparison of AI and human deliverables; the public automated grader is experimental.
