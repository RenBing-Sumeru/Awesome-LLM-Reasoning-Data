The strongest evidence is about the search contract, scaling cost, and release boundary rather than universal trace quality.

- **Task evidence.** The accepted paper evaluates the method on ComplexWebQA, GPQA, and FoolMeTwice with Qwen2.5-7B and Llama 3.1-8B. It reports improvements over its compared RAG and reasoning baselines and studies the contribution of retrieval and search. These are author-reported benchmark results; they do not demonstrate that every generated branch is factually correct or reusable as training data.
- **Budget evidence.** For Qwen2.5-7B, the paper reports 11,892 average generated tokens and 2.8x Standard-RAG latency at four rollouts. At 16 rollouts, it reports 28,972 tokens and 4.5x latency. This supports a compute-quality trade-off at the aggregate condition level, but there are no released per-example token, retrieval-call, wall-clock, hardware, seed, or cost records.
- **Repository-input evidence.** The pinned official repository contains 100 CWEBQA records, 100 GPQA records plus a separate 100-record evidence file, 200 FMT records, and 200 ScienceQA records. ScienceQA is not one of the three paper result tables. These files establish limited benchmark inputs, not a release of the generated MCTS search data.
- **Generator evidence.** The source contains the trace writers, optional tree printer, arguments output, retrieval implementation, evaluator, and discriminator. It can produce terminal and per-rollout JSON locally. The audited Git tree contains no paper-run `run_outputs`, `.tree` files, raw all-node archive, or record-level result package.
- **Failure evidence.** The paper identifies amplification of early retrieval errors, factual confusion, information overload, latency, action-selection complexity, and unnecessary expansion. The public release does not include the branch-level records needed to measure how often those failures occur.

Artifact inspection verifies official links, code paths, input counts, output schemas, and the absence of committed run artifacts at repository commit `3b4c64ebc8192916c9e671ba7e97f18d1f7b9d74`. It does not independently reproduce the paper's accuracy, latency, or model-call results.
