MCTS-RAG addresses a concrete test-time reasoning problem: static Retrieval-Augmented Generation retrieves before or separately from reasoning, while ordinary MCTS over model thoughts cannot introduce missing external facts. The paper asks whether retrieval itself can become a branch-level decision inside search, so a small language model can alternate between answering, reasoning, decomposing, retrieving, and summarizing rather than follow one fixed RAG path.

The auditable object is an inference-time search process over six actions: A1 Direct Answer, A2 Quick Reasoning, A3 Decompose Question, A4 Retrieval Reasoning, A5 Retrieval Decompose, and A6 Summarized Answer. Nodes can contain direct answers, subquestions and subanswers, retrieval-conditioned reasoning, or summaries; model-derived consistency and likelihood values guide UCT search and final answer selection. The paper evaluates this process on ComplexWebQA, GPQA, and FoolMeTwice with Qwen2.5-7B and Llama 3.1-8B.

The official artifact boundary is narrower than the method description. The repository provides generator, retrieval, evaluation, and discriminator code plus small benchmark-input subsets, but no instantiated paper-run trees, all-branch archive, structured retrieval-state ledger, discriminator score bundle, or per-example budget logs. This Card therefore treats MCTS-RAG as a construction recipe and scaling study, not as an open search-trace dataset.
