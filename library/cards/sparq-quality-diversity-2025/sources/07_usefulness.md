For `data_construction_open_release_recipes`, SPARQ is a detailed blueprint for evolving a synthetic mathematics curriculum while tracking policy-relative difficulty and learned coverage. It specifies the seed, generator/student/classifier roles, mutation prompts, rollout count, checker contract, quality thresholds, skill representation, four search policies, filtering step, tuple assembly, scale, and downstream SFT settings. The missing release means the blueprint is reproducible only after substantial implementation work.

A robust reimplementation should preserve seed and parent IDs, generator/student/classifier revisions, generated intended answer, all 16 raw student solutions, extracted answers, agreement labels, solve rate, quality, skill-label evidence, archive cell, working-set replacement, rejection reason, and final tuple ID. It should also store a one-question-one-weight view in addition to the paper’s all-successful-solution tuples so multiplicity bias can be measured.

Before reuse as training data, add an independent well-posedness and answer-equivalence gate, retain disagreement and rejected cases, quantify false acceptance, audit the skill taxonomy, compare static and dynamic invalidity propagation, and pin deduplication, decontamination, compute, and license records. Benchmark gains justify studying the recipe; they do not certify the constructed examples. This distinction is the Card’s main value to the assigned track.
