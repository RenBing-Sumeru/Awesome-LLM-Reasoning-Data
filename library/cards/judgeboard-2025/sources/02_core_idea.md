JudgeBoard is an evaluation protocol, not a released training dataset: a student response is paired with its question, a judge returns correct/incorrect plus rationale, and gold labels meta-evaluate the judge. Accuracy, Student-Wrong accuracy, and Elo expose complementary failure modes across six public benchmarks.

MAJ adds several identically based but differently profiled SLM agents, lets them debate, revise, and majority-vote. No official downloadable JudgeBoard dataset or code was confirmed from the paper’s official publication page.
