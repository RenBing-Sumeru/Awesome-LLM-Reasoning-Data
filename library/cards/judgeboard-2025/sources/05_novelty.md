Earlier LLM-judge work commonly compares answers or relies on a single agreement measure. JudgeBoard changes the evaluation object to a direct factual verdict and makes Student-Wrong accuracy a first-class diagnostic; Elo then compares judges only through gold-grounded wins. MAJ further changes a single judge into profiled, revisable deliberation.

Direct prompting, Elo, and multi-agent debate are not new individually. The contribution is their joint use to show that a model can look accurate while failing to reject wrong reasoning answers.
