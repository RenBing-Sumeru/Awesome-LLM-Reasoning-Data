The central result compares direct judge models on MATH categories. For Algebra, Qwen3-30B-A3B reaches 0.7789 overall accuracy but only 0.570 Student-Wrong accuracy (Table 1), showing that aggregate accuracy masks a tendency to endorse wrong student answers.

The MAJ test uses Qwen3-14B profiles and reports 0.7789, 0.9385, and 0.9056 overall accuracy on Algebra, Number Theory, and Counting & Probability (Table 2). The paper attributes gains to debate, but this is not a same-budget single-agent control and cannot isolate interaction cost or prompt effects.
