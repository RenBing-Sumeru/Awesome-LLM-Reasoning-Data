The paper is a bridge between reasoning-data design and inference-time scaling. Its intervention occurs offline, but its selection criterion is explicitly chosen for the future answer-generation distribution, and the reported success criterion is a better accuracy-token curve after fine-tuning. Read the efficiency ratios alongside the raw accuracy and token counts, and do not assume that a refined trace is automatically safer or more faithful. The relevant comparison is a model trained under the same source, size, and inference budget, with only the step-selection rule changed.
