Use this work when a reasoning model is accurate but expensive because its fine-tuning traces teach it to repeatedly verify and restart. It provides a concrete curation audit: inspect the proportion of progressive, verification, alternative-method, and correction text; test removal by answer confidence rather than by phrase length; and evaluate the resulting model across several token budgets. In production, retain a separate safety or tool-verification path for cases where a functional step is externally required, even if it appears removable for benchmark accuracy.
