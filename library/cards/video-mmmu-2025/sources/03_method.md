Inputs are professional educational videos, discipline labels, manually written or sourced questions, answer options, ground-truth answers, and optional transcript/audio settings. The dataset pipeline first collects 300 expert-level videos spanning Art, Business, Science, Medicine, Humanities, and Engineering, with 30 subjects distributed across those disciplines.

The annotation pipeline creates three questions per video, aligned to Perception, Comprehension, and Adaptation. For Perception and Comprehension, annotators manually create questions. For Adaptation, Science, Engineering, Medicine, and Business questions can be sourced from MMMU and MMMU-Pro when suitable, while Art and Humanities rely on manually created case-study questions. The quality-control process includes annotator cross-checking, OpenAI o1 language refinement and answer verification, domain-expert review, and Gemini 1.5 Pro analysis of whether audio helps solve the question.

Outputs are benchmark records and model scores by track, discipline, and video condition. The verifier is the ground-truth answer for each human-annotated question, usually in a multiple-choice or multiple-answer format. Reproducibility requires pinning the dataset revision, video availability, transcript policy, prompt template, frame sampling, model context budget, and evaluator script.
