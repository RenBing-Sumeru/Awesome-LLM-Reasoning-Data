The infrastructure evidence is author-reported timing under a fixed 8-H100 setup. Table 2 compares synchronized with asynchronous rollout execution: Math-TIR takes 87 versus 66 seconds, SQL 111 versus 91 seconds, and DeepSearch 193 versus 98 seconds. The largest reported ratio is 1.97× for DeepSearch. This supports the claim that independently scheduled tool calls can reduce waiting under these workloads; it does not measure episode quality or prove that a reusable trajectory release exists (paper §3.3; Table 2).

Representative task results span different models and metrics. VT-Math with GRPO reports a 61.7 average; VT-Search with GRPO reports 45.9; VT-SQL reports 83.9 on Spider dev and 81.3 on Spider realistic; VT-SWE reports 19.5 versus 10.4 for the base scaffold; VT-VisualReasoner with the complex reward reports 82.7; and VT-DeepSearch reports 32.0 with snippet evaluation and 34.0 with the external QwQ summarizer (Tables 3–6). These figures come from incompatible task surfaces and should not be combined into a single data-quality score.

The paper's ablations and execution variants provide mechanism evidence, but each remains conditional on task-specific data, reward, model, and budget. Deep-search evaluation with snippets differs from evaluation that invokes an external QwQ-32B browser summarizer; the latter is an evaluation dependency, not training supervision. The SQL appendix also conflicts internally: Appendix A.3 names Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, whereas Table 5 and the current official recipe identify Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct. The Card preserves that discrepancy rather than selecting one version.

No independent reproduction is claimed here. Benchmark improvements demonstrate behavior under the authors' configurations, not the correctness, coverage, license status, or replayability of the generated episodes. The absence of per-domain generated and retained episode counts, success/failure manifests, exact checkpoint mapping, and a paper-pinned code bundle prevents a release-quality conclusion about the underlying trajectories.
