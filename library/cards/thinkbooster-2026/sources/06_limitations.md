**Historical outputs are missing.** The central audit gap is record-level release completeness. The repository has no paper-run prompt manifest, complete candidate pools, post-hoc step boundaries, scorer requests and responses, selected-index history, pruning records, rejected paths, resolved Hydra snapshots, immutable config hashes, W&B export, or machine-readable result bundle behind the figures. Local persistence and resume support show that new runs can be captured; they do not reconstruct the historical runs.

**The public generated corpus has only two examples.** `cached_examples.json` contains two Claude Sonnet 4 math prompts, budget 8, with five demonstration runs each. Those records are useful for understanding the debugger schema but differ from the paper's Qwen2.5-Math-7B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B, and GPT-OSS-120B experiment matrix. Treating the cache as the paper corpus would conflate product demonstration, provider, model, task, and strategy settings.

**ReProbe cannot be followed end to end.** The paper, result tables, and README name ReProbes, and Appendix C reports an aggregation setting. At audited commit `561489b1e037722b4210189d9ab09a75a9fc8159`, no dedicated ReProbe scorer, config, or implementation reference is identifiable beyond two README mentions. The historical code path, probe checkpoint, feature extraction, and mapping from reported result to executable config remain unknown.

**Selectors have domain and access assumptions.** The Qwen2.5-Math-PRM-7B selector is trained for math but is used as a coding proxy because no coding PRM is released. Its code-domain score can reward plausible form rather than executable correctness. Uncertainty and probe scorers need log-probabilities or hidden states; online methods can require prefill. Hosted APIs may expose only black-box subsets, and capability changes can silently alter which strategy runs.

**Step boundaries are heuristic for native reasoning.** Unstructured thinking is segmented after generation by marker and sentence-boundary heuristics. Boundary errors change which text a step scorer sees, the number of steps, aggregation, pruning decisions, and debugger interpretation. There is no released human step annotation against which to measure this segmentation error.

**Budget and result attribution are incomplete.** Theoretical TFLOPs approximate forward computation and treat uncertainty overhead as negligible. They do not fully account for batching, KV-cache reuse, hardware, parallelism, provider optimization, evaluator execution, retries, wall-clock delay, or monetary cost. The paper does not run a fixed-serving latency comparison. Scorer aggregation and window are grid-searched per configuration, while no public immutable manifest maps every reported point to its resolved settings.

**Version, dependency, and API drift remain material.** PyPI publishes `thinkbooster` 0.1.1 while `pyproject.toml` at the audited commit declares 0.1.0. No immutable paper release tag ties the paper tables to a package build. `setup.sh` clones or pulls moving branches for LM-Polygraph, llm-uncertainty-head, vLLM speculators, and KernelAct, and most dependencies use ranges rather than an exact historical lock. Official project, debugger, and video URLs are mutable redirects; the debugger currently resolves to operational Runpod infrastructure.

**Licensing and provenance are layered.** The toolkit code is MIT and the ACL paper is CC BY 4.0, but those licenses do not automatically cover upstream benchmark mirrors, provider terms, Claude-generated cached reasoning, or unreleased paper-run model outputs. The checked Hugging Face cards do not expose dataset-specific license fields. A consumer needs a per-benchmark provenance and license ledger plus an explicit policy for generated-output reuse.

**Contamination and coverage are unknown.** The paper reports no benchmark decontamination, model-training overlap audit, or duplicate-prompt procedure. The nine evaluation surfaces are public and the live Hugging Face organization has expanded to 13 repositories. Results on math, science QA, Python, and CUDA do not establish transfer to subjective judgment, safety, agent environments, or domains without cheap graders.

The entry remains `partial` despite verified citation and code. It is suitable as an L4 infrastructure and audit Card, but direct paper-run trace reuse and exact result replay remain blocked by missing records, unresolved ReProbe implementation, mutable dependencies, and incomplete rights metadata.
