Offline replay assumes that pre-collected branches and probes adequately represent the trajectories an online controller would encounter; a controller cannot recover an unseen branch or react to live tool observations. The search is demonstrated mainly on exact-answer mathematical reasoning, and its use of Claude Code, fixed interval length, fixed probe protocol, and a five-round budget leaves several design choices unexplored. Better replay performance also does not prove that a policy will preserve latency, memory, or calibration properties in a live production system.
