On held-out AIME25 and HMMT25, the discovered controllers generally form a stronger accuracy-token frontier than the hand-crafted baselines. Averaged over the four main Qwen3 sizes, the beta 0.5 controller uses about 69.5% fewer tokens than SC@64 while keeping essentially equal average accuracy, 45.3 versus 45.2. With beta 1.0, it exceeds the peak accuracy of all hand-crafted baselines in five of the eight reported model-benchmark settings, showing that the controller is not merely a fixed-cost compression rule.

The transfer tests give more specific support for the allocation claim. On DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B with HMMT25, beta 1 reaches 27.2 accuracy with 533.9K tokens, compared with SC@64's 26.7 and 985.7K; on Qwen3-1.7B with GPQA Diamond it reaches 41.6 with 270.1K tokens, compared with 41.3 and 510.0K. The paper also reports a total discovery cost of 39.9 US dollars and 160 minutes, which is evidence that replay makes policy search operationally cheaper than repeated online experimentation.
