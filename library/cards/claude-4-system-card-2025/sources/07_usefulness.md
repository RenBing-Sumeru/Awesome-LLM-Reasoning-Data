Within `frontier_reports_data_disclosure_ledger`, this Card provides a closed-model comparison point with unusually clear source-class and evaluation-governance disclosures. It helps reviewers separate training ingredients, human labor roles, alignment labels, visible reasoning interfaces, iterative safety evaluation, and final deployment decisions.

For post-training audits, it motivates requests for source and rights manifests, preference-record schemas, the actual constitutional prompts or principles used, reward and calibration details, snapshot lineage, mitigation-data provenance, split and decontamination evidence, and release-version logs. It should not be used as evidence of a reusable preference dataset, a disclosed verifier, or an environment suitable for replay.
