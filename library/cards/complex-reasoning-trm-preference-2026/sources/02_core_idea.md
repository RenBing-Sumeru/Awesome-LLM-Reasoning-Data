The paper introduces the ME² principle for reasoning quality, represents traces as directed acyclic graphs, constructs TRM-Preference, and trains a Thinking Reward Model. Pairwise feedback is based on structured reasoning quality rather than answer correctness alone. The resulting reward can rank completed traces for test-time selection and guide RL training. The main contribution is a unified chain from characterization to dataset, evaluator, and optimization, with the reasoning DAG as the common representation.
