The paper evaluates both test-time selection and RL optimization across diverse tasks. Selecting traces with the learned reward improves outcomes by as much as 19.3%, while adding thinking reward during RL yields gains up to 3.9%. Testing both uses is important because it shows the model is not only correlated with existing good answers but can also provide an actionable training signal. Dataset and model release further supports inspection. The evidence applies to the included trace parser, tasks, and reward definitions, not to an objective universal measure of thought.
