TRM-Preference can train a reasoning-quality ranker, while the TRM can rerank sampled solutions or supply auxiliary reward in RL. The DAG representation is also useful for error analysis, such as identifying redundant branches or unsupported dependencies. Researchers can compare answer-correct traces that differ structurally, isolating process quality from outcome. The method is less suitable when traces are unavailable, deliberately compressed, or not faithfully related to model computation, and it should not replace executable correctness checks.
