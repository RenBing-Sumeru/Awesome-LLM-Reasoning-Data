The paper defines reasoning quality with ME², represents traces as DAGs, builds TRM-Preference, and trains a Thinking Reward Model. The reward yields up to 19.3% improvement through test-time selection and up to 3.9% during RL. Its value is a common structured signal usable at both inference and training. The required audit is DAG validity: if segmentation or dependency edges are wrong, the reward’s apparent process awareness may be an artifact of the parser.
