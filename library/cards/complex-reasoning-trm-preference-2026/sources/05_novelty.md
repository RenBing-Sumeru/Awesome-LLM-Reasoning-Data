Prior reasoning rewards focus on final correctness, step labels, or a linear sequence. This paper changes the representation to a DAG and evaluates both local reasoning units and global structure under one ME² framework. Its novelty is the structural feedback contract and its end-to-end use in preference-data construction, reward modeling, test-time selection, and RL. DAGs and pairwise reward models are not individually new; the contribution is adapting them to implicitly structured long reasoning and validating both inference and training use.
