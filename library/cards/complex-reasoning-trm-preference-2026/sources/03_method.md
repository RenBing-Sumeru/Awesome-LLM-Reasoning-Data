The method parses or transforms reasoning traces into DAGs whose nodes and edges represent reasoning units and dependencies. ME² evaluates macro- and micro-level efficiency and effectiveness, enabling pairwise comparison of traces with different structure. These comparisons form TRM-Preference and supervise a Thinking Reward Model. At inference, several candidate traces are scored and the highest-reward trace is selected. During training, thinking rewards are combined with task signals in RL so the model is encouraged to produce traces that are both useful and structurally efficient.
