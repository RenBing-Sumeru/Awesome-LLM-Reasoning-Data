The primary object for this Card is arXiv:2510.16252v2, dated 17 May 2026 but first submitted in 2025. It is titled “WEBSERV: A Full-Stack and RL-Ready Web Environment for Training Web Agents at Scale” and lists thirteen authors. An earlier eight-author version with a different title appeared as a poster at the 2025 NeurIPS Workshop on Multi-Turn Interactions in Large Language Models. The Card keeps these versions linked but does not merge their titles or author lists.

The paper addresses a full-stack bottleneck in web-agent post-training. Browser-side environments can expose noisy observations, unreliable actions, and weak interactivity cues. Server-side environments must repeatedly isolate and reset stateful applications, yet conventional Docker deployment is costly at the parallelism required by on-policy reinforcement learning. WebServ combines a DOM-derived observation/action interface, network-aware action execution, and per-agent Incus containers with block-level copy-on-write.

Four data objects must remain separate. First, WebArena and WebArena-Lite provide task specifications and evaluators. Second, Claude 4.5 Sonnet produces SFT bootstrap trajectories. Third, Qwen3 policies generate on-policy RL rollouts during GRPO. Fourth, the public artifact actually releases 726 success-filtered Claude message sequences plus environment, evaluator, and training code. It does not release failed Claude sessions, source result files, Qwen RL rollout logs, or model checkpoints.

This distinction is the reason for inclusion in the environment and agent trajectory track. WebServ makes observation state, action execution, environment reset, verifier output, and trajectory throughput first-class training infrastructure. It also provides a concrete audit case: paper-scale rollout generation is substantially broader than the trajectory data retained in the public release.
