Do not read OmniGUI as an end-to-end autonomous-agent success benchmark. The protocol is teacher-forced at each step, so it measures perception-to-action under ground-truth history. Keep TM, EM, SR, and GP separate: TM can be high while coordinate grounding fails, and SR can be low even when many individual steps are correct.

Read the task taxonomy and evaluation metrics before the leaderboard. When comparing papers or models, record whether the claim uses the 709/2,579 paper version or the 708/2,572 filtered public release, and whether audio/video were included or ablated.
