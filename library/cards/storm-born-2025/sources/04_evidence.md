The strongest difficulty evidence is expert-scored model evaluation on the 100 selected problems. Each model produces three responses per question; experts award 1 for a fully correct derivation and proportional partial credit for completed key steps. Figure 3 reports solved-problem rates of 0.0% for GPT-4o (20240806), 2.7% for GPT-o1-Mini, 3.6% for GPT-o1-Preview, 3.0% for GPT-o1, 4.6% for GPT-o1-Pro, and 3.3% for DeepSeek-R1. These results show that this particular expert-selected set was difficult for the tested snapshots. Selection explicitly targeted difficult items, so they are not an unbiased estimate of mathematical ability.

Table 1 provides mixed downstream SFT evidence, not uniformly positive results. LLaMA3-8B changes from 15.39 to 42.91 on GSM8K zero-shot and from 0.12 to 7.96 on MATH zero-shot, but falls from 50.27 to 45.49 on GSM8K 8-shot and from 17.08 to 13.82 on MATH 4-shot. Qwen2.5-7B changes from 80.67/83.32/67.82/54.42 to 81.96/83.70/67.96/63.54 across those four settings. The headline 7.84 and 9.12 figures are absolute MATH gains in selected settings, not aggregate accuracy improvements.

Table 2 reports Qwen2.5-7B increasing from 0.00% to 3.33% on AIME 2024 and from 3.33% to 6.67% on AIME 2025; Qwen2.5-32B moves from 20.00% to 23.33% and remains 15.00% on the two sets. These evaluation sets are tiny, uncertainty is unreported, and paper/source overlap is not audited.

For formula derivation, the paper fine-tunes Qwen2.5-7B and evaluates on a test set extracted from NuminaMath-1.5. DeepSeek-R1 compares model answers to ground-truth proofs and scores correctness, completeness, and similarity on 0–2 scales. The mean rises from 0.91 to 1.07 (reported as 17.58% relative); component means change from 1.10/1.14/0.50 to 1.23/1.32/0.66. Because this is a single LLM judge and the paper itself observes that LLMs can overrate flawed derivations, it is weaker than the expert benchmark evidence.

The quality ablation trains LLaMA2-7B on top-100, top-500, 2K, and a separately labeled fullset. Top-100 gives the best listed GSM8K zero-shot/8-shot results, 8.80/16.98, versus top-500 at 8.11/15.09 and 2K at 6.70/14.87. The fullset reaches 5.16/14.10, below the 7.96/14.33 base on those GSM8K settings. This supports the paper's selection argument for the tested model but does not isolate expert revision from difficulty selection or source mixture, and none of the larger ablation datasets is in the current repository.

Artifact inspection confirms 100 unique questions, 28 source identifiers, an exact 73/27 question partition, and 100-row and 27-row choice variants. It also reveals source/filter and split facts absent from the paper: two 2025 arXiv IDs, three books, and 17 of 18 test sources shared with train. Benchmark performance must not be treated as evidence of decontamination, license compatibility, or record-level correctness.
