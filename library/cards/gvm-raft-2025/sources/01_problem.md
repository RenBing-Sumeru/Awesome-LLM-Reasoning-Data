The authoritative sources are the NeurIPS 2025 proceedings paper, its 40-page PDF with appendices, arXiv:2505.02391, and the official `RLHFlow/GVM` repository. The paper studies online mathematical-reasoning post-training when each training item supplies a prompt `x` and oracle final answer `z`, while the chain-of-thought `y` is latent and correctness can be checked programmatically (Paper §2).

Uniform RAFT-style acquisition generates the same number of candidates for every prompt. That ignores both how often the current policy solves a prompt and how much an accepted response contributes to the stochastic gradient. Under a fixed global generation budget, uniform sampling can therefore spend too much on low-contribution prompts and estimate the EM/RAFT gradient with unnecessary variance (Paper §1, §3).

The relevant data object is not a static dataset download. It is an iteration-level acquisition record: prompt and oracle answer; policy checkpoint; pilot rationales and extracted answers; Math-Verify outcomes; estimated acceptance `p_i`; accepted-response gradient statistic `G_i`; assigned count `n_i`; additional rationales and outcomes; and the selected responses or reward groups consumed by RAFT++ or GRPO. GVM belongs in Data Construction and Open Release Recipes because it changes which online reasoning records are generated and admitted under a matched total budget. It does not address process-level proof of a rationale, open-ended judging, or prompt creation.

The card is review-ready at the evidence level because the method, settings, main and negative results, affiliations, and code paths are traceable. Direct training-data reuse remains blocked: the exact Numina-Math revision, item split, paper-run commit/configuration, decontamination, and public rollout/allocation ledger are unavailable.
