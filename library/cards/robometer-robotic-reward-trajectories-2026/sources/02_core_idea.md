The main artifact is RBM-1M, containing more than one million robot trajectories with frame-level progress and same-task preferences. Each record organizes a task input, visual or environmental state, candidate response or trajectory, evaluation evidence, and a preference, score, success, or critique label, together with task provenance and capability categories. Quality control relies on collecting successful, failed, and suboptimal trajectories across robots and tasks and annotating progress plus pairwise preference, making the release suitable for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than ordinary question answering.
