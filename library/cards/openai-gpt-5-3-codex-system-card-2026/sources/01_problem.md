The official *GPT-5.3-Codex System Card*, published by OpenAI on February 5, 2026, is not a general training-data report. Its relevant post-training disclosure is much narrower: Section 4.1.2 names one reinforcement-learning intervention intended to reduce destructive reversions when a coding agent encounters user-produced edits. The report does not release the records or implementation needed to audit that intervention as a dataset or recipe.

For the Frontier Reports and Data Disclosure Ledger track, the concrete data question is therefore bounded: what was presented to the model during those RL rollouts, what behavior received feedback, and what remains undisclosed? The named objects are a "user model," conflicting edits during a rollout, and positive reinforcement when GPT-5.3-Codex did not revert the user's changes. Task prompts, repositories, trajectories, reward records, environments, and lineage are not published. A destructive-actions evaluation is reported separately as a measurement surface; it is not identified as the training reward.
