The official ACL publication verifies the 2025 EMNLP Industry Track paper, its authors, DOI, and the reported construction recipe. Its method section supports the 20-rollout, 4,096-token construction setting; TLB-based reward calibration; DCP/DICP filtering; and SimPO training configuration. The arXiv record also reports that the authors observed average token-use reduction of over 30% while preserving accuracy on complex problems; this is a reported experimental result, not an independently reproduced finding.

The paper-linked GitHub repository is a verified public artifact, but the inspected README and file tree provide evaluation instructions and model-access directions rather than the documented data-construction pipeline. No official public link was identified for the retained preference pairs, all candidate rollouts/trace logs, pair identifiers, reward/checker implementation, or full SimPO training code.
