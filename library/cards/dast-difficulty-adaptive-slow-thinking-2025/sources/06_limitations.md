DAST is only partially reusable. The publication specifies aggregate counts and much of the recipe, but does not disclose the 20-rollout records, retained/rejected pair identities, full preference datasets, or their licenses. The paper also leaves the sampling temperature, seeds, prompt format, answer extraction, correctness checker, checker error behavior, decontamination procedure, and immutable data/model revision unknown.

The rule-based reward depends on a truncation-sensitive length budget. The authors note threshold sensitivity, STEM-only evaluation, and constraints from off-policy preference learning; they also warn that low-discriminability long DICP pairs can create reward-hacking risk. The linked repository should not be treated as a verified release of the construction code, trace corpus, or training data.
