For Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data, the paper offers a concrete schema for adaptive agent compute: episode and timestep IDs, environment seed, observation and truncated-history boundary, previous plan, prompt style, teacher planning interval, optional plan block, plan-token count, action, parser/fallback result, transition, sparse achievement reward, planning penalty, and later decision to retain or replace the plan. Keeping plan decisions separate from actions permits analysis of where compute was allocated, whether the plan was followed, and whether reward arrived only after several steps.

The 1,024 teacher trajectories are suitable in principle for SFT priming, and PPO environment rollouts support agent training; however, neither use is directly reproducible without the records and a runnable implementation. The method also provides an evaluation design for comparing equal action sequences with and without plan text, fixed planning schedules, learned dynamic allocation, and human-supplied plans. Reusers should not broaden the evidence to arbitrary RL domains: they should first pin model and BALROG revisions, recover seeds and terminal predicates, inspect teacher and rejected outputs, measure true inference cost, and separate autonomous, Best-of-N, and human-steered outcomes.
