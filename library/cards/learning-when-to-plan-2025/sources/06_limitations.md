The study is restricted to POGS and Crafter, Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct as teacher/zero-shot model, and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct as the trainable actor. The optimal planning frequency can change with model capability, environment dynamics, observation format, context limit, action space, or reward horizon. POGS and Crafter pause while the agent responds, so real latency cost is absent. Planning cost is approximated by generated tokens, while wall-clock latency, price, energy, and memory are not evaluated.

The synthetic SFT set inherits the teacher's plan quality and 16 hand-written plan styles; intervals are sampled from a fixed 2–12 range, and undisclosed temperature, seeds, filtering, and split rules limit auditability. The main PPO reward leaves instability implicit; early explicit shaping caused planning avoidance, showing reward sensitivity. Natural-language plans can drift, contradict the state, or be correct while the acting policy fails to verify prerequisites. History truncation to 16 observations can remove evidence needed to judge plan validity. Human-guided completion uses repeated Best-of-N attempts and is not autonomous success-rate evidence. Decontamination, data license, raw trajectory release, model release, exact terminal rules, and an accessible pinned implementation remain unknown. Reported benchmark gains do not establish trace or reward quality.
