1. **Collect software trajectories:** Save screenshots, actions, and interface feedback from browser, office, system, and related tasks.
2. **Define two evaluation levels:** Judge action validity and progress at the step level and completion, efficiency, and side effects at the trajectory level.
3. **Apply expert review:** Inspect before-and-after screens to verify clicks, typing, and waiting, resolving interface ambiguity.
4. **Release the protocol:** Organize annotations by ten task categories and uniformly evaluate multimodal RMs on action and trajectory scoring.
