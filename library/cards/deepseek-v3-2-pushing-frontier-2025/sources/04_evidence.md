The strongest data evidence is the task accounting and the distinct verifier descriptions. The four agent categories sum exactly to 85,267 prompts, and the general-agent branch gives both 1,827 synthetic environments and 4,417 retained tasks. The code branch reports tens of thousands of multilingual executable issue-resolution environments, while its table gives 24,667 tasks. The discrepancy is not necessarily contradictory, but the report does not publish an environment-to-task manifest.

The report tests 50 random general synthetic tasks. DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp obtains 12% Pass@1 and the strongest listed closed model obtains 62%; higher Pass@K increases all scores. This is evidence that the sampled tasks are not trivial to those models, not evidence that all 4,417 tasks are realistic, correctly verified, uncontaminated, or legally reusable. A separate ablation applies non-reasoning RL only to synthetic general-agent tasks and reports improvements over the SFT checkpoint on Tau2Bench, MCP-Mark, and MCP-Universe. That supports transfer under the reported setup, not record-level quality.

Model evaluations provide a fourth, separate evidence layer. The report gives reasoning, code, search, and tool-use scores, but MCP-Universe and MCP-Mark use internal environments that may differ from official ones. Tool-use evaluations use temperature 1.0 and 128K context. BrowseComp rises from 51.4 without context management to 67.6 with a discard-all strategy, showing that inference scaffolding materially affects the score. The primary SWE-bench result also comes from an internal framework, though additional frameworks produce a 72–74 range.
