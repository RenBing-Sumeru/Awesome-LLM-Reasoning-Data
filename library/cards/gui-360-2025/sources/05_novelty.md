Prior GUI-agent datasets and benchmarks already contain screenshots, actions, accessibility state, grounding labels, or environment tasks; the related Atlas references are OS-Genesis, OpenCUA, and AndroidControl. GUI-360° does not introduce GUI control, accessibility metadata, LLM-as-judge validation, MCP, or supervised fine-tuning in isolation.

The first concrete change is the released episode object at Windows Office scale: temporally aligned screenshots, UI Automation state, observations, thoughts, GUI or app-API actions, status, and an episode-level evaluation record are linked through execution/task/template identifiers. This exposes both behavior and the judgment attached to it rather than only a final benchmark score.

The second change is failure visibility and multi-view transformation. The 17,189 successful trajectories are accompanied by 62,170 separately released failed trajectories, and the same successful logs are converted into grounding, screen parsing, action prediction, and action prediction+A11y SFT-ready views. Failures are therefore not simply absent from the public artifact, although the release does not supply a complete failure taxonomy, attempt manifest, or reward conversion.

The third change is the end-to-end construction recipe: public query sourcing, manual template instantiation, LLM matching and feasibility filtering, staged GPT-4o/GPT-4.1 execution, GPT-4.1 whole-trajectory judgment, sanitization, split, and packaging. Its direction value for reasoning-data research lies in making state, action, feedback, failure retention, and derived supervision visible in one pipeline. This is an engineering and release integration contribution, not proof that every sample is high quality or that the corpus is replayable.

Before treating the recipe as reusable, a builder must reconcile query counts, recover record-level source/template/attempt lineage, publish the collection and validation stack, pin Windows/Office/MCP and VM/reset configuration, expose immutable split manifests, and resolve third-party content rights and privacy controls. Without those checks, the verified novelty is the static release and its auditable data transformations—not an open environment or RLVR system.
