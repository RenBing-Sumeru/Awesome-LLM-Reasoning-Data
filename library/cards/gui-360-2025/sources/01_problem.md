The primary paper record is arXiv:2511.04307v2, revised on 10 November 2025. OpenReview identifies it as a submission to ICLR 2026; no acceptance decision was verified. This Card also relies on the official GitHub repository at commit `a9f9d2e6f125c8cbc176b46a74aeef76ed16f0f6` and the official Hugging Face dataset inspected on 20 July 2026.

GUI-360° addresses a concrete data gap for computer-using agents: converting heterogeneous Office requests into executed, multimodal Windows episodes that retain the state, reasoning, action, environment response, and outcome boundary needed for SFT and evaluation. Its scope is bounded to Word, Excel, and PowerPoint productivity tasks; cross-application, version-management, privacy-, security-, and safety-risk tasks are filtered, so it is not evidence for general desktop deployment.

The canonical raw object is an Office episode composed of JSON step records. Steps in one episode share `execution_id`, `app_domain`, `request`, and `template`; each step records `step_id/total_steps`, EvaAgent evaluation reason/evidence/sub-scores/completeness, synchronized screenshots, Windows UI Automation state, application/control metadata, `subtask`, `observation`, `thought`, the executed GUI or MCP-backed Office API action with function, arguments and spatial target, and a `CONTINUE`, `FINISH`, or `OVERALL_FINISH` status. The episode is therefore more than a screenshot-action pair, although the released artifacts do not make it replayable in a pinned VM.

The reported release combines 17,189 successful trajectories and 62,170 separately published failed trajectories, yielding 79,359 trajectories and 1,225,177 steps; Hugging Face shows approximately 574 GB. This makes the work central to `environment_agent_trajectory_data`, while the query-to-template, collection, filtering, sanitization, split, and packaging pipeline also supports `data_construction_open_release_recipes`. Paper, appendix, repository, evaluator/converter code, and dataset trees support L4 content depth, but the canonical curation level remains `L3_summary_ready` and unresolved replay, verifier, split, rights, and privacy facts remain explicit.
