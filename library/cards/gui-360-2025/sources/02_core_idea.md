GUI-360° turns real-world Office query patterns into executed Windows trajectories, validates whole episodes with GPT-4.1-based EvaAgent, preserves both successful and failed runs, and derives four step-level SFT-ready views from the same underlying interaction records.

The construction mechanism starts from help/tutorial material, online forums and Q&A/community sites, and search-engine queries. An unspecified LLM matches a query to one of 66 manually instantiated templates—30 Word, 30 Excel, and 6 PowerPoint—and concretizes it. A five-way LLM gate rejects `NONEXEC`, `CROSSAPP`, `VERCTRL`, `TPLMISS`, and `INVALID` tasks. TrajAgent then uses a master planner, execution agents, screenshot and accessibility perception, hybrid GUI/MCP actions, and a recorder; GPT-4o makes the first attempt and GPT-4.1 retries failures.

The feedback contract has two non-interchangeable layers. During collection, EvaAgent is a GPT-4.1 LLM-as-judge over the complete trajectory, screenshots, accessibility data, actions, thoughts, and final application state; it stores rationale, evidence, sub-scores, and binary completeness, and the paper reports 86% agreement with humans on 100 sampled trajectories. For released benchmarks, programmatic step scorers instead test point-in-box grounding, greedy IoU/text screen parsing, or exact function/argument/status action prediction. Those scorers observe logged labels, not the consequences of executing a prediction in a live Office environment.

The four processed SFT-ready directories are action prediction, action prediction with accessibility context, grounding, and screen parsing, all described as Qwen2.5-VL-7B-oriented transformations. In the first two, targets are structured next actions; grounding targets coordinates from a screenshot and current-step thought; screen parsing targets actionable controls with names and boxes. These support `sft`, GUI `agent_training`, and static `evaluation`, but do not establish a reusable RL reward or `rlvr` contract.

The nearest Atlas comparisons are OS-Genesis, OpenCUA, and AndroidControl. GUI-360°'s distinguishing combination is its Windows Office substrate, hybrid GUI/API step schema, whole-trajectory LLM judgment, separately retained failure tree, and multiple derived static views. It does not make GUI agents, accessibility grounding, LLM judging, or SFT individually new; the contribution is the integrated data object and release pipeline at this scale.
