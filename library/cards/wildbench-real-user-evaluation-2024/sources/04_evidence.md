WildBench model rankings align closely with Chatbot Arena: WB-Reward reaches a correlation of about 0.98 with Arena rankings, while WB-Score reaches about 0.95. This indicates that offline automatic evaluation can reproduce live human preferences reasonably well. The paper also analyzes judges, baseline choices, and length-control strategies, showing that task-specific checklists and multi-baseline comparison improve stability and reduce rewards for verbosity alone. These findings support WildBench as a proxy for real-user capability, although the reported correlations apply mainly to the evaluated model set and should be revalidated for new models, non-English users, and later time periods.
