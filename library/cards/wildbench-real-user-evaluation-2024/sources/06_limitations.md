1. **Privacy-filtering bias:** Removing sensitive or personalized requests for public release changes the real-user distribution.

2. **Static snapshot:** The 1,024 tasks represent one period and may saturate as user needs and frontier-model capabilities change.

3. **Judge bias:** Checklists and multiple baselines reduce but do not eliminate self-preference, wording, or model-family bias.

4. **Open-ended criteria:** Some tasks have many valid answers, and fixed checklists may miss creative but effective solutions.

5. **Language coverage:** The dataset is primarily English and does not directly represent Chinese, multilingual, or specialized professional users.

The task pool should be refreshed and periodically recalibrated against human preferences.
