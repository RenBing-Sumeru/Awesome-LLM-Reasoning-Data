1. **One-sentence positioning:** WildBench compresses more than one million real chat logs into 1,024 difficult and reproducible open-ended evaluation tasks.

2. **Data object:** Real prompts, task-specific checklists, candidate and multiple baseline responses, and pairwise and pointwise scores.

3. **Metric design:** WB-Reward performs relative comparison, while WB-Score performs item-level absolute evaluation with explicit length-bias control.

4. **Evidence anchor:** The two metrics correlate with Chatbot Arena rankings at approximately 0.98 and 0.95, respectively.

5. **Main risk:** A static English dataset and automatic judges cannot permanently or completely represent real-user distributions.
