How Far Is Document Parsing from Solved? PureDocBench: A Source-Traceable Benchmark across Clean, Degraded, and Real-World Settings 定义了 document-benchmark-审计, benchmark-quality-审计 的评测面：模型输出或智能体轨迹必须满足任务特定的反馈契约。主来源与产物是 paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07492; project: https://zhihengli-casia.github.io/PureDocBench/review/gt_case_compare_all_fixed7/index.html?cb=puredocbench_2026_06_14_clean_ui; code: https://github.com/zhihengli-casia/puredocbench; data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhihengli-casia/puredocbench; huggingface: https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhihengli-casia/puredocbench; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.07492；本地元数据记录的年份/来源为 2026 / arXiv preprint。

收录边界是 benchmarks_evaluation_surfaces，来源角色为 benchmark，验证契约为 混合。一个评测实例 里包含：document-page parsing record with source-traceable HTML/CSS, rendered image track, GT text/formula/table/layout and reading-order structures, model prediction, and metric outputs。除非官方产物另有训练数据说明，否则这张卡把它视为评测、审计，不是训练配方。它对 atlas 的价值在于把任务对象、输出和验证器/裁判/奖励/环境谓词 绑定起来，而不是只记录抽象能力结论。
