The prior-work baseline is document, chart, multimodal QA, and visual evaluation benchmarks. The change introduced by How Far Is Document Parsing from Solved? PureDocBench: A Source-Traceable Benchmark across Clean, Degraded, and Real-World Settings is the particular task object and feedback contract: document-page parsing record with source-traceable HTML/CSS, rendered image track, GT text/formula/table/layout and reading-order structures, model prediction, and metric outputs. scored by official scoring CLI compares predictions with GT structures using TextEdit, FormulaCDM, TableTEDS, ROEdit, per-track Overall, and Avg3 across clean, digital-degraded, and real-degraded tracks. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07492; project: https://zhihengli-casia.github.io/PureDocBench/review/gt_case_compare_all_fixed7/index.html?cb=puredocbench_2026_06_14_clean_ui; code: https://github.com/zhihengli-casia/puredocbench; data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhihengli-casia/puredocbench; huggingface: https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhihengli-casia/puredocbench; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.07492), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
