The report's useful data contribution is contract separation across response mode, context length, teacher source, policy distribution, and feedback type. Standard versus CoT responses, 32K versus 256K curricula, off-policy responses versus on-policy logit targets, deterministic reasoning verification, and rule/judge General RL are not collapsed into one “post-training data” bucket.

The individual techniques are not wholly new. The contribution is their coordination across text, images, video, grounding, and agent tasks in one released model family, plus enough aggregate detail to identify audit questions. It remains a disclosure recipe rather than an open dataset or replayable RL environment.
