SFT samples are selected/synthesized from open datasets and web resources. Query filtering uses Qwen2.5-VL to remove unverifiable or content-poor queries and minimally clarify ambiguity. Rule filters remove repetition, incompleteness, bad format, off-topic/harmful pairs; Qwen2.5-VL-derived reward models judge correctness, completeness, clarity, helpfulness, and visual use. Long-CoT curation favors low-pass-rate or longer-response problems, discards vision-language math solvable by Qwen3-30B-nothink without the image, removes wrong finals, and filters repetition, language mixing, or unsupported guessing.

Distillation uses off-policy teacher responses followed by student on-policy sequences aligned to teacher logits. For Reasoning RL, open and proprietary sources undergo preprocessing/manual annotation. A preliminary Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B samples 16 responses for each multimodal query and 0/16-success queries are removed. Weak data sources are removed after pilot RL. The resulting approximately 30K queries are trained with 16 responses each; queries above 90% pass rate are removed, task ratios are fixed from pilots, and SAPO optimizes the policy.

General RL covers VQA, captioning, OCR, document parsing, grounding, clock recognition, instruction following, and targeted failure prompts. Rule rewards handle ground-truth and format constraints; Qwen2.5-VL-72B-Instruct or Qwen3 judges compare responses with references for nuanced tasks. Exact verifier implementations, judge prompts/revisions, reward weights, rollout temperature, acceptance yields, and training hyperparameters are undisclosed.
