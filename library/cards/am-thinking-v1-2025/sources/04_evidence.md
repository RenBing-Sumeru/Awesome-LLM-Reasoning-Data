Under the reported evaluation protocol, AM-Thinking-v1 scores 85.3 on AIME 2024, 74.4 on AIME 2025, 70.3 on LiveCodeBench, and 92.5 on Arena-Hard. The paper also reports SFT learning-pattern changes and tracks generation length and stop ratio during training. These results support the effectiveness of the complete pipeline, but they do not isolate the contribution of each source, filter, reward, or RL stage. The official releases independently confirm an Apache-2.0 model checkpoint and a 54.8K-row RL train split with source, prompt, ability, reward, and auxiliary metadata.
