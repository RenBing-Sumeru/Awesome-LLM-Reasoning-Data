The paper and current project evaluate multiple open and proprietary LLMs by execution accuracy, showing that the cleaned data consistently separates model scales and few-shot settings; current unified leaderboards place strong models above 90% while small models are much lower. More important is the error audit: original WikiSQL contains systematic defects that make correct SQL fail or incorrect SQL appear valid, while cleaning aligns labels with executable oracles. Because the leaderboard evolves, scores must be tied to dates and inference backends.
