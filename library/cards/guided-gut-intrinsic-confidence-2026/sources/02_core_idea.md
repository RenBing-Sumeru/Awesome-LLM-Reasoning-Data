Guided by Gut treats the model’s own probability assigned to a completed reasoning step as an intrinsic, imperfect estimate of that step’s value. Instead of asking an external process reward model to score every branch, it runs a lightweight diverse tree search and ranks branches with intrinsic confidence together with a novelty term, so the search is neither blind sampling nor a single greedy chain.

The important move is to train the confidence signal for its eventual decision role. A GRPO stage rewards correct solutions whose confidence agrees with correctness and penalizes confident wrong solutions. At deployment, the calibrated model carries both the generator and the guide: search capacity is concentrated on branches that it judges promising, while novelty keeps the tree from repeatedly spending tokens on near-duplicate reasoning.
