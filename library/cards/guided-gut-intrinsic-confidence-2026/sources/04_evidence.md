On AIME 2024/2025, the 7B Guided by Gut model at 32 paths reaches 73.3% and 53.3%, exceeding the reported chain-of-thought scores of much larger 32B models on both sets and approaching the 70B comparison. At 64 paths it reaches 76.7% and 53.3%. Against matched Best-of-N, its 1.5B and 7B variants match or improve the reported average accuracy across AIME, MATH500, and AMC while using about half as much KV-cache memory.

The paper also isolates the cost of an external scorer. With Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B-Instruct, intrinsic-guided search reaches 65.0% on AMC23 at 32 paths, compared with 60.0% to 63.3% for the three listed process reward models, while using 5 GB rather than 23–25 GB and about one eighth of their inference time. The confidence-reward ablation improves AIME24 score from 54.5% without RL to 58.9%; a math-calibrated 1.5B model also rises from 16.5% to 20.1% on LiveCodeBench.
