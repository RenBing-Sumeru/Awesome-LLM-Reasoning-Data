The strongest evidence is concentrated on mathematical reasoning, and the calibration stage itself uses LIMO mathematics; the LiveCodeBench transfer is encouraging but small. Intrinsic probability can remain poorly calibrated under distribution shift, the search needs several hyperparameters such as branching and novelty weight, and the reported comparison does not establish that every proprietary or domain-specific verifier can be replaced. The paper also does not release a dedicated implementation repository, so exact engineering reproduction requires reconstructing the described search on top of its cited components.
