The authors adapt Diverse Verifier Tree Search to step-delimited chains of thought. At each expansion, the model proposes step completions, derives a confidence score from its output probabilities, measures branch novelty, and uses the combined score to choose which branches continue. The search stops on a maximum depth or token limit, a final answer, or detectable text degeneration; final candidates are aggregated with confidence-weighted voting.

Before this search is used, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen 1.5B and 7B models are LoRA fine-tuned with GRPO on 817 LIMO mathematical examples. The reward is designed to separate confidence on correct and incorrect outputs rather than simply reward final correctness. Experiments compare matched 32- and 64-path budgets against chain-of-thought, Best-of-N, and several process reward models on AIME, MATH500, AMC, and a small transfer test on LiveCodeBench.
