This work does not merely use self-confidence as a final answer filter. It makes step-level intrinsic confidence the control signal for a branching search procedure and explicitly trains that signal to distinguish justified confidence from overconfident errors. That shifts the model’s internal probability from a passive diagnostic into a resource-allocation mechanism that determines where further decoding occurs.

It also argues for an operational substitute for verifier-heavy search rather than only presenting a new score. The comparison keeps the same tree-search family while replacing the external PRM with confidence and a cheap novelty proxy, then reports accuracy together with wall-clock time, GPU memory, and KV-cache use. This makes the claimed benefit about serving efficiency as well as reasoning quality.
