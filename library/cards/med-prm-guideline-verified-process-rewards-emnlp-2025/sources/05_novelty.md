Compared with assigning a whole chain of thought one correct-or-incorrect label, this work changes the unit or acquisition path of process evidence. MedPRM verifies medical reasoning steps with clinical guidelines and literature retrieval, providing traceable process rewards for a high-risk domain. Each judgement is linked to retrieved guideline or literature evidence for evaluating diagnostic, examination, and treatment reasoning. This is its direction signal; the reusable contribution is the data contract, quality control, and evaluation boundary.
