Compared with assigning a whole chain of thought one correct-or-incorrect label, this work changes the unit or acquisition path of process evidence. PRM800K provides 800,000 human step-correctness labels on MATH solution traces and demonstrates that stepwise supervision outperforms final-answer-only supervision. It segments model solutions to the same problem into judgeable steps and labels them correct, incorrect, or neutral. This is its direction signal; the reusable contribution is the data contract, quality control, and evaluation boundary.
