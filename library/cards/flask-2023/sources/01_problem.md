FLASK asks how to evaluate instruction-following models by decomposing alignment into fine-grained skills instead of one overall score. The primary sources are arXiv:2307.10928 and the official KAIST AI repository/project, which report ICLR 2024 Spotlight status. It belongs here as a rubric-based model-evaluation surface, not as pairwise preference data. A record contains an instruction, model response, skill rubric, and 1-5 judge score for relevant skills. Its atlas value is the explicit mapping from broad alignment to auditable skill-level feedback.
