Prompt construction starts from two demonstration pools: diverse real-user prompts from WildChat and fact-seeking prompts from the non-test LongFact split. Llama-4-Maverick-17B-128E-Instruct-FP8 is prompted to synthesize questions that resemble real use while requiring factual knowledge. The dataset card attributes the released prompts and responses to Llama 4 and Llama 3.3 outputs, respectively.

Offline supervision is selected from ten responses per prompt. SFT stores the best-precision response as `src`/`tgt`, with the target formatted as a `think` reasoning block followed by an `answer` final-response block. DPO stores `src`, `tgt_chosen`, `tgt_rejected`, `pairwise`, `veriscore_margin`, and chosen/rejected lengths. Pair selection controls both score separation and length, reducing a shortcut in which factual responses win merely because they are shorter. The release does not include the other candidates or the evidence used to score them.

Online GRPO uses Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct initialization after SFT, four rollouts per prompt, temperature 1.0, and a 2,048-token rollout cap in the released policy config. ScalableVeriScore parallelizes claim extraction, Serper-backed Google Search retrieval, and claim verification; the paper reports Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct workers for its training-time service. An LLM judge compares a rollout with a reference answer for relevance and overall quality. The paper defines a three-part reward family, while the released main `grpo.yaml` sets the explicit detail weight to 0.0 and the judge weight to 0.1. Exact reproduction therefore requires pinning the config, verifier models, search results, and judge prompt rather than relying on the high-level formula alone.
