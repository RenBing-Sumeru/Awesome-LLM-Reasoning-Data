The work combines an offline release with an online training recipe.

For the offline plane, Llama 4 synthesizes 7,000 fact-seeking prompts from demonstrations drawn from WildChat and the non-test portion of LongFact. The paper assigns 3,000 prompts to SFT and 4,000 to an RL split. Ten candidate long-CoT responses are generated per prompt. SFT keeps the candidate with the highest VeriScore factual precision. DPO chooses the pair with the largest precision gap, provided the gap exceeds 0.1 and the relative length difference is at most 0.1; otherwise the prompt is discarded. The released Hugging Face files contain exactly 3,000 SFT rows and 3,706 DPO rows.

For the online plane, GRPO samples fresh responses from the current policy. ScalableVeriScore extracts claims, searches for evidence, and counts supported and total claims; an LLM comparison supplies a relevance/quality signal. The general reward design exposes factual precision, supported-detail, and relevance components so that the trainer can trade off accuracy, coverage, and pertinence. The release provides code and prompt-preparation scaffolding, but not the original 4,000 online prompts, policy rollouts, evidence histories, or per-component reward logs.
