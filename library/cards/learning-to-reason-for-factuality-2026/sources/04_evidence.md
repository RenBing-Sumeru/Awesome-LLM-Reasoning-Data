The paper evaluates Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct variants on LongFact, FAVA, AlpacaFact, Biography, Factory-Hard, and FactBench-Hard. Averaged across these six sets, the reported SFT+GRPO model reaches 68.1% factual precision versus 45.0% for the base model, raises supported-claim detail from 23.5 to 29.0, and obtains a 54.4% judge win rate against the base model. The SFT+DPO model reaches 67.8% precision and 25.7 supported claims but only a 37.8% win rate. The ablation is relevant to the data contract: factual precision and detail can rise even as judged usefulness falls, showing that a high verifier score is not by itself evidence of a good training example.

Artifact inspection supplies a separate kind of evidence. The official release has two raw JSONL files: 3,000 `src`/`tgt` SFT rows and 3,706 DPO rows with chosen/rejected responses, a VeriScore margin, and lengths. No GRPO trajectory or reward-log file is present. The current Hugging Face viewer fails when trying to cast the different SFT and DPO schemas together, although both raw files remain downloadable and the repository preparation script reads them separately.

These results demonstrate behavior of the reported trained models, not intrinsic quality, independence, or completeness of every released row. No human audit of the full release, candidate pool, or retrieved evidence is reported.
