Typical visual-data synthesis rewrites complete questions, so structural diversity remains limited by the seed set and intermediate reasoning lacks independent verification. COGS decomposes questions into reusable factors, recomposes them across images, and outputs an intermediate answer and reward for each factor. The novelty is composition-grounded generation and local feedback rather than synthetic imagery or RL itself.

The system can therefore identify which perceptual or logical factor fails within a compositional chain.
