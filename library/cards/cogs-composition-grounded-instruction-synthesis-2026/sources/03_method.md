1. **Decompose seeds:** Parse a small set of human questions into primitive factors such as visual objects, relations, operations, and composition logic.
2. **Match new images:** Select elements and states in rendered charts or webpages that satisfy factor preconditions.
3. **Compose examples:** Recombine factors into new questions while generating subquestions, execution order, and intermediate answers.
4. **Verify and train:** Check answers against rendering data and program state, assign factor-level process rewards, and train an MLLM.
