The official ACL Anthology record identifies FLAMES as a Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2025 paper, published in November 2025 on pages 24746–24766. The full 21-page paper, appendices, Responsible NLP Checklist, ACL BibTeX, DOI, and arXiv:2508.16514 are available. The paper and the checked author publication page do not link an official FLAMES dataset, construction repository, model, or project page.

FLAMES asks how individual choices in a synthetic-mathematics pipeline affect the student model when other choices are fixed. Earlier problem-synthesis papers used different seed sets, problem generators, solution teachers, filters, students, and evaluation setups, making cross-paper attribution difficult. FLAMES holds a common scaffold and varies 10 inherited plus 2 new problem agents, 6 quality-control strategies, 2 problem generators, 2 solution generators, data mixtures, and student models.

One intended SFT record consists of a synthetic math problem, one Qwen2.5-Math-7B-Instruct worked solution, and its extractable final answer. Before that record exists, an agent may consume a GSM8K/MATH seed problem and solution or a topic taxonomy and emit intermediate key concepts, edit suggestions, composed subproblems, reverse questions, or distractors. The final paper recipe exact-deduplicates problems, applies a GSM8K/MATH 8-gram overlap filter, and then keeps the **first** teacher solution without independent solution verification.

The paper belongs in `data_construction_open_release_recipes` because it provides a controlled recipe for prompt sourcing, agent transformation, teacher-solution generation, filtering, mixture design, SFT, and evaluation. It is not evidence of an open FLAMES corpus: downloadable records, stable IDs, row schema, accepted/rejected ledgers, and construction code were not verified. The bilingual Card is `L4_chinese_review_ready`, while its release status remains `partial` because the claimed artifacts are unavailable.
