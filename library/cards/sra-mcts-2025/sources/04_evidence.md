The paper evaluates on Human-Eval, MBPP, Human-Eval+, and MBPP+, with GPT-4o-derived Full and Complex difficulty splits. It reports improvements for several backbone-dataset combinations, including an approximately 11-point gain on MBPP-Complex for Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, but also reports declines on some Human-Eval-related settings and a seven-point MBPP decline for Qwen2.5-14B relative to external-model distillation. The authors additionally use GPT-4o to label thinking as low, medium, or high quality and observe cases where this judge score disagrees with downstream performance; they note the judge may overvalue unnecessary overthinking. These results support studying self-generated search traces, but neither pass@1 nor an LLM quality label proves that the released reasoning path is correct or that the hidden tree record is complete.
