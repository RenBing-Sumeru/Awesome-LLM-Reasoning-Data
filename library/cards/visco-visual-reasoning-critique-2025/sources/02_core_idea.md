VISCO is a fine-grained critique-and-correction benchmark for visual reasoning. It spans 18 datasets and eight tasks, including mathematical and scientific reasoning as well as OCR, spatial understanding, and hallucination prevention. Records contain an image, question, initial reasoning trace, step-level correctness labels, natural-language critiques, and correction targets. The paper also proposes LookBack, which asks models to revisit visual evidence while critiquing each step.
