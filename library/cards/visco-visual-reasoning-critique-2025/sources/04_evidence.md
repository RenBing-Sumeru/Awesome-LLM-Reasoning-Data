Across 24 LVLMs, human critiques substantially improve corrected answers, whereas model-generated critiques are often ineffective and can reduce performance, indicating that diagnosis rather than rewriting is the bottleneck. Common failures include overlooking visual-perception errors, reluctance to declare the original reasoning wrong, and exaggerating error propagation. LookBack improves critique and correction performance by up to 13.5%, supporting explicit reinspection of image evidence.
