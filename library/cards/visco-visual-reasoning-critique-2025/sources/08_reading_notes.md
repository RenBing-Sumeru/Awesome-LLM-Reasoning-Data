1. **One-sentence positioning:** A step-level critique and closed-loop correction benchmark for visual reasoning.
2. **Method takeaway:** It spans 18 datasets and eight tasks, labeling step errors before testing whether critiques improve corrections.
3. **Data takeaway:** Records include images, questions, initial reasoning, step labels, natural-language critiques, and corrected outcomes.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Human critiques help, model critiques often hurt, and LookBack improves performance by up to 13.5%.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is highly relevant to multimodal process supervision, with over-criticism as a key bias to control.
