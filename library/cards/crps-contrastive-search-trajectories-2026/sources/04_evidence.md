The strongest primary result is on DeepSeekMath-7B. At 30K nominal examples, CRPS reports an average accuracy of 50.4 across MATH, GSM8K, CollegeMath, DeepMind Mathematics, OlympiadBench, and TheoremQA, compared with 48.3 for 590K RFT and 49.4 for 590K DART-Math. At 60K, CRPS reaches 52.0. Parallel 30K results are 39.6 for LLaMA3-8B and 36.8 for Mistral-7B-v0.1, each above the reported same-scale SIGMA baseline. These are downstream results under the authors' pipeline, not validation of every critique or released row. (Paper Tables 1 and 5.)

Ablations separate synthesis from search and contrast selection. On DeepSeekMath-7B at 30K, black-box generation averages 45.1, Vanilla MCTS 47.7, synthesis without contrast 47.5, random contrast 48.3, and full CRPS 50.4. Removing global or local critiques lowers the reported average to 48.7 or 49.0, and increasing sampled-path diversity from `K=3` to `K=15` raises it from 48.1 to 50.6. These comparisons support the construction choices inside the reported setup, while the shared analyst and terminal verifier still confound attribution to data quality alone. (Table 3.)

The generalization analysis reports 40.3 average accuracy on four out-of-domain math benchmarks, versus 38.0 for SIGMA, and 64.5 strict consistency on 200 perturbed GSM8K pairs, versus 52.1 for Vanilla MCTS. Appendix experiments report 57.9 pass@1 on HumanEval with Qwen2.5-Coder-7B and 75.8 exact match on StrategyQA with LLaMA3-8B. These are useful transfer signals, but the non-math seed counts, construction budgets, and public artifacts are not disclosed at the same depth as the math release. (Table 4, Table 6, Appendix C-E.)

The size-efficiency claim requires arithmetic care. The abstract pairs 60K CRPS with 590K baselines and calls this a 20x reduction, but `590K / 60K` is approximately 9.8. A roughly 20x comparison is supported by nominal CRPS-30K versus 590K (approximately 19.7x) and by the reported target-model SFT cost of 10 versus 192 GPU-hours (19.2x). Table 7 covers recurring target-model training; the paper acknowledges additional MCTS and analyst inference overhead but does not publish a complete API-token, monetary, explorer-compute, verification, or retry cost ledger.

Release inspection is separate evidence from benchmark performance. The Hugging Face card reports 27,256 rows, 9,939 unique problems, two string fields, average 3.3 steps, and 100% boxed-answer coverage. The official code exposes pipeline modules and configurations, but at checked commit `0d8a807c7783e1e15c24668e17a8450dc028c6a9` it has one commit, no tagged release, no LICENSE file, and only lower-bound dependency constraints. Its paper-oriented config routes a model named `gpt-5-mini` through an `anthropic` backend with `ANTHROPIC_*` environment variables. These facts limit executable-replication confidence even though code and final data are public.
