For the assigned **Data Construction and Open Release Recipes** track, CRPS is a concrete case study in converting a costly search trace into compact SFT data. It supports controlled comparisons among selection-only MCTS, local refinement, unguided teacher generation, random contrasts, and distribution-aware contrastive synthesis when methods share seed problems and the raw trajectory pool.

A reusable trace-level release would need more than the current two fields. At minimum, record an immutable seed/source ID and revision; explorer checkpoint and prompt version; every MCTS node, parent, semantic step, action, depth, visit count, Q estimate, UCT inputs, and terminal reward; positive-anchor choice and tie-break; hard/soft candidate pools; sampling probability and chosen pair IDs; global/local critique JSON; parser repairs; synthesis request, response, and retry count; extracted answer; exact/SymPy verifier version and decision; rejection reason; final sampling mode; RNG seeds; and checksums.

Those records would enable search-policy replay, contrast-selection studies, process-reward or critic training, critique-faithfulness evaluation, and audits of whether gains come from search diversity, proprietary analyst knowledge, or extra inference. Terminal correctness, search confidence, analyst critique, semantic-divergence label, and final SFT acceptance should remain separate signals rather than being collapsed into one quality score.

For direct CRPS-30K SFT reuse, pin the Hugging Face revision, record the observed 27,256 rows and 9,939 unique problems, hash data files, inspect duplicate solutions and normalized prompts, sample-audit intermediate mathematics, and verify GSM8K/MATH versions, licenses, and overlap with evaluation sets. Do not relabel the artifact as exactly 30,000 records or infer process labels from final prose.

For recipe reproduction, pin the Git commit and model revisions, clarify the `gpt-5-mini` backend, publish a tested lockfile and software license, disclose the hard/soft mixture and retry policy, and report end-to-end cost including explorer GPU work and analyst/synthesis API use. The current release is suitable for SFT experiments and recipe comparison after these checks; it is not sufficient for reconstructing each contrastive decision, training directly on negative trajectories, or auditing critique fidelity.
