1. **Positioning:** English LLM judges transfer poorly to non-English settings, where preference and reasoning data are scarce.
2. **Method handle:** The decisive actions are collect multilingual reward tasks, distill target-language reasoning, arrange language and difficulty curricula, followed by train and evaluate across 72 languages.
3. **Artifact handle:** mR3 extends rubric-agnostic reward reasoning to 72 languages and releases models, distilled multilingual data, and code.
4. **Evidence anchor:** mR3 reaches state-of-the-art multilingual reward-model performance and exceeds models such as GPT-OSS-120B while being up to nine times smaller.
5. **Reuse decision:** Low-resource languages rely heavily on translation and teacher distillation.
