The paper outperforms non-retrieval PRMs across real mathematical datasets and trajectories from different generators. The key controlled difference is access to similar labeled cases. Gains still depend on index coverage.

The most credible comparison holds the backbone, candidate trajectories, and inference budget fixed while changing only the data or feedback construction. The reported results support the utility of retrieval-augmented mathematical process supervision in the tested setting, but do not establish equal label accuracy for new models, domains, or longer trajectories. Reproduction should report data volume, label balance, verifier versions, and sampling budgets.
