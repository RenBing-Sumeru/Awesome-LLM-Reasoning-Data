At the score position, LAGER collects the embedding and every layer's hidden state, projects each through the frozen LM head, and keeps only allowed score-token logits. It sums these raw logits with equal or learned layer weights, normalizes within the score set, and outputs the expected score. Only the L+1 weights are fitted with CE+MAE on 1,000 held-out HelpSteer instances; the judge, prompt, and next-token behavior remain fixed.
