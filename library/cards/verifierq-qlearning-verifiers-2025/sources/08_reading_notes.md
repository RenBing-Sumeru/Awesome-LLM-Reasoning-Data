The paper's scale lever is the verifier, not the language model being sampled. Its action granularity is an utterance rather than a token, which motivates IQL approximation and the conservative term. Keep the evaluation boundary visible: TinyLlama, GSM8K, and a 500-question MATH subset. The strongest claim is that offline critic learning can improve test-time selection under those conditions; it is not yet evidence of broad large-model scaling or released-code reproducibility.
