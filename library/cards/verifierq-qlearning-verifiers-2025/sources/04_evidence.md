The paper reports that VerifierQ outperforms one- and two-epoch PRM training and majority voting on the reported GSM8K and MATH solution-count curves. The comparison is relevant because the generator and test-time candidate pool are held within the same mathematical-reasoning setting, so the measured difference is attributed to the selector rather than only to additional sampling.

The evidence is qualified by model scale. On MATH, the authors note that all methods can underperform majority vote with the 1.1B model, and the best expectile differs by dataset. They also report roughly tenfold training-time savings over step-by-step Q estimation in preliminary experiments. These results support feasibility, but not an established advantage for large reasoning models or all verifier data distributions.
