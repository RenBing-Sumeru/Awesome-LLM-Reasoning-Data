Praetor can serve as a locally deployable Chinese-English evaluator: given a question, response, reference, and custom rubric, it produces a score, preference, and explanation. Praetor_trainset can also support judge SFT or critique-model training and studies of transfer between pointwise and pairwise supervision. For strict factual, legal, or safety compliance, a generative judge should be supplemented with retrieved evidence, deterministic checks, and human review, with calibration errors reported for each criterion.
