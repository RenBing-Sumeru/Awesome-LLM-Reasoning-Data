Existing LLM-as-a-judge systems are often built around fixed benchmarks, fixed dimensions, or a single comparison protocol, making them inflexible when each instance requires its own evaluation criteria. Many judges support only holistic scoring or pairwise choice and cannot consistently handle both Chinese and English, limiting customizable and interpretable feedback. The paper asks how a unified training resource can teach an open evaluator to understand instance-level criteria and switch among pointwise grading, pairwise comparison, and critique generation.
