Earlier systems such as JudgeLM, Auto-J, and Prometheus showed that open judges can be trained with supervised data, but they often emphasize one language, a fixed input structure, or one evaluation protocol. Praetor unifies pointwise and pairwise judgment, Chinese and English, and instance-level criteria in one generative interface, with a hierarchical guideline organizing the training data. Multi-task SFT and critique generation are not new; the main novelty is configurable evaluation conditions and protocol unification rather than a new backbone architecture.
