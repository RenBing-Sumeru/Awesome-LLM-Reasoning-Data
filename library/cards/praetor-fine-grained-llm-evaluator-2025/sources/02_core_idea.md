The paper introduces Praetor, a 7B generative judge supporting Chinese and English as well as pointwise and pairwise evaluation, and releases Praetor_trainset. Guided by a hierarchical evaluation guideline, the training data covers diverse tasks and organizes questions, candidate responses, instance-level criteria, references, natural-language critiques, and final judgments in a unified supervision format. Rather than learning one fixed rubric, the evaluator receives the criterion as input and judges each response under the user-specified dimension.
