Post-training is commonly named after objectives—SFT, preference optimization, RL, or distillation—without first asking where the trajectories come from or which behavioral bottleneck a stage changes. The primary source is the 2026 arXiv preprint at https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07941. It asks how to view supervised fine-tuning, preference optimization, reinforcement learning, process supervision, verifier-guided methods, distillation, and multi-stage pipelines in one framework.

Its decision boundary is conceptual routing, not a new data release or optimizer. The central data surface is a trajectory: either externally supplied for off-policy learning or produced by the learner for on-policy learning. That distinction matters because a supplied trace needs source and annotation details, while a rollout needs generation policy, budget, filtering, and verifier or reward details. The official arXiv record and complete local bilingual Card make this ready as background material.
