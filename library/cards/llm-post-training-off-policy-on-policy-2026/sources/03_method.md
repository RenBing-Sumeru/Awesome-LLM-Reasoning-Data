This is a literature framework, not a training recipe. Its inputs are published descriptions of post-training methods and their trajectories. The pipeline first classifies trajectory provenance, then interprets a method as support expansion, policy reshaping, behavioral consolidation, or a coordinated combination. The output is a way to diagnose a behavioral bottleneck and reason about composition across stages.

The paper surveys rewards, preferences, process signals, verifiers, and RL, but it does not release a universal reward, judge, environment, dataset, or code artifact. Reuse therefore means applying its questions to a primary release: record whether the traces are supplied or generated; preserve the role of any reward/verifier/judge; and capture rollout sampling, filtering, and transition between stages. Reproducibility evidence—splits, data licenses, contamination checks, and model budgets—must be recovered from the cited primary source.
