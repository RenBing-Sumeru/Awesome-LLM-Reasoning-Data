Earlier accounts often organize post-training by an objective name or a historical lineage. This paper changes the comparison axis to trajectory provenance and behavioral intervention, making it possible to say why the same named objective has different audit needs in different stages. Its direction signal is the claim that coordinated systems design and stage composition increasingly matter alongside single-objective improvements.

Off-policy learning, on-policy RL, SFT, preference optimization, and distillation are not introduced by this paper. Nor does it establish a new benchmark or dataset. Before reusing the framework, inspect whether a concrete pipeline truly has a stable provenance boundary: online preference collection, teacher-guided rollouts, and replay buffers can mix regimes. As an arXiv preprint, it has no confirmed archival venue.
