1. **Position:**  Turn RAG error diagnosis into trainable feedback that controls an agent.
2. **Method:**  Nine datasets, fifteen models, strong-model critique, hierarchical clustering, and a 3B critic.
3. **Data:**  100K question–evidence–answer–judgment–tag–rationale records under MIT.
4. **Evidence:**  Fine-grained tagging is much harder than correctness, and critic guidance improves RAG.
5. **Reuse:**  Suitable for retrieval-reasoning feedback; verify attribution and corpus transfer.
