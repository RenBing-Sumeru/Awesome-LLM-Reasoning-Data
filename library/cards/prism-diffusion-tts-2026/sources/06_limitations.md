Prism's self-evaluation signal is produced by the same model whose candidates it ranks, so correlated reasoning errors or confident but incorrect intermediate judgments can misallocate the budget. The results are limited to three discrete diffusion language models and four math/code benchmarks, and the reported cost is function evaluations rather than end-to-end latency or energy. Reuse should therefore test the calibration of self-verification, the remasking policy, and the actual serving cost on the target model and hardware before treating the reported speedups as transferable.
