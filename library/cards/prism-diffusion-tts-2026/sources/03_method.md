For each problem, Prism begins from the model's masked denoising trajectory and opens a search window before the sequence is fully resolved. Hierarchical Trajectory Search maintains candidate states in that window. At specified stages it obtains intermediate completions, prompts the model for self-evaluation feedback, uses the resulting signal to rank candidates, and prunes or reallocates the remaining number of function evaluations instead of extending every candidate equally.

When a state deserves another exploration step, local branching preserves tokens the model considers reliable and remasks only a selected region. The diffusion model then refines that region in parallel, producing alternative implementations or solution paths without restarting from an all-mask state. The paper evaluates this procedure on four math-reasoning and code-generation benchmarks with LLaDA 8B Instruct, Dream 7B Instruct, and LLaDA 2.0-mini; correctness or task success is the terminal outcome, while function evaluations are the reported compute unit.
