OpenDiscoveryTrace can train early outcome predictors, first-error localizers, process scorers, or claim verifiers for scientific agents and compare model behavior in retrieval, tool use, and revision. Evaluation should be split by domain, workflow phase, and error type and reported alongside final success. Real scientific decision-making still requires domain-expert review of claims, evidence, and tool results; logged success is not scientific ground truth. Final reports can include phase-level outcome prediction, error heatmaps, revision-trigger patterns, and claim-support chains to reveal systematic workflow weaknesses. Success checks should report final success, error rate, step count, revisions, evidence coverage, and early-prediction calibration by domain and phase. Outputs may be error maps, first-error locations, or claim-support chains, not expert conclusions.
