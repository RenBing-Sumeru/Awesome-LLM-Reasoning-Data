AI scientists may reach the same final conclusion through radically different processes: one retrieves and verifies efficiently, while another repeatedly misuses tools or continues after failures. Final success rates treat these paths as equivalent and cannot predict whether early steps will lead to failure. Identical terminal success may also hide very different reproducibility, cost, and safety risks, so research agents require process-level comparison rather than answer-only evaluation. Equal task success can hide very different tool misuse, reasoning errors, revision costs, and evidence coverage. Final claims alone cannot guide model choice for reproducibility or workflow risk.

OpenDiscoveryTrace stores complete scientific-agent trajectories with errors, revision triggers, and confidence, providing a common structure for outcome prediction, first-error localization, claim verification, and process-quality assessment.
