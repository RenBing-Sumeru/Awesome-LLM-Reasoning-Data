OpenDiscoveryTrace initially releases 432 complete trajectories: 372 from GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Pro, plus 30 Qwen2.5-1.5B and 30 live-retrieval trajectories. It covers 124 tasks across drug discovery, materials, genomics, and literature. Every step has nine extended-ReAct fields, including thought, action, observation, error, revision trigger, and confidence. The JSON data is released under CC-BY-4.0. Each trajectory also includes task difficulty, model, domain, total steps, failure count, and final claim, enabling links between early process features and outcomes. Files are separated into frontier-model, open-model, and live-retrieval trajectories, with an additional task bank and analysis results; this organization supports comparisons of models, retrieval conditions, and autonomy on matched tasks.
