Existing AI-scientist benchmarks usually store final reports, scores, or partial tool logs and cannot compare how models conduct research. OpenDiscoveryTrace standardizes complete scientific workflows in an extended ReAct schema and preserves errors, revision motives, and self-reported confidence, enabling quantitative analysis of identical outcomes produced by different processes. The novelty is open end-to-end scientific-agent traces and multiple evaluation surfaces rather than a new agent algorithm. Models with equal success rates can therefore receive different reliability profiles based on error sources, revision efficiency, and evidence use. The nine fields per step also reconstruct why strategies change and how recovery follows errors. Saving agent logs is not new. The change is a unified scientific-workflow schema with error, revision trigger, confidence, and final claim, making process cost and risk comparable under equal success.
