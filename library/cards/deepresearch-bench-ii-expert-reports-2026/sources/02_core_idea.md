DeepResearch Bench II contains 132 grounded research tasks across 22 domains and 9,430 binary rubrics covering information recall, analysis, and presentation. Criteria are derived from expert articles through a four-stage LLM-plus-human pipeline for extraction, atomisation, verification, and hierarchy construction. The public JSONL retains tasks, sources, criteria, evidence, and per-task licences, supporting deep-research judges and report-gap diagnosis.
