Deep-research benchmarks often let an LLM define criteria on demand, which may not match expert priorities. Even human rubrics can be too coarse and allow plausible but unsupported reports to pass. Holistic writing scores also cannot separate recall, analysis, and presentation failures.

DeepResearch Bench II extracts verifiable content from expert investigative articles and applies more than 400 hours of human review to create fine-grained hierarchical criteria anchored in expert reports rather than model preference.
