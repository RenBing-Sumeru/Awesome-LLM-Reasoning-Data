1. **Select expert reports:** High-quality investigative articles are selected when their questions can become open research tasks with accessible evidence.

2. **Automatic extraction:** An LLM extracts candidate facts, analyses, and presentation requirements and forms an initial hierarchy.

3. **Human review:** More than 400 human hours remove unverifiable items, split compound criteria, add evidence, and check atomicity.

4. **Evaluate reports:** A judge evaluates all 9,430 criteria and aggregates three dimensions; retrieval dates, evidence access, and licences must be fixed.
