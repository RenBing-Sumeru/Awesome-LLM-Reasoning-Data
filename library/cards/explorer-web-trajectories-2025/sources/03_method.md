Inputs are seed sites from the Similarweb top 100 and approximately 49K Tranco URLs; a 4K subset begins with a GPT-4o-generated Google query. Sites containing violent or explicit content are filtered. Playwright opens each live site at up to 1980 × 1080 and records screenshots, raw and set-of-mark views, HTML/accessibility-tree elements, and grounded plus natural-language actions. The paper calls the four role backbones GPT-4o; Appendix B calls the billed synthesis model GPT-4o-turbo, so the exact dated service snapshot is unknown.

The reconstruction pipeline is: seed URL → proposer emits an abstract task and first atomic action → Playwright executes it → refiner conditions on the current page and complete action history, updates the task, and emits the next action → repeat until stop, a step boundary, or a safety/feasibility boundary → summarizer derives a high-level final task description from the full action and screenshot history → task verifier judges the task, actions, screenshots, and last-page Markdown → discard incoherent, misaligned, or unsuccessful episodes. CAPTCHA, login, and payment prompts cause termination; a separate prompt detects CAPTCHA. (Paper §3.2; Appendix D, Tables D.7–D.12.)

The reported collection used 60 parallel processes for 50 hours. Table 3 records 175K raw attempts, 94K accepted trajectories, a 7.7-step accepted-set average, 720K images, 830M tokens, and 33.3M web elements. Appendix B estimates $0.15 per raw attempt and $0.28 per accepted trajectory from a 53.1% success rate. Temperature, seeds, maximum horizon, timestamps, site-level traffic, retries, and an end-to-end run manifest are unknown.

Training adds another selection layer: sample 40K records from the accepted pool, remove episodes with more than two scroll actions, and retain approximately 30K. These state-action records provide SFT targets for Phi-3.5-Vision and Qwen2-VL-7B variants; no RL objective is reported. Appendix A reports batch size 64 and two epochs for Mind2Web-Live synthetic-data runs, with learning rates 4e-5 and 1e-5 respectively. The official repository supplies trajectory-generation, training, and evaluation code, but it expects local raw trajectories and does not provide the original dataset, membership manifests, page snapshots, or fixed environment. Exact reconstruction therefore requires those missing records plus browser, dependency, model-service, locale/session, and website versions.
