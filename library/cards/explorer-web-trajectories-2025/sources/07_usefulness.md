For the `data_construction_open_release_recipes` track, Explorer is a concrete blueprint for turning mutable environments into post-training episodes. A reimplementation can separate seed selection, action-conditioned task refinement, state capture, trajectory summarization, episode judgment, success filtering, and training-subset filtering, while logging every transition between raw, accepted, sampled, and final SFT populations.

The paper is also useful for verifier design and audit. Its 100-item human comparison shows why an LLM success filter needs a confusion matrix rather than an unqualified “successful” label. A stronger replication should preserve verifier logits or rationales, human disagreements, all rejected and near-miss paths, task/site/length strata, hidden-state checks where possible, and sensitivity to an independent judge family.

For controlled experiments, the reported recipe motivates comparisons between fixed tasks and evolving tasks; raw versus accepted episodes; success-only SFT versus mixtures containing failures; alternative scroll filters; independent versus shared generator/verifier models; and 25/50/100% training subsets under fixed model and compute. The official code is a starting point for these modules, but live-page drift and missing run settings prevent exact reconstruction of the original records.

The justified reuse class is **reading/audit and recipe reimplementation reference**. Training reuse of the reported corpus is blocked because the corpus, membership manifests, content rights, privacy review, and split/decontamination records were not verified. The 94K figure should be cited as a paper-reported accepted collection, not as a downloadable dataset.
