Frontier system cards can disclose that models receive post-training, AI feedback, and human preference/safety work without exposing the records or feedback contract needed to evaluate those claims. The resulting gap is especially important for an audit of data provenance, reward design, contamination, and reproducibility.

Claude Sonnet 4.6 is therefore included as a disclosure ledger rather than as a reusable data release. The official materials make some broad source and process claims checkable, while leaving the record-level training and evaluation evidence unavailable.
