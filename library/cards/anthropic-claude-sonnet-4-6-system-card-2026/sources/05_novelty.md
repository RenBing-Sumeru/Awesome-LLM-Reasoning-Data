The contribution for this atlas is not a new open training corpus. Its value is that an official frontier report makes several layers of a closed pipeline visible at once: broad source classes, web-collection constraints, cleaning, AI-feedback language, worker roles, transcript screening, training-snapshot observations, audit boundaries, and contamination caveats.

This breadth makes it possible to state a precise negative result: the report supports high-level provenance and process claims, but not source-to-record lineage, a reusable preference or trajectory dataset, a reward/verifier contract, or a reproducible reinforcement-learning recipe.
