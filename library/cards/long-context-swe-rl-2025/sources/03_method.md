Inputs are SWE-rebench repository-repair tasks. The upstream pool has 21,336 rows. The paper removes cases with metadata indicating invalid references or import failures, keeps tasks changing at most 7 files and fewer than 500 lines, excludes problems assigned an LLM quality score of 3.0, and rejects tests that vary over 50 repeated executions. The resulting 7,249-task manifest is not released. May and June SWE-rebench monthly splits are excluded from training; a random Verified-50 subset is used for intermediate checkpoint evaluation.

For rejection fine-tuning, Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct runs 10 attempts on selected tasks. A successful one-shot trajectory is appended only while collecting these base-checkpoint attempts; its provenance is unknown and it is removed for later RFT and RL agents. The pipeline retains 6,548 complete test-passing episodes, masks assistant turns that caused environment-formatting errors, and performs 1 SFT epoch. Reported RFT settings are 65k context, AdamW at 5e-6, weight decay 0.1, batch size 64, 10 warmup steps, cosine decay to 0, and 50 updates.

RL is synchronous and on-policy. In each iteration, the current policy samples 10 complete trajectories per problem at temperature 1.0 with `top_p`, `top_k`, `min_p`, repetition penalty, and other decoding filters disabled. The environment executes commands and tests the submitted patch. Final reward is binary test success plus the turn-length penalty; rewards are normalized within the 10-trajectory group, broadcast to tokens, and optimized with DAPO's clipped token-level objective. All parameters are updated with AdamW at 1e-6, 1 epoch per iteration, and gradient clipping 1.0.

Stage 1 uses 300 problems per iteration, batch size 128, a 65k-token context, and at most 40 turns. Stage 2 uses 100 problems per iteration, batch size 256, a 131k-token context enabled by YaRN factor 4.0, and at most 80 turns. Stage 2 uses 2,028 problems after removing tasks reported as having a 2/3 training success rate and tasks never solved; the exact inequality, measurement window, and manifest are unknown. Total online rollout count, random seeds, and retention after synchronous use are not disclosed.

Training ran on 16 H200 nodes with 8 GPUs each, context parallelism, Kubernetes pods for agents, Tracto AI evaluation, an internal JAX trainer, and vLLM 0.7.4 inference. Reproduction requires the exact 7,249/2,028 manifests, container digests and task mapping, repository/test snapshots, dependencies, scaffold revision, prompts, decoding configuration, seeds, reset/timeout/network semantics, rollout records, checkpoints, and internal trainer. Only the upstream task rows, runner fork, container registry, paper prompt/tool appendix, and reported settings are public.
