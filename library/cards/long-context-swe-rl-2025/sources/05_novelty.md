The prior-work baseline has three separable layers: SWE-rebench supplies real repository tasks and executable environments; SWE-agent-style systems supply the shell/edit/search interaction loop; DAPO supplies the clipped token-level group-relative optimization objective. Binary test validation and success filtering are also established ideas. The paper's contribution is not any one of those components in isolation.

What changes is their coupling at long horizon and training scale. The authors specify a paper-specific 21,336-to-7,249 task filter, create 6,548 self-generated successful episodes for 1-epoch RFT without a stronger teacher, then train on synchronous groups of 10 repository episodes in two stages that expand from 65k/40 turns to 131k/80 turns. The same terminal interface connects repository execution, binary validation, a soft length penalty, group normalization, and token updates.

For reasoning-data research, the direction signal is the contrast between outcome-conditioned warm-up data and mixed-outcome on-policy data. RFT erases whole failed attempts but retains successful episodes with selected malformed turns masked; RL can learn from relative differences among both successes and failures, while homogeneous groups are dropped. This makes sample retention and environment version part of the learning-data definition, rather than incidental preprocessing.

The quality signal is operational, not semantic: a trajectory is useful to the recipe when it leads to executable test success or has informative relative reward within its group. The verifier cannot establish correctness beyond the test suite, and the reported benchmark gains are not evidence that every retained trajectory is high quality. The closest reusable object remains upstream SWE-rebench; the paper-specific manifests and trajectories are not available.

Before reuse, a curator must verify the exact task IDs and filter implementation, the 2,028-task Stage 2 rule, group outcome statistics, test-oracle reliability, container/runtime pins, reset and replay semantics, trajectory rights, and contamination controls. Without those, the work is a precise method and audit reference, not a released training corpus.
