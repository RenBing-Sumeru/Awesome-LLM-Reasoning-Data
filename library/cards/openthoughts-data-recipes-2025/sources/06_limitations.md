- **No answer-level correctness contract.** OpenThoughts3 intentionally retains every successful QwQ-32B response without a semantic verifier. Incorrect derivations, wrong final answers, hallucinated science, non-executable code, and persuasive but unfaithful reasoning can therefore enter SFT unchanged. A successful API completion is the terminal predicate, not task success.
- **Verifier conclusions are conditional.** The headline no-filter math comparison uses 63,200 examples against 31,600 for filtered conditions and is not compute-controlled. The code unit-test and GPT-judge results cover particular generators, prompts, and benchmarks. Generalizing them to “answer verification never helps” would contradict the paper's own 32B-versus-7B scale result.
- **Lexical decontamination is incomplete.** The Indel/13-gram detector has observed false negatives and false positives on its constructed testbed, cannot rule out paraphrastic or semantic overlap, and ships without match/removal manifests. Eight recurring benchmarks guide most recipe choices, so four held-out benchmarks reduce but do not eliminate benchmark-selection pressure.
- **Row-level provenance is too coarse.** `source`, `domain`, and `difficulty` do not recover upstream item IDs, URLs, revisions, transformations, dedup groups, selection decisions, teacher request IDs, seeds, or decontamination outcomes. This blocks exact attribution and makes source-level error or contamination removal difficult.
- **Rights are unresolved.** The HF dataset is labeled Apache-2.0, but Appendix Q names upstream CC BY, CC BY-SA, OpenAI Terms, educational-use material, a no-license book, and multiple all-rights-reserved organic-chemistry books. No row-level rights manifest reconciles those terms. Curator conclusion: direct training redistribution or commercial reuse is blocked pending legal review and provenance repair.
- **Failures and rejected candidates are absent.** Successful QwQ answers are retained, but removed prompts, failed or content-filtered generations, rejected answers, dedup groups, and decontamination decisions are not released. Researchers cannot estimate selection bias, reconstruct false-reject rates, or train from informative failures.
- **Final-run reconstruction is incomplete.** Representative C1 YAMLs expose plausible decoding knobs, but the exact 1.2M-run manifest, random seeds, per-source counts, retry policy, accepted/failed yields, container hashes, and checkpoint crosswalk are not pinned. Repository and dataset drift can therefore change a reproduction.
- **Security blocker.** Three official QwQ annotation YAMLs at the inspected repository SHA contain a plaintext API credential. The secret is not reproduced here. It must be removed and rotated, and the configs must use secret injection before the repository is safe to reuse or fork.
- **Teacher-trait transfer.** Because the answer and reasoning are generated by QwQ-32B and supervised together, the student can inherit teacher verbosity, formatting, unsupported reasoning patterns, or domain biases. This is a curator inference; the release provides no row-level faithfulness labels or comparison of hidden teacher traits.
- **Evaluation does not certify the data.** OpenThinker3-7B benchmark scores combine source choice, scale, teacher, training setup, and evaluation. They are author-reported, were not independently reproduced for this Card, and do not establish row-level correctness, privacy/consent, rights, or deployment robustness.
- **Bibliographic version drift.** ArXiv v2 lists 50 authors; the official ICLR 2026 Oral page lists 51 and adds Stutee Acharya. The Card follows the accepted venue page while preserving the discrepancy as an audit note.
