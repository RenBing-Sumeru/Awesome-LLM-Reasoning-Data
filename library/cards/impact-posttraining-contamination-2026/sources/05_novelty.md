Earlier controlled contamination work usually measures immediately after pre-training, while post-finetuning studies used smaller classification models. This paper changes the decision point: it follows matched billion-parameter math and code models through SFT and GRPO and tests both leaked and counterpart tasks. The novelty is not injecting benchmarks itself, but locating contamination measurement after realistic post-training and separating local score inflation from external transfer. It does not establish a general detector or a mitigation method.
