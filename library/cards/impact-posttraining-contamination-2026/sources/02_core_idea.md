The contribution is a lifecycle contamination experiment: identical Qwen2.5 and Gemma3 checkpoints differ only by five injected copies of GSM8K and MBPP test items, then receive matched SFT or rule-reward GRPO. The evaluation surface is the clean–contaminated score difference on leaked versus counterpart benchmarks. Unlike base-only studies, it attributes the visible gap to the interaction between exposure, continued pre-training, post-training recipe, and scale; no official downloadable dataset beyond paper materials was identified.
