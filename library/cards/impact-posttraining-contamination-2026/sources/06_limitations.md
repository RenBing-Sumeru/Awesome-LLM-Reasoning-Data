The authors isolate a late five-copy injection, whereas real leakage can be paraphrased, repeated, or distributed through pre-training; the reported dynamics may change. They also cover only two open families up to 4B parameters and rule-reward GRPO, so proprietary models, other RLHF objectives, and human preferences remain untested. Reuse should reproduce the clean counterpart and assess both leaked and matched clean benchmarks rather than treating one inflated score as memorization proof.
