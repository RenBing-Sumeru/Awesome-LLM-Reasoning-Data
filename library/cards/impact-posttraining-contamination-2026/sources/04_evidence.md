Claim: post-training can reveal contamination that base-model evaluation masks. In the controlled Qwen2.5 and Gemma3 comparisons, the same 25B-token continuation and matched update budgets isolate the injected test items and recipe. After post-training, leaked-task gaps exceed 2 percentage points for every reported math and code setting except Qwen2.5-0.5B SFT on GSM8K, reaching 4 points for Qwen2.5-0.5B (Figures 3–4). SFT's extra gap is largely absent on GSMPlus/HumanEval, whereas GRPO also produces a smaller positive gap there (Figure 5). This supports a post-training audit requirement, not universal claims about proprietary or larger models.
