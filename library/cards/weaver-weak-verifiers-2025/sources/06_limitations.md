- Conditional independence is the central modeling risk. Verifiers often share base architectures, reward-model training data, benchmark exposure, prompts, and failure modes. Pairwise moment fitting can therefore underestimate dependence and produce overconfident posterior scores.
- Normalization, binarization, and marginal filtering are dataset-conditioned. A 5th/95th percentile transform and class-balance thresholds can change under verifier revisions, candidate-distribution shift, or a new domain; binarization also discards score magnitude. The 1% labeled development sample is small but not label-free.
- Weaver always ranks the available pool. If no sampled response is correct, selection cannot recover one. AIMO is explicitly cited as difficult because correct generations are rare.
- The optional difficulty-cluster analysis uses ground-truth correctness to define query difficulty and tunes thresholds on held-out labels. It is an oracle exploration, not evidence that unsupervised routing is already solved.
- Distillation transfers Weaver's posterior errors as soft supervision. The reported cross-encoder results show system-level retention on studied splits, not per-record pseudo-label correctness, calibrated uncertainty on new domains, or safety for RLHF use.
- The proceedings and arXiv materials expose version differences in title and headline averages (86.2% versus 87.7%, and 98.7% versus 98.2% in related retention wording). Results must be reproduced against pinned paper, code, data, and model revisions.
- The public release improves reproducibility but does not by itself provide a complete record-level lineage from every table row to generator run, verifier checkpoint, raw score, binary vote, fitted parameter set, posterior, and selected candidate. Exact release-to-paper coverage remains to be audited.
- Decontamination is unknown. Benchmark prompts, candidate generators, reward models, and judges may have overlapping training exposure. Record-level upstream license and attribution across heterogeneous benchmarks, generations, and model outputs are also unknown even though the code repository is MIT.
- Evaluation concentrates on fixed-answer reasoning and mathematics benchmarks. The paper discusses code, multimodal verification, data curation, and RLHF as broader directions, but does not validate Weaver as a programmatic verifier, multimodal judge, or safe training-data gate in those settings.
