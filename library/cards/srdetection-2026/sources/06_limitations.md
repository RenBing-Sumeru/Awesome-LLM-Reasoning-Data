The detector assumes that generated variants preserve functionality and are sufficiently diverse; weak transformations weaken the contrastive signal. Reuse should execute or otherwise validate variants before interpreting a flag.

The authors note that subtle output differences may be masked for very large or instruction-tuned black-box models, and they test code only. A non-flag does not establish absence of leakage; audit additional signals and modalities before making release claims.

The controlled continued-pretraining labels are useful ground truth but do not reproduce every unknown web-scale pretraining mixture.
They also do not show that a detected item is the only cause of an observed benchmark score.
