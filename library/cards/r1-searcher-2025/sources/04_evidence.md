Stage 1 combines retrieval and format rewards; its purpose is protocol acquisition, so answer correctness is intentionally absent. Stage 2 keeps format checks and uses token-level F1 between predicted and reference short answers as the answer reward. Experiments use Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen-2.5-7B-Base, evaluate multi-hop QA, and test online-search transfer on Bamboogle. These model results test the training design; they do not prove that retrieved documents or generated reasoning traces are factually clean.
