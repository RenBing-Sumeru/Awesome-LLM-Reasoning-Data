Questions come from the HotpotQA and 2WikiMultiHopQA training splits. Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct with a local retriever is rolled out repeatedly for data selection: fewer than 10 successful-answer rollouts is “easy,” 10–20 is “medium,” and more than 20 is “difficult.” Stage 1 uses 200 medium HotpotQA and 150 medium 2Wiki examples. Stage 2 reports 2,561 medium plus 2,000 difficult HotpotQA examples and 1,087 medium plus 2,500 difficult 2Wiki examples. The paper's labels are difficulty bins derived from rollout behavior, not human process annotations.
