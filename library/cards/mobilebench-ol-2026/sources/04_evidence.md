All performance figures below are author-reported and have not been independently replayed. Under a three-times-golden-step budget with three seconds after each action, UI-TARS-1.5-7B is the strongest Table 5 baseline: 60.97% Base SR, 41.76% Long-Tail, 15.00% Long-Horizon, 38.33% GUI-Reasoning, and 56.77% Noise-Robust. Its corresponding Sub-SR values are 64.84%, 46.32%, 45.25%, 40.83%, and 62.18%. The 15.00% Long-Horizon result is the clearest evidence for the paper's claim that full multi-subtask completion remains difficult; it is not evidence that each failure arose from reasoning rather than grounding, verifier, state, or budget effects (Table 5).

The verifier is empirically calibrated against humans on 1,080 UI-TARS-1.5 episodes. Table 12 reports 534 true positives, 5 false positives, 22 false negatives, and 519 true negatives: 97.50% overall agreement, with Auto-Eval SR 49.91% versus human SR 51.48%. The 27 disagreements matter more than the aggregate accuracy for reuse: false positives become verifier-gaming opportunities, while false negatives discard valid behavior. Long-Horizon happened to show 100% agreement on only 60 examples and should not be generalized beyond that sample.

Reset validation executes 255 task-level/app-level reset tasks across Base, Long-Tail, GUI-Reasoning, and Noise-Robust. Human success rates in Table 13 are 96.92%, 94.35%, 95.65%, and 92.31%; Auto-Eval estimates are close but not identical. These results support the narrower claim that inverse tasks usually restore observable state. They do not show bitwise state restoration, cover Long-Horizon resets, or eliminate account/server drift and infeasible actions.

Budget changes performance. On Base, UI-TARS-1.5 pass@1/3/5 is 60.97%, 69.03%, and 73.87%; Qwen2.5-VL is 17.10%, 31.29%, and 39.35% (Appendix H.3, Table 20). Scores must therefore report retry count, and comparisons cannot silently mix single-run SR with best-of-k success.

The release itself is verifiable but incomplete. The official data tree exposes 310 Base, 340 Long-Tail, 60 Long-Horizon, and 60 GUI-Reasoning task rows plus reset files; README documents local trajectory output. GitHub flags illegal quoting in `longtail.csv` at line 118. No frozen baseline-result bundle or the ≥5-per-task validation trajectories are present. The paper also observes no more than 5% score fluctuation after updating apps two months later, which demonstrates version sensitivity rather than eliminating it (Appendix A.2).
