**Inputs.** The benchmark starts from 80 versioned Chinese apps in 12 categories and manually authored tasks. Table 4 contains 310 Base, 340 Long-Tail, 60 Long-Horizon, 60 GUI-Reasoning, and 310 Noise-Robust cases. The last subset reuses Base tasks. Each test begins at the relevant app home page; paper devices have installed apps and logged-in accounts.

**Construction.** Annotators create tasks from scratch and execute them on real devices to obtain golden trajectories and step counts. Domain experts derive success conditions from task-trajectory pairs. UI-TARS-1.5 and annotators sample diverse completion paths; Auto-Eval labels them; experts inspect discrepancies and revise rules. The paper reports at least five samples per task, but not the exact human/model mix, total attempts, rejected attempts, or record-level rule-revision history (Paper §3.3, Figure 3).

**Interaction.** A device supplies a screenshot and XML UI hierarchy; the agent also receives its interaction history according to its scaffold. Model-specific actions are translated into a unified space: click, type, scroll, press home/back, wait, long press, and finished. The maximum episode length is three times the human golden steps, a three-second delay follows every action, and the loop stops on completion signaling or the step limit (Appendix A.3-A.4, G.2).

**Noise and reset.** Noise-Robust injects one of repeat, unexecuted, delay, or pop-up with 20% probability at each step. After a benchmark round, a reliable agent executes 255 fine-grained inverse tasks. Resets are task-level, app-level, unnecessary, or infeasible; infeasible cases widen the success condition instead of fully restoring state. Exact noise seeds, account/server fixtures, reset retry policy, and a state-diff ledger are not disclosed.

**Verification and outputs.** Success conditions are normalized XPath-like conjunctions over GUI elements and critical actions. All conditions plus Complete yields Success; all conditions at the step limit yields Overdue; Complete before all conditions yields Early Termination; otherwise the label is Failure. The released harness writes task/success pairs and per-task `trajectory.json` objects with action, image, response, summary, and success; Sub-SR and Step Ratio add partial and efficiency metrics.

**Release and use.** The official repository provides Python code, configs, evaluator modules, and seven task/reset CSVs. Dataset terms are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and source code is Apache-2.0. Reproduction needs a physical Android device with ADB/uiautomator2 plus appropriate apps/accounts. The paper promises all 80 APKs, but no official APK bundle was verified. There is no train/dev/test split, frozen paper-trajectory release, semantic version, or complete device/account/server snapshot, so the supported use is evaluation, not SFT or RL training.
