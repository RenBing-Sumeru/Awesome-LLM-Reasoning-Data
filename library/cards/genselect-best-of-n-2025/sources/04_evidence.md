On QwQ-32B's 64-candidate Comp-Math-24-25 pools, Table 3 reports 68.4% for Majority@64, 66.8% for Qwen2.5-Math-RM-72B, 69.1% for the pointwise QwQ GenRM, 72.1% for QwQ GenSelect@1, and 73.4% when GenSelect is repeated eight times and its selected answers are majority-voted; Pass@64 is 85.2%. These are selector accuracies over the same reported pool, not evidence that any released dataset is complete or high quality. The gap to Pass@64 also shows that selection remains materially imperfect even when a correct candidate is present.

Table 4 varies the tournament branching factor over \(N \in \{2,4,8,16\}\) while retaining 64 candidates. GenSelect@1 ranges from 72.1% to 72.6%, and GenSelect@8 ranges from 73.0% to 73.4%. The authors interpret this as stability across the tested brackets, which supports reducing sequential rounds when context permits. It does not establish order invariance: the final system still repeats random candidate permutations 32 times, and the paper does not publish per-permutation variance or bracket-level decision logs.

The equal-generation-call ablation in Table 2 is conditional on model and budget. For DeepSeek-R1-0528, \(2N\) generation plus majority voting versus \(N\) generation plus \(N\) GenSelect generation yields 82.8% versus 82.4% at \(N=4\), 84.4% versus 85.9% at \(N=8\), and 84.4% versus 87.1% at \(N=16\). For QwQ-32B, the corresponding values are 66.4% versus 69.5%, 66.8% versus 69.5%, and 68.0% versus 71.9%. The paper explicitly notes that the selector allocation has worse latency because verification starts after generation; no token-normalized or wall-clock comparison is reported.

In the final 32-repeat, \(N=8\) tournament with 64 candidates, Table 5 reports DeepSeek-R1-0528 pass@1 of 71.2%, maj@64 of 84.0%, and Self-GenSelect@32 of 87.1% over all 256 problems. QwQ-32B reports 60.0%, 68.4%, and 73.0%, respectively. Most of the stated gain is on the HMMT subset, whose correctness uses an LLM judge. All results are author-reported and were not independently reproduced for this Card.
