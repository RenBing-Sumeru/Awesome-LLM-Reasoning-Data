Read the decomposition examples before reading the aggregate tables. The central object is the atomic fact, not the paragraph-level answer and not the final percentage.

Keep three quantities separate: response ratio, number of facts per response, and FActScore. The first says whether the system answers, the second says how much factual content it attempts, and the third estimates precision among attempted facts.

When comparing systems, pin the source snapshot and estimator. A higher FActScore under Wikipedia 2023-04-01 does not prove that the answer is complete, up to date, or correct under a different domain-specific source.
