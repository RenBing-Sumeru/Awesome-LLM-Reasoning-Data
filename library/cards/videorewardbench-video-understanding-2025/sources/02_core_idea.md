The main artifact is VideoRewardBench, containing 1,563 video preference examples over 1,482 videos. Each record organizes a task input, visual or environmental state, candidate response or trajectory, evaluation evidence, and a preference, score, success, or critique label, together with task provenance and capability categories. Quality control relies on generating chosen and rejected answers for video questions and verifying preferences across perception, knowledge, reasoning, and safety, making the release suitable for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than ordinary question answering.
