For a video reward-model evaluation system, VideoRewardBench can serve as training data, a hidden evaluation set, or a pre-deployment audit set. Users can map tasks, states, and candidate responses or trajectories to preferences, scores, success labels, error types, or structured critiques. Success should combine agreement, classification or ranking metrics, unseen-task generalization, and real gains in evaluating video reward models and multimodal judges. Large distribution shifts, high error costs, or weak verification require human, executable, or expert review.
