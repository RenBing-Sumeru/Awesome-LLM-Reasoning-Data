1. **Positioning:** DiscoverLLM trains assistants to help users form intentions, not merely reveal fixed hidden requirements.
2. **Method handle:** Intent-tree construction, partial-state user simulation, candidate generation, transition judging, and discovery reward are the decisive steps.
3. **Artifact handle:** The released dataset contains 9,318 English candidate-level records with context, completion, score, intent history, and provenance.
4. **Evidence anchor:** The best Qwen variant improves Discovery by 13.7%–14.3% with 31.9% fewer tokens; 75 users also report better satisfaction and efficiency.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is suitable for multi-turn preference learning in open-ended creation, but independent human checks are required to detect simulator bias and user steering.
