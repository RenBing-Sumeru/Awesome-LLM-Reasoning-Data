The reference label is a consensus of three author-recruited experts after debate, not verified ground truth. Their initial pairwise disagreement is substantial, so reuse should measure inter-rater agreement and preserve adjudication records rather than treating alignment as evaluator correctness.

The proof of concept uses relatively small AI-development workspaces. Search, planning, and memory reduced alignment in this setting, and black-box trajectory access changes the evidence available; validate the module set, tool access, and cost on the target environment before deployment.
