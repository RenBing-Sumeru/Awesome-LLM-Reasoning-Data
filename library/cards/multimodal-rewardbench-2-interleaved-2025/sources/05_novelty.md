Closest prior work uses general preference pairs, terminal success, or step accuracy without representing the domain-specific states and failure modes of interleaved multimodal reward evaluation. This paper changes the record structure and feedback contract by centering Multimodal RewardBench 2 and combining inputs, candidates, environmental or evidential state, and labels in one auditable example through constructing two candidate outputs for image generation, editing, interleaved generation, and image reasoning and labeling them with task-specific expert criteria. The contribution is task-specific data construction, capability decomposition, and practical evaluation rather than sample count alone.
