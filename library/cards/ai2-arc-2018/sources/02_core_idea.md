The contribution is the AI2 Reasoning Challenge: 7,787 grade-school science multiple-choice questions, with 5,197 ARC-Easy items and 2,590 ARC-Challenge items. The core mechanism is to gather real science exam questions and define the Challenge set as questions answered incorrectly by both a retrieval-based solver and a word co-occurrence solver. Compared with ordinary exam QA sets, ARC makes the filtering baseline part of the evaluation surface. The direction label is static benchmark plus answer-key verifier. Reuse must preserve split, train/dev/test files, official scorer, and the fact that the Challenge label reflects historical baseline failures, not a proof of intrinsic difficulty.
