ARC was released by the Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence with a 2018 arXiv paper and official dataset page. The problem is that many QA systems appeared strong on earlier benchmarks while still failing grade-school science questions requiring broad knowledge, multi-sentence evidence, or simple reasoning. The data object is a multiple-choice science exam question with answer choices, a gold option, and Easy/Challenge split metadata. The feedback contract is answer-level multiple-choice accuracy under the released split and scorer. ARC belongs in the atlas as a static science-QA evaluation surface, especially for studying how a benchmark subset can be constructed by filtering out shallow retrieval and word-association successes.
