ARC validates only final multiple-choice answers. It does not verify explanations, retrieved facts, or whether the model used legitimate science knowledge. The Challenge split is conditioned on older shallow solvers, so its difficulty definition can age as systems and retrieval corpora change. Public release creates substantial contamination risk. Some questions may be answerable by test-taking heuristics or artifacts, and score comparisons can change with prompt formatting, option order, or whether a model is allowed external retrieval.
