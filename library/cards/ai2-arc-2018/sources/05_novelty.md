The prior baseline was multiple-choice science QA or open-domain QA scored by answer keys. ARC changes the construction rule by explicitly using simple solvers to split out a Challenge subset that resists retrieval and word association. The quality signal is the use of real exam questions plus public train/dev/test splits; the direction signal is benchmark design where a baseline failure condition becomes part of the data lineage. What is not new is multiple-choice scoring, static answer keys, or exam-question sourcing. Before reuse, inspect version, split, licensing, answer-label format, contamination, and whether the target claim is about ARC-Challenge rather than all science QA.
