ProJudgeBench contains 2,400 test cases and 50,118 expert step labels across multimodal mathematics, physics, chemistry, and biology, with correctness, error type, and explanation for each step. ProJudge-173k provides about 173K related training records. The two resources separate strict human evaluation from scalable training rather than treating automatic labels as ground truth. The central artifact is a data system for judges that detect, classify, and diagnose multimodal process errors.
