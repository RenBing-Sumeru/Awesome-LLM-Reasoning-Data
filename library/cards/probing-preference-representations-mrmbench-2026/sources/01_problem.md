Aggregate RewardBench accuracy only shows whether an RM selects the chosen response and cannot reveal whether it represents correctness, helpfulness, safety, or superficial cues such as length. Models with similar totals may encode very different preferences and have different value in PPO.

The paper introduces a multidimensional probing framework and MRMBench, decomposing preference capability into harmlessness, helpfulness, correctness, coherence, complexity, and verbosity, with easier binary probes and harder ternary judgments of reward representations.
