Check whether the comparison includes all verification tokens, whether the verifier sees a gold solution during training but not at test time, and how correlated the sampled solutions and verifier verdicts are. For replication, sweep S and V jointly rather than holding one fixed, report the entire success-rate–compute frontier, and stratify by task difficulty. The important deployment question is not whether a verifier can identify a correct solution, but whether doing so beats generating the additional solutions that its verification cost would otherwise have purchased.
