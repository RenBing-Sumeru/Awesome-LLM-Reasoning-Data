Judge prompts are fragile. Length bias, position bias, self-preference, verbosity, criterion ambiguity, refusal bias, and score-range collapse can change outputs. A model may generate a detailed explanation that rationalizes a wrong verdict. Before using it as a reward source, evaluate response-order swaps, multiple rubric phrasings, calibration curves, ties, and disagreement with independent experts. Do not use a single judge call as the only safety or correctness gate.
