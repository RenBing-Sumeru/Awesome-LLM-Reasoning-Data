A replication log should keep the raw prompt and raw judge completion for every decision. Record model revision, evaluation-instruction template, criterion, candidate ordering, temperature, max tokens, direct-score mapping, pairwise tie rule, and benchmark-specific aggregation. Analyze separately whether an error came from misunderstanding the task, ignoring the rubric, choosing the wrong answer, or emitting an unparsable format. These diagnostics are more informative than an average agreement alone.
