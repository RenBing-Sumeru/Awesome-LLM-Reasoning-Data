The paper's principal quantitative evidence is a progressive AIME 2024 avg@32 table on Qwen2.5-32B. DeepSeek-R1-Zero-Qwen-32B is listed at 47; naive GRPO reaches 30; then cumulative additions reach 36 with Overlong Filtering, 38 with Clip-Higher, 41 with Soft Overlong Punishment, 42 with Token-level Loss, and 50 with Dynamic Sampling/DAPO (Paper §4.2, Table 1). Figure 1 further reports that DAPO reaches 50 using half the gradient-update steps shown for the DeepSeek-R1-Zero comparison.

The conditions matter. Paper §4.1 specifies 512 prompts, 16 responses per prompt, 20,480 maximum response tokens, Clip-Higher 0.2/0.28, AdamW at 1e-6, and AIME evaluation repeated 32 times with temperature 1.0/top-p 0.7. Table 1 is a cumulative progression, not a factorial ablation that isolates every component. The paper evaluates the final method only on AIME 2024, so it does not establish transfer to other math sets or domains.

The official verl-recipe separately reports a reproduction at 52% AIME 2024 for full DAPO and 50% without Dynamic Sampling on 128 H800 GPUs at verl commit `4f80e4`; this later artifact is evidence that a pinned implementation can run, not proof that Table 1's final eight-point difference is an invariant causal effect. The paper itself also notes that training-set reward has little correlation with validation accuracy, an important negative result for using reward curves as quality evidence (Paper §4.3).

None of these scores validates individual dataset rows, transformation fidelity, intermediate reasoning, source rights, or decontamination. The evidence supports the reported system behavior under a narrow benchmark and configuration; it does not by itself make the public dataset safe for training reuse.
