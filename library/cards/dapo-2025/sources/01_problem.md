DAPO is an arXiv preprint first posted in March 2025; this Card uses arXiv v2 dated 20 May 2025 and the official project, DAPO, Hugging Face, and verl-recipe artifacts. The paper addresses a specific reproducibility problem: naive GRPO on Qwen2.5-32B reached only 30 AIME 2024 avg@32, while large-scale long-CoT RL was affected by entropy collapse, zero-gradient prompt groups, length-dependent loss weighting, and noisy penalties on truncated responses (Paper §§1, 3-4).

The relevant Atlas object is a two-layer RLVR record. The released layer is intended to contain 17K processed math prompts paired with integer answers. The online layer adds 16 policy responses per prompt, extracted-answer correctness, a length adjustment, a prompt-group keep/drop decision, and token-level loss terms. Only the processed prompt-answer rows are released; transformation traces, online rollouts, rejected groups, and update logs are not.

This belongs to Data Construction and Open Release Recipes because source selection, integer-answer rewriting, verifier design, rollout allocation, and dynamic filtering jointly determine the effective training distribution. It is not evidence for step-correct reasoning, general-domain RL, or a static CoT corpus. The Card reaches L4 by grounding the pipeline, hyperparameters, progressive AIME result, public row schema, and release drift, while preserving unknown source lineage, decontamination, training verifier implementation, and transformation error rate.
