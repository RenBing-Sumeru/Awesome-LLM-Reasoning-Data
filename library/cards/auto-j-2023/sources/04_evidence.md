The paper reports that Auto-J improves pairwise evaluation over prior open-source models by 8.9 percentage points and outperforms a closed-source chat baseline and Claude-2 by 12.1 and 12.4 points in the reported pairwise setting. Its pairwise testbed has 1,392 samples across 58 scenarios, each with two model responses and a human-annotated preference. For single-response critique generation, both GPT-4 and expert human evaluations are used; for rating usefulness, best-of-N selection and response-level correlations with GPT-4 ratings are reported. On AlpacaEval system ranking, the paper reports Spearman 0.97 and Pearson 0.96 correlation with GPT-4 ranking. Evidence is bounded by GPT-4-as-teacher labels, selected scenarios, prompt formats, and parser reliability; a generated critique is evidence of the judge rationale, not proof that the rating is objectively correct.
