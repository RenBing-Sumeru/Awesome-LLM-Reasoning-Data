The contribution is a 13B generative judge trained to emit both decisions and structured critiques for pairwise and single-response evaluation. The core mechanism is scenario-aware data construction: define 58 scenarios and 332 criteria, use GPT-4 to produce reformatted judgments and critiques, filter inconsistent outputs, then train Auto-J to apply the criteria without requiring them in the inference input. The data object is a query-response record with source dataset, scenario, protocol, judgment, and critique. The feedback contract is model-generated but criterion-guided: pairwise winner/tie or single-response rating plus critique. Closest comparisons include PandaLM, JudgeLM, Prometheus-style critics, reward models, closed-source chat baselines, Claude-2, and GPT-4. The direction label is open generative judge with critique supervision.
