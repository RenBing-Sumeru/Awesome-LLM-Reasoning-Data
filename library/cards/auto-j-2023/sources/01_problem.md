Auto-J targets the evaluation gap for aligned chat models: open-ended user queries often lack references, and evaluators must support both pairwise comparison and single-response scoring with explanations. The primary source is the arXiv paper, ICLR 2024 status in the official repository, the GAIR-NLP repository, and the Hugging Face model card. The evaluation surface is a user query, one or two model responses, a scenario label or criterion set, a preference or numeric rating, and a natural-language critique. It belongs in the atlas as a generative judge and judge-data release, not as a general instruction-tuning corpus or a neutral human benchmark. Its value is the explicit judge feedback contract over 58 scenarios and two evaluation protocols.
