The prior baseline is specialized evaluators that usually support one protocol, rely on narrower synthetic data, or provide little explanation. Auto-J changes the object by combining pairwise and single-response evaluation in one generative judge and by attaching scenario-aware critiques to judgments. The direction signal is critique-bearing evaluator data that can be inspected beyond scalar agreement. The quality signal is performance against open and closed baselines across the 58-scenario testbed plus released checkpoints, criteria, prompts, and data. What is not new: LLM-as-judge, GPT-4 teacher labeling, and reward-model evaluation are existing components. Reuse requires checking license compatibility for model checkpoints and source datasets, scenario balance, language coverage, GPT-4 label bias, and whether critiques are parsed consistently.
