This is a map of the literature, not a release of one PRM. It covers process-data generation, PRM construction, test-time scaling, reinforcement learning, applications, and emerging benchmarks. The cited work varies in where traces come from, how finely they are labeled, who or what supplies feedback, and how scores shape search or training. Alongside the ACL page, PDF, checklist, DOI, and arXiv record, the authors provide a GitHub reading list organized around the same lifecycle; it is a curation resource, not a common PRM codebase or dataset.
