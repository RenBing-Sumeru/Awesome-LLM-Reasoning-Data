The paper evaluates verifier-guided beam search with Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B and Qwen2.5-Math-7B generators, together with Math-Shepherd-Mistral-7B-PRM and Skywork-o1-Open-PRM-Qwen-2.5-1.5B verifiers. Its evaluation covers AIME 2024, AMC 2023, and MATH-500, and the artifact appendix describes automated benchmark workflows, configuration files, JSONL logs, and reproduction scripts. The target hardware is a single NVIDIA RTX 4090 with 24 GB of memory, which makes the reported results directly relevant to constrained edge serving rather than unconstrained cluster inference.

Against the vLLM baseline, FastTTS reports an average 2.2-times improvement in goodput and a 38% to 68% reduction in latency while preserving the algorithmic output of the underlying verifier-guided search. The paper attributes the gain to complementary effects: speculative execution hides irregular beam lengths, prefix-aware ordering prevents avoidable KV-cache loss, and asymmetric memory allocation improves the generator-verifier balance. It also profiles the workload to show that verification has stable prefill behavior whereas generation becomes underutilized as beams finish unevenly.
