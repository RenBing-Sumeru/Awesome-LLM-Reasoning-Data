FastTTS targets a specific hardware and workload regime: verifier-guided tree search on a memory-constrained edge GPU. Its gains depend on enough beam parallelism, useful correlation between past verifier scores and survival, exploitable prefix sharing, and the selected generator-verifier pair. The paper does not establish that the same scheduling choices dominate for long sequential chains, tools with variable external latency, different accelerators, or reward models whose step scores are unreliable. It describes a reproducibility artifact but the official paper does not provide a public repository URL, so independent reuse still requires locating its release and checking its terms.
