Prior controlled work used small synthetic settings, while suites such as Pythia expose training dynamics without known interventions for diverse sensitive texts. Hubble changes the data object: paired, larger open models differ by randomized and quantified insertions across copyright, privacy, and contamination domains. This makes duplicate frequency and insertion time causal variables rather than post-hoc hypotheses. The model architecture and standard capability evaluation are not themselves new; the reusable contribution is the controlled release and its linked evaluations.
