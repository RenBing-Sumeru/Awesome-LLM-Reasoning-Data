Claim: memorization depends on a sensitive text's frequency relative to corpus size and on when it appears. The core experiment holds architecture and perturbation design fixed while comparing 1B/8B models, standard versus perturbed data, and 100B versus 500B tokens. Figure 2 shows weaker memorization at the same duplication level after 500B than after 100B tokens; timing runs show that text inserted earlier and followed by further training is more readily forgotten. These controlled results support dilution and early placement as mitigations within Hubble's corpus and architecture, not as a guarantee for proprietary training pipelines.
