Hubble is a fully open suite of paired Llama-style models: standard models train on a fixed English corpus, and perturbed counterparts receive controlled insertions covering copyright passages, biographies and chats, and test sets. Its core 2×2×2 release contains eight 1B/8B models trained on 100B/500B tokens, plus timing and auxiliary runs. The feedback contract is known membership and duplicate count, enabling causal evaluations of extraction, membership inference, unlearning, and contamination. Models, datasets, and code are released through the official project.
