A memorization researcher can use a standard/perturbed pair and its known member labels to evaluate membership inference or extraction without natural-data confounders. An unlearning researcher can use the biographies, duplicate counts, and matched models to test whether removal works at different memorization strengths. The output is a controlled measurement curve rather than an estimate of a production model's training corpus. Do not use Hubble as evidence that a particular proprietary model trained on a named document; it supplies experimental counterfactuals, not provenance.
