The baseline is the growing set of OS, browser, office, and tool-use agent benchmarks with shorter or less domain-specialized tasks. APEX-Agents changes the evaluation surface by using expert-authored professional service worlds, gold deliverables, binary criteria, and an execution stack that records cross-application work. The quality signal is the combination of open benchmark metadata, gold outputs, criterion-level grading, and Archipelago infrastructure. It does not make model-as-judge grading or office applications new; reuse should inspect judge calibration, rubric ambiguity, licensing and gated access terms, and whether professional assumptions are simulated rather than legally or financially authoritative.
