The conclusions concern the reported translation models, languages, prompts, and automatic or LLM-based metrics; a different quality target, such as human literary preference, may change the observed optimum. The paper evaluates controlled budget forcing rather than every possible search or verification strategy, and its translation-specialized result is strongest in-domain. Post-editing also adds latency and can propagate a bad first draft. A deployment should therefore measure user-facing quality and cost separately rather than infer that any second pass is beneficial.
