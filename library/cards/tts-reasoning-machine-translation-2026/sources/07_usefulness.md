Use this paper when deciding whether a translation system should buy more internal thinking or an explicit revision pass. Log allocated and actual thinking tokens, natural stopping points, direct and post-edited outputs, each quality metric, and end-to-end latency. Compare fixed-budget direct translation with naturally stopped reasoning and a matched-cost post-editing pipeline. The work is especially useful as a caution against importing math-oriented “think longer” recipes into translation without checking whether the model has task-specific reasoning competence.
