Read the actual-token curves beside quality curves. A requested budget is not the same as a budget the model uses, and a plateau may reflect either natural stopping or a lack of useful task knowledge. Inspect the direct-translation, forced-wait, and post-editing prompts separately, as they represent different interventions. A faithful replication should stratify in-domain and out-of-domain tasks, report multiple translation metrics, and match total tokens across workflows. That design reveals whether a gain comes from more computation, better task alignment, or merely a second opportunity to rewrite.
