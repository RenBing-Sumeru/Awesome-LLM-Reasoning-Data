Claim: factual augmentation makes multimodal RLHF more resistant to hallucination-driven reward hacking. Under the paper's LLaVA comparison, the RLHF model reaches 94% of text-only GPT-4 on LLaVA-Bench and the authors report a 60% improvement over other baselines on MMHal-Bench; their GPT-4-based MMHal judge agrees with human judgments on 94% of the checked cases. These are reported benchmark results, not proof that captions prevent hallucination outside the supplied factual contexts.
