LLaVA-RLHF combines 10,000 crowdworker comparisons of held-out image conversations with Fact-RLHF: the reward model receives factual context—five COCO captions or A-OKVQA rationales/options—alongside the image, prompt, and response. The feedback contract records a relative human choice centered on grounding and helpfulness; factual context calibrates the learned score but does not replace that preference label or establish universal visual truth.
