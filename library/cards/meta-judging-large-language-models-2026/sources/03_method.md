This is a literature survey. It takes publications on automated judging and meta-judging as input and organizes them under six perspectives. Its output is a framework for reading mechanisms, alignment training, evaluation protocols, limitations, and future directions. It does not create a new dataset, benchmark harness, reward model, or environment.

To use its framework on a concrete judge, store the exact evaluator prompt or rubric, model/version, temperature or decoding policy, candidate ordering, reference-answer access, output schema, and aggregation rule. The verifier is a judge rather than a deterministic checker, so evaluation should compare against suitable human or trusted references where available and test prompt, position, verbosity, and model-overlap sensitivity. Licenses, splits, contamination controls, and annotation methods are unknown at the survey level and must be checked in the underlying release.
