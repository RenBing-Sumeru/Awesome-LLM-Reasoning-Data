Earlier driving-language datasets commonly emphasize captions, proximity-based interactions, perception questions, or much smaller collections. WOMD-Reasoning changes the construction target toward traffic-rule-induced and intention-induced interactions that can exist even when agents are initially far apart, and scales this target across 63,000 real-world WOMD scenes.

The specific contribution is the combination of a rule-based motion-to-text translator, a structured five-part Q&A chain, future-aware interaction and intention generation, and optional simulated vision aligned to the same motion scene. The split between factual questions and interaction reasoning also supplies a concrete scaffold for Motion-LLaVA training and inference.

What is not new should remain explicit: WOMD-Reasoning does not introduce WOMD, general-purpose chat-model prompting, rule-based scene translation, LLaVA, MultiPath++, ScenarioNet, MetaDrive, SFT, or reference-based language evaluation. Its reported scale and benchmark results do not themselves establish record-level correctness. Reuse should be evaluated by lineage completeness, causal input availability, verifier coverage, and release reproducibility rather than by dataset size alone.
