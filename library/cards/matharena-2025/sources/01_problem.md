Primary source: Balunovic et al., "MathArena: Evaluating LLMs on Uncontaminated Math Competitions," arXiv 2025, with the official MathArena site, GitHub, and Hugging Face organization. The paper asks how to evaluate frontier models on math competitions that are newly released enough to reduce benchmark contamination. The decision boundary is live or recent competition evaluation, not a static textbook dataset and not formal proof verification. The data object is a competition problem, official answer or proof target, model output, parsed answer or judged proof, and score. Its atlas value is contamination-aware math evaluation.
