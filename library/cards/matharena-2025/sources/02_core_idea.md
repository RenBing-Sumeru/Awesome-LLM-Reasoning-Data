The contribution is a competition-backed math evaluation platform that refreshes the benchmark surface with recently released contests and releases model outputs/logs for audit. The mechanism is to collect current contest problems, run many models under a controlled evaluation setup, parse or judge outputs, and publish leaderboard artifacts. Closest comparisons are static MATH/MATH-500-style benchmarks and other math leaderboards. The feedback contract is mixed: answer-key checking for final-answer tasks and judge or expert assessment for proof-style tasks. The direction label is live contamination audit plus benchmark/evaluation surface.
