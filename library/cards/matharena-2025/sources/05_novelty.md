The prior baseline is static public math benchmarks, where contamination and overfitting are hard to separate from capability. MathArena changes the evaluation surface by anchoring it to recently released competitions and by releasing logs for audit. The direction signal is live, versioned math evaluation under a contamination-aware protocol. The quality signal is official platform artifacts plus raw model outputs. What is not new is math contest problem solving or leaderboard scoring. Reuse should inspect contest rights, platform snapshot, hidden/public policy, judge prompts, parser failures, model settings, and whether results are from the 2025 paper or a later platform state.
