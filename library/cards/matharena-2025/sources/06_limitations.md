Uncontaminated is a time-relative claim, not a permanent property. Once problems, answers, and logs are public, future training contamination becomes likely. Proof-style scoring is harder to audit than final-answer checking and can depend on judge instructions or human adjudication. Contest licenses and redistribution rights are competition-specific and should not be assumed. Leaderboard comparisons require pinned model versions, prompts, sampling budgets, and platform date. Do not mix 2025 paper results with later MathArena platform updates without version labels.
