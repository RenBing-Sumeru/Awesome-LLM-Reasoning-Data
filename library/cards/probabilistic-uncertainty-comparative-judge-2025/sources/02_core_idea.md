The core object is a probabilistic ranking model whose experts encode judge logits for pairwise or absolute scores. Soft Bradley--Terry becomes one special case; generalized Beta or Gaussian experts and a Laplace covariance yield a score distribution, a pairwise variance, and a probability that two items will reorder. That last quantity targets ranking error directly, so it can spend comparisons where a ranking is most likely to change. The paper reports no confirmed public code or dataset release.
