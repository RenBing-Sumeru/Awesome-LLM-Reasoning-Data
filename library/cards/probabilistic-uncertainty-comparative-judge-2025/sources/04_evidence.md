**Claim.** Reordering-aware acquisition reaches accurate comparative rankings with fewer judge calls. **Setup.** On SummEval attributes, Flan-T5-3B and Qwen judges provide biased or debiased probabilities; the baseline is soft Bradley--Terry with minimum-uncertainty selection. **Result.** To reach 90% of full-comparison performance, the proposed uncertainty metrics require about 60% fewer comparisons for Flan-T5 and 50% fewer for Qwen than that baseline; probability of reordering is the most robust selector. **Boundary.** This supports efficiency on these ranking settings, not calibrated uncertainty for arbitrary judges or domains.
