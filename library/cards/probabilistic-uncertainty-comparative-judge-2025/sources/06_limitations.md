The authors identify the Laplace approximation as the central limitation: it assumes an approximately Gaussian posterior, which can be inaccurate for multimodal or skewed score posteriors; then the acquisition signal can be wrong. Reuse should compare its uncertainty against held-out ranking errors rather than trust it by construction. The reported efficiency is centered on SummEval and two judge families, so transfer to a new rubric, prompt, or candidate distribution requires a fresh calibration check. No confirmed code release also limits exact replication.
