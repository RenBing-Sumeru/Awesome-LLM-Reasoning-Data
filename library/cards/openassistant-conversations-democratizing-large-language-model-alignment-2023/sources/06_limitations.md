Tree data has specific leakage hazards. If a train example uses one child and a test example uses its sibling, the model has seen most of the context and may have seen an almost identical answer. Multilingual paths can also have uneven quality, and volunteers can include unsafe, biased, or personal content. Filtering by ratings may over-select particular writing styles. Audit at the tree and language levels, not only at the flattened-row level.
