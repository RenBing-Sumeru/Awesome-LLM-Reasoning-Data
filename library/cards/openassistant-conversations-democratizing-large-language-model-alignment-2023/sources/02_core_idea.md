The 461,292 quality ratings create several possible supervision views. One may use high-rated assistant nodes for SFT, compare siblings for preference learning, train a critic on ratings, or sample full root-to-leaf trajectories for dialogue modeling. These choices are not equivalent. A pair made from siblings holds prompt and history fixed; a pair made from different trees may confound quality with topic, language, or context. The tree is the contract that lets a downstream user make this distinction.
