OpenAssistant Conversations is a release of interaction structure as well as text. The 161,443 messages are arranged in more than 10,000 conversation trees, allowing one user request to have multiple assistant continuations and later turns. A message-level row is therefore not self-contained: it inherits the path before it, may have siblings with comparative ratings, and can affect how a derived training example should be split. This is why the paper belongs in an alignment-data card rather than merely an instruction corpus list.
