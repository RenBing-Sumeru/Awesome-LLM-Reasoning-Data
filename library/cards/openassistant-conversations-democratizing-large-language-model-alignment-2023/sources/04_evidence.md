The paper reports that models trained on OpenAssistant Conversations consistently improve over corresponding base models on standard benchmarks. That result supports the usefulness of open human-created alignment data, but it is not a universal quality certificate for every node. The corpus contains a mixture of languages, tasks, contributors, and rating situations, and performance changes depend on the model, traversal policy, filtering, and benchmark. A meaningful report should say which derived subset was used rather than simply citing “OASST.”
