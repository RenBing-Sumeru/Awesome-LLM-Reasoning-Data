1. **Prepare instructions:** Collect mathematical and coding tasks with reference answers or tests.
2. **Generate responses:** Produce eight answers for each of 88,455 instructions, yielding about 708K records.
3. **Verify and label:** Check mathematics with answer rules and code with tests, then assign chosen or rejected labels.
4. **Release the data:** Store conversations, labels, sources, and generator models in Parquet.
5. **Validate through training:** Train an implicit PRM and use its token-level rewards in online RL.
