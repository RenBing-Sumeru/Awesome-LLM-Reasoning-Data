The core experiment compares PRIME with outcome-only RLOO using the same Eurus-2-7B-SFT model, rollouts, and verification. After 240 steps, the seven-benchmark average rises from 36.9 to 41.0, and PRIME needs about 40% of the steps to reach the same training reward. The fully trained model scores 43.9 versus 28.8 for SFT. This supports sample efficiency on verifiable tasks, not open-ended tasks. The conclusion covers only mathematical and coding tasks with deterministic verifiers.
