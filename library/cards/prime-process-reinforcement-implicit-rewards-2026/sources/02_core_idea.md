PRIME’s main contribution is to turn an implicit PRM—trained only with response-level correctness labels but capable of producing token-level rewards at inference—into an online reinforcement-learning component. The policy, reward model, and reference model can be initialized from the same SFT or base model, while the PRM is continuously updated on policy rollouts.

The authors also release EurusPRM-Stage1-Data, containing 88,455 instructions with eight generated responses each, or approximately 707,640 response-level records. Each record includes the complete instruction-response conversation, a binary chosen/rejected label, the source instruction dataset, and the generator model. It is distributed in Parquet format, covers mathematical and coding reasoning, and can train implicit PRMs or outcome reward models.
