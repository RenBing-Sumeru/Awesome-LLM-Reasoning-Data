For LLM SFT, the report groups data into ten topical domains and separates reasoning from non-reasoning tasks. Some reasoning queries receive multiple responses with distinct chains; the resulting 2.3M-sample corpus is trained for about two epochs. The reward stack uses rule verifiers, reference-guided LLM-as-a-judge, a code sandbox, and a reference-guided discriminative reward model for reasoning; open-ended tasks use checklist-aware verifiers, generative reward models, and discriminative reward models. PPO-based progressive RL proceeds from logic to math/code and then to a general mixture. UPO adds a DPO pairwise loss to PPO: online UPO rejection-samples pairs from multiple responses during each iteration, whereas offline UPO pre-generates them. Prompts with verifier accuracy 0 or 1 and groups with little reward variance are removed; rewards are separated by source and domain and normalized independently.

VLM post-training has three SFT stages. It synthesizes puzzles, geometry, and mathematical-function images, and recaptions natural STEM images; a caption is retained only when repeated text-only inference recovers the correct answer, while OCR-solvable samples are removed. Text-only reasoning provides a cold start after agent/rule filtering; rejection sampling then creates multimodal trajectories, and successful RL trajectories are recycled into SFT. Thinking and non-thinking data are mixed, with empty masked thinking tags for non-reasoning records, and experts are merged. RLVR covers open and proprietary K-12 visual STEM, more than 10K synthetic puzzles, and UI2Code/Image2Struct. Puzzle answers require two LLM judges to agree; UI2Code compares rendered HTML with a reference image. Hybrid VLM RL combines these verifiers with a Bradley-Terry multimodal reward model and GRPO with dynamic sampling and overlong filtering.
