Frontier model reports often name SFT or RL without exposing the data object or feedback contract. ERNIE 4.5 is useful because its report goes further: it separates language and vision-language post-training and specifies how reasoning traces, preference pairs, rule verifiers, learned judges, sandboxes, and multimodal environments interact. The audit question is not whether ERNIE 4.5 was post-trained, but which disclosed mechanisms are reproducible and which remain family-level descriptions without released records.
