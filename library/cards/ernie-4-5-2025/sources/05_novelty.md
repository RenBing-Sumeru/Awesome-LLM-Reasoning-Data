The distinctive contribution is the explicit matching between task epistemology and feedback: strict reasoning tasks favor rules, references, sandboxes, and discriminative scoring; open-ended tasks use checklist and learned reward models; multimodal tasks add answer verification, dual-judge puzzle checks, and rendered-UI comparison. UPO further combines scalar PPO rewards with pairwise DPO signals, while per-source/domain normalization addresses heterogeneous reward scales. This is more informative than merely naming an RL algorithm, although it remains a report-level recipe rather than a fully released training ledger.
