ERNIE 4.5 uses modality-specific post-training. The LLM pipeline trains on 2.3 million SFT samples, then applies PPO-based progressive RL and Unified Preference Optimization (UPO) against a heterogeneous reward system. The VLM pipeline constructs perception and reasoning traces through synthesis, recaptioning, rejection sampling, and thinking/non-thinking fusion, then applies verifiable-reward training on visual STEM, puzzles, and UI-to-code tasks. The central data contribution is therefore a recipe for routing different task types to different feedback contracts—not a released unified dataset.
