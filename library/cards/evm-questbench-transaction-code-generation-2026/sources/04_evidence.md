ACL 2026 evaluates 20 models over five independent rounds each and finds large gaps. Single-action precision on atomic tasks is substantially higher than completion of composite workflows. Many failures involve approvals, balance preconditions, transaction ordering, or unnecessary actions rather than syntax. Dynamic parameters and snapshot isolation make fixed-answer or single-state exploitation difficult. This supports execution-grounded evaluation, but testing uses controlled wallets and forked chains and does not establish mainnet safety.
