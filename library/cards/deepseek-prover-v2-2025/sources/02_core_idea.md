The central construction starts with DeepSeek-V3. It produces a natural-language proof sketch and a Lean skeleton whose have-style steps contain sorry placeholders. A 7B prover recursively resolves the resulting subgoals; when every subgoal is solved, their proofs are composed into a proof of the original theorem and appended to the DeepSeek-V3 reasoning trace as synthetic cold-start data.

The feedback contract is programmatic and terminal. GRPO samples candidate completed proofs and gives a binary reward of one to Lean-verified proofs and zero otherwise. Early RL adds a structural consistency reward that penalizes generated proofs which diverge from the proposed decomposition. This is not a released interactive verifier service, and its exact configuration is not disclosed.
