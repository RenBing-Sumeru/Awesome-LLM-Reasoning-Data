DeepSeek-V3 first analyzes a formal theorem in natural language, decomposes it into proof steps, and formalizes the steps as Lean statements. The 7B prover turns each extracted subgoal into a locally solvable lemma, optionally carrying earlier subgoals as premises. Two subgoal-theorem variants enter a curriculum, and a successful set of subgoal proofs is composed into a complete proof.

Cold-start examples are restricted to difficult problems that the 7B prover cannot solve end-to-end but whose decomposed subgoals it can solve. The complete Lean proof is paired with the DeepSeek-V3 chain-of-thought. In non-CoT expert iteration, a current best policy attempts unsolved hard problems; Lean-verified successes are added to SFT. The paper additionally states that autoformalized and unspecified open-source problems broaden the training distribution, and that miniF2F-valid is augmented with decomposed subgoal problems.

For the 671B model, SFT on DeepSeek-V3-Base-671B uses a 16,384-token context and constant 5e-6 learning rate. GRPO then samples 256 theorem prompts per iteration, 32 candidate proofs per theorem, and up to 32,768 tokens; its reward is binary Lean correctness plus an early decomposition-consistency signal. The 7B model extends DeepSeek-Prover-V1.5-Base to 32K, distills 671B RL rollouts with non-CoT proof data, and receives another RL stage. Prompt text, source manifests, rollout records, temperatures, full optimizer/KL settings, and yields are unavailable.
