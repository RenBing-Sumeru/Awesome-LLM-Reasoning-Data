On InternVL2.5-78B, the paper reports MMMU-PRO overall accuracy of 0.537 for Socratic-MCTS versus 0.517 for direct answering, and MMStar accuracy of 0.711 versus 0.692. On the filtered English multiple-choice MathVista subset, it reports 0.782 versus 0.740 for direct answering. The largest highlighted MMMU-PRO change is a nine-percentage-point gain in Liberal Arts; STEM+B changes from 0.507 direct to 0.492 under Socratic-MCTS. Least-To-Most underperforms direct and CoT in the reported tables.

The results show that this search configuration can improve aggregate answer selection on the studied frozen VLM and benchmarks, with uneven effects across domains. They do not prove that generated subquestions are faithful, node values are calibrated, or internal agreement is a reliable verifier. The paper reports single runs and no broad hyperparameter tuning. The official ACL paper, DOI, pages, and citation are verified; no official code, data, or search-log artifact was confirmed.
