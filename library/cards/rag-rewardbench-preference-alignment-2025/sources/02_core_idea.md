The central artifact is RAG-RewardBench, containing 1,485 prompt–context–chosen–rejected preference pairs. Each record organizes a task input, candidate response, reference evidence or evaluation condition, and a feedback label, together with fields needed to trace provenance and labeling. The authors use fixed retrieval evidence and hard cases for citation, multi-hop reasoning, and conflict handling for quality control, making the resource a feedback dataset for training, evaluation, or auditing rather than a generic question-answer collection. Its primary use is evaluating or training evidence-aware RAG judges.
