Results can drift with dependencies, hosted services, model APIs, hidden tests, and evaluator updates. Harness-Bench diagnoses measurement effects; it does not remove them.

The result should not be read beyond the disclosed source, split, scorer, judge, runtime, or artifact policy. Public releases can also become future training contamination.

If the benchmark signal is reused for training, filtering, or reward design, the feedback contract needs a separate audit from the headline score.
