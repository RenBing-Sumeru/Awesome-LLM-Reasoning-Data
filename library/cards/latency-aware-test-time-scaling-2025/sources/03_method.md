**Inputs → generation.** MATH-500, AIME24, AIME25, or GPQA-Diamond supplies the prompt and answer. Target models are s1.1-32B, LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B, QwQ-32B, and s1.1-3B. Their reported draft partners are respectively s1.1-7B, EAGLE3, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B (for both 32B reasoning models), and Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct. OpenR provides the experimental scaffold. Budget forcing controls sequential outputs at 1,024, 2,048, 4,096, or 8,192 tokens. (Paper §4.1)

**Concurrency search → candidates.** A configuration chooses output length, branch count `B`, and speculative draft length. Branch-wise scaling produces multiple response candidates in parallel; sequence-wise scaling asks the draft model to propose multiple tokens for target-model verification. Appendix A.2 reports aggregate acceptance rates of 0.831, 0.897, 0.781, 0.904, and 0.701 for the five target/draft pairings. Its sweeps include draft lengths `{2, 4, 5}` and branch counts `{1, 4, 16, 64}`; reported task-specific optima use `B` in `{8, 16, 32}` and draft length in `{3, 4, 5}`. (Appendix A.2, Figures 11–13)

**Selection → evaluation.** Candidate final answers are normally aggregated by majority voting. The v4 ablation combines minimum or average token confidence with either max selection or voting over identical answers. Accuracy and wall-clock latency are recorded; Tables 1 and 3 report means and standard deviations over three repetitions. Answer extraction, normalization, tie handling, per-candidate correctness, and confidence records are not released.

**Configuration optimizer.** Starting from a baseline, Algorithm 1 evaluates a branch-wise expansion and a sequence-wise expansion, retains the more useful feasible direction under the latency budget, and iterates until further expansion no longer improves the configuration. Branch counts grow in powers of two and draft length in unit steps. Table 2 compares the resulting search with a 56-step grid. This is evaluation and test-time compute only: no SFT/RL data are produced or used.

Reproduction requires pinning the accelerator and host, target/draft checkpoints, OpenR/runtime versions, precision and kernels, request/batch conditions, prompt ordering, decoding parameters, seeds, answer scorer, and timing protocol. Those details and the raw run manifest are not disclosed.
