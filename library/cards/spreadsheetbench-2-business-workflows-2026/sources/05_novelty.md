The prior-work baseline is SpreadsheetBench 2024 and other office-agent benchmarks that emphasize individual spreadsheet manipulation or general app control. SpreadsheetBench 2 changes the unit of evaluation from a short spreadsheet operation to a business workflow with many dependent cells, multi-sheet inspection, domain assumptions, and task-level consistency.

The direction signal is the move from checked ranges in manipulation tasks to end-to-end workbook deliverables. The quality signal is the expert-built gold workbooks, four business categories, independent expert validation, public code/data artifacts, and explicit scaffold. What is not new is spreadsheet editing, exact workbook comparison, or agent scaffolding itself. Before reuse, inspect the raw-source licensing boundary, dataset/evaluation-code license conflict, hidden or absent split policy, VLM checklist dependence, Windows-only visualization export path, model API versions, and whether public tasks can contaminate training.
