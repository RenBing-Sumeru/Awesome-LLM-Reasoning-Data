Do not read SpreadsheetBench 2 as simply "SpreadsheetBench but larger." The main change is the task unit: a business deliverable with many linked cells and workflow-level consistency. Modification and Accuracy answer different questions, and Visualization uses a different judge from workbook comparison.

Read the task-category definitions, evaluator, and scaffold before comparing model names. The strongest claim is not that one frontier model wins; it is that even strong agents often make partial workbook progress without completing the end-to-end business task.
