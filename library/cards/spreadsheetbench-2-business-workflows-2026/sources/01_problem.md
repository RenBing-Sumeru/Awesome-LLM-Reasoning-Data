SpreadsheetBench 2 asks whether spreadsheet agents can complete end-to-end business workflows, not isolated spreadsheet edits. The primary source is the arXiv paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29955, submitted on 2026-06-29; the official project page, GitHub repository, and Hugging Face dataset provide the public artifacts. No conference or journal acceptance is confirmed from the primary sources as of 2026-07-17.

The decision boundary is both an agent environment surface and a benchmark surface. Each instance is a business workflow instruction, an input workbook, a gold workbook, and an evaluation contract over modified cells or visualization checklists. It is not a general office benchmark, a spreadsheet QA dataset, or a disclosed training recipe. It belongs in the atlas because it extends spreadsheet reasoning from single-step workbook edits to multi-sheet, domain-heavy, file-state workflows with executable or rubric-grounded feedback.
