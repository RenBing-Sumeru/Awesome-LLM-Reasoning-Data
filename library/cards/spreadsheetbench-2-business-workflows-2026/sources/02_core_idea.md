The contribution is a 321-task benchmark for realistic business spreadsheet workflows. SpreadsheetBench 2 moves beyond forum-derived manipulation instructions by asking agents to construct, debug, template, or visualize business workbooks that may require many sheets, long instructions, and hundreds of cell edits.

The data object is a workflow task directory containing `dataset.json` records and spreadsheet files for four categories: Financial Modeling, Debugging, Template, and Visualization. The feedback contract is mixed. Financial Modeling, Template, and Debugging compare the produced workbook against the gold workbook with cell-level modification scores and task-level accuracy. Visualization uses expert-designed checklist items judged by a VLM, reported as pass rates for data correctness and format compliance. The closest comparisons are SpreadsheetBench 2024, Office/desktop agent benchmarks, and SWE-agent-style environment harnesses. The direction label is business spreadsheet workflow evaluation with file-state and checklist feedback.
