Correctness is relative to the gold workbook, selected target cells, preservation rules, and visualization checklist. Passing Modification does not necessarily mean a full business workbook is semantically sound, and failing one cell can make task-level Accuracy harsh even when much of the workflow was completed.

Several assumptions affect reuse. The paper says the dataset is CC BY-SA 4.0 and evaluation code is MIT, while Hugging Face metadata reports MIT, so the artifact-level license needs verification. The sources used to build tasks are public business and financial materials, but raw source documents are not fully redistributed. Visualization scoring depends on chart export through Excel/WPS COM and a GLM-4.6V judge. API model versions, scaffold implementation, turn budget, no-network Docker setup, spreadsheet engine, and absence of a confirmed train/dev/test split all limit comparability.
