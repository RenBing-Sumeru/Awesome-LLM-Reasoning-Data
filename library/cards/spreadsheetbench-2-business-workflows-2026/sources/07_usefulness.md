Use SpreadsheetBench 2 as a high-friction evaluation surface for spreadsheet agents that must inspect workbooks, reason over business structure, edit many cells, and preserve file consistency across a long episode. It is also useful as a schema reference for environment tasks: category, instruction, input workbook, gold workbook, modified-cell target set, preserved-cell checks, action/tool transcript, turn budget, and final score.

For atlas use, keep Modification separate from Accuracy, and keep workbook-comparison tasks separate from Visualization checklist tasks. This benchmark can inform verifier design, business workflow evaluation, and file-state agent audits. Turning it into a reward source or training set requires license resolution, contamination controls, and careful separation of public benchmark instances from hidden evaluation.
