The evidence is a public benchmark with difficult workflow-level tasks and low reported model accuracy. The paper reports 321 tasks averaging 11.8 worksheets, 429.0 instruction words, and 593.5 modified cells per task. Financial Modeling is especially large, averaging 15.3 sheets and 1,164.5 modified cells; Debugging averages 21.1 sheets.

In the main results, the highest reported model is Claude Opus 4.6 with 34.89% overall Accuracy and 77.20% overall Modification, while Debugging Accuracy remains 12.00%. Other reported overall Accuracy scores include GPT-5.2 at 26.79%, Gemini 3.1 Pro at 23.68%, GLM-5 at 17.14%, Deepseek-V3.2 at 15.58%, Kimi K2.5 at 14.64%, Qwen3.5-397B-A17B at 11.22%, and MiniMax M2.5 at 7.17%. A scaffold comparison using GLM-5 reports 46.32 Modification / 15.45 Accuracy for the authors' SWE-agent-based scaffold, versus 43.47 / 14.20 for Claude Code. Spreadsheet products are evaluated only on a 30-example subset and should not be compared as full-benchmark scores. The evidence boundary is the official harness, API model versions, no-network Docker setting, VLM checklist judge, and absence of reported confidence intervals.
