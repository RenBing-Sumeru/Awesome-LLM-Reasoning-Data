The contribution is a calibrated synthetic-reference interface for dataset inference. Each natural suffix and generated suffix share the same prefix, reducing position and local-context differences. A text-only classifier measures the remaining natural-versus-generated gap. A combined classifier sees the same text together with likelihood-derived membership-inference features from the target model. The feedback contract asks whether the combined classifier's paired separation is greater than the text-only separation; repeated one-sided tests are aggregated with Sidak correction, and p below 0.05 is treated as set-level membership evidence.

This interface observes aggregate separability in text space and target-model score space. It does not observe the target model's actual training manifest, cannot prove membership for an individual suffix, and cannot distinguish authorization or legal status. Failure to reject the null is inconclusive rather than evidence of absence.

The closest predecessor is *LLM Dataset Inference: Did you train on my dataset?*, which aggregates membership-inference signals but assumes a matched held-out set. The present paper replaces that unavailable object with suffix-completed synthetic references and adds post-hoc generation-gap calibration plus positive feature weights. Its direction is therefore provenance auditing through constructed evaluation data, not reasoning-trace training or process supervision.
