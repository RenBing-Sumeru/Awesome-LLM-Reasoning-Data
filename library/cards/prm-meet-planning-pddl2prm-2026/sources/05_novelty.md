Compared with one label for a whole chain of thought, this work changes the unit or acquisition path of process evidence. PDDL2PRM converts executable planning problems into about one million verifiable process-reward training examples. A planner supplies hard constraints on state transitions and action feasibility, so each partial plan can be judged automatically to yield low-ambiguity positive and negative steps and future-reachability information. Its reusable contribution is the data contract, quality control, and evaluation boundary.
