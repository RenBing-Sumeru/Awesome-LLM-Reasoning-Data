The release-scale evidence is independently inspectable at the artifact level. The current Hugging Face configuration has one train split with 1,348,799 examples and one 3.13 GB JSONL file. The schema exposes all four claimed final objects—problem, language solution, extracted answer, and program—as `instruction`, `output`, `answer`, and `code`. The file is pinned in this Card by its exact byte size and LFS SHA-256. The complete corpus was not downloaded and recomputed row by row, so content-frequency and duplicate claims remain author/artifact reports rather than an independent census.

Table 1 reports monotonic gains with more Caco data on three base models. For DeepSeekMath-7B, the six-benchmark average is 51.1 with Seed109K, 54.5 with 596K, and 57.1 with 1.3M; the 1.3M row reports MATH 68.2, GSM8K 85.1, CollegeMath 46.0, DeepMind-Mathematics 80.2, OlympiadBench-Math 29.5, and TheoremQA 33.8. Qwen2.5-Math-7B averages 65.1, 66.6, and 67.7 across the same sizes, while LLaMA3-8B averages 46.7, 52.8, and 57.3. This is strong within-paper utility evidence for SFT scaling, but it is not an independent audit of row correctness or contamination.

The verification ablation draws random 100K subsets with and without the final verification process and has Qwen3-32B evaluate them. Reported solvable counts rise from 91K to 97K and correct counts from 88K to 93K; an LLaMA3-8B model trained on the verified subset averages 21.8 versus 20.8, including a 1.1-point OlympiadBench gain. Because Qwen3-32B is also the construction judge, this is a same-judge-family audit rather than an independent human or formal calibration. The paper gives no confidence intervals or judge confusion matrix.

Appendix controls partially separate data scale from a larger teacher. With 300K records, the Qwen2.5-Math-7B teacher-distillation baseline averages 65.5, Caco-300K 66.2, and Caco-1.3M 67.7; for DeepSeekMath-7B the reported averages are 51.1, 53.2, and 57.1. STaR-style self-improvement baselines are also lower. These comparisons support the claim that the construction recipe contributes beyond merely copying one teacher in the tested setup, but they do not establish a causal contribution for each filtering component.

The paper samples 5K records, embeds them with all-MiniLM-L6-v2, visualizes them with t-SNE, and applies K-means with 12 clusters as diversity evidence. This can show broad topical groupings but is not a duplicate or evaluation-contamination test. A science extension grows 5.2K MegaScience seeds to 37K valid records and reports a LLaMA average over AGIEval, ARC-c, and MMLU-STEM increasing from 59.0 to 63.4. Those 37K records are an auxiliary experiment, not verified as part of the public Caco-1.3M file.

All performance results are author-reported, use the paper's training/evaluation setup, and lack uncertainty estimates or independent reproduction. They establish that the released data is associated with improved benchmark scores under SFT. They do not prove semantic correctness, verifier reliability, provenance completeness, license compatibility, or absence of benchmark leakage.
