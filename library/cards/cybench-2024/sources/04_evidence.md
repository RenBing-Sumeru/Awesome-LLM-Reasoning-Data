The paper reports forty professional-level CTF tasks from four competitions and evaluates eight models, with additional scaffold comparisons for top models. It reports that agents using Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o, OpenAI o1-preview, and Claude 3 Opus solve some complete tasks without subtask guidance, while the hardest human-calibrated task took teams 24 hours and 54 minutes. The decisive row-level evidence is a valid flag or subtask check produced inside the challenge environment. The evidence boundary is narrow: success depends on scaffold, command environment, web-search policy, budget, and any answer-leak fixes; partial subtask progress must not be reported as full task success.
