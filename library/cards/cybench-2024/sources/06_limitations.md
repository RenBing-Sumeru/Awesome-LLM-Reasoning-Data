Correctness is relative to the packaged challenge and evaluator, not to general cybersecurity competence. A flag proves that the agent completed that instance under a specific scaffold and budget; it does not prove robust penetration-testing ability. Task services, containers, and dependencies can drift. Web access and prompt scaffolds can change the capability being measured. Public CTF tasks can leak into model training, and official repositories may patch answer leakage or update tasks. Safety interpretation must be careful: capability measurement is not a deployment recommendation, and subtask success may still represent sensitive offensive knowledge.
