OfficeBench: Benchmarking Language Agents across Multiple Applications for Office Automation defines an evaluation surface in office-agents, desktop-agents, multi-application-workflows: whether model outputs or agent trajectories satisfy the task-specific feedback contract. The primary source and artifacts are paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.19056; project: https://github.com/zlwang-cs/OfficeBench; code: https://github.com/zlwang-cs/OfficeBench; data: https://github.com/zlwang-cs/OfficeBench; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.19056; the local metadata records the source as 2024 / arXiv preprint.

The decision boundary is environment_agent_trajectory_data with roles benchmark, agent_environment and verification contract environmental, programmatic. One benchmark instance is: office tasks, app states, document/spreadsheet/email artifacts, actions, and evaluator outputs. This is not a model-training recipe unless the paper separately releases training data; the card treats it as evaluation, audit. It matters for the atlas because the reusable object is an evaluation/data record with a named scorer, judge, verifier, reward, or environment predicate rather than an abstract capability claim.
