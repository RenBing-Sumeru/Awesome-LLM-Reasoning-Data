WebChain's data unit is a task-conditioned human web trajectory rather than an isolated screenshot-action pair. An unnamed LLM first synthesizes tasks from a functional schema extracted from a target site. Human annotators then execute those tasks on live websites through WebChain Builder. Each step aligns visual state, structural state, and the grounded action; post-processing adds annotations for other interactive elements in the viewport and may add an unnamed-VLM-generated action rationale.

The release reports 31,725 human-verified trajectories, 317,993 steps, and 428 domains. These are different denominators. A trajectory averages 10.02 steps and 1.07 minutes. The Hugging Face artifact exposes raw archives plus two processed views: high-level samples for planning from the instruction, history, and current screenshot, and low-level samples for grounding from the current-step instruction and observation. Full-page screenshot coverage is explicitly partial.

The associated Dual Mid-Training recipe separates spatial grounding from long-horizon planning. The paper studies Spatial-Grounding-oriented RLVR with Visual Grounding Densification, CoT-SFT using 5,000 synthetic-rationale samples, and later Long-Chain-oriented RLVR. The accepted taxonomy for this Card remains `sft` and `agent_training`; the RL stages are reported as evidence about the recipe, not used to broaden that canonical field.

The supervision should not be read as a unique gold plan. A human trajectory is one successful-looking route through a non-stationary website, while many alternative element targets, orders, shortcuts, and recovery actions may be valid.
