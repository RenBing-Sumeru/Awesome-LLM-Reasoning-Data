The official paper and project describe 116 Android tasks across 20 apps, with task pages published by the project site and code in the Google Research repository. The reported baseline evidence includes the multimodal M3A agent, with the paper showing that even strong contemporary agents complete only a minority of tasks; the arXiv abstract reports 30.6% success for the strongest model in that setup. Row-level evidence is the task validator's final-state check, not the aggregate success rate. The boundary is the released task list, installed app versions, emulator state, prompt budget, and evaluator implementation.
