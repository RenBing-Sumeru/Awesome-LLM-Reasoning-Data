TritonBench evaluates multiple state-of-the-art code LLMs on both real-world and interface-aligned channels with uniform compilation, numerical testing, and GPU profiling. The paper finds that current models already struggle to produce correct operators and that functionally valid outputs often fail to deliver stable speedups, showing that performance is not reducible to correctness. The 184 real operators add source diversity, while the PyTorch-aligned channel supports controlled comparisons. This demonstrates the benchmark’s ability to expose high-performance-code gaps, but model rankings and speed conclusions are tied to the evaluated GPUs and require remeasurement on other architectures.
