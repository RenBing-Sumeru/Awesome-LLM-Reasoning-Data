Each round creates or receives reasoning problems, generates long CoTs, segments them, obtains stepwise LLM-as-a-Judge decisions, and constructs chosen/rejected supervision. Reproducibility requires recording problem source, seed model, prompts, delimiters, sampling, judge schema, pair-selection rule, and iteration count. Inputs are problems, traces, and intermediate states; outputs are step labels, preferences, or continuous rewards. Reproduction should verify the generator, terminal verifier, sampling budget, and filtering rules.
