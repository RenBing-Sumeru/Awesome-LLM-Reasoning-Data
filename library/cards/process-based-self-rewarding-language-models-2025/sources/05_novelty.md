Compared with one label for a whole chain of thought, this work changes the unit or acquisition path of process evidence. The model plays two roles in one iterative system: it writes long reasoning traces as a policy and judges individual transitions step by step. Stepwise preference optimisation then trains the next model to retain correct prefixes and prefer corrected continuations; policy and judge share operational definitions of a step and local validity. Its reusable contribution is the data contract, quality control, and evaluation boundary.
