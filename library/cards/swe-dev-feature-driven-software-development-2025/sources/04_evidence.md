The benchmark evaluates 17 chat models, 10 reasoning models, and 10 multi-agent systems. Even Claude-3.7-Sonnet reaches only 22.45% Pass@3 on the hard split, showing that the task is not ordinary completion. Fine-tuning a 7B model on the 14K training set raises its hard-split result from about 6.68% to 18.89%, approaching GPT-4o, and RL experiments show that executable tests provide useful rewards. This supports both difficulty and training value. However, PRDs are reconstructed from known implementations and all tests come from the PyPI ecosystem, making tasks more constrained than true design from requirements; model comparisons also depend on scaffolds and sampling counts.
