TMAS combines parallel exploration with sequential exploitation through five roles: solution, verification, summary, experience, and guideline agents. Every solution trajectory receives multiple verification passes and a compact summary. An experience bank stores reliable low-level conclusions, reusable skills, and identified mistakes; a guideline bank stores higher-level solution directions and already explored strategies.

The two memories have different jobs. Later agents use the experience bank to refine promising work, while the guideline bank redirects exploratory rollouts away from redundant patterns. Hybrid-RL then trains behavior to preserve basic correctness, use the accumulated experience, and explore beyond prior strategies. Thus the scaled record is not just a set of candidates but an evolving, selectively retained multi-agent history.
