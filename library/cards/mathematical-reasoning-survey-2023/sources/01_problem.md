Mathematical reasoning requires systems to solve problems and prove theorems in language, making it a demanding testbed for deep learning. The area includes different mathematical objects, answer forms, datasets, and notions of correctness, so a result on one benchmark does not automatically answer a question about another. This ACL 2023 survey reviews a decade of work at the intersection of mathematical reasoning and deep learning, including new opportunities from large-scale neural language models. It gives readers a way to locate a claimed advance within a concrete problem and evaluation setting. Before accepting a broad claim, readers should identify whether the underlying task asks for a numerical answer, a written derivation, or a formal or informal proof. The paper is a survey, not a new dataset, theorem prover, or problem-solving system.
