Its method is a literature review over the past decade, not a pipeline for training one new model. The survey brings together mathematical tasks, datasets, learning methods, and benchmarks, then assesses existing benchmark and method choices. The main objects are a mathematical problem or theorem, a solution or proof in language, the data supporting a method, and the criterion used to judge success. Keep those objects linked when reading a cited result: a system may be evaluated on producing an answer, a derivation, or a proof, and those outputs need not be interchangeable. The survey supplies the map; the cited studies provide the exact data construction, model configuration, and evaluation implementation.
