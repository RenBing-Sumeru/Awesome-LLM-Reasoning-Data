For STEM, the report mixes open-source datasets, public competitions, and proprietary collections, then cleans prompts and synthesizes long-CoT traces through model generation, human annotation, cold starts, and Seed-Verifier rejection sampling. It reports 100k cleaned STEM tasks, but does not publish source identities, proportions, item records, or licenses. The exact base checkpoint is undisclosed; the final model is described as a 20B-active/200B-total MoE.

For code, the pipeline uses competitive-programming tasks with unit tests or checker scripts, while logic uses generated puzzles with task-specific generators and verifiers. Non-verifiable prompts derive from Doubao-1.5 Pro RL data and are filtered for diversity and difficulty before pairwise generative-RM feedback. Details such as exact model versions, prompts, thresholds, calibration, rollout counts, decoding settings, and retained-versus-rejected yields are undisclosed.

The supervised stage contains 400k long-CoT examples: 300k verifiable and 100k non-verifiable. The paper reports two SFT epochs, a 32k-token SFT truncation, and subsequent unified long-CoT RL combining verifier, RM, and hybrid feedback. It does not provide a public SFT/RL data split, a reusable rollout corpus, or a full optimizer and inference-budget configuration.
