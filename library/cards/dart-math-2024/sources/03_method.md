Inputs are the original MATH training queries and the authors' corrected GSM8K training queries. DeepSeekMath-7B-RL generates zero-shot natural-language CoT responses through vLLM with a 2,048-token output cap, top-p 0.95, and temperature 1.6. Appendix B.2 reports choosing 1.6 after a 0.3–1.8 sweep at 0.1 intervals: higher temperatures reached coverage faster, while settings at least 1.7 produced nonsensical output. The generation model is also the difficulty-estimation model.

Each raw response goes through final-answer extraction based on regular expressions and then SymPy-based comparison with the reference. The implementation covers numbers, symbols, matrices/vectors, intervals, Boolean expressions, dates, and times. Passing supplies an answer-level Boolean acceptance label; no intermediate step receives a label. The paper explicitly warns that final-answer correctness need not imply intermediate-reasoning correctness (Paper §2.1 note 2; Appendix B.2).

Allocation has three alternatives. VRT samples 52 raw trials per query in the repository's reproduction command and retains correct ones. Uniform samples toward 40 correct responses per query in the final release. For the larger pool used to estimate pass rates, Appendix B.2 instead reports collecting toward 192 correct responses per query and reusing those candidates. Prop2Diff computes `1-pass_rate`, turns it into a target up to 192 accepted responses for the hardest prompts, rounds upward to preserve at least one synthetic response where possible, and keeps sampling until the target or a practical cap is reached. The paper reports about 150 million raw samples, over 95% target attainment, and about 160 A100 GPU-days; the repository acknowledges that exact Prop2Diff reproduction still requires manual steps.

Accepted synthetic responses are combined with the original MATH/GSM8K training records. DART-Math-Hard and DART-Math-Uniform expose train-only `query`/`response` tables; at the checked revisions they contain 585,392 and 590,705 rows. Supplementary pools expose accepted-response provenance and correctness fields, and query-info releases expose per-job/total sample counts, correct counts, pass rate, and Bernoulli standard deviation. Complete rejected-response text is not established as released.

Training uses the Alpaca instruction template and Transformers SFT with sequence packing to 4,096 tokens, batch size 64 computation sequences, Adam with zero weight decay, 3% warmup, and cosine decay. Reported maximum learning rates are 1e-5 for Mistral-7B, 5e-5 for DeepSeekMath-7B and Llama3-8B, and 2e-5 for Llama3-70B; Llama3 runs use one epoch and the default elsewhere is three. Reproduction should pin the GitHub commit, every HF revision, corrected GSM8K revision, model checkpoint, random seeds, parser/SymPy versions, and exact Prop2Diff caps and curation manifest.
