For the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track, the paper supplies a concrete audit schema for selection trajectories: prompt and reference-policy identity; every candidate response; its policy likelihood and proxy-reward score; candidate budget (N); regularization β; estimated normalizer; per-candidate rejection probability and acceptance event; fallback path; selected response; and a true-evaluation outcome kept separate from the selection signal. Such records could support selector evaluation, reward-hacking audits, coverage studies, and comparisons of test-time compute policies. They could also help distinguish failures of generation from failures of reward modeling or selection. The evidence supports evaluation and inference-time construction use only: it does not establish SFT/RL training use, permission to redistribute benchmark-derived prompts or model outputs, or availability of the paper's experimental traces.
