Using five judges—GPT-3.5-turbo, GPT-4-turbo, GPT-4o, Llama-3-8B/70B-Instruct—EMBER measures change from neutral-marker accuracy. On EMBERQA (2,000) and EMBERIF (823), every judge is affected and weakeners cause the largest loss. Adding a weakener to a stronger model's answer reverses over 30 percentage points of preference toward a weaker answer, while human judges follow correctness. This establishes a failure in these two settings, not all styles or domains.
