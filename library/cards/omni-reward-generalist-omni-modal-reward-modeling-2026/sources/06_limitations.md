Free-form criteria can be ambiguous, underspecified, or adversarially phrased, so agreement on one criterion need not transfer to another. Modality coverage can conceal uneven data quality across text, image, video, audio, and 3D; audit per-modality counts and errors. Finally, benchmark contamination is a direct risk when the same task family supports training and Omni-RewardBench evaluation; inspect prompt and asset overlap.
