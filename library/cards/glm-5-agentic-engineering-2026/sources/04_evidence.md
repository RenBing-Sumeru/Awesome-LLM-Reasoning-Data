The base-model budget is reported as 28.5T tokens for a 744B-total/40B-active MoE. The disclosed schedule is an initial 27T-token corpus plus 32K-context mid-training for 1T tokens, 128K for 500B, and 200K for 50B. These rounded stage figures describe training scale; they do not provide record manifests or exact source proportions.

The software-engineering mid-training pool contains approximately 10 million issue–PR pairs and about 160B unique tokens after filtering. For agentic training, the report claims more than 10K RepoLaunch-built SWE environments across thousands of repositories and nine languages, thousands of terminal environments, and a Web Knowledge Graph built from more than two million deduplicated pages. The orchestrator's support for over 1,000 concurrent rollouts is system capacity, not an observed training rollout count.

Reported evaluation scores are conditional on their harnesses. Table 7 reports 77.8 on SWE-bench Verified and 73.3 on SWE-bench Multilingual using the paper's OpenHands setup. Verified Terminal-Bench 2 results are 56.2 and 60.7 under the stated Terminus-2 configurations. BrowseComp results depend on the search tool harness and context-management policy; the paper reports an increase from 55.3% to 62.0% with keep-recent context folding. MCP-Atlas uses its 500-task public subset, a 10-minute timeout, and Gemini 3 Pro judging. These values are report-supplied evaluations, not independent reproductions.

Inference limits are also benchmark-specific: most reasoning evaluations permit up to 131,072 generated tokens, while HLE-with-tools uses a 202,752-token limit. Temperatures and agent harnesses vary across SWE-bench, Terminal-Bench, HLE, and other evaluations. None of these settings establish the temperature, rollout count, or token budget used during RL training.

The release includes model weights, the open `slime` framework, deployment recipes, and a corrected Terminal-Bench evaluation dataset. It does not include the GLM-5 pretraining/SFT/RL records, production task services, complete rewards or judges, TITO rollout logs, loss masks, or immutable source-to-checkpoint lineage.
