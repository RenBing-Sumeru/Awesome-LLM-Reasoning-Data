The core idea is a staged transformation from broad corpora to verifiable long-horizon behavior. GLM-5 first trains a 744B-total, 40B-active MoE base model on a reported 28.5T tokens. The report describes an initial 27T-token corpus, followed by context-expanding mid-training stages at 32K context for 1T tokens, 128K for 500B tokens, and 200K for 50B tokens. The figures are reported at rounded scale and do not constitute a released corpus inventory.

Post-training then combines three SFT categories—General Chat, Reasoning, and Coding & Agent—with four-domain Reasoning RL, Agentic RL, General RL, and on-policy cross-stage distillation. Agentic RL connects real or synthesized executable tasks to policy trajectories through task-specific environment and reward services.

The infrastructure contribution is as important as the model recipe. A Multi-Task Rollout Orchestrator connects independent task microservices to `slime`; TITO preserves exact token identities across heterogeneous rollout engines; direct double-sided importance sampling masks excessive policy mismatch; stale and environment-collapse samples are filtered. This makes an asynchronous system trainable, but the production configurations and records are not released.
