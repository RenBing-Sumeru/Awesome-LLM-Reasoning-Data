For Track 12, Kimi K2 offers a useful disclosure template for agentic frontier reports. A curator can distinguish real versus synthetic tool specifications, simulator versus real-execution feedback, programmatic versus judgment-based verification, and RLVR versus self-critic preference signals while retaining unknowns about the actual corpus and calibration.

It is especially useful for comparing agent-training claims across tool use and SWE: check source lists and rights, task and trajectory schemas, environment availability, rubric/judge behavior, rejection yields, real-to-simulated transfer, critic updates, and split or contamination controls. Public weights and a model repository are valuable release artifacts, but they do not replace these data and audit objects.

This Card is deliberately scoped to disclosure and audit risk. It does not classify Kimi K2's training-scale, optimizer, token-budget, or benchmark-performance statements as a Track 8 scaling contribution.
