The report does not release the real MCP source list, synthetic tools, tool-set samples, agents, tasks, rubrics, personas, trajectories, simulator states, real sandbox images, executable tests, RL prompts and rollouts, preference records, or rejected candidates. It also omits source versions, per-stage mixture weights and counts, sampling settings, prompts, generator checkpoints, human-review details, retained yields, train/validation splits, and contamination results.

Verification quality cannot be independently established. LLM judges, hack checks, faithfulness judges, safety judges, and the self-critic are described at a high level, but their prompts, model versions, calibration, error rates, aggregation rules, and reward-hacking evaluations are unavailable. The report itself acknowledges simulation-fidelity limits and reports that unclear tools or hard reasoning can lead to excessive tokens, truncated outputs, or incomplete tool calls; unnecessary tool use can also degrade performance.

Modified MIT applies to official code and weights, not necessarily to the GitHub-derived, pre-training-derived, open, internal, or synthetic data materials described in the report. These evidence gaps make the Card partial despite the detailed pipeline narrative.
