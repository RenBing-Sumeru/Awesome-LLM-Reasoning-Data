
The paper reports that tool-augmented agents issue 9.2 times as many LLM calls as CoT on average and that LATS reaches 71.0 calls per request; LLM inference and tools account for 69.4% and 30.2% of latency on average, while CPU or external tools can leave the GPU idle for up to 54.5% of runtime. Prefix caching reduces prefill latency by 60.1% and agent end-to-end latency by 15.7% on average in the reported settings. Under LATS parallel scaling on HotpotQA, increasing child nodes from 1 to 16 raises accuracy by 14.4 percentage points while reducing latency by 196.3 seconds, but increases concurrent resource pressure. Table III reports measured aggregate per-query results for selected HotpotQA configurations: Reflexion uses 41.53/348.41 Wh and LATS 22.76/158.48 Wh with 8B/70B, versus ShareGPT at 0.32/2.55 Wh. Table III is different evidence: its MW/GW values are projections obtained by multiplying per-query GPU energy by assumed traffic, not direct datacenter measurements. None of these results establishes that raw measurement data were released or that the recorded traces are intrinsically high quality.
