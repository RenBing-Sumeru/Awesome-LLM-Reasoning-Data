The key audit question is whether the observed gain comes from shorter retained context, from positional re-encoding, or from their combination. Read the cache update procedure closely, including what is copied, which keys retain position-free versions, and how prompt information remains accessible. A rigorous reproduction should vary the retention rule while holding the cycle-generation prompt and model fixed, then report the entire accuracy-versus-induced-thought curve rather than only its peak. It should also test whether a model’s shortest cycles are genuinely less error-prone or simply more likely to terminate early.
