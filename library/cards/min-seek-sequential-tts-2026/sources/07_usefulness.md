Use Min-Seek as a systems-oriented baseline when a reasoning application already induces sequential revisions and suffers from overthinking or KV-cache pressure. Log each cycle’s length, retained state, answer change, correctness, cache size, token count, and latency; then compare full-history prompting, fixed-window retention, and shortest-cycle retention under identical generation budgets. The paper is especially helpful for distinguishing two different interventions that are often conflated: asking for more reasoning and deciding which past reasoning is allowed to influence the next step.
