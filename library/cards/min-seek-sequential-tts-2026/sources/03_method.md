The method repeatedly induces reconstruction cycles after an initial reasoning thought. At each stage, it compares the current cycle with retained cycles by length and keeps only the shortest one in the cache, while preserving the prompt and initial thought. The design deliberately uses length as a rule for excluding potentially problematic history, not as a claim that the shortest text is always the complete or logically best solution.

For cache management, keys are retained without positional embeddings and are dynamically encoded contiguously when the next cycle is generated; values are retained as usual. Because only a fixed small subset of cycles remains in the cache, the method can continue producing thoughts beyond the ordinary maximum context length under the paper’s stated conditions. The paper evaluates this training-free procedure on reasoning tasks with DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen 1.5B and 7B models.
