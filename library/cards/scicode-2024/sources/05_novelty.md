The prior-work baseline is code-generation and executable-program benchmarks with unit-test or judge feedback. The change introduced by SciCode: A Research Coding Benchmark Curated by Scientists is the particular task object and feedback contract: scientific problem, required function/code, generated solution, tests, and pass/fail score. scored by unit tests or official scientific-code judge. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13168), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
