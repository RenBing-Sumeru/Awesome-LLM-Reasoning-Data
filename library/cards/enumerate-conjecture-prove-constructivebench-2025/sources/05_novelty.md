Existing Lean theorem provers usually receive a fixed theorem statement, so their search space contains only proof steps. Program-synthesis methods search over candidates, but correctness is often judged through tests or finite inputs. ECP makes the answer itself a search variable and connects candidate search to theorem proving through a conjecture interface. A further novelty is admissibility checking: even a valid Lean proof is insufficient if the construction exploits a loophole in the specification. The paper therefore changes the task definition and verification contract rather than merely replacing the prover with a larger model or increasing proof samples.
