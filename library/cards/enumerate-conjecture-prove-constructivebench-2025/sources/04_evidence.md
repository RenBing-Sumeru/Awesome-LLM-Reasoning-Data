Under aligned inference budgets, ECP solves 17 of 346 answer-construction tasks in PutnamBench and 18 of 75 tasks in MathArena. Every successful result contains both an admissible candidate and a proof accepted by the Lean kernel, rather than being determined by string-level answer matching. The authors also analyze failures in enumeration, conjecturing, and proving, showing that strengthening the prover alone cannot compensate for inadequate candidate search. The results establish that ECP can solve a subset of difficult construction problems end to end, but the low overall success rate does not imply general competition-level construction ability.
