1. **One-sentence position:** ECP separates answer-construction problems into candidate search and Lean proving, filling the gap left by fixed-theorem benchmarks that do not evaluate answer discovery.
2. **Method takeaway:** Candidate enumeration, conjecture generation, kernel proving, and admissibility auditing are all necessary.
3. **Data takeaway:** ConstructiveBench contains roughly 3,640 formalized construction tasks with problems, candidates, specifications, proofs, and statuses.
4. **Evidence anchor:** ECP solves 17/346 PutnamBench and 18/75 MathArena tasks, with every success confirmed by dual verification.
5. **Reuse decision:** It suits bounded construction spaces; candidate recall and specification loopholes are the primary risks requiring human audits.
