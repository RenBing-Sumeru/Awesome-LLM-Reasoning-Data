The central contribution is to separate “finding an answer” from “proving the answer” into two search spaces that can be alternately verified. ConstructiveBench contains approximately 3,640 answer-construction tasks with Lean formalizations, constraints, and verification interfaces. ECP enumerates bounded candidates, proposes conjectures from intermediate results, and asks a Lean prover to complete the proof. A task is solved only when the candidate is admissible and its proof passes kernel checking. The resulting object is more than a theorem–proof pair: it contains the problem, candidate construction, formal specification, proof, and admissibility status, supporting both evaluation and verified trajectory generation.
