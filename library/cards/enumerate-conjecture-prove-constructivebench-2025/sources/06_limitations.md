1. **Restricted search space:** Enumeration must reduce an infinite or combinatorial construction space to a bounded candidate set. If the LLM never proposes the key structure, a stronger Lean prover cannot recover it. Reuse should report candidate recall.

2. **Specification loopholes:** Admissibility rules cover known shortcuts but may still allow formally valid answers that violate the natural-language intent. Successful samples and specifications require human review.

3. **Domain coverage:** Current results focus on formalizable competition problems with finitely checkable constructions, and overall solve rates remain low. They do not directly generalize to continuous optimization, open-ended proofs, or tasks without explicit construction objects.
