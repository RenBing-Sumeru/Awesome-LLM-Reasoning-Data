Answer-construction problems in mathematics competitions require a concrete object satisfying constraints, such as a permutation, coloring, or set, rather than a proof of a pre-specified theorem. Most formal-reasoning benchmarks provide a fixed theorem and ask a verifier only to check its proof. Construction tasks additionally require the model to discover an answer, prove that it satisfies the conditions, and avoid shortcuts such as circular definitions, trivial constants, or access to canonical answers.

The paper introduces Enumerate–Conjecture–Prove (ECP), connecting candidate enumeration, answer conjecturing, and Lean proof generation, together with ConstructiveBench. A general language model searches for candidates, a prover model supplies machine-checkable evidence, and admissibility rules reject constructions that are formally accepted but violate the intended task.
