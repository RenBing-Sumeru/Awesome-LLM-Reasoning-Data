The prior-work baseline is document, chart, multimodal QA, and visual evaluation benchmarks. The change introduced by OmniDocBench: Benchmarking Diverse PDF Document Parsing with Comprehensive Annotations is the particular task object and feedback contract: official records expose PDF parsing, layout, OCR, table, and formula recognition, prompt/task metadata, a model output surface, and a score or success target. scored by the official evaluator, answer key, judge, hidden test, metric, or environment predicate determines success. That combination is the direction signal, not a generic claim that models are better or worse.

The quality signal is the presence of official artifacts (paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07626; project: https://opendatalab.com/omnidocbench; code: https://github.com/opendatalab/OmniDocBench; data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/opendatalab/OmniDocBench; doi: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.07626), source metadata, and explicit audit fields for split, license, lineage, and failure modes. What is not new is the general idea of benchmarking or judging model outputs. Before reuse, inspect artifact version, license, split, evaluator/judge implementation, data lineage, prompt/scaffold settings, and contamination risk.
