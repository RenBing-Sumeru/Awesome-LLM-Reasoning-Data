For the data-construction/open-release track, SCOP is a worked design for a current-policy, feedback-bearing buffer. Builders can use it to separate five modules: teacher-initialized Pair-SFT, model-relative prompt gating, current-policy episode collection, segment-specific reward calculation, and PPO refresh. A controlled study should hold prompts, rollouts, and optimizer budget fixed while changing only buffer age or critique reward.

An auditable implementation should serialize source dataset/revision and prompt ID; teacher and policy/value checkpoints; buffer round; reasoning and raw extracted answer; all four critiques; the hallucination decision and rule branch; all eight corrections per critique; normalized answers; verifier outputs; `m/n`, `h(c)`, reasoning/correction rewards; sampling settings and seeds; PPO step; and acceptance/rejection reason. Failed and filtered prompts are necessary for measuring distribution shift rather than merely describing it.

The recipe can also seed verifier studies. Compare correction-success reward with step-error localization, human critique judgments, or calibrated learned critics; report how often a valid critique is unrepairable and how often an invalid critique precedes an easy repair. Audit correct-to-wrong transitions, prompt-band coverage, reward variance, extraction failures, policy collapse, and sensitivity to 4-by-8 sampling.
