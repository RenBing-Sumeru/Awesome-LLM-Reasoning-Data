Table 1 reports greedy Accuracy and BoN@16 on GSM8K, SVAMP, MATH-OAI, and OlympiadBench. With DeepSeek-Math-7B-Base, SCOP averages 68.78/83.97 versus 63.38/78.91 for Critic-CoT (Iter@3), a 5.40-point greedy difference. With Qwen2.5-7B-Base, SCOP averages 78.41/87.16 versus 74.75/84.35, a table-derived 3.66-point greedy difference; the main-results prose rounds this to 3.65. Critique self-consistency at inference raises SCOP to 69.34/84.19 and 79.06/87.58, respectively. These are author-reported downstream scores, not trajectory-quality labels.

Table 2 isolates correction behavior under greedy decoding. DeepSeek SCOP moves from `Acc@R=53.2` to `Acc@S=57.4`, with 7.2% wrong-to-correct and 3.0% correct-to-wrong transitions. Qwen moves from 71.3 to 76.6, with 11.7% wrong-to-correct and 6.4% correct-to-wrong. Self-critique therefore repairs some failures while still damaging some initially correct answers.

The on/off-policy comparison in Figure 4 shows off-policy evaluation accuracy peaking and then declining, while on-policy accuracy continues toward convergence under the paper's simulation. Table 7 reports that removing reward shaping lowers average Accuracy from 68.78 to 64.22 for DeepSeek and from 78.41 to 73.23 for Qwen; replacing PPO with rejection fine-tuning lowers it to 63.48 and 72.45. This supports the combined training recipe but does not isolate whether critique-reward accuracy, buffer freshness, or another PPO interaction causes the gain.

Sampling cost is material. Table 6 reports 1,924,239 generated tokens per optimization step for SCOP with four critiques and eight corrections; reducing sampling to 1-by-1 lowers Qwen GSM8K/MATH-OAI from 93.1/60.4 to 87.8/50.3. Appendix Figure 6 finds the largest self-critique gains at middle MATH-OAI difficulty levels rather than the easiest or hardest. Together these results bound the claim: the tested recipe benefits current 7B policies under high sampling compute, but benchmark gains do not prove that critiques are faithful, rewards unbiased, data uncontaminated, or artifacts reproducible.
