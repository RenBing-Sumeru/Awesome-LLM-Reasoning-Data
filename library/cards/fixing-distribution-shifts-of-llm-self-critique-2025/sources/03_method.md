The reconstructable pipeline is:

1. **Reference and policy initialization.** Use GSM8K and MATH training questions. Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct rewrites reference reasoning. Fine-tune DeepSeek-Math-7B-Base or Qwen2.5-7B-Base to produce reasoning, sample multiple paths, and label each path correct or incorrect from its final answer.
2. **Teacher critique synthesis.** Give the annotator the question, policy reasoning, and reference solution. Generate critique/correction targets. Re-enter the incorrect reasoning plus critique and sample eight corrections; retain the item only when at least six corrections are correct. The paper reports 60K examples divided into correct-path `D+` and incorrect-path `D-` for Pair-SFT.
3. **Pair-SFT.** On `D+`, train the full reasoning-critique-correction sequence; on `D-`, train critique and correction conditioned on the supplied incorrect reasoning. Use mixture weight 1, batch 256, AdamW, cosine scheduling, warmup 0.03, five epochs, and learning rates `5e-5`/`1e-5` for DeepSeek/Qwen.
4. **RL prompt selection.** Start from cleaned Numina-CoT after removing proof questions without standard numerical answers. For each prompt, sample eight answers from the initial policy and eight from the annotator. Remove prompts solved by the policy 8/8 times and prompts solved by the annotator 0/8 times; the surviving count is not reported.
5. **On-policy episode collection.** The current policy generates reasoning, four critiques, and eight corrections per critique: 32 correction rollouts per reasoning episode. Sampling uses temperature 0.6, top-p 0.9, and at most 2,048 output tokens. Assign reasoning correctness, Monte Carlo critique reward plus hallucination adjustment, and correction correctness plus bonus.
6. **PPO and refresh.** Optimize the summed stage rewards with PPO and KL penalty 0.05 against the frozen Pair-SFT policy, then sample the next buffer from the updated policy. PPO uses batch 1,024, policy/value learning rates `2e-6`/`5e-6`, GAE lambda 0.95, gamma 1, and at most 1,500 steps. (Appendix Tables 9-10.)

Experiments run on Linux with 8 NVIDIA A100 GPUs, PyTorch 2.4.0, verl, FSDP, FlashAttention-2, and vLLM; one complete RL run reportedly costs about 960 GPU-hours. A faithful build must additionally pin source revisions, prompt templates as executed, answer extraction/equivalence code, all failed samples, seeds, policy/value checkpoints, `h(c)` semantics, and buffer-round manifests. None is available in the official repository.
