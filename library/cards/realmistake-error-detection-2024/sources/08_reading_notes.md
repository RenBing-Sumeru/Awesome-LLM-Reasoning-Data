1. **One-sentence positioning:** A benchmark testing LLM critics on naturally occurring strong-model errors rather than injected mistakes.
2. **Method takeaway:** It combines three tasks, two response models, expert verification, and four error categories.
3. **Data takeaway:** It contains 900 responses with correctness verdicts, error evidence, and explanations.
4. **Evidence anchor:** Twelve evaluators show broadly low recall on real mistakes, with limited help from voting or self-consistency.
5. **Reuse decision:** It is highly useful for judge robustness audits, and training should preserve the difficulty of natural errors.
