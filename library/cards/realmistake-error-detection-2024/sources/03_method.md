1. **Select tasks:** Three realistic tasks requiring reasoning, constraint following, and contextual or parametric knowledge are chosen without deliberately injecting errors.
2. **Sample responses:** GPT-4 and Llama-2-70B answer normally, preserving naturally occurring correct and incorrect outputs.
3. **Expert verification:** Domain annotators determine whether each response contains a substantive error, provide evidence, and assign one of four error sources.
4. **Test evaluators:** Twelve LLM evaluators produce verdicts and explanations, compared using precision, recall, and cross-task stability.
5. **Analyze strategies:** Self-evaluation, cross-evaluation, repeated sampling, and majority voting are tested for their ability to reduce misses and false alarms.
