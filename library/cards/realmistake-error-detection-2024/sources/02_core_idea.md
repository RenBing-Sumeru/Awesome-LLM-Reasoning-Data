The paper releases ReaLMistake, containing 900 responses generated by GPT-4 and Llama-2-70B on three realistic tasks and verified by experts. Errors are categorized into reasoning correctness, instruction following, context faithfulness, and parametric knowledge, with labels and explanations. Twelve LLM evaluators are tested, revealing generally low recall on real mistakes and unreliable explanatory confidence; self-consistency and majority voting do not fundamentally solve the problem.
