Existing error-detection datasets often start from a correct answer and programmatically inject obvious mistakes, or focus on verifiable domains such as mathematics and code. ReaLMistake begins with real model responses and relies on experts to determine whether an error exists, preserving subtle, partially correct, and fluent failures. Its novelty is evaluating LLM critics on natural errors while jointly analyzing task source, error type, and judging strategy, revealing that synthetic benchmarks may overestimate critic capability.
