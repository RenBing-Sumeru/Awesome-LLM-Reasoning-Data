The paper evaluates GPT-4o, Claude-3.7-Sonnet, and Gemini-2.5-Flash with three independent runs per task and an environment reset between runs; Appendix E adds a single-run-per-domain Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct baseline. Immutable model/API snapshots, seeds, decoding settings, turn/token limits, retry rules, and judge repetitions are not disclosed, so the reported comparisons are tied to incompletely specified closed-model configurations.

Table 5 reports airline-new goal-shift recovery of 92.2% for GPT-4o, 79.2% for Claude-3.7-Sonnet, and 48.6% for Gemini-2.5-Flash. The same table reports retail-new TCRR of 89.14% for GPT-4o and 66.45% for Gemini-2.5-Flash. These are author-reported benchmark outcomes, not independently reproduced results. They show that the proposed metrics distinguish model/configuration behavior on the stated tasks; they do not establish data quality, simulator fidelity, replayability, or suitability for training.

The paper also contains material internal inconsistencies. Table 7 and the immediately following persona-analysis prose give different recovery values for several personas, so persona-specific recovery numbers should not be reused until the raw metric inputs are reconciled. The 315-task total is not transparently recovered from the stated source components, which add to 314. The abstract's 2,835 sequences are not tied to an accessible record manifest; the 315 × 3 models × 3 runs explanation remains arithmetic inference.

The evidence for construction and QA is procedural rather than release-verified. Appendix A supplies the task schema, ten-candidate generation batches, five Markdown references, manual database/tool checks, and goal-shift integration. Appendix B states three runs, state reset, a 10% task review, criteria cross-checking, inter-rater reliability analysis, and updates. It does not publish the generator/judge/simulator configuration, annotator statistics, rejected items, raw run table, or revision history in the accessible text.

Artifact evidence stops at the listing boundary. The MTI-LLM OpenReview page officially exposes a supplementary-material endpoint and the paper says that the full benchmark, harness configurations, and experiments are released there. Because the ZIP itself could not be retrieved, the Card does not claim that code, task JSON, run logs, split files, licenses, hashes, or replay fixtures were inspected. No separate official GitHub, project page, or Hugging Face dataset was confirmed.
