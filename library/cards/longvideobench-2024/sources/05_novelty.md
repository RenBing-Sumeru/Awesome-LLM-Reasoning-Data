The prior baseline is short-video QA, image-based VQA, or long-context text benchmarks without a real video substrate. LongVideoBench changes the object by combining long-duration video, language context, multiple-choice QA, and referred temporal evidence in one release. The direction signal is that multimodal long-context evaluation needs video availability, sampling, subtitle, and evidence-window metadata. The quality signal is the official paper, project page, code, and dataset release. What is not new: multiple-choice QA, video QA, and accuracy scoring. Before reuse, inspect video licenses, takedown risk, subtitle provenance, frame sampling, option artifacts, split policy, and contamination after public release.
