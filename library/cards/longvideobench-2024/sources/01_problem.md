LongVideoBench asks how to evaluate video-language models on long videos where answering requires locating and combining evidence across time, subtitles, and visual content. The primary sources are the 2024 arXiv paper, official project site, GitHub repository, Hugging Face dataset, and NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks listing on the project page. Its decision boundary is long-context multimodal evaluation, not video pretraining data or a video agent environment. A benchmark item contains a video or video reference, duration metadata, question, multiple-choice options, answer key, and referred-context evidence fields. The atlas value is the explicit temporal evidence surface for long multimodal reasoning.
