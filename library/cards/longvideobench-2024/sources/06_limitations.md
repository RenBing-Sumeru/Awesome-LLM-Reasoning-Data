Correctness is answer-key correctness, not guaranteed evidence faithfulness. A model may choose the right option from priors, language cues, or option artifacts without using the intended video segment. Video availability and decoding can change over time, especially for web-sourced media. Results depend heavily on frame sampling rate, temporal coverage, transcript availability, modality budget, and prompt format. Referred-context annotations help audit temporal grounding but can leak answer-relevant information if used as input. The benchmark should not be read as a general video-agent environment or as proof of robust long-horizon visual memory.
