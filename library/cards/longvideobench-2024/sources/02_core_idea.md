The contribution is a long-video multiple-choice QA benchmark with interleaved video-language context and referred-context annotations. The core mechanism is to collect long videos, create questions whose answers require specific temporal evidence, provide candidate options, and score model choices against a fixed answer key. The data object is a video QA instance, not a frame-level caption dataset or free-form video summary. The feedback contract is answer-level multiple-choice accuracy, with evidence fields useful for audit but not replacing the final answer key. Closest comparisons include short-video QA and image-centric multimodal benchmarks; LongVideoBench changes duration, temporal grounding, and multimodal evidence requirements.
