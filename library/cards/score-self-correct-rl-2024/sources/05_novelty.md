Earlier intrinsic-correction methods commonly use prompting, a separate refiner or teacher, or SFT on offline before/after traces. SCoRe changes the construction distribution and the objective together: the learner generates its own first and second attempts online, Stage I deliberately holds the first-turn policy near the base distribution while improving the second turn, and Stage II rewards changes in correctness in addition to terminal performance.

The direction signal for this atlas is the separation between collecting a plausible repair pair and training a policy that can repair its own current errors. The progress bonus also distinguishes wrong-to-right correction from a policy that merely raises first-turn accuracy and then leaves the answer unchanged. This makes policy checkpoint, training stage, both outcome rewards, and sampling metadata necessary lineage fields for an auditable trace.

SCoRe does not introduce process-level verification, a public correction dataset, or an oracle-free training reward. Ground-truth answers and unit tests still define training correctness, and REINFORCE with KL regularization is existing machinery. The contribution is the two-stage organization and reward shaping for on-policy intrinsic correction.
