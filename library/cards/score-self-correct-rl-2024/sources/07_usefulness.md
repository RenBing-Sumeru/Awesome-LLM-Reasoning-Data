For the Rollout, Search, and Test-Time Trace Data track, SCoRe supplies a concrete schema for a sequential correction record: task and split identity, policy checkpoint, training stage, first attempt, correction instruction, second attempt, extracted answers, `r1`, `r2`, progress bonus, KL reference, sampling parameters, and terminal checker result. Retaining only the second answer would erase the transition that the objective is designed to reward.

As a construction reference, it helps distinguish offline repair pairs from on-policy episodes and identifies where policy drift enters lineage. As an RLVR reference, it shows how programmatic outcome rewards can supervise a multi-turn episode without becoming process labels. As a test-time-compute reference, it motivates comparing sequential correction and parallel sampling under an equal total sample budget.

Audits should measure no-change edits, wrong-to-right and right-to-wrong transitions, answer-extraction errors, test coverage, checkpoint-selection bias, split overlap, and whether stored traces came from the stated policy version. Because no raw trace artifact was confirmed, these are requirements for a future release rather than claims about an existing public dataset.
